# How to Delete Your Account

How a Black Desert Online player removes the information given at sign-up, by deleting a Pearl Abyss account or by unlinking an SNS login, and what happens to game data.

## Account deletion: overview

Using Black Desert Online services (website, game, customer support and so on) requires a membership. The information collected at sign-up depends on how the player signed up. To remove it, a player goes through Unlink Accounts, Account Deletion, or both, depending on the membership type. Deleting an account also deletes all game data (items, characters and so on), and that data cannot be recovered afterwards, so the decision should be made carefully.

## Account deletion: membership types

There are three kinds of membership. A Pearl Abyss Account is a single sign-up that works for the Pearl Abyss homepage and games. Currently only Shadow Arena and Black Desert Online NA/EU accept it, and the player must accept each game's Terms of Service first. An SNS Account login lets a player sign in through another site or social account such as Facebook or Google, although some services may ask for a switch to a Pearl Abyss account. A Changed SNS login is an SNS account converted into a Pearl Abyss account by adding more information after logging in; the player can then log in with either the Pearl Abyss email or the SNS account.

## Account deletion: information collected per sign-up method

The information collected at sign-up depends on the sign-up method:

| Sign-up method | Collected information |
| --- | --- |
| Pearl Abyss Account | Email Address (Account Name), Password, Name, Place of Residence, Date of Birth |
| Steam Account | Email Address (Account Name), Member Identification Number, Name, Place of Residence, Date of Birth |
| SNS Account | Member Identification Number, Name, Place of Residence, Date of Birth |
| SNS Account Changed to Pearl Abyss Account | Email Address (Account Name), Password |

## Account deletion: steps for a Pearl Abyss Account

To delete a Pearl Abyss account:

1. Sign in on the Black Desert Online website.
2. Click the profile icon at the top right to open the Account Information page.
3. Click Delete Account in the left-hand menu.
4. Read all the account deletion notices. All game data, such as items and characters, is erased with the account and cannot be restored.
5. Tick the account deletion checkbox and click the Delete Account button.
6. On the email verification page, click Send Verification Email.
7. Enter the verification code that arrives by email and click Confirm.
8. Check that the deletion request went through and note the scheduled deletion date.

## Account deletion: waiting period and canceling

After a player applies for account deletion, the account is permanently deleted after 15 days. During those 15 days the player can still cancel the deletion by logging in to the account.

## Account deletion: steps for an SNS Account login

To delete the information collected through an SNS login, the player unlinks the account:

1. Sign in on the Black Desert Online website with the social media account.
2. Click the profile icon at the top right to open the Account Information page.
3. Click Link Account in the left-hand menu.
4. Click the Open ID to unlink. It is unlinked immediately and its data is deleted.

Conditions: if the SNS login has not yet been changed to a Pearl Abyss account, the player must first enter the additional information and click Sign Up to finish the account change before going further. After unlinking, that Open ID can no longer be used to log in, but the transferred Pearl Abyss account still works. To log in with an Open ID again, log in to the website with the email account and re-link it in the Link Account menu.

## Account deletion: steps for an SNS Account changed to a Pearl Abyss Account

To delete all the information collected when a player logged in with an SNS account and then changed to a Pearl Abyss account, the process is the same as deleting a Pearl Abyss account. Follow the Pearl Abyss account deletion steps: open Account Information from the profile icon, click Delete Account, read the notices, confirm with the email verification code, and wait 15 days for permanent deletion.
