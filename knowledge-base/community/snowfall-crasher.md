# Snowfall Crasher

Snowfall Crasher was a winter event in January 2025 built around snowboarding. The event has ended, but the snowboarding activity stays available all year.

## Snowfall Crasher: tracks and preparation

There are two snowboarding tracks: Snowfall Crasher: Alpine Glide, near Jade Starlight Forest, and Snowfall Crasher: Powder Rush, near Eilton. The wiki suggests starting at Alpine Glide. There, talk to Lando and buy the Classic Snowboard (7 Days). Also buy Snowfall Crasher Alpine Glide Flares and Powder Rush Flares, which teleport you straight to the tracks. You can instead ride the logs carried by a griffon for a scenic trip to the tracks.

## Snowfall Crasher: snowboard controls

Near the NPC Uyanga, equip the Classic Snowboard from your inventory and your character begins gliding down the mountain automatically. Controls on the page: D or A turns, and D or A with Shift drifts. Space jumps, and Q or E performs an aerial trick during a jump. The page also lists keys for accelerating and decelerating, but that part of its table is garbled, so the exact keys are not confirmed. If you fall off the board, register the [Event] Classic Snowboard in a quick slot for easier remounting. If you fall off the track, walk through the portal to return to the top of the mountain.

## Snowfall Crasher: quest challenge

The NPC Uyanga, in Alpine Glide or Powder Rush, gives a quest that rewards 100 Contribution Points. To complete it, finish the snow track in under 3 minutes without falling. Any fall makes the quest fail.
