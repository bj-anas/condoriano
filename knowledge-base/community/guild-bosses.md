# Guild Bosses

Guild Summon Bosses are bosses a guild summons and fights in an exclusive Expedition Region. This page covers how summoning works, entry restrictions, and each boss with its tips and loot: Khan, Org, Moghulis, Ferrid, Giant Mudster and Gumiho & Duoksini.

## Guild Bosses: how summoning works

The Guild Summon Bosses are Khan, Org, Moghulis, Ferrid and Giant Mudster. Their Summon Scroll Pieces are earned from particular Guild Missions. A guild member needs the Boss Subjugation permission to summon any of them. Once all pieces are collected, the Find Location and Summon windows become available. At the summoning location, press Enter to go into the boss-exclusive Expedition Region. After entering, you set the difficulty and summon the boss. The Find Location button guides you to the spot, for example Oquilla's Eye for Khan. The Enter/Exit Expedition Region button moves you in and out. The Expedition Region can be created by the Guild Master or a member with permission, and cannot be created on Node War, Conquest War or Arsha servers.

## Guild Bosses: when you cannot enter the Expedition Region

Entry to the Guild Boss Expedition Region is blocked in these cases:
- You are an Apprentice Guild Member or a War Hero.
- Forced PvP (Alt+C) is on, or you attacked or killed another Adventurer while in Forced PvP. This does not apply if your Karma is negative or you have Outlaw status.
- You are in duel mode.
- You are mounted.
- Your character is not in Standby.
- You are in the Elvia Realm.

## Khan, Eye of the Great Ocean: summoning and cannons

In the Guild (G) window, under the Summon Guild Boss category, Khan can be summoned once per week from the summon area near Oquilla's Eye. To fight Khan you need a [Guild] Monsterbane Cannon Assembly Kit and [Guild] Monsterbane Cannonballs, obtained from the Guild Manager or bought in the Guild Shop with Guild Funds. The page's table pairs each of Old Moon Combat Seal and Old Moon Life Seal with 1 kit and 10 cannonballs. The cannon is destroyed 30 minutes after being installed or by Khan's attacks, and all loaded cannonballs vanish with it. It builds Points, which power special shots:

| Fire method | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Normal Shot | Like a standard Cannon but recharges faster, and each shot adds Points |
| Overcharged Shot | Slower to fire and recharge, but deals more damage, especially after stacks are built |
| Rapid Fire | Press X after building stacks; reloads faster and still builds stacks |

## Khan, Eye of the Great Ocean: fight tips

When Khan is summoned, other Adventurers at Oquilla's Eye, not only the guild's members, can be hurt. Khan's lightning targets whoever has done the most damage to it. It summons Black Crystal Seaweed during the fight, and leaving the seaweed alive makes the fight much harder and lets Khan recover HP. Protty monsters guard the seaweed and vanish when it is destroyed. Destroying it drops Black Crystal Cannonballs, which are stronger than [Guild] Monsterbane Cannonballs. A warning message appears before Khan's powerful attacks, so use nearby terrain as cover.

## Khan's Heart: crafting

Khan's Concentrated Magic is the material for crafting Khan's Heart. Craft it in Processing (L) using Simple Alchemy with Khan's Concentrated Magic x1, Alchemy Stone Shard x400, Magical Shard x200, Trace of Nature x100, and one spirit stone that decides the type: Destruction Spirit Stone x1 for Khan's Heart: Destruction, Guardian Spirit Stone x1 for Khan's Heart: Protection, or Life Spirit Stone x1 for Khan's Heart: Life.

## Org guild boss

Org is summoned at the Western Cliff near Glish. It uses many flame attacks, including chasing fireballs and setting the area alight. At a certain HP level, a message says Org is preparing to set everything ablaze, followed by a large flame wave that deals heavy damage. Avoid it by standing in the circular Safe Zone marked by a yellow glow.

## Moghulis guild boss

Moghulis is summoned in the Stonetail Wasteland, which lies east of the Stonetail Horse Ranch in Stonetail Hills. Its rocky body smashes the ground and rolls after Adventurers. When its Strength falls to a certain level, it summons Golem minions and goes idle. In that state it is invincible, so defeat the summoned Golems first.

## Ferrid guild boss

Ferrid is summoned in Omar Lava Cave. Its main attacks are an AoE stun in front of it, Summon Lava and a ranged knockdown made of two hits, though most of its damage is from basic attacks. Fight from behind, since standing in front exposes you to the stun. After Ferrid slams the ground with its fist, Summon Lava follows.

## Giant Mudster guild boss

The Giant Mudster is summoned in the Glish Ruins. It is slow but hits hard over a wide area and can lower Movement Speed and Attack Speed. Its strong attacks usually start when it suddenly shrinks. A shrink with no warning means it charges at its target, so leave its path. If it scatters mud first, it summons small Mudsters that explode after a set time, so kill them or dodge to a safe spot. Its most dangerous attack stuns nearby characters, then shrinks, and spikes burst from the floor dealing heavy damage. If you are caught in the stun, use Emergency Escape (V key).

## Gumiho & Duoksini guild boss

Summoning Gumiho & Duoksini costs 25,000 Guild Fame. The summon happens near the Western Guard Camp, once a week, and beating them earns 10,000 Guild Fame. Both appear together. Defeat both to make a Treasure Chest appear, then destroy it to claim the rewards.
