# Music Album, Compose & Play

The Compose & Play system lets Adventurers write, save, share and play their own music with instruments. This page covers the Music Album menu, the compose window, Florchestra instruments and effectors, Musicianship, and how to get beginner, Florchestra and Marni's instruments.

## Music Album: how to open it and its options

Open the Music Album from the main menu (hotkey: Esc), then Community, then Music Album. The window offers these options: My Instruments (lists the instruments you can use), Compose (opens the Compose/Score window), Album (scores shared by other Adventurers that you can listen to or download), Volume (opens the Audio Settings), and Close. In the Album you can press the heart icon to like someone's music.

## Music Album: Compose window settings

In the Compose/Score window you enter notes. The settings are:
- Effector: effector adjustments for Florchestra instruments.
- BPM: speed of the music. The fastest is 180, and lowering it slows the music.
- Meter: the number of beats and how they are divided.
- Note Attribute: note length such as 1/4, 1/8 or 1/16.
- Velocity: the default maximum is 127, and you can click individual notes to change their velocity.
- Instrument: four types, wind, string, keyboard and percussion. In a party, an ensemble is possible. Florchestra and Marni's instruments are highlighted in different colors.
- Edit Mode adds notes. Select Mode edits notes already placed, and you can drag over several notes to move or change them together, for example to drop them an octave.
- Reset deletes the whole score.
- Temporary Save and Save are at the bottom right.

Press Play on any character that has unlocked instruments to hear a song.

## Music Album: sharing compositions

To share a song, open the Compose/Score window, press the cogwheel under Etc. and choose Share. Toggle Allow Download so other Adventurers can download your music. Toggle Register Music so it is shared in the Album. If both stay off, the song appears only in My Folder.

## Music Album: Musicianship skill and song length

Before the April 17 update, daily quests raised Musicianship EXP, and the grade limited how long songs could be. Those daily quests are now deleted, and the grade no longer affects song length. The current limit for the Music Album is 10,000 notes. Musicianship EXP is gained by performing a song and is now used for a ranking of Adventurers with the highest EXP.

## Florchestra instruments and effectors

There are 8 Florchestra instruments in total. Five are upgraded versions of the old beginner instruments and 3 are completely new ones. They look more refined and can use effectors, which change the sound. Florchestra effectors fall into two kinds: spatial (gives a sense of space, like a room or hall) and modulation (changes tone, such as deeper or stranger). Effectors work only with Florchestra instruments. Their settings are saved with the song, not with each instrument, and are adjusted in the Compose window. Effectors use a lot of PC CPU, so they are limited this way. As a result, if another group plays a different song beside you, you hear their sound with your song's effector settings.

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Reverb Time | Adds reverberation for a sense of space |
| Delay Feedback | Adjusts the number of echoes |
| Chorus Feedback | Adjusts the amount of repeated sound |
| Chorus LFO Depth | Adjusts modulation depth |
| Chorus LFO Frequency | Adjusts modulation frequency |

AuxSend in the Florchestra Instruments category controls how much sound goes to the effector, and it lights up when sound is being sent. Musical techniques, set at the upper right of the Compose window, differ by instrument type. Notes take the color of the selected technique. You cannot enter many techniques on the same beat, and the last one you choose applies to all of them.

## Beginner instruments: where to buy

You can buy the 5 basic instruments at Artina's Shop in Calpheon, Kamasylvia, O'dyllita or Valencia. Use the magnifying glass beside the minimap to find the exact shop. Each costs 5,000,000 silver. They are Beginner Violin, Beginner Cymbals, Beginner Recorder, Beginner Piano and Beginner Harp.

## Florchestra instruments: how to obtain

Using a character of level 56 or higher, finish the quest Artina's Flondor Instrument Shop, which Artina gives at Lake Flondor. This lets you start the [Florchestra] Legendary Musician quest. Finishing it gives all 8 Florchestra instruments: Handpan, Drum Set, Contrabass, Violin, Harp, Piano, Flute and Acoustic Guitar. Two newer instruments, the Florchestra Horn and Florchestra Clarinet, cost 5 million Silver each from Artina after you finish the quests in the [Music] The Heavenly Sound of Florchestra Instruments! suggested quest category.

## Marni's instruments: overview and requirements

Marni's instruments are 4 kinds of Marnian, the Marnibass and 3 Marni Electric Guitars. The Marnians are Wavy Planet, Illusion Tree, Secret Note and Sandwich, each with 4 sounds (16 styles in all). The guitars are Silver Wave, Highway and Hexe Glam, each with 3 sound types. You craft Marnians and the Marnibass through Manufacture (hotkey: L) using the matching Tremor Converticator. First finish the questline unlocked by buying Music Score: Genius of Keplan from Artina, available once per family. Toggle the [Life] quest type to accept the music quests. All classes can use these questlines and shops. The materials are expensive, so save silver first.
