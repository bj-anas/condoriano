# Party & Platoon

How Party and Platoon grouping works: inviting, managing members, finding groups, experience sharing and party elixirs.

## Party and Platoon: size and limits

A Party holds up to 5 Adventurers. A Platoon is a larger group of up to 20 Adventurers. Being in a Party makes fighting strong monsters easier, but boss encounters in the main questline may not work while you are in one, so leave the Party before doing an affected quest. Some content can also only be started by the Party leader.

## Party and Platoon: inviting players

For a Party, stand near the other Adventurer, hit Ctrl to turn on the mouse cursor, then click the Party Invite button that shows up when you interact with them. You can also click their name in chat and invite them there, but that only works if they are on your server. Once a Party exists, the mouse cursor must be active to use party functions. To invite someone to a Platoon, click their name in chat and invite them from there.

## Party and Platoon: managing members

In a Party, the leader can banish a member or hand the leader role to someone else. Click the arrow to the right of the member's name to see these options. Banishing asks you to confirm. To leave a Party, click the red icon next to your own character's name. Icons beside each member show their distance from you, which affects experience sharing, plus a follow icon. Following puts your character into a non-combat stance if they are in one, and the follow ends if the person you follow enters combat stance. The [-] icon on the left shrinks the party panel. The Platoon panel is simpler: you can leave it, and a Platoon leader can name a new leader or banish members.

## Party and Platoon: Find Party/Platoon recruitment

Open Find Party/Platoon from the Main Menu (hotkey: Esc), in the Community tab. It lists Adventurers recruiting for Parties and Platoons, and a search window narrows the list. To recruit yourself, press Post Ad at the top left, write the ad, set the minimum level of members and register it. The ad can be advertised in chat with the megaphone button left of the chat name, as a server message once every minute, costing 1 energy per message. To join a listed group, press Apply. If that group is on another server, press Change Server first, then Apply. Your class and level are sent to the leader, who can then invite you.

## Party and Platoon: map sharing in a Party

Party members appear on the World Map (hotkey: M) and the minimap in the top right corner. A member can share a location with Shift+LMB on their own world map, or pin a flag with Shift+double click. This also works on the minimap. A member who is too far away is shown as a blue arrow pointing in their direction.

## Party and Platoon: Party experience sharing

Monster EXP grows with Party size, but is split among members:

| Party size | Monster EXP | EXP per person |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Adventurer | 100% | 100% |
| 2 Adventurers | 120% | 60% |
| 3 Adventurers | 150% | 50% |
| 4 Adventurers | 200% | 50% |
| 5 Adventurers | 250% | 50% |

Members who are far away do not get this benefit. The Wi-Fi-style party distance bonus icon shows how close members are, and full EXP comes when it is full. All PvE Monster Zones allow Parties, but some are built for them and are labeled on the World Map (hotkey: M) and in the Monster Zone Information window from the main menu.

## Party and Platoon: Party elixirs

When you are in a Party, prepare elixirs for everyone. Blue grade elixirs apply to the whole Party. If the party distance bonus is active, the elixir is applied to everyone in the group immediately.
