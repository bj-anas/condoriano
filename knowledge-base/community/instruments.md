# Instruments

The instruments a character can play in Black Desert: the Flute, Small Drum, Scream Ent Cymbals and Garnier Guitar, with where to get each and how to play it.

## Instruments: Flute

The Flute is rented from several NPCs for 1 contribution point. Examples are Pulvio in Velia and Puia in Heidel. After equipping it, press LMB to begin playing. A minigame starts and your character walks forward. Use LMB and RMB to avoid falling notes. Touching a falling note ends the minigame, and your character stops moving and playing.

## Instruments: Small Drum

The Small Drum costs 2 contribution points to rent, from the Choir Leader NPC in Calpheon City's Noble Quarter. After equipping it, press the spacebar to start playing. The minigame works the same way as the Flute one. Your character walks forward while drumming, and hitting a falling note ends the action.

## Instruments: Scream Ent Cymbals

The Scream Ent Cymbals are a quest reward. The Choir Leader NPC starts a short questline whose first quest is Choir Supporters Request. Each character of Lv. 35 or above can do it once. To play, equip the cymbals and press the spacebar. The character must stand still while playing, and moving stops the action.

## Instruments: Garnier Guitar

The Garnier Guitar is also a quest reward. The first quest is Ever-joyful Garnier Nomad Troupe, given by Rubin in Calpheon City, and each character of Lv. 50 or above can do it once. To play, equip the guitar and press the spacebar. As with the cymbals, the character must stand still, and moving stops the action.
