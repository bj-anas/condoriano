# Yar!

Yar! is a card minigame in Black Desert where you draw and keep cards, then commit them to scoring hands to beat an opponent's total. This page covers the rules, hands and points, NPC opponents, adventurer matches and titles.

## Yar!: what the minigame is

Yar! is a turn-based card minigame played against another Adventurer or a local NPC. On each turn you draw a hand of five cards and can keep some and redraw the rest a limited number of times. Then you commit the cards to one named hand for points. The player whose combined score across all hands is higher wins. The deck has the symbols sun, moon, star and flower, and numbers 1 (A) to 6. The number of draws and the hands available depend on the game mode or difficulty. The Yar! Invite feature lets you play with a faraway Adventurer, for example while traveling or at sea. The game was popularized by a Shai girl named Yar who appeared in Velia.

## Yar!: rules of a turn

Each turn goes like this: draw 5 cards, left-click the ones you want to keep, and redraw the others. You can unselect a kept card before redrawing, and kept cards stay through redraws that turn. The number of redraws depends on game mode. Then pick one named hand on the left side of the Yar! window by selecting the gray number beside it. You must commit to a hand by the end of your turn. If time runs out, 0 points are placed on a random remaining hand. A hand that has been scored cannot be chosen again or changed, and in later rounds only unscored hands are available. You may also deliberately score a hand at 0 points. Once all hands are scored, the player with the most points wins.

## Yar!: number hands (Bit o' Ones to Stack o' Sixes)

Each hand gives from 0 to 50 points. These hands add up the cards of one number:

| Hand | Max points | How it scores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bit o' Ones | 5 | Sum of all Aces (1s) |
| Bunch o' Twos | 10 | Sum of all 2s |
| Lump o' Threes | 15 | Sum of all 3s |
| Nest o' Fours | 20 | Sum of all 4s |
| Clump o' Fives | 25 | Sum of all 5s |
| Stack o' Sixes | 30 | Sum of all 6s |

For example, 4,2,2,4,4 scores 12 in Nest o' Fours. The wiki's tips: aim for at least 3 matching cards for the higher numbers, and use Bit o' Ones or Bunch o' Twos as a cheap place to dump a bad hand.

## Yar!: matching and special hands

| Hand | Points | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mates | up to 12 | 2 cards of the same number, sum of those cards |
| Peak | up to 18 | 3 cards of the same number, sum of those cards |
| Dragon Claw | up to 24 | 4 cards of the same number, sum of those cards |
| Double Trouble | 0 or 15 | Two pairs, with different numbers |
| Den of Wolves | 0 or 25 | Three of one number plus two of another number |
| Yar! | 0 or 50 | 5 cards of the same number |
| Broken Arrow | 0 or 30 | 4 cards in sequence |
| Straight Shot | 0 or 40 | 5 cards in sequence |
| Chance | up to 30 | Sum of all five cards |

Tips from the wiki: go for Dragon Claw first, and place three of a kind in Mates or Peak if the fourth never shows. For Straight Shot, start from 2,3,4,5 so either a 1 or a 6 completes it. Chance works best with 5s and 6s and is a safe fallback. Example: A/A/A/2/2 scores 3 in Bit o' Ones, 4 in Bunch o' Twos, or a flat 25 in Den of Wolves.

## Yar!: matches against other Adventurers

Open the main menu (hotkey: Esc), go to Community, then Yar!. Choose a game mode first, because it decides the hands available and the number of draws. Then enter your opponent's family name to send an invite, and the match starts when they accept. Click the icons at the top to see the hands for each mode. If you have no opponent, use the Random mode to be matched with another Adventurer.
