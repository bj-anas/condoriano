# Guild Mounts

Guild mounts belong to a guild rather than one player. This page covers the Cadria elephant (land) and the guild galley (sea): how to obtain them, craft them, upgrade the galley and use them.

## Guild mounts: overview

A guild mount is owned by the guild, so any member can use it once the guild has obtained it. There are two kinds. The land guild mount is the Cadria elephant, important in Node War and Conquest War for destroying barricades and defensive structures. The sea guild mount is the galley, which carries many guild members across the sea.

## Guild elephants: ways to obtain one

A guild gets an elephant in one of two ways. Either win a guild house auction and open an elephant nursery there, or, if the guild holds an old Valencian Elephant registration emblem, complete a special quest. The nursery needs the guild skill Monstrous Force. In the Valencia territory, guild house auctions are held at Sand Grain Bazaar and at the capital, Valencia. The guild master opens the nursery and raises the elephant, and members can send workers to help.

## Guild mounts: guild house auction rules

As written on the Guild Mounts page:
- The auction starts every other Friday at 6:00 PM and ends after 24 hours.
- Only guild masters can bid, through guild managers in Olvia, Velia, Heidel, Calpheon, Altinova, Sand Grain Bazaar and Valencia.
- The starting price is the previous owner's winning bid. If nobody bids on a house, its start price drops by 50%. Collect your previous bid before bidding again.
- Up to 3 bids, on only one house per auction.
- It is a blind auction, and a system message says whether you won.
- A winner gets a mail. The maintenance fee is 5% of the winning bid, paid at midnight daily from guild funds.
- A bid within the last 2 minutes extends the auction by 1 minute. The auction extends by 24 hours if some houses got no bids. You can use the house until the next auction begins.

## Guild elephants: taming a baby elephant

Baby elephants live on the plains and wild regions of Valencia. Taming works like taming horses: throw a rope at the elephant, move to it with the direction keys, and keep tapping the spacebar to calm it if it rears up. When you reach it, open the inventory and feed it [Guild] Desert Peanut until satisfied. Buy the peanuts from the Guild Stable Keeper NPC with guild funds. Taming succeeds if the elephant does not kick you and run away. A tamed elephant follows you. Take it to the Guild Stable Keeper NPC and press Register to exchange it for a guild crafting item. You must register it before it can be raised.

## Guild elephants: raising the Cadria elephant

Put the registered baby elephant in the guild storage and send workers to raise it. The other materials can be bought in the guild shop, and crafting uses Guild Processing (hotkey: L) inside the guild house. Materials for the Cadria Elephant:

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| [Guild] Clearance Permit | 450 |
| [Guild] Baby Elephant | 1 |
| [Guild] Cadria Elephant Drinking Water | 480 |
| [Guild] Cadria Elephant Food | 960 |
| [Guild] Restored Cadry's Forbidden Book | 100 |
| [Guild] Old Moon Magic Neutralizer | 100 |

Clearance Permits come from giving clearance requests to a Guild Manager NPC, and the requests are crafted by Guild Processing. The fully grown elephant arrives in the guild storage as a mount registration emblem, which you register at the Guild Stable Keeper NPC. The Cadria elephant is fast, has high HP, good acceleration, handling and brakes, and 5 battlefield skills. See the Crafting Notes (hotkey: F1 or F2) for details.

## Guild elephants: quest shortcut with a Valencia Elephant registration

If your guild has a [Guild] Registration: Valencia Elephant, only the guild master can take it to Yan in Valencia. Yan's quest asks for [Guild] Cadria Elephant Drinking Water x500, [Guild] Cadria Elephant Food x1,000 and the [Guild] Registration: Valencia Elephant x1. The reward is [Guild] Registration: Cadria Elephant.

## Guild galley: obtaining it through the quest

A galley is easier to get than an elephant, and there are two ways. The first is the quest [Guild] I Have a Dream, available only to the guild master. Find it in the Quest window (hotkey: O) under the Suggested tab. Give NPC Yan 150 [Guild] Old Moon Combat Seal and 150 [Guild] Old Moon Life Seal, and the reward is a [Guild] Ship License: Galley mount emblem. A guild can own at most 3 guild galleys, and since one guild master may complete this quest only once, the other 2 must be crafted. Register the license at any Guild Wharf Manager NPC. The guild master and officers can take the galley in and out of the wharf.

## Guild galley: crafting it in a guild house shipyard

The second way is to win a guild house auction and open a shipyard, which needs the guild skill Building Boat. The page names guild houses in the territories of Balenos, Calpheon and Mediah. The guild master opens the shipyard, and members can send workers. Materials:

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| [Guild] Clearance Permit | 300 |
| [Guild] Galley Plan | 25 |
| [Guild] Iron Core Timber Square | 800 |
| [Guild] Desert Alloy Ingot | 600 |
| [Guild] Spirit-laden Plywood | 1500 |
| [Guild] Wind-laden Flax Cloth | 160 |

Blueprint pieces are now sold without conditions by Guild Wharf Manager NPCs in all towns, and the other materials can be crafted or bought there. Clearance Permits come from Guild Processing, and once everything is in the guild storage you can craft the galley.

## Improved guild galley: skills

The upgraded galley has better performance and a rowing minigame for faster sailing:
- Ram (Sailing Beginner 1, F): charges forward into the enemy ahead.
- Deck (Sailing Beginner 1, interact with R): up to 10 guild members enter the deck and play the Cap'n's Orders minigame.
- Cap'n's Orders (Sailing Beginner 1, E): the deck crew charges Wind's Favor based on minigame success.
- PowerAccel. (Sailing Apprentice 1, SPACE + Up): quick forward acceleration.
- BreezySail (Sailing Skilled 1, SHIFT + Up at a certain speed): faster acceleration.
- Fair Winds: BreezySail (Sailing Skilled 1, SHIFT + Up with Wind's Favor full): uses all the energy to go even faster.
- Quick-Retreat (Sailing Professional 1, SHIFT + Down): fast reverse.
- AbruptTurn (Sailing Artisan 1, Left or Right + Down): fast turn while sailing.
- Smooth-Turn (Sailing Master 1, Left or Right on standby): turn quickly while stopped.
- Bull's-Eye (Sailing Guru 1): cannon reload 1 second faster on board.

## Improved guild galley: Cap'n's Orders minigame and Wind's Favor

Sailors go to the inner deck by interacting with the deck, which fits up to 10 members. The captain and the crew on deck see the Wind's Favor gauge and Sailing Status, and the Galley widget can be moved and toggled with the Rowing Status button. While sailing, using Cap'n's Orders (E) starts a minigame for deck members. When the oar comes down, press and hold SPACE over the yellow area and release when it ends. If there is no input during the first rowing, the minigame ends. When Wind's Favor is full, Fair Winds: BreezySail (SHIFT + W) spends it to sail faster.

## Improved guild galley: upgrade materials

Upgrade materials can be bought with guild funds at the guild shop, and you can reuse items from sea monsters in naval combat quests. The Guild Wharf Manager NPC sells: [Guild] Galley: Ivory Figurehead, Enhanced Plating, Maria Cannon, High Wind Sail, [Guild] Goddess's Tear Crystal x100, and [Guild] Blueprint: Improved Galley x10. Other materials:
- [Guild] Abyss-drenched Ingot x100: weekly quests from Elro, the Guild Wharf Manager at Oquilla's Eye, give 10 as a select reward, or craft it by Manufacturing from [Guild] Steel Candidum Shell x1 with [Guild] Lavania Special Alloy Ingot x100 (makes 100), or [Guild] Candidum Shell x2 with Lavania Special Alloy Ingot x10 (makes 1-2).
- [Guild] Great Ocean-drenched Plywood x100: [Guild] Nineshark's Fin x1 with [Guild] Trent Treant Plywood x100 (makes 100), or Nineshark's Horn Fragment x10 with Trent Treant Plywood x10 (makes 1).
- [Guild] Deep Sea-drenched Timber Square x100: [Guild] Black Rust Tongue x1 with Trent Treant Plywood x100 (makes 100), or Black Rust Jawbone x10 with Trent Treant Plywood x10 (makes 1).

## Guild mounts: how they are used

Elephants help win Node War and Siege War, especially on offense, since they destroy barricades and defensive structures. With the skill Fore Chop, an elephant can also block an enemy access point. Guild galleys carry the guild at sea for activities such as hunting sea monsters or challenging Khan, Eye of the Great Ocean.
