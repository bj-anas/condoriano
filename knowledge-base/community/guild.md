# Guild

A guild is a group of Adventurers sharing exclusive content and shared rewards. This page covers creating and joining a guild, ranks and authorities, Guild Fame, funds, missions, skills, guild war, War Heroes, the guild manor and alliances.

## Guild: creating a guild

Visit a Guild Manager NPC, found in most large cities, to create a guild. It costs 100,000 silver. 20,000 silver is a commission fee and the other 80,000 silver becomes guild funds, which cannot be taken out directly. They pay members' daily pay, guild items and missions. A new guild is small, holds 15 Adventurers and can only take small guild missions. Requirements: you cannot already be in a guild, a 24-hour wait applies after you leave one before you can join or create another, and the name must be 3-16 alphanumeric characters with no spaces or special characters and not already taken. Most members can use the guild shop, but only high-ranking members can buy items such as conquest war structures and naval combat supplies. Shop purchases come straight out of guild funds.

## Guild: emblem and alliance emblem

To register a Guild Emblem, buy a Guild Emblem Certificate from a Guild Manager. Put a 60 x 60 pixel file named Gmark.png in Documents/Black Desert and register it through the Guild (G) UI. This is restricted to the Guild Master. For an Alliance Emblem, use another Guild Emblem Certificate and a 60 x 60 pixel file named GAllianceMark.png in the same folder. Only the Alliance Master can set it. Click the Register/Change Guild Emblem button in the guild window while holding a [Guild] Emblem Certificate. A confirmation window appears first.

## Guild: base servers

Guild base servers let members switch freely between the chosen servers with no cooldown. Only the Guild Master can choose them, and the choice can change every 30 days. The groups are Valencia 1-3, Valencia 4-6, Balenos 1-3, Balenos 4-6, Serendia 1-3, Serendia 4-6, Mediah 1-3, Mediah 4-5, Calpheon 1-3, Calpheon 4-5, Ulukita 1-3 and Edania 1-3.

## Guild: contract and joining

You can invite a player to a guild on any channel or server, using the radial interaction menu or the Recruit Guild Member tab of the Guild window (hotkey: G). Membership is by Family, and each Family can be in only one guild. The invitee signs the contract to join. For the first week they have the Apprentice rank. Only members with an active contract get benefits such as the EXP bonus, HP increase or damage increase from guild skills. Check contract status in the rightmost column of the Guild Member Status tab. Expired contracts must be renewed, on any channel or server.

## Guild: contract renewal, leaving and kicking

A contract can be renewed 24 hours after signing. Each 100 Activity points raises your current Daily Pay by 1%, up to 100% per renewal, and Daily Pay is capped at 5,000,000 Silver. Leaving after the contract ends carries no penalty, but leaving during the contract requires paying a penalty. A member who is kicked gets three times their current pay as compensation. Ranks above officer can renew their own contract and those of lower ranks. Officers can renew contracts of mission specialists, cannoneers, quartermasters and general members, but not their own.

## Guild: activity points

Every member with a contract adds to the guild's EXP through guild activity, whatever their level. Sources include guild missions, guild crafting, guild boss subjugations, leveling life skills, guild wars, node and conquest wars, and Blue Battlefield. The point rules for these can be changed, except for node and conquest wars. In the Activity column of the Guild Member Status tab, the white number is overall activity and the green number is the current contract's contribution.

## Guild: ranks and default authorities

A guild has one advisor, who helps the master oversee it. Officers are unlimited in number, and the guild master can make everyone or no one an officer. Default authority by rank:

| Rank | Default authority |
| --- | --- |
| Master and Advisor | All authorities |
| Staff and Secretary | Everything officers have, plus Ban, Set activity points, Grant authorities, Reset guild skills, Appoint and demote positions, Set member capacity, Succeed guild master when offline, Guild crafting. Up to 3 staff, only 1 secretary |
| Officer | Invite and renew contracts, intro and recruiting, notices, missions, guild skills, summon bosses, mounts, storage, war declaration, War Hero hiring, Node War application, forts and flags, elephant nursery, siege weapons, Conquest gates, colors, Arena of Arsha, Guild League, shop, allowance, support funds |
| Quartermaster, Cannoneer and Mission Specialist | Missions, storage, forts and annexes, flags, elephant nursery, siege weapon removal, Conquest gates, shop |
| General Member | Guild storage and guild shop |
| Apprentice | Guild shop only; lasts 7 days |

New members start as Apprentices and are promoted to general members after 7 days. Guilds can set custom permissions per rank. Change positions from the member list in the Guild (G) UI.

## Guild: authorities in the Management and Activities groups

Authorities fall into four groups. Management: Invite/Renew Contract, Kick, Appoint/Demote Positions, Set Member Capacity (using Guild Funds), Set Activity Points, Set Intro/Promote/Recruit, Edit Notice/Saying, Refresh Member Info, Change Guild Name/Emblem, Grant Authorities. Activities: Accept/Complete Missions, Learn/Use/Reset Guild Skills, Guild Crafting, Manage Guild Mounts (summon, recover and manage equipment of Elephants, Galleys and other mounts), Summon Bosses, Use Guild Storage, and Handle Doomcaller (a powerful Guild Mount). Set a member's position or authorities from Guild window, Guild Member Status, click the member.

## Guild: authorities in the Combat and Finances groups

Combat: Declare War, Hire War Hero, Apply for Node War, Install/Demolish Fort/Annex, Collect/Install Flag (the Indomitable Flag outpost), Use Elephant Nursery, Remotely Remove Siege Weapons/Interact with Conquest Gates, Change Node/Conquest Colors, Apply/Reserve Arena of Arsha and Apply for Guild League, and Change Node War Mode (switch Tier 2 or higher Node Wars to Occupation or Construction mode). Finances: Use Guild Shop, Distribute Allowance/Bonus, Collect Support Funds, and Guild House Auction/Pay Fees (bid and pay maintenance fees).

## Guild: leadership and protection notes

Only the Guild Master can disband a guild. Only Apprentices, general members and mission specialists can be protected members. When the Guild Master stays away from the game for 15 days or longer, a member who has been given the authority can take over as Guild Master. The Use Guild Skills authority does not cover Command to Gather, Battle Command to Gather, Breath of Life or Blessing of Battle.

Guild Fame drops by a set amount each week by guild size, and it also falls when some content is used. The lowest it can go is -2,000,000. If Fame is below 0, the guild name turns red, and the guild cannot join Conquest War or the War of the Roses. Its members cannot be War Heroes for other guilds.

## Guild War: declaring and rules

Declaring a guild war costs 400,000 Guild Fame. A war lasts 6 hours by default, or 7 days if the other guild accepts. Requirements: at least 10 members online and 7 days since guild creation. A declaration cannot be withdrawn. A war starts about 5 minutes after declaration. You can declare war on up to 6 guilds at once, and not on the same guild again within 48 hours. War can be declared on negative-Fame guilds without spending Fame and without restriction. Guilds below -1,600,000 Fame cannot declare war. If you propose war and the other guild accepts, the war is mutual and neither guild loses Fame. Turn on Reject Guild War Proposals in Settings to automatically reject proposals.

## Guild rankings

Guild Rankings in the ESC menu show each guild's activity stats: Fame, territory occupation, member count and scale. This helps find a guild that suits your playstyle.

## Guild Funds: sources and bonus payouts

Guild Funds start at 80,000 Silver and can be used only for guild operations. No member, including the Guild Master, can withdraw them personally. Sources are Guild Missions, Node/Conquest War wins (a share of territory taxes), and selling Great Ocean sea monster loot at the Guild Shop. The Bonus lets you give 10-40% of Guild Funds to members, across 10 tiers, once every 7 days, with at least 30 normal-rank or higher members. A bonus above 500 million Silver pays a 50% tax on the excess, and above 1 billion Silver a 70% tax on the excess.

## Guild Funds: Guild Tax and Support Funds

The Guild Tax hits guilds whose funds reach 1 billion Silver or more, collected each Sunday at midnight. Guilds under 1 billion Silver pay nothing.

| Guild Funds | Tax |
| --- | --- |
| 1 billion+ | 1% |
| 5 billion+ | 3% |
| 10 billion+ | 5% |
| 20 billion+ | 10% |
| 30 billion+ | 15% |
| 50 billion+ | 20% |

Guild Support Funds can be collected weekly: Small 100,000,000 Silver, Medium 200,000,000, Large 300,000,000, Extra Large 400,000,000. A member with the proper authority must collect them with the collect button while at least 10 members are online.

## Guild Funds: negative funds

If Guild Funds fall below 0 Silver, for example from maintenance fees, members can pay personal Silver to restore them to 0. When the due amount is filled, the guild master or advisor can settle the negative funds to restart guild activity. While funds are negative, guild skills give no buffs and guild missions cannot be started. If funds run low, the guild leader can deposit a small amount, or purchases may be taken from high-ranking members' own silver.

## Guild: Guild House summary

A Guild House holds many members and is won by auction rather than bought with Contribution Points. Auctions run every two weeks, Friday to Saturday. In EU it is Friday 16:00 CET/17:00 CEST to Saturday 16:00 CET/17:00 CEST, and in NA Friday 16:00 ET/13:00 PT to Saturday 16:00 ET/13:00 PT. The winner owns the house for two weeks. Bidding starts at 100,000 Silver, halves after each failed auction, and starts at the previous owner's winning bid if there was one. The maintenance fee is 5% of the winning bid, paid daily at midnight from guild funds. Guild Houses are used for Guild Processing and crafting, and Valencia houses can raise elephants while certain others can build galleys.

## Guild Missions

Guild Missions are cooperative tasks that give Guild Funds, Fame, Points and guild items, plus Silver to members based on individual contribution. Missions depend on guild scale and category: Subjugation, Life, Trading and Boss Subjugation. The mission count recharges by 10 per day (up to 20 depending on Fame), stacking to a maximum of 30. Glorious Guild Missions are added to Subjugation, Life and Trading missions while Guild Fame is at least 800,000 and pay more Silver. Subjugation and Trade/Bartering missions give scroll pieces used to challenge powerful bosses. The mission window shows the mission in progress and who started it, a filter for available missions per channel, and the objective, progress and remaining time.

## Guild Missions: guild juices

After a mission ends, the guild receives juices that members can store in the Guild storage. A character can drink them while the matching skill is below Lv. 29.

| Juice | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| [Guild] Fresh Orange Juice | Greatly increases Health EXP |
| [Guild] Sweet Wild Berry Juice | Greatly increases Breath EXP |
| [Guild] Sour Green Grape Juice | Greatly increases Strength EXP |

## Guild Skills: passive and active buffs

Guild Skills are passives that always apply to all members and active skills used by the Guild Master. They are unlocked or activated with Guild Points. Skills such as Command to Gather, which calls scattered members to one place, matter in Node War, Conquest War and guild war. Popular Guild Buffs last 2 hours on any server and any character, with a 3-hour cooldown. More skills unlock and existing ones improve as Guild Fame rises.

## Guild Skills: Fame-unlocked skills

The passives unlocked by higher Guild Fame are Monster Specialist (Extra AP Against Monsters +8), Light Footsteps (Weight Limit +100 LT), Skilled Hands (Life Skill Mastery +50), Fortune's Guidance (Item Drop Rate +20%), Energetic Hands (Life EXP +50%) and Mercenary's Resolve (Combat EXP +100%). ## Guild Skills: combat and war skills

Combat passives are Furious Roar (accuracy), Battle Roar (AP), Flame of Life (Max HP), Keen Insight (evasion) and Flame of Protection (Damage Reduction). Node War and Conquest skills:
- Battle Command to Gather: teleports responding members to the caster.
- Horrors of Battle: Max HP +2000 for 10 min, nullified on death.
- Battle Reorganization I & II: instantly restores all members' HP to full.
- Healing Touch: recovers 200 HP every 3 sec for 1 min, nullified on death.
- Boost Morale: Movement Speed +20% for 1 min, nullified on death.
- Breath of Life: removes the revival cooldown of dead members, around a Fort or Command Post.
- Blessing of Battle: recovers 500 HP every 3 sec, All Damage Reduction +200, Movement Speed +10%, Super Armor, around a Fort or Command Post.

Blue Battlefield skills: Seaforge Blessing (repairs 15% ship durability, not the Guild Galley), BreezyVoyage (ship speed +20% for 5 min), Seawatch Pulse (shows enemy ships on the World Map for 5 min) and Admiral's Rally (extra damage against ships and forts +5200 for 5 min).

## War Hero: joining rules

The War Hero system lets an Adventurer temporarily join another guild for its guild wars, Node Wars, Conquest Wars or War of the Roses, and get rewards for contributing. To join you need a War Hero Employment Contract from someone with the authority in that guild, and you must be on the same channel as the sender. Guild-exclusive items in your inventory block joining, so sell them at the Guild Shop or put them in Guild Storage. You cannot join while Node War, Conquest War, War of the Roses or similar content is running, or between 00:00 and 01:00 AM when contracts reset. Guilds that already occupy a territory cannot hire War Heroes and their members cannot be War Heroes. Guild Masters cannot be War Heroes. Guilds in an alliance can hire War Heroes. War of the Roses leading guilds or alliances can recruit without limit until it begins. Their War Heroes cannot apply for the Third Legion, and nobody can leave until claiming participation rewards.

## War Hero: hire limits and permissions

A guild can hire War Heroes only up to its maximum member capacity, for example up to 34 in an Extra Large guild with 66 of 100 members, or 20 in a Large guild with 50 of 70. Node War and Conquest War are limited: each participating guild can hire up to 3 in Node War and 5 in Conquest War. A War Hero can fight, view member status, guild skills, alliance and war information, spend allocated guild funds, ride two-seater mounts, use guild summon scrolls, operate guild mounts such as Galleys, use installed annexes, receive guild skill buffs and use the recruiting guild's chat. A War Hero cannot build new annexes, use guild management menus, use Guild Storage, learn Guild Skills, get occupation benefits such as the Item Drop Rate buff, or join the Blue Battlefield.

## War Hero: pay, rewards and contract end

When the contract is made, up to 10,000,000 Silver of the guild's funds is paid at once as contract payment. A victory reward of up to 20,000,000 Silver can also be set, paid if the guild wins that day's Node or Conquest War. Payments cannot exceed available funds. Victory rewards are held out of the guild funds and refunded if the goal is not met, or at midnight (24:00) if the area is liberated or the guild did not take part. Claim rewards under Guild (G), War Hero, Participation Reward, and they go to your inventory. A War Hero cannot cancel or leave midway. All contracts end at midnight (24:00), you reconnect automatically after a loading screen, and the guild's exclusive items in your inventory are removed.

## Guild Manor: setting and using it

A member's manor can be set as the guild's representative manor, letting members teleport to it and use guild buff items. The Guild Master or an Advisor sets it by clicking the no-manor-set text in the Guild Window (G). Then type the owner's Family Name in the Guild Manor Settings window and pick the manor they own. The owner's Family name then shows in the Guild Window. Any member can enter from almost anywhere using the Guild Manor Teleport button, though some content and regions are excluded and teleporting takes time. To leave, use the Guild Manor exit, which returns you to your previous location.

## Guild Manor: guild furniture

Stuffed guild furniture placed in the manor gives guild members temporary buffs. Rules:
- It can only be placed in the guild manor and is stored or retrieved through guild storage.
- A message goes to online members when it is placed.
- It cannot be traded on the Central Market.
- Breath of Resentment is a personal item, so guild storage will not accept it.
- If the manor is removed or changed, placed furniture moves to guild storage.
- Personal furniture with used durability cannot become guild furniture.
- Members cannot use personal furniture in the manor, only [Guild] furniture.

Example recipe for [Guild] Master's Special Stuffed Shadow Lion Head: Master's Special Stuffed Shadow Lion Head (from the Central Market, or by upgrading a Stuffed Shadow Lion Head through Manufacture), [Guild] Essence of Fury (from Guild Quests), Breath of Resentment (Boss Blitz Hwanghae Subjugation weekly rewards) and a [Guild] Furniture Crafting Permit (sold by the Guild NPC). Many [Guild] statues, stuffed heads and ornaments can be crafted, such as [Guild] Kzarka Statue, [Guild] Karanda Statue and [Guild] Blue Whale Wall Decoration.

## Guild Alliance: rules

An alliance is a group of guilds acting under one name. To create one, the Guild Master invites another Guild Master on the same server and sets a ratio between both guilds.
- A guild occupying a Node or Territory cannot join.
- Each guild needs at least 10 members.
- Maximum 10 guilds or 150 members.
- Alliance membership applies only during Node or Conquest Wars, when the alliance name shows instead of the guild name.
- Node and Territory taxes are split by the set ratio when one is occupied.
- Elephants and galleys of guilds other than the leading guild stay at the wharf and cannot be used until the war ends.
- The alliance disbands if a guild leaves, but cannot be disbanded while a Node or Territory is occupied.
- Alliances cannot form while a guild is signed up for a Node War.

## Guild: replacing the Guild Master and disbanding

After 15 or more days of the Guild Master being offline, an Advisor or Guild Officer may step in as the new master. A Guild Master may also give up the post willingly, but several conditions apply, such as holding no territory or node, and 50% of Guild Funds is taken. Disbanding is a last resort. It requires that the Guild Master be the only member left, that Guild Storage be empty and that no forts be built.
