# Chat Group

The Chat Group is a social feature for talking with other Adventurers about specific topics. This page covers how to open it, the topics on offer, the joining rules and the quarterly titles.

## Chat Group: how to open it

The Chat Group window is opened from the main menu (hotkey: Esc). In the new menu, go to the Community tab. In the old menu, it is on the bottom line of the menu.

## Chat Group: discussion topics

Chat Groups are organized by topic, and the list changes over time as the game evolves. Topics named on the wiki:
- Lunar Halo Inn (New Adventurers): a place for new and returning Adventurers to ask experienced players any game question.
- Battle Arena (PvP): PvP questions, finding opponents and PvP equipment advice.
- Hystria Ruins (PvE): where to level up with your gear, which monster zones suit your level, and where items drop.
- Florin Neighborhood Meeting (Life Skills): life skill questions, such as crafting furniture or finding where to catch yvrugs.
- Dancing Marlin Tavern (Sea Content): finding crew for sea monster hunts, help with building ships, and veteran captain stories.
- Heidel's Street (General): any general topic that fits no other group.

The title table also lists Garnier Troupe and Hall of Adventurers as chat groups.

## Chat Group: joining and leaving rules

When you pick a Chat Group, you get a system message saying you are joining it. A new account is automatically placed in the Lunar Halo Inn chat group. Each family can be in only one Chat Group at a time. Choosing another topic automatically removes you from the previous group and puts you in the new one. If a group is at full capacity, you cannot join until space opens up, so wait and try again later. To show or hide Chat Group text, use the Chat Group option in the Chat Window Settings window, opened with the cog icon above your chat windows.

## Chat Group: likes and 90-day titles

Every three months, the most active Adventurers in each Chat Group receive a special title that lasts 90 days. Players holding the gold, silver or bronze Black Spirit icons get these titles. The badges are based on likes from other Adventurers in the Chat Group. Each Adventurer has 3 likes to give per day. To give one, click the Adventurer's name and choose Likes (3/3). The wiki notes that the in-game guide can differ from the live game after updates.
