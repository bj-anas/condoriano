# Guild House

Guild Houses are large residences a guild gets through an auction. This page covers the bidding rules, maintenance fees, storage, guild crafting, elephants, galleys and what happens when a house is lost.

## Guild House: what it is and how to get one

A Guild House is a large residence that holds many people, found only in certain large towns and cities. Unlike regular houses bought with contribution points, a Guild House is won in an auction paid with guild funds. You bid through the Guild Manager NPC in a town that has Guild Houses.

## Guild House auction: who can bid and when

Only the Guild Master, or a Guild Member with Guild House Auction authority, can bid. Regular auctions run every 2 weeks from Friday to Saturday:

| Region | Regular auction | Extended auction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU | Friday 16:00 CET / 17:00 CEST to Saturday 16:00 CET / 17:00 CEST | If no winning bid, extended 24 hours from 16:00 CET / 17:00 CEST |
| NA | Friday 16:00 ET / 13:00 PT to Saturday 16:00 ET / 13:00 PT | If no winning bid, extended 24 hours from 16:00 ET / 13:00 PT |

## Guild House auction: minimum bid and usage period

The minimum bid in a regular auction is 100,000 Silver. In an extended auction it is half of the previous minimum, for example 50,000 Silver after the 1st failed auction and 25,000 Silver after the 2nd. If another guild owned the house before, its winning bid becomes the minimum, for example 50,000,000 Silver if Guild A won for that amount. Guild funds spent on a winning bid are consumed. A regular-auction win lasts 2 weeks until the next regular auction. An extended-auction win lasts from the win date until the next regular auction, which is under 2 weeks.

## Guild House auction: rules

- Auctions happen once every two weeks, and a server-wide notice announces the start. If nobody bids, the auction may fail and be extended.
- Bid through the designated NPC in the house's own city. You cannot bid on a Velia Guild House from Mediah.
- You may bid on one Guild House per auction period, with up to three bidding attempts.
- Auctions are blind: you cannot see other guilds' bids.
- If you hold the highest bid, a notice tells you so.
- If you lose, collect your bid from the Guild House Auction NPC in the city where you bid. A bid on an Olvia house can only be withdrawn in Olvia.
- To keep using the house after the period ends, win the next auction.

## Guild House management: maintenance fee and upgrades

A guild that wins a Guild House pays a daily maintenance fee at midnight. It is 5% of the winning bid, taken automatically from guild funds. For example, a 100,000,000 Silver winning bid means a 5,000,000 Silver fee. If funds run short, the house goes into unpaid status, which may restrict its use. Guild funds come from activities such as Node Wars and selling loot from ocean monsters. Guild Points can be used to upgrade the Guild House, which unlocks more craftable items and larger Guild Storage.

## Guild House storage

Every guild member can open the Guild Storage by using the house's doors or windows, and in town it can also be opened any time from the Guild UI. Storage accepts only guild-exclusive items whose names include "[Guild]".

## Guild House: Guild Processing and Guild Crafting

Materials for Guild Crafting can only be processed in a Guild House. Enter the house, interact with the door to open the Guild Storage and place materials in it, press the L key, and select Guild Processing. This produces permits and Guild Crafting materials, and a successful process gives Guild Activity. Guild Crafting uses those materials. Only the Guild Master has crafting authority, managed with Manage Production in the Guild UI, and members can send workers to help. Finished items, such as siege weapons, go to the Guild Storage. Without Guild House permissions, you can still collect finished items but cannot add materials to ongoing projects.

## Guild House: elephants and Elephant Nursery

Captured Baby Elephants can be raised in a Guild House, but only in Valencia ones (the page also mentions Sand Grain Bazaar and Valencia City). Your guild must learn the Guild Skill Monstrous Force first. Raising materials come from the Guild Shop or Guild Processing. With Monstrous Force Lv. 2 and an Elephant Nursery upgraded to Lv. 2, you can craft elephant equipment.

## Guild House: galley and Shipyard

To craft a Galley, your guild must have the Guild Skill Building Boat and win certain Guild Houses with a shipyard function, listed on the page as Balenos, Mediah or Olvia. An older section of the page names Balenos, Calpheon and Mediah instead. Another way to get a guild galley is the once-only quest [Guild] I Have a Dream, which asks for Old Moon Combat Seals and Old Moon Life Seals. Learning Ruler of the Sea to Lv. 2 unlocks the Shipyard to Lv. 2, allowing galley equipment crafting. With the Guild Skill Fully Prepared, you can make items that restore your elephant's endurance and your guild galley's power.

## Guild House: losing ownership

If another guild wins your Guild House, its upgrades, Guild Storage items and ongoing crafting information are all kept. Until your guild gets the house back through a later auction, you cannot use it and cannot do Guild Crafting.
