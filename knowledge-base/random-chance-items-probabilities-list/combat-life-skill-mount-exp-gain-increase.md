# Combat/Life/Skill/Mount EXP Gain Increase

How Extra EXP Scrolls increase Combat, Life, Skill and Mount EXP gain, with the formula and an example.

## Combat/Life/Skill/Mount EXP Gain Increase: how the effect works

EXP gain for Combat, Life, Skill and Mount can be raised with the Extra EXP Scroll for each field. An increase of +n% adds an amount proportional to your default EXP gain to the total. It is not a flat addition.

## Combat/Life/Skill/Mount EXP Gain Increase: example

Take a character whose Life EXP gain is at the default 100% and who applies an Extra Life EXP Scroll worth +50%. The scroll adds 50% of the default gain (50%), so Life EXP gain rises from 100% to 150%.
