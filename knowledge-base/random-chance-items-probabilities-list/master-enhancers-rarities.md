# Master Enhancer's Rarities

Rewards and chances for Master Enhancer's Rarities, with quantities.

## Master Enhancer's Rarities: what it is and how to get it

Master Enhancer's Rarities is a Pearl Shop item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it gives one reward from the table, with the quantity shown.

| Black Gem | 5 | 20% |

| Hard Black Crystal Shard | 5 | 17% |
| Valks' Cry | 3 | 14% |

| Cron Stone | 25 | 8% |

## Master Enhancer's Rarities: notes

The most likely reward is Black Gem x5 at 20%. The rarest is Advice of Valks (+45) at 3%, and the other Advice of Valks reward, (+25), is 8%. Cron Stone comes as x25 at 8%.
