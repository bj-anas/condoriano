## Wondrous Adventure Box: notes

The Bloody Outfit Box from the Wondrous Adventure Box cannot be opened on Archer, Shai, Guardian, Hashashin, Nova, Sage and Corsair characters. The most likely rewards are Marmot x1 at 11.00% and Cron Stone x130 at 11.00%, then Young Griffon x1 at 10.00%. The rarest is Cron Stone x2500 at 0.10%. The box can also contain more Wondrous Adventure Boxes (x10 at 0.50%, x2 at 4.00%).
