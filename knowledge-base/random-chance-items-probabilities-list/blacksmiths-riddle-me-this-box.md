# Blacksmith's Riddle-Me-This Box

Everything the Blacksmith's Riddle-Me-This Box can give, with the chance of each item and, for the Emberseal, the chance of each quantity.

## Blacksmith's Riddle-Me-This Box: what it is and how to get it

The Blacksmith's Riddle-Me-This Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it gives one item according to a set probability. The rewards are the enhancement boxes and blacksmith's boxes in the table, plus [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseal in a quantity from 2 to 5.

## Blacksmith's Riddle-Me-This Box: possible rewards and probabilities

| Item | Probability | Min | Max | Probability per quantity (within Min - Max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transcended Premium Enhancement Box | 0.10% | 1 | 1 | 100% |
| Ultimate Premium Enhancement Box | 0.30% | 1 | 1 | 100% |
| Radiant Premium Enhancement Box | 10.00% | 1 | 1 | 100% |
| Artisan Blacksmith's Box | 18.00% | 1 | 1 | 100% |
| Skilled Blacksmith's Box | 18.00% | 1 | 1 | 100% |
| Beginner Blacksmith's Box | 18.00% | 1 | 1 | 100% |
| [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseal x2-5 | 35.60% | 2 | 5 | 25.00% |

## Blacksmith's Riddle-Me-This Box: notes

Each quantity of [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseal has the same chance. Within the 35.60% Emberseal result, getting x2, x3, x4 or x5 is 25% each. The rarest item in the Blacksmith's Riddle-Me-This Box is Transcended Premium Enhancement Box at 0.10%. The Emberseal result is the most likely at 35.60%.
