# Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle

Everything the Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle can give, with Cron Stone amounts and chances.

## Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances add up to 100%, so each bundle opened gives one result. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle gives one of these results.

| Item Name | Rate(%) |
|---|---|
| Bundle of 5,000 Cron Stones | 0.05% |
| Bundle of 2,000 Cron Stones | 0.25% |
| Bundle of 1,000 Cron Stones | 0.50% |
| Cron Stone x500 | 3.00% |
| Cron Stone x400 | 10.00% |
| Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle x2 | 20.00% |
| Cron Stone x200 | 17.00% |
| Cron Stone x250 | 18.00% |
| Cron Stone x300 | 31.20% |

## Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle: notes

The most likely result from the Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle is Cron Stone x300 at 31.20%, then Resplendent Cron Stone Bundle x2 at 20.00%. The rarest is the Bundle of 5,000 Cron Stones at 0.05%.
