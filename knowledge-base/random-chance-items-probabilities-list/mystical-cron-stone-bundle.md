# Mystical Cron Stone Bundle

Everything the Mystical Cron Stone Bundle can give, with Cron Stone amounts and chances.

## Mystical Cron Stone Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Mystical Cron Stone Bundle is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances add up to 100%, so each bundle opened gives one result: either a Cron Stone Bundle of 2000 or 1000, or a plain amount of Cron Stone.

## Mystical Cron Stone Bundle: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Mystical Cron Stone Bundle gives one of these results.

| Item Name | Quantity | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Cron Stone Bundle | 2000 | 0.25% |
| Cron Stone Bundle | 1000 | 0.50% |
| Cron Stone | 500 | 1.00% |
| Cron Stone | 300 | 1.25% |
| Cron Stone | 200 | 5.00% |
| Cron Stone | 150 | 15.00% |
| Cron Stone | 100 | 25.00% |
| Cron Stone | 75 | 28.00% |
| Cron Stone | 50 | 24.00% |

## Mystical Cron Stone Bundle: notes

The most likely result from the Mystical Cron Stone Bundle is Cron Stone x75 at 28.00%, then x100 at 25.00% and x50 at 24.00%. The rarest result is the Cron Stone Bundle of 2000 at 0.25%.
