# Wonderland Bundle

How the Wonderland Bundle gives Cron Stone and bonus items, with the chance of each item.

## Wonderland Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Pearl Shop sells the Wonderland Bundle for a limited period. Opening it gives one or more items according to set probabilities. Every bundle opened gives Cron Stone x300 at 100%.

## Wonderland Bundle: how the chances work

The chance of each item in the Wonderland Bundle is applied separately, so a single bundle can give many items. For example, the first bundle might give only Cron Stone x300, while the second might give Cron Stone x300, Wizard Gosphy and Wonderland Outfit Box x1.

## Wonderland Bundle: notes

Besides the guaranteed Cron Stone x300, the rarest item in the Wonderland Bundle is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x10 at 0.1%. The most likely extra item is Advice of Valks (+40) x1 at 13.0%. The bundle can also give more bundles: Wonderland Bundle x5 at 1.5% and x1 at 5.5%.
