# Dream Horse Awakening

How a Tier 8 Courser becomes a Dream Horse through Courser Training, and how the training stats decide which Dream Horse you can get.

## Dream Horse Awakening: how it works

A Tier 8 horse can become a Dream Horse by going through Courser Training. At the Stable, choose Courser Training with a Tier 8 Courser. Special ingredients raise the Courser's Skill, Elegance and Strength stats. Once the training stats add up to a total of 200%, the Courser is able to attempt Dream Horse Awakening. Each stat can be trained up to 180%. When a stat is already above 100%, each training item raises it by only 0.5.

## Dream Horse Awakening: which Dream Horse you get (Arduanatt, Diné, Doom)

The chance of each Dream Horse type (Arduanatt, Diné or Doom) is shown in the Probability Information window of the Courser Training menu. A high Skill training level raises the chance of Arduanatt, a high Elegance level raises the chance of Diné, and a high Strength level raises the chance of Doom. If the training total is 200%, you cannot get the Dream Horse that matches a stat left at 0. For example, a Courser trained to 200% total with 100% Skill and 100% Strength has a 50% chance of Arduanatt or Doom, and 0% for Diné.
