# Shining Pouch of Enhancement

How the Shining Pouch of Enhancement chain works, with the chance at each pouch, the contents of each box and the exchange for Tranan Underfoe's Seals.

## Shining Pouch of Enhancement: what it is and how to get it

The Shining Pouch of Enhancement is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. It works in stages: Shining Pouch of Enhancement, then the Good, Great, Marvelous and Astonishing pouches. At each of the first four pouches there is a 50% chance of moving to the next pouch and a 50% chance of a reward. At the Marvelous pouch, the alternative to the Astonishing pouch is two Shining Pouch of Enhancement.

## Shining Pouch of Enhancement: possible rewards and probabilities

The "% per pouch" column is the chance at that pouch. The "Overall chance" column is the chance of ending with that result when opening one Shining Pouch of Enhancement.

|---|---|---|---|
| Shining Pouch of Enhancement | Shining Pouch of Good Enhancement | 50% | - |
| Shining Pouch of Enhancement | Artisan's Enhancement Box and [Event] Tranan Underfoe's Seal x1 | 50% | 50% |
| Shining Pouch of Good Enhancement | Shining Pouch of Great Enhancement | 50% | - |
| Shining Pouch of Good Enhancement | Master's Enhancement Box and [Event] Tranan Underfoe's Seal x2 | 50% | 25% |
| Shining Pouch of Great Enhancement | Shining Pouch of Marvelous Enhancement | 50% | - |
| Shining Pouch of Great Enhancement | Guru's Enhancement Box and [Event] Tranan Underfoe's Seal x3 | 50% | 12.5% |
| Shining Pouch of Marvelous Enhancement | Shining Pouch of Astonishing Enhancement | 50% | 6.25% |
| Shining Pouch of Marvelous Enhancement | Shining Pouch of Enhancement x2 | 50% | 6.25% |

## Shining Pouch of Enhancement: contents of the boxes and the Astonishing pouch

Opening each Enhancement Box from the Shining Pouch of Enhancement chain gives all of the items listed for it.

| Box | Items obtained |
|---|---|
| Artisan's Enhancement Box | Cron Stone x100, Advice of Valks (+50) x1, Memory Fragment x20 |
| Master's Enhancement Box | Cron Stone x200, Advice of Valks (+60) x1, Memory Fragment x30 |
| Guru's Enhancement Box | Cron Stone x300, Advice of Valks (+70) x1, Memory Fragment x50 |

Opening the Shining Pouch of Astonishing Enhancement gives all of these: Radiant Premium Enhancement Box x1 and Advice of Valks (+80) x1.

## Shining Pouch of Enhancement: exchanging [Event] Tranan Underfoe's Seal

The [Event] Tranan Underfoe's Seal, given by the Shining Pouch of Enhancement chain, can be exchanged at any time through the Blacksmith Tranan Underfoe in Velia. The page lists two exchanges: 10 seals for Advice of Valks (+100) x1, and 20 seals for Radiant Premium Enhancement Box x1.

## Shining Pouch of Enhancement: notes

Overall odds from the first Shining Pouch of Enhancement: Artisan's Enhancement Box at 50%, Master's Enhancement Box at 25%, Guru's Enhancement Box at 12.5%, and each of Shining Pouch of Astonishing Enhancement and Shining Pouch of Enhancement x2 at 6.25%. The Shining Pouch of Enhancement x2 result restarts the chain.
