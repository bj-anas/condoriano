# Reset Mount Growth

What the Reset Mount Growth item does at a stable, and which skill a horse learns at Lv. 1 afterwards, by tier and breed.

## Reset Mount Growth: what it does and how to use it

Reset Mount Growth is an item you use at a stable to reset a mount's level, ability and every skill it has learned. If you have the item, open the Reset Mount menu to use it. The mount goes back to Lv. 1, all its stats (Speed, Accel., Turn and Brake) are reset, and all learned skills are removed. It works on Horses. It cannot be used on Guild Mounts or on mounts used through NPCs.

## Reset Mount Growth: the skill learned at Lv. 1 after a reset

After a reset to Lv. 1, the mount learns one random skill, with the chance set by its breed and tier. Some mounts have a designated skill at Lv. 1, and for those the skill stays the same after a reset. The chance is not affected by the character's Training Mastery. Only the skills shown in the table can be learned this way, and mounts with a predetermined first skill are not in the table. You can see a mount's tier and breed in the Mount Information window. The breed does not change even if a Premium Horse Appearance Change Coupon is used. The wiki says the displayed probabilities are rounded up to the second decimal place.

## Reset Mount Growth: Lv. 1 skill probabilities by tier and breed

Chance of learning each skill at Lv. 1 when Reset Mount Growth is used on a mount. For Tier 8 breeds the wiki lists Sprint 100% twice, in two columns.

| Mount tier | Breeds | Lv. 1 skill and probability |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 8 | Breed 1 to Breed 5 | Sprint 100% / Sprint 100% |
| Tier 7 | Breed 1 to Breed 9 | Sprint 100% |
| Tier 6 | Breed 1 to Breed 20 | Sprint 100% |

Every listed breed of Tier 8 (5 breeds), Tier 7 (9 breeds) and Tier 6 (20 breeds) learns Sprint with a 100% chance.
