# Spooky Scary Halloween Box

Everything the Spooky Scary Halloween Box can give, with chances.

## Spooky Scary Halloween Box: what it is and how to get it

The Spooky Scary Halloween Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances in the table add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Spooky Scary Halloween Box: notes

The most likely reward of the Spooky Scary Halloween Box is Cron Stone x200 at 35.10%, then Mystical Artisan's Memory Bundle x1 at 26.50%. The rarest is Cron Stone x5000 at 0.15%, followed by Mystical Cron Stone Bundle x20 at 0.25%.
