# Winter Adventure Box

Everything the Winter Adventure Box can give, with quantities and chances.

## Winter Adventure Box: what it is and how to get it

The Winter Adventure Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances in the table add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Winter Adventure Box: notes

The most likely rewards of the Winter Adventure Box are Artisan's Memory x30 and Cron Stone x120, each at 13.00%. The rarest is Cron Stone x5000 at 0.01%. The box can also return more of itself: Winter Adventure Box x10 at 0.50% and x2 at 4.00%.
