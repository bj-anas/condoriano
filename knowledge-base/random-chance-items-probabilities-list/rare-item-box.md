# Rare Item Box

Everything the Rare Item Box can give, with chances.

## Rare Item Box: what it is and how to get it

The Rare Item Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The four chances add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward: a Hedgehog of a certain tier, or a Snowflake Reindeer Box.

## Rare Item Box: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Rare Item Box gives one of these rewards. The Snowflake Reindeer Box gives Snowflake Reindeer x2.

| Item Name | Rate(%) |
|---|---|
| Hedgehog (Tier 4) | 3.00% |
| Hedgehog (Tier 3) | 16.50% |
| Hedgehog (Tier 2) | 40.50% |
| Snowflake Reindeer Box (Obtain Snowflake Reindeer x2) | 40.00% |

## Rare Item Box: notes

The most likely reward of the Rare Item Box is Hedgehog (Tier 2) at 40.50%, just ahead of the Snowflake Reindeer Box at 40.00%. Hedgehog (Tier 3) is 16.50%. The rarest reward is Hedgehog (Tier 4) at 3.00%.
