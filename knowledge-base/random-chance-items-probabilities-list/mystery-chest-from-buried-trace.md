# Mystery Chest from Buried Trace

How to find and open a Mystery Chest from a Buried Trace, and what it can contain.

## Mystery Chest from Buried Trace: how to get it

A Buried Trace can turn up while Lumbering, Tanning, Collecting Fluid, Hoeing, Mining or Butchering. Digging into the buried trace reveals a Mystery Chest. The chest opens only with a Gold Key.

## Mystery Chest from Buried Trace: possible rewards and probabilities

The "Probability" column is the chance that the item drops at all. The last column is the chance of each exact quantity between the minimum and maximum.

| Item | Probability | Min Qty | Max Qty | Probability per item (between min/max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fruit of Nature | 100.00% | 22 | 26 | 20% |
| Ancient Spirit Dust | 50.00% | 5 | 20 | 3.125% |
| Sharp Black Crystal Shard | 100.00% | 15 | 22 | 12.5% |

## Mystery Chest from Buried Trace: notes

Sharp Black Crystal Shard is guaranteed (100%), and the wiki text also names Hard Black Crystal Shard as guaranteed, although the table does not list it. The other items are added at their set probabilities. For instance, for Fruit of Nature, exactly 22 pieces has a 20% chance, 23 pieces 20%, and 26 pieces 20%, so each quantity in the range has its own 20% chance.
