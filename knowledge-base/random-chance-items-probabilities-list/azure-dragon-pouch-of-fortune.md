# Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune

How the Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune chain works, with the chance at each stage, the overall chance of each reward, and what the last pouch and the Choose Your Draconic Pet Box contain.

## Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune: what it is and how to get it

The Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. It works in stages. Each of the first four pouches (Fortune, Good Fortune, Great Fortune, Marvelous Fortune) gives either the next pouch in the chain or a reward, at 50.00% each. The final pouch is the Azure Dragon Pouch of Astonishing Fortune.

## Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune: possible rewards and probabilities

The "% per stage" column is the chance at that pouch. The "% per item" column is the overall chance of ending with that reward, as listed on the page.

| Pouch | Content | % per stage | % per item |
|---|---|---|---|
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune | Azure Dragon Pouch of Good Fortune | 50.00% | - |
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune | Rare Cron Stone Bundle x2 | 50.00% | 50.00% |
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Good Fortune | Azure Dragon Pouch of Great Fortune | 50.00% | - |
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Good Fortune | Choose Your Draconic Pet Box | 50.00% | 25.00% |
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Great Fortune | Azure Dragon Pouch of Marvelous Fortune | 50.00% | - |
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Great Fortune | Enhancement Help Bundle | 50.00% | 12.50% |
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Marvelous Fortune | Azure Dragon Pouch of Astonishing Fortune | 50.00% | 6.25% |
| Azure Dragon Pouch of Marvelous Fortune | Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune x2 | 50.00% | 6.25% |

## Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune: Astonishing Fortune pouch contents

The Azure Dragon Pouch of Astonishing Fortune contains one of these three items: [Event] Premium Outfit Box, [Event] Classic Outfit Box, or Advice of Valks (+80). The page does not give a separate chance for each of the three. The Marvelous Fortune pouch can also give two Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune (6.25% overall), which starts the chain again.

## Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune: Choose Your Draconic Pet Box contents

The Choose Your Draconic Pet Box, a reward of the Azure Dragon Pouch of Good Fortune, has two bundles of selectable items. The first bundle offers Young Golden Dragon or Young Baphomet. The second bundle offers Cron Stone x200, Inventory +8 Expansion Coupon, or Artisan's Memory x30.

## Azure Dragon Pouch of Fortune: notes

Overall odds from the first pouch: Rare Cron Stone Bundle x2 is 50.00%, Choose Your Draconic Pet Box is 25.00%, and Enhancement Help Bundle is 12.50%. The page notes that Shai cannot open the [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box.
