# Purified Lightstones

Which lightstones each Purified Lightstone (Fire, Earth, Wind, Flora and the generic one) can give, with chances.

## Purified Lightstones: how they work

Using a Purified Lightstone gives one of the items listed for its type, at the set probabilities. The types are Purified Lightstone of Fire, Purified Lightstone of Earth, Purified Lightstone of Wind, Purified Lightstone of Flora, and the plain Purified Lightstone. All five can give an Iridescent Lightstone: 0.0030% for Fire, Earth, Wind and Flora, and 0.0070% for the plain Purified Lightstone.
