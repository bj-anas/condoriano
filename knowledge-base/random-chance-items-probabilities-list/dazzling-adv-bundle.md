# Dazzling ADV Bundle

The items the Dazzling ADV Bundle can give, with the rate of each.

## Dazzling ADV Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Dazzling ADV Bundle is a Pearl Shop item that is on sale for a limited period. Opening it can give 1 or more items. The chance of each item is applied separately, so one bundle can give many items. Every bundle you open gives Cron Stone x300 at 100%. For example, a first bundle might give only Cron Stone x300, while a second might give Cron Stone x300, Value Pack (7 Days), and [Event] Classic Outfit Box x1.

## Dazzling ADV Bundle: notes

The only guaranteed item in the Dazzling ADV Bundle is Cron Stone x300 at 100%. The rarest bonus item is [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x10 at 0.2%. The most likely bonus item is Advice of Valks (+40) at 15.0%. The bundle can also contain more bundles: Dazzling ADV Bundle x5 at 1.5% and Dazzling ADV Bundle at 5.5%.
