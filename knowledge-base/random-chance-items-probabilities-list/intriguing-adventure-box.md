# Intriguing Adventure Box

Everything the Intriguing Adventure Box can give, with the rate of each item.

## Intriguing Adventure Box: what it is and how to get it

The Intriguing Adventure Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The page gives each possible reward with its rate. The list includes outfit boxes, Cron Stone bundles, Value Packs, Advice of Valks and short-duration Blessing of Kamasylve and Secret Book of Old Moon items.

## Intriguing Adventure Box: notes

The most likely item from the Intriguing Adventure Box is Cron Stone x50 at 27.38%, then [Event] Blessing of Kamasylve (1 Day) x2 and [Event] Secret Book of Old Moon (1 Day) x2 at 17.00% each. The rarest is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x10 at 0.02%.
