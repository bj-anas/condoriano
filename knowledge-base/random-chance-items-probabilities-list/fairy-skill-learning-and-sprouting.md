# Fairy Skill Learning and Sprouting

How to see fairy skill and sprouting chances in game, and the chance of each first skill when a fairy reaches level 10.

## Fairy Skill Learning and Sprouting: where to see the probabilities

Probability information for the skills a fairy can learn when its skills are changed with items such as Theiah's Orb can be checked in the in-game tooltip. After growing a fairy with items such as Sweet Honey Wine and Ornette's Dark Honey Wine, the success rate of growing it to the next level through sprouting can be checked too. Both are found under Fairy, then Fairy Information. Click the Sprouting tab for the next-level success rate, and the Skill Change tab (shown as Change Skill) for the skills that can be learned.

## Fairy Skill Learning and Sprouting: first skill at level 10

For each first skill a fairy can learn when it has grown to level 10, the wiki gives a table of probabilities by fairy grade (Faint, Glimmering, Brilliant, Radiant) and skill level. The skills are Tingling Breath, Feathery Steps, Fairy's Tear, Inexhaustible Well, Miraculous Cheer, Continuous Care and Morning Star. The wiki labels the skill levels I, II, III, VI and V in that order, and the notes keep those labels. A dash means the grade cannot learn that skill level.
