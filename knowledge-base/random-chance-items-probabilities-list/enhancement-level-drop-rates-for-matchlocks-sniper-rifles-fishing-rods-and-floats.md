# Enhancement Level Drop Rates for Matchlocks, Sniper Rifles, Fishing Rods & Floats

The chances that a failed enhancement drops the level or holds it for matchlocks, sniper rifles, fishing rods and floats.

## Enhancement level drops for matchlocks, sniper rifles, fishing rods and floats: how it works

Matchlocks, sniper rifles, fishing rods and floats do not lose enhancement levels when enhancement fails at +1 through +6. At +7 or higher, a failed enhancement can either drop the level or hold the current level, depending on the chances for that step. The chances are the same for all four equipment types: a failure at 7 to 8 drops the level 10% of the time and holds it 90%, a failure at 8 to 9 drops 50% and holds 50%, and a failure at 9 to 10 always drops (100%) with no hold chance.

## Matchlock enhancement level drop and hold chances

The matchlocks covered are [Hunting] Beginner Matchlock, [Hunting] Apprentice Matchlock, [Hunting] Skilled Matchlock, [Hunting] Professional Matchlock, [Hunting] Artisan Matchlock, [Hunting] Master Matchlock and [Hunting] Gauche Master Matchlock. Chances on a failed enhancement, from +7:

| Enhancement Level | Drop Chance | Hold Chance |
|---|---|---|
| 7→8 | 10% | 90% |
| 8→9 | 50% | 50% |
| 9→10 | 100% | - |
