# Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp

Chances of getting the crafting materials for Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp, Faelight Fragment and Lumentrace, from Imperial Horse Delivery.

## Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp: how to get the crafting materials

Horses of Lv. 15 or above can be sent on an Imperial Horse Delivery at the stable. A delivery can give the crafting materials for Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp, Faelight Fragment and Lumentrace, at the probabilities below.

## Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp: Faelight Fragment and Lumentrace probabilities

| Faelight Fragment: Imperial Horse Tier | Probability |
|---|---|
| Tier 6 | 0.1% |
| Tier 7 | 0.25% |
| Tier 8 | 0.5% |
| Dream and Mythical Horse | 20% |

| Lumentrace: Imperial Horse Type | Probability |
|---|---|
| Imperial Steed | 5% |

## Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp: notes on Lumentrace

Lumentrace can only be obtained with an Imperial Steed acquired after the Nov 14 (Thu) maintenance. An Imperial Steed that was bred but not collected before that maintenance also does not give Lumentrace.
