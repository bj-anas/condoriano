# Sealed Fairy Wings

Chances of getting each tier and Laila type of fairy wings from Sealed Fairy Wings, as listed on the official wiki.

## Sealed Fairy Wings: what it is

Sealed Fairy Wings is an in-game system item in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki gives the chance of obtaining each fairy wings result by tier (Radiant, Brilliant, Glimmering, Faint) and by Laila personality type (Joyful, Meticulous, Prim, Aloof, Serene).

## Sealed Fairy Wings: possible results and probabilities

Chance in percent of each Fairy Wings result from Sealed Fairy Wings. The four non-Serene types share the same rate in each tier.

| Tier | Joyful Laila | Meticulous Laila | Prim Laila | Aloof Laila | Serene Laila |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Radiant Fairy Wings | 0.075% | 0.075% | 0.075% | 0.075% | 0.2% |
| Brilliant Fairy Wings | 0.75% | 0.75% | 0.75% | 0.75% | 2% |
| Glimmering Fairy Wings | 3.75% | 3.75% | 3.75% | 3.75% | 10% |
| Faint Fairy Wings | 12.5% | 12.5% | 12.5% | 12.5% | 19.5% |

## Sealed Fairy Wings: notes

Adding up the listed chances gives these totals per tier: Radiant Fairy Wings 0.5%, Brilliant Fairy Wings 5%, Glimmering Fairy Wings 25%, Faint Fairy Wings 69.5%. Serene Laila wings have a higher chance than the other four types in every tier. For example, Radiant Fairy Wings (Serene Laila) are 0.2%, while Radiant Fairy Wings of the other four types are 0.075% each.
