# Black Mystery Bundle

The guaranteed items and the bonus items of the Black Mystery Bundle, with quantities and chances.

## Black Mystery Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Black Mystery Bundle is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Every time you open one, you are guaranteed Memory Fragment x10 and Cron Stone x300. You can also receive several bonus items, each according to its own set probability.

## Black Mystery Bundle: guaranteed items

| Item | Quantity | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| Cron Stone | 300 | 100% |
| Memory Fragment | 10 | 100% |

## Black Mystery Bundle: notes

The Choose Your Krogdalo's Feather Box, a 2.00% bonus item of the Black Mystery Bundle, lets you choose 1 of these: Krogdalo's Feather: Speed x1, Krogdalo's Feather: Acceleration x1, Krogdalo's Feather: Turn x1, or Krogdalo's Feather: Brake x1. The rarest bonus item is Bundle of 50,000 Cron Stones at 0.01%. The most common bonus item is [Event] Courser Training Box at 40.00%. The bundle can also contain another Black Mystery Bundle (5.00%).
