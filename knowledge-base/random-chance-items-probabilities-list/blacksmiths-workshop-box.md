# Blacksmith's Workshop Box

How the Blacksmith's Workshop Box chain works, what the Material Boxes, Blacksmith's Advice and Masterpiece Box give, how Blacksmith's Soulforged Seals are exchanged, and the contents of the Premium Enhancement Boxes.

## Blacksmith's Workshop Box: what it is and how to get it

The Blacksmith's Workshop Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. It works in stages through the Blacksmith's Workshop Box, Solid Box, Sturdy Box, Exquisite Box and Masterpiece Box. Each of the first four stages gives either the next box or a Blacksmith's Material Box, at 50% each. A Blacksmith's Workshop Box can also be obtained for 10 Blacksmith's Soulforged Seals.

## Blacksmith's Workshop Box: possible rewards and probabilities

The "per stage" column is the chance at that stage. The "final" column is the overall chance of ending with that reward.

## Blacksmith's Workshop Box: contents of the Material Boxes and Masterpiece Box

Opening any of these gives all of the items listed.

- Blacksmith's Material Box I: Cron Stone x100, Blacksmith's Advice x2, Blacksmith's Soulforged Seal x1.
- Blacksmith's Material Box II: Cron Stone x300, Blacksmith's Advice x3, Blacksmith's Soulforged Seal x3.
- Blacksmith's Material Box III: Cron Stone x500, Blacksmith's Advice x5, Blacksmith's Soulforged Seal x4.
- Blacksmith's Masterpiece Box: Artisan Premium Enhancement Box x1 and Blacksmith's Soulforged Seal x5.

## Blacksmith's Workshop Box: Guru Blacksmith's Advice contents

The Guru Blacksmith's Advice is the last tier of Blacksmith's Advice from the Blacksmith's Workshop Box. Opening it gives all of these items: Memory Fragment x50, Cron Stone x50, Sharp Black Crystal Shard x10, and [Event] Dim Origin of Dark Hunger x3.
