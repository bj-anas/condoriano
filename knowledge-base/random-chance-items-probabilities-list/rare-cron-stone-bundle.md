# Rare Cron Stone Bundle

Everything the Rare Cron Stone Bundle can give, with Cron Stone amounts and chances.

## Rare Cron Stone Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Rare Cron Stone Bundle is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances add up to 100%, so each bundle opened gives one result. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Rare Cron Stone Bundle: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Rare Cron Stone Bundle gives one of these results.

| Item Name | Rate(%) |
|---|---|
| Bundle of 2000 Cron Stones | 0.25% |
| Bundle of 1000 Cron Stones | 0.50% |
| Cron Stone x500 | 1.00% |
| Cron Stone x400 | 2.00% |
| Cron Stone x300 | 4.00% |
| Rare Cron Stone Bundle x2 | 8.00% |
| Cron Stone x250 | 12.00% |
| Cron Stone x200 | 25.00% |
| Cron Stone x150 | 20.25% |
| Cron Stone x100 | 15.00% |
| Rare Cron Stone Bundle x1 | 12.00% |

## Rare Cron Stone Bundle: notes

The most likely result from the Rare Cron Stone Bundle is Cron Stone x200 at 25.00%, then Cron Stone x150 at 20.25%. The rarest is the Bundle of 2000 Cron Stones at 0.25%. The bundle can also give more of itself: Rare Cron Stone Bundle x2 at 8.00% and x1 at 12.00%.
