# Shining Adventure Box

Everything the Shining Adventure Box can give, with quantities and chances.

## Shining Adventure Box: what it is and how to get it

The Shining Adventure Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances in the table add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Shining Adventure Box: notes

The most likely reward of the Shining Adventure Box is Advice of Valks (+50) at 9.00%, then Valks' Cry x40 at 8.50%. The rarest are Pearl Box - 5000 and Combat/Skill EXP Exchange Coupon, each at 0.10%. The box can also return more of itself: Shining Adventure Box x2 at 3.00%.
