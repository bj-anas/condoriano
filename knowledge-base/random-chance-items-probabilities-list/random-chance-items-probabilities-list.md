# Random Chance Items & Probabilities List

This index page of the official wiki lists the in-game items and systems that have random results, and links to a separate probability page for each one. It is split into In-game Systems, Events and Pearl Shop.

## Random Chance Items & Probabilities List: what this page is

The Random Chance Items & Probabilities List is an index of boxes, pouches, bundles and game systems whose outcomes depend on chance. Each entry has its own wiki page with the rewards or rates. The entries are grouped under three headings: In-game Systems, Events and Pearl Shop. The wiki states that the information will be updated whenever changes are made to it.

## Random Chance Items & Probabilities List: in-game systems covered

The In-game Systems group covers these pages: Sealed Fairy Wings, Worker Promotion, Dream Horse Awakening, Dream Horse Increased Awakening Probability, Increased Item Drop Rate Effect, Combat/Life/Skill/Mount EXP Gain Increase, Mystery Chest from Buried Trace, Rough Lustrous Gems, Treasure Keys, Purified Lightstones, Items from Production Node Obtained Sacks, Items from Monster Zone Drop Boxes, Fairy Queen's Might, Skill Learning Probability upon Pet Registration, Desert/Everfrost Illness, Fairy Skill Learning and Sprouting, Higher Grade Knowledge Gain, Reset Mount Growth, List of Bookshelves (Pearl Shop Items) and Obtainable Knowledge, Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp, Mount Level Down Ticket, and Choose Your Landscape Paint Box.

## Random Chance Items & Probabilities List: event items covered

The Events group has two pages. Black Spirit Pass / Daily Special Pass gives the odds for the event boxes of that pass, such as the Shakatu boxes and chests. Special ADV Manual Box gives the odds for the Old Moon manuals that come out of the event box. Both are separate wiki pages with their own reward tables.
