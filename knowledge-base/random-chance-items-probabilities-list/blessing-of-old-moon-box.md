# Blessing of Old Moon Box

The Blessing of Old Moon Packs the Blessing of Old Moon Box can give, with the chance of each pack length.

## Blessing of Old Moon Box: what it is and how to get it

The Blessing of Old Moon Box is a limited-time Pearl Shop item, so it may not always be available to buy. Opening it gives one Blessing of Old Moon Pack according to a set probability. The packs range from 3 Days up to 180 Days.

## Blessing of Old Moon Box: possible rewards and probabilities

| Item Name | Quantity | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Blessing of Old Moon Pack (180 Days) | 1 | 0.05% |
| Blessing of Old Moon Pack (90 Days) | 1 | 0.10% |
| Blessing of Old Moon Pack (30 Days) | 1 | 3.00% |
| Blessing of Old Moon Pack (20 Days) | 1 | 4.00% |
| Blessing of Old Moon Pack (15 Days) | 1 | 5.00% |
| Blessing of Old Moon Pack (7 Days) | 1 | 25.00% |
| Blessing of Old Moon Pack (3 Days) | 1 | 62.85% |

## Blessing of Old Moon Box: notes

The most likely result of the Blessing of Old Moon Box is the 3-day pack at 62.85%, then the 7-day pack at 25.00%. The longest pack, 180 Days, is the rarest at 0.05%, followed by the 90-day pack at 0.10%.
