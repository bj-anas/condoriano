# Rabbit Pouches of Fortune

How the Rabbit Pouch of Fortune chain works, with the chance at each pouch and the overall chance of each result.

## Rabbit Pouches of Fortune: what it is and how to get it

The Rabbit Pouch of Fortune is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. It works in stages: Rabbit Pouch of Fortune, then the Good, Great, Marvelous and Astonishing pouches. At each of the first four pouches there is a 50% chance of moving to the next pouch and a 50% chance of a reward. At the Marvelous pouch, the alternative to the Astonishing pouch is two Rabbit Pouch of Fortune.

## Rabbit Pouches of Fortune: possible rewards and probabilities

The "% per pouch" column is the chance at that pouch. The "Overall chance" column is the chance of ending with that result when opening one Rabbit Pouch of Fortune.

| Pouch | Contents | % per pouch | Overall chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rabbit Pouch of Fortune | Rabbit Pouch of Good Fortune | 50% | - |
| Rabbit Pouch of Fortune | Cron Stone x150 | 50% | 50% |
| Rabbit Pouch of Good Fortune | Rabbit Pouch of Great Fortune | 50% | - |
| Rabbit Pouch of Good Fortune | Rabbit Selection Box | 50% | 25% |
| Rabbit Pouch of Great Fortune | Rabbit Pouch of Marvelous Fortune | 50% | - |
| Rabbit Pouch of Great Fortune | New Year's Selection Box | 50% | 12.5% |
| Rabbit Pouch of Marvelous Fortune | Rabbit Pouch of Astonishing Fortune | 50% | 6.25% |
| Rabbit Pouch of Marvelous Fortune | Rabbit Pouch of Fortune x2 | 50% | 6.25% |

## Rabbit Pouches of Fortune: selection box contents

The Rabbit Selection Box from the Rabbit Pouch of Good Fortune has two bundles of selectable items. First bundle: [PREMIUM] Choppy or [CLASSIC] Fluffy. Second bundle: Inventory +1 Expansion Coupon x2, Elion's Tear x5, Theiah's Orb x1, or Pet Appearance Change Coupon x1.

The New Year's Selection Box from the Rabbit Pouch of Great Fortune has one bundle of selectable items: Mount Skill Change Coupon x9, Cron Stone x400, Advice of Valks (+70), Artisan's Memory x60, or Choose Your Horse Gear Set Box.

The Choose Your Horse Gear Set Box offers four horse gear sets. Any horse gear set obtained from this box is taxed when sold on the Central Market.

| Choose Your Horse Gear Set Box |
|---|
| Oquilla Dream Horse Gear Set |
| Dark Thorn Horse Gear Set |
| Trina Knight Horse Gear Set |
| Anemos Horse Gear Set |

## Rabbit Pouches of Fortune: Astonishing pouch and pets

The Rabbit Pouch of Astonishing Fortune, the last pouch in the Rabbit Pouch of Fortune chain, contains [Event] Premium Outfit Box and [Event] Classic Outfit Box. Shai and Woosa cannot open the [Event] Premium Outfit Box.

The two pets in the Rabbit Selection Box share one special skill and differ in talent:

| Pet | Special Skill | Talent |
|---|---|---|
| [PREMIUM] Choppy | Find Resources | Life EXP Boost |
| [CLASSIC] Fluffy | Find Resources | Death Penalty Resistance |

## Rabbit Pouches of Fortune: notes

Overall odds from the first Rabbit Pouch of Fortune: Cron Stone x150 at 50%, Rabbit Selection Box at 25%, New Year's Selection Box at 12.5%, and each of Rabbit Pouch of Astonishing Fortune and Rabbit Pouch of Fortune x2 at 6.25%. The Rabbit Pouch of Fortune x2 result restarts the chain.
