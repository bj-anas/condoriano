# Dream Horse Training Rarities

Rewards and chances for Dream Horse Training Rarities, the box of Courser training ingredients.

## Dream Horse Training Rarities: what it is and how to get it

Dream Horse Training Rarities is a Pearl Shop item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it gives one reward from the table, with the quantity shown.

## Dream Horse Training Rarities: notes

Six items share the top chance of 12% each, all at quantity 8: Rainbow Gem Fruit, Pure Forest Breath, Stonetail Fodder, Deep Blue Hoof Root, Breezy Conch Seaweed and Spirit Pouch of Ferocious Beast. The other four rewards, Fruit of Yianaros x9, Brilliant Crimson Fire Flower x9, Mysterious Blue Conch x9 and Krogdalo's Origin Stone x1, are 7% each.
