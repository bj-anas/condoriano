# Magic Crystal Rarities

The Black Magic Crystals and JIN Magic Crystals that Magic Crystal Rarities can give, with chances.

## Magic Crystal Rarities: what it is and how to get it

Magic Crystal Rarities is a Pearl Shop item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it gives one crystal from the table.

## Magic Crystal Rarities: notes

Black Magic Crystal - Valor, Precision, Swiftness and Armor are the most likely at 14% each. The other six Black Magic Crystals (Viper, Cobelinus, Hystria, Carmae, Harphia, Addis) are 6% each. The JIN Magic Crystals are rare: Cobelinus and Addis are 2% each, and Hystria, Carmae, Harphia and Viper are 1% each.
