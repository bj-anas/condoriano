# List of Bookshelves and Obtainable Knowledge from Bookshelves (Pearl Shop Items)

How Pearl Shop bookshelves give Knowledge, the rules and formula for the chances, the list of bookshelves, and every Knowledge category with its entries and probability.

## Pearl Shop bookshelves: how reading works

You place a bookshelf in your residence and use it to gain Knowledge from many categories. One reading takes an hour. You can keep reading even if you switch characters through Character Selection, but disconnecting or changing server ends the reading. When the reading is complete you obtain Knowledge from one of the many categories. You can get Knowledge you already own, and the bookshelf durability is still used up in that case. A bookshelf at zero durability gives no more Knowledge, but the installed furniture does not disappear.

## Pearl Shop bookshelves: Knowledge probability rules and formula

When reading ends, the Knowledge category is chosen with equal probability. Then the specific Knowledge inside that category is chosen with equal probability too. If the chosen Knowledge is Ecology type, its grade is also random with equal chance among S, A+, A, B and C. So the chance of a specific Knowledge entry is 1 divided by the number of categories, multiplied by 1 divided by the number of entries in the chosen category. For Ecology Knowledge, multiply again by 1 divided by the number of grades. Example 1: the Character Knowledge Rosa Kloko is 1/183 x 1/15 = 0.0364%. Example 2: the Ecology Knowledge Goblin Fighter (S) is 1/183 x 1/10 x 1/5 = 0.0109%. Percentages are rounded to the fourth decimal place.
