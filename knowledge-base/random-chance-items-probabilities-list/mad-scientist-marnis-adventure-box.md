# Mad Scientist Marni's Adventure Box

Everything the Mad Scientist Marni's Adventure Box can give, with amounts and chances.

## Mad Scientist Marni's Adventure Box: what it is and how to get it

The Mad Scientist Marni's Adventure Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances in the table add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward, in the amount shown.

## Mad Scientist Marni's Adventure Box: notes

The most likely rewards of the Mad Scientist Marni's Adventure Box are Cron Stone x150, Ribbon Cat x1 and Advice of Valks (+50) x1, each at 12.00%. The rarest is Cron Stone x5000 at 0.05%. The box can also return more of itself: Mad Scientist Marni's Adventure Box x2 at 10.00%.
