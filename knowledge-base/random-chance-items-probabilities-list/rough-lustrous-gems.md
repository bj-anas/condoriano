# Rough Lustrous Gems

Items you can get by exchanging Rough Lustrous Gems from Starlight Vein mining, with quantities and probabilities.

## Rough Lustrous Gems: how to get them and what they do

Mining the Starlight Vein gives Rough Lustrous Gems. These gems can be exchanged for some of the items in the table below, at set probabilities.

## Rough Lustrous Gems: possible items and probabilities

The last column is the probability of getting each item, per item and per quantity between the minimum and maximum.

## Rough Lustrous Gems: notes

Rough Stone and Black Stone Powder are guaranteed whenever the Starlight Vein is mined. Other items come additionally, and the chance of each of them is determined on its own, except for Rough Stone and Black Stone Powder. For instance, Rough Ruby comes with a 10.00% chance for each of 2, 3 or 4 pieces.
