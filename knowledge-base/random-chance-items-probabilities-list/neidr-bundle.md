# Neidr Bundle

Guaranteed items and chance-based bonus items of the Neidr Bundle.

## Neidr Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Neidr Bundle is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. Opening it always gives a Choose Your Combat/Life Scroll Box, a Neidr Outfit Box and a Box of 100 Cron Stones. It can also give several bonus items at set chances.

## Neidr Bundle: guaranteed items

Every Neidr Bundle opened gives these three items at 100.00%.

| Item | Quantity | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| Choose Your Combat/Life Scroll Box | 1 | 100.00% |
| Box of 100 Cron Stones | 1 | 100.00% |
| Neidr Outfit Box | 1 | 100.00% |

## Neidr Bundle: notes

The rarest bonus item of the Neidr Bundle is Ultimate Premium Enhancement Box x2 at 1.00%. The most likely bonus item is Mystical Cron Stone Bundle x1 at 80.00%, then Ellie's Magical Elixir and Fughar's Magical Elixir at 70.00% each.
