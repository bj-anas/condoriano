# Dream Horse Increased Awakening Probability

How the Dream Horse awakening success rate can rise after failed Courser training, and where to see it.

## Dream Horse Increased Awakening Probability: how it works

The success rate of awakening a Courser into a Dream Horse can go up in real time depending on how many times Courser training has failed. The current rate is displayed directly in the game.

## Dream Horse Increased Awakening Probability: where to check it

If you do Courser Training at the Stonetail Horse Ranch through Gula, the UI shows how likely the awakening of the Courser into a Dream Horse is to succeed. Courser training can also be done through Hyacinth and Melabee in Grána.
