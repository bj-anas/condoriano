# Black Friday Bundle

Everything the Black Friday Bundle can give, with the rate of each item.

## Black Friday Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Black Friday Bundle is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it can give 1 or more items, each according to its own set probability. Two items, Advice of Valks (+50) and [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box, are listed at 100.00%.

## Black Friday Bundle: notes

The rarest listed item in the Black Friday Bundle is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x10 at 0.10%, followed by Cron Stone x10,000 at 0.30%. The items at 100.00% are Advice of Valks (+50) and [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box. Advice of Valks (+60) is listed at 70.00% and Artisan's Memory x10 at 50.00%.
