# Mysterious ADV Bundle

Guaranteed items and chance-based bonus items of the Mysterious ADV Bundle.

## Mysterious ADV Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Mysterious ADV Bundle is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. Opening it always gives a set of guaranteed items, and it can also give items at the set chances shown on the page.

## Mysterious ADV Bundle: guaranteed items

Every Mysterious ADV Bundle opened gives these items at 100%. Fruit of Yianaros, Mysterious Blue Conch and Brilliant Crimson Fire Flower come in a quantity of 1 to 3 each.

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Advice of Valks (+50) | 1 |
| Fruit of Yianaros | 1-3 |
| Mysterious Blue Conch | 1-3 |
| Brilliant Crimson Fire Flower | 1-3 |
| Item Collection Increase Scroll | 5 |
| Cron Stone | 300 |

## Mysterious ADV Bundle: notes

The rarest bonus item of the Mysterious ADV Bundle is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x20 at 0.05%. The most likely is Choose Your Halloween Buff Box x2 at 20.00%. The bundle can also give another [Event] Mysterious ADV Bundle at 8.00%.
