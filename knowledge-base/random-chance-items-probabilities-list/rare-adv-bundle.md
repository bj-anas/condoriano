# Rare ADV Bundle

Guaranteed items, bonus items and the contents of the Rare Scroll Box and Mysterious Treasure Chest for the Rare ADV Bundle.

## Rare ADV Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Rare ADV Bundle is available for purchase in the Pearl Shop only for a limited time, and it is not sold all year. Opening it always gives a Rare Cron Stone Bundle x1 and a Rare Scroll Box x1, and it can also give several bonus items, each at its own set chance.

## Rare ADV Bundle: guaranteed items

Every Rare ADV Bundle opened gives these two items at 100%.

| Item | Qty | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| Rare Scroll Box | 1 | 100% |
| Rare Cron Stone Bundle | 1 | 100% |

## Rare ADV Bundle: notes

The rarest bonus item of the Rare ADV Bundle is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x50 at 0.01%. The most likely is Memory Fragment x20 at 70.00%. The bundle can also give another Rare ADV Bundle at 15.00%.
