# Choose Your Landscape Paint Box

What the two paint selection boxes (the standard one and its [Event] version) let you pick, how Allan Serbin's Landscape bonus drops work, and the bonus-item chances of each paint.

## Choose Your Landscape Paint Box: what it is and how it works

The Choose Your Landscape Paint Box and the [Event] Choose Your Landscape Paint Box let you select 1 of 5 options, quantity 1 each: [Event] Choose Your Landscape Paint Box (Archaeologist's Map), [Event] Choose Your Landscape Paint Box (Upgraded Compass), [Event] Choose Your Landscape Paint Box (Rich Merchant's Ring), [Event] Choose Your Landscape Paint Box (Upgraded Telescope), and La Orzeca Paint. If new paint items are added later, they may be added to the selection options of both boxes.

## Choose Your Landscape Paint Box: Allan Serbin's Landscape effects

While Allan Serbin's Landscape is active, defeating monsters gives a chance at two bonus rewards on top of the normal rewards: the active zone's Treasure material and an Empty Picture Frame. Each paint you use refills the Landscape of its monster zone to 10,000 points, and every monster defeated uses up 3 Landscape points from the active zone. Zones recommending less than 300 AP, Party Zones, and monster zones that are the same as the activated Landscape do not get these effects. Item Drop Rate effects do not apply to Landscape items. Example: with the Pila Ku Jail Paint activated in a zone where the effects apply, monsters there can also drop Pila Ku Jail Treasure materials and Empty Picture Frames, in addition to their normal rewards.

The [Event] Choose Your Landscape Paint Box (Archaeologist's Map) lets you select 1 of 2 paints.
