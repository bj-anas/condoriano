# Fantastic Young Dragon Surprise

The four Young Dragon pets that Fantastic Young Dragon Surprise can give, with their percentages.

## Fantastic Young Dragon Surprise: what it is and how to get it

Fantastic Young Dragon Surprise is a Pearl Shop item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. It gives one Young Dragon of a random tier.

## Fantastic Young Dragon Surprise: possible rewards and probabilities

| Item Name | Quantity | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Young Black Dragon (Tier 1) | 1 | 2% |
| Young Golden Dragon (Tier 2) | 1 | 60% |
| Young Golden Dragon (Tier 3) | 1 | 30% |
| Young Golden Dragon (Tier 4) | 1 | 8% |

## Fantastic Young Dragon Surprise: notes

Young Golden Dragon (Tier 2) is the most likely result at 60%, then Young Golden Dragon (Tier 3) at 30% and Young Golden Dragon (Tier 4) at 8%. Young Black Dragon (Tier 1) is the rarest at 2%.
