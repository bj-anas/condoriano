# Shining Travel Bundle

Guaranteed items, bonus items and the contents of the Resplendent Premium Enhancement Box and Blessing of Old Moon Box for the Shining Travel Bundle.

## Shining Travel Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Shining Travel Bundle is sold at the Pearl Shop only during a specific period. Opening it always gives Item Collection Increase Scroll x1 and Cron Stone x300, and it can also give additional bonus items at set chances.

## Shining Travel Bundle: guaranteed items

Every Shining Travel Bundle opened gives these two items at 100%.

| Item Name | Quantity | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| Cron Stone | 300 | 100% |
| Item Collection Increase Scroll | 1 | 100% |

## Shining Travel Bundle: notes

The rarest bonus item of the Shining Travel Bundle is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x30 at 0.03%. The most likely is Memory Fragment x5 at 50%, then Cron Stone x100 at 40%. The bundle can also give another Shining Travel Bundle at 4.5%.
