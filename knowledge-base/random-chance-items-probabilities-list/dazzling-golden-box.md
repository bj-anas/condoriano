# Dazzling Golden Box

The six possible results of the Dazzling Golden Box, with the rate of each.

## Dazzling Golden Box: what it is and how to get it

The Dazzling Golden Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The page gives a rate for each of six possible results, including outfit boxes, Cron Stone bundles and Dazzling ADV Bundles.

## Dazzling Golden Box: possible rewards and probabilities

| Item Name | Rate (%) |
|---|---|
| [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x10 | 2.0% |
| Rare Cron Stone Bundle x10 | 10.0% |
| Dazzling ADV Bundle x5 | 15.0% |
| [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x1 | 23.0% |
| [Event] Classic Outfit Box | 24.0% |
| Choose Your Dazzling Outfit Set | 26.0% |

## Dazzling Golden Box: notes

The most likely result of the Dazzling Golden Box is Choose Your Dazzling Outfit Set at 26.0%, then [Event] Classic Outfit Box at 24.0%. The rarest result is [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x10 at 2.0%.
