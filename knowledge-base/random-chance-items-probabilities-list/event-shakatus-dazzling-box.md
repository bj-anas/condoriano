# [Event] Shakatu's Dazzling Box

How the [Event] Shakatu's Dazzling Box leads to the Dazzling Treasure Box and Dazzling Adventure Box, and what those boxes and the boxes inside them can give.

## [Event] Shakatu's Dazzling Box: what it is and how to get it

The [Event] Shakatu's Dazzling Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it gives either the [Event] Shakatu's Dazzling Adventure Box or the [Event] Shakatu's Dazzling Treasure Box. Both boxes have their own reward tables.
