# Black Tiger Pouch of Fortune

How the Black Tiger Pouch of Fortune chain works, with the chance at each stage and the overall chance of each reward.

## Black Tiger Pouch of Fortune: what it is and how to get it

The Black Tiger Pouch of Fortune is a Pearl Shop item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. It works in stages. The first four pouches each give either the next pouch in the chain or a reward, at 50% each. The last pouch, Black Tiger Pouch of Astonishing Fortune, gives its rewards at 100%.

## Black Tiger Pouch of Fortune: possible rewards and probabilities

## Black Tiger Pouch of Fortune: notes

The [Event] Choose Your 20-Day Box lets you pick one of these items: [Event] Blessing of Kamasylve (20 Days), [Event] Secret Book of Old Moon (20 Days), [Event] Sealed Book of Combat (20 Days), or Sealed Book of Life (20 Days). The Marvelous Fortune pouch can give two Black Tiger Pouch of Fortune (6.25% overall), which restarts the chain. Overall odds from the first pouch: Mystical Artisan's Memory Bundle 50%, Sooty Roary 25%, Choose Your 20-Day Box 12.50%.
