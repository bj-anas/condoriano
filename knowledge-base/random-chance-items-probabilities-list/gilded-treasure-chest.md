# Gilded Treasure Chest

What the Gilded Treasure Chest gives, how the Radiant Treasure Chest, Gold Treasure Bundle and Silver Treasure Bundle work, and the chains for [Event] Advice of Immortality and [Event] Advice of Safety.

## Gilded Treasure Chest: what it is and how to get it

The Gilded Treasure Chest is a Pearl Shop item that is on sale only for a limited time. Opening it gives one of three results: a Radiant Treasure Chest, a Gold Treasure Bundle or a Silver Treasure Bundle.

## Gilded Treasure Chest: possible rewards and probabilities

| Item | Quantity | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| Radiant Treasure Chest | 1 | 1.00% |
| Gold Treasure Bundle | 1 | 49.00% |
| Silver Treasure Bundle | 1 | 50.00% |

## Gilded Treasure Chest: [Event] Advice of Immortality chain

[Event] Advice of Immortality, a bonus item of the Gold and Silver Treasure Bundles, works in steps. Each step gives either the next Immortality item or Caphras Stones. The "step" column is the chance at that step and the "overall" column is the overall chance of that content.

| Item | Content | Step probability | Overall probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Event] Advice of Immortality | [Event] First Immortality | 60% | 60.00% |
| [Event] Advice of Immortality | Caphras Stone x2 | 40% | 40.00% |
| [Event] First Immortality | [Event] Second Immortality | 55% | 33.00% |
| [Event] First Immortality | Caphras Stone x4 | 45% | 27.00% |
| [Event] Second Immortality | [Event] Third Immortality | 50% | 16.50% |
| [Event] Second Immortality | Caphras Stone x6 | 50% | 16.50% |
| [Event] Third Immortality | [Event] Last Immortality | 40% | 6.60% |
| [Event] Third Immortality | Caphras Stone x8 | 60% | 9.90% |

Opening [Event] Last Immortality gives all of these: Caphras Stone x10 and [Event] Remnants of Destiny x1.

[Event] Advice of Safety, a bonus item of the Gold and Silver Treasure Bundles, works in steps. Each step gives either the next Safety item or Cron Stones. The page headed this table [Event] Advice of Immortality, but its rows are for [Event] Advice of Safety.

| Item | Content | Step probability | Overall probability |
|---|---|---|---|

| [Event] First Safety | [Event] Second Safety | 45% | 22.50% |
