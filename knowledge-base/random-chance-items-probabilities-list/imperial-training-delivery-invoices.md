# Imperial Training Delivery Invoices

The Horse Emblems that two event boxes can give, the [Event] Horse Emblem Box and the [Event] Tier 8 Horse Emblem Box, with the chance of each.

## Imperial Training Delivery Invoices: what they are and how to get the boxes

Both the [Event] Horse Emblem Box and the [Event] Tier 8 Horse Emblem Box are received by trading in [Event] Imperial Training Delivery Invoices through the Manage Currency UI. Each box gives one Horse Emblem with the probability shown in its table. The page does not say how many invoices each box costs.

## Imperial Training Delivery Invoices: notes

In the [Event] Horse Emblem Box, the Tier 6 emblems (6-4 and 6-7, both sexes) are the most likely at 15.00% each, and the Tier 8 emblems (8-4 and 8-5) are the rarest at 4.00% each. In the [Event] Tier 8 Horse Emblem Box, Tier 8-1 (Female) is the most likely at 21.00%, and the Tier 8-5 and 8-4 emblems are the rarest at 2.50% each.
