# Guardians of Atoraxxion Box

The chance of each Atoraxxion pet and pet tier from the Guardians of Atoraxxion Box, and the special skill and talent of each pet.

## Guardians of Atoraxxion Box: what it is and how to get it

The Guardians of Atoraxxion Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The page lists six Atoraxxion pets (Cartios, Merrashan, Tarkhes, Lahtron, Monoceros and Putrakium) in four tiers, with a chance for each pet in each tier. The six pets share the same chance within a tier.

## Guardians of Atoraxxion Box: possible rewards and probabilities by tier

Every pet in a tier has the same chance. The "by tier" figure is the total chance of getting that tier.

| Tier | Chance for each of the six pets | Percentage by tier |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 0.17% | 1.00% |
| 3 | 0.83% | 5.00% |
| 2 | 1.67% | 10.00% |
| 1 | 14.00% | 84.00% |

The pets in every tier are Cartios, Merrashan, Tarkhes, Lahtron, Monoceros and Putrakium.

## Guardians of Atoraxxion Box: Atoraxxion pet details

These are the special skill and talent of each Atoraxxion pet from the Guardians of Atoraxxion Box.

| Pet | Special Skill | Talent | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monoceros | Hostility Detection | Weight Limit Up | - |
| Putrakium | Hostility Detection | Combat EXP Up | Can loot items from a slightly further distance. Only one of this pet can be taken out at a time. |
| Tarkhes | Finds Rare Monsters | Item Drop Rate Up | Only one of this pet can be taken out at a time. Only 1 pet with the Talent: Item Collection Increase can be called out. |
| Lahtron | Auto-fishing Time Reduction | Durability Reduction Resistance | - |
| Cartios | Finds Resources | Skill EXP Boost | - |
| Merrashan | Taunt Monster | Death Penalty Decrease | - |

## Guardians of Atoraxxion Box: notes

The most likely result from the Guardians of Atoraxxion Box is a Tier 1 pet, at 84.00% in total (14.00% for each pet). A Tier 4 pet is the rarest at 1.00% in total (0.17% for each pet).
