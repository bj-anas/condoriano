# Triple-Float Fishing Rod

Chances of getting one, two, three or four catches with the Triple-Float Fishing Rod.

## Triple-Float Fishing Rod: how it works

The Triple-Float Fishing Rod is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only the chances of getting multiple catches with this rod, and the four outcomes add up to 100%. It does not describe how the rod is obtained.

## Triple-Float Fishing Rod: probabilities of multiple catches

These are the chances of the number of catches the Triple-Float Fishing Rod gives.

| Outcome | Probability |
|---|---|
| Chance to get 1 catch | 51.2% |
| Chance to get 2 catches | 38.4% |
| Chance to get 3 catches | 9.6% |
| Chance to get 4 catches | 0.8% |

The most likely result with the Triple-Float Fishing Rod is 1 catch at 51.2%, then 2 catches at 38.4%. Four catches is the rarest at 0.8%.
