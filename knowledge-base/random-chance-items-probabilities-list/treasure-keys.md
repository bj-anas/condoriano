# Treasure Keys

The three treasure keys (Glimmering, Sparkling, Dazzling), the treasures they open, and what each opening can give.

## Treasure Keys: what they open

Three keys open treasures: the Glimmering Treasure Key, the Sparkling Treasure Key and the Dazzling Treasure Key. Each of the three keys opens the same list of treasures: Shining Softly, Small Gold Bar Box, Clattering Rattle, Fragrant Scent of Soil, Someone's Trade Goods, Oquillan Turban Shell, Cracked Pot, Biraghi's Secret Chamber. Opening a treasure gives items at the probabilities in the key's table. The Glimmering key gives a Silver Cache, while the Sparkling and Dazzling keys give a Loaded Silver Cache.

## Glimmering Treasure Key: possible rewards and probabilities

| Obtainable Item | Min Qty | Max Qty | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mass of Pure Magic | 1 | 1 | 50.00% |
| Narc's Lightning | 1 | 1 | 50.00% |
| Shard of the Drained Night | 1 | 1 | 50.00% |
| Revived Lunar Necklace | 1 | 1 | 1.00% |
| Seed of Void | 1 | 1 | 30.00% |
| Black Stone | 4 | 8 | 16.67% |
| Silver Cache | 1 | 1 | 100.00% |

The Silver Cache is guaranteed when you use the Glimmering Treasure Key. The other items are added at their set probabilities. The chance listed for Black Stone (Weapon/Armor) is per opening.

## Sparkling Treasure Key: possible rewards and probabilities

| Obtainable Item | Min Qty | Max Qty | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mass of Pure Magic | 1 | 5 | 10.00% |
| Narc's Lightning | 1 | 5 | 10.00% |
| Shard of the Drained Night | 1 | 5 | 10.00% |
| Revived Lunar Necklace | 1 | 1 | 1.00% |
| Seed of Void | 1 | 3 | 16.67% |
| Black Stone | 40 | 100 | 1.61% |
| Loaded Silver Cache | 1 | 1 | 100.00% |

The Loaded Silver Cache is guaranteed with the Sparkling Treasure Key, and the other items are added at their set probabilities. The chances for Seed of Void, Black Stone (Weapon/Armor), Shard of the Drained Night, Narc's Lightning and Mass of Pure Magic apply per opening. For instance, Mass of Pure Magic has a 10.00% chance for each of the quantities 1, 2 and 3.

## Silver Cache and Loaded Silver Cache: silver amounts

| Cache | Item | Min Qty | Max Qty | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silver Cache | Silver | 4,000,000 | 4,000,000 | 100.00% |
| Loaded Silver Cache | Silver | 4,000,000 | 40,000,000 | 0.000003% |

The Silver Cache always holds exactly 4,000,000 Silver. The Loaded Silver Cache holds anywhere from 4,000,000 to 40,000,000 Silver. The wiki gives the formula for the Loaded Silver Cache chance as 100/36,000,001 = 0.000003%.
