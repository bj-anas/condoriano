# Bombastic ADV Bundle

The guaranteed and chance-based items of the Bombastic ADV Bundle, with the rate of each.

## Bombastic ADV Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Bombastic ADV Bundle is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it can give 1 or more items. The chance of each item is applied separately, so one bundle can give many items. Each bundle you open always includes three guaranteed items: Advice of Valks (+50), Item Collection Increase Scroll x5 and Cron Stone x300.

## Bombastic ADV Bundle: notes

The three guaranteed items of the Bombastic ADV Bundle are the ones listed at 100.00%. The rarest bonus item is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x20 at 0.05%. The most likely bonus item is [Event] Lara's Warm Black Tea x2 at 50.00%, then Memory Fragment x30 at 25.00%.
