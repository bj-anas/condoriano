# Ba-dump Gift Box

How the Ba-dump Gift Box chain works, what each gift box and Sweet Enhancement Box contains, the final chance of each result, and how Sugary Sweet Souvenir Seals are exchanged.

## Ba-dump Gift Box: what it is and how to get it

The Ba-dump Gift Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. It works in stages through the Ba-dump Gift Box, Bashful Gift Box, Heart's A-flutter Gift Box and Thrill Me Gift Box. Each stage gives a Sweet Enhancement Box, a Meant-to-be Gift Box, or the next gift box. A Ba-dump Gift Box can also be obtained by exchanging 5 Sugary Sweet Souvenir Seals at Velia's Blacksmith Tranan Underfoe.

## Ba-dump Gift Box: contents of the Sweet Enhancement Boxes and Meant-to-be Gift Box

Opening any of these gives all of the items listed.

- Sweet Enhancement Box I: Cron Stone x120, Memory Fragment x10, Sugary Sweet Souvenir Seal x1.
- Sweet Enhancement Box II: Cron Stone x200, Memory Fragment x20, Sugary Sweet Souvenir Seal x2.
- Sweet Enhancement Box III: Cron Stone x400, [Event] Dim Origin of Dark Hunger x1, Memory Fragment x45, Sugary Sweet Souvenir Seal x3.
- Meant-to-be Gift Box: [Event] Dim Origin of Dark Hunger x3 and Sweet Premium Enhancement Box x1.

## Ba-dump Gift Box: Sugary Sweet Souvenir Seal exchange

Sugary Sweet Souvenir Seals from the Ba-dump Gift Box can be exchanged at any time through Velia's Blacksmith Tranan Underfoe.

| Required seals | Item received |
|---|---|
| 1 | Box of 15 Cron Stones x1 |
| 5 | Ba-dump Gift Box x1 |
| 20 | Sweet Premium Enhancement Box x1 |
| 50 | Sweet Premium Enhancement Box x3 |
