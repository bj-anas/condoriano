Everything the Snowy Winter Adventure Box can give, with the chance of each reward.

## Snowy Winter Adventure Box: what it is and how to get it

The Snowy Winter Adventure Box is a Pearl Shop item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it gives one of the rewards below at the listed percentage. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Snowy Winter Adventure Box: notes

The most likely single reward is Artisan's Memory x30 at 15.00%, followed by Cron Stone x150 at 13.00% and Little Lamb x1 at 11.00%. The rarest reward is Cron Stone x5,000 at 0.01%. The box can also give more Snowy Winter Adventure Boxes: x10 at 0.50% and x2 at 4.00%.
