# Wishful Pouch of Fortune

How the Wishful Pouch of Fortune chain works, with the chance at each stage and the overall chance of each reward.

## Wishful Pouch of Fortune: what it is and how to get it

The Wishful Pouch of Fortune is a Pearl Shop item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. It works in stages. The first four pouches each give either the next pouch in the chain or a reward, at 50% each. The last pouch, Wishful Pouch of Astonishing Fortune, gives its reward at 100%.

## Wishful Pouch of Fortune: possible rewards and probabilities

The "% per stage" column is the chance at that pouch. The "% per item" column is the overall chance of ending with that reward.

## Wishful Pouch of Fortune: notes

Overall odds from the first pouch: Cron Stone x100 is 50%, [Transaction Maid] Fairy Irene is 25%, Artisan's Memory x33 is 12.50%, Canape Classic Box is 6.25%, and [Event] Premium Outfit Box is 6.25%.
