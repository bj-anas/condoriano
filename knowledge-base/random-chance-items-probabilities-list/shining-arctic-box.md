# Shining Arctic Box

Everything the Shining Arctic Box can give, with chances.

## Shining Arctic Box: what it is and how to get it

The Shining Arctic Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The six chances add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one pet: an Arctic Fox or a Polar Bear, of Tier 2, 3 or 4.

## Shining Arctic Box: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Shining Arctic Box gives one of these pets.

| Item Name | Probability |
|---|---|
| Arctic Fox (Tier 4) | 5% |
| Arctic Fox (Tier 3) | 15% |
| Arctic Fox (Tier 2) | 30% |
| Polar Bear (Tier 4) | 5% |
| Polar Bear (Tier 3) | 15% |
| Polar Bear (Tier 2) | 30% |

## Shining Arctic Box: notes

The most likely results from the Shining Arctic Box are Arctic Fox (Tier 2) and Polar Bear (Tier 2), at 30% each. The rarest are the Tier 4 versions of both pets, at 5% each. Arctic Fox and Polar Bear have identical chances at each tier.
