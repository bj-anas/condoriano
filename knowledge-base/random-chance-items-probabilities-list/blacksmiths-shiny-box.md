# Blacksmith's Shiny Box

How the Blacksmith's Shiny Box chain works, what its enhancement boxes contain, how [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseals are exchanged, and the contents of the Premium Enhancement Boxes.

## Blacksmith's Shiny Box: what it is and how to get it

The Blacksmith's Shiny Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. It works in stages through the Blacksmith's Shiny Box, Beginner Blacksmith's Box, Skilled Blacksmith's Box and Artisan Blacksmith's Box. Each stage gives either the next box or a Blacksmith's Enhancement Box, at 50% each. A Blacksmith's Shiny Box can also be obtained for 5 [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseals at <Blacksmith> Tranan Underfoe in Velia.

## Blacksmith's Shiny Box: possible rewards and probabilities

The "by box" column is the chance at that stage. The "overall" column is the overall chance of ending with that reward.

| Artisan Blacksmith's Box | Blacksmith's Shiny Box x2 | 50% | 6.25% |

## Blacksmith's Shiny Box: contents of the Enhancement Boxes and Guru Blacksmith's Resplendent Box

Opening any of these gives all of the items listed.

- Blacksmith's Enhancement Box I: Cron Stone x150, Memory Fragment x30, [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseal x1.
- Blacksmith's Enhancement Box II: Cron Stone x250, [Event] Dim Origin of Dark Hunger x1, Memory Fragment x40, [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseal x2.
- Blacksmith's Enhancement Box III: Cron Stone x400, [Event] Dim Origin of Dark Hunger x2, Memory Fragment x60, [Event] Blacksmith's Emberseal x3.
- Guru Blacksmith's Resplendent Box: Radiant Premium Enhancement Box and Advice of Valks (+100).
