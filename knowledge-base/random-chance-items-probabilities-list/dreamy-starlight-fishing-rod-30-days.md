# Dreamy Starlight Fishing Rod (30 Days)

The effects, limits and multiple-catch chances of this fishing rod, which lasts 30 days.

## Dreamy Starlight Fishing Rod (30 Days): how it works

The Dreamy Starlight Fishing Rod (30 Days) lets you catch several fish at once, in the same way as the Triple-Float Fishing Rod. Its effects are Max Auto-fishing Time Reduction, Fishing Mastery +100, and Max Durability of 2,000. It expires 30 days after you receive it and cannot be used afterward. It cannot be repaired and cannot be branded.

## Dreamy Starlight Fishing Rod (30 Days): multiple catch probabilities

These are the chances of getting 1, 2, 3 or 4 catches at once with this rod.

| Result | Probability |
|---|---|
| Chance to get 1 catch | 51.2% |
| Chance to get 2 catches | 38.4% |
| Chance to get 3 catches | 9.6% |
| Chance to get 4 catches | 0.8% |

## Dreamy Starlight Fishing Rod (30 Days): notes

With the Dreamy Starlight Fishing Rod (30 Days), a single catch is the most likely result at 51.2%, then 2 catches at 38.4%. Getting 4 catches at once is the rarest at 0.8%. The rod cannot be used after 30 days and cannot be repaired.
