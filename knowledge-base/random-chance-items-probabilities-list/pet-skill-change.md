# Pet Skill Change

How the Pet Skill Change Coupon works: what it changes, how to use it, and where to see the odds.

## Pet Skill Change Coupon: what it is and how to get it

The Pet Skill Change Coupon is bought from the Pearl Shop (F3). It changes one of your pet's current skills once, without using the pet exchange function. Use it by right-clicking the item while the pet is checked in. Pet talents cannot be changed. The Polar Bear, for instance, cannot change its Auto-Fishing Time Reduction special ability or its Increase Weight Limit talent. Skills tied to the pet's tier, such as Fishing +1, Gathering EXP Increase and Hunting EXP Increase, can be changed.

## Pet Skill Change Coupon: how to use it

1. Keep the Pet Skill Change Coupon in your Pearl Inventory, then open the pet window, either from the top left of the screen or via Menu (ESC) > Function (F8) > Pet.
2. Click the Change Skill button in the list of checked-in pets.
3. Pick the skill to change by clicking its Change button (talents are excluded).
4. A list of available skills appears on the right. Click a skill to see its change probability.
5. Press Apply. One coupon is consumed and the skill changes to one of the listed skills at the stated probability.

## Pet Skill Change Coupon: probabilities and results

The chance of each skill is shown during the skill change and differs depending on the skill type you select from the wanted skill list. If the change succeeds, the pet gets the skill you wanted. If it fails, the pet gets a different skill and its Skill Change Point count goes up by 1. The wiki adds that the guide may differ from the actual game depending on updates.
