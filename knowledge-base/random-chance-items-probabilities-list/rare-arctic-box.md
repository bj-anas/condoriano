# Rare Arctic Box

Everything the Rare Arctic Box can give, with quantities and chances.

## Rare Arctic Box: what it is and how to get it

The Rare Arctic Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances in the table add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward, in the quantity shown.

## Rare Arctic Box: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Rare Arctic Box gives one of these rewards.

| Item Name | Quantity | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| Krogdalo's Origin Stone | 1 | 1% |
| Advice of Valks (+50) | 1 | 1% |
| Valks' Cry | 10 | 5% |
| Cron Stone | 30 | 5% |
| Concentrated Magical Black Gem | 3 | 10% |
| Forest Fury | 1 | 12% |
| Concentrated Magical Black Stone | 6 | 24% |
| Black Gem | 10 | 12% |
| Advice of Valks (+30) | 1 | 15% |
| Memory Fragment | 10 | 15% |

## Rare Arctic Box: notes

The most likely reward of the Rare Arctic Box is Concentrated Magical Black Stone x6 at 24%, then Advice of Valks (+30) x1 and Memory Fragment x10 at 15% each. The rarest are Krogdalo's Origin Stone x1 and Advice of Valks (+50) x1, each at 1%.
