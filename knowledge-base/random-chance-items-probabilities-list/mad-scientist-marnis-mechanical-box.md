# Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box

Everything the Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box can give, with amounts and chances.

## Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box: what it is and how to get it

The Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The six chances add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward.

## Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box gives one of these rewards, in the amount shown.

| Item | Amount | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Cron Stone | 1000 | 1% |
| Marnist Outfit Box | 1 | 18% |
| Clickety-clack 0910 | 1 | 15% |
| Blessing of Kamasylve (15 Days) | 1 | 15% |
| Secret Book of Old Moon (15 Days) | 1 | 17% |
| Mad Scientist Marni's Experiment Byproducts | 1 | 34% |

## Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box: notes

The most likely reward of the Mad Scientist Marni's Mechanical Box is Mad Scientist Marni's Experiment Byproducts x1 at 34%, followed by Marnist Outfit Box x1 at 18%. The rarest reward is Cron Stone x1000 at 1%.
