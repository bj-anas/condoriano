# Grandpa Cron's Fishing Rod

The chance of getting multiple catches at once with Grandpa Cron's Fishing Rod.

## Grandpa Cron's Fishing Rod: how it works

Grandpa Cron's Fishing Rod can bring in more than one catch at a time. The page lists the chance of getting 1, 2, 3 or 4 catches with this rod in the table below.

## Grandpa Cron's Fishing Rod: multiple catch probabilities

These are the chances of getting 1, 2, 3 or 4 catches at once with this rod.

| Result | Probability |
|---|---|
| Chance to get 1 catch | 51.2% |
| Chance to get 2 catches | 38.4% |
| Chance to get 3 catches | 9.6% |
| Chance to get 4 catches | 0.8% |

## Grandpa Cron's Fishing Rod: notes

With Grandpa Cron's Fishing Rod, a single catch is the most likely result at 51.2%, then 2 catches at 38.4%. Getting 4 catches at once is the rarest at 0.8%.
