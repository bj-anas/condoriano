# Radiant Adventure Box

Everything the Radiant Adventure Box can give, with quantities and chances.

## Radiant Adventure Box: what it is and how to get it

The Radiant Adventure Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances in the table add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Radiant Adventure Box: notes

The most likely rewards of the Radiant Adventure Box are Horse Emblem: Tier 8-5 (Male), Artisan's Memory x22 and Cron Stone x100, each at 10.00%. The rarest are Advice of Valks (+150) and Cron Stone x2,500, each at 0.10%. The box can also return more of itself: Radiant Adventure Box x10 at 0.50% and x2 at 3.00%.
