# Items from Monster Zone Drop Boxes

Monster zone drop boxes and what each one can give: Black Stone Bundle, weapon boxes, defense gear boxes, earring box, magic crystal boxes, soiled accessories and Marni's Research Boxes.

## Monster zone drop boxes: overview

Opening a monster zone drop box gives one of the items listed for that box at the stated probability. The boxes covered on the wiki page are: Black Stone Bundle, Offensive, Accuracy, Defensive, Krea and Rosar Sub-weapon Boxes, Bares, Yuria, Krea, Kalis, Rosar and Seleth Main Weapon Boxes, eight Defense Gear Boxes (Zereth, Hercules', Heve's, Agerian, Grunil, Fortuna's Luck, Talis and Rocaba), Reliable Adventurer's Earring Box, Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, four Soiled accessories and the Marni's Research Boxes.

## Black Stone Bundle: quantity and probability

Opening a Black Stone Bundle gives between 25 and 50 Black Stone.

| Obtainable item | Min Qty | Max Qty | Probability (%) |
|---|---|---|---|
