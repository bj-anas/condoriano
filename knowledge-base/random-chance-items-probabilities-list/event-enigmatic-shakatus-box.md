## [Event] Enigmatic Shakatu's Box: what it is and how to get it

The [Event] Enigmatic Shakatu's Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The page lists each possible item with its rate. The list includes Resplendent Alchemy Stones, Garmoth's Heart, Vell's Heart, Cron Stone bundles, horse gear boxes, Advice of Valks and more copies of the box itself.

## [Event] Enigmatic Shakatu's Box: notes

The most likely item from the [Event] Enigmatic Shakatu's Box is Cron Stone x20 at 23.36%, then Memory Fragment x15 at 15.00%. The rarest are the three Resplendent Alchemy Stones (Destruction, Protection, Life) at 0.01% each. The box can also give [Event] Enigmatic Shakatu's Box x2 (6.00%) or a single [Event] Enigmatic Shakatu's Box (10.00%).
