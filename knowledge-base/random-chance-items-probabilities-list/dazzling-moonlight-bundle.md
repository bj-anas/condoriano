# Dazzling Moonlight Bundle

The guaranteed items, bonus items, and the contents of the Valks' Fortune Box and Adventurer's Scroll Box inside the Dazzling Moonlight Bundle.

## Dazzling Moonlight Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Dazzling Moonlight Bundle is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. Opening it always gives Adventurer's Scroll Box x1 and Cron Stone x250. You may also receive additional items according to set probabilities.
