# Gleaming Starlight Bundle

The guaranteed and bonus items of the Gleaming Starlight Bundle, and the contents of the Luminous Outfit Box, Radiant Journey Box and Choose Your Resplendent Journey Box.

## Gleaming Starlight Bundle: what it is and how to get it

The Gleaming Starlight Bundle is a Pearl Shop item that is on sale only for a limited time. Opening it always gives [Event] Giovan Grolin's Support Scroll x1 and Cron Stone x300. You can also receive additional bonus items according to a set probability.
