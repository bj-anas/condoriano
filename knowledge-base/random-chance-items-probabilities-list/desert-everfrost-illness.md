# Desert/Everfrost Illness

Default chances of catching an illness in the Desert and Everfrost regions, from the official wiki.

## Desert/Everfrost Illness: how often the chance is rolled

In the Desert and Everfrost regions, an illness check happens once every 10 seconds at the set probabilities below. The illness depends on the region and on whether it is day or night.

## Desert/Everfrost Illness: probabilities by region and time

| Region | Time | Illness | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desert | Day | Heatstroke | 5% |
| Desert | Night | Hypothermia | 4.5% |
| Everfrost | Day | Frostbite | 5% |
| Everfrost | Night | Hypothermia | 4.5% |
