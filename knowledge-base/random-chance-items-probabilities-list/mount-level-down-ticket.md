# Mount Level Down Ticket

How the Mount Level Down Ticket lowers a horse's level by one at a stable, and the rules for using it.

## Mount Level Down Ticket: what it does

If you dislike the stat gains a mount got from leveling up, the Mount Level Down Ticket lets you undo the last level at a stable. The mount goes down by 1 level and goes back to the stats and skills it had at that level. Example: a level 30 mount drops to level 29 with its level 29 stats. If right after a level up you learned a skill with a Mount Skill Selection Coupon or a Mount Skill Change Coupon, that skill disappears.

## Mount Level Down Ticket: how to use it

Talk to a Stable Keeper in any town to open the stable menu, select a mount that has leveled up, and press the Mount Level Down button. The mount must be checked into the stable and you must have a Mount Level Down Ticket. The Mount Level Down menu shows which stats will decrease and which skills will be lost. A chat window then lets you use the ticket.

## Mount Level Down Ticket: restrictions

Only horses can use it. It does not work on non-horse mounts, guild mounts or NPC mounts. It cannot be used on a mount at level 1 or at level 31 or higher, or on a mount that has not leveled up since the ticket was released. After a level down, the next ticket can be used only once the mount levels up again. Mount stat gains depend on your Family's Training level and the horse's tier, and the Growth Calculator shows them.
