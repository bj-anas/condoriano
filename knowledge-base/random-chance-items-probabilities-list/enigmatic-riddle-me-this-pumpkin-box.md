# Enigmatic / Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box

Probability information for the Enigmatic Pumpkin Box and the Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box, including the Golden Pumpkin Box and Golden Pumpkin Key.

## Enigmatic / Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box: what they are and how to get them

The Enigmatic Pumpkin Box is a limited-time Pearl Shop item. Opening it gives one item at a set probability. One possible result is the Golden Pumpkin Box, which contains the Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box. The Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box also gives one item at a set probability.

## Golden Pumpkin Box and Golden Pumpkin Key

The Golden Pumpkin Box, an 8.00% result of the Enigmatic Pumpkin Box, gives all of these items when opened: Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box x1 and [Event] Golden Pumpkin Key x1. [Event] Golden Pumpkin Keys can be exchanged: 2 keys for Advice of Valks (+110) x1, or 3 keys for Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box x1.

## Enigmatic / Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box: notes

Shai characters are unable to open the [Event] Premium Outfit Box that is obtained from a Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box. The most likely result of the Riddle-Me-This Pumpkin Box is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x1 at 30.59%, and the rarest is Cron Stone x30,000 at 0.01%. In the Enigmatic Pumpkin Box, the most likely result is Cron Stone x200 at 33.04% and the rarest is [Event] Premium Outfit Box x20 at 0.01%.
