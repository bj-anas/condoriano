# Dimensional Adventure Box

Everything the Dimensional Adventure Box can give, with quantities and chances.

## Dimensional Adventure Box: what it is and how to get it

The Dimensional Adventure Box is an item listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The page gives each possible reward with its quantity and percentage, including outfit swap boxes, pets, Cron Stone and Artisan's Memory.

## Dimensional Adventure Box: notes

The most likely results of the Dimensional Adventure Box are Artisan's Memory x30 and Cron Stone x200, both at 22%. The rarest is Outfit Swap Box II x5 at 1%. The box can also contain 2 more Dimensional Adventure Boxes at 3%.
