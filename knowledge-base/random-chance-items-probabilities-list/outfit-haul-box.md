# Outfit Haul Box

Everything the Outfit Haul Box can give, with chances.

## Outfit Haul Box: what it is and how to get it

The Outfit Haul Box is listed in the Random Chance Items & Probabilities List. The wiki page gives only its contents and chances, not where it is sold or obtained. The chances in the table add up to 100%, so each box opened gives one reward, either [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Boxes or [Event] Classic Outfit Boxes. Quantities are part of the item name.

## Outfit Haul Box: possible rewards and probabilities

Each opening of the Outfit Haul Box gives one of these rewards.

| Item Name | Rate(%) |
|---|---|
| [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x10 | 0.10% |
| [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x5 | 1.00% |
| [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x2 | 10.00% |
| [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box x1 | 35.00% |
| [Event] Classic Outfit Box x10 | 0.10% |
| [Event] Classic Outfit Box x5 | 1.00% |
| [Event] Classic Outfit Box x2 | 17.80% |
| [Event] Classic Outfit Box x1 | 35.00% |

## Outfit Haul Box: notes

The most likely rewards of the Outfit Haul Box are x1 of [Event] Choose Your Premium Outfit Box and x1 of [Event] Classic Outfit Box, each at 35.00%. The rarest are the x10 versions of both boxes, each at 0.10%.
