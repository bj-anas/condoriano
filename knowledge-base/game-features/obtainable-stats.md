# Obtainable Stats

Extra stats you can earn without gear, through level-ups, quests, buffs and adventure logs. They are useful once your gear is maxed out.

## Obtainable Stats: overview and totals

Besides equipment, stats come from achievements, quests and completing adventure logs. Most of these stats apply to every character in the Family. The exception is level-up stats: AP +1 and DP +1 come only at Lv. 56 and Lv. 60. The sources are Level Up, Happy Black Spirit, Barrier of Infestation, the Kamasylvia and O'dyllita Main Quests, and Adventure Logs. The grand totals available are AP 10, DP 10, All Accuracy 33, All Evasion 12, Max HP 932, Max Stamina 458, Max MP/SP/WP 10, Weight Limit 50 and Inventory Slot 6.

## Obtainable Stats: Level Up

Leveling gives AP +1 and DP +1 in total. You get DP +1 at Lv. 56 and AP +1 at Lv. 60, automatically when you reach those levels.

## Obtainable Stats: Happy Black Spirit

Happy Black Spirit gives Max HP +30, Max Stamina +20, Max MP/WP/SP +10, Accuracy +4, Evasion +4, and also AP +1 and DP +1. It is a buff granted to all characters in the Family to celebrate the global start of Black Desert self-publishing, with no requirements. Hover over the Happy Black Spirit icon on the Family Buff UI for details.

## Obtainable Stats: Barrier of Infestation

Barrier of Infestation totals AP +1, DP +1 and Max HP +20 for all Family characters. It is a questline about defeating the evil god Kzarka in the Elvia Realm. Start it by accepting [Elvia] Dreary Moonlight from the Black Spirit after finishing the [Elvia] Obsidian Ashes questline. It has five levels, and you must complete all of them to get the stats. See the Kzarka: Barrier of Infestation page.

## Obtainable Stats: Kamasylvia main quest A Gift for Papu

The Kamasylvia main quest reward is DP +1 for the whole Family, from completing A Gift for Papu. Merindora in Grána gives it once you know the Light of Kamasylvia knowledge, gained from the main quest Healing Light before the main quest renewal, or [Kamasylvia] The Heart of Kamasylve after it. To finish it, bring three accessories to Merindora, and repeats of the same one count: Forest Ronaros Ring, Sicil's Necklace, Serap's Necklace or Ring of Cadry Guardian. The quest is listed in the Suggested tab as [ADV Support] [Lv. 58] Family Empowered by Merindora's Blessing.

## Obtainable Stats: O'dyllita main quest Mother's Oracle

The O'dyllita main quest reward is AP +1 for the whole Family, from the Mother's Oracle questline. Viorencia Odore in O'draxxia gives it once you know the Day of the Final Battle knowledge, learned by finishing the O'dyllita PT2 main quest [O'dyllita] Hadum's Realm. To finish it, bring three accessories to Azure Mantis at the Crypt of Resting Thoughts, repeats allowed: Tungrad Ring, Tungrad Necklace, Tungrad Earring or Tungrad Belt. It is listed in the Suggested tab as [ADV Support] [Lv. 60] Family Empowered by Viorencia's Blessing.
