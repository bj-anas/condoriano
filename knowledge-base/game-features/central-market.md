# Central Market

The Central Market is the player-to-player marketplace for buying and selling items, using silver and items held in your market warehouse.

## Central Market: what it is

The Central Market is the only way Adventurers can trade items with each other. You can open it from anywhere through the main menu (hotkey: Esc), and it works with the silver and items stored in your market warehouse.

## Central Market: interface

The Central Market window has these parts:

- Basic Information: the silver in your market warehouse, its volume capacity, a button to manage it and move items between inventory, town storage and warehouse, and your market silver collection percentage benefit. Family fame gives extra silver from sales.
- Buy & Sell: the Buy menu lets you pick a category or search. The Sell menu shows items in your warehouse with their average sale price, your recent purchases and sales, market notifications, and your current listings (Sell tab) and pre-orders (Buy tab).
- Item Categories, each with subcategories.
- Item List: items currently listed or listable. Click one to open its purchase window.
- Search Type: all categories or only the selected one.
- Search Bar: type an item name.
- Status: shows your listed items and pending pre-orders, the same as the Sell & Buy tab.

## Central Market: warehouse basics

To buy or sell, you need silver for buying and the items for selling, both stored in your warehouse. Manage it through Central Market Director NPCs or transaction maids/butlers. You can deposit silver by pressing the Central Market button when interacting with a Central Market Director, the Open Central Market button when using a transaction maid/butler, or the Warehouse button with a storage keeper in any major city or town. Director and storage keeper NPCs also let you move items from the town storage.

## Central Market: warehouse capacity

The warehouse is capped at 5,000 VT, expandable by another 2,000 VT as family fame rises. A transaction maid/butler can move up to 200 VT of one item type at a time. Each item's description gives its warehouse volume, which may differ from its weight. The warehouse is shared by all characters in a family across all territories.

## Central Market: registering items for sale

Once an item is in your warehouse, open Central Market - Warehouse, select it, and register it in the Sell window by choosing the price and quantity. For example, 440 Bottle of River Water at 6,250 silver each totals 2,750,000 silver. After listing, you wait for a buyer and then receive the silver. If pre-orders exist at your price, such as 54,445 orders for Bottle of River Water at 6,250 silver, the item sells immediately and you are paid just as fast.

## Central Market: taxes

The Central Market takes a 30% tax commission per sale and each territory takes 5%, a total of 35% removed, so you collect 65% of the selling price, sent automatically to your warehouse. With a Value Pack you collect an additional 30%, bringing your share to 84.5%. The 30% commission does not apply to items bought directly from the Pearl Shop and then sold. Pearl items from events or random boxes still pay the 30% silver tax.

## Central Market: +5% silver collection items

Two rare items give +5% silver collection. The Old Moon Trade Pass is single-use and gives a one-time 5% bonus. Get it from special events, by exchanging 150 Radiant Shakatu Seals, or from a Shimmering Piece of the Old Moon in Dark Rifts. The Rich Merchant's Ring is permanent, extremely rare, and needs five ring pieces to complete. The Dark Rift and Treasures wiki pages explain how to get them.

## Central Market: selling expensive items

Listing very expensive items, those whose set price reaches 20 billion silver or above, has special rules:

- Notification: when one is queued for listing, all Adventurers see a notice at the upper middle of the screen.
- Random order matching: purchase orders at the same price (not necessarily the maximum price) are filled in random order.
- Failed listing: the item returns to your warehouse, and you wait about 1 minute before relisting, followed by the standard 15 minute queuing period.
- Only one item with the same name and enhancement level can be listed at a time.
- Pre-orders at a higher price cannot be placed during the 15 minute wait before sale registration.
- Certain PEN (V) Enhanced Gear follows these rules even if its set price is below 20 billion silver.

## Central Market: buying and pre-orders

Purchases spend silver from your warehouse. Select the item, choose a price, and press Buy. If the item is in stock, you get it immediately. If not, a pre-order is placed and the Central Market takes your silver right away. When someone lists the item, the highest pre-order price gets it first, and ties at the highest price are decided randomly. If top prices change for items you pre-ordered, you can re-list at new prices. Canceling a pre-order returns the silver.

## Central Market: pearl item pre-orders

You can pre-order Pearl Shop items, but only one item at a time. Check the item information on the Central Market to see which pearl items sell more often. Not all Pearl items can be sold on the Central Market.

## Central Market: item prices

Every Central Market item has a fluctuating base price. Buying at the top of the price range raises the base price, and selling at the minimum lowers it. Volatile Price items change often and show on the first screen. A blue background means a recent price drop, and a red background means the item now costs more.
