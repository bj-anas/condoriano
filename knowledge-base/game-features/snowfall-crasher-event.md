# Snowfall Crasher Event

A limited-time snowboarding event with three courses, each with its own leaderboard.

## Snowfall Crasher Event: how to join

The Snowfall Crasher Event has three events: Speed, Jump and Score. Open Menu (ESC), search for Snowfall Crasher Event, and press Enter to enter any course. The courses run in the Snowfall Crasher: Alpine Glide zone. You need a Classic Snowboard (7 Days) or a back outfit board to enter. If you are not in that zone, Navigation guides you there. Press ESC during a course to retry or quit. When you leave, you are teleported back to where you were before.

## Snowfall Crasher Event: rankings

Each course shows the top 10 at all times, and you can look up your rank up to 200th place. Rankings refresh daily at 00:15 (UTC). On Speed and Jump courses only, you can press Download and then Challenge to race the top 10 Adventurers. A replay button lets you watch how top 10 players ran those courses. Entering through Challenge adds a ghost of the top-ranked Adventurer to race against.

## Snowfall Crasher Event: Speed course

In the Speed course you pass every checkpoint and the fastest time ranks highest. Cutting through tight checkpoints saves time. Each missed checkpoint adds a penalty of +5 sec to your finish time. Ties share a rank, but whoever reached the time first is placed higher.

## Snowfall Crasher Event: Jump course

In the Jump course you hit every checkpoint and use the jump pads as shortcuts. Each missed checkpoint adds a penalty of +15 sec to your finish time. The fastest time ranks highest. Ties share a rank, but whoever reached the time first is placed higher.

## Snowfall Crasher Event: Score course

The Score course gives you 60 seconds to collect as many points as you can. A Blue Box is worth 1 point and gives [Event] Snowfall Crasher Seal x1. A Yellow Box is worth 5 points and gives [Event] Snowfall Crasher Seal x5. When time runs out, all boxes vanish and the run ends. Even if you retire, you still get Snowfall Seals for every box you grabbed, visible in the Manage Currency UI. The highest score ranks first. A points tie goes to the quicker finisher, and a tie on both shares the rank. Exceeding the time limit counts as retiring, with no rank.
