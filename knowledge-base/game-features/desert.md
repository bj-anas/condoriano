# Desert

The Great Desert of Valencia: its dangers, illnesses and sandstorms, ancient teleporters, jail, outlaw town and desert bosses.

## Desert overview

The Great Desert of Valencia, also called the Black Desert, covers most of Valencia's territory. The scorching day and bitter cold night make illness easy. In the Desert, mounts such as horses and wagons move slower, and finding your way with the World Map (hotkey: M) and minimap gets harder. Sandstorms can arrive suddenly and endanger unprepared Adventurers. Camels and elephants adapted to the terrain help make its riches more accessible.

## Desert compass

Before your first Desert trip, get a compass. Buy Part for Explorer's Compass x3 on the Central Market and merge the three parts into a temporary compass lasting 3 days. With a compass you can see the minimap and use automatic navigation in the Desert.

## Desert illnesses: heatstroke and hypothermia

In the Desert, daytime heat can give you the heatstroke debuff, which slowly drains health until cured, and nights can cause hypothermia. Potions and other healing methods restore health until the debuff fades, but a dedicated cure makes travel smoother. Star Anise Tea cures hypothermia, and you make it by processing (hotkey: L) star anise from star anise trees with purified or distilled water through simple cooking. Purified Water cures heatstroke, made by collecting river or muddy water in an empty bottle and using the filtering option (hotkey: L). Carry both when crossing the Desert.

## Desert illnesses: Ibellab Oasis

If you have heatstroke or hypothermia in the Desert and no cure, go to Ibellab Oasis. The bonfire there cures hypothermia, and getting into the water cures heatstroke.

## Desert sandstorms

Sandstorms deal great damage to Adventurers caught in them. If one approaches, run away. If caught, set up a tent and hide until the sandstorm ends or moves away. Desert tent tools are sold by Stable Keeper NPCs in Mediah and Valencia, can be set up quickly by right-clicking them, and only work inside the Desert.

## Desert Ancient Teleporters

Ancient devices, columns around an unstable portal, are scattered across the Desert. Crossing one teleports you to Aakman Temple or Hystria Ruins, which are Valencia's two deadliest underground, dungeon-style monster zones. The portal type on the left leads to Aakman Temple, and the type on the right leads to Hystria Ruins. Both zones also have normal entrances.

## Desert jail: villain mark and Pila Ku Jail

In the Desert, killing an Adventurer by forcing PvP marks you as a villain for 30 minutes. The mark is a large sword next to your name, visible even in a camouflage set. It disappears after 30 minutes or when you go to jail. A villain gets the same penalties as negative Karma players. If you die in the Desert with negative Karma or the villain mark, you are sent to Pila Ku Jail.

## Desert jail: debuffs and restrictions

Everyone sent to the Desert jail gets 2 debuffs lasting 30, 50 or 60 minutes depending on your Karma. In jail you cannot fight, your movement speed drops by 50%, you cannot use the menu escape function, and you cannot call your mount. Dying in jail has no penalty. You cannot resurrect outside the jail, and resurrecting inside costs nothing.

## Desert jail: how to escape

There are 3 ways out of the Pila Ku Jail:

- Serve your time until the debuffs end.
- Sneak out without being seen. Any warden who spots you attacks instantly and can hurt an unarmed prisoner badly. The jail has three spawn locations, and if you die you respawn at one of them at random.
- Finish a quest from the NPC next to the jail entrance, which removes the debuff and frees you early. You cannot sneak out while that quest is active.

After escaping you are moved to Ibellab Oasis or Muiquun, depending on your Karma.

## Desert town Muiquun

Muiquun is the only safe zone in the Desert. Adventurers with negative Karma can respawn there and use all its amenities, and the guards leave them alone. For players with positive Karma it is hostile: NPCs refuse to talk, guards attack, and local amenities are unusable.

## Desert bosses: Kreator and Laytenn

Two huge figures roam the Great Desert of Valencia: Kreator by day and Laytenn by night. Kreator is an Aakman tribe member who rejected the group's traditional views and uses ancient wisdom and black energy to seek revenge on the Kingdom of Valencia. When its HP is low, it summons the ancient weapons Alten and Kalten. Laytenn is an ancient weapon that wanders the Desert and appears only at night. It is much stronger than Kreator, with lightning-imbued axe attacks and movement debuffs on hit. Defeating Laytenn can drop Laytenn's Power Stone, a popular yellow-grade accessory with the same stats as the Ogre Ring.
