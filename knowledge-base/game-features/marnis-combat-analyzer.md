# Marni's Combat Analyzer

Marni's Combat Analyzer is a tool that tracks the damage you deal and take, DPS, combat duration and detailed per-hit logs.

## Marni's Combat Analyzer: opening it and the main window

Open it from Menu (ESC) - Character (F2) - Marni's Combat Analyzer. The main window displays how long combat lasted, DPS (Damage per Second), and the damage you dealt (DMG Dealt) and took (DMG Taken). The longest recording is 1 hour and 10 minutes, after which you must reset before recording again. The Sticker UI button pops the window out of the game screen, and the Open/Close Widget button toggles the widget. If Hide UI when Struck is on, the analyzer UI disappears when you are hit, but the widget stays on screen.

## Marni's Combat Analyzer: recording controls

Press Start to begin recording and press it again to pause. Start and Reset can be assigned to Quick Hotkeys. Pressing Reset deletes all recorded data.

## Marni's Combat Analyzer: Individual Combat Analysis tabs and filters

Individual Combat Analysis holds detailed logs, with tabs for DMG Dealt, DMG Taken and CC records. You can filter by skill, monster or character name. Targets sharing a name get a number added. To see one target, press the All Characters button, pick a target, and enter its number in the input field. The All Skills button shows logs by skill. The record list shows Time, Detailed Information (whether damage was dealt or taken, plus the skill name) and Damage (amount, CC type and whether it was a Critical Hit).

## Marni's Combat Analyzer: what each stat means

Damage Dealt is your total damage, combined across all targets. Damage Taken is your total damage received, combined across all attackers. DPS is DMG Dealt divided by Combat Duration. Combat Duration is how long the analyzer has been recording.

## Marni's Combat Analyzer: how hits are logged

Every damaging hit is logged with its target and the attacker's skill. Attacks without skill damage show as Normal Attack, or as Charge, Pull, Push, Spin or Taunt when they have a specific effect. Fixed damage from skills and damage over time such as Poison, Burn and Bleeding go under the skill name. Fixed damage from consumables goes under the consumable's name. Summon attacks appear as the summon name followed by its skill name. Special attacks shown are Back Attack, Down Attack, Air Attack, Counter Attack and Speed Attack, along with Critical Hit and Miss. Final Damage includes Critical Hits and Misses, and CC effects are logged separately.

## Marni's Combat Analyzer: where it works

Available in: Field Monster Zones, Battle Arena, Arsha Server (including Anonymous), Red Battlefield, Pit of the Undying, Elvia Realm, Marni's Realm and Golden Pig Cave. Unavailable in: Arena of Solare, Node War and Conquest War battlefields, Atoraxxion Co-op zones, Black Shrine (Donghae and Hwanghae), Altar of Blood, cutscenes, the Old Moon Grand Prix racetrack, War of the Roses, Guild League, Edania's Throne, Global Friendly Match, Arena of Arsha and Abyss One: The Magnus. The analyzer is blocked whenever you control a mount, whether a horse, Heuklang or wagon.
