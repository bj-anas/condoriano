# Marniwave

Marniwave is a feature for listening to Black Desert's background music at any time and place.

## Marniwave: how to unlock it

Marniwave unlocks after you finish the whole Marniwave questline from Wacky Toshi with a character at Lv. 45 or above. Wacky Toshi appears in various monster zones and in these places: Eilton, Duvencrune, Old Wisdom Tree, Valencia City, Shakatu and Glish. The Find NPC tool helps locate him. If the questline does not show up, turn on [Other] under Quest Type Preferences to receive it. The quest uses Energy, so restore your Energy or bring Energy Potions first. After completing the quest, Marniwave becomes available.

## Marniwave: opening and options

Open Marniwave from Menu (ESC) - Adventure (F5). Choose a channel to hear music of different genres and themes. Option settings let you turn on simplified mode and set music to play automatically while you are mounted.
