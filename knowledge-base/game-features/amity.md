# Amity

How the Amity system works: the friendliness value you build with NPCs, what it unlocks, and the ways to raise it through conversation and greetings.

## Amity overview

Amity is a number that shows how friendly an NPC is toward you, and it is shared across your whole family. You raise it by talking with an NPC about subjects they care about, by greeting them, or by finishing certain quests. It goes down if an NPC catches you stealing or you do something they dislike. To see an NPC's Amity, interact with them (hotkey: R). Their name, your Amity and the rewards you can unlock appear in the top left corner of the window. For example, one Velia fish vendor unlocks a knowledge entry at 150 Amity and quests at 200 and 400. Your Amity also shows during a conversation, and when you stand near an NPC a large circle displays your Amity and their Amity functions.

## Amity rewards and territorial fame

Raising Amity with an NPC can earn rewards. Some rewards spend your energy, for instance when you learn a knowledge entry or get a short buff. Others are simpler, such as the NPC trusting you enough to give you a new quest. Some knowledge entries can only be gained through Amity with a specific NPC, and many merchants sell valuable items only to customers they trust. Gaining Amity also raises territorial fame. Territorial fame is the sum of all the Amity you have earned from NPCs living in one territory. A higher territorial fame makes it easier to gather information for knowledge entries that can only be earned in that territory.

## Amity conversation

Conversation is the easiest and most efficient way to raise Amity. Press the Conversation button on the NPC's interface to begin. You must meet a requirement by the end of the round or you get no Amity. You also need enough energy and enough of the topics the NPC is interested in. The more knowledge entries you collect, the more topics you can offer. The NPC's list of favored topics is on the left of their interface. Starting a conversation costs energy, and the cost depends on your current Amity with that NPC. On the conversation screen, the NPC's constellation appears. Right click knowledge entries in the bottom circle, or drag and drop them, to fill the constellation. Each entry has an interest level, which affects success rate, and a favor value, which affects the Amity gained.

## Amity consecutive conversations

After a successful Amity conversation you can choose to continue with consecutive conversations. These cost no energy, so you can earn more than by starting separate conversations. The risk is that a failed consecutive conversation also removes the Amity gained in the previous consecutive conversation. Items can boost the Amity you gain. Elixir of Amity raises Amity gained by 20% for 10 minutes. Wearing the Calpheon Noble Suit or Dress gives a permanent 15% Amity gain.

## Amity horoscope

Sharing the same horoscope constellation with an NPC makes Amity easier to raise. Your first encounter starts at Amity 10 instead of 1, and conversations give extra Amity. You can see an NPC's horoscope constellation in the background picture shown while talking to them. For instance, one NPC's constellation is the hammer.

## Amity greetings

Greeting an NPC is a slower way to raise Amity, but it never fails. It trades energy for Amity evenly: 3 Amity for every 3 energy spent. It also needs no prepared conversation topics.

## Amity and NPC shops

Some NPCs sell items only to Adventurers they deeply trust, so you must reach the required Amity before buying. Items that require Amity are hard to find elsewhere and usually cost less than on the Central Market. Buying such an item consumes Amity with that NPC. To buy more, you need to raise your Amity again through conversation and greetings. The NPC Shops wiki page covers this in more detail.
