# NPC Shops

How NPC merchants work in Black Desert: finding them, the shop window, the many vendor types and the Black Market.

## NPC Shops: overview and finding NPCs

Some NPCs finish their shift and go home at night, while others appear only at night to sell rare items. NPCs have an Amity system. Good relations can change their comments, unlock quests and knowledge entries, and let some merchants sell certain items only to high-Amity Adventurers. Tax rates do not apply to items bought from NPC shops. If you know an NPC, the Find NPCs tool, left of the minimap at the top right, shows their location, services and opening hours. Its search accepts an NPC's name, region or service. For example, Lorenzo Murray, Velia's stable keeper, sells some items and offers quests only after you reach certain Amity points, and he is available all day.

## NPC Shops: favorite locations

You can save frequently visited locations to your Favorites list. Open the map (hotkey: M), right-click a location, and click Set Favorite. You can then give it any name, and find it in the favorites tab of the search function.

## NPC Shops: purchase, sell and buyback tabs

A usual NPC shop window has three tabs. In Purchase, select an item and click Buy, or right-click it. Stackable items use Buy Multiple after you enter a quantity. In Sell, the Sell button sells one unit at a time, and Sell all selected items sells all units of a stackable item. You can also right-click items in the Sell tab or inventory. Merchants will not buy everything, such as bound items, trade items and valuable equipment. In Buyback, select an item and press Buyback, or right-click it, to get back something you sold. NPCs always take a 10% profit cut, so for example a Magical Haystack sold for 100,000 silver costs 110,000 silver to buy back.

## NPC Shops: general vendor types

- Storage keeper: access to the town storage, only in major cities.
- General Goods Vendor: mainly potions and alchemy tools.
- Arms Dealer: various weapons, and repair at some NPCs.
- Innkeeper & Food Vendor: seasonings and cooking ingredients.
- Material Vendor: items for life skills, such as gathering tools.
- Stable keeper: items for land mounts, plus check-in and management of horses, wagons and more.
- Luxury Vendor: clothes useful for life skills.
- Furniture vendor and Furniture Dealer: furniture for that region and for your residences.
- Jeweler: magic crystals for equipment through transfusion.
- Fish Vendor: fishing-related items.
- Bookseller: books for residences and knowledge entries.
- Old Moon Manager: items for many activities and content features.
- Instruments vendor: instruments and related items, some locked behind requirements, Amity or quests.

## NPC Shops: service NPCs

- Silver (Church) Buffs: buffs bought with silver.
- Black Spirit's training: takes you to the nearest training area, usually in major cities, where you can train AFK for combat and skill experience. Combat experience currently has a cap.
- Guild Manager: create a guild, buy guild items and manage guild house activities.
- Blacksmith & Armor Vendor: sells armor and usually offers repair and extraction. They repair current durability, restore the maximum durability lost to enhancement failure, and repair mount equipment such as horse gear. Extraction gets black stones and other items back from enhanced or altered equipment. The Extraction wiki page has more.
- Central Market: takes you to the nearest Central Market NPC.
- Transport: shows the nearest storage keeper so you can send stored materials between major cities for a small silver fee. Bartering and trading materials cannot be sent.
- Imperial crafting delivery: sell Imperial cooking and alchemy crates daily, with more profit at higher level or mastery.

## NPC Shops: Abyssal Well, Work Supervisor, Wharf Manager and Trade Manager

- Abyssal Well: fast travel between major cities via the Magnus. Each fast travel point needs certain quests to unlock. See the Abyss One: The Magnus wiki page.
- Work Supervisor: hire workers by spending energy, or buy and sell workers at the Worker Exchange with silver. You need enough lodging in the same town. See the Workers page.
- Wharf Manager: items for sea mounts, plus check-in and management of fishing boats and ships. See the Stable & Wharf page.
- Trade Manager: buys and sells trading life skill goods and trade items such as fish. See the Trading page.

## NPC Shops: Black Market and Patrigio

Patrigio is the Black Market NPC, a special merchant who sells only at night. His secret shop's prices are not constant and you cannot know which item he will offer, but he sometimes sells rare or useful items. Each time you check a new item, it costs energy, and the price may be higher than on the Central Market. The Hold function keeps an item for 15 minutes, so you can compare prices. If you reopen the black market within those 15 minutes, you can still see the held item without spending energy. Held items are shared by all your characters. Patrigio has a hidden location in every city and can be visited during the day, but offers wares only at night. Other vendors, such as herb, seed and fruit vendors, exist in specific towns.

## NPC Shops: Amity requirements

Some merchant items list an Amity requirement, and you cannot buy them until you reach it. Such items are hard to get and are usually cheaper than on the Central Market. Buying one consumes your Amity with that NPC, so you must raise it again through conversation and greetings to buy more. For example, an Adventurer with 55 Amity with Izaro can buy 1 [Donkey] Shabby Leather Barding for 50 Amity, but cannot buy Shabby Leather Barding, which needs 300 Amity. The Amity wiki page has more.
