# NPC Exchange

How to trade monster loot to designated NPCs for fixed items, which can be worth more than selling for silver.

## NPC Exchange: what it is

NPC Exchange lets you hand a set quantity of a monster-drop item to a designated NPC and receive a fixed item of appropriate value. Players often call such drops trash loot. Many are better sold on the Central Market, and others can only go to NPC shops for silver. Some, though, return more value when exchanged. You collect the required amount and bring it to the NPC. An item's tooltip says whether it can be exchanged and where.

## NPC Exchange example: Useful Coin Pouch

The Useful Coin Pouch tooltip says it can be exchanged at Tarif. 150 pouches give an Offensive Sub-weapon Box, and 600 give a Gold Bar 100G. Selling 600 pouches to an NPC shop yields 468,000 silver. Exchanging the same 600 at Tarif gives a Gold Bar 100G, valued at 1,000,000 silver, which is more than double.

## NPC Exchange: tracking items in your inventory

Right-click an exchangeable item in your inventory to see the NPC Location and Item Count options. NPC Location is a navigation function that shows the way to the NPC who does the exchange. Item Count places the item icon and your current number next to the minimap, so you can track it while hunting. Hold-click the icon to move it. Item Count also works on items that have no NPC exchange.

## NPC Exchange: checking an NPC's options

Every NPC that can exchange items has an Exchange option in their interface. You can open it to see which items to look out for, even before you own any. The exchange window shows how many of the item you hold, to the right of its name.

## NPC Exchange and Manage Currency

Some items are exchanged in a special UI called Manage Currency instead of at NPCs. If you cannot find an item you just obtained, it was probably stored there. Open Manage Currency with the icon at the upper right of your screen. The Manage Currency Interface wiki page has more detail.
