# The Great Expedition

The Great Expedition is the maritime content of Black Desert: ocean questlines, ship building, sea monsters, Oquilla's Eye, Crow's Nest and Papua Crinea.

## The Great Expedition: overview

The Great Expedition adds sea features to the Great Ocean. You can upgrade or build ships, hire crew, barter, and fight sea monsters. Its stories include Jarette Domongatt's request about Oquilla's Eye and the Vell Pirates, the guild fight against Khan, Eye of the Great Ocean, the fog-covered Crow's Nest where the First Crow operates, and the Lamute Gang's trip to Papua Crinea from the docks of Grándiha. Sailors gather at taverns and inns in Velia and Port Epheria. Basics of sailing and bartering are on the Sailing & Bartering wiki page.

## The Great Expedition: compass for the Great Ocean

Before sailing the Great Ocean for the first time, get a compass. Buy Part for Explorer's Compass x3 on the Central Market and merge the three parts into a compass that lasts 3 days. A compass lets you see the minimap and use automatic navigation in the Great Ocean.

## The Great Expedition: Balenos questline

After finishing the Calpheon main questline, Igor Bartali, chief of Velia, tells you about a rumored creature on the islands north of town. This starts [The Great Expedition] Explore the Balenos Sea Islands, which leads you to knowledge entries for the islands north of Balenos. Main questlines for leaving the mainland appear in the Suggested tab of the Quests window (hotkey: O).

## The Great Expedition: Calpheon questline

After the Calpheon main questline, the Black Spirit uses the quest Mysterious Request to tell you that Philaberto Falasi, Port Epheria's richest resident, is looking for you. He asks you to visit the nearby islands north of town and collect local stories. This is [The Great Expedition] Explore the Calpheon Sea Islands, giving knowledge entries for those islands. After the quest Bag Full of Stories you choose one of three quests, each opening a questline. You can do the other two afterward, so the choice is not permanent. All three together tell one story involving a sea creature, pirates and ghosts.

## The Great Expedition: Oquilla's Eye questline

A character above Lv. 50 can talk to the Black Spirit to get the quest Vigorous Velia. It begins [The Great Expedition] First Sailboat into Oquilla's Eye, which introduces many Great Expedition features and places. It explores Heidel's link to the mysterious island of Oquilla's Eye and the origins of Vell, Khan, the island's inhabitants and the vanished Vell Pirates. The Calpheon questline helps give context for it.

## The Great Expedition: Crow's Nest questline

Tranan Underfoe, Velia's blacksmith, tells you of a misty island that many sailors failed to find, the Crow's Nest. The Black Spirit gives the quest [Crow's Nest] A Strange Tale from the Great Ocean once you are above Lv. 56, starting [The Great Expedition] Crow's Nest, Misty Island of Mystery. You meet the First Crow, owner and founder of the Crow Merchants Guild, and see treasures in the Hall of Heroes. Finishing the questline gives access to the Pit of the Undying, a PvE 1 vs 1 fighting arena.

## The Great Expedition: Papua Crinea questlines

At Lv. 58, the Black Spirit offers [Papua Crinea] An Amazing Gift Just for You, which takes you to Grándiha and Papua Crinea, where papus and otters have fought a war for a thousand years. Completing it unlocks the Lamute Gang's Adventure Journal adventure log, which has 5 parts split into Grándiha, Papu and Otter chapters. Completing chapters in order rewards transformation scrolls for papus or otters. The first Papua Crinea questline, Finding Papua Crinea of the Thousand Year War, cannot advance until you finish book one of the journal, and the other two stay locked until then. Press the complete button after each chapter. On Papua Crinea you must transform into an otter or papu to talk, get knowledge and do quests. Progress rewards a 1 day transformation scroll.

## The Great Expedition: Thousand Year War choice

On Papua Crinea you pick a side. Fighting for the papus makes Empress Papuraora a friend and alienates the otters. Fighting for the otters brings you close to General Crioniak and puts you at odds with the papus. Should your amity with Empress Papuraora or General Crioniak drop under -2500, that side will not speak to you, even if you are transformed into one of them. For example, low amity with Empress Papuraora stops you talking to papus while transformed as a papu. Island natives do not speak your language, so transforming is necessary.

## The Great Expedition: Stoneback Crab Training quests

Benns Moanna (papu side) and Benns Morio (otter side), both at Bloodshed Beach, give you the Stoneback Crab Training questline named [For the Thousand Year War] as you progress. Each quest rewards 1 day transformation scrolls, with better quality and more scrolls at higher difficulty. For example, quest 3 gives 3 scrolls: Brave Pine Blader, Immortal Persimmon Knight and Holy Date Priest. You cannot hold the papu and otter versions at the same time, so finish or abandon one first. They give 5 papu or otter soldier titles. A normal character cannot attack a trainable stoneback crab, so you must transform.

## The Great Expedition: Mysterious Paintings

Adventurers speak of mysterious paintings found on drifting cargo ships and around the islands. While holding one, its first quest, Mysterious Painting: Truth Behind the Painting, becomes available. Right-click the painting in your inventory (hotkey: I) to find the NPC who starts that questline. Each story explains different parts of The Great Expedition in more detail.

## The Great Expedition: daily quests at Oquilla's Eye and Iliya Island

Daily quests on islands such as Oquilla's Eye and Iliya Island give Sailing EXP, crow coins and ship upgrade materials, mostly for caravels, galleasses and carracks. They involve bartering, gathering and sea monster hunting. Names include: [Daily] We are the Owners of this Island!, Precious Coral Piece, For the Young Otter Merchants, Lively Iliya Island (and I-III), Barter Goods Support I-II, Our Guild is not a Charity Group, Do You Have What it Takes?, Win-win Situation, and Old Moon Guild's Candidum, Nineshark and Black Rust Hunter dailies, plus their Young versions. Weekly versions exist for the Candidum, Nineshark and Black Rust Hunter quests. There is also [Barter] [Daily] Sailing to a Wider World.

## The Great Expedition: Dancing Marlin Tavern quests

The Dancing Marlin Tavern in Velia offers easier bartering and sea monster quests that reward sailing EXP: Delivering Goods for Baremi Island, Narvo Island and Tinberra Island, Wanted: Hungry Sea Creatures, Wanted: Cox Scouts in Disguise, and [Daily] How to Recover Sailors. These daily quests require a sailing life skill level of beginner 2.

## The Great Expedition: Ravinia's Ship Upgrade Log quests

Ravinia, found at Crow's Nest, offers Ravinia's Ship Upgrade Log I through VII, accepted in order over 7 days. Each completion gives Ravinia's Wiggly-Waggly Letter. Opening a letter randomly gives you a quest from Ravinia, with tasks such as bartering, meeting characters or hunting sea monsters. Rewards help upgrade a sailboat or frigate into a caravel or galleass, and also give lots of sailing EXP and crow coins.

## The Great Expedition: Ravinia's Crow Coin Shop

Ravinia is the Crow Coin Exchange NPC at the Crow's Nest, a fog-covered island north of Tinberra Island run by the Crow Merchants Guild. Her shop sells Khan's Concentrated Magic, carrack equipment, ship upgrade materials and ship equipment enhancement materials. For most items the crow coin price is far higher than collecting them yourself. Buy from her only when you need a few of one type, and save crow coins for items only she sells.

## The Great Expedition: Rebinia's Shop

Rebinia, Ravinia's twin sister, runs a crow coin shop on Lema Island. It sells useful common items and rare Manos accessories. The special limited boxes it offers can only be bought from Rebinia on Lema Island.

## The Great Expedition: small and large ship types

Ships are small or large. Small ships are rowboats, fishing boats and Epheria cogs (Epheria Cog: Kalis-Certified and Epheria Cog: Fallen Vell Pirates' Legacy). Large ships are Guild galleys, Bartali sailboats, Epheria sailboats, frigates, caravels, galleasses and Epheria carracks (Advance, Balance, Volante and Valor). The Ship Upgrading page covers large ships and the Guild Mounts page covers the guild galley. If your ship is stuck on terrain, move away and use Call Ship. Its button sits next to Call Mount at the top left and works within 0 to 80 units of the ship.

## The Great Expedition: Epheria Cog

The Epheria Cog has high default sailing speed but low durability, so sea monsters hurt it. It suits trips between nearby islands and is not recommended beyond the Ross Sea. It can use Breezy Sail by default. When its lifespan ends, its speed drops sharply and you cannot use it. Both recipes below need Shipyard Level 2.

## The Great Expedition: Epheria Galleon

The Epheria galleon ranks among the finest ships the game has to offer. You can get the item Epheria's Star in two ways: a low chance from a special barter, or as a conquest war reward for the winning guild in Calpheon, Mediah and Valencia. For the conquest reward, the guild master takes it from the guild storage and gives it to a member with the Present button at the bottom of the guild storage window. Holding Epheria's Star unlocks a special quest from Philaberto Falasi at Port Epheria. It rewards the Epheria galleon ship license and the Captain Epheria title, which lasts 7 days. The Sailing & Bartering page has more.

## The Great Expedition: ship equipment and Tidal Black Stones

Green grade old ship equipment can be forcibly enhanced from any enhancement level using Tidal Black Stones. Get stones by exchanging Lv. 1 barter trade goods with Ravinia or her sisters: Ravinia at Crow's Nest, Revinia on Iliya Island, Ruvinia in Epheria, and Rovinia in Velia. Green grade equipment for the Epheria caravel and galleass is bought from Philaberto Falasi in Port Epheria, and enhanced with Tidal Black Stones from bartering, quests or the crow coin shop.

## The Great Expedition: blue and carrack ship equipment

Unlike regular ship items, blue grade caravel and galleass equipment does not lose enhancement levels when enhancement fails. Craft it at the level 4 ship part workshop at Port Epheria 1-4, 2F. Green grade carrack equipment comes from the crow coin shop via Ravinia or Rebinia: Epheria Carrack: Toro Sail, Toro Figurehead, Toro Cannon and Toro Plating, enhanced with Tidal Black Stone. Blue grade carrack equipment is crafted at Chiro's ship part workshop in the house at Iliya Island 3, which makes the 16 blue grade Epheria carrack parts and also the Tidal Black Stone. Part types must match the carrack you own.

## The Great Expedition: Oquilla's Eye waterspout

Every 3 hours a waterspout rises from the center of Oquilla's Eye. A system message warns you while you are on the island, and standing at the center can kill you, with no death penalty. The waterspout causes three large explosions and then treasure chests fall from the sky. Open them with silver keys, obtained while fishing or bought on the Central Market. The chests vanish after 1 minute.

## The Great Expedition: Khan, Eye of the Great Ocean

Khan, Eye of the Great Ocean, is a fearsome guild boss said to be linked to the world boss Vell. It is rumored to appear at the center of Oquilla's Eye. Skilled alchemists reportedly use its concentrated magic to craft powerful alchemy stones for combat and other uses. The Guild Boss wiki page has more.

## The Great Expedition: sea monsters and the Sea of Silence

Waves from Oquilla's Eye pushed many sea monsters toward the coastal seas, and they do not start fights. Hungry hekarus and ocean stalkers live along island coasts. Young hekarus and young ocean stalkers roam south of Padix Island and across the wide north of Lema Island. Hekarus and ocean stalkers lurk in the Ross Sea and Margoria. Beyond Margoria you meet ninesharks, candidums and black rusts, which are stronger, so do not fight them without a caravel or galleass. Defeating sea monsters gives sailing and sailor EXP.

## The Great Expedition: Sea of Silence and Cox Pirates' Ghost

The Sea of Silence is the area near Orisha Island in the Al Halam Sea, named by people of Altinova. Currents and wind are dead there and hostile derelict ships appear. Phantom ships often appear, and defeating them gives rewards you can sell to merchants in towns. Cox Pirates' Shadow Ghost shows up in various coastal sea areas and may drop summon scroll pieces, though rarely. Five pieces combine into a summon scroll that calls the Cox Pirates' Ghost. Defeating it gives a choice of Cox Pirates' artifact: parley beginner, parley expert or combat.

## The Great Expedition: Papua Crinea transformation scroll rules

Scrolls come in 1 day and permanent versions. The questline gives permanent scrolls for Brave Pine Blader, Gallant Conch Blader, Immortal Persimmon Knight and Undefeated Crab Knight. If you lose one, Benns Moanna or Benns Morio replaces it for two Benns coins, but only if you already received it through the Adventure Log quests. 1 day scrolls are rewards from [For the Thousand Year War] quests. Once you finish the quests Moanna, Papu's Friend and Morio, Otter's Friend, Benns Moanna and Benns Morio sell the Panipani Villager and Queekity Thumpity Moon Villager scrolls for 100,000 silver each. Talking to NPCs, gaining knowledge and progressing quests on Papuraora Island and Crioniak Island all require you to be transformed into one of the 6 forms. Each form has different skills, shown at the bottom right. Going to the opposing island gets you attacked by guards and cancels the transformation, and you cannot talk to its NPCs.

## The Great Expedition: using Papua Crinea transformation scrolls

Right-click a scroll to see a spot marked with a pillar of light where you can transform, then use the scroll and press R. Scrolls only work in certain places where otters and papus cannot see you. While transformed, actions such as summoning the Black Spirit or fishing are limited, as when riding a horse. Press Shift + R to end the transformation. After completing [Papua Crinea] Morio, Otter's Friend you receive a 1 day Queekity Thumpity Moon Villager scroll. If it is used up or lost, Benns Morio sells it again as a permanent version.

## The Great Expedition: Papua Crinea rare creatures

Two rare, well-disguised creatures live on Papua Crinea: Lazy Rock Spirit and Lazy Cloudy Rain Mushroom. They do not attack first and are hard to find. Only a transformed papu or otter can attack them, not a normal character.

## The Great Expedition: Papua Crinea coins

There are 3 coin types. Lamute Gang's Benns Coin comes from Grándiha's daily quests, and 1 can be exchanged with Benns Lamute for 1 Rainbow Petal Coin or 1 Rainbow Fish Bone Coin. Rainbow Petal Coin and Rainbow Fish Bone Coin are the papu and otter currencies, earned from the Papuraora Island and Crioniak Island quests. Rainbow Petal Coins buy Papu's Beloved Flower from Chief Benitu of the Panipani Village, and the permanent Holy Date Priest and Dreamy Bubble Shaman scrolls from Benns Moanna. Rainbow Fish Bone Coins buy Otter's Beloved Dried Fish from Jabuo of the Ardal Fish Market, and the permanent Holy Sea Cucumber Priest and Ruinous Puffer Shaman scrolls from Benns Morio.

## The Great Expedition: sea monster habitats

Sea monsters live in zones across the Great Ocean, and the farther from the continent, the stronger they are. Each zone gives a particular reward type.

## The Great Expedition: where sea monsters are located

Hekarus, ocean stalkers, their young and the Sea of Silence are in the Ross Sea or closer to the continent's northern coast. Young and adult candidums, ninesharks and black rusts live in Margoria, the center of the Great Ocean. Do not confuse normal black rusts with the much deadlier [Predator of Vadabin] ones. The saltwater crocodile habitat is in the far north and Lekrashan is in the far west, with the Goldmont Pirates fleet between them.

## The Great Expedition: Hollow Maretta, the Siren of the Ocean

Hollow Maretta, the Siren of the Ocean, is a rarely met mythical creature in the Margoria Sea. When you enter her waters the background music changes to her song, heard over a large area. You also find her by following the blue sea spirits. Fighting her means avoiding summoned typhoons and Hekaru minions with explosive attacks. Her possible drops are: Siren Song Aura (guaranteed), Moss-Covered Map, Oquilla's Flower, Tidal Black Stone, Wooden Nail Soaked in Seawater, Finely Polished Pine Plywood, Glue With Traces of Deep Waves, Margoria Crystal, Zulatia Crystal, Serni Crystal, Sethra's Artifact in Life EXP, Life Skill Mastery, Sailing EXP and Sailing Mastery versions, and Resplendent Lightstone of Flora. Siren song auras are used to craft the Manos Sailing Log.
