# Maids & Butlers

Maids and butlers are helpers that move items between your character, town storages and the Central Market.

## Maids and butlers: viewing your list

Press the Employ Maid/Butler button at the upper left of the screen, below your character's health bar. It opens the Maid/Butler List. The list shows all your maids and butlers, how many are available, the buttons to use each type, and the command to use them as a group.

## Maids and butlers: types and limits

There are two types. Transaction maids and butlers move silver or one item between your inventory and your Central Market warehouse, up to 100VT of one item at a time. Storage maids and butlers move silver or items between your inventory and a town storage, and can also move non-character-bound items from other characters in your family to your current character, up to 100LT of one item at a time. Each maid or butler has its own cooldown of 20 minutes after use.

## Maids and butlers: which town storages they reach

A storage maid or butler uses the town storage where your character currently is. In Valencia, for example, you can use the Valencia City storage but not Sand Grain Bazaar's. After finishing the Magnus main questline and unlocking every territory, you can send items from your inventory to the storage of any town you have discovered, apart from the bandit haven of Muiquun. The 100LT limit still applies. Trade and bartering goods and certain treasure items cannot be stored or removed this way.

## Maids and butlers: character-to-character item transfer

Maids and butlers can move items between the characters in your family. Open the End Game window from the main menu (hotkey: Esc) - Settings - Disconnect. Press the View Character's Inventory icon at the bottom left of a character, left of the Change button. In the View Inventory window, right-click any non-character-bound item in that character's inventory or pearl inventory to move it to your current character. Normal maid and butler limits apply, including the 100LT weight limit. Trade goods, guild items, character-bound items and certain treasure items cannot be moved.

## Maids and butlers: Find My Item (Ctrl + F)

Find My Item searches for everything you own across your family. You can retrieve the found items to your current character using maids or butlers. Items in a town storage use storage maids and butlers, and items in the Central Market warehouse use transaction ones. The Find My Item window shows how many storage and transaction maids and butlers are available right now.

## Maids and butlers: how to obtain them

Black Spirit quests give you one maid and one butler. At level 51, completing Black Spirit's Special Gift (Butler) in the Black Spirit Main Quest tab gives a butler, either transaction or storage. At Lv. 52, completing Black Spirit's Special Gift (Maid) gives a maid, again your choice of transaction or storage. Right-click the maid or butler in your inventory (hotkey: I) to register and use it. Maids and butlers are shared across all characters in your family.
