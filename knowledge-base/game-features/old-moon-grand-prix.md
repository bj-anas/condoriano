# Old Moon Grand Prix

The Old Moon Grand Prix is a horse race where mounted Adventurers compete on several racing tracks.

## Old Moon Grand Prix: how to join and schedule

The Old Moon Grand Prix needs at least 5 Adventurers, called riders, to start, and up to 10 can register per race. Races run between 16:00 and 9:00 UTC for NA, and 8:00 and 1:00 UTC for EU. It does not open during node and conquest wars, and entry is blocked from 15 minutes before those wars start until they finish. Join by pressing the Race Info icon at the upper right of your screen, left of the minimap, or open the main menu with Esc, then go to Life, then Old Moon Grand Prix.

## Old Moon Grand Prix: queue timer

When the first rider joins the queue, a 3-minute timer starts, and 3 more minutes are added for each additional rider, up to 5 riders. If fewer than 5 riders have joined when the timer runs out, the race is canceled and everyone leaves the queue. If 5 or more have joined when the timer ends, or if 10 join, the queue closes and a 1-minute prep time begins before the race. Rankings are decided by the order riders pass all checkpoints and the finish line.

## Old Moon Grand Prix: race horse registration

To enter, you must have registered a race horse at a stable keeper, and you can retrieve it from Miles, the stable keeper at the racing track. You can register up to 5 horses in the stable, plus 5 extra stable slots reserved for racehorses. Normal horses cannot race. A race horse emblem for Giorgia Brisa, required for registering race horses, is sold by Gula, the Stable Keeper NPC at Stonetail Horse Ranch, or by Miles. The racing horse costs 1,000,000 silver.

## Old Moon Grand Prix: steering controls

W accelerates, and releasing it lowers your speed. A and D steer left and right. S is a quick stop, F is Sweeping Movement, Space jumps, and Q increases the speed limit. Drift is A or D + S, holding A or D + S gives a consecutive drift, and pressing W during a drift gives instant acceleration. Sweeping Movement uses some of your acceleration gauge, shown in the horse stamina UI, which fills as you drift. Using short drifts continuously greatly reduces your speed above speed level 4. If you take a wrong path, press the Escape icon at the upper left of the screen to return to the track, usable every 5 seconds.

## Old Moon Grand Prix: horse race gear

Enhancing race gear raises horse stats and helps you rank higher. First exchange the Old Moon Grand Prix Coin you earn for the Giorgio horse gear. Then enhance each piece to +10 with Black Stones.
