# Dating NPC

The Dating NPC system lets you build high Amity with certain NPCs, gift them favorite items and win their love for a limited time.

## Dating NPC: how it works

In the Dating NPC system you gift items to an NPC to raise your Amity with them, then confess your feelings. High Amity and gifts of the NPC's favorite items can earn you their love for a short period. Each NPC's favorite items are shown in the main interface. Each gift costs 5 Energy. You can gift any item, but favorites give more Amity. NPCs refuse items that are too expensive, with a Central Market price of 50 million Silver given as the example. The Conversation Amity minigame also raises Amity, but gifting is more efficient. At 1,000 Amity, the option Confess your feelings becomes available.

## Dating NPC: confessing and the NPC's answer

After you confess to a Dating NPC, you wait until the next weekly maintenance to learn the answer, which arrives in your in-game mail (B). If the NPC accepts, you become their lover and earn a special colored title, but your Amity with them drops by 10%. If rejected, you get nothing and Amity drops by 5%. The weekly lover is chosen by comparing your Amity with the total Amity of all Confessors for that NPC, so more competitors lower your odds. A current lover with the highest Amity among Confessors keeps the status automatically. Someone must surpass their Amity for the choice to return to random the next week.

## Dating NPC: lover titles and how long it lasts

A lover earns a temporary title named after the NPC, for example Crio's Lover for dating Crio. The relationship can last only a week, since other Adventurers can keep confessing and the NPC may choose someone else. Holding the highest Amity keeps the relationship. Lover titles are lost if you lose the relationship. Winning 5 NPCs in the same week earns the title Flirt, and winning 10 NPCs in the same week earns the title Heartbreaker.
