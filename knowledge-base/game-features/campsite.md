# Campsite

The campsite is a tent you can set up anywhere to repair gear, store items and buy potions and buffs without returning to town.

## Campsite: what it is for

A campsite can be installed in any suitable location and lets you repair equipment, use a shop and more. It saves hunting time, because you can repair, buy potions and get helpful buffs on the spot instead of walking to NPCs in town.

## Campsite: unlocking the Camping Tool

To unlock the campsite, finish the questline [ADV Support] Start of Camping. It is in the Suggested tab of the quest interface and is accepted from the Black Spirit. The requirement is level 50 or higher. A character on the Simplified Questline cannot accept it, but because it is a family quest, another character that did not choose the Simplified Questline can complete it. The quest Old Moon Guild's Survival Skill needs you to talk to Klau, and it gives 80 Contribution EXP, the Camping Tool that unlocks summoning your campsite, and the Old Moon Camping Tent, Old Moon Camping Anvil and Old Moon Camping Shop.

## Campsite: installing the tent and rules

After the questline, a tent icon appears under your level and health bar. Left-click it to place your tent where you stand, and right-click it to navigate to your tent. Campsites can only be summoned in Combat Zones free of large obstacles, roads and steep inclines. They cannot go in Safe Zones or in areas too small for the tent. No tent can be set up while a Node or Conquest War is on, and a campsite placed earlier is removed once the war starts. After installing, press R to open the Campsite interface. Each campsite component has a limited duration, and once it expires you need to get a new one.

## Campsite: Naphart Camping Tools from the Pearl Shop

The Pearl Shop (F3) sells a premium version, the Naphart Camping Tools. They do not expire, they let you buy Villa Buffs directly from your tent, and the tent's appearance can be dyed.

## Campsite: Shop function

The Shop function sells potions, camp buffs and church buffs, and lets you exchange Gold Bars. Gold Bars (1G, 10G, 100G) and the HP Potion (Extra Large) and MP Potion (Extra Large) were removed. Atanis Firefly and Small Atanis Firefly are shown at the top. [Scroll] items last 60 min. The offensive, defensive and EXP buffs were merged into [Blessing] Adventure's Boon, priced the same as one earlier buff, and the [Blessing] Valor, Protection and Growth buffs were removed, resetting buffs already on characters.

## Campsite: Villa Scroll and Body Enhancement changes

The Naphart Campsite (the Pearl Shop tent) has a Villa Scroll function for buying Villa Buffs from the tent. You also need an active Secret Book of Old Moon or Villa Invitation. The Skill & Experience campsite buff was removed, resetting its effects on characters, and its effects were added to Body Enhancement: All AP +10, All Damage Reduction +10, Max HP +200, All Resistance +10%, Combat EXP +50%, Skill EXP +50% and Heatstroke/Hypothermia Resistance +50%. The Combat EXP, Skill EXP and Heatstroke/Hypothermia effects are new, and apply only after you re-obtain the buff after the update. The Villa wiki page covers Villa Invitations.

## Campsite: Storage function

The Storage function in the Campsite menu requires an installed Campsite Container and holds up to 16 items or 200 LT. While items are stored in the container, you cannot place the tent on another character until you remove them. The exception is tagged characters, which can both use the tent and reach the stored items.

## Campsite: Repair function

The Repair function in the Campsite menu requires an installed Camp Anvil. For a fee, it repairs mount gear, worn gear and inventory items right where you stand.
