# Observe Mode & Photo Mode

Two camera features: Observe Mode for free-roaming views of the world, and Photo Mode for taking screenshots without UI.

## Observe Mode: what it is and how to start it

Observe Mode is a free camera for watching other players fight in the arena or admiring the scenery around your character. To turn it on, press R while your character is sitting or leaning against a wall. You can also click the Observe Mode icon in the character interaction pop-up in those situations. Locking the mouse to the screen (hotkey: Ctrl) lets you move more precisely while observing.

## Observe Mode: key controls

In Observe Mode, W, A, S and D move the camera. Q and E change the camera height. The minus key, the equals key, Shift and Caps Lock change the camera speed. Press R to end Observe Mode.

## Photo Mode: what it is and how to toggle it

Photo Mode lets you take screenshots with the UI hidden. Press Ctrl + U to activate it, and the same combination turns it off. It lets you change the blur, gamma, noise, contrast, zoom and angle of the camera view. You can also change the ambiance and the time and weather around you, from dawn to pitch dark night and from clear to cloudy skies. Rain and snow cannot be changed freely. All Photo Mode settings reset when you turn the mode off. You can load and save presets in its interface.

## Photo Mode: social actions and Slow Mode

Photo Mode includes social actions, so your character can perform them for pictures. It also adds Slow Mode (hotkey: U), which slows your character down. Photo Mode works while idle, while moving around the world outside combat zones, and while in Observe Mode.

## Photo Mode: key controls

In Photo Mode, F1 toggles the key guide shown on the right. Enter or Print Screen takes a screenshot. U toggles Slow Mode, the arrow keys move the camera, and Home makes the character face the screen. Other combinations:

- Ctrl + Shift + Left/Right: Noise and Vignetting, and Filters
- Ctrl + Left/Right: Blur Intensity
- Ctrl + Up/Down: Blur Position
- Ctrl + Alt + Left/Right: Gamma
- Ctrl + Alt + Up/Down: Contrast
- Shift + Up/Down: Zoom
- Shift + Left/Right: Angle
- Ctrl + Page Up/Page Down: Social Action
- Alt + Left/Right: Ambiance
- Alt + Up/Down: Time and Weather
