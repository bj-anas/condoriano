# Ship Upgrading

The ship upgrade path from the Bartali sailboat to the Epheria carracks, with materials, ship equipment and where to get them.

## Ship upgrading: overview and upgrade path

Large ships are needed to cross the Great Ocean, and the Epheria carracks are currently the best. The path runs from the Bartali sailboat to an Epheria sailboat or frigate, then to an Epheria caravel or galleass, then to one of four Epheria carracks. You can skip the Bartali step by buying an Epheria sailboat or frigate license on the Central Market. The choice between sailboat and frigate matters, because it decides which carracks you can reach. The Sailing & Bartering wiki page covers sailing basics.

## Ship upgrading: Bartali sailboat

The Bartali sailboat is the most basic large ship. Proix, owner of the Dancing Marlin Tavern in Velia, sells it for 10,000,000 silver. It is also a quest reward from the questline [The Great Expedition] First Sailboat into Oquilla's Eye, which starts with the quest Vigorous Velia for a character of Lv. 50 or above in Balenos. The quest that actually gives the ship is Jarette's Support. The questline is also worth doing for its sailor's oath rewards, which permanently expand your max sailor slots.

## Ship upgrading: Epheria sailboat or frigate choice

The Epheria sailboat and Epheria frigate are the second stage. A sailboat leads to the Balance carrack, with evenly spread stats, or the Advance carrack, the best for bartering because it carries more weight. A frigate leads to the Valor carrack, great for sea monster hunting and naval combat, or the Volante carrack, the fastest of all carracks. Pick a frigate for top naval combat or speed. Pick a sailboat for bartering or an all-round ship. Their licenses can be bought on the Central Market. The quest [The Great Expedition] Final Preparation for Departure rewards green gear for the Epheria sailboat: Brass Figurehead, Enhanced Plating, Julia Cannon and High Winds Sail.

## Ship upgrading: how to upgrade a ship

Register the ship at a wharf through the Wharf Manager NPC, open the Upgrade menu option, and add all required materials. You cannot upgrade if any equipment piece is below max durability, if the ship has equipment equipped or items in its inventory, or if a sailor is aboard.

You can also craft the Epheria sailboat and frigate licenses yourself in the town house at Port Epheria 3-5, 2nd floor, when it is employed as a shipyard. That way most materials needed are halved.

## Ship upgrading: improved Epheria sailboat and frigate

Both the Epheria sailboat and frigate can be improved at any wharf manager. An improved ship can use Volley, which lets you swing the cannons left or right and fire them, and Focus Fire, which fires several shots quickly at a chosen location. Some upgrade materials drop from sea monsters, and improving helps you gather materials for the next step. Improving does not change the materials needed to upgrade to a caravel or galleass.

## Ship upgrading: Epheria caravel and galleass overview

After you have an Epheria sailboat or frigate you can work toward a caravel or galleass. The Epheria caravel has 30 slots and carries 10,000LT, so it suits trading and bartering. The Epheria galleass has 4 cannons per side, 8 in total, so it suits naval combat. Both can be improved to use Volley while moving, or Focus Fire. Some materials come from sea monsters and others from bartering, so even a bartering ship needs some monster hunting and a combat ship needs some bartering.

## Ship upgrading: Tidal Black Stones

The four pieces of equipment for a caravel or galleass are bought from Philaberto Falasi in Port Epheria and enhanced with Tidal Black Stones. These stones give a 100% success chance at every level. Enhancing one piece to +10 needs 10 stones, and all four need 40. Ways to get them: exchange Lv. 1 bartering trade goods with Ravinia's sisters, Ruvinia in Port Epheria, Rovinia in Velia and Revinia on Iliya Island, for Crow Merchants Guild barter vouchers. Then bring the vouchers to Ravinia at the Crow's Nest to exchange for stones. Rumor says Morco in Velia also exchanges them. At a low rate, Young Hekaru, Young Ocean Stalker, Hekaru and Ocean Stalker also drop them.

## Ship upgrading: Crow's Nest and Ravinia

The Crow's Nest is a fog-covered island north of Tinberra Island, managed by the Crow Merchants Guild. NPC Ravinia there runs the crow coin shop that sells items for upgrading large ships, but she charges large sums of crow coins, so you cannot rely on it for everything. She also gives the Ravinia's Ship Upgrade Log questline, from Ravinia's Ship Upgrade Log I to VII. Accepting them in order takes 7 days, and each completed quest gives Ravinia's Wiggly Waggly Letter, which you use to accept the next. The quests provide materials for caravel and galleass upgrades.

## Ship upgrading: Epheria carrack types

You can upgrade to four Epheria carracks. From an Epheria Caravel you get Epheria Carrack - Advance or Epheria Carrack - Balance. From an Epheria Galleass you get Epheria Carrack - Volante or Epheria Carrack - Valor. Balance has well-balanced stats. Volante is agile with the fastest movement speed. Valor is combat-oriented with the highest attack power. Advance has the highest storage capacity and suits bartering.

## Ship upgrading: ship equipment overview

Epheria caravels, galleasses and carracks can mount ship equipment: cannons, figureheads, sails and plating. Ship equipment comes in green grade first, with blue grade as the upgrade. Make sure the equipment you buy and enhance fits your ship type. Blue grade pieces for caravels and galleasses are made at Port Epheria 1-4, 2F, in the Ship Part Workshop, and it must be raised to level 4.

## Ship upgrading: Epheria Carrack blue equipment enhancement

Enhancing carrack blue grade gear from +1 up to +10 uses Tidal Black Stones x50. The page lists these sources for obtaining stones: quest rewards (such as the Old Moon Guild Black Rust, Candidum and Nineshark Hunter dailies and weeklies, the Subjugation quests, Hungry Sea Creatures' Crystal quests, Mysterious Painting 3 quests and Ravinia's Wiggly-Waggly Letter), defeating Great Ocean sea creatures, bartering Level 2 or Level 3 sea trade goods, and defeating Vell.
