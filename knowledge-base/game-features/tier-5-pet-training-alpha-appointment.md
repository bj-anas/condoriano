# Tier 5 Pet Training/Alpha Appointment

How to upgrade a Tier 4 pet to Tier 5 and appoint a Tier 5 pet as the Alpha Pet.

## Tier 5 Pet Training: overview

Tier 4 pets can be upgraded to Tier 5 to unlock new capabilities. A Tier 5 pet can be appointed Alpha Pet, the Pack Leader. The Pack Leader raises the looting speed of all other checked-out pets of Tier 1 to 5. Tier 5 Pet Training needs no prerequisite quests. Visit Obi Bellen at Old Wisdom Tree and choose the option Tier 5 Pet Training. An upgrade is permanent: a Tier 5 pet cannot go back to Tier 4, and it cannot be used later to upgrade other pets, so choose carefully.

## Tier 5 Pet Training: Royal Plume recipe

Training consumes one Royal Plume. Craft it in Simple Alchemy (L) with these ingredients: Growth Reagent x10, Magical Shard x40 and Fine Lightweight Plume x800. With the plume in hand and your Tier 4 pet checked in, open the Tier 5 Pet Training menu to train the pet.

## Tier 5 Pet Training: where to get the materials

| Item | How to obtain |
| --- | --- |
| Growth Reagent | Buy from an Old Moon shop for 60 million Silver each. Find the nearest shop through Function (F8) - Find NPC by searching Old Moon Manager. |
| Magical Shard | Use Heating (L) on Sealed Black Magic Crystal or other yellow-grade Black Magic Crystals. Drop locations are in Adventure (F5) - Monster Zone Info. |
| Fine Lightweight Plume | Use Filtering (L) on items such as Lightweight Plume, Soft Ferri Feather and Soft Kuku Bird Plume. Lightweight Plume and Soft Ferri Feather come from skinning Ferris and Ferricas in Navarn Steppe. |

## Tier 5 Pet Training: Exchange and appearance limits

After training, the pet can no longer use the Exchange function, so change its appearance or skills through Exchange before training. Tier 5 pets gain no special appearance or skill from training. Pet Appearance Change Coupons and Pet Skill Change Coupons still work. For example, a Tier 5 Arctic Fox cannot be exchanged to look like a Tier 5 Polar Bear, but a Tier 4 Arctic Fox can be exchanged to look like a Tier 1 Arctic Fox.

## Alpha Pet appointment

To appoint an Alpha, check in a Tier 5 pet in the Pet window and press the crown icon. You can have just one Alpha at any moment, and choosing another pet the same way replaces it. Only one Alpha Pet can be checked out at once. Tier 5 pets that are not the Alpha have the same stats as a Tier 4 pet.

## Alpha Pet effects

While the Alpha Pet is checked out, all other active pets nearby get a 15% reduction in loot cooldown. The Alpha Pet's Talent also gains one extra level. For example, Combat EXP +4% becomes +5%, and HP +100 becomes +125. Special skills, such as reduced fishing cooldown or increased gathering amount, are not affected.
