# Treasures

Treasures are rare, powerful items crafted by collecting extremely low-drop materials and combining them in a set shape.

## Treasures: overview and crafting

Treasures are very hard to acquire, but their unique abilities attract Adventurers who want a challenge. You craft a treasure by gathering its materials in your inventory and arranging them in the correct shape in Yaz's Combinables Pouch. Some materials drop so rarely that gathering just one can take months. The treasures covered are Ornette's and Odore's Spirit Essences, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass, the Archaeologist's Map, Ebenruth's Nol, the Rich Merchant's Ring, Krogdalo's Sanctuary, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Telescope and Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp.

## Treasures: Ornette's and Odore's Spirit Essences overview

Ornette's Spirit Essence recovers HP and Odore's Spirit Essence recovers MP/WP/SP, like ordinary potions, but they never disappear however often you use them. They work well with Fairy Laila's ability Miraculous Cheer, which automatically consumes potions. They can be crafted using Atanis' Element, which is fairly easy to get by exchange in Manage Currency. Open Manage Currency through the icon at the top right of the screen, or Menu - Reward (F3) - Manage Currency.

## Treasures: Ornette's Spirit Essence materials

Ornette's Spirit Essence needs five materials. Place them in Yaz's Combinables Pouch (Inventory) in a plus sign shape and press the + symbol to combine.

## Treasures: Blessed Spirit Essence

Blessed Spirit Essence is a lesser version of Ornette's Spirit Essence that you can craft with just one of its materials. It heals less HP than the full treasure and is a fair stand-in until you collect everything. If you create another Blessed Spirit Essence by mistake after getting a second material, use Simple Alchemy on it to recover all the materials used.

## Treasures: Atanis' Element and pity pieces

An item called Atanis' Element can be exchanged at Merindora in Grána for pity pieces. Once you have 100 pity pieces of one type, you can craft the full material item. For example, you get x1 Ash Halfmoon Kagtunak by combining x100 Blood Wolf's Oath with x1 Crimson Breath of Resentment (also provided by Merindora) through Simple Alchemy.

## Treasures: Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass

Compasses are needed to find your exact location in the Great Desert of Valencia and the Great Ocean. Ordinary compasses expire, but Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass stays in your inventory permanently. It can also summon other Adventurers to your position.

## Treasures: Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass materials

The compass needs 6 materials: 3 different unique Upgraded Compass Parts and 3 gems (Blood Ruby, Ocean Sapphire and Gold Topaz). The parts look identical when first obtained, and their true shape is revealed when NPC Ain Greid identifies them at the Ancient Fissure node in Mediah. Which part you get depends on the monster that dropped it. They drop from monsters in the Aakman Temple and Hystria Ruins monster zones. The gems come from processing or the Central Market. Arrange everything in Yaz's Combinables Pouch in the required shape. You can combine without appraising the parts if each correct part is in its proper slot.

## Treasures: Archaeologist's Map

The Traveler's Map teleports you to the nearest town but does not bring you back. The Archaeologist's Map teleports you to the nearest town and also returns you to where you used it. It needs 4 Archaeologist's Map Pieces and 4 gems: Blood Ruby, Ocean Sapphire, Gold Topaz and Forest Emerald. The pieces look the same at first. Using the Caphras Journal in Caphras Cave, Calpheon, reveals what each one really is. Four different unique pieces are required. They drop from monsters in the Roud Sulfur Mine and Pila Ku Jail monster zones. Gems come from processing or the Central Market. As with the compass, you can combine without appraising the pieces if each is in its proper slot.

## Treasures: Ebenruth's Nol

Ebenruth's Nol is equipped on an Epheria Carrack or a Panokseon and helps cross the ocean between the known lands and the Land of the Morning Light. It lets you use the sea mount skill Breezy Sail once more without spending stamina, and raises its movement distance by 50%.

## Treasures: Ebenruth's Nol materials

Ebenruth's Nol needs Symbol-Engraved Nol and Ebenruth. To make Ebenruth, run Simple Alchemy with x1,000 Oquilla's Flower plus x1 Oquilla's Tear. The flower comes from barter, a Crow's Nest vendor, or the Treasure Chest of a Sailor, and the tear is only sold by a Crow's Nest vendor. For Symbol-Engraved Nol, defeat saltwater crocodiles to get Moss-Covered Map. Use Simple Alchemy with NPC Ravinia's Moss Remover to get one of nine Torn Treasure Map Piece items. Combine all nine into a Treasure Map of a Sailor. Rumors say Simple Alchemy on a Torn Treasure Map Piece has an extremely low chance of giving it too. Then do a quest to receive the Treasure Chest of a Sailor, which gives Symbol-Engraved Nol at a certain chance.

## Treasures: Rich Merchant's Ring

The Rich Merchant's Ring adds +5% Central Market silver collection per sale. Unlike the single-use Old Moon Trade Pass, the ring is permanent. Rumors say Adventurers are searching for it using hints from an image on the wiki page.

## Treasures: Krogdalo's Sanctuary

Krogdalo's Sanctuary is a personal stable that holds as many as 5 horses of any type, Krogdalo included. You need to have finished the Mythical Awakening of all three Dream Horses: Mythical Arduanatt, Mythical Dine and Mythical Doom. To get it, visit Merindora in Grána while mounted on one of the three mythical horses and accept the [Krogdalo's Sanctuary] questline. You need all three mythical horses to complete it. The Mythical Horses wiki page has more.

## Treasures: moving Krogdalo's Sanctuary with a maid or butler

A maid or butler can move Krogdalo's Sanctuary from another character to your current one. The call distance depends on the flute item your current character owns: Celestial Horse Calling Horn gives unlimited distance, Trainer's Flute gives 500, and the default is 150. For example, if the horse is out 600 units from a Ranger who owns the Sanctuary, a Warrior bringing it over must be able to call the horse, so the Warrior needs a Celestial Horse Calling Horn.

## Treasures: Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Telescope

Lafi Bedmountain made this invention after the region Ulukita opened, using parts found only there. It is the opposite of the Upgraded Compass: instead of bringing others to you, it moves you to their position. It needs 5 materials: 3 unique Upgraded Telescope Parts and 2 gemstones, Star Diamond and Gold Topaz. The parts are rarely found across the lawless lands of Ulukita. Alternatively, NPC Ulubala exchanges 3 Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass Parts for a telescope part, and only compass parts appraised by Ain Greid are accepted. Arrange the items in Yaz's Combinables Pouch in the required shape. You can combine without appraising parts if each is in its proper slot.

## Treasures: Remitaronsom's Lumaloft Lamp

Remitaronsom is the chieftain of the Tooth Fairy Forest, and the lamp is his legendary artifact made from the forest's light. When placed on the Peridot Forest Path Wagon, it strengthens and refreshes the wagon's energy. It floats above the wagon, glowing and lighting the way. The pieces come from delivering horses to the Imperial Delivery, with very low chances. Faelight Fragment comes from Imperial Delivery of Tier 6-8 horses or Dream horses, and Lumentrace from Imperial Delivery of Imperial Steeds. Once you hold all the pieces, arrange them in the required shape in Yaz's Pouch.
