# Agris Fever

Agris Fever is a point-based feature that raises junk item drops from monsters and gathering, with a per-monster and per-gather point cost.

## Agris Fever: what it is

Agris was an ancient Balenosian god of prosperity and abundance who left a power for later generations. You inherit it as Agris Fever, which increases how many junk items drop when you defeat monsters and when you gather. It is meant to help Adventurers with little playtime get the most profit from their effort.

## Agris Fever: activation and starting points

Agris Fever activates automatically the first time you reach Lv. 56, and every character in your family can use it. You start with a 50,000 point cap and recover up to 15,000 points each day at 6 AM. After activation, talk to the Black Spirit to begin the [Adventure Log] Old Moon Logs quest. Completing chapters of the Book of Margahan Adventure Log, found in the Adventure Log bookshelf window from the main menu (hotkey: Esc), raises your point cap and daily recovery. The two volumes can be completed at Lv. 58 and Lv. 60. At most you reach a 100,000 point cap and 20,000 points recovered per day.

## Agris Fever: how to use it

Right-click the Agris Fever icon to toggle it on or off. When it is active the icon moves. With it on, defeating monsters and gathering raise your junk (gray) loot drop rate by an extra 100%, or 150% after you finish the first 5 chapters of the books. Every monster kill or gathered object consumes points, and once all points are used the drop rate goes back to normal. Agris Fever stacks with item drop rate buffs, and many Adventurers have researched the best combinations.

## Agris Fever: gathering rules

Agris Fever only affects basic resources such as trees and ores, in the same way it only applies to common gray items from monsters. It does not apply to rare resources such as Black Crystal Shards or special ones such as Truffle Mushrooms and Dead Tree Essence. The hedgehog's special skill Gathering Item Quantity Increase is not boosted by Agris Fever. Scooping water, shoveling and milking cows are not affected either.

## Agris Fever: resources affected when gathering

Agris Fever applies to many basic gatherable resources. Groups include ores and stones, trees, herbs, flowers, crops and mushrooms. Examples of affected items are Iron Ore, Lead Ore, Copper Ore, Tin Ore, Zinc Ore, Coal, Vanadium Ore, Titanium Ore, Sulfur, Maple Timber, Pine Timber, Birch Timber, Maple Sap, Pine Sap, Wheat, Barley, Potato, Corn, Cotton, Wool, Flax, Deer Hide, Wolf Hide, Wolf Blood, Deer Meat, Lion Meat and Snake Meat. The wiki page lists many more, and its full list of gatherable resources covers ores, stones, trees, herbs and mushrooms.

## Agris Fever points per monster: changes

Agris Fever points are spent each time you defeat a monster, and the amount depends on the monster and its zone. The per-zone tables are grouped by region, from Calpheon and Mediah through Elvia Realm, Dehkia's Lantern and Ulukita, to the sea monster zones and Edania. The values can change in future updates, so the numbers may differ from the live game.
