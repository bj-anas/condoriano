# Ranking

The in-game Ranking window, where Adventurers compare standings in several fields.

## Ranking fields and positions

The Ranking window compares Adventurers across the various Life Skill levels, overall wealth, character growth, and Red Battlefield score. For each field it lists family name, character name and guild for places 1 to 30. Whoever sits in the top 5 of a field gets a special icon over their character name while they keep that position. If you are outside the top 30, you are placed in a category from A to E. Ranking only counts Adventurers on your own server.

## Ranking: where to check it

You can see your own position and every field's ranking through the in-game main menu. Another route is the Rankings button in the lower right of the Basic tab in the Character Profile (hotkey: P).

## Ranking symbols above character names

While traveling you may see Adventurers with symbols above their names. Each symbol matches a field in the Ranking window, and its color shows their rank. Only top positions in a field show the symbol. One Adventurer can show several medals at once. For example, one with four medals ranks in Alchemy, Trading, Farming and Barter.
