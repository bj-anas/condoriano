# Character Tag

The Character Tag system lets you link a second character to your main one, switch between them, copy gear, and share EXP.

## Character Tag: overview

Tagging a character lets you play a second class using the gear of your main character. While a character is tagged, you can swap between the two at any time and place, and the EXP you earn is shared between them.

## Character Tag: how to tag a second character

Click the tag icon next to your mount and fairy icons. Then pick the character to tag. Both characters must be in the same town. Characters that meet the conditions appear in color and the others are grayed out. Some villages, such as Kusha and Abun, are excluded. Tagging or untagging also requires both characters to be in the same town. After tagging you can swap between the two characters wherever you are.

## Character Tag: switching limits

Switching between tagged characters puts the Emergency Escape skill on a 5 minute cooldown. Switching itself has no cooldown. Switching is blocked at sea and in Node/Conquest War, Old Moon Prix, Battle Arena, Arena of Arsha and Red Battlefield.

## Character Tag: Item Copy requirements

Item Copy shares your equipped gear with the tagged character. Both characters must be level 56 and the quest Black Spirit's Gift (Marni's Suspicious Device) must be completed. It appears in the Suggested quest tab and only needs a talk with the Black Spirit. Then press the Item Copy button. Before copying, your Karma must not be below 0 and the original gear's durability must be maxed. Copied items' main stats may change with your class. Later changes to the original, such as enhancement levels or Caphras, carry over to the copy.

## Character Tag: gear that cannot be copied

Item Copy cannot copy: costumes and outfits, Tuvala and Naru gear before graduation, gear rented with Contribution Points, Jetina gear not enhanced to PEN, Spirit Stones, fishing rods, floats and hunting matchlocks, and gear from questlines. Equipment shown in red in the window is restricted. Do not add Lightstones to copied artifacts, because untagging loses Lightstones added to copied artifacts.

## Character Tag: Item Copy cost

Item Copy is paid with Marni's Unstable Fuel. Every Old Moon Manager, in any town, sells it at 10,000,000 silver apiece. The cost formula is: Central Market price of all 3 weapons x 0.15%, plus the number of Caphras Stones used for Caphras Enhancement on all 3 weapons x 2,000 Silver. The Single Copy tab copies additional single pieces using Marni's Processed Fuel, which comes from the Pearl Shop or special events.

## Character Tag: effect dye and copied gear

Sovereign Weapons and Edana's Defense Gear can have their effects dyed with Primordial Pigment. Item Copy after a tag copies the effect dye information, but later dye changes to the original are not reflected. Effects on copied gear cannot be dyed. For example, if character A's items are copied to character B, B's copied equipment cannot be effect-dyed.

## Character Tag: EXP sharing

EXP is shared by transferring it from one character to the other. Log in with one character and earn EXP from monsters or other activities. Then log in with the tagged character and press the Get EXP button. All Combat EXP and Skill EXP transfers, including EXP from buffs and items. A level 49 character that goes past 100% EXP because of the stored EXP cannot be untagged. To untag without leveling past 49, contact Support through Hot Issues - Lvl49 Untag Support, which removes the EXP. Earn no more EXP and untag right after the issue is resolved.

## Character Tag: travel together or alone

You can choose whether your tagged character follows you or travels independently. This decides where each character is located when you swap. Changing this option needs both characters to be in the same city or town.
