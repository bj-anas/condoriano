# Adventure Log

The Adventure Log bookshelf: how the logs are organized, how to unlock them, and the rewards for each log's chapters.

## Adventure Log: overview and bookshelf

Adventure logs are books about characters you meet in Black Desert. Completing their chapter quests, which retrace a person's steps or fulfil their dream, gives rewards. Unlocked books go onto your adventure log bookshelf. To reach it, press Esc for the main menu, then Adventure (F5), then Adventure Log Bookshelf. The bookshelf is family-based. Progress gives stats such as AP, DP, HP, stamina and weight limit to all characters in your family. It can also raise the maximum Agris Fever points and give Valks' Cry for enhancing equipment. Unlocking and completing every available log is highly recommended.

## Adventure Log: structure of themes, logs, volumes and pages

The bookshelf is divided into main themes, each holding one or more adventure logs. A log has several volumes (books), and each book has several pages with individual rewards. For example, the Crow Merchants' Records theme contains the Caphras' Record log, which has four volumes, and its pages reward Caphras stones. Finishing a volume unlocks the next one. Page tasks vary: finishing other questlines, defeating monsters, doing a life skill activity, exploring, or giving NPCs certain items.

## Adventure Log: claiming page rewards

A page's Reward button at the bottom starts gray. When you finish the page's objective, the button glows red and a checkmark appears on the goal. Press the button and the page gets a completion stamp. Rewards are received at that moment. Items go to your character's inventory, and stats apply to your whole family automatically. Finishing a chapter also unlocks the next chapter.

## Adventure Log: unlock conditions

You cannot start every log at once. Their unlock conditions are:

| Log theme | Unlock conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Igor Bartali's Adventures | Lv. 51, plus completing one of Looking for Adventurers, [Boss] Witch of Horrors, or [Special Growth] Fughar's Memorandum - Chapter 6. These stand for finishing the Calpheon main questline: the first for most characters, the second for characters made before the 2018 revamp, the third for season characters. |
| Crow Merchants' Records (Rulupee's Travels, Lamute Gang's Adventures, Caphras' Record, Fughar's Road to Success) | Lv. 55, and complete the quest [Adventure Log] Crow Merchants' Records from the Black Spirit. It unlocks the logs together. |
| Shakatu Merchants' Archive (Deve's Encyclopedia, Alustin's Alchemy, Dorin Morgrim's Secrets) | Lv. 57, and complete [Adventure Log] Shakatu Merchants' Archive from the Black Spirit. It unlocks all 3 logs. |
| Old Moon Logs (Book of Margahan, Herald's Journal, Pavino Greko's Collection) | Lv. 58, and complete [Adventure Log] Old Moon Logs from the Black Spirit, which unlocks Book of Margahan and Herald's Journal. Pavino Greko's Collection volumes 7 to 10 need separate quests from Pavino Greko, the storage keeper of Port Epheria in Calpheon. |
| Land of the Morning Light (The Eyes of Adventure, Boss Storybook) | Lv. 11, and unlock the Abyssal veins from the Magnus to fast travel to Land of the Morning Light, or start a new character there. Finish the Boss stories to unlock each boss in Boss Blitz. |

A separate event theme exists for seasonal logs. They can only be progressed during the event.

The Storybook - Morning Bosses log has Calamity 1 to Calamity 7 volumes. Each volume has a chapter per boss, and each chapter's reward is named C-n Boss Blitz Reward followed by the boss, at Rank 1, where n is the Calamity number.
