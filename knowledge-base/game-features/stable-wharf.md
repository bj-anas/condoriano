# Stable & Wharf

Stables and wharfs are where you check in and manage land mounts and ocean mounts.

## Stable & Wharf: overview

Stables handle land mounts such as horses, elephants, camels and wagons, through stable keeper NPCs in every town and city and even some crossroads. Wharfs handle ocean mounts such as ships, through wharf keeper NPCs in towns, cities and other piers on a river, the coast or an island.

## Stable: interface and slots

The stable shows information and stats of the mounts you keep in stables around the world. Open it via the main menu (hotkey: Esc) - Life (F6) - Stable, via any stable keeper, or via the world map (hotkey: M). A stable holds 3 checked-in mounts at first, and Stable Expansion Coupons increase that number. The coupons are mostly bought in the Pearl Shop with pearls and loyalties, and can also come from events as rewards and coupon gifts. The selected mount's details appear in the information window on the right. You can equip and unequip gear on mounts, wagons and ships from this interface: right-click an item in Equipper Gear & Inventory to unequip, or in Unequipped Gear to equip.

## Stable: check in, take out and recovery

- Check In: checks in a mount that is out. It fails if the stable has no empty spot or the mount is too far away.
- Remote Collection: checks in a mount far from the stable. Trade goods it carries may be damaged and lost.
- Recover: restores a mount's depleted health or stamina.
- Take Out: takes out the selected mount. Only one mount of the same type, land or ocean, can be out at a time, so check in the current one first.
- Transport: moves the selected mount to another stable, only within territories you have discovered.
- Register Mount: registers a mount if you have its emblem in your inventory.

## Stable: selling, markets and renting mounts

- Register at Horse Market: lists the mount for other Adventurers to buy. The price depends on the mount's tier, level, skills and gender.
- Sell Mount: sells the mount to the stable NPC for silver and Combat EXP. The price depends on tier, level, skills and gender. Mounts of level 1 to 14 can only be sold to stable keepers, and mounts above Lv. 15 can be sold to stable keepers outside cities.
- Imperial Horse / Delivery: sells the mount to the Imperial Court. Only mounts Lv. 15 or higher qualify, and only to the Imperial Court in cities.
- Rent Out: rents your horse to other Adventurers. Dream horses cannot be rented.

## Stable: name, appearance, brand and reset options

- Change Name: uses a Mount Name Change Coupon.
- Change Appearance: uses a Horse Appearance Change Coupon.
- Reset Death Count: uses a Mount Resurrection Reset item.
- Breeding Count Reset: uses a Horse Breeding Reset item.
- Reset Mount: fully resets the mount's growth, level and acquired skills, with a Reset Mount Growth item.
- Brand: uses a Mount Brand Spell Stone. Branded mounts have lower resurrection and recovery costs and a higher chance to learn skills when leveling up. Usable on Horse, Camel, Miniature Elephant, Donkey and Alpaca, but not guild mounts.

## Stable: managing mount skills

For a learned skill, Train masters the skill and needs a Mount Skill Training Coupon, Delete removes it, and Train All masters every skill and needs a Mount All Skill Training Coupon. For an unlearned skill, you pick a learned skill and an unlearned skill to change it into, using a Mount Skill Change Coupon. The change may not give the desired skill. Each failure raises the skill change count, and a higher count means a higher chance of learning the desired skill.

## Stable: Connect Wagon

Connect Wagon links horses to a wagon, but the wagon and all the horses must be checked in at the same stable. Connected horses show their own appearance and gain experience while you travel with the wagon, so many horses can be trained at once. Different wagons accept different numbers of horses. A wagon takes a stable slot like a mount, so connecting 4 horses to a noble wagon needs 5 open slots. The window shows a chain icon and a count such as 1/4 for connected horses.

## Stable: Exchange Horse

Exchange Horse trades one male and one female horse checked in at the stable for a new horse. The two selected horses disappear, since it is not breeding. You cannot know the new horse's tier in advance, so exchange horses only after using up their breeding count.

## Stable: Breeding Market

- Register at Breeding Market: only male horses can be registered. Tick Show Only Me before registering to let only you see your horse, so you can breed at your own pace.
- Market List: shows male horses registered by other Adventurers with their skills. Click the silver button at the bottom, choose a female horse from your stable, and select Request Breeding. You need enough silver or breeding fails.
- My Registrations: your Show Only Me horses. Breeding with them costs no silver.
- My Horse List: every male horse you registered.

## Stable: Horse Market and Rental Market

In the Horse Market's Market List you can filter by tier, skills and gender, and buy with the purchase button. Coursers have a horse head icon under their gender. In My Registrations, press Collect on sold mounts to get the proceeds. A higher Training level lowers the market tax. In the Rental Market you view horses others have rented out and get one after paying a deposit. The lender can withdraw it by force, or it is withdrawn automatically after 7 days. Click Collect Rent in your returned horse list to get part of the deposit back.

## Wharf: check in, take out and transport ships

- Check In: checks in a ship that is out. It fails if the wharf has no empty spot or the ship is too far away.
- Take Out: only one ship can be out at a time, so check in the current one before taking out another.
- Remote Collection: collects a distant ship to the wharf. Barter items loaded in it are destroyed and trade goods may be damaged.
- Transport: moves the ship to another wharf, only within discovered territories.
- Register Ship: registers a ship if you have its license in your inventory.
- Sell: sells the ship to the wharf manager NPC.

## Wharf: ship maintenance, cargo and upgrades

- Load Cargo: moves items between your inventory or town and city storage and the ship, up to its maximum weight and inventory slots.
- Buy Supplies: restores ship supplies. Rations drain while sailing or during remote collection, and low rations reduce ship speed.
- Repair Damage: repairs a checked-in ship's durability with silver.
- Change Name: uses a Mount Name Change Coupon.
- Upgrade: raises the ship's tier if you have the materials, and shows the whole ship upgrade tree.

## Wharf: sailors

Manage Sailors is only available once you have hired sailors. It lets you embark them, recover their condition and fire them. Hiring needs a Sailor Contract Certificate, sold by Islin Bartali, Proix and Philaberto Falasi. You then hire sailors at the Lunar Halo Inn, the Dancing Marlin or the beach at Port Epheria, though some sailors may refuse your offer.

## Wharf: negative naval fame and pirates

With negative naval fame you count as a pirate and cannot use most wharfs on the continent. The one wharf still open to you is in the pirate haven on the Kuit Islands, where the Cox Pirates live. Defeating sea monsters and doing a repeatable quest regain naval fame points.
