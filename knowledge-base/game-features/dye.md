# Dye

How dyeing works for armor, weapons, mount equipment, campsites and Pearl Shop outfits: getting dyes, storing them, applying them and combining them.

## Dye: what it does

Dye items color armor, weapons, mount equipment, campsites and Pearl Shop outfits. With a Value Pack you can freely use every dye in the game, but when the Value Pack expires, your armor color goes back to the default.

## Dye: how to obtain it

Dyes come from certain challenges, quests and seasonal event rewards. You can also buy them from the Central Market, in the Dye tab, and from the Pearl Shop, in the Beauty tab. Dye boxes do not give a chosen color but a random dye from the bundle's category. The Dye Boxes wiki page lists the probabilities. Dyes cannot be purchased by Adventurers residing in the Netherlands and Belgium.

## Dye Palette: storing dyes

Open the Dye Palette by clicking the Dyes button in your inventory (hotkey: I). To store a dye, right-click it in your inventory. To remove one, select it and click Take Out. The palette shows all dyes, including stored ones, with their names and colors. Storing dyes there frees space in your pearl inventory.

## Dye: Merv's Palette

Merv's Palette gives access to all dyes while it is active, so you can dye gear without owning the dyes. When it ends, its dyes vanish from your equipment, but the game saves them internally. If you activate Merv's Palette again, the dyes you applied earlier reappear.

## Dye: how to apply it

Dye works anywhere and anytime if the dye is in your palette. Steps:

1. Open the Dye screen with the J key or from the main menu.
2. Select the equipment item.
3. Select the part to dye, or press Dye All Parts to use one color on every part.
4. Select the dye. With Merv's Palette active, you can pick from it here.
5. Press the Dye button.

A preview on the right shows the result first, and the [x] above a selected color cancels it. Equipment pieces differ in how many parts they offer. The same color can look different because of the equipment's texture or the dye's source. If you try a dye you do not own, the counter goes negative and the Dye button only shows a message that you lack it.

## Dye: territorial varieties

Dyes with the same name can have different textures depending on origin. For example, Vivid Red from Heidel and from Valencia look very different. Icons above the color list show the territorial types, and you can pick the variety that gives the texture you want.

## Dye: Color Palette

With Merv's Palette active you also get the Color Palette window. It has an RGB color selector for customizing dyes on your equipment as fully as possible.

## Dye: combining dyes

Combining two dyes gives an Unknown Dye Box, which opens into a new random dye. Steps: open the main menu (hotkey: Esc), select Character, select Combine, choose two dyes from your inventory, and press the Combine button.
