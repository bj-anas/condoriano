# My Attendant

My Attendant lets you create a maid or butler in your own style that follows you in towns, performs Social Actions and more.

## My Attendant: getting started

My Attendants are managed from the My Attendant tab of the Maid/Butler List. Creating one requires at least one maid or butler already employed. The first My Attendant you create becomes the main attendant by default. The Apply Attendant feature changes the appearance of all your maids and butlers to My Attendant. To summon My Attendant, right-click the Maid/Butler List icon.

## My Attendant: customizing and dyeing

Customizing My Attendant needs the attendant summoned and an active Value Pack or Appearance Change Coupon. Dyeing needs the attendant summoned, a dyeable My Attendant exclusive outfit worn, and either dyes in your possession or Merv's Palette. In both cases, changes are lost if you dismiss the attendant while editing.

## My Attendant: Social Actions and following you

The Social Action window gains Attendant and Together options, and Together makes both your character and your attendant perform the action. You can set My Attendant to follow you around town. If the distance gets too large, it teleports to you, and leaving town dismisses it. It can stay summoned in Velia, Heidel, Calpheon City, Altinova, Valencia City, Grána, Nampo Moodle Village, Duvencrune, O'draxxia, Oquilla's Eye, Eilton, Muzgar and Hakinza Sanctuary.

## My Attendant: replacing maids and butlers

When Apply Attendant is on, all your maids and butlers look like My Attendant. Storage and Transaction maids and butlers wait while the storage or warehouse is open and are dismissed after use. A summoned My Attendant also waits while it is open, but is dismissed a short time after use. If this appearance is applied and the Maids/Butlers feature is enabled, only My Attendant appears in your residence.

## My Attendant: Tailoring Coupon

The My Attendant Tailoring Coupon turns your character outfits into exclusive My Attendant outfits. Buy it from any Old Moon Manager in towns and major cities for 1,000,000 Silver, then right-click it to convert owned outfits. Converted outfits can no longer be equipped by characters and cannot be reverted. Outfits that can convert to box form, such as Nouverikant and La Orzeca, can still be turned back into boxes, which give a normal outfit when opened.

## My Attendant: Tailoring Coupon conversion rules

Converted outfits carry the My Attendant exclusive icon and only My Attendants can equip them. Characters in your Family can summon My Attendant and move exclusive outfits into the Pearl Inventory. The number of coupons needed equals the outfit slots occupied. For example, Ranger Atanis Armor takes Top and Gloves slots, so it needs 2 coupons. Outfit and accessory effects are removed, as are extra crystal socket effects. Outfits socketed with the Black Spirit's Claw can be converted. Converted outfits cannot be registered on the Central Market, but converted Pearl outfits can be extracted.

## My Attendant: limits and name display

My Attendant is dismissed automatically after 30 min idle, even during a Social Action that persists while idle. Summoning is blocked in Node/Conquest War, War of the Roses and similar content. If summoned outside towns and major cities, it is dismissed after a short time. To show or hide its name, use My Attendant Name Settings under ESC - Settings - Show/Hide.
