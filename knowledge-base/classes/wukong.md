# Wukong

Profile of the Wukong class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Ascension.

## Wukong overview

Wukong is a male class called the Heaven-Breaker on the wiki. His Golden Gaze reads what enemies intend, and his Power Pole strikes deliberately. His base style centers on an extending magic pole and a mystical Gourd Bottle that boosts every attack.

The wiki lists only a Succession column for Wukong. His combat style is Melee. His Succession weapons are the Power Pole and the Gourd Bottle. His Ascension weapon is the Jade Pillar.

## Wukong playstyle

Wukong wields a shape-shifting Power Pole and attacks with burning karmic fury. The Flying Nimbus carries him across the battlefield as swiftly as a storm. His combat style mixes dazzling speed with scorching flames, which makes him a relentless and unpredictable force. The wiki says sacred trials awakened him and divine nectar powers him.

## Wukong Succession

Succession Wukong fights with the Power Pole and the Gourd Bottle at Melee combat style. The Power Pole is an extending magic pole, and the Gourd Bottle is a mystical item that boosts every attack. The wiki says his style mixes dazzling speed with scorching flames. The wiki lists only a Succession column for him, and his Ascension weapon is the Jade Pillar.

## Wukong Ascension

With Ascension, Wukong wields the mystical Jade Pillar as his Awakening weapon. It is the Ascension weapon listed for Wukong, whose combat style is Melee. The wiki says its ancient power makes his attacks extremely precise and lethal, making the battlefield his own domain.
