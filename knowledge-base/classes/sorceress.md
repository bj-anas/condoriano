# Sorceress

Profile of the Sorceress class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Sorceress overview

Sorceress is a female class that wields dark magic to bring destruction. She can attack both in melee and at range. Her base style is skills infused with dark power.

Sorceress's Succession weapon is the Amulet, and her combat style is Melee and Ranged. Her Awakening weapon is the Scythe, and her combat style is Melee.

## Sorceress playstyle

Sorceress specializes in instant movements that let her strike enemies at close range. She also has several skills to evade or neutralize enemy attacks, which matters for taking as little damage as possible. Her attack range depends on the skill she uses. She depends on Shards of Darkness, so she must always watch her MP. Beginners may find her controls overwhelming, but the wiki says players who master her will find her among the most fun classes to play, with a unique play style and hit mechanics.

## Sorceress Succession

Sorceress Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Sorceress fights with the Amulet at Melee and Ranged combat style. The wiki says she deploys a mix of ranged magic and melee attacks.

## Sorceress Awakening

Sorceress Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Sorceress swings a giant Scythe at Melee combat style. The wiki says the Scythe covers a wide area.
