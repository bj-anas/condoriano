# Sage

Profile of the Sage class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Sage overview

Sage is a male class, described on the wiki as the Last Ancient, who shut himself away in the voids of eternity and prepared for the end times. His skills use the forces that rule space and time. His base style is chains of destructive skill combos that mix area-of-effect magic with unpredictable skills.

Sage's Succession weapon is the Kyve, and his combat style is Ranged. His Awakening weapon is the Kibelius, and his combat style is Ranged.

## Sage playstyle

Sage delivers physical strikes by twisting space and time, and his hard-to-anticipate attacks leave enemies in disarray. He can move from one point to another by tearing open space, which he uses to gain a vantage point over opponents. He can also speed up time, which cuts the cooldown of some of his skills. This lets him vary his skill combos and adapt to the fast pace of combat.

## Sage Succession

Sage Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Sage fights with the Kyve, a cube-shaped weapon, at Ranged combat style. The wiki says he uses it to cast area-of-effect magic that lets him morph the physical realm and accelerate time to his advantage in battle.

## Sage Awakening

Sage Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Sage uses the Kibelius, his spear, at Ranged combat style. The wiki says he draws on lightning power stored in the Kibelius to strike enemies down, and can make unpredictable moves at lightning speed.
