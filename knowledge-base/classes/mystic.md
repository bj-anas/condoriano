# Mystic

Profile of the Mystic class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Mystic overview

Mystic is a female martial artist whose martial prowess is described on the wiki as shaking the earth. She was found and saved from the ruins of her village, which war had destroyed. She then spent years honing her skills under the same master as Striker. Her base style is exhilarating martial arts skill combos.

Mystic's Succession weapon is the Gauntlet, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Cestus, and her combat style is Melee.

## Mystic playstyle

Mystic mixes eastern martial arts with practical combat skills. She is known for flurries of punches and kicks, fluid combos, close-range strikes and the ease with which she evades enemies. She also has a high level of defense, which she uses to harass opponents on the battlefield.

## Mystic Succession

Mystic Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Mystic fights with the Gauntlet at Melee combat style. The wiki says she overpowers enemies with acrobatic flurries of movement and attacks.

## Mystic Awakening

Mystic Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Mystic uses the Cestus at Melee combat style. The wiki says her skills draw on the Sea Dragon's blessings, in both strength and splendor.
