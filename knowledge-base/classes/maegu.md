# Maegu

Profile of the Maegu class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Maegu overview

Maegu is a female fox-summoning Do wielder of the Jwado School. She forged a pact with a fox spirit from another plane. As her name suggests, she is as agile as a fox and nimble at evading attacks. Her base style is a flurry of melee and ranged spells that turn the world red.

Maegu's Succession weapon is the Foxspirit Charm, and her combat style is Ranged/Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Foxtail Fans, and her combat style is Melee.

## Maegu playstyle

Maegu casts a variety of ranged attacks as spells through her foxspirit charm. She can also throw the charms around herself to cause explosive close-range damage. As her name suggests, she is agile like a fox, so she is nimble at evading enemy attacks. Her base style is a flurry of both melee and ranged spells.

## Maegu Succession

Maegu Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Maegu uses the Foxspirit Charm at Ranged/Melee combat style. The wiki says she can magically conjure an illusory double, now that she has the fox spirit's full power. She moves more agilely, casts fancier spells, and can even trade places with her double.

## Maegu Awakening

Maegu Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Upon Awakening, Maegu uses the Foxtail Fans at Melee combat style. The fans act as a conduit for the power of the Foxspirit's flames. She can evade enemy attacks swiftly while chaining her own smoothly. She fights in close quarters with graceful movements that swirl foxfire with each twirl, like a classical dance.
