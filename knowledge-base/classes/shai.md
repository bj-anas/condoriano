# Shai

Profile of the Shai class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, support role, Normal and Talent states.

## Shai overview

Shai is a small female class raised by the Chief of Florin. She is a support class that balances support skills with combat skills. Her base traits are an adorable look and stats that favor life skills. She enters the game with Professional Lv. 1 in alchemy, and she plays music she composed herself in the game.

On the wiki, Shai's states are called Normal and Talent rather than Succession and Awakening. In the Normal state her weapon is the Florang and her combat style is Ranged. In the Talent state her weapon is the Sol and her combat style is Ranged.

## Shai playstyle

The Florang she wields is roughly her own size. Her sub-weapon, the Vitclari, lets her heal and shield her allies. Her small figure gives her high evasion, and simple movement skills let her avoid close encounters with enemies. She has notably fewer skills than other classes.

## Shai Normal

In the Normal state, Shai uses the Florang as her main weapon at Ranged combat style. Her sub-weapon is the Vitclari, which heals and shields allies. The wiki highlights her adorable look and stats that favor life skills.

## Shai Talent

In the Talent state, Shai uses the Sol at Ranged combat style. The wiki says she can cast healing and other supporting buffs. She can also compose music, play musical instruments and register music albums. These abilities fit her role as a support class.
