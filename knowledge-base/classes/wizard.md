# Wizard

Profile of the Wizard class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Casting Speed advice, Succession and Awakening.

## Wizard overview

Wizard is a male class, described on the wiki as the one who reached the top of magical skill. He channels the power of the natural elements into his staff. His base style is destructive elemental magic, and he can heal and give buffs to himself and his allies. His allies feel reassured when he is nearby.

Wizard's Succession weapon is the Staff, and his combat style is Ranged. His Awakening weapon is the Godr Sphera, and his combat style is Ranged.

## Wizard playstyle

Wizard pummels enemies with huge fireballs, repeated lightning strikes and fierce blizzards. He can also teleport and deliver a barrage of critical strikes at range. Most of his skills are the same as Witch's, so he is more efficient against large groups of enemies than in 1-on-1 fights. Wizard attacks faster as his Casting Speed level goes up, and the wiki recommends that players keep his Casting Speed at Lv. 5 or higher.

## Wizard Succession

Wizard Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Wizard fights with the Staff at Ranged combat style. The wiki says he casts explosive area-of-effect spells with elemental power pushed to the limit.

## Wizard Awakening

Wizard Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Wizard uses the Godr Sphera at Ranged combat style. The wiki says he calls Guardians that carry water and fire elemental power, and also casts spells.
