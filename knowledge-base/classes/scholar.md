# Scholar

Profile of the Scholar class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Ascension.

## Scholar overview

Scholar is a female alchemist who fights with hammers crafted from the elements of the earth. In her base style she hits fast, with precise aim and enormous force, using two hammers.

Scholar's Succession weapon is the Hammers, and her combat style is Melee. Her Ascension weapon is the Sledgehammer, and her combat style is Melee.

## Scholar playstyle

Scholar prevailed over the Black Death with exceptional knowledge of alchemy and physics, and she can control gravity freely. Her command of gravity lets her fool enemies with erratic movement and crushing counterforce. In her base style she attacks with her two hammers, hitting fast and precisely with enormous force.

## Scholar Succession

Succession Scholar fights with two Hammers at Melee combat style, and the Hammers are the weapons she uses before Ascension. The wiki says she hits fast and precisely with enormous force. Her Ascension weapon is the Sledgehammer.

## Scholar Ascension

With Ascension, Scholar achieves the alchemist's true desire of transmuting gold. She uses the Sledgehammer at Melee combat style to make the most of her metal transmutations, and her strikes cover a larger area. Her attacks bend the speed, mass and metals of the earth's counterforce as she likes. The wiki says she abandons the laws of matter and creates her own formulas that force enemies to kneel.
