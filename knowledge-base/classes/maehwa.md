# Maehwa

Profile of the Maehwa class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Maehwa overview

Maehwa is a female class that represents the elegance and grace of eastern martial arts and archery. The wiki describes her as a shining blade among falling petals. Her base style is hit-and-run combat, using ranged attacks with a Horn Bow to keep enemies at bay.

Maehwa's Succession weapon is the Blade, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Kerispear, and her combat style is Melee.

## Maehwa playstyle

Maehwa overwhelms enemies with her sword or spear and leaves foes falling amid flower petals. Her precise, agile ranged strikes with her Horn Bow leave enemies distraught and immobile. She also has the skill Chase, which the wiki says is akin to Musa's and gives her added maneuverability. Unlike Musa's attacks, Maehwa's attacks are marked by falling petals.

## Maehwa Succession

Maehwa Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Maehwa fights with the Blade at Melee combat style. The wiki says she delivers deadly strikes that trail a flurry of red petals drifting in the wind.

## Maehwa Awakening

Maehwa Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Maehwa uses the Kerispear at Melee combat style. The wiki says each strike of her spear engulfs her enemies in blue flame.
