# Berserker

Profile of the Berserker class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Berserker overview

Berserker is a male melee brawler known for an iron-hard physique and resolve. He is the largest of all Black Desert classes. His base trait on the wiki is a frightening show of raw strength. Although his huge size can make him look unwieldy, his attacks hit hard enough to deliver a decisive blow to enemies.

Berserker's Succession weapon is the Axe, and his combat style is Melee. His Awakening weapon is the Iron Buster, and his combat style is Melee and Ranged.

## Berserker playstyle

Berserker bashes, pummels and strikes enemies with his mighty axe. He uses earth-shattering skills that knock enemies off their feet. He can leap in huge strides through the air and crash down on enemies. He also has several grappling and holding skills, throws included, that let him pull enemies toward his allies.

## Berserker Succession

Berserker Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Berserker's Succession uses the Axe as the weapon, with a Melee combat style. The wiki says Succession Berserker uses ruthless attacks and grappling skills that frighten his enemies.

## Berserker Awakening

Berserker Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakening Berserker wields the Iron Buster, and his combat style is Melee and Ranged. The wiki describes this style as a volley of ground-shaking attacks from the explosive Iron Buster.
