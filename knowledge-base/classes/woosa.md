# Woosa

Profile of the Woosa class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Woosa overview

Woosa is a female class from the Woodo School, whose members train body and mind to use the Do. The wiki describes her as a butterfly flying through the sky of enlightenment. Her main weapons are a fan and a Do stave, which summon butterflies. She is a mid-to-melee range class, and her base style is delivering quick melee strikes and mid-range area-of-effect attacks.

Woosa's Succession weapon is the Swallowtail Fan, and her combat style is Melee to Mid-range. Her Awakening weapon is the Soul Tome, and her combat style is Melee to Mid-range.

## Woosa playstyle

Woosa's attacks split into two kinds. Melee attacks are fast hits with her fan folded. Area-of-effect attacks mix Do arts with her fan spread attack. Her Do arts use a butterfly motif. She can call up a huge storm from a butterfly's beating wings, conjure rain clouds in a bright blue sky, and tap the power of the Sagoonja within her. She moves gracefully around the battlefield and overwhelms foes with smooth, water-like combos.

## Woosa Succession

Woosa Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Woosa fights with the Swallowtail Fan at Melee to Mid-range combat style. The wiki says that with firm resolve she can absorb an enemy's attack and use Do energy to return it. The returned attack ignores the enemy's defense, even though she is not invincible while doing it.

## Woosa Awakening

Woosa Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Woosa uses the Soul Tome at Melee to Mid-range combat style. She binds charms into a tome and uses a calligraphy brush for writing the names of the dead, drawing on the power of the Seocheon Flower Garden, a field that blooms in the netherworld. She calls the souls of the dead out of the tome with the brush to attack. She can also summon flowers of death to block enemies strategically. These flowers work like mines or timed attacks and disrupt enemy actions. The wiki says she uses both long-range and short-range attacks depending on the distance to the enemy.
