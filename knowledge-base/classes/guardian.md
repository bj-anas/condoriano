# Guardian

Profile of the Guardian class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Guardian overview

Guardian is a female warrior who serves Omua, an ancient dragon that once ruled the Mountain of Eternal Winter. The wiki describes her as fearless: she bound herself to the Black Spirit while trying to take back Ynix, a flame said to be able to burn even gods. On the battlefield she is full of strength and vigor and makes an unmistakable show of force.

Guardian's Succession weapon is the Battle Axe, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Jordun, and her combat style is Melee.

## Guardian playstyle

Guardian is a melee fighter who rushes enemies with her Battle Axe and Shield and hits with crushing force. She seizes enemies by the throat, smashes them with her Shield, then ends them with the Battle Axe. She also repels enemies with the Shield and holds the line against many enemies while waiting for the right moment to strike back. Her movements can look slow and unwieldy, but players tend to value her long-reaching attacks and heavy blows.

## Guardian Succession

Guardian Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Guardian fights with the Battle Axe at Melee combat style. The wiki describes a skilled, combat-eager warrior who pairs a one-handed Battle Axe with a Shield.

## Guardian Awakening

Guardian Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Guardian uses the Jordun at Melee combat style. The wiki describes a powerful warrior who attacks with heavy blows and long reach using her two-handed Jordun.
