# Dark Knight

Profile of the Dark Knight class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Dark Knight overview

Dark Knight is a female class built around a mysterious, dark swordswoman archetype. On the wiki she is described as a fallen swordswoman swept by darkness. She wields the Kriegsmesser, and each of her attacks discharges the energy of spirits.

Dark Knight's Succession weapon is the Kriegsmesser, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Vediant, and her combat style is Melee and Ranged.

## Dark Knight playstyle

Dark Knight can land heavy melee blows with her Kriegsmesser and also make ranged attacks by casting spells that hit unwitting enemies from a distance. Her large weapon can suggest that she is slow, but she has a number of skills that let her move quickly over short distances. She uses them to distract enemies and find openings for devastating strikes.

## Dark Knight Succession

Dark Knight Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Dark Knight fights with the Kriegsmesser at Melee combat style. The wiki says she cuts down enemies closing in from every direction, with each swing of the Kriegsmesser carrying dark power.

## Dark Knight Awakening

Dark Knight Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Dark Knight uses the Vediant, with a Melee and Ranged combat style. The wiki describes a style that deals heavy magic area-of-effect damage by calling on the power stored in her sword.
