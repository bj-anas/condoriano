# Striker

Profile of the Striker class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Striker overview

Striker is a male master of eastern martial arts, described on the wiki as breaking through physical limits. His body is harder than steel, built through years of training and the odd street fight. His base style is exhilarating sequences of martial arts skill combos.

Striker's Succession weapon is the Gauntlet, and his combat style is Melee. His Awakening weapon is the Gardbrace, and his combat style is Melee.

## Striker playstyle

Striker unleashes a barrage of skill combos on enemies and is good at evasive maneuvers in close quarters. He can cross short distances at lightning speed and attack an enemy's rear. He can lift enemies into the air and chain more combos. He also has a few grappling skills that help neutralize opponents and give him more options in different combat situations. His combos flow so smoothly that they feel like skills in their own right.

## Striker Succession

Striker Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Striker fights with the Gauntlet at Melee combat style. The wiki says he pushes his endless stream of kicks and punches to the limit.

## Striker Awakening

Striker Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Striker uses the Gardbrace at Melee combat style. The wiki says it raises his combat sequences another level by bringing out a manifestation of his inner self called an Echo Spirit.
