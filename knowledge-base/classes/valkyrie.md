# Valkyrie

Profile of the Valkyrie class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Valkyrie overview

Valkyrie is a female holy knight blessed by Elion. She takes the front in battle with Elion's sacred powers. Her base style includes skills that heal and buff her allies as well as her attacks.

Valkyrie's Succession weapon is the Longsword, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Lancia, and her combat style is Melee.

## Valkyrie playstyle

Valkyrie fights in a style similar to Warrior's, but she also has skills that restore HP and give assorted buffs to her allies. The wiki says she uses the fewest healing potions of any class. She has skills that restore both her HP and her Sacred Power, and also skills that restore Sacred Power every time she defends successfully.

## Valkyrie Succession

Valkyrie Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Valkyrie fights with a sword and shield, using the Longsword at Melee combat style. The wiki says she mixes attack and defense using the strength of the Sacred Power.

## Valkyrie Awakening

Valkyrie Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Valkyrie uses the Lancia at Melee combat style. The wiki says she can land a decisive blow by pushing far into the enemy formation.
