# Ranger

Profile of the Ranger class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Ranger overview

Ranger is a female class, described on the wiki as the daughter of the goddess Sylvia and a protector of Kamasylvia. She is among the most popular bow-using ranged characters in Black Desert. Her base style uses the power of the spirits in the blood of the goddess Sylvia to deliver a storm of deadly arrows or to fight in melee.

Ranger's Succession weapon is the Longbow, and her combat style is Ranged. Her Awakening weapon is the Kamasylven Sword, and her combat style is Melee and Ranged.

## Ranger playstyle

Ranger's early game is less impressive than that of other classes, but she compensates later by gaining skills that boost her strength. She is particularly vulnerable to counterattacks from enemies who specialize in melee, especially when they get close. The wiki advises her to keep her distance unless enemies stay stationary. She also has a number of Flow skills, which open up extra skill combos if used properly.

## Ranger Succession

Ranger Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Ranger fights with the Longbow at Ranged combat style. The wiki says she makes long-range bow attacks infused with spirit power, and that Succession Ranger remains strong as a ranged fighter.

## Ranger Awakening

Ranger Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Ranger fights in melee with the Kamasylven Sword. Her combat style is Melee and Ranged: the wiki says she mixes melee and ranged attacks mid-fight. This is a sharp contrast with Succession Ranger, who stays a ranged fighter.
