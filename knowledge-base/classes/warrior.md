# Warrior

Profile of the Warrior class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Warrior overview

Warrior is a male melee class with balanced offense and defense. The wiki describes him as the model medieval knight, with a sturdy shield, a sharp longsword and firm resolve. His base style uses a Sword and Shield or a two-handed Great Sword.

Warrior's Succession weapon is the Longsword, and his combat style is Melee. His Awakening weapon is the Great Sword, and his combat style is Melee.

## Warrior playstyle

Warrior spends willpower (WP) during combat. He replenishes it by landing normal attacks or by blocking enemy attacks. He has several strong attack skills with his sword and some defensive skills, and he can charge into battle quickly with various movement skills. He also has grapples and crowd control, so he can fill several roles in a fight.

## Warrior Succession

Warrior Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Warrior fights with the Longsword and Shield at Melee combat style. The wiki says Succession Warrior balances offense, defense and mobility well.

## Warrior Awakening

Warrior Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Warrior wields the two-handed Great Sword at Melee combat style. The wiki says he hits hard at close quarters.
