# Hashashin

Profile of the Hashashin class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Hashashin overview

Hashashin is a male class described on the wiki as the Blade of Aal and the bringer of Blackstar's paradise. His base style combines assassin-like agility with spellcasting. Blessed with the divine power of Aal, he can move extremely fast without sinking into the desert sands.

Hashashin's Succession weapon is the Shamshir, and his combat style is Melee. His Awakening weapon is the Dual Glaives, and his combat style is Melee.

## Hashashin playstyle

Hashashin carries a crescent-shaped Shamshir and a Haladie filled with the magic of the desert sands, and uses them for unexpected strikes. He can blink out and back in instantly, or call up countless whirlwinds that tear foes apart. His surprising movements confuse enemies, leaving them no way to counter.

## Hashashin Succession

Hashashin Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Hashashin fights with the Shamshir at Melee combat style. The wiki says he throws enemies into chaos with sand spells and sudden attacks.

## Hashashin Awakening

Hashashin Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Hashashin wields the large Dual Glaives at Melee combat style. The wiki also says he summons a sand serpent in this state.
