# Ninja

Profile of the Ninja class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, how it differs from Kunoichi, Succession and Awakening.

## Ninja overview

Ninja is a male assassin with skills similar to Kunoichi's. The wiki calls him the assassin you will never see coming. His base style is surprise attacks that mix stealth with diversionary tactics.

Ninja's Succession weapon is the Shortsword, and his combat style is Melee. His Awakening weapon is the Sura Katana, and his combat style is Melee. Like Kunoichi, he can use two types of sub-weapons, and he prefers Shuriken.

## Ninja playstyle

Ninja relies on quick feet and uses crowd control skills together with a mesmerizing barrage of attacks. He prefers Shuriken as his sub-weapon because his skills are specialized for them. Most of his skills are the same as Kunoichi's, but Ninja has no blocking skills, which makes him harder to control than Kunoichi. To make up for that, he moves better, dodges better and has crowd control skills built for him. That makes him lean on nonstop chains of attacks, which sets his playstyle apart from Kunoichi's.

## Ninja Succession

Ninja Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Ninja fights with the Shortsword at Melee combat style. The wiki says he pushes his action-heavy attacks and diversionary tactics to the limit.

## Ninja Awakening

Ninja Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Ninja uses the Sura Katana at Melee combat style. The wiki says he overwhelms enemies by brandishing six katanas at once.
