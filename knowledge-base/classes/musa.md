# Musa

Profile of the Musa class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Musa overview

Musa is a male class, described on the wiki as the blade that cuts through the wind. He shows off eastern martial arts with his Blade and Horn Bow. His base style is hit-and-run combat, made possible by high maneuverability, with ranged attacks from the Horn Bow against enemies he wants to keep at bay.

Musa's Succession weapon is the Blade, and his combat style is Melee. His Awakening weapon is the Crescent Blade, and his combat style is Melee.

## Musa playstyle

Musa is known for sudden bursts of strength followed by a rapid string of agile skill combos. His moves need high-precision, well-timed strikes that can feel overwhelming at first, but the wiki says mastering them is well worth the effort. His greatest advantage is maneuverability. His skill Chase lets him move behind and in front of enemies at incredible speed, which gives him many openings. He can also lift enemies into the air and slash and stab them there, which suits players who like to keep attacking without a break.

## Musa Succession

Musa Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Musa fights with the Blade at Melee combat style. The wiki describes the peak of eastern martial arts, shown by overpowering enemies with attack after attack.

## Musa Awakening

Musa Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Musa uses the Crescent Blade at Melee combat style. The wiki says each swing of the Crescent Blade delivers a frenzy of flames.
