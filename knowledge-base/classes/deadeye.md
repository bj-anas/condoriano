# Deadeye

Profile of the Deadeye class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Ascension.

## Deadeye overview

Deadeye is a female ranged class, described on the wiki as an outsider from another world whose bullets trace constellations. She switches effortlessly between an arsenal of weapons and takes down enemies with the accuracy and quickness of an outlaw.

The wiki lists only a Succession column for Deadeye. Her combat style is Ranged. Her Succession weapons are Revolvers/Shotgun. Her Ascension weapon is the Lil' Devil.

## Deadeye playstyle

Thanks to an unerring eye, Deadeye is quicker and more accurate than anyone else. She sometimes dives headlong into the fray to disrupt foes without mercy. The wiki says that once she has an enemy in her sights, there is no escape.

## Deadeye Succession

Succession Deadeye is a Ranged class that uses Revolvers/Shotgun and switches between an arsenal of weapons. She plays like a fast, accurate outlaw. She sometimes dives into the middle of the fight to disrupt her foes.

## Deadeye Ascension

With Ascension, Deadeye gets the Lil' Devil as her Ascension weapon. The wiki says she gains new power supported by Marni's technology. She unleashes resonating, explosive rounds that light the battlefield like a sky full of stars. Her combat style stays Ranged.
