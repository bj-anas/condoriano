# Dosa

Profile of the Dosa class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Dosa overview

Dosa is a male class described on the wiki as appearing from the clouds and vanishing with the fog. He carries the Hwando sword and the Gombangdae pipe, which conjures clouds. His base style is to shroud the battlefield in fog and then close in to finish off opponents with his powerful blade.

Dosa's Succession weapon is the Hwando, and his combat style is Melee. His Awakening weapon is the Enlightened Blade, and his combat style is Ranged/Melee.

## Dosa playstyle

Dosa's clouds can release summoned archers and spearmen that carry umbral and luminous energies. The wiki warns that a moment's distraction can let him emerge suddenly from the clouds right in front of you. His base style is to shroud the battlefield in fog, then close in and finish opponents off with his strong blade.

## Dosa Succession

Dosa Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Dosa fights with the Hwando at Melee combat style, together with the cloud-conjuring Gombangdae pipe. He fills the battlefield with fog, then closes in and finishes opponents with his blade. He can also summon archers and spearmen from his clouds.

## Dosa Awakening

Dosa Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Dosa is described as an Awakened Do wielder who has been enlightened to the Five Elements. His weapon is the Enlightened Blade, and his combat style is Ranged/Melee. The blade helps generate and amplify elemental energy, which lets him command the elements freely on the battlefield.
