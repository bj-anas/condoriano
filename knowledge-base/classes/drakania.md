# Drakania

Profile of the Drakania class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Drakania overview

Drakania is a female class whose story is about destroying the god-consuming flame to bring back the dragons' long-lost paradise. Her base style is to close the distance to the enemy quickly at both mid and close range, then release her destructive energy or interrupt the opponent's attacks.

Drakania's Succession weapon is the Slayer, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Trion, and her combat style is Melee.

## Drakania playstyle

Her Shard collects ions from the surrounding air and channels that energy into her Slayer for powerful, destructive attacks. The Shard also quietly steals her opponents' energy for her own use. Her second heart, the dragon heart, lets her recover quickly from injury and also sends her rushing across the battlefield like a storm.

## Drakania Succession

Drakania Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Drakania fights with the Slayer at Melee combat style. The wiki says she builds up even more energy in her Shard. Sending that energy into the Slayer lets her land devastating strikes.

## Drakania Awakening

Drakania Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Drakania uses the Trion, a pair of spears, at Melee combat style. "Trion" means "spear." The left-hand spear is empowered with raging winds and the right-hand spear embodies a raging inferno. She wields both together, stabbing, slicing and striking without pause.

She uniquely switches between a Hexeblood state and a Dragonblood state. Hexeblood is her human form, with swift and precise strikes using both Trion spears. In Dragonblood she fights in a dragon form: the left-hand Trion becomes a dragon's horn that she uses to crush enemies or to brace herself. The wiki says this state gives the destructive power of dragons.
