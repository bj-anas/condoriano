# Witch

Profile of the Witch class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Witch overview

Witch is a talented young girl who knows elemental magic thoroughly. The wiki describes her as commanding the power of polygons. Her base style is destructive elemental magic, and she heals herself and grants buffs to herself and her allies. She uses the natural elements with ease to freeze, incinerate or topple enemies.

Witch's Succession weapon is the Staff, and her combat style is Ranged. Her Awakening weapon is the Aad Sphera, and her combat style is Ranged.

## Witch playstyle

Her ability to unleash an instant barrage of area-of-effect magic is considered second to none. She can move through space to deliver area-of-effect attacks, and she gives her allies healing and defense buffs. She casts area-of-effect spells at range using fire, lightning and ice. She can revive allies with her skill Resurrection or restore their health with healing magic. She can also turn a battle around with ranged attacks and debuffs on enemies.

## Witch Succession

Witch Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Witch fights with the Staff at Ranged combat style. The wiki says she casts explosive area-of-effect spells with elemental power pushed to the limit.

## Witch Awakening

Witch Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Witch uses the Aad Sphera at Ranged combat style. The wiki says she calls elemental beings that carry earth and lightning power.
