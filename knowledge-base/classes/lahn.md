# Lahn

Profile of the Lahn class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Lahn overview

Lahn is a female class from a country in the Far East. The wiki describes her fighting as a dance of death. Her base style looks like a well-choreographed dance, and she can cross long distances by gliding through the sky with her skill Nimbus Stride.

Lahn's Succession weapon is the Crescent Pendulum, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Crimson Glaives, and her combat style is Melee. Her sub-weapon is the Noble Sword.

## Lahn playstyle

Lahn throws or swings her Crescent Pendulum, a weapon fastened to her arm, to deliver wide area-of-effect attacks and skill combos. Enemies who get too close are punished with her sub-weapon, the Noble Sword. Her skill Nimbus Stride lets her glide a long way through the air, and every further mid-air jump or somersault stretches the stride even farther, so she covers long distances quickly. She is not good at quick maneuvers over short distances, so once a fight starts she needs to subdue opponents fast with a series of skill combos.

## Lahn Succession

Lahn Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Lahn fights with the Crescent Pendulum at Melee combat style. The wiki says she delivers whipping blows by throwing or swinging it.

## Lahn Awakening

Lahn Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Lahn uses a pair of Crimson Glaives at Melee combat style. The wiki says she chains rapid attacks that land at times and places opponents do not expect.
