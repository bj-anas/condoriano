# Corsair

Profile of the Corsair class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Corsair overview

Corsair is a female class with free-form blade work, fluid combos and unique rope movement skills that keep her afloat. Her base feature on the wiki is a light, unusual type of movement based on those rope maneuvers. Using the powers of the Mareca, she can turn into a mermaid and fight in that form. Her enemies are often confused by her unpredictable, free-spirited behavior.

Corsair's Succession weapon is the Serenaca, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Patraca, and her combat style is Melee/Ranged.

## Corsair playstyle

The Serenaca, her main weapon, is a bendy blade that she can lengthen or shorten whenever she wants, so enemies struggle to guess how far her strikes reach. Her fighting centers on fluid combos and rope movement. Her loyal Scallywags are armed with cannons and are ready to fire an artillery barrage as soon as their captain gives the command.

## Corsair Succession

Corsair Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Corsair fights with the Serenaca at Melee combat style. The wiki says she combines sword techniques with the power of the waves to break through enemy defenses with her Dream Whales. She also turns into a mermaid and calls up giant tidal waves.

## Corsair Awakening

Corsair Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Corsair uses the Patraca, a harpoon sword, with a Melee/Ranged combat style, so she can fight at both short and long range. The wiki describes fierce long-range fighting with the Patraca, plus team attacks with her loyal crew, the Slippery Scallywags, who fire cannons at her command.
