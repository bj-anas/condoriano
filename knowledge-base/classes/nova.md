# Nova

Profile of the Nova class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Nova overview

Nova is a female class who is the sole heiress to the Calpheon royal bloodline, which ended with the rise of the Republic. The wiki describes her as the last star of the Kingdom of Calpheon, who embraces the distorted goddess. Her base style effortlessly combines offense and defense by summoning spirit guards.

Nova's Succession weapon is the Morning Star, and her combat style is Melee and Ranged. Her Awakening weapon is the Sting, and her combat style is Melee. She also carries a shield called Quoratum.

## Nova playstyle

Nova wields a Morning Star and a shield named Quoratum, which is imbued with the power of the distorted goddess. Her weapons give her both mid-range and melee attacks. The shield covers her like an iron fortress, and she can reach distant enemies and flatten them by stretching out the chains of her Morning Star. The souls of the Thornwood Guard inhabit Quoratum and obey her. She sends these spirit guards to attack while she stays protected behind her shield.

## Nova Succession

Nova Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Nova fights with the Morning Star at Melee and Ranged combat style. The wiki says she summons Axion, a creature with far greater power than the Thornwood Guards.

## Nova Awakening

Nova Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Nova uses the Sting at Melee combat style. The wiki describes it as a morphable weapon. She makes magic attacks with it and uses unusual strikes that draw on supernatural power.
