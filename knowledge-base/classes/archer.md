# Archer

Profile of the Archer class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle and what each branch offers.

## Archer overview

Archer is a male ranged class, described on the wiki as the forgotten son of Sylvia and a protector of the realm. He is the one who came back from Aduir, near the roots of the Sacred Kamasylve. His base combat style is built on nimble footwork that lets him evade enemies easily. Unlike other classes, Archer can choose at the start of the game whether to use his Crossbow or his Greatbow, and he can switch between them. This choice makes him more flexible when a battle changes.

Archer is a Ranged class in both of his weapon states. The wiki lists the first state as Original and the second as Awakening. In the Original state his weapon is the Crossbow and his combat style is Ranged. In the Awakening state his weapon is the Greatbow and his combat style is still Ranged.

## Archer playstyle

Archer plays as a mobile ranged fighter. He has a number of skills that let him move around the battlefield easily while attacking enemies. He also fires powerful, concentrated bursts of energy. Being able to switch between the Crossbow and the Greatbow lets him adjust to whatever a fight demands.

## Archer Succession

On the wiki page the first column for Archer is labeled Original rather than Succession. In this state Archer uses the Crossbow as his weapon and fights at Ranged combat style. The wiki says he uses the Crossbow to take on enemies with flair, while the Greatbow handles his long-range shots.

## Archer Awakening

Archer Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Archer's Awakening state uses the Greatbow as the weapon, and the combat style is Ranged. The wiki says the Greatbow handles his long-range shots. The page's Key Features list a feature under the label Ascension: that Archer has the longest range of all Black Desert classes.
