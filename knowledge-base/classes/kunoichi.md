# Kunoichi

Profile of the Kunoichi class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle, Succession and Awakening.

## Kunoichi overview

Kunoichi is a female assassin from the shadows who is equally capable with sword strikes, diversionary tactics and ninjitsu. Her feature shared across all states is surprise attacks that mix stealth with diversionary tactics.

Kunoichi's Succession weapon is the Shortsword, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Chakram, and her combat style is Melee.

## Kunoichi playstyle

Kunoichi can throw smoke bombs, go into stealth mode and use crowd control moves. In stealth mode she stays hidden from enemies, and she can deliver strikes that stun, stiffen or inflict other status effects. Her defense is weak next to other classes, so she depends on well-timed, well-placed skills that need expert control.

## Kunoichi Succession

Kunoichi Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Kunoichi fights with the Shortsword at Melee combat style. The wiki says she delivers stealthy, swift attacks that suit her character as an assassin.

## Kunoichi Awakening

Kunoichi Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Kunoichi uses the large Chakram at Melee combat style. The wiki describes a unique style of combat built around the Chakram.
