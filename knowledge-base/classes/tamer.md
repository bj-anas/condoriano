# Tamer

Profile of the Tamer class in Black Desert Online: its weapons, combat style, playstyle with Heilang, Succession and Awakening.

## Tamer overview

Tamer is a petite female class, described on the wiki as the princess of a fallen kingdom who was raised by a mystical beast. Her base style depends on Heilang, a beastly creature in the form of a giant wolf. Heilang is a summoned creature that helps her in battle and also works as a mount.

Tamer's Succession weapon is the Shortsword, and her combat style is Melee. Her Awakening weapon is the Celestial Bo Staff, and her combat style is Melee.

## Tamer playstyle

Tamer's small body gives her exceptional agility and dodging ability, which help her even without summoning Heilang. With her trusty Heilang at her side she fears nothing. The wiki warns enemies not to underestimate her size, because her attacks hit hard.

## Tamer Succession

Tamer Succession is the level 56 path that keeps the class's original combat system. Succession Tamer fights with the Shortsword at Melee combat style. The wiki says she keeps up an apparently endless stream of attacks, helped by a stronger Heilang.

## Tamer Awakening

Tamer Awakening is the level 56 path that gives the class an Awakening Weapon and a new style of gameplay. Awakened Tamer uses the Celestial Bo Staff at Melee combat style. The wiki says she delivers concise, well-placed attacks with a staff imbued with the power of Heilang, her beastly summon.
