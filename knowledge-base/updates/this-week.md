## Patch notes Sep 23, 2026: classes, new Common Skill

The Sep 23, 2026 patch introduces the Common Skill, released with the finale of Black Desert Act 1. It is a skill that all characters can use, and its effects and animations are the same across classes. Players who played Inner Edania may find it familiar. The new Common Skill inflicts Knockdown on monsters and dismounts Adventurers. The developers say they are fine-tuning it so hit detection and combo flow do not differ between classes, and that further improvements to make it more viable in PvP are in the works, along with news on class balance adjustments in progress.

## Patch notes Sep 23, 2026: class changes

Class changes in the Sep 23, 2026 patch:

- [Corsair] Wind-piercer Patraca: fixed slower skill activation when used from a Quick Slot, and corrected the skill's control description to match the actual controls.
- [Wukong] Extend Longer!: improved to combo from the Magnus skill Strike 'Em!.

- The only summons that monsters still target are Heuklang, Gorr, Tett, Arne, and Marg. Examples of summons now ignored: Woosa's Cloudrise and Sari Flowers, Nova's Thornwood Guards, Hashashin's Sandstorm.

## Patch notes Sep 23, 2026: items, La Orzeca Landscape

In the Sep 23, 2026 patch, La Orzeca Paint now drops as loot in these monster zones: Ash Forest, Olun's Valley, Tunkuta, Thornwood Forest, Crypt of Resting Thoughts, Thornwood Forest [Dehkia's Lantern], Tunkuta [Dehkia's Lantern], Ash Forest [Dehkia's Lantern], Ash Forest [Dehkia's Lantern II], Olun's Valley [Dehkia's Lantern], and Olun's Valley [Dehkia's Lantern II]. ## Patch notes Sep 23, 2026: items, Blessing of Agris Scroll

The Blessing of Agris Scroll is an item sold by Agris's Avatar in Velia and Angavu Outpost. Its effect is Item Drop Rate +50%, which applies beyond the default 300% limit, so Item Drop Rate can reach up to 500% including that 300%. Duration is 60 min and cooldown is 60 min. Price is 1 billion silver, with a purchase limit of 10 per week that resets every Monday at 00:00. It can be bought from after the September 23 (Wed) maintenance until the October 22 (Thu) maintenance. During that period a login challenge also gives Blessing of Agris Scroll x2 once per Family. Unused scrolls are removed during the November 5, 2026 (Thu) maintenance.

## Patch notes Sep 23, 2026: monsters, Event Horizon

Event Horizon monster zone changes in the Sep 23, 2026 patch: the special mechanics no longer trigger at the first wormhole. The chance of the special mechanics at the third wormhole was increased by 1.3 times. If the second wormhole did not produce the special mechanics, the chance at the third wormhole becomes 2.1 times the normal chance.

## Patch notes Sep 23, 2026: UI, ship and Barter improvements

Ship and Barter quality-of-life changes in the Sep 23, 2026 patch:

- Aboard a big ship, you can use the wharf remotely without disembarking: chat, accept and complete quests, buy and sell at the shop, mount equipment and Durability repair, recover mount stats from the Stable/Wharf UI, and use Repair and Supply, Manage Sailors and Load Cargo. Conversation, mount check-in and remote collection are not available.
- Wharf Manager repair and supply are now one button, and storage items can be sold from the Load Cargo UI.
- Barter Information now shows the distance to each barter route and has a Nearby Town/Wharf button, so you can set navigation without the World Map. The Nearby Town/Wharf button was removed from Mount Info for big ships.
- Ship navigation routes are more natural, which mitigates mounts stopping while using Auto-Move (T).

## Patch notes Sep 23, 2026: UI, Treasure Codex

The Sep 23, 2026 patch added the Treasure Codex, opened from Menu (ESC) > Adventure (F5). It lists treasures, including their Masterpiece Quests, with their types, crafting materials, how to obtain them and crafting methods. It records the first completion date of each completed treasure, including treasures completed before the update, and shows completed treasures in full color. Progress can be viewed according to the key crafting materials you hold, and choosing Monster Zone Info or How to Obtain shows where materials drop, and once you have all materials click the Yaz's Combinables Pouch button to open the pouch with materials registered automatically. A Treasure Codex button was also added to Allan Serbin's Landscape, showing the monster zone info for the treasure materials from that zone.
