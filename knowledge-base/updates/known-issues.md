# Known Issues

The official list of in-game known issues that are being investigated, as of the page's last edit on Oct 8, 2026. The developers keep updating it as issues are found and resolved.

## Known issues overview

The Known Issues page lists in-game known issues that the developers are investigating and working to resolve. It is updated continuously as issues are discovered and resolved. It has three parts: Latest Known Issues, Current Known Issues, and a long history of Resolved issues. The page was last edited on Oct 8, 2026, so the issues below may have been fixed since.

## Latest known issues: items, fairies and guild

Latest known issues about items, Fairy skills and guilds (page edited Oct 8, 2026): Guild Officers and Secretaries cannot summon the Guild Boss Gumiho & Duoksini. The Sealed Rare Book of Combat (7 Days) cannot be used with the Fairy's Continuous Care skill. The [Event] Bountiful Autumn Pear cannot be sold. This was to change during the Oct 15, 2026 (Thu) maintenance, after which the item can be sold for 1,000,000 Silver.

## Latest known issues: events and quests

Latest known issue about the event quest "[Event] [Weekly] Decisions and Results" (page edited Oct 8, 2026): the quest cannot be restarted if you hand over the [Event] MOD Oog Power Core and then forfeit the quest, or if the item's validity period expires. The item needed to complete the quest can be obtained with coupon codes MARN-IQUE-STIT-EM01 and MARN-IQUE-STIT-EM02. Each coupon gives [Event] MOD Oog Power Core x1 and is valid until Oct 29, 2026 (before maintenance).

## Latest known issues: trading and Agris Fever

Latest known issues about trading and Agris Fever (page edited Oct 8, 2026): since the Oct 8, 2026 (Thu) maintenance, selling trade items, including Imperial Crafting Delivery items and Fish, grants less Trading EXP than intended. The developers will review the records and grant affected Adventurers the Trading EXP they did not receive. Also, the changes to Agris Fever cost from the Sept 3, 2026 (Thu) update were not applied. They were to be applied during the Oct 15, 2026 (Thu) maintenance.

## Current known issues: quests and questlines

Current known quest issues: during "[Hermesia] Key to the Inner Castle", combat fails to start when you arrive at a particular objective, and walking a bit further from it makes combat start as usual. In the Seraph Ascension quest in Gamepad Mode, the input key for the "Sacramentum" skill shows as [] instead. For Warrior, Ranger, Sorceress, Berserker, Ninja and Kunoichi characters that finished Succession quests before the Awakening & Succession questlines were combined, the Quest window (O) shows an uncompletable Succession questline in the unaccepted state. A character at level 59 with 99.99% EXP cannot level up by receiving EXP as a quest reward.

## Current known issues: monster zones, Karma and Black Shrine

Current known issues in monster zones and dungeons: frame rate drops may occur in the 'Event Horizon' monster zone. In Honglim Base and Dokkebi Forrest, certain classes get Karma penalty messages after killing monsters, though the Family's actual Karma does not go down. In the Gumiho Boss Blitz of Black Shrine - Donghae (Solo), a resurrected character can die again immediately, and is moved to Yeowoo Cave on revival. AP is not properly decreased when it rains or snows.

## Current known issues: items, enhancement and Treasure Codex

Current known issues about items: equipping Vahmalkea Matchlock applies DP and Damage Reduction effects, so the item has been temporarily disabled. Those effects will be adjusted in line with the changes in the Life Skill integration update. The enhancement gauge and success or failure visual effects may not appear when enhancing items in certain circumstances. The Treasure Codex can show a wrong First Completion Date for Oceanteared Ebenruth's Nol in certain situations. Visual effects occasionally look unnatural with the Fairy skill "Morning Star" or the "Atanis Firefly" item.

## Current known issues: interface and gamepad

Current known interface issues: containers installed in residences do not appear in the list of the Contribution Point Usage UI. Private/Personal Trade cannot be used with the gamepad UI. In the Processing (F2) window you cannot scroll down the items list when the client language is English. Background terrain appears abnormal when the camera direction changes.

## Current known issues: launcher and Black Desert+

Current known launcher and app issues: the Black Desert launcher can fail to open correctly while programs that track your PC's status in real time are running, and the developers are looking into it. You can temporarily solve this by closing those programs. Under certain circumstances, an error message appears when you log in to Black Desert+ through Steam.

## Resolved known issues on the Known Issues page

The Known Issues page also keeps a very long Resolved issues section listing issues that have been fixed over time. This note does not list them. To check whether a past problem was fixed, read the Resolved issues part of the official Known Issues page.
