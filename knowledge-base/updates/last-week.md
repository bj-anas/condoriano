## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: Inner Edania main questline

The Sep 17, 2026 patch added the Inner Edania main questline, the final chapter of Black Desert Act 1, set in Inner Edania where the gods Agris, Markthanan, Elion, Aal, and Hadum appear. It becomes available after completing the Outer Edania main questline, and a character of Lv. 60 or above can finish it. Playing through it grants a large amount of Agris Gold Coins once per Family. The starting quest is [Edania] A Secret Meeting from Black Spirit - Main Quest. It requires Lv. 60 or above, the knowledge "Purest Union", and having completed "[Edania] The Pact". If any character in your Family completed "[Edania] The Pact" and your Family has "Purest Union", you can start immediately. Tip: during quests, the "Auto-skip lines in sync with audio (TAB)" option, found at the bottom right, advances dialogue along with the voice-over without keyboard input.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: Tachyon Legacies and Inner Edania Adventure Log

The Sep 17, 2026 patch added 20 new Tachyon Legacies and Traces throughout Inner Edania. Tachyon Legacies start at locations marked by a blue effect, and discovering one and completing its trial gives Agris Gold Coins and special rewards. Tachyon Traces XLI was changed so it can be completed after progressing the Inner Edania main questline. The patch also added an Inner Edania Adventure Log under Menu (ESC) - Adventure (F5) - Adventure Log. It has 7 stories to complete in order: Doors of Alyaelli, Aphrodon, Hermesia, Magaia, Aresion, Voidekaia, and Angavu Outpost. Selecting a story guides you to its starting point.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: Hadum boss subjugation in Inner Edania

The Sep 17, 2026 patch added the Inner Edania boss subjugation against Hadum, with a weekly Event Horizon boss subjugation quest. You must have accepted the weekly quest, and you need 428+ AP and 520+ DP. Entry requires gear of red grade or better on weapons, defense gear and accessories; artifacts and alchemy stones do not count. Heuklang cannot be mounted. Only one throne can be challenged per week (Outer Edania counts toward this), and you can hold just one weekly boss subjugation quest from the Inner and Outer Edania sets. After accepting the quest, an exclusive UI lets you enter a private challenge area. The weekly quest [Weekly] For the Throne: Voidekaia (displayed AP and DP 428/520+) gives an Edana Rising Token - Voidreach and an Edania: Voidreach Chest.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: Edania: Voidreach Chest rewards

The Edania: Voidreach Chest from the weekly Hadum quest [Weekly] For the Throne: Voidekaia gives all of these when opened: Piece of Edana x7, Choose Your Twilight of the End III x4, Embers of Ynix - Helmet x1, Armor x1, Gloves x1 and Shoes x1, Fusion Shard x2, Nev's Fragment x2, Caphras Essence x70, Perfume of Envy x7, Perfume of Tenacity x7, Gold Bar Box x5, and Gold Bar 1,000G x12. At a low probability it can also give Apeiron Necklace, Apeiron Ring, Apeiron Belt, Apeiron Earring, Silent Crystal of Origin x1 and Distorted Crystal of Origin x1.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: Throne of Edana for Event Horizon

Defeating the Event Horizon boss in the Sep 17, 2026 patch qualifies a player to become the region's ruler, the "Edana". Each Sunday, other Adventurers challenge the selected Edana, and the last one standing takes the Throne of Edana with special privileges and rewards. Apply with the Edana Rising Token - Voidreach from the weekly quest. Entry needs AP 428 and DP 520 or higher with red-grade or higher gear equipped. Only Dawn Crystal - All AP transfused is allowed, which also applies to the existing Edania thrones. Edana benefits: Accumulated Tax base amount 25,000,000,000 Silver, Marni's Realm Entry Scroll price 150,000,000 Silver, tax rate 0.5%, plus the Crown of Voidreach, sent to Angavu Outpost Storage. The token can only be used in the application period active when obtained and expires three hours after acquisition. The application period, battle rules and privileges match Outer Edania. Event Horizon was added to Menu (ESC) - Throne of Edana.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: Maehwa Succession changes

In the Sep 17, 2026 patch, Maehwa Succession got more PvE damage, mainly on wide-range skills, and smoother combos. Main Weapon, Succession changes: Prime: Blooming cooldown changed from 15 sec to 12 sec, so its Skill Add-on tier changed from 3 to 2. It now triggers faster, and with LMB, when comboed after Decapitating Dragon. The "All Evasion -12 for 10 sec on hits" effect moved to Blooming Stride and the bleeding damage effect was removed. Prime: Whirlwind Cut and Ultimate: Whirlwind Cut have no character collision while moving laterally. Decapitating Dragon combos into Whirlwind Cut and Dragon Bite flow more naturally. Backstab's attack area now matches Prime: Blind Thrust. Razorblade Corolla has a weaker brief slowdown between attack 3 and the last attack. The developers will monitor PvP effects.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: Archer changes

In the Sep 17, 2026 patch, Archer was adjusted because he dealt more damage than other offensive classes in small and large PvP. Common, Ascension: the Watcher skill's Down Attack, Air Attack, Back Attack and Critical Hit Extra Damage bonuses changed from +15% to +10% for 30 sec, while All AP +20, All Accuracy +48 and Critical Hit Rate +100% for 30 sec stayed. Core: Uproot: Super Armor after attack 1 now lasts only until landing. Mountain Breeze: Super Armor lasts until the end of the landing motion, and Stamina no longer recovers during the entire skill motion. These changes make it harder to keep defensive effects up and recover Stamina for long periods.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: contents, Lejenti and Distorted Energy

The Sep 17, 2026 patch added Lejenti, an NPC at Angavu Outpost who removes the blessing of the god effects. Adventurers with 413 AP or higher can obtain Distorted Energy from Lejenti. Using it at once strips the blessing of the god effects that come from the Vestiges in Aresion, Scales of Judgment and Event Horizon, which frees you to apply other stat-enhancing buffs. Distorted Energy is bound to the character, and your Inventory (I) can hold at most 3 of it.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: items, Apeiron recipes and Causality Shardstones

In the Sep 17, 2026 patch, the Processing recipes that crafted Apeiron accessories by Heating Twilight of the End accessories were removed, because crafting and enhancing Apeiron accessories can now be done directly with Twilight of the End. You can still heat Apeiron accessories to get Twilight of the End accessories. New Processing (L) - Heating recipes turn Causality Shardstones into Twilight of the End accessories: Necklace, Earring, Ring or Belt, each giving x5 of the matching Twilight of the End item. Shardstones must be in your inventory to process.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: items, new Arsha's gear for Trial Characters

The Sep 17, 2026 patch added new Arsha's gear for Trial Characters, bought from Haz in the Battle Arena. To equip it, complete the quest [Trial Character] Proof of Unrivaled Power, taken from the Black Spirit with a Normal Character, which needs 860+ Gear Score (excluding the Blessing of the Morning buff) and rewards the ability to open Arsha's Chest IV. Arsha's Chest IV holds weapons, armors, accessories and an alchemy stone with Attack / Casting Speed Increase effects, added so Trial Characters feel closer to Normal Characters.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: monsters, Hermesia Inner Castle and Magaia Temple

Monster zone changes in the Sep 17, 2026 patch. Hermesia Inner Castle: when the Black Crystal's energy is unstable and an Enslaved Overseer has been defeated, the delay before nearby Enslaved Miners drop loot was shortened. Magaia Temple, one of the highest-tier zones, got 4 new monster spawn locations (it had only 3), with more braziers for combat. The Marni's Realm entrance moved to an area where spawn locations are concentrated, the Vestige of Everlight was relocated, and the objectives of Magaia Subjugation I to III quests changed. Sinners of Rejection and Sinners of Blind Faith spawn slightly faster, and a repeated animation of Sinners of Rejection was fixed. Monster attack hit delay was reduced in Magaia Temple, Aresion Temple, Scales of Judgment and Event Horizon.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: monsters, Event Horizon

Event Horizon monster zone changes in the Sep 17, 2026 patch: Wormhole objects now spawn no more than two Despair-Consumed Archers at once. Despair-Consumed Edana engages sooner after being attacked, so they are quicker to gather. Spacetime Debris dropped after defeating Ibedor lasts 3.5 times longer, so it can be looted after the mechanic ends. Fixes: Failed Edana and Despair-Consumed Edana not spawning properly, their weapons appearing early, extra monsters not summoned after moving far away, and other Adventurers being able to loot the mechanic that begins after Ibedor is defeated.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: UI, party recruitment and Enhancement Chance

UI changes in the Sep 17, 2026 patch: party recruitment ads disappear automatically from the list after 1 hour (hover the red clock icon for remaining time). A button sets the max Party or Platoon members, the maximum AP/DP for recruitment ads rose to 430/530, and General or Guild is chosen automatically from the current tab. The Infusion UI shows only combat lightstones (Fire, Wind, Earth, Iridescent) for combat artifacts and only Life Skill lightstones (Flora, Iridescent) for Life Skill artifacts. Fame Usage History shows the last 7 days in Guild Usage History. The Find NPC search bar and list moved next to the Minimap. The Enhancement Chance UI shows the current chance on the left, Bonus Enhancement Chance on the right, and Total Enhancement Chance and final success rate at the bottom.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: bug fixes, Life Skill gear and equipment UI

Bug fixes and changes in the Sep 17, 2026 patch for Life Skill gear: equipped Life Skill gear can be found with Find My Item (CTRL+F). Empty Life Skill gear slots have icons and descriptions, and the Life Skill Equipment UI has buttons to hide Pearl outfits and accessories. The Weight description says Life Skill gear weight does not count toward Weight Limit. The knot slot is deactivated unless you wear a Pearl outfit reformed with a Black Spirit's Claw. Pressing ' to return to Default stance then pressing ' again reselects the previous Life Tool. [Hunting] Master Matchlock and [Hunting] Marni Sniper Rifle can be reformed with Gauche Rawr-Rawr's Heart. Fixes also cover Preonne accessory enhancement messages, knots in Patrigio's Apparel Bag, Life Skill gear in Morco's Gear Bag, and the Bear Necessities Knot.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: bug fixes, barter, storage and other systems

Other bug fixes in the Sep 17, 2026 patch: the overweight warning no longer appears when a ship's Weight equals its Max Weight Limit after a barter. Level 4, 5 and 6 Barter goods at Dallae Pier connect after a Barter Refresh. In the Great Ocean, firing a cannon with LMB during Auto-run (T) no longer cancels it. Season items in storage disappear after graduation. Transferring 9 or more full Clorince's Travel Bags into or out of storage in one go no longer disconnects the game. Blessed Perilla's Star and Exalted Perilla's Star no longer apply Increase All Resistances as +5%. Navigation to Cheongsa Island works without a compass. Several World Map UI, Enhancement - Target UI and Pearl Inventory purchase issues were fixed.

## Patch notes Sep 17, 2026: bug fixes, gamepad

Gamepad fixes in the Sep 17, 2026 patch: certain Life Skill artifacts can be equipped from the Ring Menu and by drag and drop from the inventory. The Another Adventurer's Choice button visual effect and auto-open in the Enhancement UI were fixed. Certain Guild League UI functions work again. Use Alchemy Stone no longer remains under Menu in Ring Menu settings. Durability values in the Life Tool Ring Menu are shown as percentages. Equipping Life Skill gear switches to the relevant tab, and the Life Skill Gear Info UI refreshes immediately. The Guild Fame decrease notification no longer closes automatically.
