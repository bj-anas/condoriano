# Season Server & Characters

An overview of Black Desert's season server and season characters: EXP buffs, character creation rules, the Season Pass, leveling, Tuvala gear, graduation and what comes next.

## What the season server is

The Season Server is a special server built to help Season Characters progress quickly. It gives always-on (24/7) EXP buffs: Combat EXP +1,000% (from Lv. 50) and Skill EXP +100%. Its world data is identical to that of normal servers, but monster zones here also drop season-exclusive items, which eases early gear progression. Once a Season Character has advanced far enough, it can move to a normal server whenever you like and carry on playing.

## What a season character is and how to create one

A Season Character can enter Season Servers and receive all season-exclusive benefits, and it levels far faster than normal characters. To create one, choose "Create Season Character" on the Character Selection screen. Rules: you can have only one Season Character per Family at a time, and you need a Season Character Ticket to create one. If you have a ticket, the creation option appears on the Character Selection screen. The ticket is consumed when the character graduates. After graduation the character becomes a normal character, and you can create a new Season Character if you have tickets.

## Season Pass for season characters

The Season Pass is the biggest benefit of a Season Character. It is a progression guide that shows new Adventurers what content to do and how to get stronger. To check it, select the yellow Black Spirit icon beside the minimap, on its left, in the top right corner of the screen; it shows current objectives, progress and available rewards. Just by leveling your Season Character you earn Season Pass rewards such as crystals, Fitness EXP, Skill EXP, mounts and Tuvala gear.

## Season character progression: main questline and crossroad quests

The fastest and most reliable way to progress a Season Character is the main questline. Main quests level you, introduce regions, help you get essential Knowledge and give Contribution EXP. Summon the Black Spirit and select Main Quest (Q), then right-click the quest in the quest widget on the right and press T to auto-run to the objective. Along the way crossroad quests let you choose between story paths. Your choice does not affect progression balance or performance, and the story rejoins the main questline afterward.

## Season character main questline versus simplified questline

The main questline suits new Adventurers: it gives a guided introduction and leads you through suitable monster zones as you level. The simplified questline suits experienced Adventurers: it skips much of the early story, including cutscenes and quest routes, and you advance mostly by hunting monsters and leveling by yourself, with less step-by-step guidance. It is best for Adventurers already familiar with Black Desert.

## Season character gear: Tuvala gear

Season Characters cannot equip normal gear and use Tuvala gear, which is designed to make early progression easier. Basic Tuvala weapons and defense gear can be bought from Blacksmith NPCs in major cities at a consistent price, and enhancing Tuvala gear takes the same Black Stones that normal gear uses. By leveling and completing Season Pass objectives in order, you can get a full PEN (V) Tuvala set made up of a necklace, a belt, rings, earrings, 4 pieces of defense gear, a sub-weapon, an awakening weapon and a main weapon. This is the [HYPERBOOST] route to Tuvala gear.

## Season character graduation

Once you complete every Season Pass objective and claim all rewards, you can graduate. The Season Character becomes a normal character, can no longer enter Season Servers or receive season benefits, and can equip normal gear free of season restrictions, such as Boss gear and Slumbering Origin gear. Some season-exclusive items in Inventory and Storage are deleted automatically according to season rules. Season-exclusive utility gear such as Fughar's Riding Crop is permanently deleted, so keep this in mind before graduating.

## After season graduation: Olvia Academy

After graduating, continue exploring and progressing in the wider world of Black Desert. The wiki strongly suggests joining the Olvia Academy, a program meant for newly graduated Adventurers who want to keep learning the game. Its courses teach essential knowledge and techniques for continuing your adventures.
