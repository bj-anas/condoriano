# Season Progression Basics

How to level and gear a season character on a season server: the main questline, the simplified questline, season gear, the Season Pass and the Seals of Journey exchange.

## Season progression: main questline

The wiki recommends progressing a season character through the main questline. This applies to anyone new to season servers, and also to veterans who are not used to the world of Black Desert. Completing main quests takes you across the world and gives rewards that help progression. Summon the Black Spirit (hotkey: ,) and accept a quest with the Main Quest option (Q). Accepted main quests show in the Main Quest window on the right of the screen. Right-click a quest to navigate to it, then press T (Auto Run) while Navigate is active to run to the destination.

## Season progression: simplified questline

The simplified questline lets a normal or season character skip the main questline up to and including the Mediah questline. You still receive the main rewards each time you reach a certain level, but there are no main quests to level you, so you must find monster zones yourself. It can be a fast way to progress for Adventurers who are well-adapted to Black Desert, but players unfamiliar with the game may find it difficult, so think carefully before choosing it. Once a character on the simplified questline reaches a certain level, it can finish the Season Pass by speaking with the Black Spirit (hotkey: ,).

## Season character gear and Naru gear

Normal equipment is off limits to season characters; only season-exclusive gear and gear from the main questline can be equipped. The usual route is to enhance Naru gear to PEN (V), trade it in for PRI (I) Tuvala gear, and then enhance that Tuvala gear to PEN (V). Tuvala gear can be enhanced up to TRI without failing. If you started in the Land of the Morning Light or Mountain of Eternal Winter and are missing Naru gear, you may not get it until you select all your quest types in the quest window; after that, talk to the Black Spirit to start receiving the gear.

## Season Pass overview

The Season Pass window on a season server gives rewards for character progression. It also works as a guide to the content available on a season server and how to progress further. After every season pass objective is finished, the season character can be converted into a normal character; this conversion is season graduation.
