# Season Server-exclusive Content

Content that exists only on season servers to help season characters progress: Rift's Echo boss scrolls, ancient weapon invasions, the season PvP server and the seasonal Atoraxxion dungeons.

## Season server Rift's Echo boss summon scroll

On a season server you can use the party boss summon scroll item Rift's Echo. Defeating monsters drops Rift's Fragments, which you combine into Rift's Echoes. Using one at a designated location on the season servers spawns violent boss monsters, and defeating them rewards your season character with useful items. Each encounter has three stages. After the first two stages, a special monster may spawn for the third one, and beating it within a time limit gives extra rewards and a chance of better loot. Soloing is extremely difficult, so use Find Party/Platoon (main menu hotkey Esc, under Community (F8), or below your health bar) to find a group of five.

## Season server ancient weapon invasions

Ancient weapon invasions can randomly occur while you defeat monsters in the territories of Mediah and Valencia on a season server. When one starts, the area darkens, a system warning appears, and after 30 seconds a horde of ancient weapons appears. More Adventurers nearby means more ancient weapons and more possible rewards. Defeating all of them and the final boss can give these rewards:

| Reward | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Time-filled Black Stone | - |
| Specter's Energy | - |
| Sharp Spirit Stone of Destruction | - |
| Sharp Spirit Stone of Protection | - |
| Tuvala Accessory Box | up to DUO (II) |
| Artifacts | Marsh's, Kehelle's, Lesha's |
| Purified Lightstones of Fire, Earth and Wind | - |
| Enchanted Scroll | (+20), (+30) and (+40) |
| Gold Bar | 1G, 10G and 100G |
| Soiled Ogre Ring | - |

Dying to ancient weapons can cause death penalties.

## Season PvP server

The season servers also offer PvP content, including a seasonal Red Battlefield. On the Season Arsha Server you can experience PvP battles without penalties: Karma penalties are not applied there when you kill other Adventurers. The wiki's PvP page covers this kind of content in more detail.

## Season Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea

Season characters of Lv. 60 or higher can enter the seasonal Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea dungeon, which needs a party of five. A full party wearing DUO (II) Tuvala gear or better is recommended. You must be on a season server, because on a normal server you enter the normal dungeon. The weekly quest "[Co-op] [Weekly] Vestige of the Rifts Bestowed by Vaha" comes from Fughar: conquer Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea and examine Vaha's Time Capsule for an Atoraxxion: Rift Chest. Weekly quests reset every Thursday at midnight (00:00 UTC). The once-per-Family challenge "[Season] On the Lucretia Dagger!" (defeat Apex Urukios and learn the "On the Lucretia Dagger" knowledge) also gives an Atoraxxion: Rift Chest, claimed after reconnecting.

## Season Atoraxxion: Sycrakea

The second wing of the Atoraxxion dungeon is Atoraxxion: Sycrakea. A season character must be Lv. 60 or higher to enter, and DUO (II) Tuvala gear or higher with a full party of five is recommended. You must be on a season server. The weekly quest "[Co-op] [Weekly] Vestige of the Rifts Bestowed by Syca" is accepted from Fughar with a character Lv. 57 or above: conquer Atoraxxion: Sycrakea and examine Syca's Time Capsule for an Atoraxxion: Rift Chest. It resets every Thursday at 00:00 UTC. The once-per-Family challenge "[Season] On the Corrupt Yolun Dagger!" (defeat Maleficent Centilutos and learn the "On the Corrupt Yolun Dagger" knowledge) gives an Atoraxxion: Rift Chest after you reconnect.

## Season Atoraxxion: Yolunakea

The third wing of the Atoraxxion dungeon is Atoraxxion: Yolunakea. A season character must be Lv. 60 or higher, and TRI (III) Tuvala gear or higher with a full party of five is recommended. You must be on a season server. The weekly quest "[Co-op] [Weekly] Vestige of the Rift Bestowed by Yolu" is accepted from Fughar with a character Lv. 57 or above: conquer Atoraxxion: Yolunakea and examine Yolu's Time Capsule for an Atoraxxion: Rift Chest. It resets every Thursday at 00:00 UTC. The once-per-family challenge "[Season] On the Voidlight Dagger!" (defeat Impassioned Amarillos and obtain the "On the Voidlight Dagger" knowledge) gives an Atoraxxion: Rift Chest after you reconnect (hotkey Y for challenges).

## Season Atoraxxion: Rift Chest contents

The Atoraxxion: Rift Chest from the season Atoraxxion weekly quests and challenges (Vahmalkea, Sycrakea, Yolunakea) can give: Rift's Echo x1, Time-filled Black Stone x320, Advice of Valks (+30) x1, Advice of Valks (+20) x2, a PRI (I) - TRI (III) Tuvala accessory x1 (set probability), and Frozen Tides Black Stone x1 (set probability).
