# Season Graduation

How season graduation works: converting a season character to a normal character, what happens to Tuvala gear and season items, graduation gifts, support funds, and what to do after graduating.

## What season graduation is

Season graduation converts your season character into a normal character, so you can equip normal gear without season restrictions. After graduating, the character can no longer log into a season server. The wiki strongly recommends that novice Adventurers take their time leveling and gearing their season character as far as possible before graduating, even if the Season Pass is already complete.

## Season graduation requirements and process

Graduation is always available, but only after you complete your season character's Season Pass and receive all its rewards. Graduating consumes a season character ticket, and without at least one ticket you cannot create a season character or log into a season server again. If you use the Black Spirit Pass, claim all its rewards too. An unused purchased Black Spirit Pass cannot be used after graduation, and unclaimed rewards are lost, so use it first. When every requirement is met, press the Graduate button in the Season Pass window. The character converts and automatically relogs. Graduation gifts arrive in Mail (hotkey: B).

## Season graduation: how Tuvala gear is converted

On graduation, you can no longer equip season-exclusive Tuvala gear. For each piece of your season Tuvala gear set, the one with the highest enhancement level converts to normal Tuvala gear, and duplicates are removed, except that up to two rings and two earrings convert (maximum of 13 pieces). For example, with PEN (V) and TET (IV) Tuvala Gloves, only the PEN (V) converts. Ties are broken first by Tuvala gear branded with the Item Brand Spell Stone, then by the most recently crafted piece. If your Inventory is full, converted pieces go to your storage in Heidel. Converted Tuvala gear cannot be enhanced further, and all unconverted Tuvala gear is removed.

## Season graduation: items and benefits lost

After season graduation you lose season server entry, the Season Pass, exchange quests, challenges and rewards, and season-exclusive items. These items are removed: Black Spirit Pass, all unconverted Tuvala equipment, Time-filled Black Stone, Rift's Fragment, Rift's Echo, Frozen Tides Black Stone, Boiling Tides Black Stone, Fughar's Riding Crop, Fughar's Fishing Rod, [Season] Secret Book of the Misty Breeze, [Season] Secret Book of the Crimson Fog, Atoraxxion: Rift Chest, [Event] Fughar's Special Season Gift Box, and [Season] exclusive gear such as Militia, Azwell and Elsh weapons. Use or sell Time-filled Black Stones and leveling gift boxes before graduating. If your family has not logged in for 180 days or more, the season character is graduated automatically, unclaimed Season Pass rewards are deleted, and graduation gifts go to mail.

## Season Special Gift from Fughar

A season character at Lv. 10 can earn a Season Special Gift Exchange Coupon on the season pass. It is not in your inventory: it is added to Fughar's Special Season Gift window once you complete the challenge and claim the reward. Talk to Fughar in town to pick special rewards. These are once per family, so you cannot take a reward you already received. The all-new season grants 3 season character tickets, so you can get up to 2 Season Special Gift Exchange Coupons. Adventurers with 2023 Summer Season Characters who could not finish the removed [Season Special Gift] Unstoppable Wave of Dreams quest automatically get an extra Season Character Ticket.

## Gift Box Full of Memories after season graduation

After graduation, the Gift Box Full of Memories arrives in your in-game mail. It contains: a Graduation Cap (wearable, press RMB); Advice of Valks (+60), which adds +60 enhancement chance; Cron Stone x100; [Event] Secret Book of Old Moon (7 Days), which raises Combat/Skill/Life EXP for 7 days and allows skill reset and changing skill add-ons; Golden Graduation Gown, sellable to NPC vendors for 10,000,000 Silver; various scrolls and elixir boxes, such as [Event] Item Collection Increase Scroll (60 min), Blessed Message Scroll (100 min), [Event] Giovan Grolin's Support Scroll and Premium Elixir Box; a [Season] Boss Gear Exchange Coupon; and [Event] Tachros' Spirit Stone. The coupon exchanges converted Tuvala gear for Boss gear one enhancement level lower (for example PEN (V) Tuvala weapon for a TET (IV) Boss weapon), and that Boss gear cannot be registered on the Central Market. Tachros' Spirit Stone gives, when you press U after equipping it, Extra AP Against Monsters +5, Combat EXP +50%, Skill EXP +30%, Attack Speed +4%, Casting Speed +4% and Weight Limit +50LT, with a 5 min duration and cooldown.

## Special Support Funds for New Adventurers

New Adventurers earn Special Support Funds from challenges (press Y for the Challenge Window), up to 10,000,000,000 silver in total. Each of these five challenges rewards Gold Bar 10,000G x2: reach Level 61; complete one main quest (Mediah "Apocalyptic Prophecy", Everfrost "[Everfrost] Beyond the Doors of Alyaelli", or Land of the Morning Light "[Koo Mihyun] Royal Decree"); complete the [The Magnus] Morning's Breeze main quest; achieve 50 hours of Family Playtime; and graduate the Season character by completing "[Leveling Aid] Fughar's Aid" (after graduating, accept it from the Black Spirit's suggested quest while the Graduation Cap is in your inventory or equipped). Gold Bars can be exchanged at any Storage Manager for Silver. A new Adventurer is one for whom 60 days (1,440 hours) have not passed since starting the game, shown by an icon at the top of the screen.

## After season graduation: gear paths and Progression Pass

After graduating, explore regions, fight stronger monsters or Adventurers, or develop life skills. To get stronger, try the quests for guaranteed PEN (V) accessories (daily quests) and guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear (upgrade PEN (V) Tuvala gear via reform stones at a guaranteed rate). After graduation, talk to Fughar to accept "[Leveling Aid] Breaking Limits" to enhance one PEN (V) Tuvala weapon and one accessory to DEC (X). A DEC (X) weapon exchanges for a PEN (V) Blackstar weapon via "[Leveling Aid] PEN (V) Blackstar Weapon", and a DEC (X) accessory for a TET (IV) Kharazad accessory via "[Leveling Aid] TET (IV) Kharazad Accessory". The Progression Pass, started from the icon at the upper right of the screen, guides you through content such as Hunting, Cooking and Gathering with rewards, but cannot be used with a season character.

## Blackstar weapon after reaching level 61 on a season character

A season character that reaches level 61 and has completed the main questline as far as Mediah, Mountain of Eternal Winter or Land of the Morning Light receives 1 PEN (V) and TET (IV) Blackstar weapon, delivered to the Challenge window (Y). You cannot equip it with your season character but can after graduating. This weapon and its challenge are available to all characters, season or not. It is described as the best weapon in the game and a good substitute for a Tuvala weapon. Enhance it to PEN (V) once you have enough Cron Stones.

## Tag Characters and Item Copy after graduation

You can tag two normal characters in your family, both at least Lv. 7, with the "Tag Characters" icon at the upper left of the screen. Tagged characters switch instantly, share their in-game location, and share EXP gains. Leveling with character A stores the same EXP for B, and you press "Get EXP" in the Tag Characters window on B to claim it. Combat/Skill EXP from Black Spirit's Training does not accumulate, and only Skill EXP from PvE combat accumulates. Claiming pauses leveling at Lv. 49, Lv. 56 and Lv. 60 until you press "Get EXP" again, and going from Lv. 49 to Lv. 50 needs the "[To Level 50!] Go Beyond Your Own Limits" quest. From Lv. 56, the Marni's Suspicious Device questline (Quest window (O) > Suggested > [ADV Support] [Lv. 56] Item Copy with Marni's Device!) unlocks Item Copy, which copies all equipped gear, tools, alchemy stones and artifacts into the tagged character's bags using Marni's Unstable Fuel. The copy is a snapshot, so later upgrades are not reflected.
