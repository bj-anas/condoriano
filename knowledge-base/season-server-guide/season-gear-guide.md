# Season Gear Guide

How season characters get and enhance Tuvala gear: the Season Pass rewards that give PEN (V) Tuvala gear, enhancement rules, Agris Essence, DEC (X) enhancement after graduation, and the Shai Artina Sol exchange.

## Tuvala gear for season characters

Season characters cannot use normal combat gear and can only equip Tuvala gear. The recommended path for new Adventurers is to finish the Season Pass and graduate from the season, which hands over a complete PEN (V) Tuvala gear set. With a normal character you can also get Tuvala gear as a reward for [Balenos] Main Story quests and enhance it to PEN (V) yourself. The stats of PEN (V) Tuvala gear match PEN (V) Boss gear.

## Enhancing Tuvala gear: weapons, armor and accessories

Tuvala gear you got from [Balenos] Main Story quests must be enhanced to PEN (V) yourself. Weapons and armor use Black Stones up to +15, and the enhancement level cannot drop on failure. From PRI (I) to PEN (V), Concentrated Magical Black Stones are needed. PRI (I) to TRI (III) has a 100% chance and no level drop. TET (IV) to PEN (V) can drop the level on failure. Tuvala accessories can only be enhanced using the same type of accessory (rings, earrings, necklace, belt) and break on failure. PRI (I) to TRI (III) is 100% with no drop, but TRI (III) to PEN (V) can break the accessory. Graduating after completing the Season Pass gives a full PEN (V) set including accessories, so this path is recommended.

## Tuvala gear Agris Essence limits

When enhancement fails, Agris Essence fills in the Ancient Anvil. When activated, it grants a 100% enhancement success rate for that item. Max storable Agris Essences for Tuvala gear and weapons: levels 7 to 13 store 2, level 14 stores 3, level 15 and PRI (I) to DUO (II) store none (-), TRI (III) stores 4, TET (IV) stores 4. For Tuvala accessories: level 0, PRI (I) and DUO (II) store none (-), TRI (III) stores 3, TET (IV) stores 4.

## Tuvala gear recommended Enhancement Chance stacks

Many Adventurers use these Enhancement Chance stacks when enhancing Tuvala gear, though success rates may vary:

| Grade | Weapons / defense gear | Accessories |
| PRI (I), PRI (I) > DUO (II), DUO (II) > TRI (III) | 100% success | 100% success |
| TRI (III) > TET (IV) | 40 (+10) | 35 (+10) |
| TET (IV) > PEN (V) | 60 (+15) | 40 (+10) |

## DEC (X) Tuvala gear quests after graduation

After graduation, talk with Fughar to accept two quests that let you enhance one PEN (V) Tuvala weapon, one defense gear piece and one accessory to DEC (X). "[Leveling Aid] Breaking Limits" gives Essence of Ascent - Weapon, Essence of Ascent - Accessory and 22 Advice of Valks (+150 x1, +130 x2, +100 x3, +80 x6, +60 x10). "[Leveling Aid] Breaking Limits II" gives Essence of Ascent - Defense Gear and 11 Advice of Valks (+150, +130, +100, +80 x3, +60 x5). Each Essence of Ascent enhances the matching PEN (V) Tuvala piece to HEX (VI) with a guaranteed 100% success.

## Tuvala weapon enhancement from HEX (VI) to DEC (X)

HEX (VI) Tuvala weapons are enhanced to DEC (X) with Concentrated Magical Black Stones:

| Step | Concentrated Magical Black Stone | Enhancement Chance example | Success rate | Cron Stones | Agris Essence |
| HEX (VI) to SEP (VII) | 15 | +100 | 16.50% | No downgrade on failure | 10 |
| SEP (VII) to OCT (VIII) | 25 | +120 | 13.00% | 220 | 11 |
| OCT (VIII) to NOV (IX) | 35 | +140 | 11.25% | 260 | 12 |
| NOV (IX) to DEC (X) | 50 | +160 | 10.20% | 355 | 13 |

A DEC (X) Tuvala weapon can be used in the "[Leveling Aid] PEN (V) Blackstar Weapon" quest to exchange it for a PEN (V) Blackstar weapon.

## Tuvala accessory enhancement from HEX (VI) to DEC (X)

HEX (VI) Tuvala accessories are enhanced to DEC (X) with Essence of Dawn. Each step has a 100.00% success rate with no failure and does not consume Enhancement Chance: HEX (VI) to SEP (VII) uses 10, SEP (VII) to OCT (VIII) uses 20, OCT (VIII) to NOV (IX) uses 25, NOV (IX) to DEC (X) uses 35. Essence of Dawn comes from heating certain TET and PEN accessories or certain crystals from Morning Light monster zones. A DEC (X) Tuvala accessory can be used in the "[Leveling Aid] TET (IV) Kharazad Accessory" quest to exchange it for a TET (IV) Kharazad accessory.

## Shai Artina Sol exchange

Shai characters can swap a PEN (V) Tuvala Sol for a PEN (V) Artina Sol, but only once per Family. Complete "[Shai Talent] The Musician of Light, Shai", then do the quest "[ADV Support] Shai: Artina Sol" by handing over a PEN (V) Tuvala Sol to receive the PEN (V) Artina Sol.
