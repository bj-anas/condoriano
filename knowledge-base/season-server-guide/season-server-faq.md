# Season Server FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Black Desert season servers: basics, season character tickets, questlines, graduation, gear and Tuvala enhancement.

## Season server FAQ: basics and what makes season content special

Season content lets you level your season character and its gear much faster than normal characters outside the season. When you graduate, the season character is not lost: it keeps being playable on normal servers. Creating a new character for the season is helpful even if you have an existing one, since families gain from more characters (for example a bigger energy pool), the Tag Characters feature shares locations and equipment between two family characters, and the season gives rewards and buffs that also help existing Adventurers. A season character can also be played on regular servers, but Tuvala enhancement materials such as Time-filled Black Stones can only be obtained on the season servers. Seasons have no time limit, but if you do not log in for 180 days, your character is automatically graduated into a normal character.

## Season server FAQ: season characters, deleting and converting

You can only have one active season character. After deleting an existing one you can create a new one, but everything on the deleted character is permanently lost, except that the Season Pass stays at the same progress. Season Pass rewards can only be claimed by your active season character and cannot be claimed again by a new one. Move your items into a town storage before deleting. Deleting does not affect your ticket count, because tickets are only consumed when a season character graduates. You cannot convert an existing character to a season character: you must create a new character with a season character ticket. Besides an extra character slot and a ticket, nothing needs preparing, though season characters can equip quest gear and accessories from the main questlines of Valencia, Drieghan, Kamasylvia and O'dyllita, so veterans can prepare those in advance.

## Season server FAQ: Season Character Tickets

Season Character Tickets do not expire, so you can create a season character right away or store the ticket. If you do not log in for more than 180 days, automatic graduation is processed. Tickets are issued periodically and can be obtained when logging in, and also through the Pearl Shop. You can check your tickets in the character creation window. A family can hold up to 5 season character tickets, but only one season character can be active. After graduating, you can create a new season character if you have a ticket. If you have none, wait for the next ticket release or buy one in the Pearl Shop. Every new season character gets its own Season Pass with all rewards. Rewards marked "Once per Family" do not refresh with a new season character, though new rewards may be added or limits raised.

## Season server FAQ: faster leveling and Patrigio's Pocket Watch

To level a season character faster, open the Pearl Shop (hotkey: F3), go to "Black Spirit's Pick" > "Silver Event Items", and buy the [Event] Combat & Skill EXP 530% Scroll (60 min). Use these scrolls in monster zones from Lv. 50 or higher. Fughar's Timepiece was removed and cannot be obtained anymore. Instead, Patrigio's Pocket Watch, obtainable after reaching level 60, instantly levels a character to level 61. At Lv. 60 on your season character you can claim 2 Radiant Start Box from Challenge (Y), which contain Patrigio's Pocket Watch and other rewards. It is available for non-seasonal characters below Lv. 25.

## Season server FAQ: season server goals

The goals on a season server are: 1. create a season character; 2. level up through the main questline; 3. reach Lv. 56 and complete the Awakening & Succession questlines; 4. grow stronger with Naru gear, enhance it to PEN (V), convert it to Tuvala gear, and enhance that to PEN (V) as well; 5. complete the Season Pass; 6. explore the rest of Black Desert. You can also do Life Skill content such as fishing, farming and cooking on the season server.

## Season server FAQ: Season Pass challenges and weekly quests

Season Pass challenges must be claimed in order starting from the topmost challenge, so if you cannot claim a reward, collect the rewards for earlier completed challenges first. Season weekly quests are in the Quest window (hotkey: O) > Recurring > [Season Weekly] Stronger Tuvala Gear, where you pick one quest for a monster zone. Once you complete one, the others become unavailable until the weekly reset on Thursday at 00:00 UTC. The special season reward needs the Season Special Gift Exchange Coupon, given after your season character reaches Lv. 10 or higher and completes the corresponding Season Pass challenge. It is not a physical item but is added directly to Fughar's special season reward interface, and you can also pick rewards from past seasons not yet claimed (only one per season character).

## Season server FAQ: simplified questline versus main questline

The simplified main questline skips the main questline. It focuses heavily on leveling the season character but gives rewards similar to the main questline up to Mediah. It is available to season and normal characters and requires completing the main questline up to meeting Alustin, the resident alchemist of Velia. Once you choose the main or simplified questline by the end of Balenos' main questline, you cannot switch to the other. The simplified questline unlocks Valencia's main questline at Lv. 56.

## Season server FAQ: when to graduate

You can graduate as soon as you complete the Season Pass challenges and claim all rewards in the window, and you cannot graduate until you do. Season monster zones drop seasonal materials such as Time-filled Black Stones in addition to normal loot, and once all Tuvala gear is PEN (V) you can exchange leftover materials for valuable items. Some Adventurers delay graduation to earn silver, while others graduate quickly to start the guaranteed PEN (V) accessory and boss gear questlines. If unsure, the wiki recommends continuing to gather silver on the season server before deciding.

## Season server FAQ: after graduation and Fughar's Letter of Encouragement

After graduation you can try the variety of content and earn rewards via the Progression Pass, and try the "Guaranteed PEN (V) Boss Gear" and "Guaranteed PEN (V) Accessory" questlines. You can also talk with Fughar and accept "[Leveling Aid] Breaking Limits" to enhance one Tuvala weapon and one Tuvala accessory to DEC (X), then exchange them for a Blackstar weapon and a Kharazad accessory through "[Leveling Aid] PEN (V) Blackstar Weapon" and "[Leveling Aid] TET (IV) Kharazad Accessory". Fughar's Letter of Encouragement is only a congratulation letter with no use, so keep or delete it. It shares an icon with the Season Special Gift Exchange Coupon, which is the item exchanged for a special season gift.

## Season server FAQ: Quit Season

If you return after a long time and are still in the season, you can use the Quit Season function in the Season Pass to start a new season. Quitting consumes 1 Season Character Ticket. Only Tuvala gear is converted into normal gear, and all other seasonal items are deleted. Quitting removes access to the Season Server, Season Pass rewards, Season content, challenges and progress event rewards.

## Season server FAQ: PEN (V) boss gear and accessories

The "Guaranteed PEN (V) Boss Gear" and "Guaranteed PEN (V) Accessory" questlines can be done after graduating from the season server. The Guaranteed PEN (V) Boss Gear questline requires TET (IV) Tuvala gear from the season servers. You can also obtain one piece of PEN gear after reaching level 60 from the Challenge (Y) window.

## Season server FAQ: Season Pass gear challenges and durability

Some Season Pass challenges require enhancing Naru or Tuvala gear to a certain level. If you enhanced above the required level, summon the Black Spirit and speak with it to complete the challenge. If Tuvala gear is at 0 durability and cannot be enhanced, talk to a town blacksmith, choose Repair > "Recover Max Dur." and use Time-filled Black Stone, or use unenhanced Tuvala gear of the same type, to recover max durability.

## Season server FAQ: Time-filled Black Stones and Tuvala accessories

Time-filled Black Stones drop from all season server monster zones, in larger amounts in Valencia, Kamasylvia, Drieghan and O'dyllita. They also come from weekly quests, monster-zone quests, Rift's Echo and Season Pass rewards. Tuvala accessories can be destroyed when enhancement fails after TRI (III). Raise your Enhancement Chance and try spare accessories first. The Season Pass gives a PEN (V) Tuvala Ring at Lv. 58 and a PEN (V) Tuvala Earring at Lv. 60, so you only need to enhance one of each to PEN (V). Cron Stones can prevent destruction.

## Season server FAQ: multiple Tuvala gear sets

When a season character graduates, only one full set of Tuvala gear converts, with one piece per gear slot (two for earrings and rings), choosing the highest enhancement grade for each slot. All other Tuvala pieces are deleted automatically. Tuvala gear cannot be enhanced outside the season, so enhance it to the maximum before graduating.
