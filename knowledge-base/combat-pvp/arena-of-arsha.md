# Arena of Arsha

The Arena of Arsha in Black Desert Online: a guild-hosted PvP arena where an operator reserves the arena, invites players, sets rules and runs matches.

## Arena of Arsha overview

The Arena of Arsha lets guild leaders host their own PvP tournaments. A guild master reserves the arena for a full day through the Arena of Arsha window in the main menu (hotkey: Esc), invites Adventurers, and runs either a team match or survival mode. Reservation is paid with guild funds, but any Adventurer holding an invitation can enter, whatever guild they belong to.

It differs from Red Battlefield and other large-scale battles because players can form teams ahead of time, and the host decides rules such as the time limit and the victory conditions. Spectators watch but cannot take part directly.

## Arena of Arsha how to reserve

Only operation rights holders can host a match, and those rights can go only to the guild master, advisors and guild staff. To reserve, open the main menu, then War (F7), then Arena of Arsha. The window explains reservation and shows the status. Reservation options appear only for Guild Master, Advisor and Staff ranks.

Pick a day that no other guild has booked yet. The fee is 10 million silver from guild funds, covering the whole day. You can cancel anytime, but no refund is given. The arena runs separately on every server, so if a date is taken on your server, check another one.

## Arena of Arsha server limits and operation rights

The arena cannot be used on the servers where Conquest Wars take place:

| Serendia - 1 | Calpheon - 1 | Mediah - 1 |
| Valencia - 1 | Balenos - 1 | |

On your reserved day you must claim operation rights: open the Guild window (hotkey: G) and click the "Host AOA" button on the main page. These rights cannot be transferred or returned. After claiming, you can enter and set up the arena, invite Adventurers and choose the match rules.

## Arena of Arsha invitations and roles

Inside the arena, open the Arena of Arsha window again to reach the Arena of Arsha Settings window. Enter a name and click "Invite" on the left. Choosing "Invite Spectator" lets the person enter as a spectator, skipping the waiting list, while "Invite Suboperator" brings them in with suboperator rights. To promote an invitee afterward, right-click the name and click "Promote". A suboperator can invite people and remove them.

| Role | Invite | Remove | Change Teams | Match Setting | Spectator Mode |
| Operator | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Suboperator | yes | yes | no | no | no |
| Spectator | no | no | no | no | no |
| Invitee | no | no | no | no | yes |

## Arena of Arsha teams and entry lock

Invitees appear on a waiting list, and the fighters get sorted into teams. The operator can right-click a name and pick the change-teams option, or the invitee can choose their own team. Players can return to the waiting list before the match starts.

The lock icon in the top left corner of the settings window stops anyone from entering, even invited Adventurers. Operators, suboperators and invitees can walk around the arena freely, but their skills are restricted and other characters are hidden until the match starts, when you see only yourself and the participants. Invited spectators watch from the spectator's bench.

## Arena of Arsha match types

After teams are set, the operator picks the match type. Team Match pits 2 teams against each other. Team Survival Match lets several teams fight together, and the last teams standing win. A third format, Individual Battle (Team Battle), has each team member fight in 1:1 duels.

## Arena of Arsha general rules

- On entering the arena, all buffs (food, elixirs, furniture effects and so on) are removed and your Black Spirit's Rage becomes 0%.
- Buffs from your own character skills still work.
- Normal potions and battlefield-exclusive potions are both allowed.
- Black Spirit's Rage and Emergency Escape reset every round.
- Effects that lower maximum HP and heal over time apply, so you cannot heal above a reduced maximum.
- In individual battles, consecutive winners get a maximum HP reduction that grows stronger with each victory.
- Should time expire, each team's remaining members' HP percentages are added up, and the higher total wins.

## Arena of Arsha Team Match rules

- Participants are split into 2 teams.
- A team that wipes out the other wins the round, and the first team to reach the target score wins the match.
- Anyone who dies stays dead until the round ends.
- Before the match, everyone is teleported to the arena starting point.
- After each round, all participants are resurrected and returned to their starting point. When the match ends, results are announced and the dead are resurrected.

## Arena of Arsha Team Survival Match rules

- Several teams fight at once, and surviving teams win.
- A team counts as alive while at least one member lives.
- There is a single round, and the dead stay dead until the match ends.
- The operator sets the victory condition as a number of teams left standing. For example, a setting of 2 teams means the match goes on until two teams remain.

## Arena of Arsha Individual Battle rules

- Two teams are formed and each member fights 1:1.
- The operator can mark one member of each team as captain.
- At the start, each captain has 30 seconds to choose who fights. If they do not, a member is chosen automatically.
- The winner of a 1:1 stays in to keep fighting.
- A team with no survivors that can no longer send fighters loses.

## Arena of Arsha starting the match

Click "Start Match" and participants are told the match begins after 20 seconds of prep time. In Team Match, participants are teleported to starting positions and can buy battlefield-exclusive potions and repair gear from NPCs there. In Team Survival Match, participants are teleported into the open arena with no time to buy or repair, so they must prepare before the operator starts. At the start you can buy potions and repair at the AOA Guard NPCs.

After the 20-second prep, team names appear above heads, HP of participants shows on the left, and the current score and remaining time show in the middle of the screen. The next round starts automatically after 20 seconds of prep. The operator's rules decide the winners.

## Arena of Arsha health limits during a match

When 1/3 of the match time has passed, every participant's maximum health is capped at 80%, and health potions and restoration skills recover only 80% of their usual amount.

When 2/3 of the match time has passed, anyone above 50% of maximum health loses the amount over 50%. Potions and recovery skills heal 50% less, and nobody can heal beyond 50% of maximum health.

## Arena of Arsha operator controls and spectating

Operators can pause or stop a match in progress and click "Resurrect All" to revive everyone who died.

Operators, suboperators and Adventurers on the waiting list can press F5 during a match to enter Spectator mode, which shows the match from participants' viewpoints. F5 or F6 cycles between participants, and F7 returns to your own character.

You can also watch in observer mode, which gives a free camera that ignores obstacles. Lean on a wall by standing with it behind you and walking backward into it, or sit on a bench by pressing Q near one, then press R.
