# Blue Battlefield

Blue Battlefield in Black Desert Online: a guild naval PvP mode fought with ships over forts on islands, including how to apply, rules, ships, guild skills and rewards.

## Blue Battlefield overview

Blue Battlefield is a guild-based PvP mode where adventurers fight from ships for control of forts on islands. Guilds work together to capture and defend forts and to sink enemy guild carracks and galleys. To win, occupy a fort created for Blue Battlefield and defend it until the timer ends.

Main rewards: a guild tax of 2 to 4 billion Silver, loot boxes holding items such as up to Crow Coins x5,000, Oquilla's Origin Stone, Deep Sea Crystal Box and Gold Bar 5,000G, and family buffs of up to Life Skill Mastery +75, Life EXP +40% and Sailor EXP +30%.

## Blue Battlefield how to join

The Guild Master, or a member with the "Apply for Node War" authority, applies with the Blue Battlefield button (Balenos Sea). Find it under Menu (ESC), then War (F7), then Node War. Applications close up to 1 hour before the start time on war day.

Participants are a maximum of 20, the same limit for guilds and alliances; a War Hero cannot take part. Participating guilds are finalized 15 minutes before the battle, and you can check the list in the Node War menu. Guild members can change their participation status under Guild (G), then the Guild Member Status tab.

## Blue Battlefield schedule

War days are Sunday to Friday; Saturday is excluded. War time is 1 hour: 20:00-21:00 CEST/CET for EU and 18:00-19:00 PT for NA. The battle is held on the server O'dyllita-1.

## Blue Battlefield participation opportunity

Joining Blue Battlefield or Node War needs a Node War Participation Opportunity. It is renewed every Saturday following the Conquest War. You can hold only one at a time, and using it in either Node War or Blue Battlefield means you cannot join the other content. Applying for Blue Battlefield therefore stops you joining Node War that day.

For Blue Battlefield, a spent opportunity is refunded if your guild or alliance has a combined total of 10 or more allied or enemy ships destroyed, or temporarily occupies a fort at least once. If it is not returned after you join, you cannot join Node Wars or Blue Battlefield until the reset.

## Blue Battlefield rules: location, forts and duration

Blue Battlefield takes place in the Balenos Sea. Forts appear on islands depending on how many guilds take part.

| Fort tier | Islands |
| Tier 5 | Marlene, Balvege, Baremi, Mariveno, Eveto |
| Tier 4 | Invernen, Angie, Duch, Weita, Luivano |
| Tier 3 | Al-Naha, Paratama, Narvo, Louruve, Marka |

Battles last 20 to 60 minutes, with a randomized end time, and some forts show a "?" end time. As in Occupation Node War, fort placement depends on the number of participating guilds and on guild and alliance members' participation status 15 minutes before the war. You cannot switch your status then. If a guild or alliance cancels its Node War application, all its members' status resets to "No". Track active forts and time left with the World Map and the widget on the right.

## Blue Battlefield rules: occupying forts

Other guilds can attack a fort and reduce its durability. The guild that depletes it temporarily occupies it. Attacks by characters do negligible damage, so use ship cannons. When a fort's timer ends, the guild or alliance occupying it becomes its final owner.

Each guild or alliance can secure only one fort per day. If a guild holds several, only the first one it held until the end counts and the others turn neutral. When the final remaining fort still has over 10 minutes on its timer, the display changes to a question mark and a fresh random end time is chosen.

## Blue Battlefield ships

Large ships that can take part:

- Guild Galley / Improved Guild Galley: carries many members on the inner deck and port and starboard sides. Crew on cannons fire at forts and ships. Only the Improved Guild Galley has an inner deck and the Cap'n's Orders minigame, which can grant Fair Winds: BreezySail for mobility. Galleys take little damage from ships but do massive damage to forts, and fixed forts are easier to hit than moving ships.
- Epheria Galleon: personal ship, available for a limited time through special barters. No crew, tougher plating than Epheria Carracks, similar firepower. It can be overwhelmed by several carracks.
- Epheria Carrack: versatile personal ship. Its cannons can damage forts but it is best for escorting friendly galleys or destroying enemy galleys.
- Panokseon: personal ship with high durability and a large cabin for crew. Its skill Death's-Pivot: SmoothTurn rotates the ship in place and fires port and starboard cannons alternately.
- Other ships: Destroyers, Traders, Improved Epheria Frigates, Epheria Sailboats and similar can also be used.

## Blue Battlefield system changes for ships

- The Oquilla Current has no effect here. Movement stats of ships (Speed, Accel, Turn, Brake) drop 30%, or only 15% for the Epheria Carrack - Volante.
- Ships destroyed or collected remotely neither lower sailors' satiation nor cause illness, but trading and barter goods are lost.
- Ship repair items and mount resurrection items are blocked on the battle server while the war runs.
- Annexes and siege weapons cannot be built in the battlefield.

## Blue Battlefield Assemble and resurrection

An "Assemble!" button shows at the top right 10 minutes before the start. Using it sends each guild to a random spot in one of three areas: Velia, Iliya Island or Lema Island. A ship you have out is remotely collected to the local wharf, with no sailor satiation loss or sickness, but trading or barter goods on board are destroyed. Keep guild ships in the Guild Wharf. With no ship out, use the Wharf Manager's Transport function to fetch stored ships.

If your ship is destroyed, you revive at a wharf through a special UI: Velia (Olvia Academy Grounds, Olvia Coast), Iliya Island or Lema Island. Your character can also resurrect instantly when it dies. Names of other adventurers are visible from farther away, and you can set a color for each participating guild.

## Blue Battlefield rewards: guild funds and family buffs

Guilds that secure a fort at the end receive node tax as guild funds by tier.

| Fort tier | Guild funds |
| Tier 5 | 6 to 8 billion Silver |
| Tier 4 | 5 to 7 billion Silver |
| Tier 3 | 4 to 6 billion Silver |

Participants and winners get family buffs for 48 hours. The Victory Buff goes to all guild or alliance members who secure a fort. The Participation Buff is for guilds or alliances that fall short of final victory but did both of these: temporarily held a fort at least once, and reached 10 or more allied and enemy ships destroyed combined.

| Buff | Effects |
| Victory | Life Skill Mastery +75, Life EXP +40%, Sailor EXP +30% |
| Participation | Life Skill Mastery +50, Life EXP +30%, Sailor EXP +20% |

The family buffs from Blue Battlefield and from Node Wars do not add together. Only the latest one is active.

## Blue Battlefield rewards: Bluewave Loot Box and other containers

A Bluewave Loot Box gives one of these at set probabilities: Oquilla's Origin Stone x1, Crow Coin x5,000, x3,000, x2,000 or x1,000, Gold Bar Box x1, Manos Necklace, Earring, Ring or Belt x1 (up to DUO), Perfume of Deep Sea x5, Perfume of Courage x5, or Concentrated Magical Black Gem x10.

Pirates' Sunken Treasure Chest drops at a low chance from the final occupation and participation boxes. It contains Crow Coin x5,000 and a [Guild] Goldmont Pirates' Pure Gold Goblet, which sells at the Guild NPC Shop for 10,000,000,000 Silver of Guild Funds.

Deep Sea Crystal Box holds yellow-grade or higher Sea Crystal materials. One of these comes out at set probabilities: 1 to 3 Origin of Margoria, 1 to 3 Origin of Rusalka, 1 Margoria Crystal, or 1 Rusalka Crystal. Oquilla's Origin Stone turns Margoria Sea Crystal into Margoria Crystal, or Rusalka Sea Crystal into Rusalka Crystal, via Simple Alchemy, one of each per craft.

## Blue Battlefield rewards: top contributor and titles

The top contributor of the winning guild gets extra: Crow Coin x1,000 and Secret Book of Florin x3.

Titles last 7 days after you get them. Naval Hero requires destroying 10 ships in Blue Battlefield, Fleet Commander requires 25 ships, and Naval Weapon requires 50 ships.

## Blue Battlefield rewards: Wavelit Crystal

Wavelit Crystal is stored in the Manage Currency UI, and the amount depends on your results: Tier 5 fort gives 20 (+10 bonus for a Question Mark Fort), Tier 4 gives 17, Tier 3 gives 15, and a participation reward gives 15.
