# Guide to Annexes

A guide to the annex buildings used in Node and Conquest Wars in Black Desert Online, and a step-by-step walkthrough for building a Node War fort and its annexes.

## Annexes in Node and Conquest Wars overview

Joining Node and Conquest Wars means building annexes as well as forts. Each annex has its own role and helps in taking down enemy forts. However, the grids you can occupy and the number of annexes you can build are capped by the Node War tier, or by the fort type in Conquest War. So plan which annexes to build as you set up your fort. Each annex below lists the grids it uses.

## Barricade and Flimsy Barricade annexes

A Barricade (1 grid, Node and Conquest War) has no special function but has more durability than other annexes. It blocks enemy entry and is placed around the fort or near key annexes. Guild elephants or horses charging at it can break it. Place barricades so they limit movement through gaps, rotate them precisely with the [,] keys, and with enough grids put up to 10 around an installed Indomitable Flag. You can craft a Sturdy Barricade at Siege Weapon Workshops, and once a war starts you can reinforce with Iron Barricades.

A Flimsy Barricade (1 grid) serves the same purpose with lower durability but faster construction. It is a useful temporary measure under constant attack and, unlike a Barricade, cannot be expanded.

## Recovery Center annex

A Recovery Center (1 grid) shortens the resurrection time of allies in Node and Conquest Wars by 6 seconds per center. Its durability is relatively low, and Cannons are especially dangerous to it. The base resurrection time is 10 seconds and rises by 1 second for every minute of the war and for each player's death, to a maximum of 60 seconds. Keep installing Recovery Centers throughout the war to keep the tempo of combat.

## Cannon Observatory annex

A Cannon Observatory (3 grids) gives a Lynch Cannon Assembly Kit 5 minutes after you interact (R) with it once the war has started. Place the Lynch Cannon on high ground or rocks to strike enemy forts and annexes. Load its mount inventory with Cannonballs and Flame Cannonballs bought at the Supply Depot. Buying a Siege Weapon Factory with Contribution Points (CP) raises their damage.

The cannon is very effective against defensive annexes such as Flame Towers and Hwachas. Enemies it hits get the Knockdown debuff. You can set as many Lynch Cannons as you have Cannon Observatories, and already installed cannons stay even if the Observatory is destroyed, so protect your Observatories.

## Elephant Nursery annex

An Elephant Nursery (5 grids) raises elephants for Node and Conquest Wars. After the war starts, interact (R) to start production, and about 5 minutes later you get a Guild Elephant. Only guilds with the guild-exclusive Guild Elephant mount can use it. Elephants are large with high HP and use Charge and Stomp, but are very vulnerable to Flame Tower and Hwacha attacks. You need a recovered elephant in the guild stable to use the nursery. An elephant that lost 100% health can be restored at the nursery for some guild funds.

## Flame Tower annex

A Flame Tower (3 grids) is a defensive annex that a player rides directly (R). Pressing attack (SPACE) releases a cone of flame hitting all enemies in front, doing heavy damage to approaching enemies, guild elephants and horses. In Node Wars at Tier 3 or higher, and in Conquest Wars, it can be upgraded to a stronger Enhanced Flame Tower with proper materials. Each attack uses [Guild] Flame Tower Oil from the Supply Depot. Enemy Lynch Cannon bombardment damages it heavily. Before the war, choose the best people to man it and fill it with oil in advance.

## Hwacha annex

A Hwacha (3 grids) is a defensive annex that a player boards (R) and aims with control keys to fire a ranged circular attack from far away. In Node Wars at Tier 3 or higher and in Conquest Wars, it can be upgraded to a Big Hwacha with the right materials. It uses [Guild] Arrow for Hwacha from the Supply Depot and hurts groups of enemies, but Lynch Cannon bombardment damages it heavily. It needs precise aiming, so place it on a rock or somewhere with clear sight. It is less durable than the Flame Tower, so put it where allies or Flame Towers can support it.

## Supply Depot annex

A Supply Depot (5 grids) lets players recover wounded horses, repair equipment, and buy traps, building tools and Flame Tower and Hwacha supplies without returning to town. Put it behind the expected enemy attack routes, ideally within range of defensive annexes like a Hwacha or Flame Tower, so it stays protected.

## Wooden Fence Gate annex

A Wooden Fence Gate (1 grid) is a fortified entrance to a fort, usually paired with Barricades. Open and close it with the interaction key (R). It is tougher than a Barricade but weak against guild elephants or charging horses. Allies and enemies can both use it, so close it after use.

## Indomitable Flag Factory annex

An Indomitable Flag Factory (3 grids) produces an Indomitable Flag. After the war starts, interact (R) with it and you obtain the flag about 10 minutes later. Once installed, the flag is a forward base where fallen allies can resurrect. Flags already collected or installed stay active if the factory is destroyed. If you made several flags beforehand, you can re-install a destroyed one immediately.

## Conquest War Wooden Fence and Defense Tower annexes

The Wooden Fence (1 grid) is the Conquest War counterpart of Node War Barricades. It is taller, with footholds at the back so players can climb up and fight approaching enemies. It has high durability and resists enemy and cannon attacks, but is vulnerable to guild elephants, charging horses and especially the Ballista. Flimsy Wooden Fences can be built quickly in emergencies. Conquest Wars allow more annexes, so build the fort with a wide perimeter.

The Defense Tower (0 grids) attacks nearby enemies by itself with no player mounting. Its durability is low. Because it uses 0 grids, install as many as you like, mainly outside the Wooden Fences.

## Conquest War Ballista Workshop and Siege Tower Factory annexes

The Ballista Workshop (5 grids) produces the Conquest War siege weapon, the Ballista. After the war starts, interact (R) and you get a Ballista Assembly Set after about 15 minutes. Ballistas hit enemy Wooden Fences and Gates hard. Ballista Arrows from the Supply Depot go into the mount's inventory, and arrows you craft yourself do more damage. A mount hit or a Black Spirit's Rage skill can deactivate an installed Ballista. It moves and rotates slowly, so install it once the area is safe.

The Medium/Large Siege Tower Factory (3/5 grids) produces Medium or Large Siege Towers, exclusive to Conquest Wars. Interact (R) after the war starts to get one 5 or 10 minutes later. Towers are slow, so install them on smooth terrain beforehand, and summon them at some distance from the enemy fort.

## Node War fort construction: choosing the war and preparing tools

First decide which Node War region and schedule to join. Each region has its own war days and tiers. Check them with the Node War filter at the top right of the world map (M) and pick the nodes you want.

To build a fort, buy the Node War fort and annex assembly tools from the Guild Military Supply Vendor NPC in a nearby town. Find the vendor by clicking the magnifying glass icon at the left of the Minimap, then the "Guild" button. Bring more than 20 Assembly Tools.

## Node War fort remote installation

You can build the fort either by going to the Node area, or by using the Node War Fort/Annex Remote Installation feature, handy when the node is far away. It requires being on the Node War server you want to join, being in a safe zone, and being your guild's Cannoneer, Quartermaster or higher.

1. Apply the Node War Information filter at the top right of the world map (M) and click the node.
2. Move the camera with WASD, move vertically with Q and E, move fast with SHIFT and slowly with Caps Lock.
3. Click the fort item at the spot you want.
4. After construction starts, place annexes around it the same way.
5. Press "Confirm Installation" to build all placed annexes at once.

## Node War fort location tips

The fort is both your guild's base and the enemy's target. Good locations have these traits:

1. Close enough to attack and move around while guarding against intrusion, such as high ground or a narrow entrance.
2. Hard for enemy siege weapons to hit and easy to predict, such as where elephants cannot enter or cannons cannot reach.
3. Defensible from one or two sides, such as the end of the node area or limited access points.
4. Near objects that help defense, such as barricades or buildings guild members can climb.

Terrain works both ways, so watch for advantages that enemies could use against you.

## Node War fort installation and grids

Install the purchased fort by right-clicking it in your inventory and placing it in the Node area. Light blue marks allowed spots and red marks unavailable ones. The fort finishes by itself in about an hour and cannot be demolished once complete. If the war starts during construction, it completes immediately.

Hover over your fort on the world map to see how many grids are available for annexes, which depends on the Node War tier. Tier 1 allows a maximum of 30 grids, and the grids each annex uses can be checked in the War Installation Info UI and while using assembly tools. A Tier 3 example fort had 40 grids.

## Which annex to build first: fences, barricades and supply

No fixed order exists, but a suggested start follows.

1. Wooden Fences first. The Wooden Fence Gate (1 grid) is a passage for allies and a wide outer annex. Its height can block enemy cannons depending on terrain, so consider enemy attack routes and likely cannon positions.
2. Barricades around the fort and the gate, placed so enemies cannot squeeze through gaps, rotating with the [,] keys.
3. A Supply Depot on the opposite side from the expected attack route, for resurrecting mounts, repairs and building other annexes.

## Which annex to build first: defenses, specialist annexes and finishing

4. Defensive annexes such as the Flame Tower. Enemies can still surround the fort, so build 2 Flame Towers facing opposite directions: one protecting the Supply Depot from behind and one blocking attacks from the front.
5. Other annexes on the outskirts and inside the Barricades: an Elephant Nursery for Valencia or Cadria elephants, a Cannon Observatory for cannons, and an Indomitable Flag Factory for quick revival at the outpost. Space is limited by the Node War tier.
6. Surround the key buildings with Barricades and Recovery Centers as grids allow. You can buy a Sturdy Barricade through the Siege Weapon Workshop. Using every grid on barricades is not required, and terrain can replace some of them. Indomitable Flag poles can be rotated and used as relatively strong barricades.

When construction is done, pre-load Flame Towers and Hwachas with oil or arrows. Buildings that craft items or mounts, such as the Elephant Nursery, Cannon Observatory and Indomitable Flag Factory, need "Start Function" selected through interaction after the war starts.
