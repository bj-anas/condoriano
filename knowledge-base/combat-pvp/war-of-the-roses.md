# War of the Roses

War of the Roses in Black Desert Online: a 300 vs 300 faction war across Kamasylvia and O'dyllita, with leading guilds, the Third Legion, sanctums, the tactical map, weapons, penalties and rewards.

## War of the Roses overview

War of the Roses is a large-scale event of 300 vs 300 set across the regions of Kamasylvia and O'dyllita. It blends PvP and PvE: players fight for an assigned faction, control key locations and defeat the enemy's commander NPC to win. There are no stat limits, but a minimum gear score is required.

## War of the Roses how to join

Apply through the main menu (ESC), then War (F7), then War of the Roses. There are two ways in. The Leading (Captain's) Guild is for guilds and alliances that apply to command a faction, and The Third Legion is for individual adventurers who apply as mercenaries. Once a guild is selected as a Leading Guild, its members join with the "Participate" button on the War of the Roses server, like Node and Conquest War. Factions are randomly assigned, Kamasylvia or O'dyllita.

## War of the Roses schedule

The war is held on the O'dyllita-1 server once every two weeks, and the schedule may change during preseason. Each war lasts two hours on a Sunday. NA time is 03:00 PM to 05:00 PM (PT) and EU time is 17:00 to 19:00 (CET/CEST).

| Step | NA (PT) | EU (CET/CEST) |
| Apply for Leading Guild (about 24 hours) | Fri 11:00 PM - Sat 11:10 PM | Sat 01:00 - Sun 01:10 |
| Leading Guild announcement | Saturday 11:10 PM | Sunday 01:10 |
| Apply for captain and Third Legion (about 14 hours) | Sat 11:10 PM - Sun 01:05 PM | Sun 01:10 - Sun 15:05 |
| Captain and Third Legion announcement | Sunday 01:05 PM | Sunday 15:05 |

By Sunday 01:05 PM (PT) or 15:05 (CET/CEST), a Leading Guild (Alliance) once announced has to lock in how many of its members take part and name its captain. A confirmed captain cannot be swapped later.

## War of the Roses conditions and costs

| Topic | Leading (Captain's) Guild | The Third Legion |
| Participants | 30 to 100 per faction, including alliance members and War Heroes | Max 200 to 270 per faction |
| Condition | The guild or alliance won at least once in Node/Conquest War in the past 2 weeks (lowest tier Node War wins excluded) | Total gear score of at least 700 (AP + DP), using the higher of main and awakening weapon |
| Cost | 5 billion Silver from guild (alliance) funds | 500 Million Silver |
| Selection | Highest accrued points | Random from applicants |

Both costs are refunded if you are not selected. Press "Refund" in the main menu under War, then War of the Roses. Unclaimed fees are lost for good once the next leading guild application period starts.

## War of the Roses Leading Guild points

Each Node or Conquest War victory adds battlefield points, and the guilds or alliances with the most accrued points over the past two weeks are appointed Leading Guilds. Ties for the top are broken at random. Points gained after the first application still count in the final tally.

| Territory | Tier 3 | Tier 4 | Tier 5 |
| Balenos & Serendia | none | none | none |
| Calpheon & Ulukita | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Mediah & Edania | 9 | 12 | 15 |

Winning a Conquest War gives 100 points, whatever the territory.

## War of the Roses filling Leading Guild slots with the Third Legion

If a Leading Guild has fewer than 100 participants, Third Legion applicants fill the remaining slots, but only if the total Third Legion applicants exceed 200 per faction (400 total). Because a Leading Guild needs at least 30 members, at most 70 extra members can be recruited per faction, so Third Legion capacity per faction is 200 to 270. Those recruited get Third Legion rewards.

Example 1: Leading Guild A has 30, B has 70, with 600 Third Legion applicants. Kamasylvia gets 30 + 270 = 300, O'dyllita gets 70 + 230 = 300, and 100 applicants are not selected.

Example 2: same guilds with 100 applicants. Kamasylvia gets 30 + 50 = 80, and O'dyllita gets 70 + 50 = 120.

## War of the Roses rules for guilds, alliances and War Heroes

- Application is separate from nodes and territories occupied in Node/Conquest War. Members of any rank in a Leading Guild can take part.
- You can opt in or out until the war begins, then your status is locked, as in Node/Conquest War.
- After applying as a Leading Guild, you cannot disband the guild, or form or break alliances, until it ends. Opting out before selection frees you. Forming or breaking an alliance resets accrued points.
- You cannot invite or kick members once applied, until the war ends.
- A selected Leading Guild can appoint any number of War Heroes until the war begins, but none after it starts.
- War Heroes of a Leading Guild cannot apply to the Third Legion, and Third Legion applicants cannot become Leading Guild War Heroes.
- You cannot leave a guild without claiming outstanding War of the Roses rewards.

## War of the Roses captain and lieutenants

The Leading Guild's master can appoint a captain or become one. Each faction has one captain. Captains use the tactical map to give orders and set objectives for the Third Legion, use exclusive faction skills, and are the only ones who can set and edit platoons. If no captain is appointed in time, the guild master becomes the captain. A captain must appoint three lieutenants before the war starts or enters without any.

Captains can edit platoons during the war but cannot remove platoon members once it begins, and cannot edit Third Legion platoons with assigned missions. They can follow allies and occupied points on the tactical map and keep issuing or canceling missions (canceling counts a mission as complete). Lieutenants also assign orders and missions to the Third Legion through the tactical map.

## War of the Roses war area and starting positions

The war covers parts of Kamasylvia and O'dyllita. No safe zones exist: the whole map becomes a combat zone. Ornette and Odore Castles have stable keepers and wharf managers at certain spots, as do Old Wisdom Tree and Starry Midnight Port.

When the war begins, participants standing in the war area are teleported to one of three random coordinates inside their faction's castle walls, unless already inside the castle. Everyone must move to the war area to take part. There is no "Assemble" button, and people outside the area, or in Grána or O'draxxia, are not moved.

## War of the Roses faction buffs and ranks

Standing near your faction's commander inside Ornette or Odore Castle grants a faction blessing. Kamasylvia gets Narchillan's Blessing and O'dyllita gets Sephir's Blessing, each Extra AP Against Monsters +30 for 10 min.

During the war your name shows as faction (Kamasylvia: White Rose Legion, O'dyllita: Black Rose Legion), rank and Family name. Rank follows performance, except Captain and Lieutenant, which stay fixed.

| Rank | Requirement |
| Champion | Base rank |
| Elite Champion | 30 kills |
| Devourer | 50 kills |
| Hero | 100 kills |

## War of the Roses server rules during the war

- Black Spirit's Rage resets for everyone when the war begins.
- Guild skills are restricted on the server.
- As in Node/Conquest War, monsters on the server disappear 15 minutes before the start and return 10 minutes after the war ends.
- Using the Archaeologist's Map, Lafi Bedmountain's Improved Compass or Telescope, or the Magnus Abyssal Wells is blocked.
- Non-participants on the server have DP reduced to 0 and can be attacked by participants. Only those with the "War Correspondent" title are unaffected.
- All areas of the war become Combat Zones.
- Consumables such as Pure Black Stones have no special restrictions, but Node/Conquest War exclusive items like Energy of All Creations work only in their own content.

## War of the Roses sanctums and guardians

Occupied sanctums give buffs to your faction and the Third Legion, and act as resurrection points. The sanctums are N1 to N7 (Narchillan), S1 to S7 (Sephir) and the Spirit's Altar in the center. Each faction starts with its first sanctum (N1 or S1) occupied. Other sanctums are neutral at the start, with passive "Faded Relics" and different monsters at each.

To take a sanctum held by the enemy, kill all monsters spawned there, after which an allied guardian monster appears. Guardians are fixed per sanctum, and a new Sanctum Guardian gets a 3 min invincibility buff. You can only capture a sanctum connected to ones you hold. A successful attack on a sanctum that links others frees all the connected ones. Interrupting your chain of sanctums can cost you all buffs.

| Faction | Guardian monsters |
| Kamasylvia | Urugon, Ronin, Narc, Old Mirumok, Fadus Warrior, Fadus Shaman, Fadus Archer |
| O'dyllita | Olun's Golem, Ulutuka, Turo Berserker, Dark Knight, Ahib Salun Wolf Spearmaiden, Ahib Salun Bear Spearmaiden, Ahib Dark Champion |

## War of the Roses Spirit's Altar

The Spirit's Altar grants the "Spirit's Blessing" and should be held before besieging the enemy castle, since castle guards are tough. To take it, pour Black Spirit's Rage into the monsters that appear around it; whichever faction adds the last needed amount takes control. Mounts with high HP, such as the elephant, are recommended for the siege. Occupying it also spawns Ancient Chariots, Ancient Trolls and elephants, and grants a temporary, powerful boon for the castle assault.

Castle guards do not respawn after defeat, so killing them under the Spirit's Blessing is key. The altar activates 10 minutes after the war starts, and after it activates it cannot be re-occupied for 20 minutes. The timer shows on the tactical map.

## War of the Roses Sanctum Stable Keeper Musical Spirits

Stable Keeper Musical Spirits appear at each sanctum when the war begins and return to nature when it ends. You can take out horses and use remote collection or repair, but cannot transport horses to sanctums. Mounts checked in at sanctum keepers go to either the Grána or O'dylita stables after the war, depending on the sanctum. Dead mounts are not transported and can be retrieved from any stable's recovery menu.

## War of the Roses victory conditions

The main goal of both the Leading Guild and the Third Legion is to defeat the opposing faction's commander, Narchillian or Sephir, inside the castle. To win you must defeat that commander. If both are alive at the end, the faction whose commander has the most HP wins, and equal HP is a draw. Both commanders unleash powerful attacks at 60% and 30% HP. Guards on each castle's inner and outer walls are formidable and do not respawn. A Leading Guild leads up to 200 Third Legion members.

Participants see their kill/death record below the minimap, and clicking it opens the War Stats UI. On the tactical map press F5 for it. Non-participants can open it through Menu, War (F7).

## War of the Roses tactical map

The Tactical Map replaces the world map during the war and shows sanctum occupation status and HP, the faction commander's status, and platoon placement. Allies show in blue and enemies in red. Captains and lieutenants see all allies and enemies in revealed areas. Platoon leaders see their own and platoon members' locations, and other members see only their own.

Captains and lieutenants issue attack, defense and suppression missions, which show on the Third Legion's screens with navigation. Captains also use captain-only faction skills.

## War of the Roses Third Legion missions

The Third Legion roams freely, and completing missions from its faction earns more rewards. During the preseason, mission completion rewards are not applied. Missions can be done at platoon level, one at a time, and can be assigned back to back. More successes mean higher rewards.

## War of the Roses weapons

Annexes such as the Elephant Nursery and Cannon Observatory are not used. Five weapons are available from the start: Elephants, Chariots, Trolls, Ballistae and Cannons. Elephants, Chariots and Ballistae appear on each faction's castle bridge, and Cannons appear at set spots. The Ancient Troll siege mount appears only when the Spirit's Altar is occupied. Weapons do not respawn if destroyed and vanish at the end. All allies can use them, and chariots, ballistae and cannons use no ammunition.

| Elephant | Melee | High | Mid | Low vs adventurer; Knockback, Knockdown |
| Ancient Chariot | Short | Low | High | High vs adventurer |
| Ballista | Mid | Low | Low | High vs Sanctum Guardians |

## War of the Roses revival

When you die you pick a revival location on the Tactical Map: your faction's castle or an ally-held sanctum that is not on the frontline. You can choose Quick Revival, which is faster but restores less HP, or Revival, which takes longer and restores full HP. You are invulnerable for 15 seconds afterward. Your mount is moved to your location and revived if it died. Enemies entering castle revival locations get a debuff (lower AP, cast speed and movement speed) and take powerful damage.

## War of the Roses captain tips: before and during the war

Leading Guild members must press "Participate", as in Node War. Since there is no Assemble button, be inside the war area, such as the Old Wisdom Tree, when the war begins to be moved to the castle. Gather your faction's guild members and Third Legion at Ornette or Odore Castle.

The tactical map replaces the World Map (M). Captains should watch it closely and command from a safe spot, since a fallen captain cannot order easily. Rally Forces works at sanctums outside your faction's own. Assign missions often with your lieutenants. HP of each sanctum shows on its icon, pings are set with ALT+RMB and canceled with LMB, and F4 toggles chat.

The platoons form automatically at the start from the pre-arranged setup, and anyone in another party or platoon is moved out. Without missions the Third Legion's focus may fade.

## War of the Roses penalties

You are penalized for any of these:

1. Staying disconnected from the O'dyllita-1 War of the Roses server longer than 10 min.
2. Leaving your character without any movement for over 10 min during the war.
3. Being outside the war area for over 10 min in total, or leaving it 10 times.

Leaving the area shows a warning on the minimap, and over 1 minute outside shows a warning message. You are penalized once that message has appeared 10 times. A penalty means you cannot collect War of the Roses rewards and cannot join the next 4 wars. If the war does not take place, the penalty is not deducted. Penalized members' guild and Family details are shared with their captain and lieutenants for platoon reorganizing.
