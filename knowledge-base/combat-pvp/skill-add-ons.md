# Skill Add-ons

How skill add-ons work in Black Desert Online: unlocking them, applying them at a Skill Instructor, tiers, groups, usage rules, resetting and tips.

## Skill add-ons: requirements and slots

Skill add-ons are extra effects that you attach to individual skills to strengthen them in combat. Level 50 is the minimum to use them. Six add-ons can be applied in total, and each one unlocks at a different level: 50, 52, 54, 56, 58 and 60.

## Skill add-ons: how to apply them

To set a skill add-on, reach level 50 and visit a Skill Instructor NPC in any major city. All of these NPCs offer the same functions, so use whichever is nearest.

1. Talk to the Skill Instructor and choose "Skill Add-ons".
2. Select the skill you want to add an effect to. A skill you use often with a short cooldown is the most efficient choice.
3. Pick the effect from the list.

Each skill can hold up to two different effects, and they trigger when the skill hits a target. Hits from summoned minions do not trigger skill add-on effects.

## Skill add-ons: stacking rules and efficient use

The same skill add-on effect cannot be put on one skill twice, but it can be placed on several different skills. For example, an Extra Damage to Monsters +20 for 7 sec effect cannot be added twice to skill A, but it can be added to both skill A and skill B. Using it on two skills does not lengthen the duration to 14 sec; instead, the original effect is re-applied each time either skill hits.

So think about which skills you combo together, which effects matter most to keep running, and avoid putting everything into one single effect. Spreading across several effects makes better use of the system.

## Skill add-ons: tiers and duration

The effect of a skill add-on is always the same, but its duration depends on its tier. The tier is set by the skill's cooldown at max rank.

| Tier | Skill cooldown rule |
| Tier 1 | 5 seconds or below. |
| Tier 2 | Over 5 sec but below 13 sec, or 13 sec or above when the skill can still be used during cooldown. |
| Tier 3 | 13 sec or above. |

For example, Eradication (I to III) has cooldowns of 8, 7 and 5 seconds by rank; going by max rank III at 5 sec, its add-on is tier 1.

When a skill also has a Prime version, the tier for every variation (I, II, Absolute and so on) is decided by the Prime version's max rank cooldown. For example, Flash Slash has a Prime version, Prime: Flash Slash IV, with a 12 sec cooldown, so every variation of Flash Slash is tier 2.

## Skill add-on groups

Skill add-on effects fall into groups: offensive A and B, defensive, debuffs A and B, and other effects.

| Group | Effects |
| Offensive A (Stat buffs) | Extra AP Against Monsters+, Extra Damage to Humans+, Critical Hit Rate+, Attack/Casting Speed+, Movement Speed+, All Accuracy Rate+ |
| Offensive B (Damage buffs) | Down Attack damage+, Back Attack damage+, Critical Hit damage+, Air Attack damage+ |
| Defensive (Stat buffs) | All DP+, All Evasion Rate+ |
| Debuff A (Stat debuffs) | Extra Damage to Humans-, All DP-, All Evasion Rate-, All Accuracy Rate- |
| Debuff B (Speed debuffs) | Attack/Casting Speed-, Movement Speed- |
| Other effects | Bleeding damage, recover HP per hit, recover resource per hit, Down Smash, Air Smash |

## Skill add-ons: usage rules

Skill add-on effects follow the normal rules for buffs and debuffs.

- When two identical effects are active, the one with the longer duration takes priority, and the shorter one has to run out before it can apply.
- Identical effects with identical durations simply refresh the duration when re-applied.
- A skill's add-on effects and its own built-in effects can be active at the same time.

## Skill add-ons on Prime and Ultimate skills

Skill add-ons are normally chosen for the lowest level of a skill. If you keep leveling that skill and learn the extra skills that branch from it, the add-on effects carry over to those skills too. For example, add-ons on Raging Thunder I also apply to Absolute: Raging Thunder.

## Skill add-ons: what Extra Damage to Humans means

Extra Damage to Humans+ works against all playable characters and against monsters labeled as human. Every playable character counts as human regardless of appearance, including Shai and the pointed-eared Dark Knight.

Monsters in monster zones are split into four types: Human, Demihuman, Kamasylvian and Neutral. An icon next to the monster's health bar shows its type, for example Kagtum Raider. An add-on for extra damage against humans therefore works on human monsters and on all PvP targets (other Adventurers). On a human monster, both the extra attack power against monsters effect and the extra damage to humans effect apply.

## Skill add-ons: resetting and presets

You can change the skill that carries an add-on, or only the add-on effect, by spending Memory Fragment x1. While the Cliff's Skill Add-on Guide or Secret Book of Old Moon buff effect is active, you can change add-ons as often as you like without using memory fragments.

Skill presets in the Skills window (hotkey: K) save your skill add-on effects together with your learned skills.

## Skill add-ons: getting them from an Unrivaled adventurer

There is another way to get skill add-ons. Look for a player whose title reads "Unrivaled" over their head, and interact with that character. Holders of that title have a special ability that lets other Adventurers obtain skill add-ons.
