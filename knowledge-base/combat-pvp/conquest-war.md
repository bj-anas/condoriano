# Conquest War

Conquest War in Black Desert Online: weekly large-scale guild and alliance PvP over territories, with participation rules, Field HQs and Command Posts, points, rewards, buffs, taxes and Dekrogda relics.

## Conquest War overview

Conquest War is large-scale PvP in which guilds and alliances fight for supremacy over the territories of Balenos, Serendia, Mediah or Valencia. The winner becomes the territory's lord, gains its accumulated taxes, and receives personal rewards for members who took part. Losing participants still get rewards.

## Conquest War schedule

The war is held each Saturday starting at 08:00 PM, and it runs for 2 hours.

## Conquest War how to join

To take part, a guild or alliance must have joined a Node War that week and occupied at least one Tier 2 (Limited) or Tier 3 (Unlimited) node. Then install a Field HQ or Command Post 1 hour before the Conquest War starts. Guild members join with the participation button in the guild window (G). No more than 100 members of a guild or alliance can participate. Whoever installed the fort is stuck in the guild until that fort is destroyed.

## Conquest War Field HQs and Command Posts

You must have a Field HQ or Command Post installed to take part. Buy either from the Guild Military Supply Manager for 1,000,000,000 Silver each. They allow different maximum numbers of annexes around them, so the choice matters for your chances. After installation, construction takes 1 hour. You can dismantle it at any time except when the war has begun, when less than 1 hour remains before the war, or after construction is complete.

## Conquest War victory rules

Once the war has begun, the fight follows different rules depending on whether a defending side exists. The offensive side wins if the defensive Command Post or Field HQ is destroyed within 2 hours. The defensive side wins if its Command Post or Field HQ survives 2 hours. If no one defends, all participants fight until a single guild or alliance has a Command Post or Field HQ standing. If 2 or more remain at the end, the territory is liberated.

You can attack both sides with Forced PvP enabled during Conquest Wars without penalties.

## Conquest War with no defending guild

With no defender, victory conditions match Node Wars: destroy every other guild's Field HQ or Command Post while keeping your own. But if two or more guilds remain at the end, the territory is not liberated as in Node Wars. The guild or alliance with the higher score from the point system wins and occupies the territory. Points come from contributing to the war, such as defeating enemies or destroying buildings. The point system is used only when there is no defender and two or more guilds remain.

Score is calculated from each guild or alliance's remaining Command Post or Field HQ durability, the number of Command Posts, Field HQs and annexes destroyed, and ally kill and death counts.

## Conquest War ranking by points and time lasted

At the end of the war, the top 5 guilds or alliances by points, not counting the one that becomes Lord, receive the Burning Spirit Reward matching their ranking. Rewards are also tied to how long each guild lasted. Guilds whose Command Post or Field HQ falls within the first hour get the Fighting Spirit Reward. If fewer than 6 guilds remain at the end, rewards go in the order their Command Posts or Field HQs were destroyed. For example, with 4 guilds left, the top scorer becomes lord and gets the Glorious Victor Reward, and 2nd to 4th place each get a Burning Spirit Reward.

## Conquest War defended castle

When a defending guild exists, the attacking guilds team up to attack its castle. The defensive side wins by holding its Command Post or Field HQ for 2 hours. The offensive side wins if it destroys the defensive Command Post or Field HQ within 2 hours, with the winner decided by points. A victor becomes the territory owner and gains its accumulated taxes.

## Conquest War Merit Rewards eligibility

A total of 200 billion Silver is shared among participating guild or alliance members according to contribution. Amounts round up to the nearest 10,000 Silver, so the total can slightly exceed the pool. The silver goes automatically to the Black Spirit's Safe under "Collectible Silver" after the war, and the Reward Log shows details.

Merit Rewards exist only for the Calpheon, Mediah and Valencia Conquest Wars, since those use an offensive versus defensive setup; Balenos and Serendia do not have them. Only members, War Heroes included, who pressed "Participate" and actively joined the battle qualify. No rewards are paid if there is no defensive guild, if the war ends instantly because only a defensive guild was present, or if the guild's Command Post or Field HQ is destroyed before the war ends. That last rule applies even to offensive guilds with high contribution.

## Conquest War Merit Reward distribution rules

- Defensive success: the defensive side gets 100% of the Merit Reward, split equally among its members.
- Defensive failure: the defensive side gets a share by how long it held out, split equally.
- Offensive success: the remaining pool, after the defensive side's time-based share, is divided among surviving offensive guilds by points, then split equally among members.

Defensive success means every offensive Command Post or Field HQ is destroyed, or the defensive one survives to the end. Offensive success means the defensive one is destroyed before the end.

## Conquest War taxes for the lord

The guild that becomes lord gets the territory's collected taxes as guild funds. Tier 1 pays 30,000,000,000 to 40,000,000,000, Tier 2 pays 50,000,000,000 to 60,000,000,000, and Tier 3 pays 60,000,000,000 to 70,000,000,000. A victor can obtain both the accumulated node tax and the personal victory rewards.

In the first week of occupation, 50% of the accumulated tax is held as a security budget and only the other 50% is received. The security budget shrinks with consecutive occupations, and at 5 or more consecutive weeks the lord obtains 100%.

## Conquest War tax plunder

Every 10 minutes after the Conquest War starts, each guild or alliance in the siege can plunder 2% of the scheduled tax collection, up to a maximum of 20%. For each consecutive week the defending guild holds the territory, the maximum rises by 2%, up to 30% after 5 weeks in a row.

The plundered amount is set by when the last siege guild's Command Post or Field HQ is destroyed, and is shared equally among all participating siege guilds. It is paid only if the defender successfully holds the territory. If a siege guild wins and occupies the territory, no plundered tax is obtained.

## Conquest War Dekrogda relics

Dekrogda relics put a barrier around a castle until all 5 are destroyed. While it is up, Dream and Mythical Horses using certain skills fall at once: Wings of Wind, Wings of Freedom and Wings of Swiftness on Arduanatt and Mythical Arduanatt; Mighty Leap and Flight on Mythical Arduanatt; and Triple Jump on Mythical Diné. Airborne movement skills are restricted throughout the inner castle area. The wave affects both allies and enemies, including the occupying guild's horses.

The 5 relics are placed as follows: two by the outer gate, two by the inner gate, and one next to the Command Post. Destroying all 5 removes the barrier and lets horses use airborne skills freely.
