# Guild League

The Guild League in Black Desert Online: ranked 10 vs 10 guild battles, covering how to apply, matchmaking groups, rules, ranking points, dormancy, matching and rewards.

## Guild League overview

The Guild League is PvP in which guilds fight each other in 10 vs 10 battles. Once the league starts, guilds that applied are paired for matches, and up to 10 guild members who want to fight can join the match against the matched guild.

## Guild League how to join

Press the "Apply" button in the Guild League menu, found either in the Guild window (G) or under War (F7) in the main menu (ESC). A guild member with "Apply for Guild League" authority can apply for matching. Entry is not available in some areas, such as Atoraxxion, Battle Arena and Old Moon Grand Prix. It is also unavailable during the Node/Conquest War period, even if those wars end early.

## Guild League schedule

Applications are open on weekdays from 5 PM to 1 AM and on weekends from 1 PM to 1 AM, in NA PT and EU UTC. Matchmaking is processed at :00, :20 and :40 of every hour, and a match begins once an opponent is found. For example, if you apply at 14:05, matchmaking processes at 14:20.

## Guild League matchmaking groups

A guild can request matches in three groups at once: Guild Defender, Defender Unit I and Defender Unit II. Only Guild Defender results change the league points.

- Guild Defender groups are paired against each other with priority.
- Defender Unit I and Defender Unit II can pair in any order.
- If no opponent exists, a Guild Defender can face Defender Unit I or II, but only the Guild Defender result affects points.

| Group | When to apply |
| Guild Defender | 7 or more guild members online |
| Defender Unit I | 14 or more members online, after Guild Defender has applied |
| Defender Unit II | 21 or more members online, after Guild Defender has applied |

## Guild League rules: participants, duration and victory

These Guild League rules cover team size, match length, how to win and how points are scored.

| Rule | Detail |
| Participants | 7 to 10 members |
| Match duration | 12 min. After the match is set there is a 4-min wait to enter; 1 min after the match ends you leave automatically. You can come and go freely once it starts. |
| Victory | First to 600 points, or the higher score when time ends. If tied, the battle continues and the first guild to get a kill wins. |
| Points per kill | First kill 30 points; each later kill 10 points |

## Guild League rules: revival and penalties

Each guild has one revival point. After dying, characters resurrect automatically and, after a short time in a protective veil, return to the combat zone. There is no revival limit. If you are out of combat on revival, you get Invincibility and a Damage Reduction effect for a time. EXP loss and crystal destruction do not apply.

Penalties: collecting 3 penalties means a 1-week ban from the league. A guild must have at least 7 participants before the match begins or it loses instantly with a penalty. If participants fall below 4 during a match, it counts as a defeat, the match ends automatically, and 1 penalty is issued.

## Guild League other rules

- If you leave mid-match, any guild member can replace you, but damage and debuffs recorded for you reset.
- Members who joined the guild after matching cannot enter an ongoing match.
- The consecutive kill notification lasts 15 sec.
- Equipment durability does not drop during the match.
- Escape cannot be used on the battlefield.
- Consumables are unlimited, except Medical Kits, which cannot be used.
- Install items such as Stun Trap and Cannon, and transformation items, are not allowed.
- You are put in a platoon on entry and leave it on exit. If you are already in a party or platoon, leave it before entering.
- Adventurers who joined as War Heroes cannot see the Guild League menu or join.

## Guild League battlefields

Guild League matches are played on these battlefields: Navarn Steppe, Heidel Northern Guard Camp, Battlefield of Honor, Taphtar Plain and Gyeongbokgung Palace (Geunjeongjeon Hall).

## Guild League rankings and points

Rankings follow Guild League points. A guild starts with 400 points when the league first begins. A win gives +30 ranking points and a loss gives -15, with a bonus adjustment by point range. Matches are made by score range.

| Range | Win | Loss |
| 401-600 | 30 + 3 = 33 points | 15 - 3 = 12 points lost |
| 201-400 | 30 + 5 = 35 points | 15 - 5 = 10 points lost |
| 0-200 | 30 + 7 = 37 points | 15 - 7 = 8 points lost |

## Guild League dormancy penalty

A guild that skips matches for a while at a higher score loses points periodically.

| Points | Penalty |
| 3401 or more | Not playing for 5 days costs 50 points every 2 days |
| 2601-3400 | Not playing for 5 days costs 40 points every 2 days |
| 2001-2600 | Not playing for 5 days costs 30 points every 2 days |

For example, a guild with 3,500 points that last played on Jan 1 and sat out until Jan 6 loses points on Jan 8, 2 days later.

## Guild League matching system

Matching works like this.

1. Guild Defenders match each other first.
2. Defender Units match each other first, whether Unit I or II.
3. With no opponent, a Guild Defender can be matched with Defender Unit I or II.
4. The search begins in the highest score bracket.
5. Guilds in the same bracket get top priority; if several are there, one is chosen at random.
6. If nobody is in the same bracket, the guild closest in score from the bracket above or below is chosen.

| Score brackets | | | |
| 3,801+ | 3,601 - 3,800 | 3,401 - 3,600 | 3,201 - 3,400 |
| 3,001 - 3,200 | 2,801 - 3,000 | 2,601 - 2,800 | 2,401 - 2,600 |
| 2,201 - 2,400 | 2,001 - 2,200 | 1,801 - 2,000 | 1,601 - 1,800 |
| 1,401 - 1,600 | 1,201 - 1,400 | 1,001 - 1,200 | 801 - 1,000 |
| 601 - 800 | 401 - 600 | 201 - 400 | 0 - 200 |

## Guild League regular season final rank reward rules

When the regular season ends, Guild Funds, exclusive titles and Reward Boxes are given by final rank. Final rank rewards go to guilds with at least 10 matches played when the season ends. Guild member rewards go to members who joined the guild at least 14 days earlier. Season titles are kept until the end of the next regular season.
