# PvP

An overview of Player versus Player play in Black Desert Online: stats, crowd control, defensive effects, resistances, penalty-free PvP modes and penalized forced PvP.

## PvP overview and level requirement

PvP means Player versus Player, where Adventurers challenge each other. The PvP features include Node/Conquest Wars, Guild Wars, Red Battlefield and several kinds of arena content. It rewards precise command inputs and fast reactions, and gets better the more you learn how each class's skills work. PvP content only unlocks at level 50.

## PvP stats and abilities

Open the My Information window (hotkey: P) to see Fitness EXP, Ability levels and combat stats. They are not required to enjoy PvP but can make you faster or raise your critical hit chance. Movement and Attack/Casting Speed can reach at most 150% from buffs, ability levels, skill add-ons and other effects, while debuffs can lower them by only 50%.

Fitness EXP comes from walking, running, eating food, carrying a trade pack on foot, and juice items that boost it. Ability levels come from transfusing magic crystals into your Crystal Preset, equipping gear, and using consumables such as dishes and elixirs.

## PvP skill descriptions and filters

Open the Skill window (hotkey: K) and hover over a skill to see its damage, offensive effects, defensive effects, usage, cost and cooldown. Effects marked (PvE only) do not apply in PvP. Turn on the Skill Demo at the bottom of the window to watch the animation and effects. The skill filter icon at the top of the window highlights which skills have offensive or defensive effects.

## PvP crowd-control effects

Skills and consumable items such as traps and flashbangs can crowd-control (CC) an opponent. The CC effects are Stun, Stiffness, Freezing, Knockdown, Bound, Grab, Knockback, Floating, Air Smash and Down Smash. Stun, Stiffness and Knockback stop the target from moving; Freezing also reduces damage taken; Knockdown, Bound and Floating enable extra Air Attack or Down Attack damage; Grab holds the target for the skill's whole motion and counts as only 1 CC; the two Smash effects send a target flying.

## PvP CC count rules and getting up from knockdown

Most CC effects carry a count. Once a character's count reaches 2 or more, it is immune to CC for 5 seconds. Air Smash and Down Smash are excluded from the count.

- Stiffness and Knockback: 0.7 count each.
- Stun, Knockdown, Floating, Bound, Grab, Freezing: 1 count each.

Emergency Escape (hotkey: V) cannot be used while knocked down. The CC count shows when you hit Training Dummies in the Battle Arena. To get up from a knockdown faster, repeatedly tap or hold one of W, A, S, D, press Shift + W, A, S or D, click the left or right mouse button several times, or click both buttons at once.

## PvP special damage types

Special damage types add damage in the right situation.

| Type | Damage |
| Critical Hit | 200%, same in PvP and PvE |
| Back Attack | 150% (120% in PvP) from behind |
| Down Attack | 150% (120% in PvP) on a prone target |
| Air Attack | 200% (170% in PvP) on an airborne target |
| Speed Attack | 150% against a fast-moving target; PvE only |
| Counter Attack | 200% when countering; PvE only |

A skill's description says whether it deals special damage.

## PvP defensive effects

Each class has skills with defensive effects: Invincibility (no damage, effects, buffs or heals from enemies or allies), Super Armor (takes damage but no CC; grabs and debuffs still apply) and Forward Guard (blocks damage and effects from the front, grabs still apply, back is exposed, and its Guard Gauge can break). Using them at the right time blocks enemy attacks and opens a counter chance.

Some classes get Forward Guard when walking backward, called an S-block. Each class also has a guard skill on the default key Q that can have a powerful defensive effect.

## PvP resistances

Resistance gives you a chance to avoid enemy debuffs, starting from a 20% base. When you resist, the skill's damage still lands but the debuff does not, creating a chance to strike back or escape. The maximum resistance that applies in PvP is 80%. So even a target with 100% resistance is calculated at 80% against your attack with base Ignore Resistance. A target hit by 2 or more counts of CC is fully immune to CC for 5 seconds.

## PvP modes without penalties

These PvP modes carry no death penalty:

- Battle Arena: go to the main menu (hotkey: Esc), then War, then Battle Arena at any time. Arenas near Velia and other major cities work the same way.
- Duels: challenge an Adventurer anywhere except safe zones. The duel starts when they accept. You resurrect on the spot, though buffs normally lost on death, such as elixirs, are still lost.
- Red Battlefield: main menu, War, Red Battlefield. Fight for 15 minutes; deaths return you to your team's start point.

## PvP guild and ranked modes without penalties

More modes with no death penalty:

- Node/Conquest Wars: guild only. Your guild builds a fort or Field HQ in an area and fights other guilds there; the last guild with its fort or Field HQ wins. You respawn at your guild's fort or Field HQ. Siege War also exists, where siege weapons attack enemy forts.
- Guild Wars: a guild declares war on another so members can fight without forced PvP. After death you can use an Elion's tear or resurrect at the closest safe zone or closest town.
- Team Battle: a multi-round team arena, usually 1v1 duels with a team round if tied. Guild versus guild versions exist.
- Arena of Arsha: an operator hosts matches through the guild, chooses the mode (Team Match or Team Survival Match) and rules, invites participants and allows spectators.
- Arena of Solare: seasonal matchmaking in teams of 3, best of 5 rounds, with equalized gear and points. Ranked Mode is solo only; practice allows a premade team of up to 3.
- Guild League: ranked 10 vs 10 guild battles using your own gear.

## PvP forced PvP and karma loss

Any character level 50 or higher can press Alt + C to force PvP and attack any Adventurer who is not in your party, platoon or guild. This can cost karma for your whole family: -3,000 for the first blow and -50,000 to -120,000 for a kill. You lose no karma if you defend against someone who attacked first with forced PvP, or attack someone with negative karma. Forced PvP is not possible on season servers. On Arsha (PvP servers), attacking or killing Adventurers does not cost karma.

With negative karma, town guards attack you and dying can drop gear enhancement levels. In all regions a negative karma death can break crystals and lose EXP, and PvE deaths can downgrade gear. In the desert, PvP deaths do not break crystals or lose EXP.

## PvP fugitives in the desert

The desert is lawless. If you force PvP and kill another Adventurer there, you also become a fugitive for 30 minutes on top of the karma loss. Fugitives can be attacked even with karma above 0 and without forced PvP, and fugitives killed by other Adventurers go to jail. To leave jail, work off the crime or dodge past the guards. The mark ends after 30 minutes or when you go to jail, but you stay a fugitive if you die outside the desert.
