# Hardcore Server

The Hardcore Server in Black Desert Online: a survival PvP and PvE mode in the Great Desert with limited daily playtime, Survival Points, Quest Points, offerings and season rewards.

## Hardcore Server overview

The Hardcore server asks players to spend 2 hours a day in the Great Desert. There they earn Survival Points through kills of monsters or other adventurers, and Quest Points through finishing quests. Points are offered at a sanctum and turned into "Hardcore Honor", which is spent on rewards on the live server through the Manage Currency UI. In the pre-season only Warrior, Sorceress, Ranger and Berserker are available.

## Hardcore Server how to join

Connect through Server Selection, then the Hardcore tab. You must first create a Hardcore-exclusive character. Only one character slot exists for it. It starts at Lv. 63 with 3,000 Skill Points, and you can choose Succession or Awakening. Only the Warrior, Ranger, Sorceress and Berserker classes can be created.

## Hardcore Server schedule and playtime

The pre-season ran from Mar 13, 2025 (Thu) after maintenance until Apr 10, 2025 (Thu) before maintenance, and the period is subject to change.

Each Family gets 2 hours of playtime per day. The time starts draining the moment your character enters, and keeps running while you are logged out. For example, logging in at 7 PM and out at 8 PM leaves no time at 9 PM. Playtime resets 23 hours after your last Hardcore login, so a 10:30 AM login lets you return at 9:30 AM next day. You can connect during Node/Conquest Wars. After you make an offering, you cannot play for the rest of the day, even with an hour left; you are logged out to the Character Selection window.

## Hardcore Server gameplay loop

1. Defeat monsters to earn Survival Points and Junk loot. Dying costs 30% of your Survival Points. Sell Junk loot to a sanctum NPC for "Hardcore Coin", which buys items from the NPC shop.
2. Complete quests for Quest Points, which stay even if you die.
3. Offer Survival and Quest Points at a sanctum to earn Hardcore Honor by total points. View it in the Manage Currency UI.
4. Exchange Hardcore Honor for items.

## Hardcore Server Survival Points rules

- Defeating monsters gives points, more for stronger foes. Defeating another adventurer takes 10% of their Survival Points.
- If your Survival Points are 3,000 or more and you defeat an adventurer with under 20% of your points, you take a -30% penalty. For example, with 5,000 points you lose 1,500 by beating someone holding 1,000 or fewer.
- The penalty is skipped in some regions: where bosses Laytenn and Puturum appear, where [Furious] Gahaz Bandits appear, where [Furious] Pila Ku Jail monsters appear, and in [Black Spirit Infused] Aakman Temple and Hystria Ruins.
- Points belong to a character, so deleting and recreating resets them. Character deletion has a 3 min wait.
- Unoffered Survival Points do not count toward the next day's Offering Points, but stay in your total. Earn 500 on day 1 and exit, and next day Offering Points are 0 while total Survival Points start at 500.

## Hardcore Server Quest Points rules

Quest Points come from quests on the Hardcore server, with a large amount on completion. You can take one quest per day, assigned at random by your Survival Points. Under 200 Survival Points the quest is Defeat Ancient Weapons x100-150. At 200 to 1,499, it is to defeat Desert Naga/Fogan or Crescent Shrine monsters. You may forfeit a quest but not change its type.

Quest Points do not vanish on death and do not reset after an offering. If you earn 1,500 Quest Points and disconnect, you begin the next day with 1,500 Offering Points available. A quest completed after midnight blocks starting another for that day.

## Hardcore Server items, death, revival and escape

- Items: Hardcore-exclusive gear is provided, and cannot be repaired or enhanced. Maximum durability differs from the normal server.
- Death: you lose 30% of Survival Points (1,000 points become 700), including from fall damage or monsters. Quest Points stay. Equipped gear can lose Durability at a set probability, while inventory gear is safe.
- Revival: you spawn at one of four designated sanctums on first login or revival. Revival is instant and unlimited. If you do not revive within 60 sec, it happens automatically.
- Escape: ESC > Escape works if you get stuck, and moves you to a safe monster-free area, not a sanctum. Its cooldown is unchanged.

## Hardcore Server rankings rules

Survival Point rankings are tallied periodically and exclude characters with 100 points or fewer. Rankings are per character, and the top 10 adventurers by total Survival Points are marked on the World Map. Server rankings update when you log out or make an offering, and refresh for everyone daily at midnight. Titles and rewards by rank come at the season's end. The ranking window also shows enemies defeated and death counts.

## Hardcore Server system limits

Connecting turns on Privacy Mode automatically. You can reset skills only in sanctums and Mysterious Light safe zones. Skill add-ons are unavailable. You can use the World map and minimap and auto-navigate the desert without a compass. Pets are unavailable, but your character auto-loots at the speed of one Tier 5 Alpha Pet and four Tier 4 "Agile" pets.

Restricted or unavailable: Combat/Skill EXP, opponent Family/Guild names, Party/Platoon, non-server chat and rankings, Food/Elixir buffs, Pearl Outfits, Family Inventory, Storage, Central Market Warehouse, Maids and Butlers, Artifact Inventory, Agris' Fever, Fairy Skills, Crystal Presets, non-server Challenges, worker stamina recovery, gathering interactions, Black Spirit quests and chat, Black Spirit's Safe, Mailbox, Adventurer's Log stat gains, Item Drop Rate Increase buffs, Old Moon Treaty Token, and timed Pearl items. Value Pack's weight limit increase and Inventory +16 slots still apply. Only server chat works, and it uses Energy.

## Hardcore Server daily quests

The first time you enter, take the "Perilous Journey Begins" quest from the NPC Faisal beside the starting sanctum. After that, Faisal gives one random quest a day, never two at once, based on your Survival Points, shown above your character name. If you die mid-quest and your points drop, you can continue it; if you forfeit, you can only get quests for 200 to 1,500 points.

## Hardcore Server safe zones: Pilgrim's Sanctum

Pilgrim's Sanctums are the Hardcore server's safe zones, for taking daily quests, buying and selling items and preparing gear. If you disconnect after an offering, you start at a Pilgrim's Sanctum next login. The four sanctums are Obedience, Abstinence, Sincerity and Purity.

## Hardcore Server Dark Fog

Dark Fog activates when enough adventurers gather in an area. Those inside cannot leave until only one is alive.

- The fog is invisible to people outside, who can still enter at any time and place.
- Inside, you cannot unequip or replace gear, open the map or use Escape.
- Quitting abnormally inside gives a death penalty and revives you at a random sanctum on reconnect.
- When created, the fog teleports those trapped into a separate combat space, and outsiders cannot intrude.
- The last survivor returns to their original location.

## Hardcore Server offering

Offerings convert Quest and Survival Points into Hardcore Honor. After an offering you are logged out and cannot return until 23 hours have passed since your last login. You can offer up to 10,000 points (Survival plus Quest) per day. Make an offering at the Mysterious Light safe zone or at the randomly appearing "Light of Offering"; sites show on the World Map (M). Unoffered Survival Points stay in your total but start the next day as 0 Offering Points, while Quest Points always carry over.

## Hardcore Server merchant, challenges and titles

The Wandering Merchant shows up at random times and places on the Hardcore server to sell rare accessory items. There are Hardcore-only Challenges that award special titles; the highest title you have earned is applied automatically and works only on the Hardcore server. Deleting your Hardcore character resets its challenges and deletes its titles.
