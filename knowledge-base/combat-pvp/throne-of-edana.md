# Throne of Edana

The Throne of Edana in Black Desert Online: a weekly Edania PvP contest to rule a castle region as Edana, covering how to apply, schedule, battle rules and Edana privileges.

## Throne of Edana overview

Defeating a regional Boss in Edania earns the right to become the Edana, the ruler of that region. Every Sunday the Edana must defend the throne against challengers, and battles decide who rules next. The Adventurer who finally holds the throne gets special privileges and rewards.

## Throne of Edana how to apply

Apply by using the "Edana Rising Token", which comes from completing the weekly boss subjugation quest in each Edania region. The application period runs from Monday 00:00 (UTC) until Sunday 00:00 (UTC). The deadline is NA Sunday 00:00 UTC (17:00 PT, 20:00 ET) and EU Sunday 17:00 UTC (18:00 CET, 19:00 CEST).

| Castle | Token |
| Aetherion Castle (Jordine) | Edana Rising Token - Azureach |
| Nymphamaré Castle (Rusalka) | Edana Rising Token - Emeraltides |
| Orbita Castle (Enslar) | Edana Rising Token - Amberwilds |
| Tenebraum Castle (Cartian) | Edana Rising Token - Ebonshade |
| Zephyros Castle (Caphras) | Edana Rising Token - Crimsonroot |

## Throne of Edana application rules

- You fight with the character that uses the token, and an application cannot be canceled.
- The weekly boss subjugation quest resets every Thursday at 00:00 (UTC), which is Wednesday 17:00 PT, Wednesday 20:00 ET, Thursday 02:00 CEST or 01:00 CET. You can accept and complete one weekly quest per week, so after applying for one castle's throne in a week you cannot apply for another.
- The token is bound to your Family when acquired and is used up when applied. If you will not apply, you can sell it through the NPC Shop.
- After applying, you may update the AP and DP shown in the Throne of Edana UI until the 10 challengers are picked on Sunday 00:00. For example, a character that applied with 355 AP and 430 DP can update to 360 AP and 435 DP.
- An Edana can challenge castles in other regions, but cannot compete against the final challenger for the Edana seat in their own castle.

## Throne of Edana challenger selection

Up to 10 challengers are chosen per region. Up to 7 come from the adventurers who used a token for that region, ranked by highest combined AP and DP, with ties going to higher Level and EXP. Up to 3 more are drawn at random from the rest who used the token. Selection happens on Sunday 00:00 (UTC). Chosen players see a special navigation UI at the left of their minimap.

## Throne of Edana entry conditions

Only characters meeting these minimums can enter.

| Castle | Minimum AP | Minimum DP |
| Aetherion Castle (Jordine) | 350 | 427 |
| Nymphamaré Castle (Rusalka) | 375 | 440 |
| Orbita Castle (Enslar) | 385 | 445 |

Everyone must also wear at least Red grade gear in weapons, defense gear and accessories. Artifacts and alchemy stones are excluded from that check.

## Throne of Edana schedule

The contest has two matches on Sunday. Entry opens 5 minutes before each one.

| Match | NA | EU |
| Candidates (10-challenger battle) | Sunday 00:10 - 00:20 UTC, 17:10 - 17:20 PT, 20:10 - 20:20 ET | Sunday 17:10 - 17:20 UTC, 19:10 - 19:20 CEST, 18:10 - 18:20 CET |
| Selection (final challenger vs Edana) | Sunday 00:30 UTC, 17:30 PT, 20:30 ET | Sunday 17:30 UTC, 19:30 CEST, 18:30 CET |

## Throne of Edana Candidates battle rules

Starting 5 minutes before the match, talk (R) to the "Throne" NPC at the castle to enter. Only the character that used the token can enter, and it must meet the castle's AP and DP minimums. The battle is a single free-for-all round among up to 10 challengers, lasting up to 10 minutes or until one challenger is left. Anyone not inside by 20:10 is disqualified and cannot enter later. Challengers wait inside a marked circle before the start and cannot step outside it.

If more than one challenger remains when time ends, the one with the highest remaining HP% becomes the final challenger, with ties going to higher AP and DP, then Level and EXP. A shrinking veil shrinks the area and damages anyone outside it. Emergency Escape cooldown resets when the battle begins. Everyone shows as "Challenger", and a dead challenger can choose Spectator Mode or Exit.

## Throne of Edana Selection battle rules

The final challenger talks (R) to the "Throne" NPC at the castle they applied to, and the current Edana enters through the third option in the UI. Both must use the character that used the token, or for Edana the same character previously chosen, and meet the AP and DP minimums.

- Best of three, first to 2 wins, each round up to 3 minutes.
- Both fighters spawn at fixed spots.
- Emergency Escape cooldown resets 30 seconds before each round.
- If a round has no clear winner, the higher remaining HP% wins, then higher AP and DP, then Level and EXP.
- With no final challenger, the current Edana keeps the throne. With no current Edana, the final challenger standing becomes Edana.

## Throne of Edana match rules and notes

- Everyone fights with their currently equipped gear and cannot change it after entering. Equipment and Crystal Preset changes are not allowed, and Tamers cannot ride Heuklang.
- Matches happen in daytime, like Guild League.
- There are no death penalties such as Combat EXP loss or crystal destruction.
- In the 10-challenger battle, chat is off and all participants show as "Challenger". In the Edana battle, Family names are visible, but a camouflage outfit shows "Unknown Adventurer".
- On entering the match location, all skill cooldowns, Black Spirit's Rage and Emergency Escape cooldowns reset. After entering and before the start, you cannot leave or change your position.
- Pure Black Stone cannot be used and its buffs are removed on entry. Other consumables are allowed, except items like Devil's Elixir and Mermaid's Tear, whose buffs are removed on entry.

## Throne of Edana disconnects and tie-breaks

If you disconnect during a match you are marked defeated. In the Edana selection match you can still take part if you reconnect before a round starts, but you lose any round that starts while you are not inside. If everyone left falls at the same moment, the winner is the one with the highest displayed AP and DP, then higher level, then more EXP. In the 10-challenger battle, guild members appear white on the minimap and hovering shows "Challenger".

## Throne of Edana privileges: karma, drop rate and revival

- No Karma loss: Edana does not lose Karma when using forced PvP (Alt+C) inside their domain, which you can view by selecting the Throne's Dominion in the Throne of Edana UI. Attacked adventurers can counterattack, and Edana cannot force PvP on guild, platoon or party members.
- Higher drop rate: Edana can activate the Edana's Blessing buff (Item Drop Rate +100%) for up to 1 hour, up to 10 times per week. It works on all servers and stacks with Arsha server and Conquest Lord buffs, up to Item Drop Rate +500%. The uses reset when a new Edana is crowned or the term is renewed.
- Instant revival: Edana can revive instantly with no death penalty inside their domain, even with Karma below zero.

## Throne of Edana privileges: crown, server change and teleport

Edana gets a crown for the occupied castle, delivered to the Hakinza Sanctuary Storage. It is a temporary 7-day item that fits the Pearl Outfit Helmet slot. While wearing it, Edana can use World Chat without Energy or a Megaphone, and chat messages show a special icon and colors. The crowns are Crown of Azureach, Crown of Emeraltides, Crown of Amberwilds, Crown of Ebonshade and Crown of Crimsonroot.

Edana can also change servers freely inside their domain with no server change cooldown, and can move instantly to their castle, though not while trade items are in the Inventory (I). The World Map (M) shows each castle's Edana Family name and the taxes the previous Edana collected.

## Throne of Edana privileges: accumulated tax

Entering Marni's Realm in an Edana's domain needs an entry scroll bought with Silver from Failed Edana, found near each castle. A portion of each scroll price is collected as tax, and only Edana can see the total. When Edana is replaced, taxes go automatically to the previous Edana's Black Spirit's Safe, with details by in-game Mail (B). If Edana keeps the seat another term, one week of taxes is distributed automatically. Tax is a set base amount plus a share of scroll prices. No taxes build up in regions without an Edana, and scrolls bought by Edana are not taxed.

| Castle | Base amount | Entry scroll price | Tax rate |
| Aetherion Castle | 3 billion Silver | 50 million Silver | 0.4% |
| Nymphamaré Castle | 5 billion Silver | 70 million Silver | 0.3% |
| Orbita Castle | 8 billion Silver | 80 million Silver | 0.25% |
