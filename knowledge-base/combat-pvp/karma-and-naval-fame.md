# Karma and Naval Fame

How Karma and Naval Fame work in Black Desert Online: what lowers them, what negative values cost you and how to recover.

## Karma: what it is

Every character in Black Desert has Karma, shared across your family. Killing other Adventurers, wild horses or other protected fauna, or getting caught stealing from an NPC, lowers it. Check your value in the My Info window (hotkey: P): the first icon is land Karma and the second is sea Karma (Naval Fame). You can still fight back against someone who attacks you, using the same PvP system. New Adventurers start at 0 Karma, so negative Karma can affect their play quickly.

## Karma: how PvP changes it

- If someone attacks you first, they are shown in red with a knife-shaped icon next to their name.
- The first attacker's family Karma drops by 3,000 points. Attacking again within the 3 minutes of self-defense time does not lower it further.
- Whether or not the victim counterattacks, killing them lowers the killer's Karma by at least 50,000 and at most 120,000 points, depending on the gap between their attack and defense values. A strong character killing a weak one loses more.
- Karma cannot go above 300,000, so killing at least two Adventurers drops you below 0. The lowest possible value is -10,000,000.
- A victim who counterattacks and kills the initiator suffers no penalty.

To attack first, activate PvP mode with Alt+C. Defending against an unprovoked attack does not require PvP mode.

## Karma: reading the color next to a name

The color symbol beside an Adventurer's name hints at their current Karma.

- Light red: 300,000 points or below.
- Brown: 150,000 points or below.
- Red: below 0.

## Karma: consequences of negative Karma

When family Karma falls below 0, several penalties apply.

- Town guards in nearly every city and post turn hostile and attack on sight.
- Your names appear in red and other Adventurers can attack you.
- You lose more Combat EXP when you die.
- Magic Crystals are more likely to break on death.
- Your equipment may lose an enhancement level on death, though that and crystal loss never happen on the same death.

Negative Karma Adventurers can be attacked by anyone even in towns and cities. From 10 pm to 7 am, negative Karma Adventurers are completely safe.

| Negative Karma | All regions | Desert |
| Crystal break | PvE yes, PvP yes | PvE yes, PvP no |
| Loss of EXP | PvE yes, PvP yes | PvE yes, PvP no |
| Chance of gear downgrade | PvE yes, PvP no | PvE no, PvP no |

Dying in the desert softens these penalties because the area is lawless.

## Karma: how to recover it

Karma is recovered by killing common monsters around the world, except those that lower it, namely wild horses and protected fauna. Monsters in Pila Ku Jail (Valencia Region) give the most Karma per kill.

## Karma: Muiquun, the Outlaw City

In Muiquun, guards attack any Adventurer with positive Karma. With negative Karma you can use the NPCs and stay safe for a limited time, though other players can still attack you there. It is a handy resting spot while recovering Karma near Pila Ku, and death penalties do not apply inside. The city has a Stable Manager, Storage and Blacksmith.

## Naval Fame: what it is and what negative Naval Fame does

Naval Fame is Karma for the sea, shared across your characters and separate from normal Karma. Attacking and destroying other Adventurers' ships or boats lowers it. A ship that has flagged for PvP with Alt+C blinks red.

With negative Naval Fame, some Wharf Managers refuse to deal with you and soldiers attack your ship or boat. Only the Wharf Manager on Kuit Island can repair and resupply it. If your ship is destroyed while Naval Fame is negative, at least one of its items drops an enhancement level.

## Naval Fame: how to recover it

Defeat sea monsters with your ship to recover Naval Fame, which can be slow. A faster way is the repeatable quest "[Repeat] Hidden Item" from the NPC Hans in Velia. Each completion restores 2,500 Naval Fame points.
