# Red Battlefield: Valencia City

The Valencia City map of Red Battlefield in Black Desert Online, a team PvP battle where points come from holding five areas.

## Red Battlefield: Valencia City overview

Red Battlefield: Valencia City is the Red Battlefield map set in the heart of the city at the desert's edge. It has urban obstacles such as houses, roofs and stairs, which you can use to your advantage. Any character level 50 or higher can join, with no guild required and any karma. You cannot enter while in a party. Win or lose, you receive Combat EXP, Skill EXP, silver and Battlefield Tokens.

Unlike the older battlefield where kills give points, this map has areas to occupy, and a team scores by keeping them occupied. A player without top gear can still help the team win by leading it well.

## Red Battlefield: Valencia City how to join

Open the main menu (hotkey: Esc), then War (F7), then Red Battlefield, from anywhere in the world. Under "Battlefield: Valencia City", choose the server you want to enter.

## Red Battlefield: Valencia City teams and rules

Adventurers are split into two teams, Black Desert and Red Desert. On entry you see the state of the battle and where each camp and each occupiable area is on the map. The battle starts after a short prep time once each team has at least 20 Adventurers. Adventurers can enter during the first 3 minutes of the battle.

The battle lasts 15 minutes. Five areas can be occupied, and each occupied area gives its team 1 point per second. A gray area is unoccupied, blue is occupied by Black Desert and red is occupied by Red Desert. A team wins by being first to 3,000 points. If nobody gets there, the team with the higher total when the 15 minutes run out wins.

## Red Battlefield: Valencia City death and area states

Dying in the Red Battlefield has no penalty. Your character can resurrect instantly at your team's camp and go back to contest the areas. The UI shows each area's state, whether occupied, contested or failed. Help teammates who are fighting over an area.

## Red Battlefield: Valencia City occupied area debuff

When your team occupies an area, your team gets an Evasion and Damage Reduction debuff while near that area. An icon beside the area info shows it.

| Stat | Reduction near occupied areas | Floor |
| All Damage Reduction | 218 | will not go below 612 |
| All Evasion | 286 | will not go below 1034 |

## Red Battlefield: Valencia City tips and potions

Regular potions work in the Red Battlefield, but the Quartermaster NPC Daz sells battlefield-exclusive potions much cheaper, at 1 silver each and 0.01LT in weight. Daz also gives the Strong Protection buff, which raises your Evasion and Damage Reduction stats.

You can also enter with a Lv. 60 trial character, created instantly without prior gameplay, and buy items from the Red Battlefield NPC Haz.

## Red Battlefield: Valencia City rewards

At the end of a match you receive Combat EXP, Skill EXP, silver and Battlefield Tokens. Victory gives Battlefield Token x25 and defeat gives Battlefield Token x10, both with Combat and Skill EXP. The EXP goes straight to the participating character. Tokens go to your Manage Currency UI (chest icon next to the minimap).

Tokens can be exchanged for items. With 50 Battlefield Tokens you can get a Red Essence, which can be heated with certain magic crystals to make a more powerful crystal.
