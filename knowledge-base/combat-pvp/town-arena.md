# Town Arena

The Town Arena in Black Desert Online: free duel arenas next to major towns where PvP carries no penalties.

## Town Arena overview

Town arenas are simple duel arenas found next to major towns. Anyone can take part just by stepping in and facing another Adventurer in the combat area. PvP is switched on inside, even for members of the same guild and for characters at level 50 or lower.

## Town Arena rules and penalties

The main feature of a town arena is that it has no death penalty of any kind. Dying costs no Combat EXP and destroys no magic crystals, and killing others costs no karma, so you can try PvP without worry.

## Town Arena locations

Town arenas stand just outside these towns:

| Town | Territory |
| Velia | Balenos |
| Heidel | Serendia |
| Calpheon | Calpheon |
| Valencia City | Valencia |

The arena in Altinova works differently: it is in the middle of the city and counts as a combat area by default. To fight others there, you must flag up for PvP or challenge them to a duel, and penalties will apply.
