# Skills

How skills work in Black Desert Online: learning them, the skill window, presets, crowd-control effects,
defensive effects and special damage types.

## Skills: how to learn them and skill points

Skills in Black Desert are learned with skill points, which come mostly from fighting. Defeating monsters and completing quests gives skill experience, and life skills give a small amount. Monster kills and dummy training can give at most 3,000 skill points in total, but quests and adventure journals can push you above that number. Past a certain point, earning more points gets harder.

There are three ways to learn a skill. The first level of a skill is learned automatically once you reach the required level. You can also open the skill menu with hotkey K and left click the skill. Or you can talk to a skill instructor in a city. A crossed knife and shield icon shows how many skills you can currently learn, and the World Map (hotkey M) marks every skill instructor you have met.

Higher skill levels cost more points, so learning everything as early as possible can leave you short for the skills you actually want. Check descriptions and demo videos first.

## Skills: level 55 and below, Awakening and Succession

Skills that require level 55 or below are learned automatically at no skill point cost. Skills and passives that add extra effects to those skills still cost points.

After level 56, finishing the Awakening, Ascension (Archer) or Talent (Shai) quests from the Black Spirit unlocks skills that use a different weapon for stronger attacks. To keep your existing combat style, finish the Succession quests, which empower your earlier skills. Awakening skills only come from the Awakening quests, and Succession skills only from the Succession quests. The order of these quests can differ by class.

## Skills: active and passive types, horseback use

Skills are either active or passive. Active skills are used through hotkeys or by entering precise commands. Passive skills work as soon as you learn them. Some skills cannot go into a quick slot, so you must know their command. A skill without the "Can be added to a Quick Slot" line is command-only; for example, one might be triggered by pressing S and right mouse button together.

Most skills also work on horseback, but you must equip your horse with stirrups first. Horseback commands can differ slightly from the ground versions, so read the skill description.

## Skills: the Skill window

Press K to open the Skill window. Skill demos are on the left and the skills on the right. Succession, Awakening, Talent and Ascension skills are reached from the top-right once you complete the matching quests, although you can still browse the main, awakening and skill enhancement categories before that. Click a skill icon or the playback button to watch its demo, and use the toggle button to hide the demo screen. Main weapon skills are grouped into main, secondary and passive sections that you can collapse.

Left-click an icon to learn a skill. Shift + Left-click keeps learning it up to the maximum level available. Characters under level 56 can reset all learned skills freely. Right-click resets a single skill, and Shift + Right-click sets it back to level 1.

Two buttons at the lower right let you turn off Black Spirit's Rage for individual skills and choose which skills appear in the cooldown slots of the combat UI.

## Skills: skill presets

A skill preset saves your learned skills and your skill add-ons. More preset slots come from the Skill Preset Coupon. Completing each character's Awakening and Succession quest (Talent or Ascension for Shai and Archer) rewards two Skill Preset Coupons. If you already finished those quests, take the Skill Preset Coupon quest called Using a Variety of Skills from the Black Spirit to get a coupon.

You can save a preset anywhere at any time. Loading one requires either the Secret Book of Old Moon or a Skill Instructor NPC. With the Secret Book of Old Moon you can load at any place, in safe zones and combat zones. Loading has a 1 minute cooldown and plays a character animation.

## Skills: crowd-control effects

Many skills carry extra effects, and combining them makes fights easier. A skill with a Down Attack bonus deals more damage to a knocked-down enemy, in the same way a Back Attack hits harder from behind. These are the crowd-control (CC) effects:

| Effect | What it does |
| Stun | Target cannot move or use skills for a fixed time. |
| Stiffness | Target cannot move or use skills for a fixed time, shorter than a stun. |
| Freezing | Target is frozen, takes reduced damage, and is knocked down when it ends. |
| Knockdown | Target falls on its back; takes extra damage from Air Attacks in the air and Down Attacks on the ground. |
| Bound | Target falls on the spot; takes extra Down Attack damage; shorter than a Knockdown. |
| Grab | Target is held for the whole skill motion, then gets the second CC from the skill description. Counts as only 1 CC. |
| Knockback | Target stutters backwards and cannot move for a time similar to Stiffness. |
| Floating | Target floats then falls; extra damage from Air and Down Attacks; shorter than a Knockdown. |
| Air Smash | Powerful attack that sends an airborne target flying. |
| Down Smash | Powerful attack that sends a grounded target flying. |

Some enemies are naturally always in the air or on the ground, so knowing this helps.

## Skills: defensive effects and special damage types

Skills can apply defensive effects:

| Effect | What it does |
| Invincibility | Target takes no damage, effects, buffs or heals from enemies or allies. |
| Super Armor | Target takes damage but no CC; grabs and debuffs still apply. |
| Forward Guard | Blocks all damage and effects from the front (grabs still apply); attacks from behind still hit; has a Guard Gauge that can break. |

Special damage types raise damage in the right situation:

| Type | Damage |
| Critical Hit | 200%, based on critical hit chance; same in PvP and PvE |
| Back Attack | 150% (120% in PvP) from behind |
| Down Attack | 150% (120% in PvP) on a prone target |
| Air Attack | 200% (170% in PvP) on an airborne target |
| Speed Attack | 150% against a quickly moving target; PvE only |
| Counter Attack | 200% when countering the target's attack; PvE only |

## Skills: using commands versus quick slots

Most active skills can be used by command, which makes combat more dynamic. You can also drag skills into quick slots for a more relaxed pace. Command use can activate some skills faster and slightly lowers resource cost (WP, MP, SP) compared with quick slots. Commands also let you chain skills into combos. Skills that can link into combos appear in the Action Combo Guide beside your character. Turn this guide on or off through the main menu (hotkey Esc) under Edit UI. On a gamepad you can set your own shortcut keys.

## Skills: skill add-ons requirement

Skill add-ons are extra effects that you attach to individual skills to help in combat. In the Skills system, they are unlocked at level 50 or higher. The separate Skill Add-ons note explains how to apply and reset them.
