# Arena of Solare

The Arena of Solare in Black Desert Online: 3 vs 3 matchmade PvP with equalized gear, covering modes, schedule, rules, ranking points, penalties, replays and rewards.

## Arena of Solare overview

The Arena of Solare is 3 vs 3 match-based PvP in which gear differences do not matter, because everyone uses the arena's own equipment. In the game's story it was an old friendly tournament between Calpheon and Valencia that has faded from history. There are ranked and practice matches, plus custom matches with friends. A season carries ranks, an Emblem of Solare, and rewards.

## Arena of Solare ranked mode and schedule

In Ranked Mode your ranking points rise with wins and fall with losses. Ranked matches are open only at certain hours, and a team of three characters of the same class is not allowed.

## Arena of Solare practice mode and schedule

Practice Mode is the Arena of Solare without ranking-point pressure. Rewards are smaller than ranked season rewards, but a loss costs you nothing. You can find a practice match even outside a ranked season: main menu (hotkey: Esc), War (F7), Arena of Solare, then [Find Match]. You are matched against players of similar skill.

Practice hours: NA 17:00 - 08:00 (next day) UTC, or 10:00 - 01:00 PT; EU 10:00 - 01:00 (next day) UTC, or 12:00 - 03:00 (next day) CEST.

In practice mode you can see opponents' defense effects (Super Armor, Forward Guard and so on) over their heads, and a team may have three characters of the same class. You can also play in a party of 2 or 3.

## Arena of Solare practice party and custom matches

For practice party matching:

- A party of up to 3 people can start a practice match.
- All party members must be on the same server before the party leader starts matchmaking.
- During matchmaking, any member can cancel the search.
- While searching you cannot invite members, change the party leader or leave the party. Matchmaking is also canceled if a member switches servers or characters, or disconnects.

A custom match lets you play with friends. Create it in the Arena of Solare UI, invite participants directly and assign the teams.

## Arena of Solare how to join

You can enter from level 60 with a character that has completed its Succession or Awakening questlines. Season and non-season characters can both take part. Open the Arena of Solare menu through the main menu (hotkey: Esc), then War (F7). Ranked Mode is open only during its set hours, and Practice Mode is available outside them.

You must set your exclusive gear loadout first, using the [Set Gear] button next to your family name on the menu. You can also pick the crystals to socket into the gear. Then press [Find Match]. If all 6 participants accept once a match is found, you enter the battlefield automatically.

## Arena of Solare rules: equalized stats

Besides equal gear, the arena bans potions, meals, elixirs and similar items. Recovery effects, AP, DP, Accuracy and Evasion are applied by set ratios. Your default HP/MP/WP/SP recovery is greatly raised, to about the level of Ornette's or Odore's Spirit Essences with a Fairy that has the skill Miraculous Cheer V. Certain skills are adjusted, and the Guard Gauge is equalized across all classes.

Active meal, elixir and perfume effects are nullified and their remaining time paused on entry, and they resume when you return after the match. Inside, max HP rises by 10,000, and Critical Hit Rate, Attack, Casting and Moving Speeds are set to level 5. On entry, Black Spirit's Rage, HP, MP, SP, WP and cooldowns are fully restored. Emergency Escape cooldown resets each round. Only the arena-exclusive "Elemental trace" buffs apply for skills, gear and battlefield, and Family Adventure Log stats do not apply.

## Arena of Solare rules: rounds and winners

- A team cannot contain duplicate classes in ranked play.
- The first team to win 3 of 5 rounds wins the match. Each round lasts at most 3 minutes.
- When a round ends on time, the party with the highest total HP among survivors wins.
- If total HP ties, the higher total damage wins.
- If both tie, the round is a tie.

## Arena of Solare battlefields and elemental traces

You enter one of the battlefields at random: Olun's Valley, Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Upper), Kermelun Wilds, Neftak Outpost, Yolu's Teeth or Darkseekers' Retreat.

Elemental traces spawn in places on the map, with a light effect marking the spot 10 seconds before they appear. Destroying one gives you and nearby party members its boon, except Wind's Song. The buff shows as an icon over your head.

| Trace | Effect |
| Flora's Breath | Recovers HP; cannot be shared with the party |
| Fire's Warmth | All AP +30 for 20 seconds |
| Wind's Song | Attack, Casting and Movement Speed +25% for 30 seconds; cannot be shared |

## Arena of Solare placement matches and ranks

Each season starts with Placement Matches. Your starting score comes from your previous season's rank, and a first-time player starts at 700 points. Points are added or removed after each match, and you earn an Emblem of Solare for your point bracket. The ranks run: Beginner Soldier, Apprentice Soldier, Skilled Soldier (up to 1200), Dawn Spear (1201 - 1500), Shining Spear (1501 - 1800), Twilight Spear (1801 - 2100), Radiant Spear (2101 - 2400) and Solare Incarnate (2401+).

## Arena of Solare lose streak penalty

A lose streak penalty applies only to Twilight Spear, Radiant Spear and Solare Incarnate. Twilight Spear hits its maximum penalty after 3 straight losses, while Radiant Spear and Solare Incarnate reach theirs after 4.

| Rank | Penalty |
| Twilight Spear (1801 - 2100) | -5% from 3 losses in a row |
| Radiant Spear (2101 - 2400) | -5% at 3 losses, -10% at 4 |
| Solare Incarnate (2401+) | -5% at 3 losses, -10% at 4 |

## Arena of Solare dormancy penalty

Players in higher score ranges lose ranking points if they skip matches for a while. The deduction is checked at the 2:00 AM ranking tally.

- Solare Incarnate (over 2401 points): 50 points lost per day.
- Radiant Spear (2101 - 2400): 40 points lost per day.
- Twilight Spear (1801 - 2100): 30 points lost every other day.

For example, if a Solare Incarnate or Radiant Spear player's last ranked match ends Monday night and they skip Tuesday's ranked hours and Wednesday's early window, points drop at 2:00 on Wednesday. For Twilight Spear with the same Monday match, the first drop comes at 2:00 on Thursday.

Participation counts at the moment a result is decided and the score updates, not when you enter the battlefield. A match finished at 23:59 and another at 00:01 count as different days.

## Arena of Solare deserter penalty

If you leave mid-match, you can use the reconnect window to return before it ends. What you lose depends on what happens next: if you never re-enter, you take the deserter penalty; if you return and lose, you get loss points; if you return and win, you get victory points.

| Your score | Deserter penalty |
| 0 - 2,100 | -50 points |
| 2,101 - 2,400 | -46 points |
| 2,401 and above | -40 points |

## Arena of Solare rankings and records

The rankings and records menu shows details about other Adventurers. Click the magnifying glass next to a name to see which style they play (Awakening or Succession), plus their skill add-ons and the gear they currently have set. Choose "Class" at the top right of the ranking screen to view the top 20 ranked Adventurers per class.

## Arena of Solare replay

The Replay feature, opened with the "Replay" button on the Arena of Solare menu, lets you watch matches of top-ranked adventurers. On update day, ranked matches from 17:00 are recorded and replays arrive about 30 minutes later. Matches of the top 10 adventurers per region and class are saved automatically, based on standings at tally time.

Click "Download" in the Replay window to see downloadable replays, browse by Family name, region and class, and set your own title. Downloaded replays play for up to one week after recording, and skill or motion updates can make some unplayable. They can be downloaded only while idle or out of combat, and watched only in a Safe Zone.

## Arena of Solare victory pose

Set a victory pose with "Set Victory Pose", next to "Set Gear" in the Arena of Solare UI. It plays only after a final victory, not in a draw. The social actions Forward Sweep, Graduation! and Ride Board cannot be chosen.

## Arena of Solare exclusive rewards

- NPC statue in the Battle Arena: characters who rank 1st in each class in an official season become NPC statues in the Battle Arena. Talking to one and exchanging 20 Energy Points gives a buff of Breath EXP +10%.
- Solare Knight Outfit: the top 100 of an official season get the Solare Knight Outfit Box by in-game mail (hotkey: B) and open it with the character that should receive the outfit.
- Titles: the same top 100 also receive the title "Solare Knight" by mail; right-click the title item. It lasts until the end of the following official season. A separate title comes with your rank emblem.
- Name frame: a grade-specific name frame shows in all Arena of Solare matches until the next season ends.
- Glorious Solare Box: Cron Stone x1,000, Corrupt Oil of Immortality x20, Perfume of Courage x30, Khalk's Elixir x30, [Party] Harmony Draught - Human x30, Elixir of Deep Sea x30, Perfume of Charm x30, Perfume of Insight x30, Item Collection Increase Scroll x30.
