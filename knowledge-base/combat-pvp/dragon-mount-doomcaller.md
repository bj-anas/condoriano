# Dragon Mount: Doomcaller

The Doomcaller dragon guild mount in Black Desert Online: how a guild obtains, registers and summons it, its flight controls and its skills.

## Doomcaller overview

The Doomcaller is a powerful mythical dragon mount for large-scale guild warfare. It appears in big PvP events, namely Unlimited Conquest Wars and the War of the Roses, where its abilities can shift a battle. Only strong, well-coordinated guilds are likely to earn the chance to use it fully.

## Doomcaller how to obtain

A guild needs [Guild] Trace of Doom x100 and must complete the quest "[Doomcaller] Drieghan's Prophecy of Old" from [Chief] Durgeff of Duvencrune to receive the [Guild] Registration: Doomcaller item. For the quest, find the [Exploration Node] Ominous Altar at Garmoth's Nest and offer the [Guild] Trace of Doom x100 there.

[Guild] Trace of Doom comes from two sources. Guild Masters (Alliance Leaders) who win an Unlimited Conquest War get a [Guild] Sealed Trace of Doom, and opening it gives [Guild] Trace of Doom x12. Leading Guild Masters (Alliance Leaders) who win the War of the Roses get [Guild] Trace of Doom x25. Alliance Leaders also receive these if allied to guilds that meet those requirements.

## Doomcaller registering the mount

Guilds register the [Guild] Registration: Doomcaller item at any Guild Mount Manager to manage the mount. Each registered Doomcaller stays active for 14 days and is then removed automatically. At most two Doomcallers can be registered at the same time.

## Doomcaller summoning

Summoning needs the Handle Doomcaller authority, set in Guild (G), Guild Member Status, by selecting the member and choosing Set Authorities. It lets a member summon, recover and delete the guild mount Doomcaller.

Any Doomcaller active when a Node War, Conquest War or War of the Roses starts is checked in automatically, under the same rules as Guild Elephants. When no Node War, Conquest War or War of the Roses is ongoing, summon it through the Guild Mount Manager. During an ongoing Unlimited Conquest War, defensive guilds or alliances summon it with the guild skill "Doomcaller's Oath", found in the Guild (G) Guild Skills menu. With Doomcaller's Oath only one Doomcaller can be summoned at a time, however many are registered.

## Doomcaller movement controls

While riding the Doomcaller dragon mount, these keys control its flight.

| Control | Action |
| W / S while moving, A / D | Move and turn |
| W + Shift | Instant Accel |
| E | Ascend |
| Q | Descend |

## Doomcaller skills

The Doomcaller dragon mount has three skills.

| Skill | Effect |
| Flame of Doom | Attack skill. Damage Reduction -45, Evasion -18%, and Dismount on hit. Max 10 targets. |
| Hellfire Blaze | Attack skill. Damage Reduction -45, Evasion -18%, and Dismount on hit. Max 10 targets. |
| Roar of Doom | Black Spirit's Rage Recovery +1.5% for 30 sec to all allies beneath the Doomcaller. Buff applies to max 40 targets. |
