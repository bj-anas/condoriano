# Black Spirit's Rage

The Black Spirit's Rage system in Black Desert Online: the rage gauge, injury, Rage Absorption and Rage Transfer.

## Black Spirit's Rage: the rage gauge

Black Spirit's Rage is a system that turns on automatically at level 35. From then on, a rage gauge shows under your HP bar. It charges up to 100% by default. Skills that use the Black Spirit's rage deal more damage when the gauge reaches certain levels: 10%, 25%, 50%, 100% and 200%. Think of it as the Black Spirit powering up your skills with its rage.

To see which skills use the rage, check their tooltip in the Skills window (hotkey: K), or click the rage gauge to open the Black Spirit's Rage window.

## Black Spirit's Rage: injury effect

Damaging an opponent with a Black Spirit's rage skill can injure them. Injury lowers the maximum HP the target can recover, which hinders their HP recovery.

- The target is injured for a percentage of the damage dealt.
- Every character heals 10 injury per tick, and a tick is 5 seconds.
- Continuous damage and extra % damage on HP/MP do not cause injury.
- Injury resets when the character dies.
- Injury does not heal while the character is logged out; it recovers gradually once the character has loaded into the game world.

| Black Spirit's Rage used | Injury rate |
| 10% | 10% |
| 25% | 20% |
| 50% | 30% |
| 100% | 50% |
| 200% | 100% |

Each skill's tooltip lists its injury percentage.

## Black Spirit's Rage: injury example

For example, a Warrior hits a Berserker who has 5,000 HP, using 100% of the rage, and deals 2,000 damage. The injury rate at 100% is 50%, so 1,000 of that damage becomes injury. The Berserker has 3,000 HP left and can use recovery methods to heal up to 4,000. The remaining 1,000 HP only returns naturally, at 10 every 5 seconds, until all the injury is gone.

## Black Spirit's Rage: Rage Absorption

Rage Absorption is activated with the Z key once your Black Spirit's rage is at 100%. It grants class-specific buffs or increases your attack/casting speed. It is a very useful combat tool, so time it well. Rage Absorption can also use up to 200% of the Black Spirit's rage.

## Black Spirit's Rage: Rage Transfer

Rage Transfer sends your Black Spirit's rage to an ally. Target the ally and press the X key. This consumes your whole rage gauge and fills the ally's gauge by half the amount consumed. For example, transferring 100% leaves you at 0% and gives your ally 50%.

Rage gained from Rage Transfer can accumulate beyond your character's usual maximum Black Spirit's rage, depending on your gear, such as Tungrad and Kharazad accessories and Artifacts. Some skills can be used by consuming that extra rage, so use Rage Transfer at the right moment.
