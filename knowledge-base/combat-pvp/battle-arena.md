# Battle Arena

The Battle Arena in Black Desert Online: a penalty-free PvP and practice area open from level 50, with its own NPCs, potions, observer mode and trial characters.

## Battle Arena overview

The Battle Arena is PvP content open to any Adventurer of level 50 or above. The only requirement is character level, so you can get into PvP quickly. It is hosted on a different server from the one you normally play on, and it combines free-for-all combat with practice areas.

## Battle Arena how to join

You need full health to enter. With full health, open the main menu (hotkey: Esc), then War (F7), then Battle Arena. You can do this from anywhere. Then pick which instance of the arena to enter.

To leave, you also need full health, and you cannot leave until at least one minute has passed since you entered. Press the same Battle Arena button in the main menu. Other actions, such as going to the character selection screen, also take you out of the arena.

## Battle Arena rules and penalties

Dying in the Battle Arena brings no penalties at all: no combat EXP loss, no magic crystal destruction, no trade goods destruction and no karma loss from defeating other Adventurers. PvP is on by default in the arena's combat zone, so you do not need Alt+C. Members of your own party cannot attack each other.

## Battle Arena NPCs and practice dummies

The arena has several helpful NPCs: a skill instructor, a quartermaster who sells cheap arena-only potions, a vendor for trial characters, and an arena manager for 1:1 combat rounds.

Drill sergeants and wooden scarecrows stand around for practice against PvE and human-style targets. Wooden scarecrows ignore skill effects such as stun, stiffness, knockdown and bound, while drill sergeants are affected by them. Drill sergeants also show hit effects such as down attack, back attack and critical hit, and come in beginner, intermediate and advanced training levels that react differently to your attacks. Practice here to build combos before using them outside.

## Battle Arena 1:1 ring

You can fight 1 vs 1 in a small ring. Talk to the NPC Hafun, then wait until one of the two Adventurers currently fighting in the ring loses; that frees a spot for you. To leave this arena at any time, talk to the NPC Alun.

## Battle Arena exclusive potions

The quartermaster Robb, in the middle of the arena, sells exclusive potions for 1 silver each. They are unusable outside the Battle Arena. Their healing amount and cooldown match normal potions, and each weighs 0.01LT, so they cost less silver and carried weight. Normal potions also work inside the arena.

## Battle Arena observer mode

Adventurers new to PvP can watch fights from a safe distance. Go near a wall in the Battle Arena, press the walk backward key (S) in non-combat stance to lean on the wall, then press R to enter Observer Mode. Leaning on walls or sitting on benches and railings also works in other places.

## Battle Arena trial characters

An Adventurer without weapons or armor can create a trial character and enter the Battle Arena. Each family can have 3 trial characters. A trial character starts at Lv. 60 with 4,000 skill points available and 100,000 silver. It buys equipment boxes with high enhancement level weapons, defense gear and accessories from the vendor NPC Haz. Trial characters can also take part in Red Battlefield, but cannot travel outside the Battle Arena and Red Battlefield.
