# Node War

Node War in Black Desert Online: guild and alliance PvP over forts on nodes, with schedule, Occupation and Construction modes, limits, rules, annexes, siege weapons and rewards.

## Node War overview

Node War is guild PvP where guilds and alliances compete for supremacy over nodes across the world. It is held in the territories Balenos, Serendia, Calpheon, Ulukita, Valencia and Edania. The goal is to fight other guilds to occupy a fort and then defend it until its timer closes, which gives victory. For control over a territory larger than one node, Conquest War is the large-scale alternative.

## Node War schedule

Node Wars run every day except Saturday, which is Conquest War day. Occupation Node War is NA 18:00-19:00 (PST/PDT) and EU 20:00-21:00 (CEST/CET). Construction Node War is NA 18:00-20:00 (PST/PDT) and EU 20:00-22:00 (CEST/CET). The start time is NA 18:00 (PST) and EU 20:00 (CET). Occupation lasts 1 hour and Construction lasts 2 hours. The Node War type is chosen by the guild holding the Lord of the Unlimited (Mediah) tier territory.

## Node War Occupation mode versus Construction mode

Node War has two modes, chosen by the guild that occupies the unlimited Conquest War (Mediah) territory. Occupation is the current method and Construction is the previous one.

| Topic | Occupation mode | Construction mode |
| Applying | No fort construction needed; apply in the Node War UI | Fort and annex construction needed; apply through fort construction |
| Application time | 1 hour after the previous day's Node War ends until 1 hour before start | 1 hour after the previous day's Node/Conquest War ends until 1 hour before start |
| Region | Entire territory group applied for | Area where the fort is built |
| Victory | Your guild is temporarily occupying the fort when the timer ends | Destroy all other guilds' forts in the area and defend yours |
| Defeat or liberation | Defeat if you are not temporarily occupying when the timer ends; liberation if no guild is | Defeat if your fort is destroyed; liberation if multiple forts remain at the end |
| Reveal of opponent forts | Shows neutral forts created after the start | Shows enemy forts on the World Map 30 min after start |

If the last fort has more than 10 minutes left, its time turns into a "?" and occupying it gives extra rewards.

## Node War Occupation versus Construction mode details

More differences between the modes:

| Topic | Occupation mode | Construction mode |
| Assemble | 10 minutes before start, move to major towns with "Assemble!" | After the war starts, move to your allied fort with "Assemble!" |
| Cannons | Can be bought and installed without a Cannon Observatory | Need a Cannon Observatory and collected cannons |
| Indomitable Flag | Collect right after the Indomitable Flag Factory is done; crafting about 3 min | Collect after the factory is done and the flag is crafted; about 10 min |
| Defensive annexes | Consume extra Guild Funds | No extra Guild Funds |
| Revival cooldown | Default 10 sec, max 30 sec, +1 sec per minute of match, +1 sec per death | Default 10 sec, max 60 sec, same increases, and -6 sec per Recovery Center when reviving at a Fort or Command Post |
| Participation reward condition | Allies' total K/D count 100 or more and a temp fort occupied at least once | Your fort stayed standing and you destroyed an enemy fort or reached a total K/D of 100+ |
| Participation opportunity returns | Total K/D 100 or more, or a temp fort occupied at least once | Destroy an enemy fort or reach total K/D 100+ |

Both modes keep the Node War buff effects, doubled Node investment benefits and unchanged rewards. The occupying guild can take part at most 6 times per week, and War Hero hire is at most 3 regardless of territory or fort tier. Balenos and Serendia always use Occupation mode rules.

## Node War mode selection by the Mediah occupying guild

After a guild occupies the territory in the unlimited Conquest War (Mediah), it can pick the Node War mode for the next week, with no gear score restrictions. It announces the mode to the world during a window of about 1 hour after the Conquest War ends, and the choice runs from the following Sunday until the next Conquest War (Sunday to Friday). If no mode is designated, Occupation mode is the default. If the occupying guild is confirmed before the Conquest War period ends, selection stays open until about 1 hour after that period ends.

## Node War Construction mode how to participate

In Construction mode, a guild takes these steps.

1. Check the Node War tier: on the World Map (M), use the Node War filter at the top right to see Node War Information.
2. Buy a fort for the territory you want from the Guild Military Supply Manager NPC in towns. For Mediah, buy a Node War fort for Mediah. Annex Assembly Tools can be bought there too.
3. Install the fort: go to the server where the war is held, apply the Node War filter on the World Map, press the icon of your tier, enter Remote Fort Installation mode and install it. You can also walk to the Node War area and install it there.
4. Pre-build annex buildings around the fort. No additional Guild Funds are needed in Construction mode.

The number of forts built shows on the World Map 1 hour before the war. After the fort is installed, guild or alliance members move to the server and click the Join Node War button, found under Guild Information (G), Guild Member Status, by changing to Participate status. When the war starts, an "Assemble!" button at the top right moves you to your guild's fort.

## Node War focus participation and resurrection

Once a Node War starts, the Focus Node War Participation System applies. On a server with an ongoing Node War, all Adventurers who are not participating have their DP set to 0. Non-participants from guilds that joined the war are forced to the nearest town if they get near the war area. When you resurrect in a Node War, your fallen mount resurrects too.

On death you choose from preset towns in each territory:

| Territory | Resurrection towns |
| Balenos | Velia, Western Guard Camp, Olvia |
| Serendia | Heidel, Glish |
| Calpheon | Calpheon City, Keplan, Behr |
| Valencia | Valencia City, Ancado Inner Harbor, Arehaza |
| Edania | Hakinza Sanctuary |
| Ulukita | Asparkan, Muzgar, Velandir |

The resurrection cooldown starts at 10 seconds and grows by 1 second per minute of the war and by 1 second per death, up to a maximum of 30 seconds.

## Node War fort repairs and end of war rules

Guilds and alliances repair occupied forts with Polished Stone x1, which recovers 1% durability of a fort, command post or HQ.

When a Node War ends: the "Return" function becomes active immediately at the top right for that territory; monsters reappear in the territory after 10 minutes; PvP between participating guilds and alliances stops at once; and all remaining annex buildings in the territory are destroyed.

## Node War annexes and siege weapons

After a Node War starts, build annexes with the Annex Assembly Tool. Both the Annex Assembly Tool and the Node War Lynch Cannon Assembly Kit are sold by the Guild Military Supply Manager found in each town. War Heroes cannot install annexes or Node War Lynch Cannons. At most 2 Node War Lynch Cannons can be installed. Eligible positions: Guild Master, Advisor, Staff, Secretary, Officer, Quartermaster, Cannoneer. A Lynch Cannon costs 10 million Silver of Guild Funds, max x2.

Annexes can be built anywhere in the Node War area, whether or not you hold a fort, as long as guild funds suffice. They use guild funds and have limits:

| Annex | Guild funds | Max |
| Flame Tower | 30,000,000 Silver | 2 |
| Hwacha | 30,000,000 Silver | 1 |
| Elephant Nursery | 50,000,000 Silver | 1 |
| Indomitable Flag Factory | 50,000,000 Silver | 1 |

All annexes and siege weapons are destroyed automatically when the Node War ends.

## Node War annex installation, production times and upgrades

With the Annex Assembly Tool you can use Easy Annex Installation Mode: pick an annex from the list on the right of the screen, move around to find a spot, fix the position with the R key, then rotate or install. Annexes installed with the Node/Conquest War Annex Assembly Tool take 5 sec to install.

- Indomitable Flag production at the factory: 3 min in Node War, 10 min in Conquest War.
- Elephant production at the Elephant Nursery: 5 min in Node or Conquest War.

Flame Towers and Hwachas can be upgraded in Calpheon/Ulukita and Valencia/Edania, but not in Balenos/Serendia. An upgrade button appears near a completed one, costs Iron Ingot x10 and Melted Copper Shard x10, and takes some time.

## Node War taxes as guild funds

A guild that wins a Node War receives the node's accumulated taxes as guild funds immediately on capturing it. Higher tier nodes give a higher share.

| Territory group | Tier 3 | Tier 4 | Tier 5 |
| Balenos & Serendia | 3 - 5 billion | 3.5 - 5.5 billion | 4 - 6 billion |
| Ulukita & Calpheon | 6 - 8 billion | 6.5 - 8.5 billion | 7 - 9 billion |
| Edania & Valencia | 8.5 - 10.5 billion | 9 - 11 billion | 10 - 12 billion |

Conquest War taxes: Balenos 30 - 40 billion, Serendia 50 - 60 billion, Mediah/Valencia 60 - 70 billion, Balenos/Serendia Tribute Wagon 10 - 20 billion.

## Node War personal rewards requirements

Personal rewards depend on the tier of the Node War or Conquest War result. To receive them, your guild or alliance must have a total kill/death count of at least 100 and have temporarily occupied a fort at least once. Kills from siege weapons such as flame towers or Hwachas do not count. Rewards go to the Black Spirit's Safe immediately after the final occupation of the fort. In Occupation Node War you also get a bonus for occupying a "?" fort, whose end time is unknown.

Participants who met the minimum requirements but could not occupy a fort get the rewards listed in the territory group sections.

## Node War titles

Titles come from kills during a Node War and last one week (7 days) once acquired. They display in special colors. Only kills inside the territory where the Node War is happening count. Trooper needs 50 kills, Commander 100 kills and Lethal Weapon 150 kills.

Conquest War titles are the same as before: Stormtrooper (100 kills), Special Force Commander (180 kills) and Ultimate Weapon (300 kills). These also last 7 days, show special effects when applied, and count only kills within the Conquest War territory.

## Node War doubled Node Investment benefits

Occupying a node doubles the Node Investment Benefits for one week across that node's territory and other territories in its group. For example, a Tier 3 Fort in a Calpheon Node War doubles benefits in both Calpheon and Kamasylvia. Reoccupying a node in the same territory resets the period. Check the remaining time on the right of the Guild (G) window under Occupation Information.

Node Investment Benefits mean +0.5% Item Drop Rate per Node Investment Level when defeating certain monsters. At Node Level 20 for Gyfin Rhasia Temple, that is +10% there, and +20% if doubled. Occupying the Pilgrim's Sanctum: Obedience Node Fort in Valencia doubles benefits across Mediah/Valencia.

## Node War common rules shared with Conquest War

1. The occupying guild or alliance automatically collects the node's accumulated taxes after a win.
2. If an allied fort, command post or field HQ is destroyed, you stop counting as a participant and cannot attack participating guilds or alliances.
3. Guild wars are restricted on the server during the war; only Node/Conquest War attacks are allowed.
4. PvP deaths between participating guilds and alliances carry no penalty.
5. Camouflage outfits (Treant Camouflage, Desert Camouflage) do not hide your identity.
6. From 1 hour before a Node War (2 hours for Conquest War) until it ends, participating guilds and alliances cannot recruit new members.

## Node War specific rules

1. The Lord's guild can join up to 6 times a week. Each guild or alliance has one weekly opportunity and gets another only after reaching a total kill/death count of 100 or more. Opportunities reset every Saturday at 24:00.
2. Each region (Balenos, Serendia, Calpheon, Ulukita, Valencia, Edania) has its own designated servers.
3. All annexes are demolished when the war ends.
4. Consider participant limits and stat limits when choosing a territory group.
5. Once a territory is allocated, the Node War menu shows the guilds and alliances taking part.
6. In Occupation Node War, a neutral fort left unoccupied at the end liberates its node.
7. Winners get the node's accumulated taxes and the personal victory reward, which needs the minimum requirement for opportunity return.
8. Two guild skills, Breath of Life and Blessing of Battle, are activated by consuming Spirit of Battle while temporarily occupying a fort, and work only near that fort.
