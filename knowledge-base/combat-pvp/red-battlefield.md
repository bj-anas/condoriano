# Red Battlefield

Red Battlefield in Black Desert Online: a penalty-free team PvP mode open from level 50, with how to enter, the Castle Ruins rules, scoring, rewards and the Battlefield Token shop.

## Red Battlefield overview

Red Battlefield is PvP content for every character above level 50. You can join anytime and anywhere, because karma and guild membership do not matter. Dying carries no restrictions on respawning, so the page advises not fearing death and fighting actively. Quartermaster potions and trial characters are supported inside.

## Red Battlefield how to join

Open the main menu (hotkey: Esc), then War (F7), then Red Battlefield. The Red Battlefield Status window opens, and from it you reach the battlefield you want. The rules for each battlefield are in the Red Battlefield Rules section to the right of the Enter button.

The battlefields include Valencia City (points come from occupying areas), and Garmoth's Nest and Castle Ruins (team death match). At the time of the page, only the Castle Ruins map was available and the others were to follow. Maps rotate every few weeks between Castle Ruins and Valencia City, so only one map is up in any period. Valencia City has its own note.

## Red Battlefield penalties for leaving or idling

Leaving the battlefield or staying idle too long gives a penalty that blocks you from joining matches for a while. Penalties come in stages. Each penalty for leaving too long adds two stages to your level. Standing still after entering first gives you warnings; if you ignore them you get a penalty, and long idling stacks penalties and raises your level. Press the Red Battlefield entry button to see how long your penalty lasts.

| Level | Duration |
| 1 | 3 min |
| 2 | 6 min |
| 3 | 10 min |
| 4 | 30 min |
| 5 | 1 hour |
| 6 | 3 hours |
| Highest listed | 24 hours |

## Red Battlefield Castle Ruins rules

After you choose to enter a match, you wait until all players are ready; the maximum is 20 participants. While waiting you cannot use Traveler's Map, Archaeologist's Map, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Telescope or Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass.

Players are assigned automatically to one of two camps, Black Desert or Red Desert. The match lasts 15 minutes. You may join up to 3 minutes after it starts, and after that you must wait for the next one. At the start everyone is moved to the battlefield area. If you leave the area or the game closes, you return to your earlier location, and you can rejoin a match in progress if that happened within the first 3 minutes. To leave the save zone, walk into the blue flame to be teleported to the fighting area.

## Red Battlefield Castle Ruins scoring

During the time limit you may fight the opposing faction freely. Points collected until the end are totaled, and the side with the highest score wins. Points come from defeating a character of the opposing faction, and the amount depends on the points the defeated character holds.

| Situation | Score |
| You defeat an enemy | Gain half (1/2) of the enemy's points, at least 1 point |
| An enemy defeats you | Lose a quarter (1/4) of your points, at least 1 point; with only 1 point you lose nothing |

## Red Battlefield potions and consumables

You can use any potions and consumables you own in Red Battlefield, but the Quartermaster NPC inside the battlefield sells potions much cheaper: 1 silver each, weighing 0,01LT. You can also enter with a trial character and buy items from the NPC Haz inside the battlefield.

## Red Battlefield rewards from a match

After each match you receive Combat EXP, Skill EXP and Battlefield Tokens. Victory gives Battlefield Token x25 and defeat gives Battlefield Token x10, both with Combat and Skill EXP. The EXP is applied automatically when the battle ends. Tokens are delivered to the Manage Currency UI, opened with the chest icon in the screen's top right corner.

Combat EXP reward by level (percent of a level):

## Red Battlefield Battlefield Token exchange

Battlefield Tokens can be exchanged for these items, with the limits shown.

| Limit | Token cost | Item |
| Twice a week | Battlefield Token x80 | Deboreka Necklace x1, Deboreka Ring x1, Deboreka Belt x1 or Deboreka Earring x1 |
| 10 times a week | Battlefield Token x3 | Perfume of Courage x1 or Elixir of Deep Sea x1 |
| 3 times a week | Battlefield Token x1 | Superior Whale Tendon Potion x5 or Tough Whale Tendon Elixir x1 |
| Once a week | Battlefield Token x1 | Tears of War x5 |
| Always | Battlefield Token x50 | Red Essence x1 |
| Always | Battlefield Token x30 | Gold Bar Box x1 |
| Always | Battlefield Token x1 | Gold Bar 100G x1 |

Tears of War can be crafted into Elion's Blessing. A Gold Bar Box grants Gold Bar 1,000G to 10,000G at a set probability when opened.

## Red Battlefield Red Essence and crystal conversion

Heating Red Essence (hotkey: L) with certain magic crystals produces Red Battlefield crystals. The conversions:

| Starting crystal | Result |
| Ancient Magic Crystal - Harphia / Cobelinus / Viper / Hystria / Carmae / Addis | Red Battlefield Crystal with the same name |
| Ancient Magic Crystal of Crimson Flame - Power | Red Battlefield Crystal: Power |
| Ancient Magic Crystal of Nature - Adamantine / Fighting Spirit / Sturdiness / Giant | Red Battlefield Crystal with the same name |
