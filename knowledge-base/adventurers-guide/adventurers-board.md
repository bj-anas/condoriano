# Adventurer's Board

The Adventurer's Board is an in-game window that brings content from the official website into the game: news, events, the official wiki guides, a question-and-answer space and the forums.

## Adventurer's Board overview

The Adventurer's Board lets players reach, from inside the game, a lot of content that the official website offers. It shows updates and events, guides recommended by GMs, guides for new Adventurers, and the official forum where players share opinions. It also contains the Oasis of Knowledge, where Adventurers answer or post questions. When you find useful content on the board, the [Share] button links it in the in-game chat window.

## How to open the Adventurer's Board

To open the Adventurer's Board, press Esc to open the main menu, then choose Community > Adventurer's Board. The opening page of the window lists ongoing game events, fresh news and announcements posted on the official website, Adventurer's Guide pages that were changed lately, tips and guides that other Adventurers posted on the forums, popular art and screenshots, and new questions and answers.

## Adventurer's Board main categories

The Adventurer's Board has 4 main categories at the top of the window:

1. Events & Announcements: all news for Adventurers, including events, updates and notices.
2. Adventurer's Guide: Black Desert's official wiki with useful tips.
3. Oasis of Knowledge: a place to share knowledge about Black Desert through questions and answers.
4. Community: the official forums for sharing opinions on many topics.

## Adventurer's Board Events & Announcements tabs

The Events & Announcements section of the Adventurer's Board lets players read Black Desert news in-game instead of only on the official website. It has 5 tabs:

- Notices: all official notices, including server maintenance and live issues.
- Updates: all the latest game updates.
- Events: current events, finished events, and winner announcements.
- GM Notes: the latest notes written by GMs.
- Pearl Shop: the newest items and discounts in the in-game Pearl Shop.

## Adventurer's Board Adventurer's Guide section

The Adventurer's Guide section of the Adventurer's Board is the official wiki and covers all aspects of the game. For example, the World Bosses page tells you when a world boss appears, and the life skill pages explain how life skill details work. Use the search bar in the upper left to find a topic quickly. The [Share] button in the upper right posts a link to the page in the chat window. The guide content may differ from the actual game, depending on updates and content changes.

## Adventurer's Board Oasis of Knowledge

The Oasis of Knowledge on the Adventurer's Board is a space where Adventurers ask questions about content they are unsure of and answer questions from others. Anyone can leave a reply on a posted question. Its tools are:

1. Filter: sort the list by recent questions, recent replies, or most-liked replies.
2. Search: look for specific questions and replies, or search by family name.
3. Recommended: shows recommended questions and replies.
4. Question: post your own question.

## Adventurer's Board Community forums

The Community section of the Adventurer's Board opens the official forums, where players can read and write posts. To post a topic or reply with a video or image attached, open the website while logged in. The first page shows the latest and most popular topics, and you can comment on any of them. Categories on the left let you follow a specific category, such as General Gameplay. Tags above the top border show posts with a specific tag, and filters below the tags arrange posts by type. The forum is a shared space, so respect other Adventurers' opinions.
