# Adventurer's Guide Index

The start page of the official Adventurer's Guide wiki. It groups popular topics into six hubs and points to the wiki page for each one.

## Adventurer's Guide hub: Types of Gear

The Types of Gear hub of the Adventurer's Guide is about strong gear in Black Desert. It begins at the Guaranteed PEN Accessory and ends at the Slumbering Origin Gear. Its pages are: Black Star Gear (a free PEN Black Star), Guaranteed PEN (V) Accessory (gear without enhancing), Slumbering Origin Gear (top-tier gear), Sovereign Weapons (Primal grade weapons), Kharazad Accessories (Primal grade accessories), and Preonne Accessories (the best accessories for Life Skills).

## Adventurer's Guide hub: Companions and Mounts

The Companions and Mounts hub of the Adventurer's Guide covers Dream and Mythical horses and the best ships for crossing the ocean. Its pages are: Vipiko's Dreaming Horse (the fastest horses), Mythical Horses (Tier 10 horses), Types of Mounts (camels and elephants), Ship Upgrading (preparations for the ocean), Pets (help from your companions), and Old Moon Grand Prix (racing with your mount).

## Adventurer's Guide hub: Treasures and Rare Items

The Treasures and Rare Items hub of the Adventurer's Guide covers mysterious healing potions that never run dry and legendary outfits. Its pages are: Treasures (legendary artifacts), Special Outfits (including the appearance of a world boss), and Instruments (rare gameplay instruments).

## Adventurer's Guide hub: Dungeons and Challenges

The Dungeons and Challenges hub of the Adventurer's Guide covers world bosses and cooperative dungeons. Its pages are: Dark Rifts (the rift spawns bosses), World Bosses (a chance to get the best items), Black Shrine - Solo (challenge bosses on your own), Black Shrine - Party (cooperate to defeat bosses), Atoraxxion (puzzles and monsters), and Pit of the Undying (1vs1 battle with enemies).

## Adventurer's Guide hub: PvP and Battles

The PvP and Battles hub of the Adventurer's Guide ranges from the 3vs3 Arena of Solare, where skill is the only factor, to the 300vs300 War of the Roses for dominance of the territory. Its pages are: Arena of Solare (3vs3 battles), Red Battlefield (battles against two factions), Guild League (10vs10 battles), Node War (territory wars against guilds), Conquest War (fight for supremacy), and War of the Roses (300vs300 faction war).

## Adventurer's Guide hub: The World of Black Desert

The World of Black Desert hub of the Adventurer's Guide is about hunting monsters around the world map. It has one Monster Zones page for each of these regions: Calpheon, Mediah, Valencia, Kamasylvia, Drieghan, O'dyllita, Eternal Winter, Ulukita, and LotML (Land of the Morning Light).
