# Quests

How quests work in Black Desert: the Quest window, quest tracker, quest types, crossroads, and rewards.

## Quests overview

Quests come from interacting with NPCs, who know things you do not and give you what you need to explore the world and continue your adventure. Most quests are optional and skipping them will not spoil other content, but certain features and important items are unlocked only by their quests, and those may require main quest progress on at least one character. Quests give contribution experience, skill points, combat experience, consumables, and knowledge entries. Skill points and contribution experience are more efficient to get from quests than from other methods.

## Quests: Quest window and categories

Press O to open the Quest window. Its category tabs are Ongoing, Main, Suggested, Recurring, and New. Main quests advance the main story. Suggested quests unlock or teach game features or lead to important items, for example [ADV Support] Family-fitting Inventory unlocks the family inventory, and [ADV Support] Tool Bag for Better Convenience gives Liana's Tool Bag for Gathering. Recurring quests repeat daily or weekly; combat ones guide you to better equipment and Contribution ones to contribution experience. New quests are recently added and tend to relate to new features or events. You can group quests by region or type, search by name or reward, and uncheck the box left of a quest to hide it from the tracker. Quest options let you forfeit a quest, show its location on the Minimap and World Map with a navigation route, or auto-run to it.

## Quests: Quest Type filter and Preferences

The Quest Type filter picks which kinds of quests you receive from the Black Spirit and NPCs; only activated types are offered. You can choose types only from character level 20, using the Settings button in the quest tracker or the Preferences section at the bottom of the Quest window. "Life Quests" shows only Life Skill quests, "All Quests" shows every quest, and "Other quests" is for repeatable quests like dailies. Keeping all options on is recommended until you are experienced, because otherwise you may miss useful beginner quests or get stuck in quest chains. The Main Story type cannot be disabled. Clicking a quest opens the Quest Summary, where Basic Reward items are all obtained and Optional Reward lets you pick only one.

## Quests: quest tracker

The quest tracker sits on the right of the screen under the Minimap. Black Spirit and main story quests are at the top, guiding you through the story and showing Monster Zones for each level. The yellow part at the top shows the three most recently received or completed quests. Completed quests are crossed out with a golden effect and a green target, and you claim rewards from their NPC. An icon left of each name shows the quest type. Tracker options can also remove a quest from the tracker. Clicking a tracked quest opens the Quest window. In the World Map (hotkey: M) you can view quest summaries and areas, and right-click a quest icon to see the path.

## Quests: why a quest does not appear

Some quests appear only after their requirements are met, such as a character level, a life skill level, a time of day, completing earlier quests, a specific knowledge entry, or reaching a location. Quests in the Suggested list of the Quest window (O) show their requirements. If a quest does not show at its NPC, check those requirements.

## Quests: receiving and quest icon colors

Go to an NPC with a yellow-bordered icon over its body and press the interaction key (R) to hear the quest. The icon border shows status: yellow means not yet accepted, blue means in progress, and red means the objective is complete and you can turn it in. Unaccepted Life Skill quests, such as fishing, gathering, and alchemy, are light green instead of yellow. Quests can also come from the Black Spirit, world objects, and items, and guilds offer guild missions whose reward is given only if completed within a time limit after the Guild Master pays the down payment and accepts it.

## Quests: quest types

The wiki says seven types but lists eight: Main Story (world and character stories), Black Spirit (given at certain levels or conditions), General (basic NPC quests), Adventure (find new areas), Life Skills (activities like cooking or gathering herbs), Content (Atoraxxion, Red Battlefield, instrument playing), Event, and Repeatable. Daily repeatable quests reset every day at midnight UTC, and weekly ones reset on Thursday (Wednesday to Thursday), also UTC.

## Quests: crossroads quests

In a crossroads quest your choice changes its content. After accepting certain quests, another NPC, usually the Black Spirit, may give a different quest instead, and accepting it cancels the first. Check both rewards first, as they tend to differ a lot. Canceling resets your choice so you can choose again. All main quest crossroad options must be completed eventually to receive every reward, but main quests can only be done once per character, so use different characters for different options. The unique rewards are mostly knowledge entries.

## Quests: rewards and inventory space

Besides standard rewards, quests that introduce a feature give related items, such as a trade wagon, a gathering tool, magic crystals for transfusion, or furniture for a residence. For optional rewards you choose one of several. You cannot complete a quest if your empty inventory slots are fewer than the reward items, or fewer than two. Sell unused items to a vendor, store them in town storage, or discard them.
