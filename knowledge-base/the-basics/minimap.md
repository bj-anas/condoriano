# Minimap

The minimap is a small satellite-style map of your surroundings. This page covers its icons, controls, 3D mode, and the time bar above it.

## Minimap overview

The minimap shows all NPCs nearby, including villagers, guards, monsters, and animals, using about 40 kinds of icons; hovering over an icon shows its name. If a quest is active, the region where it can be done also appears. NPC icons are shown clearly within the 120 degrees in front of the camera's view, and icons outside that are faded.

## Minimap monster and Adventurer icons

- Monster: normal monsters; all nearby monsters appear as red dots.
- Quest Monster: monsters you must defeat for an active quest.
- Violent Monster: much stronger than normal monsters and tends to drop better items.
- Boss Monster: if no other Adventurer is near when this appears, running away is recommended.
- Normal Adventurer: other players. Those sitting with Q in a non-combat stance, or lying down after pressing space while sitting, do not appear.
- Party Member and Guild Member: members of your own party or guild.
- Death: where your character died.

## Minimap NPC and quest icons

- Undiscovered NPC: an NPC you have not met yet. Meeting and talking to one gives knowledge.
- Discovered NPC: after you talk to an NPC, the icon changes to match its function, such as a blacksmith.
- Quest Available: you can accept a quest from the NPC.
- Quest Completion Available: you can complete a quest with the NPC.
- Ongoing Delivery Quest: you have a quest asking you to hand items to an NPC.
- Quest Objective Direction: a triangle on the circle around your character pointing toward ongoing quests.
- Navigate: marks your destination when the guiding line is active. Right-click anywhere on the minimap to cancel it.

## Minimap controls

To change minimap scale, put the cursor on it and use the mouse wheel, or press the "+" and "-" buttons at the top right. The small button above them resets the scale. A slider on the right, below the scale buttons, adjusts transparency. The triangle at the lower left is dragged to resize the 2D minimap. The magnifying glass at the upper left opens the Find the NPC window, which lists NPCs you have met by territory with a search function. Hovering over an NPC name shows its active hours, your amity, amity goals, contribution point rental items, and more. Clicking the name shows the navigation path to it.

## Minimap 3D mode

The circle with "2D" at the top left switches the minimap to 3D; press it again, when it reads 3D, to return to 2D. Over elevated terrain like mountains, the 3D map switches automatically to a top-down view. The "+" button at the bottom left expands the 3D view, but 3D allows only two set sizes, while 2D can be any size.

## Minimap time bar: weather and terrain

The bar above the minimap shows area, server, and in-game time information. Weather affects combat:

- Clear: no effect on combat.
- Rainy: AP is reduced, and drying processing is unavailable.
- Snow: AP is reduced, drying processing is unavailable, and garden crops will not grow.

Terrain affects movement:

- Paved Road: no speed penalty.
- Midair: you move at base speed.
- Desert: some ground mounts, horses and wagons among them, slow down; camels and miniature elephants are the recommended choice.
- Rough road: movement speed 90%.
- Shallow water: movement speed 80%.
- Underwater: movement speed 30%.

## Minimap time bar: area state

The time bar shows one of four area states by PvP status and penalty:

- Safe Zone: not affected by PvP, and one of the main revive areas.
- Combat Zone: monsters appear, PvP is enabled, and death penalties apply.
- Arena: no death penalty even with PvP; found in arenas in some main towns and cities, with free instant resurrection and no equipment durability loss.
- Danger Zone: lies inside Valencia's Great Desert, and an Adventurer with negative karma suffers no negative consequences for attacking others there.

With the zone change alert on, the game notifies you when you move between zones.

## Minimap time bar: server and time

The time bar shows the current server name. Clicking it opens the server list, where you can change servers; the minimap page says there is a 3-minute cooldown before the next change, which does not apply between guild servers (the Servers page says 5 minutes). Clicking "View Server Details" at the top left of the list shows more information and an icon legend. The in-game time on the bar tells you whether an NPC is active, whether certain monsters are out in the fields, and whether Patrigio's secret shop is open.
