# Inventory

The character inventory and related bags in Black Desert: slots, weight, sorting, searching items, the pearl inventory, and what each button in the inventory window does.

## Inventory overview and slots

A character's inventory decides how long they can act, such as hunting, without returning to town. Every new character starts with 16 inventory slots, expandable up to 192 slots per character. There are also 192 separate pearl inventory slots. Slots are earned by completing certain quests, mostly main quests plus some town side quests. To find them, open the Quest window (hotkey: O) and check the Suggested tab for the "[ADV Support] Inventory Expansion!" quests. The inventory window opens with the hotkey I.

## Inventory: Newbie Inventory

When you open the Inventory (hotkey: I), the Newbie Inventory button opens too. It is visible only to new Adventurers, has no weight limit, and serves as extra space in the first weeks. Click the move button at the bottom and right click an item to store or retrieve it. Once new Adventurer benefits end, you can no longer store items there but can always retrieve what you stored. Trial characters cannot use it. It will not accept these item kinds: mount emblems, trade goods, guild-related items, junk items, quest items, contribution items, or anything bound to a character.

## Inventory: storage and mount inventory

Storage warehouses exist in all cities and some towns; talk to the town's Storage Keeper NPC. Per the Inventory page, a storage begins with 8 slots, which grow by buying houses in the town and setting them to Storage, up to 192 slots per town (the Storage page says 16 starting slots). Mount inventories (donkeys, horses, elephants, wagons, ships) have a fixed slot count per mount type and cannot be expanded, but you can check a mount with its full inventory into stables around the world to keep many slots secure.

## Inventory: Family Inventory

The Family Inventory is a bag shared by every character in the same family, for certain item types, accessed through the same window on all characters. It is a tab in the inventory window. See the Family Inventory page for what it holds and how to expand it.

## Inventory: stacking, Auto Arrange, and category sorting

General materials, manufactured materials, and consumables stack; equipment, installable objects, and trade items do not. Overlapping items, usually trade goods, will not stack if their origin area differs. Tick Auto Arrange to sort inventory items automatically by type; turn it off to place items in positions you choose. Category Sorting, at the top right next to Auto Arrange, lets you view items by usage or category.

## Inventory: Yaz's Pouch

Yaz's Pouch combines item pieces that form a new item when arranged in a specific shape in the inventory, such as Ancient Relic Crystal Shards. It can combine several summoning scroll materials at the same time and helps when inventories are full of items such as those for Atoraxxion. The pouch is at the bottom of the inventory window.

## Inventory: weight limit

Weight can block you even with empty slots. If your weight exceeds 170% of your maximum capacity, you cannot pick up more items. The heavier your character gets, the slower its movement speed and attack speed become, leaving you exposed in combat.

## Inventory: item deletion and recovery

To discard an item, drag it to the trash can icon and confirm, or drag it out of the inventory. Pressing the trash can icon (Throw Item Away) first lets you select many items to delete at once. If you delete a valuable item by mistake, press the Recover button between the Discard and Throw Away Item buttons. An item can be restored within 30 days of deletion, up to 2 times per month per family, with the count resetting on day 1 of each month and not carrying over. If the item is not judged valuable enough, contact customer support. Some items may be unrecoverable.

## Inventory: Find My Item

Find My Item checks equipped gear, silver, and items across all your character and mount inventories, town storages, the Black Spirit's safe, and the Central Market warehouse. Open it with main menu (Esc) > Function (F8) > Find My Item, through a town storage's search function, or with Ctrl+F. To search several words, put a semicolon directly before the next word with no spaces, for example Black;Cron. With a maid or butler you can retrieve items straight from the window into your inventory; items in the Black Spirit's safe need no maid, and equipped items cannot be retrieved. The Maids & Butlers page has more.

## Inventory: pearl inventory

Items bought at the Pearl Shop are normally pearl items and go to the pearl inventory; pearl items can also come from events, quests, and gifts. All 192 pearl slots are usable from character creation, and pearl items weigh nothing. To discard one, drag it to the trash can icon at the bottom right or out of the window. In the pearl inventory the Throw Item Away icon does not allow multi-select; it opens the Item Recover window, which follows the same rules as the normal inventory. If you exceed the 192 slots, Patrigio's Extra-Large Apparel Bag from the Loyalty Shop stores up to 40 outfit pieces.

## Inventory window: tabs, search, and bars

The inventory window has tabs for the Inventory (normal items), Pearl Inventory, and Family Inventory. The search bar highlights an item by name. Auto Arrange sorts by type, and Category Sorting sorts by slots or categories. A bar and number show how full the inventory is, and the weight display shows carried weight; hover over the weight icon to see the detailed weight of inventory items and equipped items.

## Inventory window: feature buttons

One group of buttons opens the Enhancement window, the Transfusion window, and the Artifacts window, in that order from the left, next to the Yaz's Pouch button. Enhancement upgrades gear using materials consumed by the Black Spirit, and magic crystals boost stats. The Artifacts window stores artifacts and lightstones, lets you infuse lightstones into artifacts, and creates presets. The second row opens the Alchemy Stone window (recharge, polish for experience, or attempt growth to a better grade and quality), Recharge Dehkia's Lantern window, Dye Palette (stores dyes for later use), Totem of the Sea window (boosts ship stats such as speed), and the Processing window (hotkey: L). In certain monster zones Dehkia's Lantern awakens affected monsters into chaos, raising their strength and making them reappear with no wait after defeat.

## Inventory window: currencies

The bottom of the inventory window shows currencies. Pearls is your family's Pearl amount. Crow Coins is a special currency used on a certain mist-shrouded island; see the Great Expedition page. Loyalties is a currency obtained daily through the Challenge reward tab of your profile and spent in the Loyalties tab of the Pearl Shop. Silver is the universal money used at NPC shops and services and in the Central Market, and it can also be kept in the Central Market warehouse.
