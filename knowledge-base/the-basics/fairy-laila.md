# Fairy Laila

Fairy Laila is a fairy companion that follows the player and offers skills such as auto-potion use. This page covers how to get her, her skills, growth, sprouting, rebirth, auto-use item lists, and appearances.

## Fairy Laila overview

Fairy Laila follows you around and offers a wide range of skills to make travel easier. Her wing tier (Faint, Glimmering, Brilliant, or Radiant) sets how high she can grow and how many skills she can learn, her persona is random, and her skills can be changed, reset, or sprouted to a higher tier.

## Fairy Laila: how to obtain

You meet Fairy Laila by using Sealed Fairy Wings, first received by finishing a questline. Two requirements apply: the character must be at least Lv. 53, and it must have finished one of the main quests in the table below. Which one applies depends on whether you are in a season and which starting zone you picked.

| Main quests that satisfy the requirement |
|---|
| [Boss] Witch of Horrors |
| Looking for Adventurers |
| [Special Growth] Fughar's Memorandum - Chapter 6 |
| [Everfrost] Beyond the Doors of Alyaelli |
| [LoML] Memories Return |
| [LoML] A Step Further |
| [Calpheon] Epilogie III |

After that, start the questline [ADV Support] [Lv. 53] Fairy, Mysterious Companion from the Suggested tab of the Quest window (hotkey: O). Its first quest is "Fairy Queen Theiah", given by the Black Spirit. Finishing it rewards your first Sealed Fairy Wings. Afterwards, give fairy queen Theiah two Laila's petals for the repeatable quest "Lifelong Friends" to receive more sealed fairy wings. Laila's petals drop randomly from life skill activities such as fishing and gathering, and from defeating monsters.

## Fairy Laila: Inexhaustible Well and Miraculous Cheer

The Inexhaustible Well skill automatically uses Purified Water or Star Anise Tea in the desert. When the character has Heatstroke or Hypothermia, the fairy uses one of these items to cure it, and a higher skill level shortens the cooldown. You do not need to set the items in the fairy interface: the fairy takes them directly from your Inventory or Family Inventory. The Desert page has more. The Miraculous Cheer skill lets you set potions that the fairy uses automatically once your health or mana falls to a chosen percentage. Higher skill levels shorten its cooldown between uses.

## Fairy Laila: Continuous Care

A radiant fairy can learn Continuous Care from Lv. 10. Once learned, an "Auto-use Settings" button appears in the fairy UI, letting you pick items to be consumed automatically when not already active; the skill rank sets how many you can designate. Eligible types: Cron Meals/Food (selected food items); Draughts/Elixirs (only 1 type of draught at a time); Perfumes (one type of perfume); Scrolls (selecting a higher grade of a scroll removes lower grade selections); and Spirit Essences. Three preset buttons save and switch selections. Left click opens Auto-use Settings and right click toggles auto-use on or off. Choosing a different item with the same effect still consumes it even if the earlier item's effect is active: for example, with [Event] Beast's Draught active, selecting Beast's Draught and turning auto-use on uses it immediately.

## Fairy Laila: persona

Her persona is picked randomly when you first obtain her, from five: Aloof, Meticulous, Joyful, Serene, and Prim Fairy Laila. Each has different voiceover, which you can switch off in the Fairy Volume option of Audio Settings. The persona also decides the light color of the Morning Star skill.

## Fairy Laila: growth and wing tiers

Using Sealed Fairy Wings gives a Fairy Laila with one of four wing tiers, which set her maximum growth and skill count. Feed her Ornette's Dark Honey Wine, Sweet Honey Wine, or Green-grade equipment to help her grow.

| Tier | Max Level | Learnable skills |
|---|---|---|
| Faint | 10 | 2 |
| Glimmering | 20 | 3 |
| Brilliant | 30 | 4 |
| Radiant | 50 | 6 |

A radiant fairy can learn 5 skills for most of her growth, and at Lv. 50 may learn a 6th by a set probability.

## Fairy Laila: sprouting

When a fairy reaches her highest level, the "Sprouting" option can bring her to the next tier. It needs Sweet Honey Wine (+1% success rate) or Ornette's Dark Honey Wine (+10% success rate). Sweet Honey Wine is made from Top-quality Cooking Honey x2, Full-bodied Makgeolli x4, Sugar x10, and Fruit x2 (Apple, Grape, Cherry, etc.). Ornette's Dark Honey Wine comes from events or the Pearl Shop. Sprouting resets all acquired abilities and can only be done once; if it fails, only Rebirth is possible.

## Fairy Laila: rebirth and changing skills

Rebirth changes or resets Fairy Laila's persona and growth, and it also resets all learned skills. It needs Fairy Queen's Might, from events or the Pearl Shop. To swap one unwanted skill, use Theiah's Orb (from events and the Pearl Shop). The number needed depends on how many skills the fairy has: a fairy with 4 skills needs 4 Theiah's Orbs to change one.

## Fairy Laila: Continuous Care scrolls, perfumes, and essences

Scrolls: Extra Combat EXP Scroll, Extra Skill EXP Scroll, Extra Life EXP Scroll, Extra Mount EXP Scroll (also 10 Hours), Max HP Increase Scroll, Max MP Increase Scroll, Skill EXP +300% Scroll, Combat & Skill EXP 530% Scroll (60 min, 180 min), Combat & Skill EXP 300% Scroll (60 min), Mercenary's Experience (60 min), Mercenary's Skill (60 min), Blessed Message Scroll (120 min), Courser's Aura, GM's Blessing 1, 2, and 3 (1, 3, 5, 10, or 24 hours), Secret Book of Florin, and various [Event] versions. Perfumes: Khalk's Elixir, Elixir of Deep Sea, Spirit Perfume Elixir, Bracing Spirit Perfume Elixir, Perfume of Courage, Perfume of Swiftness, Perfume of Charm, Perfume of Insight, with Immortal: and [Event] versions. Spirit Essences: Wind, Earth, and Water.

## Fairy Laila: Continuous Care draughts, elixirs, and food

Eligible draughts include Verdure, Beast's, Giant's, Savage, Frenzy, Celerity, Unbridled Celerity, and Armor Draughts, Elixir of Indignation, Frenzy Draught of Corruption, Marvelous Draught of Persistence and of Strength, Fiery Celerity Draught, and Immortal:, [Event], and Magical versions. Many crafted elixirs also qualify, such as Elixir of Life, Elixir of Wind, EXP Elixir, Worker's Elixir, Fisher's Elixir, Elixir of Amity, Resurrection Elixir, Golden Hand Elixir, and [Mix] elixirs. Eligible food includes Seafood, Simple, and Exquisite Cron Meals (plus [Oasis] and [Event] versions), Balenos, Serendia, Calpheon, Mediah, Valencia, Kamasylvia, O'dyllita, and Eilton Meals and their Special versions, Knight Combat Rations, Honey Wine, Fruit Wine, Makgeolli, and many cooked dishes. The wiki page holds the full item list.

## Fairy Laila: appearances

Fairy appearances can add effects. Venecil Fairy Appearance: Processing EXP +10%. Kibelius Fairy Appearance: Death Penalty -5%. Tick-Tock Fairy Appearance (Halloween season only): Skill EXP +10%. Budding Baby Fairy Appearance: Fishing EXP +20%. Dewy Baby Fairy Appearance: Combat EXP +200%. The Black Spirit Fairy Appearance (10th Anniversary Black Desert FESTA) has no effect and is no longer available. Other appearances: Rosa De Sharon, Fairy Puck (Berserk Collaboration, no longer available), and Nethersoul and Soulsoothing (only during the Cron Kingdom's Last Crown Prince event period); the page lists no separate effect for these.
