# Traveler's Horse

The Traveler's Horse is a loaner horse offered by Stable Keepers to make travel easier. This page covers where to borrow it, its skills, and its limits.

## Traveler's Horse overview

The Old Moon Guild, which manages all stables in Black Desert, added the Traveler's Horse to the town Stable Keepers so Adventurers can travel more easily. You borrow it by talking to a Stable Keeper. Only one Traveler's Horse can be borrowed at a time, and no other can be borrowed while one is out.

## Traveler's Horse skills and limits

The Traveler's Horse has Start Accel, Sprint, Drift, and Instant Accel, with Skill Training at 100%. Mount skills are covered on the Training page. A dismounted Traveler's Horse returns to the nearest Stable after 5 minutes. It also returns to a Stable when you click Disconnect, Character Selection, or Change Character. It cannot equip horse gear, though it can still Sprint without a saddle. These functions are unavailable for it: Sell, Imperial Delivery, Horse Market, Remote Collection, and Stable Recovery. Its Mount Information window can still be opened.
