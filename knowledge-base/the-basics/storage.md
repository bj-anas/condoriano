# Storage

Town storage in Black Desert: where to find Storage Keepers, how slots work, moving items between towns, gold bars, and how storage ties into crafting.

## Storage overview

Characters have limited inventory slots and weight, so town storages keep items safe. They are also central to crafting in workshops and to workers' daily routines. Each town's storage is separate: items left in Velia's storage cannot be taken out in Heidel. Not every town has storage. To check, open the World Map (hotkey: M) and press the town's node icon; if there is storage, the storage window opens right away.

## Storage: using slots and history

Talk to a Storage Keeper and choose the Storage option (hotkey: 2). Per the Storage page a town storage starts with 16 slots (the Inventory page says 8). To add slots, buy town houses with contribution points and set their usage to storage; higher house levels give more slots. A house on a farm adds slots to the nearest town. Character-bound items cannot go in storage. Stored items cannot be stolen or vanish, but anything with a price guarantee, such as fish, or with an expiry date, such as some summon scrolls and event items, can still decay or expire while sitting there. Below the slots, the window shows the approximate Central Market value in silver of the items stored in that town. Storage History lists up to 500 deposit and withdrawal records, including auto-move to storage, saved only on the PC you play on. A Storage Note function lets you record notes for each town's storage.

## Storage: Transport between towns

Transport moves items from one town's storage to another. Choose Transport (hotkey: 3) with a Storage Keeper. The Status tab shows all current transports, for your town and worldwide. The Send tab lets you choose the destination storage and the transport type (wagons #1 through #5, or a trade wagon for trade items), then right-click items to load them. It shows available wagons, the wagon's weight limit, free slots, and the silver fee. The fee is cheap if both towns are connected by the node system and tripled if not. Press Send to queue it; freight wagons and trade ships carry the items in real time. At the destination Storage Keeper, press Get All to deposit arrived items into storage. You can also do this remotely from the World Map (M), where a present icon above the town icon shows arrival.

## Storage: Magnus linked storage

Because most cities connect to the Magnus, you can move and store items from your inventory into storages in other territories remotely, through Storage Keepers, or without a keeper through a storage maid. This unlocks territory by territory as you progress the Magnus main questline. If you already have items in some town storages, the Auto-move function moves all items from your inventory to all storages, including the open one, at once. It only works in territories revealed on the map and not in the bandit town of Muiquun. Items this move cannot handle: treasure items, trade or bartering goods, and Ornette's or Odore's Spirit Essence along with their lesser versions. See the Abyss One: The Magnus page for more.

## Storage: gold bar exchange and Central Market warehouse

The Storage Keeper's Currency Exchange option opens an NPC shop for buying and selling Gold Bars, from 1G to 10,000G. Each Gold Bar 1G exchanges for 100,000 Silver. Gold bars are needed for some NPC exchanges and are rewarded by many quests, and this shop converts them to silver. Buying costs slightly more than selling: a Gold Bar 1G worth 100,000 silver costs 100,100 silver to buy. Separately, the Warehouse button in the storage window opens the Central Market warehouse window, where you can move silver and items between the town storage or your inventory and the Central Market warehouse. See the Central Market page for more.

## Storage: workers and crafting

Workers in town workshops take crafting materials from the storage of the town they belong to. For example, with a furniture workshop house in Eilton, an Eilton worker needs the materials in Eilton's storage, while an Altinova worker needs them in Altinova's storage. A worker whose town shows "Not Connected" in red cannot gather materials from their own town for a workshop elsewhere. For an Altinova worker to use Altinova's storage and craft in Eilton, both towns must be connected by the node system. See the Node and Crafting pages for more.
