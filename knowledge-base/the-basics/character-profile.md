# Character Profile

The Character Profile window shows a character's basic and battle statistics, life skill information, rankings, titles, journal, challenges, and history.

## Character Profile overview

The top left of the Character Profile shows your character picture, class icon, level, character name, family name, health, MP, weight, and the character's total playtime. Level measures progression and strength; leveling up gives skill points, more carry weight, more health points, resistances, and more. A monster's name color shows its level difference from you: low-level monsters give reduced rewards, while higher-level ones give more experience but are harder. Family Playtime is the total days played across all characters; hover over it to see hours. A character's own playtime is in the History tab.

## Character Profile: family name

The family name is unique across all your characters on a server (NA or EU) and shows above every character. After creating your first character and finishing the tutorial, a prompt to set it appears, and you can press Enter to open the interface whenever you like. Until then you have a temporary name such as Adventurer6583. Once set, it can only be changed with a coupon gifted at occasional events or bought in the Pearl Shop.

## Character Profile: health

Health points (HP) match your overall level and health level, and set how much damage you can take. Lost HP regenerates over time and can be restored quickly with potions and certain skills. At zero HP the character is knocked unconscious. You can then revive on the spot with a special item or return to the nearest node or town.

## Character Profile: skill resource types

Each class uses its own skill resource. Each class draws on one of three resources, listed in the table below. MP and WP recover gradually over time; SP does not.

| Skill resource | Classes |
|---|---|
| Willpower (WP) | Warrior, Berserker, Lahn, Musa, Maehwa, Mystic, Striker, Kunoichi, Ninja, Guardian, Drakania |
| Magic Power (MP) | Sorceress, Tamer, Witch, Wizard, Hashashin, Corsair, Sage, Ranger, Archer, Shai, Woosa, Maegu, Dark Knight |
| Sacred Power (SP) | Valkyrie, Nova |

Most skills spend resource, though some recover resource when used (sometimes only on an opponent). More powerful skills cost more, and using a skill with its own key command can cost less than using it from a quick slot.

## Character Profile: restoring skill resource

MP, WP, and SP are restored by MP potions, which General Goods Vendor NPCs sell in each town and some quests reward; the amount restored matches the potion size (small, medium, large, extra-large). To save weight you can use Herbal Juice. Pick any one of these wild plants: Weeds, Sunrise Herb, Silver Azalea, Dry Mane Grass, Silk Honey Grass, or Fire Flake Flower. Combine it with Mineral Water through Simple Alchemy (hotkey: L). Further Simple Alchemy steps turn Herbal Juice into Concentrated Herbal Juice, then High-Concentrated Herbal Juice, then Refined Herbal Juice, each more potent than the one before. Skill specialization (Skill Add-ons) can add MP/WP/SP recovery effects to a skill. A new Skill Addon slot opens at each of Lv. 50, 52, 54, 56, 58, and 60. Each slot takes one skill carrying two effects, which adds up to 10 effects. See the Skills page.

## Character Profile: weight

Weight is how heavy your carried items are compared to your weight limit. Beyond the limit, movement and attack speed gradually fall. The maximum is 170% of the limit, with 100% being where penalties start, and at that point you may not be able to acquire or buy more items. Speed is normal up to 100%, slightly slower at 101 ~ 120%, a lot slower at 121 ~ 150%, and you may be unable to move after that. You cannot jump once weight exceeds 120%. Mounts such as donkeys, mini elephants, and wagons carry more than characters.

## Character Profile: fame, Karma, and Naval Fame

Fame rises with character levels, Life Skill levels, knowledge entries, and Contribution Points; higher fame gives benefits such as more daily Silver or an additional +10% item drop rate. Karma is family-wide: it goes up by defeating monsters and goes down if you kill other players without their consent, steal from NPCs, or kill protected animals, for instance horses or the wildlife of Kamasylvia. A Lv. 50 or above character with PvP mode (Alt+C) active loses Karma for attacking or defeating another Adventurer. Below 0, your name is red, town guards attack you, and anyone may attack you without PvP mode. Dying with negative Karma can bring heavy penalties, such as lower enhancement levels on worn gear. See the Karma and Death Penalties pages. Naval Fame is a family-wide sea standing: above 0 you are a law-abiding marine, below 0 a pirate. Damaging or destroying another Adventurer's ship lowers it. Pirate ships are marked red and can be attacked without PvP mode, and the only place a pirate can restock supplies is the Kuit Islands pirate haven. Naval fame recovers by defeating sea monsters and doing repeatable quests from Hans in Velia.

## Character Profile: energy and contribution points

Energy is a renewable point currency spent on NPC interactions and certain actions, mainly to raise amity and get knowledge entries. The maximum is shared by your whole family, but current points are spent per character. It restores 1 point every 3 minutes online with a character and 1 per hour for offline characters. Repeatable town quests, a bed in your residence, certain places, and energy potions from NPC Alustin or the Central Market speed this; event versions are called energy tonics, also earned from the Black Spirit's Adventure minigame. Per this page the maximum starts at 30 and rises with each knowledge topic completed (the Energy page says 50). Contribution Points show how much you have contributed to the world's societies, and are used for node investment, trade routes, workers, houses, and rented items. They are not spent like energy, since undoing the investment or returning the rental gives them back. Raise them with quests and by exchanging life skill byproducts. See the Energy and Contribution pages.

## Character Profile: skill points, musicianship, enhancement chance, and horoscope

Skill Points come from skill experience earned by defeating monsters and completing quests, and are spent to acquire and upgrade combat skills. Musicianship rises by playing songs and has no effect on your character. Enhancement Chance is the character's total enhancement chance, and hovering over the icon shows the breakdown. Horoscope shows the character's constellation; you earn more amity when conversing with NPCs of the same horoscope, and an NPC's horoscope is in the amity window. The horoscope must be set at character creation and cannot be changed.

## Character Profile: fitness

Fitness has three parts. Breath rises by walking or running without a mount and permanently increases stamina. Strength rises by traveling with a trade pack, which shows a backpack on your character and slows movement while trade goods are in the inventory, and permanently raises the weight limit. Health rises by eating food, whether cooked yourself, a quest reward, or bought on the Central Market, and permanently raises total HP.

## Character Profile: abilities

Each ability has 5 levels, raised by equipment, dishes, elixirs, and more, and lowered by debuffs, gear that reduces it, or an expired buff. Attack Speed and Casting Speed apply to different classes, listed in the table below. Movement Speed makes you move faster. Critical Hit raises critical hit rate. Fishing shortens the time until a fish bites. Gathering shortens all gathering activities. Luck raises your item drop rate.

| Ability | Classes it affects when they use skills |
|---|---|
| Attack Speed | Warrior, Hashashin, Ranger, Guardian, Drakania, Corsair, Lahn, Berserker, Tamer, Shai, Striker, Musa, Maehwa, Mystic, Kunoichi, Ninja, Dark Knight, Archer |
| Casting Speed | Sage, Sorceress, Nova, Woosa, Maegu, Valkyrie, Witch, Wizard |

## Character Profile: AP, DP, combat type, and stamina

AP is the total attack power of worn equipment and affects damage dealt; it excludes bonuses from magic crystals or skill add-ons. DP is the total defense power of worn equipment and reduces damage taken. Combat Type is melee, ranged, or magic. Stamina is how long a character can stay active without tiring; more stamina lets you sprint (Shift + W) longer and use stamina skills more often. At zero stamina you cannot sprint and swimming speed drops drastically. Stamina recovers quickly when you are not sprinting, swimming, diving, or using a skill, and does not recover naturally during those.

## Character Profile: resistance and item drop rate

Higher resistance makes debuffs from monsters less likely to affect you. Every character has a base 20% for each type, raisable by equipment, consumables, skills, and magic crystals. Resistance does not apply in PvP. Types are Stun/Stiffness/Freezing, Knockdown/Bound, Grapple, and Knockback/Floating. Item Drop Rate shows your current total; it normally tops out at a 300% increase. Four sources can push the cap to 400%: Territory Occupation benefits, Blessing of the Thriving Earth, node investment, and playing on the Arsha Server.

## Character Profile: Life Skills information

Each Life Skill activity earns experience, and higher levels unlock extra rewards or better efficiency, plus Life Skill clothes from luxury goods merchants and better tools at certain levels. Gathering: higher levels raise the chance of not using energy. Fishing: more frequent seagull flocks (always yellow-grade fish) and slower rod durability loss; auto-fishing can discard white and green fish from Professional 1, plus blue from Artisan 1, plus yellow from Master 1. Hunting: better matchlocks, and from Guru you can load 4 shots instead of 3. Cooking, Alchemy, and Processing: more items produced and more item types. Training: more mount experience, higher horse capture rates, a better chance for skills and base speed, and more silver from the horse and breeding markets. Trading: higher bargaining profits. Farming: less energy use and faster harvest, breeding, pruning, and pest killing. Sailing: more ship skills, with experience from sailing the open sea and quests. Barter: less parley needed per attempt. The Learn More tab opens the Life Skills mastery tab; see the Life Skill Overview page.

## Character Profile: rankings and titles

Rankings compare fields such as Life Skill levels, overall wealth, character growth, and Red Battlefield score, showing family name, character name, and guild for places 1st to 30th. Outside the top 30 you get a position from A to E. Rankings only count Adventurers on the same server. Titles mark milestones such as defeating a strong boss, getting a rare item, advancing Life Skills, or joining an event. They are split into four categories: World, Combat, Life, and Fishing, and each lists how to earn it. A title can be shown above your name by pressing "Apply". One title has no effect alone, but the more titles you own, the more maximum energy and stamina, item drop rate via Luck, and experience bonus you get.

## Character Profile: journal, challenges, and history

The Journal automatically records actions from character creation, by day, with exact times on hover: level ups, enhancement attempts, quests, titles, and more. Challenges are an achievement system whose rewards help progress; all are shown from character creation. Tabs cover goal challenges such as leveling, playtime challenges, event challenges, completed ones, and rewards to claim. Playtime counts only time logged in with a character in the world, not the launcher or title screen. A bottom right notification shows unclaimed rewards. The History tab shows monsters defeated, items fished, items looted, and weight looted, for 1 day, a week, a month, and total.
