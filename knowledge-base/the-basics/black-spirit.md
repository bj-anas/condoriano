# Black Spirit

The Black Spirit is the player's companion and guide in Black Desert. This page covers how it works, how to summon it, and what each of its menus does.

## Black Spirit overview

The Black Spirit starts as a floating ball of black smoke with glowing red eyes and changes appearance as the player progresses. It offers quests throughout the game, often triggered by exploring new areas, accepting other NPC quests, or reaching certain levels. Some of its quests send the player to ancient artifacts that unlock new Black Spirit abilities. A player can summon the Black Spirit at any time with the comma key (,); the default key depends on the keyboard layout. Some Black Spirit quests let you summon a boss monster.

## Black Spirit quest required for Lv. 50

To level up to Lv. 50, a character must complete a quest from the Black Spirit. If your character has full EXP but cannot level up, the Black Spirit quest is the reason. Lv. 50 matters because it unlocks the game's PvP content.

## Black Spirit Main Quest and Quest menus

The Black Spirit's Main Quest menu lists the main quests currently available to your character: each territory's main questline story, the character's Awakening and Succession quests, and extra quests unlocked by those two, such as gift items. The Quest menu holds the Black Spirit's other quests: suggested quests (mostly introductions to game features, plus long secondary questlines such as Crow's Nest or [Elvia] Obsidian Ashes), recurring daily or weekly quests, and event quests. Many recurring quests reward boss-summon scrolls, and defeating those bosses gives looted items and black stones. Scroll quests unlock as you progress through the main quests.

## Black Spirit Exchange and Enhancement menus

The Exchange menu lets you give your character's energy to the Black Spirit in return for buffs. Energy has many other uses, so exchange it this way only when the situation calls for it. The Enhancement menu lets you improve equipment with black stones. Enhancement stats overwhelm the default values of any equipment, so it becomes necessary as you progress.

## Black Spirit Item Reform menu

The Item Reform menu gives equipment additional effects by using reform stones. Weapon and armor reform stones for non-boss equipment can be crafted by setting a house's purpose to refinery and sending workers; the stone's grade changes the result. Unique stones that improve boss awakening weapons and boss sub-weapons are looted from world bosses. Crystals called cups, crafted from materials gained in Elvia content, improve yellow-grade accessories. If you hold a valid equipment piece and a matching reform stone, the window tells you in its bottom right section.

## Black Spirit Guide menu

The Guide menu helps with non-combat activities such as fishing and trading. It has many guide objectives, and as you advance through the Life skills, the Black Spirit gives newer guide objectives for them, marked as leap quests. It is worth listening whenever the Black Spirit speaks, since it gives important information.

## Black Spirit appearance

An icon in the bottom left of the Black Spirit interface opens the appearance selection. You can only choose forms you have unlocked, and the feature becomes available after a certain point in the main questline. Most appearances come from completing each region's main quests. The ultimate Black Spirit appearance is only obtained by completing Igor's Bartali Adventure Log chapter 15.
