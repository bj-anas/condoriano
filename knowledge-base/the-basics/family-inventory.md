# Family Inventory

The Family Inventory is a shared bag that every character in a Family can open. This page covers what it can hold, how to unlock it, and how far it can be expanded.

## Family Inventory overview

The Family Inventory is a bag that all characters in the same Family can access through the same window, so items stored in it are available to all your characters. It only accepts certain item types. For the basics of the normal character inventory, see the Inventory page instead.

## Family Inventory allowed items

These item types can be stored in the Family Inventory: scrolls; food and elixirs; pet feed; worker stamina recovery food; some seals and content currencies (boss auras are one example); and items that restore stamina or health for horses, camels, elephants, donkeys, and alpacas. Always check an item's tooltip to see whether it can be placed there.

## Family Inventory forbidden items

Several things are refused by the Family Inventory: anything bound to a character, Oasis event items, purchases from the Guild Shop, items tied to specific content such as Guild or Red Battlefield, and event items whose tooltip lacks a "Can be placed in the Family Inventory" line.

## Family Inventory unlock quest

From Lv. 60, the Black Spirit can give you the quest "Black Spirit's Gift (Family Inventory)". It also shows in the Quest window (hotkey: O), Suggested tab, under [ADV Support] [Lv. 60] Family-fitting Inventory. At Lv. 60 a Family Inventory button appears in the character inventory, and pressing it leads to the quest. The questline rewards one Family Inventory +2 Expansion Coupon, which is 2 slots, and starts the Family Inventory Weight at 100LT. To move items between the normal inventory and the Family Inventory, press the Move button at the bottom right of the Family Inventory.

## Family Inventory expansion and weight limit

Accepting the Black Spirit quest "Black Spirit's Gift (Family Inventory Weight Limit+)" gives +50 weight limit for free. More slots come from Family Inventory Expansion coupons bought in the Pearl Shop with Pearls and Loyalties, and extra weight capacity comes from Family Inventory Weight Limit coupons bought the same way. The maximum is 90 slots and a weight capacity of 1,250LT. Some expansion slots can only be obtained through special events.
