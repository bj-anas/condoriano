# Pets

Pets are companions whose main job is auto-loot. This page covers how to get them, feeding, tiers, commands, special abilities, talents, exchange, and appearance.

## Pets overview

Pets mainly exist for auto-loot: a fed pet picks up the loot dropped by defeated monsters, so you can keep fighting hordes without stopping. A few pets come from main questline quests, some are login rewards, more come from temporary in-game and out-of-game events, and most are bought with pearls in the Pearl Shop (hotkey: F3). Obtained pets show up as pet emblems in your inventory (hotkey: I), in the Inventory or Pearl tab. To register one, right click the pet emblem and give the pet a name.

## Pets: quest pets

Quest pets are in the Suggested tab of the Quest window (hotkey: O) under [ADV Support] Pets, Loyal Companions, and each can be completed once per Family.

- Bartali's Buster: quest "[Pet] Bartali's Buster" from Igor Bartali or Bayan, after any Balenos main questline or progress in the Mountain of Eternal Winter main questline. Talent: +1% Skill Exp.
- Lauren's Fluffy: quest "[Pet] Lauren's Fluffy" after any Serendia main questline or Mountain of Eternal Winter progress. Talent: -1% Death Penalty Reduction.
- Kalis' Delphe: quest "[Pet] Kalis' Delphe" from Herman Feresio in Calpheon City or Puteaux in the Mountain of Eternal Winter, after the Calpheon main questline. Talent: +5% Normal and +1% Higher Grade Knowledge Gain Chance.
- Bareeds' Junaid: quest "[Pet] Bareeds' Junaid" from Prince Bareeds III after the Mediah main questline. Talent: +1% Life EXP Boost.
- Marumin's Squawky: quest "[Pet] Marumin's Squawky" after finishing the Valencia main questline. Talent: +5% Normal and +1% Higher Grade Knowledge Gain Chance.
- Prankkebi: a dokkebi pet after the Land of the Morning Light log "The Eyes of Adventure" (Chapter 8.5). Talent: +1% Item Drop Rate Increase.

## Pets: Pet List, summoning, and groups

Finishing registration opens the Pet List automatically. Later, find it in the Function tab of the main menu (Esc) or with the paw button below your health bar, which appears after you obtain your first pet. The list shows your pets, their information, and management options. Up to five pets can be summoned at once. Press the Take Out button beside a pet to summon it. For group summoning, give a pet a flagged number with the icon beside its portrait, then click the matching icon at the bottom of the menu. Pet groups help when managing many pets: set up groups for your needs and use the Call Group feature to summon them conveniently. Above the Feed All button, a Rename Group button helps you tell groups apart.

## Pets: hunger and feeding

A pet that is not picking up items is hungry. Pets start at 100% hunger level, which drops once summoned, and at 0% a summoned pet stops retrieving loot (and its talents stop working). Feed raises the hunger level. Feed is sold mainly by Stable Keeper NPCs and by other Adventurers on the Central Market, and can be crafted by cooking. When you feed one pet or all of them, you choose the feed type, and higher quality feed fills more hunger.

## Pets: tiers

A pet's maximum tier is 5. Higher tiers pick up loot faster, hold more talents, and have stronger talent buffs. A tier 1 pet has 2 talents, a tier 4 pet has 4 talents, and so does a tier 5 pet. For tier 5 details, see the Tier 5 Pet Training/Alpha Appointment page.

## Pets: behavior and commands

Pet commands toggle how a pet acts. Behavior is Cautious (slowest), Normal (default), or Agile (fastest); a more active mode picks up items faster but drains hunger faster. Right click the behavior icon to change all pets at once. Follow / Stay makes pets follow you or stand still, and a pet too far away reappears nearby. Special toggles the pet's special ability, and Fetch toggles auto-loot. Hunger 0 stops loot pickup and talents.

## Pets: special abilities

Each pet has a special ability, off when first summoned, and enabled by left click (that pet only) or right click (all summoned pets). There are seven abilities:

| Special ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hostility Detection | Marks Adventurers from hostile guilds and those with negative karma |
| Finds Resources | Goes to a gatherable resource and marks it, repeating every few seconds |
| Finds Rare Monsters | Goes to rare (elite) monsters and marks them |
| Taunt Monster | Pulls a monster toward you |
| Resistant to Desert Illnesses | Gives resistance to heatstroke and hypothermia |
| Auto-Fishing Time Reduction | Shortens the auto-fishing timer |
| Gathering Item Quantity Increase | Increases the amount of items from the gathering and hunting life skills |

Hostility Detection and Finds Rare Monsters put a red marker on your screen; toggle the pet's special command to hide it.

## Pets: stacking special abilities

Finds Resources, Hostility Detection, Taunt Monster, and Finds Rare Monsters stack across pets. Auto-Fishing Time Reduction, Resistant to Desert Illnesses, and Gathering Item Quantity Increase do not stack, so only one pet's effect applies. For example, four summoned pets all with Auto-Fishing Time Reduction still give the effect of only 1.

## Pets: talent effects

Talents are inherent to a pet and present when you first get it; main skills come when its tier improves. The number of learnable talents rises with tier. Talent types: Life EXP Boost, Durability Reduction Resistance, Combat EXP Gain Boost, Skill EXP Boost, Death Penalty Reduction, Item Drop Rate Increase, Max HP Increase, Normal and Higher Grade Knowledge Gain Chance Boost, Weight Limit Increase, Mount EXP Increase, and Big Ship Inventory Weight +. Main skill types: Luck +1, Combat EXP Boost, Karma Recovery Increase, Cooking/Alchemy/Processing/Farming EXP Boost, Fishing/Hunting/Training/Trading/Gathering EXP Boost, and Fishing/Gathering Level +1.

## Pets: exchange and tiers

Pet exchange raises a pet's tier by trading several pets for one better pet. Up to 5 pets can be exchanged for 1 at a time, and more pets exchanged at once improves the odds of a better result. Learnable talents by tier: Tier 1 and Tier 2: 2; Tier 3: 3; Tier 4 and Tier 5: 4. Only pets of the same type can be exchanged together. Types: Classic (always in the Pearl Shop), Premium (always in the Pearl Shop), Rare (limited availability in the Pearl Shop), Special (obtainable in-game), and Event (from events).

## Pets: how to exchange

Press Exchange in the Pet List to open the Exchange Pet window. Among checked-in pets, press each pet's exchange button to select or deselect it. With at least 2 selected, pick the appearance and talents to inherit. The Skill button shows obtainable skills, and the Skill Learning Chance button shows the chance of each talent. Name the new pet too. If the result has a tier that can hold more talents than you inherit, it can learn extra ones. The resulting pet inherits the talent of the appearance you choose, and appearance is not linked to tier: with a tier 3 and a tier 1 pet, picking the tier 1 appearance gives that look at any tier. The Appearance button under a pet lets it keep its current look.

## Pets: exchange odds and Wizard Gosphy

The tier increase chance depends on how many pets you exchange and their tiers. Exchanging 2 tier 1 pets gives a 55% chance of a tier 3 pet and a 45% chance of a tier 2 pet. Adding a third tier 1 pet makes a tier 3 pet certain. Higher starting tiers need more pets; for a 100% chance of a tier 4 pet, 4 tier 3 pets of the same type (rare) are needed. Wizard Gosphy pairs with a pet of any tier and adds a bonus chance of a higher-tier result. The pets it cannot be exchanged with are a second Wizard Gosphy, Young Crimson Dragon, Nouvermon, and Pit-A-Pat Mole.

## Pets: Pet Appearance Change Coupon

Some pets can change appearance through pet exchange or with a Pet Appearance Change Coupon, which alters coloring, adds a unique pet costume, or changes the look entirely. Not all pets can change. The coupon guarantees the appearance you choose, while an exchange may not change the look.

## Pets: Gathering Item Quantity Increase items

The Gathering Item Quantity Increase ability raises the quantity from both gathering and hunting. Affected gathering items include ores and melted shards (Iron Ore, Copper Ore, Titanium Ore, Mythril, Obsidian Ore), rough and cut gems (Rough Ruby, Ruby, Diamond), crystals and coral, mushrooms and hyphae, herbs and flowers, seeds and crops, timber, planks, and saps (Ash Timber, Pine Plank, Birch Sap), hides, blood, meat, and feathers, seafood such as Clam, Lobster, Oyster, Shrimp, Abalone, Kelp, and Seaweed, plus Caphras Stone, Ancient Spirit Dust, and Laila's Petal. The page also lists butchering results for hunted animals such as Grass Rhino, King Griffon, Shadow Lion, Winter Bear, Gazelle, Giant Boar, Humpback Whale, Blue Whale, and Fugitive Khalk. The wiki page has the full item list.
