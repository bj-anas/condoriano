# World Map

The World Map is Black Desert's fully 3D map. This page covers opening it, its icons, navigation, node, house, and worker tools, the top menus, and the information filters.

## World Map overview and opening

The World Map is rendered in 3D and doubles as a management screen for trade routes, workers, and resources. To open it you must be level 15 or higher, or have completed the tutorial. Press M, or press Esc and click the World Map icon in the center. Clicking the New Menu button at the bottom of the Esc menu switches to a renewed menu interface. Unexplored areas stay under fog until discovered.

## World Map: city, town, and house icons

Major city (Node): where most NPCs for trading, warehouse, housing, transport, and the character transport facility are found, such as Velia, Heidel, and Calpheon. These can be base nodes upon discovery, and a Chief or lord-grade NPC shows the Lordship Menu. Town (Node): a general town with most merchants but some services missing, such as a warehouse. Empty House: each house can be set to a different use by investing contribution points, such as more town storage, refineries, residences, and workshops; the use can be changed anytime and contribution points come back, but silver costs are not refunded. Guild House: acquired by bidding in auctions, and can produce certain items. Manors: several exist, each with its own icon; a manor works as a residence and can be assigned to your guild so all members get guild buffs from any location. An active rental contract is required to enter, though items inside stay safe if it expires. Investment Bank: assign workers and invest gold bars for profit, but deposits may lose capital, and it can only be used by Adventurers with a Rank 1 residence in that town or major city.

## World Map: node and subnode icons

Trade (Node): trade, house, and production nodes are available. Gateway (Node): a borderline area with trade at large camps, ongoing night raids, and occasional NPCs such as a General Goods Vendor. Danger Zone (Node): a dangerous area surrounded by monsters, sometimes with an affiliated Production Node. Pathway (Node): a node manager alone or an object replacing the manager. Subnodes, which provide materials when you assign a worker, are Excavation (excavation materials such as traces), Mining (minerals and varied materials), Farming (farming materials), and Lumbering (lumbering materials).

## World Map: marker icons

Cargo appears beside a major city or town when items sent by transport arrive, updating in real time. Party member shows you and your party members live. Bandits shows where bandits appear, only when you hold trade goods or ride a mount loaded with them. My Mount shows your horse or camel, and My Ship shows your raft, rowboat, or fishing boat. Death marks where you and your guild mates died. NPC Services (Knowledge) icons are gray until you gain the matching knowledge and then turn green; they show NPC location and class, and where monsters and fish appear. Abyssal Well icons are fast travel points between major cities for a small fee, and need certain quest requirements. All icons can be right-clicked to create an auto-route, which you follow by pressing T after leaving the map.

## World Map: compass, sun, and moon

A compass circle around your character shows weather and time. The sun and moon positions update in real time, and in-game time appears next to them. In Black Desert the sun and moon rise in the northwest and move to the southeast. Rainy areas show a rain effect on the map, including at sea. White translucent arrows on the circle point to discovered towns: the smallest circle's arrow marks a nearby town and the second largest marks a town farther away. Hover over an arrow to see the name. Only towns you have already found show.

## World Map: navigation and node, house, transport, and worker tools

Right-click an icon or node to show a route with a light pillar at the target, then press Auto Run (T) to move automatically. Right-click again to cancel. You can invest or retrieve contribution points in nodes on the map. For Transport, click a town node, open the warehouse menu at the bottom right, and press Transport at the bottom left. Houses show as blue (available now), gray (needs a preceding house first in some cases), or white with an icon on the right (already bought and assigned). Houses cost contribution points, returned when sold. Workers sent to invested production nodes store produce in the town warehouse, but they use Worker Stamina each time they work; an exhausted worker must be fired or have stamina restored.

## World Map: top left search, filters, and node list

The search filter finds nodes, locations, or services; typing "stable" lists every discovered Stable Keeper nearby. Left-click centers the camera on a result and right-click creates a navigation route. Map filters let you check or uncheck displayed icons to reduce clutter. Left-clicking a regional banner snaps the camera to that region's Stable Keeper. Node list overview highlights nodes that yield specific resources: choose a region and production type from the dropdowns and arrows point to matching nodes. Contribution points shows your family's maximum and available points.

## World Map: Snapshots, Connect Node Directly, Navigation Settings, and Retrieve Loop

The toolbar has thirteen buttons, listed in the table below.

| Position | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Left to right | World Map Snapshots, Connect Node Directly, Navigation Settings, Retrieve Loop, Quest, Barter Information, Find NPC, Horse Market, Stable, Transport, Central Market, Worker List, Map Guild Strategy |

World Map Snapshots saves camera bookmarks: place the camera, press Alt + 1 (or another 0-9 slot), then press that number with the map open to jump back; Alt + a slot overwrites. Connect Node Directly previews the Contribution Points and Energy needed to connect two nodes, for example Shakatu to Gahaz Bandits' Lair; if requirements are met, click "Connect Node". Navigation Settings tunes ocean autopathing: Ocean Navigation Setting cuts sharp turns for shorter routes, and Navigation Path Setting decides how strictly the vessel follows the route. Retrieve Loop saves up to 3 custom autopath routes: right-click to set a destination, hold ALT and right-click for more waypoints or a closed loop, then assign it to a slot.

## World Map: toolbar shortcut buttons

Quest shows your active quest log with the map open. Barter Information opens the Barter Information window for route and trade planning, with a pop-out button to move it to a second monitor; see the Sailing & Bartering page. Find NPC opens the Find NPCs search for any known NPC. Horse Market, Stable (all your stables from any town), Transport (list and status of active transports), Central Market, and Worker List each open that panel while viewing the map.

## World Map: Map Guild Strategy

Map Guild Strategy lets guilds plan for Node and Siege Wars by marking the map with custom shapes and colors for objectives, positions, and movement routes, shared with the guild. Using it requires the Map Guild Strategy authority; members without it can only view mappings. Drawings are saved per channel, so drawings on Edania 1 will not appear on Edania 2, and all drawings reset weekly during maintenance.

## World Map: top right information filters

The icons at the top right switch information views. General Information, the default, shows discovered areas, nodes, and quests. Groundwater Information shows water deposits in shades of blue; darker means more moisture, so farms placed in darker zones get higher humidity, and rainy areas have expanded deposits. Territory Resource Information shows bar graphs: the blue bar is fish available (at 100% abundant, dropping toward 0% as exhausted, and lower means longer fishing), and the green bar is gatherable resources for workers (lower means longer worker processes); percentages differ per channel. Temperature Information shows regional temperature, which with groundwater affects crop growth. Humidity Information shows regional humidity, and rain happens when temperature and humidity reach certain conditions.

## World Map: Node War, production nodes, and family characters

Node War Information shows Node War and Blue Battlefield occupation status where available; hover a node for schedule, max participants, and stat caps, and click a game mode name to open its wiki page. Production Node Information lets you check activated and deactivated production nodes, with a small menu at the top right that jumps the camera to the one you pick. View Family Characters shows all characters in your Family at a glance, so you can see where they are, move them, retrieve items, or leave notes.
