# Swimming & Diving

How movement in water works in Black Desert, covering swimming on the surface, diving underwater, and the Corsair's swimming skill.

## Swimming overview

Water is very different from land, where you can run and use mounts such as horses: in water you move at a slow swimming pace or go under to explore the depths. Swimming is mainly needed if your ship is destroyed at sea. The Escape function in the main menu (hotkey: Esc) moves you to the nearest safe point, but it has a long cooldown and cannot take you where you choose.

## Swimming speed and stamina

Swimming speed depends on your overall movement speed, so raise movement speed before a long swim. For example, transfuse a movement speed magic crystal into Talis equipment, which has movement speed set effects. Reaching movement speed level 5 this way greatly helps at sea. Swimming constantly uses stamina, and when stamina runs out swimming becomes much slower. To recover stamina while swimming, stop moving and press F, which recovers it slowly on the spot. Resting on an island or other dry land also works. You cannot change equipment while swimming.

## Swimming rescue with the Old Moon Flare

If you suddenly end up swimming, for example after falling off a liner or losing your ship, you can buy an Old Moon Flare. Open the main menu (Esc) and go to the Pearl Shop (also F3). Open the Loyalties tab, scroll to the Old Moon Flare, and buy one with Loyalties. Using it calls a rescue ship that carries you to either Oquilla's Eye or Lema Island.

## Diving controls and breath gauge

Press Q and use the WASD keys to dive. To rise, use the directional keys or press E. Unlike surface swimming, which only drains stamina and slows you down, diving shows a blue breath gauge. When it empties, the character stops, squirms, and floats up automatically. The gauge cannot be refilled underwater except at air bubbles found on the seafloor near the continents, and it refills instantly once you reach the surface. Watch it closely so you do not get stuck in an underwater cave.

## Swimming & Diving: diving tips and Corsair swim skill

Since breath is limited, dive to a certain depth to scout the area, then move above water to the spot you chose and dive the shortest distance to your objective. Elixirs and magic crystals increase underwater breathing time: transfuse the crystal into an equipment piece and drink the elixir. The Alchemy and Magic Crystal pages have more. Corsair is the only class with a special skill underwater: while swimming underwater, press Shift + W to turn into a mermaid for a few seconds and swim faster. The Corsair page has more.
