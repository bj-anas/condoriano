# Energy

Energy is the renewable resource spent when interacting with NPCs, objects, and many activities. This page covers what it is used for, how it recovers, and how to raise the maximum.

## Energy overview

Energy is a point pool tracked separately for each character in your family, spent mainly when interacting with NPCs and objects. For example, with a maximum of 50 you can spend 50 on character A and another 50 on character B. Its main uses are befriending townsfolk, acquiring knowledge entries, and some life skill activities. Energy can also pay for learning skills when no Skill Instructor NPC is nearby, and for content like looking up a trade good's price in a faraway town.

## Energy: recovery

Energy recovers 1 point every 3 minutes while you play that character, and 1 point every 1 hour while the character is logged off, so staying in the game refills it faster. Lying on a bed installed in a residence speeds recovery, as do certain places such as hot springs. Crafted energy potions refill energy quickly, and some quests, usually daily quests that require energy, return energy as a reward.

## Energy: maximum energy

Maximum energy starts at 50 points and rises each time you complete a knowledge topic category, for example Residents of Velia. Not every category raises it; check the Knowledge screen (hotkey: H). Completing a category usually adds a few points, and the game announces the category and the increase at the top of the screen. The easiest way to raise it is to talk to every NPC in a town and every node manager in the world. A higher maximum means a fuller pool takes longer to refill, so you worry less about spending it. With only 50, you cannot converse with NPCs, gather many items, or even reach node level 2, and viewing an item at Patrigio's secret shop needs 50 energy each.

## Energy: NPC conversation, greetings, and stealing

Conversation with NPCs raises Amity, which unlocks quests and knowledge entries. It cannot start if you lack the knowledge topics the NPC is interested in. Starting a conversation costs 2 energy at first, and the cost rises as your Amity with the NPC rises. A greeting (hand icon or hotkey F5) costs 3 energy and gives 3 Amity points. After a certain level the stealing icon becomes active when you stand behind an NPC; trying costs 5 energy and may steal their belongings, but failing greatly lowers Amity and also lowers your Karma. Learning knowledge entries from NPCs usually costs energy, with a different amount per entry.

## Energy: gathering, fishing, and trade

Gathering consumes 1 energy each time you gather something, except fishing, and the cost drops as the gathering life skill levels up; the same applies to gathering crops in farming. Fishing lets you spend up to 10 energy per line cast for a chance at one extra fish. Each bargain attempt with a Trade Manager NPC costs 5 energy. Returning a defense token for contribution experience costs 3 energy each. Every World Chat message costs 1 energy.

## Energy: workers, nodes, secret shop, and potions

Energy is consumed to view the workers available at a Work Supervisor NPC and to hire one. Investing energy in a node raises its level and the item drop rate of monsters in its area. Each time you want to see a new item in Patrigio's secret shop, you spend energy; prices there vary and are not guaranteed to beat the Central Market. Alustin (Velia), Purajin (Valencia City), and Celphie (O'draxxia) can bottle excess energy into energy potions, which can be sold to other Adventurers on the Central Market.

## Energy: raising maximum energy with knowledge

Maximum energy rises mostly through knowledge entries from discovering areas, talking to NPCs, defeating monsters, and quests. Most categories only need about 2/3 of their entries filled to give the increase. In the Ecology category, entries marked "Increase Energy by 2/2 [Obtained]" in blue are complete; white ones at 0/2 or 1/2 still have question-mark entries to fill by visiting the matching monster zone. Buffs that raise knowledge chance help but are not required. Monster knowledge can also come from gathering, such as collecting meat from a boar or ore from a stoneback crab.

## Energy: island knowledge for maximum energy

Island knowledge is among the easiest ways to raise maximum energy but needs deliberate effort. Prepare a ship (a rowboat works but is slow), sail from Velia, Port Epheria, or another wharf such as Altinova, explore each island, gather topography knowledge, and talk to each island's node manager, which sometimes requires unlocking the island entry too. This can raise maximum energy by 30~40 points. A season character that reaches Lv. 56 can get a challenge reward leading to a 7-day Splat Fisher's Clothes costume for swimming to islands quickly.

## Energy: topography and character knowledge tips

After the islands, collect topography and character entries in towns. Do topography first, then right-click the node on the world map (hotkey: M) to find its node manager. Raising Amity with nearby NPCs can sometimes give topography knowledge as a last resort. Some NPC entries need another NPC met first, named in yellow in the dialogue. Tips: Lani and Kina are wandering general goods vendors on farms near Velia, and Garnil is in a building near Tachros. Crucio Domongatt can be spoken to during the "[Crossroad] The Lamenting Head of the League of Merchants" quest. Herman Feresio needs 500 Amity with both Valks and Theophil Batian. Lehard Motenon needs Herman Feresio spoken to, 201 or more Amity with Bipache, and Leona's quest finished. Marco Faust appears during "[Crossroad] Silence, We're Done Here! in Keplan", and Tina needs 201 or more Amity with Fiory. Bavio is available after every other entry in the Stonebeak Shore category, and Porio, Latro, Actin' Cap'n Porio, and Commandant Porio are all at Sarma Outpost.

## Energy: territorial ecology knowledge tips

Monster knowledge tips by territory. Balenos: Cox Pirates need about level 55 and suitably enhanced equipment. Serendia: Big Horn Triangle Head Salamander, Strong Altar Imp Trainer, and Swamp Fogan Warrior are elite monsters that respawn slowly; Spiders need 251 Amity with Allan in Glish, and Cobwebs need 201 Amity with Amaranto in Glish; kill Briar Lightning Traps and Log Lightning Traps before they attack; Ambushed Naga is in two places under a bridge near Heidel; the only Red Orc Giant Altar is near the Watchtower; Wicked Cultist appears only at night in Dernyl Farm, with the entry from the quest Wicked Cultist (requires at least 31 Amity with Carlo DeRose); and Kzarka's entry comes from Hakkon in Serendia Shrine at Amity above 50. Calpheon: Water Strider and Jellyfish at Lake Kaia need a rented matchlock.

## Energy: Adventure Journal and Academics knowledge tips

These entries come from quests, conversing with NPCs, and interacting with objects like stone statues and bookcases. Many NPCs' stories unlock as Amity rises; the Velian Bookshelf entry needs at least 100 Amity with Sealus, the furniture dealer in Velia. The Calpheon and Valkyries category raises energy by 7 and is among the easiest, since most entries come from reading books in the Holy College of Calpheon City library. The entry A Talk with Elion needs the quest "A Talk with Elion" from priestess Annolisa Rosie, available after completing "Balker's Idol" from Balker Batian. Raise your character first and fill missing entries gradually.
