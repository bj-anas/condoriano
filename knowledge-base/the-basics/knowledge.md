# Knowledge

The Knowledge system collects what your characters learn about the world, its events, and its inhabitants.

## Knowledge overview and energy rewards

Knowledge is organized into categories, opened from the main menu (Esc) or with the hotkey H. Completing a category rewards an increase in your family's maximum energy per character; for example, collecting every entry in Character > People of Eastern Balenos > Residents of Velia raises maximum energy by 2 points. Completed categories show in blue with [Obtained]; incomplete ones show in white. A category only appears in the Knowledge screen after you obtain your first entry in it, so a category some content asks for (such as Exploration I in Academics) may be missing until you get one entry. Unobtained entries show as question marks with simple hints on hover.

## Knowledge grades and monster knowledge

Some knowledge, such as monsters in the Ecology section, has grades, and higher grades give better effects. Without knowledge of a monster, its health bar only changes color from yellow (full) to orange to red (nearly defeated); with it, you see the bar deplete in combat. To raise a monster's grade, keep defeating it for a chance at a higher grade, which automatically replaces a lower grade of the same topic. For some enemies you must delete the entry and earn it again. Deleting is done through NPC Annolisa Rosie at the Holy College of Calpheon, selecting the entry in the knowledge management window, and costs 10 energy. Some monster entries cannot be regained once deleted because they are only given once. Knowledge also helps raise Amity when talking to NPCs.

## Knowledge bookshelves

At Annolisa Rosie in the Holy College of Calpheon you can turn a completed category into a bookshelf. Entries are taken randomly from the completed category and deleted from your Knowledge screen. The bookshelf can be used 10 times with a 1-hour cooldown between uses, and can be sold on the Central Market. A bookshelf bought from the Central Market and installed in your residence can be interacted with to gain entries.

## Knowledge categories: Character, Topography & Sea, Ecology

The wiki says knowledge has 9 main categories and describes these first. Completion rewards differ by topic. Character entries are sorted by territory and type and come mostly from talking to NPCs, though some need main questline progress or befriending another NPC, shown in yellow text. Topography & Sea entries are sorted by territory and come mostly from exploring, though some need conversation, Amity, a little energy, or a quest. Ecology holds each territory's monster knowledge, earned at a chance when defeating monsters. Buffs saying "Knowledge Gain Chance +X%" raise that chance, and "Higher Grade Knowledge Gain Chance +X%" raises the chance of a better grade. Ecology entries build ecology points, which give extra item drop rate, shown on the main Knowledge screen.

## Knowledge categories: Adventure Journal, Academics, Life Skill, and others

Adventure Journal entries come mostly from quest rewards and interacting with world objects, plus NPCs you befriend. Academics covers world background such as culture, religions, history, and politics, and is obtained the same way. Life Skill entries cover life skill activities except trade goods, such as certificates for processing, gathered items, dishes, elixirs, fish, and processed materials, earned when you obtain the related item, or sometimes through quests or NPC talk. Life Skill and the categories after it do not raise maximum energy when completed. Learning the Ropes of Black Desert covers basics such as class Awakening story entries, certain special NPCs, and Fughar's basics. Trade covers trade goods once available, obtained from the Trade Manager NPCs in every city.

## Knowledge: how to get entries

Entry chance does not depend on character level, so a high-level character can get a low-level monster's entry. Sources:

- Talking to NPCs: those with an exclamation mark give their own entry, some give more for a little energy or Amity, and nameless NPCs like soldiers and villagers can give entries too.
- Quest rewards.
- Exploration: Topography & Sea entries from new areas.
- Life skill activities such as gathering, fishing, and hunting, at a chance.
- Production: cooking a dish, synthesizing an elixir, or processing a material always gives the entry.
- Hunting monsters, at a chance.
- Objects: node managers, books, and items like wall writing, campfires, and armor stands.
- Knowledge Bookshelf from the Central Market, installed in your residence.
- Knowledge Vendor NPCs, usually in the biggest cities, sell entries you learn immediately.

## Knowledge: NPC interests

An NPC's interest is the knowledge topic you talk about with them, shown as a small circle when close or in the Interest Knowledge Status window in the interaction screen. If you lack the minimum number of entries on the topic, you cannot start a conversation. Conversations earn Amity, which makes the NPC friendlier and is often required before they give further entries. The Amity page has more.

## Knowledge: hints from Dark Beer

To get hints about undiscovered entries, buy a Dark Beer from the innkeeper or cook in towns such as Velia and Heidel. While holding it, bring up particular topics with a villager or a drunk NPC inside an inn. When you leave the conversation, the result shows, either a tip or a list of NPCs who give entries, with their locations marked on your world map.

## Knowledge: family special fame

Your profile (hotkey: P) shows four family fame types: combat fame, life fame, special fame, and overall family fame. Special fame comes from the accumulated knowledge and contribution points of all your characters. Raising these fame types earns a daily silver allowance, which is delivered to the Black Spirit's Safe (hotkey: B).
