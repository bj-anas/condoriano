# Servers

How Black Desert servers (also called channels) work, and what each special server type does.

## Servers overview

Black Desert uses a One World System in which each region is its own world: North America (NA) is one world and Europe (EU) is another. Characters are reachable across the whole region regardless of server, and any character can enter any server apart from a few restrictions. Servers are grouped into server groups that share server chat, so a player in Serendia 1 can chat with players in Serendia 2.

## Servers: changing channels

Jumping from one server (channel) to another has a 5-minute waiting period. A guild can assign 3 channels as its Guild Base Servers, and all guild members can switch between those without any waiting period. In the server list, the red general prohibition icon is described as a 10-minute lockout on switching again after a move, and the Minimap page mentions a 3-minute cooldown, so the wiki gives different figures for the cooldown.

## Servers: Season servers

Only season characters can enter season servers, which are built around the season experience; some items found there have a use only in season content. Newcomers are advised to begin on the Season 1 (Novice only) server because it gives a smoother start, but it closes to an Adventurer once the combined level of all their characters exceeds 200. Seasons are now permanent and not tied to a schedule, so season servers are always available. Some items and functions are restricted there, such as Caphras enhancement and the alchemy stones Vell's Heart and Khan's Heart. The Season Server FAQ page has more.

## Servers: New Olvia growth servers

New Olvia servers are growth servers open only to new or returning Adventurers. Benefits: +150% combat experience and +50% skill experience from Lv. 50 onward; +100% life experience for any Life Skill under Artisan 1; and extra contribution experience from +150% down to +50%, decreasing as your contribution points rise. Characters can only level up to Lv. 61 (61.99%) there, and World Bosses do not appear. New Adventurers are those within 60 days (1440 hours) of creating a character family, or within 60 days (1440 hours) of setting up their secondary password. A returning Adventurer is someone who has stayed away from the game for 30 days or more (720 hours) and, over that same stretch, has not used the Web Central Market through either the Black Desert+ app or the official website.

## Servers: Arsha PvP servers

Arsha servers are PvP-centric. They give +50% item drop rate from monsters, and no form of PvP, even forced PvP, is penalized. PvP can only be activated by Adventurers above Lv. 50. Attacking to kill another Adventurer does not lower karma, but a character who already has negative karma still gets the usual non-karma penalties. In the Arsha: Anonymous server, opponents' names and guilds are hidden, and you need not worry about forced PvP being on. World Bosses do not appear in Arsha servers, normal or season. The PvP page has more.

## Servers: Hardcore Server

This PvP mode takes place in the Great Desert. You must create a fresh character for it, and only some classes can be chosen. Defeat monsters to earn Survival Points and gear, but other Adventurers can steal your points. You can play only 2 hours per day. When done, offer Survival Points at designated desert locations to receive Hardcore Honor, which can be traded for rewards. The Hardcore Server page has more.

## Servers: Node and Conquest War servers

Most servers ending in 1 host daily node and conquest wars, where monsters disappear and passersby can get dragged in. The servers are Balenos 1, Serendia 1, Calpheon 1, Mediah 1, Valencia 1, and Kamasylvia 1. Node wars are held on all of them, and conquest wars on Calpheon 1, Mediah 1, and Valencia 1. Each server fights over its namesake territory, so Calpheon 1 fights over Calpheon. Conquest war occurs on Saturday and node wars on all other days. War of the Roses takes place on Sundays and is exclusive to the Odility_1 server.

## Servers: list icons

Icons beside each server in the list mean:

- Crossed yellow swords: a node or conquest war is in progress.
- Red general prohibition symbol: maintenance.
- Yellow and red danger symbols: maintenance is scheduled or ongoing.
- Green EXP symbol: New Olvia growth server.
- Red sword and shield: Arsha PvP server.
- Yellow speech bubble: season server.
- Yellow shield with a crown: your guild's base server.
- War of the Roses symbol: War of the Roses is taking place (Odility_1 only).
