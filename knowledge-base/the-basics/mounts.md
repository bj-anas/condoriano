# Mounts

The mount types in Black Desert and how their skills, equipment, and inventory work.

## Mounts overview

Mounts are a key way to travel the world. The types are horses, donkeys, alpacas, camels, miniature elephants, wagons, boats, and ships, plus guild mounts. Mounts fall into two kinds, land and ocean, and only one mount of each kind can be out at a time. Unused mounts stay in stables and wharfs, which have limited slots, so manage them carefully. The Stable & Wharf page has more.

## Mounts: horse

The horse is the representative mount, with high-tier horses running fast over long distances. Horse tiers go from tier 6 (T6) to tier 10 (T10), and a higher tier raises basic potential for speed, acceleration, turn, and braking. Each level up can give a skill, up to Lv. 30; beyond that the horse keeps leveling to Lv. 100, but stats and skills stop growing, and monsters below the horse's level will not attack it. Sprint, instant acceleration, and drift are the most valued skills and sell for more in the horse market. Horse gear raises stats and allows mounted combat and faster base speed. Gear is sold by stable keepers and on the Central Market, or made at a horse gear workshop. You can get a horse by taming a wild one or by buying one at the horse market. The final stage is the mythical horse; see the Mythical Horse and Training pages.

## Mounts: donkey

The donkey carries goods rather than running fast. It holds more weight (LT) than a horse and has good endurance, making it useful when starting trade. Stable keepers in every town sell donkeys and basic gear. Higher-grade donkey gear is crafted in workshops, with the best made at Stonetail Horse Ranch, Mediah. Shai can get a unique donkey from a Black Spirit quest during her Talent quests at Lv. 56; it is stronger and faster than a strong black donkey and can learn skills to improve further. A donkey registered in the stable has all skills learned at 100%.

## Mounts: alpaca

The fwuzzy alpaca is a special mount only the Shai class can use. At Lv. 50, Shai can start a questline from Seiri in Duvencrune, Drieghan's capital, found in the Suggested tab of the Quest window (hotkey: O). It can level up to Lv. 15, can learn up to 7 unique skills, and cannot wear defense gear. An alpaca registered in the stable has all skills learned at 100%.

## Mounts: camel

At Lv. 55, on first reaching Sand Grain Bazaar at the edge of Valencia's inner desert, the stable keeper offers the once per Family quest "Your Best Friend in the Desert", which rewards a light brown camel. Camels cross the Great Desert of Valencia (the Black Desert) with no movement speed penalty, while most horses and wagons are slowed. They can wear horse gear, and their stats grow with levels. You can also exchange x5 Golden Seal - [Imperial Training] for a light brown camel, or x15 Golden Seal - [Imperial Training] for a deep brown camel with better stats, which helps if you lose your quest camel and need one for content such as an Adventure Log page. A camel registered in the stable has all skills learned at 100%.

## Mounts: miniature elephant

The miniature elephant is called the wagon of the desert, with a good weight limit for moving trade goods across the Valencian desert. The same Sand Grain Bazaar stable keeper offers the Lv. 55 quest "The Adorable and Trusty Porter, Miniature Elephant", leading to a questline that rewards the mount, once per character. For more with the same character, the Valencian mogul Shakatu offers a way. Gear is crafted in various workshops, with the best at Stonetail Horse Ranch and Duvencrune. A miniature elephant registered in the stable has all skills learned at 100%.

## Mounts: wagon

Wagons haul big loads and mostly serve for trade runs and horse training. No other ground mount has as many slots, and exceeding its weight limit does not slow its base speed much. Up to 4 horses can be connected, depending on the wagon type, which trains them too. Wagon speed is not especially high and does not depend on the connected horses. Wagons have durability instead of health, most cannot be repaired, and when it runs out, speed drops drastically. Stable keepers sell the flimsy wagon, shabby wagon, and farm wagon. Six wagons are not sold by stable keepers and must be crafted or bought on the Central Market, and so must their equipment: the forest path wagon, noble wagon, merchant wagon, white wagon, trade wagon, and strong wagon. The forest path wagon and its equipment can only be crafted in Grána.

## Mounts: ship

Boats serve inland rivers and the open sea. A ship is needed for ocean content like hotspot fishing, bartering, hunting sea monsters, and island quests. Some boats are crafted at a workshop or bought on the Central Market, and others come from upgrading an existing boat. Basic boats have fixed durability and must be discarded when it runs out, returning a little money; sailboats and better can be repaired. Where boats come from:

| Source | Boats |
|---|---|
| Wharf manager | A raft, and the Bartali sailboat (from Proix in Velia) |
| Workshop crafting | Rafts, a few rowboats, fishing boats, two types of Epheria cogs, Epheria sailboats, Epheria frigates (the last two only in Port Epheria) |
| Upgrade from an existing boat | Bartali Sailboat, Epheria Sailboat, Epheria Frigate, Improved Epheria Sailboat, Improved Epheria Frigate, Epheria Caravel, Epheria Galleass, the 4 types of Epheria Carrack |

Trade is the focus of sailboats, caravels, and the carracks upgraded from them, while frigates, galleasses, and their upgraded carracks are meant for sea combat. Basic boat gear comes from wharf managers, and better gear is crafted mainly at Port Epheria and Iliya Island. See the Ship Upgrading page.

## Mounts: guild mounts

There are three guild mount types: galleys, elephants, and dragons. Getting them takes a whole guild's effort, and the guild owns them, not the individual Adventurer. They are used in node and territorial conquest wars and for sea transport. See the Guild Mount and Dragon Mount: Doomcaller pages.

## Mounts: skills

Mounts can learn skills, with a chance for a new one at each level up. Vehicle mounts such as wagons and boats have fixed skills that need a certain training or sailing life skill level, and some skills need certain gear. Skills include movement skills like Sprint and Drift and attack skills like Hind Kick and Fore Chop. Using an unmastered skill raises its EXP, and failing the activation sequence makes you fall off. Failing costs more stamina but gives more EXP, so keep stamina recovery items or stay near a stable keeper. A mastered skill never fails. Sprint is already at 100% mastery when learned. You can change skills with the Manage Skills button in the stable using a Mount Skill Change Coupon, though the skill may not change to the one you picked.

## Mounts: equipment

Mount equipment raises defense and has special defaults: stirrups on horses and camels allow mounted combat, and saddles make the mount run faster. Basic gear for most mounts comes from stable keepers and wharf managers. Green grade gear for horses, camels, donkeys, and miniature elephants is crafted in horse gear workshops, and types such as light, combat, storm, and fighting spirit horse gear can also be obtained with horse race seals. Blue grade (Granverre for horses, donkeys, and camels; Muzika for miniature elephants) is crafted in Duvencrune and Stonetail Horse Ranch. Yellow grade Krogdalo's for horses and donkeys is crafted at Stonetail Horse Ranch. Wagon gear is crafted in wagon part workshops and boat gear in ship part workshops. See the Crafting page.

## Mounts: inventory and Reset Mount Growth

Mounts have their own inventory, and wagons and boats can hold more weight than a character, which helps with life skills. You can use mount slots as extra storage, but weight slows the mount. To reset a mount, use a reset mount growth item through the Reset Mount menu: the mount returns to Lv. 1, with all stats (Speed/Accel./Turn/Brake) and learned skills reset. The Reset Mount Growth page has more.
