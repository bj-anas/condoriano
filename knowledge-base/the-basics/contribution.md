# Contribution

Contribution points, the family-wide value earned through activity across territories. This page covers how to earn them, what to spend them on, and how to get them back.

## Contribution overview

Contribution is a measure of how much you have helped the territories of the world, shown as a single number of contribution points that works like your family's fame among the inhabitants. You mainly spend it to invest in nodes, buy houses, and rent special items such as garden fences. Points are easy to invest but hard to earn, and they are not permanently spent: any investment can be taken back to refill your available points. You cannot delete a character who has rented items, so return rented items to the NPC first. To see where your points went, use the Contribution Points panel at the top left of the screen, which breaks down your houses, node investments, and rentals.

## Contribution: earning points from quests and exploration

As your maximum contribution points grow, each new point needs more experience, so earning gets harder. Most quests reward contribution experience, and many main cities have quest circuits of short repeatable quests in the Recurring tab of the Quest window (hotkey: O). NPC Liana in all cities offers weekly quests, a mix of subjugation and life skill quests, for Adventurers with between 220 and 349 contribution points; they reset every Thursday, and you can do only one per week. Discovering a new area for the first time also gives a bit of contribution experience along with the area's knowledge entry.

## Contribution: byproduct items and gateway node defense

While doing life skills such as cooking, synthesizing elixirs, farming, or fishing, you may obtain "byproduct" items. Each can be exchanged at its designated NPC for rewards including contribution experience. One exchange gives little, but exchanging in bulk is one of the best ways to raise contribution. Separately, at night you may meet monster raid parties attacking a gateway node, usually a fortified camp. Helping the soldiers defeat them gives regional defense tokens, which certain NPCs in each territory trade for contribution experience. Trading these tokens costs energy.

## Contribution: node investment

Node networks grow outward from the main towns and cities, which carry a blue icon on the world map and need no investment of their own. You then spend contribution points on neighboring nodes to extend the network. To invest, select a node linked by a white line to a town, press Invest Contribution on the left, and linked nodes then show an orange line. Connected nodes allow trading between them; selling a trade good to a city from an unconnected node drops the price to 30% of the original. Once you invest in a node you can also invest in its production nodes, to which workers from surrounding towns can be sent to extract resources. The Node page has more.

## Contribution: houses

Contribution points buy houses in towns. Houses can be your decorated residence, storage that adds slots, lodging for hired workers, or workshops where workers craft items from materials in town storage. On the World Map, click a town to see house icons: blue houses can be bought, gray ones cannot until you buy nearby houses first, and white ones are already yours, with a small symbol in the lower right showing their use. Click a blue house, choose its purpose, and press Purchase. Buying needs contribution points and a small amount of silver. A storage house raises storage slots for its town or the linked town. The Workers and Houses pages have more.

## Contribution: item rental

Contribution points rent items such as equipment for new Adventurers, garden fences for farming, containers that reach town storage from your residence, a matchlock for hunting beginners, and a flute for certain quests. Rentable items carry the [CP] tag, for example [CP] Strong Fence. Rental equipment cannot be enhanced and is somewhat heavy, but it has excellent AP or DP, uses no durability, and needs no repair, making it a good stopgap until you finish your PEN (V) Naru set. Use the Find NPCs icon at the top right, left of the minimap, to search by item or NPC name, and hover over an NPC name to see what it rents. Return an item to the NPC to get the points back.

## Contribution: retrieving invested points

To withdraw a node investment, open the World Map, click the node, and press Withdraw Contribution on the left. A node in the middle of a connection must wait until dependent nodes are withdrawn first, since you cannot cut a network in the middle. Energy spent leveling a node (up to Lv. 20) is returned. For rented items, talk to the NPC you rented from, open Chat (hotkey: W), and choose the return option to get the points back. Houses can be sold in reverse order of purchase; to sell one in a chain you must first sell the others connected to it, individually or all at once. Selling returns contribution points but not silver spent on purpose changes and upgrades. Installed items in a residence move to the town storage, and wallpaper and flooring are permanently deleted on sale.
