# Collection of Tales

The Collection of Tales is how most of the Land of the Morning Light (LoML) main questline is played. This page covers its structure, how to start it, and the Donghae, Imoogi, and Hwanghae parts.

## Collection of Tales overview

The LoML main questline is split into two halves: Donghae (LoML) and Hwanghae (Seoul). Each half begins with 8 tales that are separate stories, which you may do in any order, one at a time. Once you start one of the first 8 tales, you cannot choose another until it is finished, and abandoning it does not let you start a different one. In Donghae, after the first 8 tales, 5 more tales follow, done in sequential order and starting automatically; Hwanghae mirrors this with a sequential series after its first 8. The first half of the LoML main questline has 15 parts in total. You can open the Collection of Tales from the main menu (Esc) > Adventure (F5) > Collection of Tales. Completed tales can be revisited there to view their final scene, and clicking a tale's details guides you to where it begins. Choices you make in the tales change their endings.

## Collection of Tales: Donghae start requirements

To begin the LoML main questline, finish the quests from The Magnus, then accept the first LoML quest from the Black Spirit (hotkey: ","). You can also start a new character in LoML. The LoML Abyss Episode continues from the end of Abyss One: The Magnus. Requirements: character Lv. 56 or higher, completing the quest "[The Magnus] Chest from the Abyss", and not having the quest "What Are You Doing Here?" active. Its quests include [LoML] Yeonhwa's Departure, [LoML] Snotface Wants To See You, and several [The Magnus] quests ending with [The Magnus] Morning's Breeze.

## Collection of Tales: [LoML] Emma's Invitation

Unless you start a new character in LoML, you must complete "[LoML] Emma's Invitation" to continue the Donghae main questline. Get it from Black Spirit > Main Quest (R), then go to Igor Bartali at Bartali Farm. You need the knowledge entry Abyssal Veins: Land of the Morning Light (from "[The Magnus] Morning's Breeze") and the completed quest "[LoML] Snotface Wants To See You". All Donghae main quests are labeled [LoML]. After two questlines following Emma's Invitation, you can pick any of the 8 starting tales, and after finishing all 8, 5 new sequential tales open.

## Collection of Tales: getting to the Land of the Morning Light

There are three ways to reach the Land of the Morning Light: take the Abyssal Vein to Nampo's Moodle Village after finishing the Abyss One: The Magnus main questline, including the LoML Abyss Episode; sail to Nampo's Moodle Village by ship, where an Explorer's Compass is recommended for locating yourself in the Great Ocean; or create a new character and choose "Land of the Morning Light" as the starting area.

## Collection of Tales: Tale of Imoogi

The Tale of Imoogi bridges Donghae in the east and Hwanghae in the west, giving a look at the forces behind the manipulation of yogwes and how they link to the overall Black Desert plot. After finishing the quest "[Koo Mihyun] Royal Decree", the Black Spirit offers "[Imoogi] Lee Mongryong's Call", answering Dolswe's call for help.

## Collection of Tales: Hwanghae (Seoul)

The second main questline, Land of the Morning Light: Seoul, requires finishing the Tale of Imoogi. Then comes the prequest "[Seoul] A Friendly Face", available from Lv. 56 after completing "[Imoogi] Clues of the Truth". It comes from the Black Spirit and appears on the Main tab of the Quest window (hotkey: O). Dolswe has landed in Balenos. All Hwanghae main quests are labeled [Seoul]. Like Donghae, it offers 8 tales played one at a time in the same Collection of Tales window, with choices changing endings, followed by a second collection of tales in sequential order.
