# Valencia Monster Zones

The thirteen grinding spots of Valencia, from Bashim Base to Hystria Ruins, with recommended level, AP, DP, desert status, monsters, strategy and main loot.

## Valencia monster zones: overview and preparation

Valencia's monster zones suit players who reached Lv. 56 and awakened, and difficulty depends on level, gear and skill. Prepare plenty of meals, potions, elixirs and Elion's Tear. Four zones feature the very strong Chief Gatekeeper, nearly impossible to beat alone on a first visit, so bring a party; some zones also have elite commanders that summon reinforcements. Navigation does not work in the Great Desert of Valencia, so getting lost is a risk. Carry Purified Water to cure heatstroke and Star Anise Tea to cure hypothermia, the two desert illnesses. The zones are Bashim Base, Desert Naga Temple, Titium Valley, Cadry Ruins, Crescent Shrine, Gahaz Bandits' Lair, Waragon Nest, Centaurus Herd, Basilisk Den, Roud Sulfur Mine, Pila Ku Jail, Aakman Temple and Hystria Ruins.

## Bashim Base (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Bashim Base in Valencia is a large canyon territory of the reclusive, hostile Bashim, a demibeast tribe with its own language and rich culture. It is not in the desert. Recommended level is Lvl 55~56, with recommended AP 100+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 56). For non-awakened characters, add +10 to the shown requirements. It is one of the easiest Valencia zones. Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5 (suited to solo play), hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5 (neither near nor far from Sand Grain Bazaar). There are no unique drops, but experience is good. Parking depends on the route. Marni's Stones can be bought from Wacky Toshi for more experience.

## Bashim Base (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Bashim Base (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 55 ~ 58 monsters, wide and dense. Melee types are Ohonsey, Terruda Infantryman, Terruda Reinforced Infantryman, Terruda Bandsman, Khala Elite Combatant and Khala Commanding Officer. Terruda Thrower and Khala Reinforced Thrower are melee and ranged. At range are Bashim Energy Totem, Bashim Guard Totem, Bashim Target Totem, Ohonsey Archer and Ohonsey Elementalist, plus Bashim Extermination Totem. Only the violent Terruda Reinforced Infantryman is a threat, but because many come alone, conflicts with other players can occur. Go up the road from NPC Torenandu to a circular structure with high density and run circles there; the area is so wide that you need not stick to the best spots. Party play gives less combat experience than solo.

## Bashim Base (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Bashim Base (Valencia): Asula's Weakened Magic Ring (turn it in for an Asula's Crimson Eye Ring, handy for gearing up secondary characters), Marsh's Artifact - Melee AP, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Evasion, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of the Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). Caphras Stones and scrolls drop steadily, so it is popular with those new to Valencia. There are no unique drops.

## Desert Naga Temple (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Desert Naga Temple in Valencia is the ancestral and sacred home of the Nagas, who resettled it after being driven from the southeastern swamps, and who want to avoid war with humans because both hate the Fogans. It is a desert area, so bring Purified Water and Star Anise Tea. Recommended level is Lvl 55~56, with recommended AP 100+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 56). Non-awakened characters should add +10 to +20. You can win below the minimum, but meet it for efficient combat experience. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 5 of 5 (the number one area), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park near the quest NPC, northwest of Sand Grain Bazaar. The unique drop is Serap's Necklace.

## Desert Naga Temple (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Desert Naga Temple (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 57 ~ 60 monsters spread evenly along the path into a narrow canyon: Desert Naga Combatant and Desert Naga Commanding Officer in melee, Desert Naga Combatant Monk at range, plus Desert Naga Gigantic Guardian Tower and Desert Naga Statue, and the very strong violent Desert Naga Chief Gatekeeper. The Commanding Officer has high vitality and resists some debuffs; the Monk's magic hurts when it hits from behind, so kill them first. The Chief Gatekeeper appears at specific points with very high attack and vitality, monsters respawn nearby so you never face it alone, and it uses powerful flame breath at distance. Go northwest past the quest NPC to the best monster concentration. Marni's Stones are sold by Wacky Toshi.

## Desert Naga Temple (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Desert Naga Temple (Valencia): Serap's Necklace (unique drop; cheap but an extra source of silver), Asula's Weakened Magic Earring, Marsh's Artifact - Ranged AP, Lesha's Artifact - Ranged Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Ranged Evasion, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy. Despite desert illnesses, it always has players, thanks to silver from junk loot, Caphras Stones and scrolls.

## Titium Valley (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Titium Valley in Valencia is the fresh-water valley the Fogans took from the Nagas and named after their chief, the Fogan Prince, though many Fogans have since migrated to Serendia. It is far south of Valencia City, so hard to reach, with a nearby villa. It is a desert area, so bring Purified Water and Star Anise Tea. Recommended level is Lvl 55~56, with recommended AP 100+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 56). Non-awakened characters should add +10. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5 (low because it is remote), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5. Park near the node manager in the valley. The unique drop is Serap's Necklace.

## Titium Valley (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Titium Valley (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 57 ~ 60 monsters, placed evenly so there is no best spot: Desert Fogan Sentry, Desert Fogan Fighter and Desert Fogan Hoppity in melee, Desert Fogan Seer at range, plus Desert Fogan Egg, Desert Fogan Food Depot and Desert Fogan Statue, and the very strong violent Desert Fogan Chief Gatekeeper. There are no major threats, but watch the seers' ranged attacks. The Chief Gatekeeper appears occasionally and uses a non-stop continuous attack, so melee characters must be especially careful. You gain moderately good combat experience and returns for time spent; remote, it is undervalued but well balanced. Marni's Stones can be bought from Wacky Toshi.

## Titium Valley (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Titium Valley (Valencia): Serap's Necklace (unique drop), Asula's Weakened Magic Earring, Marsh's Artifact - Magic AP, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Evasion, Part for Explorer's Compass, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy. No drop is a big stroke of luck.

## Cadry Ruins (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Cadry Ruins in Valencia is the old Cadry Shrine, now a den of Kzarka-worshiping cultists who eat black stone powder hoping for eternal life and are rumored to kidnap travelers for sacrifice. A huge canyon surrounds it, and rough terrain makes the trip arduous. It is not in the desert. Recommended level is Lvl 56~57, with recommended AP 140+ and DP 160+ (the stat table lists level 57). Non-awakened characters should add +10. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5. Park as close to the area as possible. Unique drops are the Ring of Cadry Guardian and Serap's Necklace.

## Cadry Ruins (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Cadry Ruins (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 57 ~ 60 monsters: Cadry Fighter and Cadry Armored Fighter in melee, Cadry Black Mage and Cadry Large Cannon at range, plus Cadry Small Cannon, Cadry Ruins Prison and Cadry Summoning Stone, and the violent, very strong Cadry Chief Gatekepeer and Cadry Commander. The Cadry Fighter advances behind a shield and can stun, the Armored Fighter uses a strong stun attack, and the Black Mage fires from all directions. When its HP drops, the chief gatekeeper starts powerful AoE attacks, deadly even with high defense unless avoided; the chief gatekeeper and commander are dangerous alone. Density is high from the main shrine to the road north. Some say entering from Kunid's Vacation Spot is best. Marni's Stones can be bought from Wacky Toshi.

## Cadry Ruins (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Cadry Ruins (Valencia): Ring of Cadry Guardian, which drops often, and Serap's Necklace (unique drops), Asula's Weakened Magic Belt, Lesha's Artifact - All Evasion, Lesha's Artifact - All Damage Reduction, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). The three Cadry's pieces, Obsidian Decoration, Ruby Ornament and Blue Ring, combine into a Ring of Cadry Guardian. There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Crescent Shrine (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Crescent Shrine in Valencia holds sand-buried remnants of the ancient Valencian civilization, where Argos Saunils are rumored to hold blood festivals at each crescent moon. It is a desert area, so bring Purified Water and Star Anise Tea, and it is considered lawless because desert PvP rules apply and player-killing (PK) is allowed. Recommended level is Lvl 55~56, with recommended AP 140+ and DP 160+ (the stat table lists level 57). Non-awakened characters should add +10 to +15. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5 (far from both Sand Grain Bazaar and Valencia City). Parking depends on the route. Unique drops are the Ring of Crescent Guardian and Serap's Necklace.

## Crescent Shrine (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Crescent Shrine (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 57 ~ 60 monsters: Crescent Watcher and Crescent Guardian in melee, Crescent Follower at range, plus Argos Sand Tower, Argos Artifact Repository, Argos Obsidian Energy and Argos Obsidian Altar, and the very strong violent Argos Chief Gatekeeper. Crescent Guardians are very powerful and cannot be knocked down, so use other debuffs; they also use jump attacks that knock you down. The Chief Gatekeeper keeps summoning more monsters, so it is hard even in a group. Density is highest in the main circular area, which is also the most popular; get quests from the Node Manager NPC. On a plateau up stairs just south of the Crescent Mountains Node Manager NPC, groups of Lv. 60 Argos Saunils consumed by darkness give a harder fight and better rewards.

## Crescent Shrine (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Crescent Shrine (Valencia): the Ring of Crescent Guardian, which drops often and is popular, and Serap's Necklace (unique drops), Soiled Ring of Crescent Guardian, Asula's Weakened Magic Belt, Lesha's Artifact - Ranged AP, Lesha's Artifact - Ranged Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Ranged Evasion, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy. High-level players like the zone for the ring, and the many well-geared players mean frequent conflict.

## Gahaz Bandits' Lair (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Gahaz Bandits' Lair in Valencia is the hideout of the Gahaz Bandits, driven from the bazaar by the Katan army; their leader Gahaz Tuval is rumored to have captured a black dragon. It is not in the desert and is close to Shakatu, a convenient place. Recommended level is Lvl 55~56, with recommended AP 140+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 57). Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. Park near the node manager or at the entrance from Shakatu. The unique drop is Serap's Necklace. Players pass through often because it is one of the few ways to reach Valencia City, besides crossing the desert and going by sea.

## Gahaz Bandits' Lair (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Gahaz Bandits' Lair (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 56 ~ 58 monsters spread evenly along the roads to a narrow canyon: Sandstorm Elite, Sandstorm Plunderer and Sandstorm Assassin in melee, Sandstorm Rifleman at range, plus Sandstorm Barracks, Sandstorm Watchtower, Desert Trap and Sandstorm Bear Trap, and the strong violent Shadow of Gahaz. Kill the Sandstorm Riflemen first; food and elixirs with extra damage to humans help. The Shadow of Gahaz is an elite that casts magic like the Sorceress class and summons helpers. Use the Node Manager NPC as a guide, go uphill, make a U-turn and clear the hill, parking at the Node Manager NPC and circling nearby. Wacky Toshi sells Marni's Stones.

## Gahaz Bandits' Lair (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Gahaz Bandits' Lair (Valencia): Serap's Necklace (unique drop), Asula's Weakened Magic Earring, Black Spirit's Claw Piece, Marsh's Artifact - Melee AP, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Evasion, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Waragon Nest (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Waragon Nest in Valencia is a canyon where the local waragon build bird-like nests in the shade; they differ from the underground and wetland waragon. It is not in the desert. Recommended level is Lvl 55~56, with recommended AP 150+ and DP 220+ (the stat table lists level 57). Non-awakened characters should add +10. It is designed for parties of 3. Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 1 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5 (not too far from Sand Grain Bazaar). There are no unique drops. Park outside the monster zone. Take the related quests from the Node Manager NPC.

## Waragon Nest (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Waragon Nest (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 57 ~ 58 monsters in a big interconnected canyon: Stone Waragon, Stone Mutant Waragon and Stone Prickle Lizard in melee, Stone Worm at range, Stone Waragon Egg, and the violent Stone Colossal Waragon. Stone Waragons hatch from the eggs, giving more monsters to kill. Some monsters burrow underground and emerge to hit you, and AoE attacks are a danger. The area is big, so raise your movement speed.

## Waragon Nest (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Waragon Nest (Valencia): Serap's Necklace, Asula's Weakened Magic Earring, Asula's Weakened Magic Ring, Marsh's Artifact - Magic AP, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Evasion, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy. The zone has no unique drops.

## Centaurus Herd (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Centaurus Herd in Valencia is the plain Shakatu bought for his beloved Taphtar, now roamed by fast centaurs that even the Katan Army could not handle; a sacred burning black stone is said to lie where they gather. It is not in the desert. Recommended level is Lvl. 56~57, with recommended AP 180+ and DP 200+ (the stat table lists level 57). Centaurs are strong and fast, so be above the requirements; non-awakened characters should add +10. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 5 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5 (a short distance from Sand Grain Bazaar). Parking depends on location. The unique drop is the Centaurus Belt.

## Centaurus Herd (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Centaurus Herd (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 58 ~ 61 monsters: Centaur, Centaur Hunter and Centaur Axeman in melee, Guard Tower of Earth at range, and Centaur Treasure Chest. Centaurs are the hardest group in western Valencia: they move fast and resist down attacks and more, so unless you are well above the requirements you feel chased. Group all enemies by attacking the melee ones and pulling them toward the ranged ones. It is easy to reach from Barhan Gateway and the road west, with no best location; at Barhan Gateway you can watch centaurs fight the Katan Army. A horse can get stuck on the nearby roads and die. It is a first zone crossed in Valencia's main questline and a testing ground for readiness. Marni's Stones are sold by Wacky Toshi.

## Centaurus Herd (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Centaurus Herd (Valencia): the Centaurus Belt (unique drop), Soiled Centaurus Belt, Asula's Weakened Magic Necklace, Lesha's Artifact - All Evasion, Lesha's Artifact - All Damage Reduction, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy. It is popular for frequent sealed magic crystals and good silver.

## Basilisk Den (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Basilisk Den in Valencia is the rocky home of clever, wicked basilisks that petrify intruders; almost no adventurers survived early attempts. It is not in the desert and lies between Sand Grain Bazaar and Altinova. Recommended level is Lvl 56~57, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 220+ (the stat table lists level 57). Basilisks have high vitality and attack, so bring more AP than recommended; non-awakened characters should add +10. It is designed for parties of 3. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 5 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. Park outside the zone. The unique drop is the Basilisk's Belt. Strengthen yourself in other Valencia zones first.

## Basilisk Den (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Basilisk Den (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 58 ~ 60 monsters, a mix of human and demihuman types, the demihumans being stronger: Petrified Adventurer, Petrified Soldier, Basilisk Petrifier, Basilisk Watcher and Basilisk Ambusher in melee, plus Basilist Statue. Basilisks appear when you attack the statues, so watch their numbers. Target the basilisks rather than the petrified humans, though some of those can stun. Park outside the zone, because petrified soldiers and adventurers may sneak onto your mount while you fight.

## Basilisk Den (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Basilisk Den (Valencia): the Basilisk's Belt (unique drop), Asula's Weakened Magic Necklace, Marsh's Artifact - Melee AP, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Evasion, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). Combining the three Basilisk's pieces, Alignment Pin, Ring and Snake Skin Belt, gives a Basilisk's Belt. There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Roud Sulfur Mine (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Roud Sulfur Mine in Valencia is in and around the volcano of Mount Gavinya, northeast of Valencia City, the only source of sulfur with 99% purity, with a constant risk of explosions from toxic gas. It counts as desert but gives no desert illnesses. Recommended level is Lvl 57~58, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 240+ (the stat table lists level 58), about as hard as Pila Ku Jail. Non-awakened characters should add +10. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5 (you can ride straight to Valencia City). Park by the entrance or inside the mine. Unique drops are Sicil's Necklace and Manos Craftsman's Clothes. Marni's Stones are sold by Wacky Toshi.

## Roud Sulfur Mine (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Roud Sulfur Mine (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 58 ~ 60 monsters, plentiful inside and outside the mine: Lava Searcher, Lava Tukar, Lava Fafalun, Lava Devourer and Lava Taolun in melee, Lava Faolun in melee and ranged, Lava Phlogiston Condenser and Lava Hydraulic Nozzle at range, plus Lava Sulfur Bundle. The Lava tribe gathers in small groups, each with a hydraulic nozzle, which emits toxic gas in four directions, and a condenser, which explodes seconds after being damaged and inflicts debuffs. Several members use knockback and knockdown and deal continuous damage, so bring HP potions. Visit groups both outside and inside the mine. Many players come for the Map of Unknown Piece.

## Roud Sulfur Mine (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Roud Sulfur Mine (Valencia): Sicil's Necklace and Manos Craftsman's Clothes (unique drops), Soiled Sicil's Necklace, Map of Unknown Piece, Marsh's Artifact - Melee AP, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Evasion, Part for Explorer's Compass, Black Spirit's Claw Piece, Traveler's Map, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). The chance of Specter's Energy is increased here.

## Pila Ku Jail (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Pila Ku Jail in Valencia is a prison built during the reign of Valencia's 3rd king, reportedly to hold the evil alchemist Caphras; its dangerous prisoners remain locked in. It lies south of Valencia City and northeast of Muiquun, with large rooms linked by circular corridors. It counts as desert but gives no desert illnesses. Recommended level is Lvl 57~58, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 240+ (the stat table lists level 58). Non-awakened characters should add +10. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5. Park at the Pila Ku Jail entrance. The unique drop is Sicil's Necklace. With negative karma, use nearby Muiquun as a temporary hometown.

## Pila Ku Jail (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Pila Ku Jail (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 60 monsters: Dark Eyes Warder, Iron Fist Warder, Executioner, Frenzied Executioner and Sordid Deportee in melee, Caphras' Follower at range, plus Pile of Jail Junk, Jail Alarm Device and Caphras' Torture Device. They have high vitality and attack, and the followers use long-range magic, so avoid taking many hits; never stop moving. Relaxing mid-fight can get you killed. The prison is in a mountain and has two entrances where you can park; take the quests at the main entrance. Alone, any room or corridor works; in a group, a bigger room. The main circular room can be yours alone only if you far surpass the requirements, and others may think the same.

## Pila Ku Jail (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Pila Ku Jail (Valencia): Sicil's Necklace (unique drop), Soiled Sicil's Necklace, Map of Unknown Piece, Marsh's Artifact - Ranged AP, Lesha's Artifact - Ranged Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Ranged Evasion, Part for Explorer's Compass, Black Spirit's Claw Piece, Traveler's Map, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Yona's Fragment, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy. It is remote but enticing for the treasure Map of Unknown Piece.

## Aakman Temple (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Aakman Temple in Valencia is a dungeon-like zone under the desert, home of the Aakman, who worship the ancient giants and are rumored to plan resurrecting them. It counts as desert but gives no desert illnesses, and has no mount parking. It can only be reached with a special device found in the desert, teleportation devices or a specific entrance, and you leave through gaps in the walls. Recommended level is Lvl 60~, with recommended AP 230+ and DP 300+ (the stat table lists level 60). Non-awakened characters should add +10. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars (easier than Hystria Ruins), user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 5 of 5, distance from town 0 of 5. The unique drop is the Tungrad Earring.

## Aakman Temple (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Aakman Temple (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 59 ~ 62 monsters: Aakman Punisher, Aakman Flamen, Aakman Guardian and Aakman Watcher plus the Elite Guardian and Elite Watcher variants in melee, Aakman Airbender in melee and ranged, and Aakman Flamen and Aakman Illusion Trap at range. They are far stronger than the wandering Aakman above. Most resist debuffs, so time knockdowns and stuns well, and use all suitable food and elixirs, as in Hystria Ruins. A structure sometimes charges up, then releases energy that damages everything nearby and debuffs nearby monsters. The zone spirals downward, which makes efficient routes easy. In the desert, a device sends you to Aakman Temple or Hystria Ruins by portal color: a portal without a yellow aura leads to Aakman Temple.

## Aakman Temple (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Aakman Temple (Valencia): the Tungrad Earring (unique drop), Upgraded Compass Parts, Kehelle's Artifact - Black Spirit's Rage Max Increase, Part for Explorer's Compass, Traveler's Map, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). The zone rewards challenge-seekers with treasure pieces and a Tungrad-type accessory.

## Hystria Ruins (Valencia): location and recommended AP/DP

Hystria Ruins in Valencia is a dungeon-like zone burying many of Valencia's ancient artifacts, entered only through ancient artifact teleportation devices in the Great Desert of Valencia. It counts as desert but gives no desert illnesses, and has no mount parking. Recommended level is Lvl 60~, with recommended AP 230+ and DP 300+ (the stat table lists level 60). Non-awakened characters should add +10. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars, user density 5 of 5 (very crowded because of the treasure pieces), hunting satisfaction 5 of 5, distance from town 0 of 5. The unique drop is the Tungrad Necklace. The portal with a yellow aura leads to Hystria Ruins, while the other leads to Aakman Temple.

## Hystria Ruins (Valencia): monsters and strategy

Hystria Ruins (Valencia) has mostly Lv. 64 ~ 65 monsters, the only Lv. 64-65 monsters in Valencia, all using both melee and ranged attacks: Elten, Tanco, Vodkhan, Kalqueesh, Tutuka, Tukar Demol, Tukar Burmol and Tukar Balten, plus Hystria Watch Tower and Hystria Guard Tower. They show no mercy and have high resistances, so debuffs barely work; their ranged attacks reduce attack and movement speed. Stay on guard, as monsters may ambush you anywhere. From time to time an intruder alarm system triggers waves of monsters according to the threat level, until the alarm is shut down. Leave through gaps in the walls. It is hard even for veterans but still popular for treasure pieces and Tungrad-type accessories.

## Hystria Ruins (Valencia): main loot

Main drops at Hystria Ruins (Valencia): the Tungrad Necklace (unique drop), Upgraded Compass Parts, Kehelle's Artifact - Black Spirit's Rage Max Increase, Part for Explorer's Compass, Traveler's Map, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Scroll Written in Ancient Language, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon).
