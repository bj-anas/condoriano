# Monster Zone Info

The in-game Monster Zone Info window, which helps players pick a grinding spot that fits their AP, DP and goals, and shows drops, knowledge, quests and titles for each zone.

## Monster Zone Info window: what it is and how to open it

The Monster Zone Info window is the in-game tool for finding which monster zone suits a player's gear and goals. It shows which zones fit your current AP and DP, lets you browse by territory or by trait, and lists item drops, learnable knowledge and quests available in each zone. To open it, press Esc for the main menu, choose Adventure (F5), then Monster Zone Info.

## Monster zones: recommended AP and DP in the Monster Zone Info window

To see the recommended AP and DP of a monster zone, select the zone in the Monster Zone Info window. The basic info panel shows the zone's required AP and DP next to your own current AP and DP, so you can see at a glance whether you are strong enough. The same panel shows the extra item drop rate you get from the energy you have invested in the related node. The arrow icon next to the zone name starts navigation to the zone, but this does not work for areas without an attached location-based node. For the exact recommended AP and DP of each zone, see the regional monster zone pages.

## Monster Zone Info window: filters, search and hashtags

In the Monster Zone Info window you can filter zones by territory, and filter by type: zones suited for parties, zones in the Elvia Realm, zones with Marni's Realm access (private monster zones), and zones with regional quests. The search box finds a monster zone by name or finds zones by an item drop. Selecting a zone in the results opens its details, including item drops, acceptable quests, learnable knowledge and obtainable titles. Hashtags on each zone mark special traits, such as increased #Caphras Stones, #Skill EXP or #Combat EXP; clicking a tag filters the list to zones with that tag.

## Monster Zone Info: View Total Stats button

The View Total Stats button was added in a recent update. Pressing it switches both the recommended stats and your own stats to total-stat numbers, which include any currently applied effects. Your own total stats add up Extra AP Against Monsters and also Monster Damage Reduction. Total DP is Damage Reduction plus Monster Damage Reduction. The bonuses for extra damage against Kamasylvia, Demihumans and Humans are left out of the sum. The wiki also notes that the game guide can differ from the actual game depending on updates.
