# Mountain of Eternal Winter Monster Zones

The grinding spots of the Mountain of Eternal Winter (Winter Tree Fossil, Murrowak's Labyrinth, Jade Starlight Forest, Erethea's Limbo) and the Erebjork encounter, with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and loot.

## Mountain of Eternal Winter monster zones: overview

The Mountain of Eternal Winter has four monster zones: Winter Tree Fossil, Murrowak's Labyrinth, Jade Starlight Forest and Erethea's Limbo. Players arrive through the end of Atoraxxion: Sycrakea, Drieghan's main questline, or by starting a new character there. Most zones throw endless waves of monsters at you, while Jade Starlight Forest has fewer, stronger enemies. These zones give the Flame of Frost, a crafting material for Labreska's Helmet. Snowstorms sweep the territory, and some say the Dreadwinter Guardian, Erebjork, waits at their center.

## Erebjork, the Dreadwinter Guardian (Mountain of Eternal Winter): how she appears

Erebjork is a boss encounter in the Mountain of Eternal Winter. Fight long enough in Jade Starlight Forest, or purify the dreadwinter tree fossils of the Zvier Highlands, and the Wanewinter Guardian Erebjork may cross your path. A stronger version, the Dreadwinter Guardian, can be found inside snowstorms across the territory. She arrives with her wolf to face any adventurer in the Mountain of Eternal Winter.

## Winter Tree Fossil (Mountain of Eternal Winter): location and recommended AP/DP

Winter Tree Fossil in the Mountain of Eternal Winter is a set of static spots: dreadwinter tree fossils, winter trees tainted by dark energy whose crazed spirits flood out to consume life nearby. Right next to Eilton. Recommended level is Lv. 60+. Normal difficulty recommends AP 250+ and DP 320+ (level 60+); Difficult recommends AP 280+ and DP 350+ (level 61+). If you cannot keep up on Difficult, use Normal. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 1 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 5 of 5. It has no unique drops. Park anywhere outside the range of the winter trees.

## Winter Tree Fossil (Mountain of Eternal Winter): monsters and strategy

Winter Tree Fossil (Mountain of Eternal Winter) has mostly Lv. 61-66 monsters: Panvalla, Breukia and Winter Root Treant in melee, Winter Nest Treant in melee and ranged, Titika and Komau at range, plus Takium. Choose a difficulty when interacting with a dreadwinter tree fossil and spend 100 energy to start. Waves of frenzied winter spirits appear and must be cleared fast, or too many end the encounter. Takium do not attack; they explode on the tree and clear monsters, but those killed that way cannot be looted. Stronger monsters follow, ending with a violent, gigantic Breukia as a final boss, which gives a better chance of good items. A message about powerful energy warns that Panvalla or Breukia is coming. Do not leave Komau alone too long, because they grow and fill the area with an AoE.

## Winter Tree Fossil (Mountain of Eternal Winter): main loot

Main drops at Winter Tree Fossil (Mountain of Eternal Winter): Kehelle's Artifact - Max Stamina, Iridescent Lighstone, Forgotten Witch's Token (gives access to Erethea's Limbo), Magic Crystal of Infinity - Special Evasion, Essence of Insight, Starlit Jade's Breath, and Ancient Magic Crystal of Nature - Giant, Sturdiness, Fighting Spirit and Adamantine. Heavenly Essence drops too, in the variants Ignore Knockback Resistance, Ignore Stun Resistance, Ignore Grapple Resistance and Ignore Knockdown Resistance. Common materials are Dragon Scale Fossil, Ancient Spirit Dust, Caphras Stone and Black Stone. It does not contain Flame of Frost. It is unaffected by the Item Collection Increase Gauge but has higher drop rates instead.

## Murrowak's Labyrinth (Mountain of Eternal Winter): location and recommended AP/DP

Murrowak's Labyrinth is the former Sherekhan Iron Mine, deep in the Sherekhan Abandoned Iron Mine on Black Mountain, where the black dragon Markthanan hides. It is equally distant from Duvencrune and Eilton. It can only be entered from Lv. 60 and above. Recommended level is Lv. 61+, with recommended AP 260+ and DP 350+. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 1 of 5 (limited capacity), hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. Up to 6 adventurers can be inside at once. Park at the Abandoned Sherekhan Mine entrance. The unique drop is the Flame of Frost.

## Murrowak's Labyrinth (Mountain of Eternal Winter): monsters and strategy

Murrowak's Labyrinth (Mountain of Eternal Winter) has mostly Lv. 65 monsters: Murraska, Ironclad Murraska, Murrasto and Vercedes in melee, plus Murrowak Burrow. The murrowak swarm attacks intruders. Defeat enough murraskas to open the way to the queen's lair for the final battle against Queen Vercedes and her endless swarm. Generals and commanders appear now and then, buff the murraskas and use unique attacks. General Murrasto, the Queen's Wing, keeps calling swarms of murraska, and beating him and other commanders drops the Murrowak Crystal Carapace. Below the recommended stats you will flee from the waves often. Prepare even more for Vercedes' lair, and watch for special-ability murrowaks, area attacks and her slow but powerful hits.

## Murrowak's Labyrinth (Mountain of Eternal Winter): main loot

Main drops at Murrowak's Labyrinth (Mountain of Eternal Winter): Flame of Frost (unique drop), the artifacts Lesha - Monster Damage Reduction, Lesha - All Evasion, Lesha - All Damage Reduction, Marsh's Artifact - Extra AP Against Monsters and Kehelle's Artifact - Max HP, Forgotten Witch's Token, Iridescent Lighstone, Magic Crystal of Infinity - Special Evasion, Ouk Pill of Time and Tide, Murrowak Crystal Carapace, and the same Heavenly Essence, Ancient Magic Crystal of Nature and common materials as Winter Tree Fossil. After Queen Vercedes falls, NPC Tori appears and trades Tori's Box of Valuables for a Murrowak Crystal Carapace; the box holds items from this zone's drops at certain probabilities. Ouk Pills of Time and Tide, used for dishes and draughts, are an alternative to daily quests.

## Jade Starlight Forest (Mountain of Eternal Winter): location and recommended AP/DP

Jade Starlight Forest is the land of the Okjinsinis, born from the tears of Labreska at her second death, with jadeite flames; some dark force made them attack all intruders. It is close to Eilton. Recommended level is Lv. 61+, with recommended AP 270+ and DP 350+. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars (fewer but stronger monsters), user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. Park at either entrance or the node manager. The unique drop is the Flame of Frost. Agris Fever use is reduced here, and the questline to craft Labreska's Helmet is given and done in this zone.

## Jade Starlight Forest (Mountain of Eternal Winter): monsters and strategy

Jade Starlight Forest (Mountain of Eternal Winter) has mostly Lv. 65 monsters: Okjinsini Lamp Keeper and Okjinsini Winter Keeper in melee, Okjinsini Brazier Keeper at range, plus Traditional Okjinsini Brazier, Okjinsini Stone Tomb, Okjinsini Totem and Jade Lamp. Okjinsini guard powerful structures; destroying them affects the nearby guards, so use the totems, braziers and lamps against them. But the debuffs and status effects from destroyed structures also hit you: some weaken AP and DP, others knock you back. Okjinsini explode on death, hurting everything nearby, even their own kind. Dreadwinter Possessed Okjinsini, tainted by Erebjork, are stronger with unique attacks, so recommended stats may not be enough. Your route depends on where you park and which clusters you pick.

## Jade Starlight Forest (Mountain of Eternal Winter): main loot

Main drops at Jade Starlight Forest (Mountain of Eternal Winter): Flame of Frost (unique drop), Marsh's Artifact - Extra AP Against Monsters, Lesha - Monster Damage Reduction, Forgotten Witch's Token (access to Erethea's Limbo), Iridescent Lighstone, Magic Crystal of Infinity - Special Evasion, Starlit Jade's Breath, Dragon Scale Fossil, Ancient Spirit Dust, Caphras Stone and Black Stone. Flame of Frost is needed for the Slumbering Origin piece Labreska's Helmet.

## Erethea's Limbo (Mountain of Eternal Winter): location and recommended AP/DP

Erethea's Limbo is a prison made by Erethea, the Witch of Erasure, the seventh witch to awaken from the golden dragon Labreska's first death; saunils guard it. Enter through the door to the Haven of Slumbering Origin, deep in the Mountain of Eternal Winter, and park at the mountain's door. You need a Forgotten Witch's Token, which drops in the other zones there. Recommended level is Lv. 60+, with recommended AP 250+ and DP 320+. It is, with Winter Tree Fossil on Normal, the easiest zone of the region. Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 1 of 5 (solo only), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5. The unique drop is the Flame of Frost.

## Erethea's Limbo (Mountain of Eternal Winter): trial and strategy

Erethea's Limbo (Mountain of Eternal Winter) has mostly Lv. 66 monsters, the melee Erekhantus, in maze-like chambers. The trial ends 60 minutes after it starts. Interact with Erethea's Slate and symbols appear on four gems; find the chambers with those symbols and beat the trial in each. Starting-point doors are open, others open after you clear that room. Trials vary: defeat souls of origin to weaken the Erekhantus, defend the limbo spires, or gather souls to imbue the spires. One to three doors open depending on performance. If the doors you need stay shut, go back to the start through the doors on the north side of each chamber. Read the instructions at the start of each room, or the trial ends and you restart. Take a token run whenever you get one, as it is a rare item.

## Erethea's Limbo (Mountain of Eternal Winter): main loot

Main drops at Erethea's Limbo (Mountain of Eternal Winter): Flame of Frost (unique drop), Iridescent Lighstone, Ouk Pill of Time and Tide, Essence of Insight, Starlit Jade's Breath, Ancient Magic Crystal of Nature - Giant, Sturdiness, Fighting Spirit and Adamantine, Heavenly Essence (Ignore Stun, Grapple, Knockdown and Knockback Resistance variants), Ancient Spirit Dust, Caphras Stone and Black Stone. The zone is unaffected by the Item Collection Increase Gauge. Deboreka Ring can be obtained through Erethea's Slate, or through Processing (L) - Manufacture with Limbo Star x100.
