# Kamasylvia Monster Zones

The eight grinding spots of Kamasylvia (Fadus Habitat to Ash Forest) with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and main loot, including the spirit essence treasure pieces.

## Kamasylvia monster zones: overview

Kamasylvia's main questline is offered by the Black Spirit at Lv. 58. The land is the forests of Goddess Sylvia, her children the elves and the nature spirits, under the Kamasylve tree and the Ganelle capital of Grána. Many local inhabitants turned hostile after a meteorite fell to the north and because of the conflict with the Ahib in O'dyllita to the east. Players can get powerful equipment and the much sought-after spirit essence treasure pieces here. The zones are Fadus Habitat, Polly's Forest, Navarn Steppe, Mirumok Ruins, Forest Ronaros Area, Manshaum Forest, Gyfin Rhasia Temple and Ash Forest.

## Fadus Habitat (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Fadus Habitat in Kamasylvia is a forest seized by the fadus, former allies of the Ganelle and forest spirits, who lost their senses to the white ash from the Ash Forest and now fight everyone. Recommended level is Lv. 55~56, with recommended AP 120+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 56). Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5, near Acher Guard Post for services. There are no unique drops. Monster density is high and there are several safe parking spots, so it is easy to find an area to yourself; parking depends on the route, or leave the mount at Acher Guard Post.

## Fadus Habitat (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Fadus Habitat (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 57 ~ 59 monsters: Fadus Warrior, Fadus Challenger and Fadus Dual Wielder in melee, Fadus Warrior Chief in melee and ranged, Fadus Archer and Fadus Shaman at range, and structures: Fadus Blue Pride, Fadus Blue Box and Fadus Weapon Ornament, plus Fadus Blue Barrier, Fadus Double Barrack and Fadus Supply Barrack. Corner the melee monsters toward the ranged ones, and destroy tents and barriers when you start a group. The Fadus Warrior Chief attacks at melee and range with skills worth dodging, and is dangerous at lower stats. Any location is good because density is high. The zone gives good combat and skill experience.

## Fadus Habitat (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Fadus Habitat (Kamasylvia): Atanis' Element (exchangeable for pity pieces of the spirit essence treasures), Marsh's Artifact - Magic AP, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Evasion, Lemoria Gear (Gloves & Helmet), Guardian Spirit Stone, Destruction Spirit Stone and Earth Spirit Stone Fragment. Materials include Trace of Nature, Destructive Illusion, Manshaum Vodoo Doll, Ancient Spirit Dust, Swaying Wind Shard, Forest Fury, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Polly's Forest (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Polly's Forest in Kamasylvia is a damp, dark forest of poison-making mushrooms, once home to the spirit Polly and now looked after by the mushroom spirits Looney and Weenie. It is close to Grána. Recommended level is Lv. 56~57, with recommended AP 140+ and DP 250+ (the stat table lists level 56). Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. The unique drop is Manos Craftsman's Clothes. Park at Looney's and Weenie's, away from monster groups, because many monsters have long-range attacks and may target your mount.

## Polly's Forest (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Polly's Forest (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 57 ~ 59 monsters with high density: Cotton Bubble Mushroom, Cloudy Rain Mushroom and Red Skirt Poison Mushroom in melee, Thief Imp Philums in melee and ranged, and at range the Shadow Poison Mushroom, Musk Pocket Mushroom, Snowflake Poison Mushroom and Twinkle-in-the-Dark Mushroom, plus Trumpet Bell Poison Mushroom. Many attack from range, so go group to group. Beware debuffs from mushrooms and imps; if your AP and DP are under the range, handle each group separately. After killing mushrooms, you can gather from them with a hoe. Valuable truffle mushrooms (for elixirs) and rainbow button mushrooms (for Kamasylvian dishes) attract many players, plus those wanting quick combat and skill experience and Atanis' Element.

## Polly's Forest (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Polly's Forest (Kamasylvia): Manos Craftsman's Clothes (unique drop), Atanis' Element, Marsh's Artifact - Melee AP, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Evasion, Life Spirit Stone, Lemoria Gear (Shoes & Armor), Water Spirit Stone Fragment and Truffle Mushroom. Materials include Trace of Nature, Destructive Illusion, Manshaum Vodoo Doll, Ancient Spirit Dust, Pure Forest Breath, Forest Fury, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Navarn Steppe (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Navarn Steppe in Kamasylvia is a vast grassland, with plants as tall as an adult, home to wolves, elephants, ferries, black leopards and griffons; the Manshaum tribe's hunting has broken its order. It is right next to Old Wisdom Tree, where you leave your mount. Recommended level is Lv. 57~58, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 260+ (the stat table lists level 58). Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars (watch the leopards and griffons), user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 5 of 5. The unique drop is Griffon's Helmet.

## Navarn Steppe (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Navarn Steppe (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 58 ~ 60 melee monsters. The griffon and leopard types are Immature King Grifon, Immature Griffon and Immature Black Leopard, then King Griffon, Griffon and Black Leopard. Others are Baby Belladonna Elephant and Belladonna Elephant, the ferries Ferrina and Ferrica, Phnyl and Feather Wolf, plus Griffon Egg. Do not anger too many elites at once, since they can briefly immobilize you, which can be deadly with a swarm around. Ancient Valtarra's Clairvoyance and its pity piece Valtarra's Memory come mainly from skinning the ferries (ferricas and ferrinas), so players gather where they cluster; avoid the popular ferri spots to dodge crowds. Your route depends on your target, and killing griffons may drop a Griffon's Helmet.

## Navarn Steppe (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Navarn Steppe (Kamasylvia): Griffon's Helmet (unique drop), Marsh's Artifact - Magic AP, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Evasion, Ancient Valtarra's Clairvoyance (a piece for Odore's Spirit Essence) with its pity piece Valtarra's Memory, Life Spirit Stone, Destruction Spirit Stone, Earth Spirit Stone Fragment and Wind Spirit Stone Fragment. Materials include Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Manshaum Vodoo Doll, Pure Forest Breath, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon).

## Manshaum Forest (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Manshaum Forest in Kamasylvia is home to the secluded Manshaum tribe of warriors, hunters and shamans, who worshiped Mansha and totems; a new religion has made them hostile. It is close to Old Wisdom Tree. Recommended level is Lv. 58~59, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 260+ (the stat table lists level 59). Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. The unique drop is the Narc Ear Accessory, a popular yellow-grade accessory. Park at the forest entrance to the east, at a few safe spots on the path, or near the node manager on a hill.

## Manshaum Forest (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Manshaum Forest (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 58 monsters, mainly melee: Manshaum Hunter, Manshaum Fighter, Manshaum Warrior and Manshaum Great Warrior, with Manshaum Shaman at range, plus Manshaum Hut, Manshaum Charm and Manshaum Narc's Spear. Kill the shamans first or you take their spells at full force. It is one of the ingredient locations for Odore's Spirit Essence, so expect competition, and watch other players as much as monsters. Monsters here drop the Ancient Narc's Crimson Tear and its pity piece, Narc's Solace, and it is a good place for Caphras Stones.

## Manshaum Forest (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Manshaum Forest (Kamasylvia): Narc Ear Accessory (unique drop), Ancient Narc's Crimson Tear, Narc's Solace, Marsh's Artifact - Magic AP, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Magic Evasion, Lemoria Gear (Shoes & Gloves), Guardian Spirit Stone and Water Spirit Stone Fragment. Materials include Trace of Nature, Manshaum Vodoo Doll, Ancient Spirit Dust, Rumbling Earth Shard, Pure Forest Breath, Forest Fury, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Mirumok Ruins (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Mirumok Ruins in Kamasylvia are the site of Mirumok Temple, still guarded by ancient weapons made of Mirumok. It is equally distant from both towns. Recommended level is Lv. 58-59, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 270+ (the stat table lists level 59). Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5 (good combat experience), hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. The unique drop is the Valtarra Eclipsed Belt, a popular yellow-grade accessory. It is designed for parties of 2 to 3 adventurers, though solo is possible. Park at the forest edges or in the northern caves.

## Mirumok Ruins (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Mirumok Ruins (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 60 ~ 62 monsters, fewer but stronger, with weakening debuffs: Voraro and Old Mirumok in melee, Degraded Old Tree Treant, Degraded Ruins Tree Treant and Mirumok in melee and ranged, plus Mirumok Watcher, Mirumok Lookout, Treant Old Tree and Treant Ghost Tree. Target the treant old trees and ghost trees first, or their debuffs weaken you a lot. The lookouts and watchers summon waves of Voraros, so attack them only when you will not be surrounded. Do not take on too many at once. Combat experience and Atanis' Element draw intruders, so watch for other players.

## Mirumok Ruins (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Mirumok Ruins (Kamasylvia): Valtarra Eclipsed Belt (unique drop), Marsh's Artifacts for Magic Accuracy, Ranged Accuracy and Melee Accuracy, Atanis' Element, Lemoria Gear (Helmet), Destruction Spirit Stone and Tree Spirit Stone Fragment. Materials include Manshaum Vodoo Doll, Ancient Spirit Dust, Rumbling Earth Shard, Swaying Wind Shard, Pure Forest Breath, Forest Fury, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). No spirit essence treasure piece drops, but Atanis' Element from the Mirumok ancient weapons can be exchanged for a pity piece of your choice. There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Forest Ronaros Area (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Forest Ronaros Area in Kamasylvia, also called the Tooth Fairy Forest, was home to the tooth fairies alone and is now shared with the forest ronaros, driven out of Loopy Tree Forest by crazed fadus. It is not particularly close to Grána. Recommended level is Lv. 58-59, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 270+ (the stat table lists level 59), the same as Mirumok Ruins. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5 (everyone wants Ornette's Spirit Essence), hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. The unique drop is the Forest Ronaros Ring, a yellow-grade accessory usable in recipes that need one. Several roadside spots allow safe parking.

## Forest Ronaros Area (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Forest Ronaros Area (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 60 ~ 63 monsters in small groups that are numerous but individually weaker: Forest Ronaros Scout and Forest Ronaros Guardian in melee, Forest Ronaros Catcher in melee and ranged, Forest Ronaros Marksman at range, plus White Tree Thorn Vine and Ron's Mirror. Ronaros can quickly teleport to your position and the thorn vines apply a movement debuff and poison. In a hard fight, do not hit the Ron's mirrors, because they summon extra forest ronaros. Unless you are overwhelmed by numbers, you need not be too wary.

## Forest Ronaros Area (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Forest Ronaros Area (Kamasylvia): Forest Ronaros Ring (unique drop), Marsh's Artifacts for Magic Accuracy, Ranged Accuracy and Melee Accuracy, Ancient Ron's Tintinnabulum (a treasure piece) and its pity piece Tranquil Tinniolum, Lemoria Gear (Shoes & Gloves), Guardian Spirit Stone, Earth Spirit Stone Fragment and Krogdalo's Origin Stone. Materials include Trace of Nature, Manshaum Vodoo Doll, Ancient Spirit Dust, Rumbling Earth Shard, Pure Forest Breath, Forest Fury, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Gyfin Rhasia Temple in Kamasylvia, proof of a third civilization, lies beyond the southern coast with a vast underground; its ancient weapons are brass and bronze. It is close to Grána. It is split into two zones. Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Upper), above ground, recommends Lv. 60+, AP 250+ and DP 320+, and is a group zone best with a full party of 5. Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Underground) recommends Lv. 62+, AP 290+ and DP 380+, and is as hard as Ash Forest. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. Unique drops are the Tungrad Belt and Tungrad Necklace. Park outside the temple; upper-level spots are plentiful, but underground use the temple entrance.

## Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 64 ~ 66 monsters: Gyfin Rhasia Guard, Gyfin Rhasia Crusher and Gyfin Rhasia Decimator in melee, Gyfin Rhasia Flamen at range, plus Gyfin Rhasia Statue of Earth, Tower of Transfusion and Tower of Carnage. The upper part gives good combat and skill experience and a chance at a Tungrad Belt; in a full group you suffer little, but alone you can be overwhelmed. The underground has fewer but empowered monsters and rewards a Tungrad Belt, a Tungrad Necklace and the magic crystal ingredient Elkarr. Use extreme caution underground, since burdened monsters and rival players are both very strong. Players with good gear come for Elkarr and the Tungrad accessories.

## Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Kamasylvia): burdens of ruin in the Underground

The Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Underground) in Kamasylvia has the burdens of ruin mechanic. Defeating a Gyfin Rhasia with one burden gives a buff against monsters with a different burden. Each petrified ancient weapon near the Gyfin Rhasia Tower of Transfusion carries an emotion shown by its color, and every one defeated adds a burden to the tower. The [Ruinous] Butcher of Gyfin Rhasia has a low chance to spawn among the mixed burdens, and the ancient weapons of [Anger], [Denial] and [Resolve] deactivate when it appears. Defeating it grants the Lost Civilization's Ruin buff, giving tremendous extra damage to all monsters in the underground.

## Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Kamasylvia): the Tungrad Belt, the Tungrad Necklace (Underground), Elkarr (Underground), Kehelle's Artifact – Black Spirit's Rage Max Increase, Lemoria Gear, Destruction Spirit Stone and Krogdalo's Origin Stone. Materials include Trace of Nature (Upper and Underground), Ancient Spirit Dust, Swaying Wind Shard, Rumbling Earth Shard, Pure Forest Breath, Forest Fury, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon).

## Ash Forest (Kamasylvia): location and recommended AP/DP

Ash Forest in Kamasylvia is the former forest of dreams, killed by white ash after a meteorite hit Mount Kabua to the north; ghosts of the nature spirits roam it in mist. Recommended level is Lv. 62+, with recommended AP 300+ and DP 390+. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars, user density 1 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5 (the furthest possible). The unique drop is the Deboreka Necklace. Park outside the forest; there is no real route because monsters are spread throughout. Stay on guard even when passing by.

## Ash Forest (Kamasylvia): monsters and strategy

Ash Forest (Kamasylvia) has mostly Lv. 66 monsters: Volkras and Gairas in melee and Barnas in melee and ranged. The ghosts phase in and out of existence, so ambushes are common. Make sure you meet the recommended stats. Few have the strength to challenge the specters, but the rewards attract players willing to gamble.

## Ash Forest (Kamasylvia): main loot

Main drops at Ash Forest (Kamasylvia): the Deboreka Necklace (unique drop), Quturan's Left Lung, Quturan's Black Leaf, Kehelle's Artifact - Max Stamina, Trace of Nature, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). The Deboreka Necklace is the only unique drop. These rewards draw players of all kinds willing to try their luck despite the zone's difficulty of 5 of 5 stars.
