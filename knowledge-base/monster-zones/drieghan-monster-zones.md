# Drieghan Monster Zones

The three grinding spots of Drieghan (Tshira Ruins, Blood Wolf Settlement, Sherekhan Necropolis) with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and main loot.

## Drieghan monster zones: overview

Drieghan has three monster zones, meant for adventurers who have finished Valencia: Tshira Ruins, Blood Wolf Settlement and Sherekhan Necropolis. Each drops specific accessories and magic crystals, and the three also give the pieces of the spirit essence treasures. While fighting in any of them, Garmoth may ambush you at any moment: she appears in the sky and bombards the ground with fireballs, and a chest with rewards for surviving appears where the attacks landed. Sherekhan Necropolis changes between day and night, because the Guardian Spirit elite monsters only appear at night and you choose when to summon them.

## Tshira Ruins (Drieghan): location and recommended AP/DP

Tshira Ruins in Drieghan is a ruined, ever-expanding swamp that was once a dense forest like the Khimut Great Forest, cursed when a badly wounded black dragon spilled its blood there. Recommended level is Lv. 56 and up, with recommended AP 140+ and DP 160+. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars (just dodge the down smash attacks), user density 4 of 5 (everyone wants the spirit essence treasures), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5, so it takes a while to get there. Park your mount by the node manager. The unique drop is the Eye of the Ruins Ring, which gives AP +5 and Accuracy +2 and gains Max HP Increase when enhanced. The node manager Leon offers daily quests, and Marni's stones can be used here.

## Tshira Ruins (Drieghan): monsters and strategy

Tshira Ruins (Drieghan) has mostly Lv. 56 ~ 59 monsters spread evenly across a large, flat map. Melee types are Leaf Keeper, Vine Keeper and Grove Keeper, then Murky Swamp Caller, Tree Hermit, Tree Ghost Spider and Leaf Spider; the ranged type is Swamp Imp Bronks; there are also Bronk's Hut, Venomous Swamp Nest and Bronk's Food Storage. Most are slow, so just watch and dodge. Dodge the strong attacks of the Leaf, Grove and Vine Keepers and avoid debuffs, kill the ranged Swamp Imp Bronks first, and quickly leave the green energy from a Venomous Swamp Nest. You can start from anywhere. The respawn is fast, making it good for groups, and it is a little easier than Blood Wolf Settlement but with many more monsters.

## Tshira Ruins (Drieghan): Kvariak encounters

Kvariaks appear at Tshira Ruins (Drieghan) from time to time as you hunt: a system message announces a kvariak, a brother of the field boss Katzvariak. Go to the marked spot, which has no monsters nearby and a light pillar. Kvariaks are much stronger than the normal swamp monsters and attack with spear skills and area-of-effect venom.

## Tshira Ruins (Drieghan): main loot

Main drops at Tshira Ruins (Drieghan): the Eye of the Ruins Ring (unique drop); Lesha's Artifact - Magic Evasion and Lesha's Artifact - Magic Damage Reduction; Marsh's Artifact - Magic AP; Combined Magic Crystal - Gervish, - Macalod and - Hoom; Akum Gear; Ancient Markthanan's Gland; Katzvariak's Venom; and the common materials Trace of Nature, Black Energy Residue, Garmoth's Scale, Dragon Scale Fossil, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is also a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Blood Wolf Settlement (Drieghan): location and recommended AP/DP

Blood Wolf Settlement in eastern Drieghan is home to the blood wolves, the Kagtum tribe that worships the crimson dragon Garmoth. Recommended level is Lv. 57 and up, with recommended AP 180+ and DP 210+. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5 (quite close to Duvencrune). Mount parking is at Marak Farm or Night Crow Post. Unique drops are Manos Craftsman's Clothes and the Eye of the Ruins Ring. Daily quests give contribution experience and Marni's stones can be used. The Kagtum Submission Ring (AP +5, Accuracy +2, plus Extra Damage against Monsters) also drops here.

## Blood Wolf Settlement (Drieghan): monsters and strategy

Blood Wolf Settlement (Drieghan) has mostly Lv. 56 ~ 61 monsters spread evenly from the entrances at Night Crow Post and Marak Farm throughout the area. Melee types: Kagtum Raider, Kagtum Laborer, Kagtum Warden, Kagtum Executioner, Kagtum Lookout and Blood Wolf, plus Garmoth of Fury Statue, Traitor's Jail and Garmoth of Enchantment Statue, and the violent Frenzied Kagtum. The monsters are fast and gather quickly, so you can get surrounded in an instant. The huge Frenzied Kagtum attacks non-stop with sharp claws. Routes start at Marak Farm or Night Crow Post and run all the way to where Titoku is located. It is a little easier than Sherekhan Necropolis, has a good number of monsters, and is one of the closest zones to Duvencrune, so it suits long stays.

## Blood Wolf Settlement (Drieghan): Kagtum Guard encounter

At Blood Wolf Settlement (Drieghan), breaking enough of the jails that hold traitors to the blood wolves makes a Kagtum guard appear with a pack of blood wolves to stop you. Prepare for it, or you will be torn apart. This zone also drops the Ancient Ash Halfmoon Kagtunak, one of the pieces of a spirit essence treasure.

## Blood Wolf Settlement (Drieghan): main loot

Main drops at Blood Wolf Settlement (Drieghan): Manos Craftsman's Clothes and the Eye of the Ruins Ring (unique drops); Kagtum Submission Ring; Blood Wolf's Oath; Ancient Ash Halfmoon Kagtunak and Kagtunak; Lesha's Artifact - Melee Evasion and - Melee Damage Reduction; Marsh's Artifact - Melee AP; Akum Gear; Combined Magic Crystal - Gervish and - Hoom; and the common materials Trace of Nature, Black Energy Residue, Garmoth's Scale, Dragon Scale Fossil, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). There is also a low chance of Specter's Energy.

## Sherekhan Necropolis (Drieghan): location and recommended AP/DP

Sherekhan Necropolis in Drieghan is where five warriors recognized by the Chenga Sherekhan, Belcadas, Nybrica, Garud, Lateh and Federik, returned after the crimson dragon was seen over Drieghan. Recommended level is Lv. 58 and up, with recommended AP 190+ and DP 230+. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5 (many come at night), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park by the node manager; a general goods vendor is nearby. The unique drop is Orkinrad's Belt (AP +7, Accuracy +2, Weight Limit +80LT, Max HP Increase when enhanced). The area differs a lot between day and night.

## Sherekhan Necropolis (Drieghan): monsters and strategy

Sherekhan Necropolis (Drieghan) has mostly Lv. 60 ~ monsters by day and Lv. 65 ~ at night. By day the melee monsters are the five warriors, Garud, Nybrica, Lateh, Federik and Belcadas, spread in groups; there are not many, but players come for the spirit essence piece. Every warrior is powerful, so avoid debuffs and watch for attacks wrapped in blue energy. Clusters of monsters stand just below the node manager. The zone is far harder than both Blood Wolf Settlement and Tshira Ruins, yet the loot makes it worthwhile, so players sure of their gear and control visit often.

## Sherekhan Necropolis (Drieghan): day and night Guardian Spirits

At 10:00 at night in Sherekhan Necropolis (Drieghan), the grouped monsters vanish and dreary energy fills the land. Each of the five warriors has a tomb or grave shaped like its weapon. Spend 10 energy at one to summon the Guardian Spirit, an elite version of that warrior, for an intense one-on-one fight; they appear one at a time. Elites are stronger than the day versions and better loot becomes more likely, so summon and defeat them one by one, and bring plenty of energy because each summon costs 10.

## Sherekhan Necropolis (Drieghan): main loot

Main drops at Sherekhan Necropolis (Drieghan): Orkinrad's Belt (unique drop), made by combining Orkinrad Piece - Metal Ornament and Orkinrad Piece - Leather Knot; Lesha's Artifact - Magic Evasion and - Magic Damage Reduction; Marsh's Artifact - Magic AP; Akum Gear; Dragon's Fang; Ancient Sherekhan's Panacea; Combined Magic Crystal - Macalod and - Hoom; and the common materials Trace of Nature, Black Energy Residue, Garmoth's Scale, Dragon Scale Fossil, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). The chance of Specter's Energy is increased here.
