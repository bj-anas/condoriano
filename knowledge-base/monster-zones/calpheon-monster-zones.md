# Calpheon Monster Zones

The grinding spots of southwestern Calpheon, from beginner zones around level 48 up to Star's End and the Abandoned Monastery. For each zone it gives recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and main loot.

## Calpheon monster zones: overview and order of visiting

Calpheon monster zones are the grinding spots of southwestern Calpheon. Players should be ready to go there at around Lv. 47~48. The area is large and can feel overwhelming for a beginner, but the main questline sends you there. You can visit the zones in any order that suits your level. Star's End, North Kaia Mountain and the Abandoned Monastery's restricted territory are better left until later, because their monsters are too strong for a freshly arrived character. The zones are Rhutum Outstation, Catfishman Camp, Mansha Forest, Hexe Sanctuary (with the Traitor's Graveyard inside it), North Kaia Mountain, Star's End (with the Vessel of Inquisition) and the Abandoned Monastery (Restricted Territory).

## Rhutum Outstation (Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

Rhutum Outstation in Calpheon is an easy early grinding zone held by green-skinned rhutum orcs, who speak the human tongue, craft weapons, train soldiers and keep catfishmen as slaves. It lies between Calpheon and Trent. Recommended level is 48~50, with recommended AP 50 and DP 130. Combat is not tough, so level and attack power are not a worry. Ratings: difficulty 1 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5 (you can often have it to yourself), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park your mount at the outskirts of the post. The zone's unique drops are the Rhutum Elite Belt and the Treant Spirit's Hope Belt.

## Rhutum Outstation (Calpheon): monsters and strategy

Rhutum Outstation (Calpheon) has mostly Lv. 50 monsters. Melee types are Rhutum Soldier, Rhutum Elite Warrior, Rhutum Fighter and Rhutum Elite Brawler. Ranged units are Rhutum Archer, Rhutum Elite Archer and Rhutum Wizard. There are also Rhutum Tower and Rhutum Cannon. The elite rhutums are fairly tough; Rhutum Elite Archers hit hard but have low endurance, so kill them first. Hunting inside the wooden wall is a bit better than outside it, though both work. Daily quests in the area give Contribution EXP. It is a good fallback when Mansha Forest is crowded while you level up.

## Rhutum Outstation (Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at Rhutum Outstation (Calpheon): the Treant Spirit's Hope Belt and the Rhutum Elite Belt (the zone's unique drops), Altar Imp Boss Summon Scroll, Black Stone, Ancient Spirit Dust and Ancient Relic Crystal Shard. Both unique drops are belts, and the Altar Imp Boss Summon Scroll is the item that calls the Altar Imp boss.

## Catfishman Camp (Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

Catfishman Camp in Calpheon is a beginner zone on Lake Kaia's shoreline, very close to Calpheon, where hostile catfishmen of all sizes live. Recommended level is 49~51, with recommended AP 50 and DP 120 (the stat table lists level 49). It is not hard at the recommended stats, but monsters can corner you, so keep defense in mind. Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. Parking is a little dangerous, so find a safe spot on the lake outskirts. The unique drop is the Treant Spirit's Whisper Earring.

## Catfishman Camp (Calpheon): monsters and strategy

Catfishman Camp (Calpheon) has mostly Lv. 48 monsters: Catfishman, Fat Catfishman, Catfishman Light-armored Warrior and Otter Fisher, plus Catfishman Meat and Catfishman Rotten Fish. The monsters sit close together so you can hunt continuously, but at too low a level your pet may not have time to loot. The Violent (star) Fat Catfishman appears from time to time and is tough, so do not get complacent. The spots along the lake border are good. When parking on the left side of the lake, beware of roaming Red Boars. It is the best place to reach level 50 and suits levels in the late 40s or early 50s.

## Catfishman Camp (Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at Catfishman Camp (Calpheon): the Treant Spirit's Whisper Earring (unique drop), Goblin Chief Summon Scroll, Black Stone, Ancient Spirit Dust and Ancient Relic Crystal Shard. The earring is the only unique drop, and the Goblin Chief Summon Scroll calls the Goblin Chief boss. Since this is the best place to reach level 50, players often farm here while leveling.

## Mansha Forest (Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

Mansha Forest in Calpheon holds the hut of Mansha, leader of the goblin tribe that lives across Calpheon; armed goblins rob travelers and the forest counts as one of Calpheon's danger areas. It is a little far from Calpheon. Recommended level is 49~51, with recommended AP 50 and DP 100 (the stat table lists level 48~49). With those stats the goblins are fun, but low DP makes the ogres dangerous. Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. Parking is a little dangerous. Unique drops are the Ogre Ring and the Treant Spirit's Echo Ring.

## Mansha Forest (Calpheon): monsters and strategy

Mansha Forest (Calpheon) has mostly Lv. 49 monsters in groups, so you hunt from group to group: Mansha Warrior, Brown Bear, Boar and Ogre in melee, and Mansha Javelin Thrower at range. Most attack from a distance, which is not a big problem since they are clustered. Ogres are strong, hit hard and use skill debuffs, so they are difficult for a character who just reached Lv. 50, but they may drop an Ogre Ring at a low chance. Goblins are spread widely, so finish one area before moving on. The Ogre Ring is one of the best accessories in the game, so even high-level players visit; there are enough ogres that it stays uncrowded.

## Mansha Forest (Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at Mansha Forest (Calpheon): the Ogre Ring and the Treant Spirit's Echo Ring (unique drops), Soiled Ogre Ring, Goblin Chief Summon Scroll, Black Stone, and the crafting materials Ancient Spirit Dust and Ancient Relic Crystal Shard. The Ogre Ring comes from ogres at a low chance and is one of the best accessories in the game, so even high-level players visit. The Soiled Ogre Ring is a related drop.

## Hexe Sanctuary (Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

Hexe Sanctuary in Calpheon is the witch Hexe Marie's fairytale-like hideout near Behr, guarded by undead, and is the last stop on the Calpheon journey. Recommended level is 50~52, with recommended AP 70 and DP 140 (the stat table lists level 50). With that gear it is easy, since there are few variables. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5 (popular, because everyone wants the earring), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Parking depends on your route. The unique drop is the Witch's Earring, a blue grade earring.

## Hexe Sanctuary (Calpheon): monsters and strategy

Hexe Sanctuary (Calpheon) has mostly Lv. 50~52 monsters: Skeleton, Skeleton Warrior, Skeleton Wolf, Skeleton Lizard, Skeleton Witmirth and Green Orc Skeleton Warrior in melee, Skeleton Archer at range, plus Crow, Gravestone, Wooden Column, Young Elk, Elk and Witch Tower. Witch towers summon the skeletons and skeleton archers, so hit one tower once and wait for them to come. The Green Orc Skeleton drops the Witch's Earring most often, so its spot at the Witch Chapel node is crowded and you may meet other players. If it is too busy, go to Calpheon Shrine or the Abandoned Monastery and then on to Mediah.

## Hexe Sanctuary (Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at Hexe Sanctuary (Calpheon): the Witch's Earring (unique drop, blue grade), Witch of Horrors Summon Scroll, Black Stone, Ancient Spirit Dust and Ancient Relic Crystal Shard. The Green Orc Skeleton drops the Witch's Earring most often, which is why players hunt it, and the Witch of Horrors Summon Scroll calls the Witch of Horrors boss.

## Traitor's Graveyard (Hexe Sanctuary, Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

Traitor's Graveyard is a stationary graveyard zone inside Hexe Sanctuary in Calpheon, reached after leaving Marie Cave and near Behr. Touching the graveyard raises endless waves of undead that try to destroy the traitor's gravestone. Recommended level is 56+, with recommended AP 140 and DP 300. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park near the cave's entrance (cave exit). Its unique drop is the Witch's Earring. It is popular for Caphras Stones.

## Traitor's Graveyard (Hexe Sanctuary, Calpheon): monsters and strategy

Traitor's Graveyard (Hexe Sanctuary, Calpheon) has mostly Lv. 59 monsters: Skeleton, Skeleton Warrior, Skeleton Wolf, Skeleton Lizard and Green Orc Skeleton Warrior, Skeleton Archer at range, and the Traitor's Gravestone. The skeletons come in endless waves, so you must deal with them as they appear or you will be forced to flee. Because the zone is a stationary gravestone, there are no routes to follow.

## Traitor's Graveyard (Hexe Sanctuary, Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at Traitor's Graveyard (Hexe Sanctuary, Calpheon): Witch's Earring, Caphras Stone, Mass of Pure Magic, Trace of Nature, Black Stone, and the materials Ancient Spirit Dust and Ancient Relic Crystal Shard. The large number of Caphras Stones from the waves makes it popular with players who need them.

## North Kaia Mountain (Mutant Tree Spirit, Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

North Kaia Mountain (Mutant Tree Spirit) is a dangerous Calpheon summit overlooking the whole city of Calpheon, linked to the southwestern mountain range and avoided because of dragon rumors. It is very close to Calpheon. Recommended level is 55~56, with recommended AP 120 and DP 220 (the stat table lists level 56). Mutant tree spirits are designed as party-type monsters, so decent level and gear matter. Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5 (low, since few monsters), hunting satisfaction 5 of 5 if you have it alone, distance from town 3 of 5. Park near North Kaia Mountain. The unique drop is the Tree Spirit Belt, a blue grade accessory.

## North Kaia Mountain (Mutant Tree Spirit, Calpheon): monsters and strategy

North Kaia Mountain (Calpheon) has mostly Lv. 60 monsters, all Mutant Tree Spirits, which use both melee and ranged skills. There are few of them but each is strong and widely spread, so remember where they appear. They telegraph melee attacks by how they move, giving time to dodge. Attack from behind because they bombard you with debuffs, and watch out for their 360-degree attacks. Use food or elixirs and high-level equipment, as with the Wilderness Golem in Mediah. Combat EXP is rarely worth it, but the zone is popular because a dark rift boss such as the Dim Tree Spirit can spawn here.

## North Kaia Mountain (Mutant Tree Spirit, Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at North Kaia Mountain (Mutant Tree Spirit, Calpheon): the Tree Spirit Belt (unique drop), Black Stone and Ancient Magic Crystal – Harphia. The Tree Spirit Belt is a blue grade accessory and is a lucky drop. Players also come because the Dim Tree Spirit, a dark rift boss, can spawn in this zone.

## Star's End (Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

Star's End in Calpheon is a quarantined area, fairly close to the Calpheon Northwestern Outpost, where a black meteorite crash spread a plague that turned the infected into extremely powerful monsters. Recommended level is Lv. 61 and up. Recommended AP and DP are 240+ AP and 320+ DP, with a second row listing 395+ AP and 460+ DP. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars (highest), user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 5 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. Park far away because the area is densely populated. The unique drop is the Black Distortion Earring. It can also be entered through Marni's Realm, the private monster zone, and the main questline and Blackstar equipment questlines can be done here.

## Star's End (Calpheon): monsters and strategy

Star's End (Calpheon) has monsters around Lv. 61~65, few in number but each extremely powerful, all melee: the Harbinger and Apostle of Corruption, of Defilement and of Immorality, plus Remnants of Corruption and Apostle of Malevolence. Destroy any Trace of a Corrupt Star first, because it lowers your defense. Destroy Unstable Star Debris once a good number of monsters have gathered, because it weakens them. The monsters are relentless, so stay alert. Very few players manage to subjugate them.

## Star's End (Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at Star's End (Calpheon): the Black Distortion Earring, Marsh's Artifacts (Magic Accuracy, Ranged Accuracy, Melee Accuracy), Clear Blackstar Crystal, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Ancient Magic Crystal of Nature - Adamantine, Caphras Stone, Mass of Pure Magic, Trace of Nature, Black Stone and Ancient Spirit Dust. Clear Blackstar Crystal can be used in simple alchemy (hotkey: L) to craft Corrupted Magic Crystals, granting +10% Critical Hit Damage and +2 All AP at the cost of All Damage Reduction -2, with a 2 crystal set effect of Critical Hit Damage +2%.

## Vessel of Inquisition (Star's End, Calpheon): how it works

The Vessel of Inquisition is a stationary wave-style spot in the Star's End zone of Calpheon, in parts where players rarely go. Striking a vessel calls Kzarka's apostles, who charge it in waves to take the energy leaking from the Blackstar fragment, and they disperse once it is destroyed. It works like the Traitor's Graveyard of Hexe Sanctuary. The apostles here are stronger than the ordinary Star's End monsters, and the vessel shatters if too many of them gather and attack it.

## Abandoned Monastery (Restricted Territory, Calpheon): location and recommended AP/DP

The Abandoned Monastery (Restricted Territory) in southwestern Calpheon was once a repenting ground for Elionian priests. Its Shadow Knights grew stronger through Kzarka's blessing, so the inner cave complex beneath it is restricted territory. It is close to Trent. Recommended level is Lv. 62, with recommended AP 260+ and DP 350+. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars, user density 1 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park at the Abandoned Monastery. The unique drop is the Dawn Earring. It is a party zone where a group of 2 Adventurers can loot together.

## Abandoned Monastery (Restricted Territory, Calpheon): monsters and strategy

The Abandoned Monastery restricted territory (Calpheon) has mostly Lv. 65~66 human-type monsters: Calpheon Shadow Knight, Calpheon Elite Shadow Knight, Calpheon Elite Shadow Priestess and Calpheon Shadow Guard in melee, and Calpheon Shadow Wizard at range. There are not many, but the dungeon is narrow and dark and the monsters are powerful, so being cornered is common. Falling stalactites incapacitate you for a few seconds, which can be fatal in a fight. The monastery well connects to the dungeon below, where you find the passage to the restricted territory; other entrances, including one straight to it, are in the mountains east of Treant Forest. Killing Shadow Knights builds dawn's grudges. At five, a server-wide message warns that the Specter of Belmorn is appearing. That boss has choreographed attacks and drops Dawn's Resentment.

## Abandoned Monastery (Restricted Territory, Calpheon): main loot

Main drops at the Abandoned Monastery restricted territory (Calpheon): Dawn Earring, Dawn's Resentment, Marsh's Artifacts (Magic Accuracy, Ranged Accuracy, Melee Accuracy), Mass of Pure Magic, Black Stone, and the materials Ancient Spirit Dust and Ancient Relic Crystal Shard. Dawn's Resentment is dropped by the Specter of Belmorn and, at a low chance, by the zone's regular enemies, as is the Dawn Earring itself. Dawn Earring, a yellow grade accessory, is crafted from Dawn's Resentment together with Mark of Shadow x2 and Shrine Guardian Token x2.
