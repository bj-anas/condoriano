# Ulukita Monster Zones

The grinding spots of Ulukita, south of Mediah (City of the Dead, Tungrad Ruins, Darkseekers' Retreat, Yzrahid Highlands), with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and loot.

## Ulukita monster zones: overview

Ulukita lies south of Mediah and has four monster zones: City of the Dead, Tungrad Ruins, Darkseekers' Retreat and Yzrahid Highlands. The land is wide, so exploring it feels like fighting the land itself. The zones need high AP and DP, but fights are not too stressful. Ulukita drops Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Telescope Parts, used for the Upgraded Telescope that lets you instantly move to another adventurer's location, and Origin of Dark Hunger, which raises enhancement chance and can be registered on the Central Market.

## Ulukita monster zones: recommended AP and DP

Recommended level, AP and DP for every Ulukita monster zone. Most also have an AP limit, above which only 70% of the extra AP applies.

## City of the Dead (Ulukita): location and recommended AP/DP

City of the Dead in Ulukita was a settlement of people who fled Mediah's tyranny and the Three Days of Darkness and called themselves Tehmelunians; the darkness caught up, and the soldiers rose as the undead scattering black sand. Recommended level is Lv. 61+, with recommended AP 320+ and DP 380+ and a 1100 AP Limit (only 70% of the exceeded AP is applied). Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5, a short ride from Asparkan Fort. Ride south from Asparkan Fort, past the Neruda Plain, and the dead surround you. Parking depends on your route.

## City of the Dead (Ulukita): monsters and strategy

City of the Dead (Ulukita) has mostly Lv. 66 monsters: Tehmelun Creed Knight, Tehmelun Elite Soldier, Tehmelun Seeker and Tehmelun Military Dog in melee, with the Tehmelun Seeker also ranged. A simple gimmick knocks monsters down: when the Tehmelun Messenger leading them performs a certain action, any CC skill makes all nearby monsters fall, and you get the Essence of Ulukita buff to sweep them away. Without it the zone is an uphill battle even at recommended stats. Avoid their attacks, even with the recommended stats. The longer you stay, the likelier a Tehmelun Creed Knight shows up, a commander of the black sands who keeps calling reinforcements.

## City of the Dead (Ulukita): main loot

Main drops at City of the Dead (Ulukita): Specter's Energy, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Telescope Parts, Origin of Dark Hunger, Essence of Devouring, Kuadir Fragment and Forgotten Limbo Seal. Specter's Energy is used for Blackstar Gear, Origin of Dark Hunger raises your current Enhancement Chance, and Kuadir Fragment is used to craft Visionary Crystal of Elkarr.

## Tungrad Ruins (Ulukita): location and recommended AP/DP

Tungrad Ruins in Ulukita hold the buried Tungrad School, where the Black Spirit's rage was researched. When the Three Days of Darkness reached Ulukita, the Uluan people turned to the sealed school as a last resort, but the released Black Spirit mixed with the darkness and made them monsters of rage. Recommended level is Lv. 63+, with recommended AP 330+ and DP 410+ and a 1100 AP Limit (only 70% of the exceeded AP is applied). Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Ride east from Asparkan Fort, past Atessahra, to reach the ruins. Parking depends on your route.

## Tungrad Ruins (Ulukita): monsters and strategy

Tungrad Ruins (Ulukita) has mostly Lv. 66 melee monsters: Tungrad Putarek, Tungrad Executioner, Tungrad Visionary and Tungrad Ascetic. The method is like City of the Dead: Tungrad Visionary guides are mixed into the groups, and if you attack and detonate them first, you get the Essence of Ulukita effect, which stuns surrounding monsters and lowers their DP, so kill them quickly then. Without this tactic it is an uphill battle. Avoid their attacks at all costs, even more than in City of the Dead. The flying Putarek appears from time to time, and once it attacks, hordes of dread-stricken ascetics and executioners come to overwhelm you.

## Tungrad Ruins (Ulukita): main loot

Main drops at Tungrad Ruins (Ulukita): Specter's Energy, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Telescope Parts, Origin of Dark Hunger, Essence of Devouring, Kuadir Fragment and Forgotten Limbo Seal. Specter's Energy is used for Blackstar Gear, Origin of Dark Hunger raises your Enhancement Chance, and Kuadir Fragment is used to craft Visionary Crystal of Elkarr.

## Darkseekers' Retreat (Ulukita): location and recommended AP/DP

Darkseekers' Retreat in Ulukita is the land of the darkseekers, whose origin is unclear; anyone captured is forced to labor on their citadel. Recommended level is Lv. 63+, with recommended AP 340 and DP 430 and a 1150 AP Limit (only 70% of the exceeded AP is applied). Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars (one skill from the eternal darkseeker can kill you), user density 5 of 5 (the most popular Ulukita zone because of valuable items), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5, a short ride from Asparkan Fort or Muzgar. Ride southeast from Asparkan Fort past Sezec Mercenary Camp to the sunken citadel. Parking depends on your route. The zone uses less Agris Fever than other monster zones.

## Darkseekers' Retreat (Ulukita): monsters and strategy

Darkseekers' Retreat (Ulukita) has mostly Lv. 66 melee monsters: Punitive Darkseeker, Vengeful Darkseeker and Mournful Darkseeker. Darkseekers launch powerful attacks once their strikes accumulate and leave lingering dark aura. They also use devastating AoE, so standing a few steps away may not be safe. Slain monsters respawn faster than elsewhere in Ulukita, so you may face your current targets and the ones you just killed together. The Deep Sunken Darkness appears where Mournful Darkseekers are and, at the start of the fight, summons Vengeful and Punitive Darkseekers. It has three levels, and a higher level summons more; extra Mournful Darkseekers spawn where the others disappeared.

## Darkseekers' Retreat (Ulukita): main loot

Main drops at Darkseekers' Retreat (Ulukita): Kabua's Artifact, Kabua's Fragment, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Telescope Parts, Origin of Dark Hunger, Essence of Devouring, Flame of Resonance, Embers of Resonance and Forgotten Limbo Seal. Players come for the Flame of Resonance, needed for Ator's Shoes of the Slumbering Origin set (see the Slumbering Origin Gear page), and for Kabua's Artifact, the first artifact sellable on the Central Market. Origin of Dark Hunger raises your Enhancement Chance.

## Yzrahid Highlands (Ulukita): location and recommended AP/DP

Yzrahid Highlands in Ulukita is guarded by revealed ancient weapons; the largest, Seculion, seems to be an ark storing energy for the others. Recommended level is Lv. 63+, with recommended AP 310+ and DP 420+ and a 1100 AP Limit (only 70% of the exceeded AP is applied). Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5 (few Seculion, so busy), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5 (you stay in one place fighting waves), distance from town 3 of 5, a short ride from Muzgar or Velandir. You can park your horse relatively close to the Seculion you are fighting.

## Yzrahid Highlands (Ulukita): Seculion fight and strategy

At Yzrahid Highlands (Ulukita) you fight Seculion, a big monster that is easy to spot. Your first attack makes it invulnerable and waves of monsters appear, so watch its AoEs while also hitting its vulnerable legs. One leg glows red; avoid the AoEs and damage that leg until it shuts down. Green circles then appear at random on the floor, and standing in one gives a damage buff, so hit the main body then. After enough body damage, Seculion releases energy waves that instantly destroy the spawned mechanical creatures and do not harm you. Beware its powerful AoE attacks.

## Yzrahid Highlands (Ulukita): main loot

Main drops at Yzrahid Highlands (Ulukita): Kabua's Artifact, Kabua's Fragment, Flame of Resonance, Embers of Resonance and Forgotten Limbo Seal. Flame of Resonance is used to craft Ator's Shoes (see the Slumbering Origin Gear page). Kabua's Artifact is the first artifact sellable on the Central Market, and this zone and Darkseekers' Retreat both drop it.
