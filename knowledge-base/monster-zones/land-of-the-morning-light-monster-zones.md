# Land of the Morning Light Monster Zones

The grinding spots of the Land of the Morning Light (Honglim Base, Dokkebi Forest, Fortune's Golden Pig Cave) with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and loot.

## Land of the Morning Light monster zones: overview

The Land of the Morning Light has three monster zones: Honglim Base, Dokkebi Forest and Fortune's Golden Pig Cave. The land is in turmoil in Donghae Province. Bandits posing as the old people of Dalim Village attack travelers in Bomnae County, rumors of hauntings come from Gowun Plateau, and golden pigs and the formerly friendly dokkebi are rebelling. All three zones recommend level 61+, are not far from Dalbeol, and drop Dawn Crystals and Faint Origin of Dark Hunger.

## Land of the Morning Light monster zones: recommended AP and DP

Recommended level, AP and DP for every Land of the Morning Light monster zone. Each also has a Max AP Limit, and only a share of AP above it counts.

## Honglim Base (Land of the Morning Light): location and recommended AP/DP

Honglim Base in the Land of the Morning Light is the haven the people of Dalim Village built after losing their home, whose masked residents help those in need; bandits now wear Honglim masks and cause trouble. It is not far from Dalbeol, and you park your mount on the road outside the zone. Recommended level is Lv. 61+, with recommended AP 270+, DP 360+ and an 850 Max AP Limit (only 30% of the exceeded AP is applied). Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars (renew the foxfire buff), user density 4 of 5 (it has the lowest requirements in the region, so expect competition for banner spots), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5.

## Honglim Base (Land of the Morning Light): monsters and strategy

Honglim Base (Land of the Morning Light) has mostly Lv. 63 monsters: Boar Bandit, Lynx Bandit, Raccoon Bandit and Fox Bandit Boss in melee, and Fox Bandit at range. You meet sentries guarding a banner; after you beat them, minions of the Centipede Market arrive in waves, then a Fox Bandit Boss, who carries Gumiho energy. Killing a boss frees that energy as a foxfire that follows you and gives a monster damage buff against the bandits. It stacks if you beat a second boss, and lasts about 30 seconds unless renewed by fighting bandits. With enough kills, the yogwe Gumiho appears and repeats an AoE that hurts only bandits; when she leaves, you get a 1-minute damage buff against them. Without this tactic, even recommended stats make it an uphill battle. Beware fox bandits sniping and rare AoE from melee bandits; the Fox Bandit Boss can knock you down.

## Honglim Base (Land of the Morning Light): main loot

Main drops at Honglim Base (Land of the Morning Light): WON/BON Dawn Crystal in the types Evasion, Damage Reduction, Accuracy, Black Spirit's Rage and All AP, plus Faint Origin of Dark Hunger. Dawn Crystal can be transfused into the crystal socket of a primal grade accessory, and heating it produces Essence of Dawn (see the Kharazad Accessories page). Faint Origin of Dark Hunger raises your current Enhancement Chance.

## Dokkebi Forest (Land of the Morning Light): location and recommended AP/DP

Dokkebi Forest in the Land of the Morning Light is a shaded forest ringed by high cliffs where supernatural dokkebi once helped visitors, until the Dokkebi King's rage ended visits. Recommended level is Lv. 61+, with recommended AP 335+, DP 420+ and a 1300 Max AP Limit (only 70% of the exceeded AP is applied). Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5 (the higher gear requirement keeps crowds down), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5 (not far from Dalbeol). Leave your mount outside the statue area. Be buffed with meals, draughts and perfumes, even at recommended stats.

## Dokkebi Forest (Land of the Morning Light): monsters and strategy

Dokkebi Forest (Land of the Morning Light) has mostly Lv. 65 monsters: Strongkkebi and Combatkebi in melee, Philosophkebi at range, and Prankkebi in both. Kkebifires drawn to kkebidol statues summon dokkebi to awaken Duoksini, King of the Dokkebi. Resist the dokkebi and attack the kkebifires to gather enough energy for him to appear. Duoksini does a big AoE that can kill, so be careful, and he can fling you aside. Dying to Duoksini carries no death penalties (no EXP loss, no broken crystals). When his rage fades, the kkebidol absorbs nearby dokkebi energy. Watch the dokkebi's ranged attacks and many AoE abilities. It is harder than Honglim Base, so less competition.

## Dokkebi Forest (Land of the Morning Light): main loot

Main drops at Dokkebi Forest (Land of the Morning Light): WON/BON Dawn Crystal in the types Evasion, Damage Reduction, Accuracy, Black Spirit's Rage and All AP, plus Faint Origin of Dark Hunger. Dawn Crystal can be transfused into a primal grade accessory's crystal socket, and heating it yields Essence of Dawn (see the Kharazad Accessories page). Faint Origin of Dark Hunger increases your current Enhancement Chance.

## Fortune's Golden Pig Cave (Land of the Morning Light): location and recommended AP/DP

Fortune's Golden Pig Cave in the Land of the Morning Light is a private monster zone, not far from Dalbeol, so no other adventurers are present. Recommended level is Lv. 61+, with recommended AP 345+, DP 438+ and a 1350 Max AP Limit (only 70% of the exceeded AP is applied). Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars, hunting satisfaction 2 of 5 (a stay lasts at most 30 minutes and the monsters resist harmful effects), distance from town 3 of 5. Park outside the NPC Munsik, who is also the entrance. Be buffed with meals, draughts and perfumes.

## Fortune's Golden Pig Cave (Land of the Morning Light): monsters and how it opens

Fortune's Golden Pig Cave (Land of the Morning Light) has mostly Lv. 65 monsters, melee Elite Golden Pig and Raider Golden Pig. It works like Marni's Realm: beat enough pigs, or wait a bit, and the boss Golden Pig King spawns; defeating it ends the subjugation and earns a Golden Pigs Subjugation Token. The cave is normally closed and opens randomly within a set timeframe, with a notice at the top of every player's screen. Enter through Munsik. Rare Treasures can be claimed by only one adventurer across all servers while it is open, which then closes it; the owner gets special rewards from Munsik. An always-available version exists if you hand Munsik a Primordial Crystal x1, but there you get no Subjugation Token, death penalties are the same as in regular zones, and loot differs, being limited to Faint Origin of Dark Hunger, Essence of Dawn and WON/BON Dawn Crystal - All AP.

## Fortune's Golden Pig Cave (Land of the Morning Light): main loot

Main drops at Fortune's Golden Pig Cave (Land of the Morning Light): WON/BON Dawn Crystal in the types Evasion, Damage Reduction, Accuracy, Black Spirit's Rage and All AP, Faint Origin of Dark Hunger, the Golden Pig King's Treasure Chest and the Golden Pigs Subjugation Token. The chest may hold an accessory that is TRI (III) or TET (IV). Subjugation Tokens are traded in the Manage Currency UI, in return for Essence of Dawn and Bon Dawn Crystals. Dawn Crystal goes into a primal grade accessory's crystal socket, and Faint Origin of Dark Hunger raises your Enhancement Chance.
