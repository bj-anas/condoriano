# O'dyllita Monster Zones

The grinding spots of O'dyllita (Thornwood Forest, Tunkuta, Olun's Valley, Crypt of Resting Thoughts) with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and loot.

## O'dyllita monster zones: overview and party size

O'dyllita, east of Kamasylvia, has four monster zones: Thornwood Forest, Tunkuta, Olun's Valley and the Crypt of Resting Thoughts. Its main questline opens after the Kamasylvia questline. The quests seldom ask you to defeat the powerful monsters, so a too-hard zone is no problem for the story. The Crypt of Resting Thoughts and Thornwood Forest are built for solo activity. Tunkuta suits a group of two and Olun's Valley a group of three. The crypt prison monsters look like those in the outer forest, but their deeper corruption makes them far stronger.

## Thornwood Forest (O'dyllita): location and recommended AP/DP

Thornwood Forest in O'dyllita, called the forest of salvation or the cursed forest depending on the Ahib faction, is home to Ahib who fell to the corruption. Its ruins are overrun with thorns and vines. It is not far from O'draxxia or the Old Wisdom Tree. Recommended level is Lv. 60+, with recommended AP 230+ and DP 320+. The debuffs are dangerous. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars (similar to Hystria Ruins), user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park at either entrance or at the node manager. Its monsters are the weakest in the whole Arid Land, and it is designed for solo play.

## Thornwood Forest (O'dyllita): monsters and strategy

Thornwood Forest (O'dyllita) has mostly Lv. 61 monsters in packs spread over the whole area, and many inflict debuffs. Melee types are the Ahib Salun Wolf Spearmaiden and Salun Bear Spearmaiden, Ahib Dark Champion and Ahib Condemner, Ahib Dark Chaser, then Ahib Beast Trainer, Bloodthirsty Khalk and Khalk of Darkness. Ahib Dark Mage is ranged, and there are Eye of Wrath, Eye of Despair and Eye of Pain. Debuff the monsters first or use protective skills; large monsters are immune to debuffs. Start in front of the node manager, which is densely populated and has a vendor nearby. After killing the Salun Spearmaidens you may sense ominous energy; near the spring of corruption, waves of weaker frenzied Ahib come, followed by a fallen Dark Knight, with a higher drop chance of Specter's Energy and Ah'krad.

## Thornwood Forest (O'dyllita): main loot

Main drops at Thornwood Forest (O'dyllita): Specter's Energy, Quturan's Left Lung, Quturan's Black Leaf, Ominous Ring, and Marsh's Artifacts for Magic Accuracy, Ranged Accuracy and Melee Accuracy. Ah'krad is also more likely here. Specter's Energy crafts Blackstar Gear, Ah'krad makes the Ah'krad Crystal for Awakening weapons, and Quturan's Left Lung and Black Leaf go into the La Orzeca outfit. The chance of Specter's Energy is higher than in other monster zones.

## Tunkuta (O'dyllita): location and recommended AP/DP

Tunkuta in O'dyllita is the forest of the reclusive Turos, who made a non-aggression pact with the Ahib and have long fought the Salun bears. The Turo Chief Ulutuka is said to appear when two or more intruders are detected. It is close to Starry Midnight Port. Recommended level is Lv. 61+, with recommended AP 250+ and DP 360+. Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars (pretty difficult unless in a party of two), user density 4 of 5 (high demand for Flame of Despair), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. Park at any entrance. Unique drops are Turo's Belt and Flame of Despair. A party of two is recommended.

## Tunkuta (O'dyllita): monsters and strategy

Tunkuta (O'dyllita) has mostly Lv. 64 monsters: Turo Berserker in melee, Turo Pike Warrior and Turo Scout in melee and ranged, plus Turo Totem, Turo Charm and Turo Resting Spot. They come in small packs, widely spaced, with high HP and AP, and may stun you for a quick death, so clear one pack at a time and do not pull many. High DP is recommended because each hit does massive damage. When intruders are detected, elite Turo soldiers turn up, and the commotion brings out Ulutuka, Turo Chief; beat the elites quickly to earn the chance to fight him, and he drops rewards when defeated.

## Tunkuta (O'dyllita): main loot

Main drops at Tunkuta (O'dyllita): Turo's Belt (an accuracy accessory) and Flame of Despair (unique drops), Quturan's Ashen Leaf, Quturan's Right Lung, Embers of Despair, and Marsh's Artifacts for Magic Accuracy, Ranged Accuracy and Melee Accuracy. The page also names Thick Turo Blood, which goes into new elixirs, and Turo Heart, which goes into Corrupt Oil of Immortality. Flame of Despair is needed to craft the Fallen God's Armor (see the Slumbering Origin Gear page), and Quturan's Right Lung is needed for the La Orzeca outfit.

## Olun's Valley (O'dyllita): location and recommended AP/DP

Olun's Valley in O'dyllita was once the strongest spirit land until Turasil, the divine tree, corrupted it; golems built to protect Kamasylvia now act as gatekeepers of the Ahib land. It is relatively close to O'draxxia. Recommended level is Lv. 62+, with recommended AP 290+ and DP 380+. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars (one of the most difficult zones), user density 1 of 5 (many avoid the high AP requirement), hunting satisfaction 2 of 5, distance from town 4 of 5. Park by the node manager. A party of three is recommended.

## Olun's Valley (O'dyllita): monsters and strategy

Olun's Valley (O'dyllita) has mostly Lv. 65 monsters: Olun's Statue, Olun's Heart and Olun's Power Tower, plus the golems Boulder Golem, Rock Golem, Olun's Golem and Indomitable Golem. Monsters are spaced widely because they have extremely high HP and AP. The colossal golems are slow with consistent attack patterns, so you need full mastery of movement and skills and must cooperate with your party. Colossal golems that can withstand three people's attacks can appear anywhere. It is the only place to get Olucas, the material for the Olucas' Crystal that can be infused on Awakening weapons.

## Olun's Valley (O'dyllita): main loot

Main drops at Olun's Valley (O'dyllita): Quturan's Ashen Leaf, Quturan's Right Lung, Rich Merchant's Ring Piece and Kehelle's Artifact - Max HP. Olucas, the material for Olucas' Crystal, drops only here. Quturan's Left Lung and Quturan's Black Leaf are used for the La Orzeca outfit (see the Special Outfits page).

## Crypt of Resting Thoughts (O'dyllita): location and recommended AP/DP

The Crypt of Resting Thoughts in O'dyllita is the most secretive place in the territory: only Ahib permitted by Queen Viorencia may enter, and the Ahib imprisoned underground are stronger than any other. It is in the middle of the territory under a mountain. Recommended level is Lv. 62+, with recommended AP 300+ and DP 400+. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars (the hardest in O'dyllita), user density 1 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. Park outside the cave, since it swarms with monsters. It is designed for solo play.

## Crypt of Resting Thoughts (O'dyllita): monsters and strategy

The Crypt of Resting Thoughts (O'dyllita) has mostly Lv. 65 monsters in tight packs, with high AP and DP, and many inflict crowd control, so build DP and resistance first. The prison is entered through the room where Denulo is in the crypt, or the complex's backdoor in Olun's Valley. The monsters are Abyssal versions of the Ahib types (Salun Wolf and Bear Spearmaiden, Dark Champion, Condemner, Dark Chaser, Beast Trainer, Dark Mage), plus Possessed Baby Rock Gargoyle and Rock Gargoyle, Bloodthirsty Khalk, Khalk of Darkness, and the Eyes of Wrath, Despair and Pain. Ah'krad drops here as in Thornwood Forest.

## Crypt of Resting Thoughts (O'dyllita): main loot

Main drops at the Crypt of Resting Thoughts (O'dyllita): Deboreka Belt, Kehelle's Artifact - Max HP, Forgotten Limbo Seal, Quturan's Ashen Leaf, Quturan's Black Leaf, Quturan's Left Lung and Quturan's Right Lung. Ah'krad, the main material for the Ah'krad Crystal (infused on Awakening weapons), also drops here. Quturan's Left Lung and Black Leaf both drop here, so the zone supplies both items for the complete La Orzeca outfit.
