# Mediah Monster Zones

The grinding spots of Mediah, from Abandoned Iron Mine to Kratuga Ancient Ruins, with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and main loot for each zone.

## Mediah monster zones: overview

Mediah monster zones are the mid-level grinding spots that the main questline opens up around Level 51 ~ 52. Mediah has been lawless since the Three Days of Darkness, so the zones feel very different from Calpheon's, with more EXP and loot, making them a stepping stone to mid-level play. You can visit them in any order, as the main questline guides you around. Most classes also do their Awakening and Succession questlines here, which ask you to kill monsters in several zones. The zones are Abandoned Iron Mine, Wandering Rogue Den, Manes Hideout, Helms Post (with the Abandoned Mine), Elric Shrine, Sausan Garrison, Soldier's Cemetery, Hasrah Ancient Ruins, Shultz Guard and Kratuga Ancient Ruins.

## Abandoned Iron Mine (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Abandoned Iron Mine in Mediah was the territory's main mining node and is now held by barbarians from the south, in a lawless place where only the strong survive. Recommended level is Level 51~53, with recommended AP 70+ and DP 150+ (the stat table lists level 52). Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5 (close to town, but the zone is huge). Parking depends on your starting point. Unique drops are Ridell Earring, Beia's Black Magic Gloves, Manos Craftsman's Clothes and Asula's Crimson Eye Necklace (AP +11, DP +3). Daily quests come from the Black Spirit, plus battle quests from Abun.

## Abandoned Iron Mine (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Abandoned Iron Mine (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 50 ~ 53 monsters at high density, in the largest zone of the territory, so plan where to start. The roads spiral, so route-making is hard. Melee types include the Rhutum, Saunil and Khuruto guards and sheriffs (Rhutum Guard, Rhutum Sheriff, Saunil Guard, Saunil Sheriff, Khuruto Guard, Khuruto Sheriff), plus Red Orc Guard, Red Orc Sentinel, Khuruto Chaser and Khuruto Executor. Ranged are Rhutum Chief Priest, Thunder Drummer and Lightning Trumpeter, plus Tacky Fire Cannon and Tacky Stone Wagon. The violent Imp Work Supervisor and Troll Work Supervisor give varied loot but can pin you between them with debuffs. Corner the melee monsters toward the ranged ones. Asula's Crimson Eye Necklace drops from normal monsters, not the violent ones. With fast movement, try the pit; otherwise settle near the Saunil District node or the Abandoned Iron Mine Entrance node. It is often a player's first Mediah zone.

## Abandoned Iron Mine (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Abandoned Iron Mine (Mediah): Manos Craftsman's Clothes, Beia's Black Magic Gloves, Asula's Crimson Eye Necklace and Ridell Earring (the unique drops), Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon), Forbidden Book, Ancient Magic Crystal - Cobelinus and Ancient Magic Crystal of Abundance - Armor.

## Wandering Rogue Den (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Wandering Rogue Den in Mediah is the camp that the wandering rogues set up on the cliffs of the Junaid River, relatively close to Tarif. Recommended level is Level 51~53, with recommended AP 80+ and DP 160+ (the stat table lists level 52). Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park in front of the Highland Junction Exploration Node Manager. Unique drops are the Necklace of Shultz the Gladiator and Belt of Shultz the Gladiator. Quests are available from Tarif, Highland Junction and Kasula Farm. Learn the geography for future node wars.

## Wandering Rogue Den (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Wandering Rogue Den (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 50 ~ 54 monsters at quite high density: Wandering Rogue, Wandering Rogue Fighter and Blind Pugnose in melee, Wandering Rogue Elementalist at range, plus Wandering Rogue Obsidian Altar and Wandering Rogue Prison, and the violent Wandering Rogue Giant Soldier. The Giant Soldiers and Elementalists hit hard; take out the Elementalists first and attack the high-HP Giant Soldiers from behind. High DP helps against the many ranged attacks, and resistance to stiffness and stun is useful. One spot deep inside the den has high density and is more efficient than starting outside. The Belt and Necklace of Shultz sell for decent silver. Level-ups were once the draw; now high-level players come for Black Spirit's Claw Pieces, but it stays uncrowded.

## Wandering Rogue Den (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Wandering Rogue Den (Mediah): the Necklace of Shultz the Gladiator and the Belt of Shultz the Gladiator (unique drops), Black Spirit's Claw Piece, Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon), Ancient Magic Crystal of Nature - Adamantine and Forbidden Book.

## Manes Hideout (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Manes Hideout in Mediah is the home of the hyena-like Manes, and Tarif is just around the corner, so resting is easy. Recommended level is Level 51~53, with recommended AP 80+ and DP 160+ (the stat table lists level 52). Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5 (you can have it to yourself), hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 5 of 5. Park anywhere. Unique drops are the Necklace of Shultz the Gladiator and the Belt of Shultz the Gladiator. Daily quests come from the Black Spirit, with others at Tarif. Newly arrived players come because EXP efficiency drops after Lv. 52 ~ 53, and it is a fallback when other Mediah zones are crowded.

## Manes Hideout (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Manes Hideout (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 50 ~ 54 monsters, a mix of melee and ranged: Small Mane, Big Mane and Giant Mane in melee, Agile Mane at range, with the violent Giant Mane. Kill Agile Manes first, since they harass at close range. Players with low stats should stay alert for violent Giant Manes. Monsters are spread evenly, so every route is about equally efficient; with recommended gear, aim for the southern part of the river stream, which has many Giant Manes.

## Manes Hideout (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Manes Hideout (Mediah): the Necklace of Shultz the Gladiator and the Belt of Shultz the Gladiator (unique drops), Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Ancient Magic Crystal - Addis, Ancient Magic Crystal - Carmae, Trace of Nature, Forbidden Book and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon).

## Helms Post (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Helms Post in Mediah is the home of the Helm, a barbarian tribe native to Mediah, unfriendly to outsiders and said to have once been dwarves of South Drieghan. Recommended level is Level 51~54, with recommended AP 90+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 52). DP is more efficient than AP here. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5 (repair at the nearby Node Manager). Park at NPC Bolero in front of the Helms Post. Unique drops are Asula's Crimson Eye Ring (AP 7, DP 2), Asula's Crimson Eye Earring (AP 7, Max HP +25), Ancient Guardian's Seal, Scarla Necklace and Mediah Merchants Union Helm.

## Helms Post (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Helms Post (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 50 ~ 59 monsters, mostly human type, at moderate density, with similar efficiency everywhere; the area is large, so movement speed helps. Melee types are Helm Miner, Helm Iron Shield and Helm Two-Axe Warrior, then Helm Hammer and Helm Angry Hammer, Helm Big Mace and Helm Small Mace, and Helm Big Axe. Helm Hunter and Helm Golem are ranged, with Helm Tribe Mine, Forge and Mineral Wagon structures. Most monsters have low DP but high AP, so learn skills that stop their attacks. The violent Helm Golems (Lv. 59) roam everywhere with extremely high HP and AP and cast magic from afar; even recommended stats can die instantly, so run if you cannot win. The northwest is the most popular. You may get a Scarla Necklace for money. It is not crowded unless there is a Combat EXP event.

## Helms Post (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Helms Post (Mediah): Mediah Merchants Union Helm, Scarla Necklace, Ancient Guardian's Seal, Asula's Crimson Eye Earrings and Asula's Crimson Eye Ring (unique drops), Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon), Ancient Magic Crystal - Viper and Forbidden Book.

## Abandoned Mine (Helms Post, Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

The Abandoned Mine in Mediah lies inside Helms Post, a former Altinova Merchants Guild mine abandoned after the Helms took over, where odd dwarves infused with power dwell. Recommended level is Level 54~56. The wiki gives no separate AP and DP; its Helms are infused with ancient powers, so be above the recommended stats of the Helms Post (AP 90+, DP 180+). Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 3 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5 (no towns nearby). Park at NPC Bolero in front of the Helms Post. Its unique drops are the same as Helms Post.

## Abandoned Mine (Helms Post, Mediah): monsters and strategy

The Abandoned Mine (Helms Post, Mediah) is a dungeon-like spiral reached from inside the Helms Post, with fairly high monster density and a mix of Helms Post and mine monsters; most are human type, Lv. 50 ~ 59. Melee types are Helm Devourer, Helm Crusher, Helm Destroyer and Helm Raid Captain; Helm Hunter and Helm Devourer are ranged, and the violent Helm Golem roams. Helm Devourers and Crushers are strong and immune to knockdown, bound and floating, so use stun or stiffness skills. Always be careful of the many Helm Golems. EXP efficiency is low, but a steady flow of players come for the Blackstar and Fallen God's Armor quests; they rarely stay, so you can have it to yourself. Loot is the same as Helms Post.

## Elric Shrine (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Elric Shrine in Mediah is a temple on the eastern hills of the Rumbling Land, taken over by followers of an evil god. Recommended level is Level 52~55, with recommended AP 95+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 53~54). Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5 (mainly a leveling zone), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5 (Tarif is the closest town with storage). Park at the stairs up to the Elric Shrine. Unique drops are the Outlaw's Ring (AP +3, DP +4), Asula's Crimson Eye Belt (AP +6, weight +100LT) and Derek's Light Leather Shoes.

## Elric Shrine (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Elric Shrine (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 51 ~ 54 monsters, evenly spread inside the shrine at the top and around the Canyon of Corruption below: Elric Layperson, Elric Follower and Elric Cultist in melee, Elric Priest and Elric High Priest at range, plus Rotten Sand Tower, Rotten Swamp, Ritual Tool of Corruption, Elric Shamanic Tree and Decayed Swamp Monster. The monsters have high DP and HP, so they need more AP than other zones. Kill the Elric High Priests and Decayed Swamp Monsters first because of their ranged attacks. The Canyon of Corruption is denser and more popular than the shrine node. Combat EXP is decent for levels 53 to 55, not far behind Sausan Garrison. Players overflow here from Sausan Garrison, so if many horses are parked at the stairs, go elsewhere.

## Elric Shrine (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Elric Shrine (Mediah): the Outlaw's Ring, Asula's Crimson Eye Belt and Derek's Light Leather Shoes (unique drops), Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon), Ancient Magic Crystal - Harphia, Forbidden Book and Trace of Nature.

## Sausan Garrison (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Sausan Garrison in Mediah is a hilltop fort overlooking northwestern Mediah and the sea, built by the Mediah royal family against pirates and now held by the Sausans, a native barbarian tribe. Recommended level is Lv. 53~55, with recommended AP 100+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 54). Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 5 of 5 (crowded everywhere), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5 (repair at Sarma Outpost; Kusha is a nearby town). There are no unique drops. Parking depends on the route. At the recommended stats it gives the top EXP for Lv. 54 to Lv. 55, and it is tagged #Lots of Monsters in the Monster Zone Info window.

## Sausan Garrison (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Sausan Garrison (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 54 ~ 57 human-type monsters: Sausan Assassin, Sausan Soldier, Sausan Guardian, Sausan Watch, Tough Sausan Soldier and Sausan Scout in melee, Sausan Sniper and Sausan Cannoneer at range, plus Sausan Tent, Sausan Supply Chest and Sausan Golden Wagon. The zone is large and dense, good for long stays, and daily quests at Sarma Outpost give Contribution EXP. At levels 54 to 55 it gets crowded, even to the point of too few monsters, especially at night and in the first days of a season. Corner the melee monsters toward the Snipers and Cannoneers to kill them together. Food such as Knight Combat Rations and elixirs such as Elixir of Human Hunt add damage against humans. Going inside along the cliff route gives higher density than the outskirts. If crowds bother you, try Elric Shrine or Helms Post.

## Sausan Garrison (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Sausan Garrison (Mediah): Trace of Nature, Forbidden Book, Grunil Defense Gear Box, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon) and Magic Crystal of Infinity Box. There is no unique item drop, and you may need to return to town soon without a wagon because many items drop.

## Soldier's Cemetery (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Soldier's Cemetery in Mediah is a cemetery in the southern mountain range, once the resting place of war heroes, where skeletons rose after suspicious sorcerers appeared. It is relatively close to Heidel. Recommended level is Lv. 54~55, with recommended AP 100+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 55). Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 3 of 5. Park in front of the Exploration Node Manager NPC, where daily quests are available. The unique drop is the Mesto Earring. The zone is small with uneven routes, so be careful.

## Soldier's Cemetery (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Soldier's Cemetery (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 54 ~ 57 monsters at high density, appearing as you approach: Frenzied Skeleton, Frenzied Skeleton Axeman, Grudged Skeleton and Frenzied Skeleton Watchdog in melee, Frenzied Skeleton Rifleman and Frenzied Black Sorcerer at range, Grudged Sword, and the violent Frenzied Skeleton Decurion and Centurion. Kill Riflemen first, as their ranged attacks, like the Black Sorcerers', are dangerous at low DP. Melee monsters approach invincible and attack from any side. Monsters are well dispersed, so no spot is better. It is a great place for Skill EXP after Lv. 54, but harder than Sausan Garrison or Elric Shrine, so better when above the recommended stats. The Mesto Earring earns money, so come when those zones are crowded.

## Soldier's Cemetery (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Soldier's Cemetery (Mediah): Mesto Earring (unique drop), Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Trace of Nature, Forbidden Book, Ancient Magic Crystal of Nature - Adamantine, Ancient Magic Crystal - Hystria and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). The Mesto Earring drops from time to time and sells for good money, which is why players come when Sausan Garrison or Elric Shrine is crowded.

## Hasrah Ancient Ruins (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Hasrah Ancient Ruins in Mediah are reached through a cave in Hasrah's Cliff and are full of living ancient weapons guarding buried relics. Tarif is the closest town. Recommended level is Lv. 54~56, with recommended AP 110+ and DP 180+ (the stat table lists level 55). Going above the recommended stats helps earn more silver. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, user density 2 of 5 (players doing Awakening and Succession quests), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. Park at the entrance or near the cave's entrance. The unique drop is the Ancient Weapon Core.

## Hasrah Ancient Ruins (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Hasrah Ancient Ruins (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 55 monsters in a dungeon of giant rooms linked by narrow corridors, which players call rooms 1, 2 and 3. Kamol, Balten and Ruins Stoneback Crab are melee and ranged; Kukuri and Kamel are melee; Buruko is ranged; and the Ancient Ruins Guard Tower stands in rooms. Reach the recommended stats first because many ranged monsters hit hard. Kill Kamol, Balten, Buruko and the towers first. Balten give the most loot, so seek areas with many. Settle in one of the giant rooms. Combat EXP is not great, but silver is good. Many players come for class Awakening and Succession quests, and the Ancient Weapon Core is a good source of gold, silver and sharp black crystal shards. It is the entrance to Kratuga Ancient Ruins.

## Hasrah Ancient Ruins (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Hasrah Ancient Ruins (Mediah): Ancient Weapon Core (unique drop), Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon), Ancient Magic Crystal of Abundance - Armor and Forbidden Book. The Ancient Weapon Core is a good source of gold, silver and sharp black crystal shards, and Balten give the most loot in this zone.

## Shultz Guard (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Shultz Guard in Mediah lies east of the main Sausan Garrison structures, where followers of the Sausan hero Shultz returned after seeking a power to revive the dead overseas. Recommended level is Lv. 56~58, with recommended AP 210+ and DP 320+ (the stat table lists level 58). Ratings: difficulty 4 of 5 stars (one of the hardest in Mediah), user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 5 of 5 (Sarma Outpost is nearby). It is a group area best for parties of 3, tagged #Lots of Monsters, with the Agris Fever trait (uses fewer Agris Fever points). Unique drops are the Necklace of Shultz the Gladiator and the Belt of Shultz the Gladiator. Find a quiet parking spot. A weekly quest from Sarma Outpost gives Contribution EXP.

## Shultz Guard (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Shultz Guard (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 58 human-type monsters packed into a small area: Shultz Guard Gladiator and Shultz Guard Armored Soldier in melee, and Shultz Guard Sniper at range. They are stronger than Sausan Garrison and hit harder, but give more Combat EXP and silver. Because you cannot move far, kill the Sniper that shoots from behind to avoid pointless HP loss. Knight Combat Rations and Elixir of Human Hunt raise efficiency against humans. The zone is a go-board layout; density is highest by the many red tents, and if crowded move along the coastline. Parties are present but fewer than in other party zones.

## Shultz Guard (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Shultz Guard (Mediah): the Necklace of Shultz the Gladiator and Belt of Shultz the Gladiator (unique drops), Specter's Energy, Marsh's Artifact - Melee AP, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Damage Reduction, Lesha's Artifact - Melee Evasion, Magic Crystal of Infinity Box, Forbidden Book, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon), Ancient Spirit Dust, Grunil Defense Gear Box, Caphras Stone and Mass of Pure Energy. Caphras Stones may be the zone's true value.

## Kratuga Ancient Ruins (Mediah): location and recommended AP/DP

Kratuga Ancient Ruins in Mediah is a hidden wing behind a sealed door deep in the Hasrah Ancient Ruins, opened with a Black Spirit's Claw Piece, or alternatively an Unsealed Ruins Slate. The slate comes from combining Sealed Ruins Slate x3, dropped in the zone. Recommended level is Lv. 61, with recommended AP 230+ and DP 300+. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars (one of the hardest in Mediah), user density 3 of 5, hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5 (Tarif is nearest). Park at the entrance of the Hasrah Ancient Ruins. Unique drops are the Tungrad Earring and Laytenn's Power Stone. Its monsters are stronger than Hasrah's, so take great care. It has the #Agris Fever trait.

## Kratuga Ancient Ruins (Mediah): monsters and strategy

Kratuga Ancient Ruins (Mediah) has mostly Lv. 60 ~ 65 monsters: Kimel, Elqueesh, Belloten, Bamole and Laytenn in melee, Puruko at range. Puruko applies a -10 AP debuff, Belloten can knock you down and Elqueesh can cause stiffness. Melee attacks are powerful but slow, so avoid them. The ruins are large, with big rooms of ancient weapons and, after the first corridor, a spiral room with interconnected tunnels, with no best room or route. If the security system threatens to overrun you, run.

## Kratuga Ancient Ruins (Mediah): security alert system and Laytenn

Kratuga Ancient Ruins (Mediah) has a 3-level security alert system that may trigger while you fight ancient weapons; stronger monsters appear at higher levels. Level 1 spawns monsters at new locations; defeat all in time to reach level 2. Level 2 brings larger Elqueesh and Belloten; defeat all in time to reach the maximum level. At maximum, the Ancient Extermination Weapon Laytenn appears, stronger than the desert night Laytenn, with a higher chance to drop Laytenn's Power Stone.

## Kratuga Ancient Ruins (Mediah): main loot

Main drops at Kratuga Ancient Ruins (Mediah): Tungrad Earring and Laytenn's Power Stone (unique drops), all Combat Artifacts, Elkarr, Yona's Fragment, Sealed Black Magic Crystal, Sealed Ruins Slate, Traveler's Map, Forbidden Book, Black Stone (Armor & Weapon), Ancient Spirit Dust, Caphras Stone and Mass of Pure Energy. Elkarr can be turned into Crystal of Elkarr via simple alchemy (hotkey: L), giving All Accuracy +12 and Ignore All Resistance +10%, and it is highly sought after. Three Sealed Ruins Slates combine into an Unsealed Ruins Slate, which opens the ruins without a Black Spirit's Claw Piece.
