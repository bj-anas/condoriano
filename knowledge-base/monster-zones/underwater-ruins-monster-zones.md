# Underwater Ruins Monster Zones

The offshore grinding spots of the underwater ruins: Protty Cave and the upper and lower (abyssal) zones of Sycraia Ruins, with recommended level, AP, DP, monsters, strategy and loot.

## Underwater Ruins monster zones: overview and access

The underwater ruins monster zones are Protty Cave, Sycraia Ruins (Upper Zone) and Sycraia Ruins (Lower Zone). A ship makes them much easier to reach but is not strictly required, since you can ride on another adventurer's ship. The zones differ greatly in difficulty: Protty Cave is far easier than Sycraia Ruins, and the upper zone is far easier than the abyssal lower zone. Adventurers who just moved to Succession or Awakening should start with Protty Cave, while veterans at Lv. 58 and above with good gear can try Sycraia Ruins.

## Underwater Ruins monster zones: recommended AP and DP

Recommended level, AP and DP for every underwater ruins monster zone.

| Zone | Level | AP | DP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protty Cave | 57 (recommended level Lv. 56~57) | 170+ | 200+ |
| Sycraia Ruins (Upper Zone) | 62+ | 300+ | 400+ |
| Sycraia Ruins (Lower Zone) | 62+ | 395+ | 460+ |

The upper zone is also listed by total stats: 1,100 AP and 440 DP, with an AP limit of 1,130.

## Protty Cave (Underwater Ruins): location and recommended AP/DP

Protty Cave in the underwater ruins is where the amphibious Protty gather, on Weita Island, an island just off the shore of Velia and north of it. You need a boat to get there and to return; a rowboat is enough for the round trip. Recommended level is Lv. 56~57, with recommended AP 170+ and DP 200+. It is the easiest monster zone at sea. Ratings: difficulty 2 of 5 stars, user density 3 of 5 (few come because it is far), hunting satisfaction 4 of 5, distance from town 2 of 5. There is no mount parking. The unique drop is the blue grade Rainbow Coral Ring.

## Protty Cave (Underwater Ruins): monsters and Sid Protty evolution

Protty Cave (Underwater Ruins) has mostly Lv. 59 ~ 61 monsters in tight groups, so they are easy to kill: Sid Protty, Protty, Kaz Protty and Zera Protty in melee, plus Black Crystal Seaweed, Ocean Crystal Seaweed and Protty Ootheca. It is easy to get lost inside. When a Protty Ootheca's HP drops low enough, a Sid Protty may appear, and once hit it hides among nearby monsters and disappears if left alone. Keep chasing it without killing it, because it can evolve before dying. The stages are Parasitic, Gnawing and Satiating. Parasitic becomes the Hungry Protty or a Gnawing Sid Protty; Gnawing becomes the Violent Zera Protty or a Satiating Sid Protty; Satiating becomes the Overlord Kaz Protty. Killing the evolved Protty, Zera Protty and Kaz Protty gives more rewards.

## Protty Cave (Underwater Ruins): main loot

Main drops at Protty Cave (Underwater Ruins): the Rainbow Coral Ring (unique drop); Lesha's Artifact - Ranged Evasion and - Ranged Damage Reduction; Marsh's Artifact - Ranged AP; Atanis' Element; Ancient Seal - Red Shard; Fragment of the Deep Sea; Abyssal Essence; and the common materials Trace of Nature, Ancient Spirit Dust, Mass of Pure Magic, Caphras Stone and Black Stone (Armor & Weapon). Ancient Seal - Red Shard combined with Ancient Seal - Black Shard gives a Tungrad Ring. Five Fragments of the Deep Sea combine into the key for Sycraia Underwater Ruins (Abyssal). Specter's Energy has an increased chance. Abyssal Essence is used to craft Frenzy Draught.

## Sycraia Ruins Upper Zone (Underwater Ruins): location and recommended AP/DP

Sycraia Ruins (Upper Zone) in the underwater ruins holds damaged ancient weapons that once defended the place against Protty Cave and outsiders. Recommended level is 62+, with recommended AP 300+ and DP 400+ by displayed stats; by total stats it is 1,100 AP and 440 DP, with an AP limit of 1,130. Ratings: difficulty 3 of 5 stars, hunting satisfaction 5 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5 (really far from shore). Park your boat above the ruins. The unique drop is the Deboreka Ring. To find the entrance, go to the spot on the World Map (hotkey: M) and dive; there are four entrances, North, South, East and West, and any other way in is blocked by a force field. Press F while floating above the water to recover stamina. You cannot bring a horse, so manage items carefully.

## Sycraia Ruins Upper Zone (Underwater Ruins): monsters and strategy

Sycraia Ruins (Upper Zone) in the underwater ruins has mostly Lv. 60 ~ 62 melee monsters: Damaged Kureba, Damaged Lykin and Damaged Pirash. Huge numbers of damaged pirash fill the zone, and hitting one pulls the whole nearby group. Damaged lykins and kurebas sometimes use powerful knockdown attacks, but they telegraph them, so dodge. Monsters are clumped together, so you can fight them en masse. In full boss gear with decent accessories it is easy, and because it is offshore it is rarely crowded. The zone is wide and does not show your position on the world map, so learn landmarks and build your own route. Prepare well, because you are far from shore.

## Sycraia Ruins Upper Zone (Underwater Ruins): main loot

Main drops at Sycraia Ruins (Upper Zone) in the underwater ruins: Deboreka Ring (unique drop), Tungrad Ring, Rich Merchant's Ring Piece, Sycrid's Song, Marni's Research Box, Forgotten Limbo Box, Abyssal Essence and Sealed Black Magic Crystal. The chance of Specter's Energy is increased here.

## Sycraia Ruins Lower Zone (Underwater Ruins): location and recommended AP/DP

Sycraia Ruins (Lower Zone), the abyssal zone of the underwater ruins, is a #HighestTier Monster Zone and one of the hardest in the game, for well-prepared adventurers with the best gear. Recommended level is Lv. 62+, with recommended AP 395+ and DP 460+. Ratings: difficulty 5 of 5 stars, user density 4 of 5, hunting satisfaction 5 of 5, distance from town 1 of 5. Leave your boat above the upper zone ruins. To enter, use the structure inside the upper zone with Memories of the Deep Sea, made by placing 5 Fragments of the Deep Sea in a plus shape in your inventory and combining them; the fragments drop in the upper zone and Protty Cave and may be on the Central Market. The unique drop is the Sycraia Shard.

## Sycraia Ruins Lower Zone (Underwater Ruins): monsters and strategy

Sycraia Ruins (Lower Zone) in the underwater ruins has mostly Lv. 65 ~ 66 monsters: the fully operational ancient weapons Elmermol, Kureba, Lykin and Pirash in melee, plus the support types Tower of Restoration, Eyes of the Deep Sea and Force Field Defender. Kill the defenders, eyes and towers first. The zone is large, with separate caves linked by water, so there is room for your own route, and it does not show your position on the world map. Staying alert for a long time is hard, and monsters get stronger at night. A party is recommended, though you can go solo once familiar. Skill and concentration matter more than raw stats, and expensive loot draws top-tier players.

## Sycraia Ruins Lower Zone (Underwater Ruins): main loot

Main drops at Sycraia Ruins (Lower Zone) in the underwater ruins: the Sycraia Shard (unique drop); Rich Merchant's Ring Piece; Deboreka Ring and Tungrad Ring (each up to III); and exclusive loot of #HighestTier Monster Zones, namely Primordial Pigments, Corrupt Oil of Immortality, Refined Origin of Hunger and Refined Essence of Devouring.
