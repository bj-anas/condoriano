# Outfit Additional Details

Extra notices for Pearl Shop (F3) outfits, outfit by outfit: how each outfit changes what weapons, accessories, gloves and shoes look like for each class, plus limits such as Central Market and dyeing.

## Pearl Shop outfits: availability and general rules

The outfit additional details cover the outfits sold in the Pearl Shop (F3). Some of these outfits can be bought only for a limited period, so they are not always on sale, and the information is updated when something changes. Only the class an outfit box is designated for can open and equip it. An outfit's details can also be read in its item description.

## Lovely Bloom outfit notices

The Lovely Bloom outfit gives a unique idle animation and a unique social action. Weapons are hidden out of combat, except for Archer. Female classes: the helmet may hide accessories (turn on Hide Head Gear to show them), the armor hides underwear and swimwear top, and wearing it with certain gloves changes the outfit's look. Male classes: wearing it with certain shoes changes the look. Archer and Sorceress: the awakening weapon is hidden out of combat. Sorceress: main and sub-weapons are hidden. Dark Knight, Lahn and Nova: equipping the awakening weapon changes the look. Striker and Mystic: weapons are hidden.

## Oblivion, Noel, Jolly Winter Dream and Crayodel Soldier outfit notices

Oblivion outfit: the helmet may hide accessories (turn on Hide Head Gear to show them), and certain parts of the Oblivion outfit cannot be dyed. Noel outfit: Mystic has the gauntlet, vambrace and cestus hidden; Striker has the gauntlet, vambrace and gardbrace hidden. Jolly Winter Dream outfit: Striker and Mystic have their weapons hidden. Crayodel Soldier outfit: Striker and Mystic have their weapons hidden.

## Grandpa Cron's Helper outfit notices

The Grandpa Cron's Helper outfit gives a unique idle animation and cannot be registered on the Central Market. Its helmet may hide accessories, except for Shai (turn on Hide Head Gear to show them). Nova, Lahn and Dark Knight: equipping the awakening weapon changes the armor's look. Mystic and Shai: weapons are hidden.
