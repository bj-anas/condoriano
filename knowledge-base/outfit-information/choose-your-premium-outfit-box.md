# Choose Your Premium Outfit Box

The outfits a player can pick from the Choose Your Premium Outfit Box, listed class by class.

## Choose Your Premium Outfit Box: overview

The Choose Your Premium Outfit Box offers a different list of outfits for each class. The wiki lists 30 classes: Warrior, Ranger, Sorceress, Berserker, Tamer, Musa, Maehwa, Valkyrie, Kunoichi, Ninja, Wizard, Witch, Dark Knight, Striker, Mystic, Lahn, Archer, Guardian, Hashashin, Nova, Sage, Corsair, Drakania, Woosa, Maegu, Scholar, Dosa, Deadeye, Wukong and Seraph. Kibelius Divinus is in the list of every one of those classes, and Kibelius is in every list except the Warrior's, where the page shows Kibelius Divinus twice.

## Choose Your Premium Outfit Box: Warrior outfits

The Warrior can choose from these outfits in the Choose Your Premium Outfit Box: Gavi Regan, Brut Lancelot, Doomsday, Crown Eagle, Grotevant, Kairos, Trailblazer, Ossa Cerberi and Kibelius Divinus. The page lists Kibelius Divinus twice for the Warrior and does not show Kibelius for this class.
