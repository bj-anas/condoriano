# Atoraxxion: Yolunakea

Guide to Atoraxxion: Yolunakea, the third biome of the Atoraxxion dungeon: entry, its six areas and key items, the final boss and the weekly rewards.

## Atoraxxion: Yolunakea overview

Atoraxxion: Yolunakea is a 5-player co-op dungeon and the third biome you can visit in Atoraxxion. Recommended gear is 280 AP and 340 DP, and optimal gear is 289 AP and 380 DP. It has areas called Yolu's Nail, Yolu's Query, Yolu's Spine, Yolu's Mane, Yolu's Teeth and Yolu's Cradle, with Yolu's Garden as a preparation stop before the Cradle. The final boss is Impassioned Amarillos. You see other players' groups inside, but the final boss is in a private instance for your party. Normal monsters drop materials for keys, not regular loot. You need the items from each area's boss to move on.

## Atoraxxion: Yolunakea entry requirements

Atoraxxion: Yolunakea is available from Lv. 60. Completing [Invitation from I] in the Black Spirit's Main Quest tab and your Awakening or Succession quest are optional. A character that completed [Atoraxxion] Vaha's Time Capsule can enter from above Lv. 57.

Interact with the Sol Magia in the Ancient Stone Chamber and choose Yolunakea on the Atoraxxion selection screen. You can also enter through Elribta's Door, which appears after clearing Atoraxxion: Sycrakea.

- Normal Entry: 5 party members, normal weekly rewards.
- Story Entry: for the main story or knowledge hunting, with no weekly rewards except for the first clear.

Leave the dungeon through the Sol Magia inside.

## Atoraxxion: Yolunakea progression and keys

1. Yolu's Nail: defeat the Ancient Weapons Veinturam, Oreturem, Syketurem and Vayeturem to get Pulsating Tree Vine, which is needed to meet the next boss. A detection device is active here, and Veinturam explodes once it reaches a certain power threshold.
2. Yolu's Query: defeat Keluos, the defensive protocol, for Yolu's Arid Fragment. Place it horizontally next to the Pulsating Tree Vine in your Inventory, in any order, to make Distorted Tear of Temptation. Activating the Sun Control Tower creates a blue aura that moves you to Yolu's Spine.
3. Yolu's Spine: with the Distorted Tear of Temptation, fight the area boss Lekra Vuhuma. It drops Yolu's Burning Vine, which you combine with the Tear to make Soaring Hatred Tear.
4. Yolu's Mane: after the alert system is on, defeat 30 Veinturam to move forward.
5. Yolu's Teeth: with the Soaring Hatred Tear, fight Sebekaruk. It drops Yolu's Contorted Lightstone. Combine it with the Tear, in any order, to make a Voidlight Dagger. Use the Sol Magia to reach Yolu's Garden.
6. Yolu's Garden: the last place to prepare and use the Campsite Shop. All members insert their Voidlight Daggers into the Sol Magia to enter Yolu's Cradle.

## Atoraxxion: Yolunakea boss mechanics

- Lekra Vuhuma also drops one of five Vaha's Power Stones (Monoceros, Lacerta, Cetus, Creomar or Krahtenn). Exchange the stone at the right moment for a Kyvelan Mission Statement. It lets you move to the next stage or ride that Vaha weapon to help in the fight.
- Impassioned Amarillos in Yolu's Cradle changes form using the energy of the Origin of Life and gets stronger as the fight goes on.
- Yolu's Threads are puzzles found all over Yolunakea. They are defensive devices built by Yolu. Unraveling them shows hidden stories or moves the main quests forward. Skipping a puzzle costs you the knowledge from it.
- Defeating Impassioned Amarillos also gives the Lucretia Dagger key for Vahmalkea's final boss, Apex Urukios.

## Atoraxxion: Yolunakea first-clear challenge

Clearing Atoraxxion: Yolunakea for the first time gives a one-time reward per Family. After beating the final boss Impassioned Amarillos you reach Yolu's Paradise. For the quest [Atoraxxion] Yolu's Time Capsule, examine Yolu's Time Capsule there. Rewards: 1,000 Contribution EXP, Fine Magic Crystal Box and Enchanted Scroll (+100).
