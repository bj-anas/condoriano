# Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party)

Guide to Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party), the 5-player boss content of the Land of the Morning Light, covering entry, party rules, attempts, difficulty, rewards, guild rewards and the weekly quest.

## Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party) overview

The Black Shrine is content where you fight the boss monsters that rule the Land of the Morning Light (LoML). The party version is Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party), and the solo version is Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo). As you progress the main questlines in the Land of the Morning Light, the bosses tied to each story unlock for the Black Shrine. The party version currently has these bosses: Deposed Crown Prince, Dark Bonghwang, Uturi, Bulgasal, Blue-clad Youth and Jigwi. The proof exchange list also includes Bihyung.

## Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party) entry requirements

Open the main menu (Esc), then Adventure (F5), and choose Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party). Unlike Donghae, Hwanghae needs a party of five Adventurers, made from friends or guild members. The party leader starts the encounter, and the party's ranking is recorded, so there are ranking rewards like the solo version. Buffs on your character before entering may be removed when you enter.

## Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party) equipment and attributes

Hwanghae uses your character's full equipment stats and does not use the light orbs of the solo Black Shrine. Each participant picks one attribute: sun, moon or earth.

- Sun: helps kill the boss as fast as possible, so it suits ranking.
- Moon: the more moon attributes in the party, the better your survival.
- Earth: the more earth attributes in the party, the more retries you get after failing to kill the boss.

You cannot use potions or other items in the fights. Use HP and skill resource recovery skills carefully, and look for orbs in the arena that help.

## Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party) weekly attempts

Each Family has 5 weekly attempts at the Hwanghae bosses, resetting at midnight on Sunday.

- Beating a boss at a difficulty (normal or hard) uses up an attempt. Beating the same boss again at the same difficulty with the same character and party costs no attempt.
- Losing or forfeiting does not use an attempt.
- After all 5 attempts, you can still challenge difficulties you cleared, and your best record counts for the party ranking.
- Ranking updates daily at 00:00 server time.
- The no-cost rule only applies to the same character and same party that beat the boss. A different character or party spends an attempt.

## Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party) difficulty

There are two difficulties: Normal, with AP set to 300, and Hard, with AP set to 330. The wiki says the encounters are not much harder than Atoraxxion (Elvia), but you must learn each boss's patterns. Your stats here are not changed by the light orbs from Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo). You use 100% of your equipment's stats.

## Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party) guild rewards: Hard difficulty

If five members of the same guild form a party and beat a boss, all guild members get extra rewards by recorded ranking for that difficulty. They are paid every Sunday around 00:00 to 00:10 server time, like Black Shrine (Solo). On Hard, ranks 1 to 50 receive Spoils of Conquest: 1st place gets the 1st rank, 2nd the 2nd, 3rd the 3rd, 4th-10th the 4th, 11th-25th the 5th, 26th-50th the 6th.

## Black Shrine: Hwanghae (Party) guild rewards: Normal difficulty and weekly quest

On Normal, guilds ranked 1st to 50th also receive the Boss Blitz Reward (Rank 9): Resplendent Lightstone of Fire x1, Memory Fragment x2, Caphras Stone x2, Black Stone x5 and Sangpyeong Coin x10, plus a set-probability chance of Bonghwang's Fragment, Haetae's Fragment or Girin's Fragment.

Weekly quest Ember of the Primordial: accept "Great Darkness Covering the Black Shrine" from the Black Spirit (hotkey ,). Completing it requires defeating a boss on Hard difficulty and gives Ember of the Primordial x1. It can be done once a week per Family.
