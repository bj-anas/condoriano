# Bosses

Overview of the boss encounter types in Black Desert: main quest bosses, world bosses, field bosses, guild bosses, Dark Rifts, Faded Ancient Relic summon scrolls and Rift Echoes.

## Bosses: what a boss is

A boss is a monster that stands above others in status or power. Some lead a tribe or group, some gained huge power by delving into the mysteries of the black stone, and some, like dragons, are just naturally powerful. Beating a boss gives a reward that matches the effort. Some drop enhancement materials like Black Stones, and others drop unique items such as boss equipment pieces.

## Bosses: main quest bosses

During the main quest you meet bosses while helping settlements or pursuing an objective. Many bosses seen later in other content first appear in the main quest, which explains who they are. These fights are tuned to the gear you can naturally get as you progress. Red Nose, the Imp Boss, is the first boss in the main quest.

## Bosses: world bosses

World bosses threaten an entire territory, such as Kzarka, the Lord of Corruption, and Garmoth, the Crimson Dragon. They appear at fixed locations at specific times, and a system message warns you 15 minutes beforehand. HP is shared across all servers in a region, and many Adventurers are needed. A world boss disappears if not defeated within 30 minutes of appearing.

Exceptions: Quint and Muraka disappear 15 minutes after appearing, and Vell's warning comes 30 minutes in advance because of the long travel distance. A boss may show an empowered form, for example Nightmarish Kzarka in place of Kzarka. Loot includes enhancement materials, magic crystals and unique boss equipment such as a Kzarka main weapon or Garmoth's Heart, used to make a powerful alchemy stone. Kzarka was the first world boss.

## Bosses: field bosses

Field bosses are regional threats, weaker than world bosses. Black Shadow (the young dragon) and Red Nose (the Imp Boss) are examples. They appear at fixed locations but not at scheduled times. They show up on random servers, on average once a day, so one may appear on Serendia 1 but not on Calpheon 2. Black Shadow is the exception: its first spawn location and time are fixed and it appears once per week. Loot includes enhancement materials, magic crystals, enchanted scrolls and sometimes unique boss gear such as Bheg's Gloves and Dim Tree Spirit's Armor.

## Bosses: guild bosses

Guild bosses are fought with fellow guild members: Khan, Ancient Puturum, Ferrid and the Giant Mudster. The guild master, advisor or officers summon them at fixed locations using summon scrolls, crafted from scroll pieces earned through guild boss subjugation missions. A guild boss disappears 30 minutes after being summoned. Khan stays the full 30 minutes whether attacked or not, while the others vanish if idle for 2 minutes. They cannot be attacked while a node or conquest war is running on your server. Loot includes enhancement materials, lightstones and Marni's Unstable Fuel, with major differences only for Khan. Track them in the Guild Boss Subjugation tab of the Guild window.

## Bosses: Dark Rifts

At Lv. 56, dark rifts open across the world. They are individual to each Adventurer, so you fight the boss alone, and no one else's boss appears. There is no penalty for falling, and the boss keeps the HP you already took off if you try again. After a boss is defeated its rift disappears and returns later after a variable amount of time. The main loot is the Box of the Dead, along with Remnants of the Rift and materials for the PEN (V) Boss Gear quest chain. A boss with no Box of the Dead gives lots of Ancient Spirit Dust instead, which is needed to synthesize Caphras Stones.

## Bosses: Faded Ancient Relic summon scrolls

Certain items combine into summon scrolls. For example, x5 Ancient Relic Crystal Shard makes an Ancient Relic Crystal Summon Scroll, and x5 Venomous Night Fang makes an Ibedor's Scroll. Pieces drop from monsters in certain areas or while fishing. Use the scrolls near a Faded Ancient Relic device to call bosses and minions. The device pools several scrolls at once, so monsters get stronger and rewards better.

Four scrolls work with it: Pila Fe Scroll, Ibedor's Scroll, Manshaum Narc's Stone and Ancient Relic Crystal Summon Scroll. Death carries no penalty. Party members can add their own scrolls to raise difficulty and rewards up to 5 stages.

## Bosses: Rift Echoes

Rift Echoes are for obtaining season enhancement materials and only exist on season servers. A Rift's Echo can be used from Lv. 50 and opens a rift that calls monsters and bosses such as Red Nose, Dastard Bheg, Saunil Siege Captain and Kavali. There is no penalty for being defeated, but a party is strongly recommended, and only the party leader can use the item. The boss disappears after 2 minutes idle or 30 minutes after being summoned. You can win Tuvala Ore, Tuvala accessories, Refined Magical Black Stones and Time-filled Black Stones.
