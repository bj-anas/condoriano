# Dehkia's Lantern

Guide to Dehkia's Lantern, an artifact that awakens stronger monsters in certain monster zones: how to get it, use it and recharge it, plus the supported zones, Tier 2 and loot.

## Dehkia's Lantern: what it does

Dehkia's Lantern is an artifact that Lafi Bedmountain, a member of the Secret Guards, discovered while exploring Atoraxxion, the ancient stronghold. He guessed it was an enhancement device for ancient weapons made by Atoraxxion's founder, Dehkia. Later investigation suggests that may not be true. Using it in certain monster zones awakens all nearby affected monsters into a chaotic state: they get stronger and keep reappearing right after being killed, with no waiting time. Awakened monsters disappear once the lantern's durability runs out.

## Dehkia's Lantern: how to obtain it

Use a character at Lv. 61 or above. Go to Olun's Valley in O'dyllita and talk to the NPC Arethel. Finish two family quests: first [Dehkia's Lantern] Atoraxxion Artifact, then [Dehkia's Lantern] Dehkia's Light. The Combat quest type must be active to be offered the questline. Toggle quest types at the bottom of the Quest window (hotkey O).

## Dehkia's Lantern: how to use it

Right-click Dehkia's Lantern in your inventory (hotkey I). When used, you become temporarily invincible against monsters and awaken monsters in the set area. Close its dedicated UI to stop it. There is a 1-minute cooldown after deactivating. The lantern cannot be used on servers with a Node/Conquest War in progress, and it is disabled automatically when one starts.

It has 300 durability and uses 1 durability per minute. Using it on a zone's activation objects also costs 1 durability. Recharge it with Dehkia's Light, 1 durability per light, using the Recharge Dehkia's Lantern button at the bottom of your inventory.

Dehkia's Light comes from Heating (hotkey L) certain PRI to TRI yellow-grade accessories with Magical Lightstone Crystal x10 each. The amount depends on the accessory and its enhancement level.

## Dehkia's Lantern: recrafting a lost lantern

If you lose Dehkia's Lantern, you can craft it, but only after completing the two quests that first reward it. In Processing (L) Manufacture, use Dehkia's Light x1,000, Copper Ingot x1,000, Black Crystal x1,000, Magical Lightstone Crystal x500 and Magical Shard x300.

## Dehkia's Lantern mechanics: Ash Forest and Olun's Valley

- Ash Forest: use the lantern near a [Sealed] Rift Seed to summon [Voidtouched] Rift Seeds. Hitting a Rift Seed awakens the ghosts of Ash Forest. If an awakened Barnas is nearby, awakened Volkras relentlessly chase you, so kill Barnas fast. Defeated awakened Volkras revive.
- Olun's Valley: the left-arm attack of the awakened Olun's Golem can instantly kill everyone nearby. All awakened golems must be destroyed to beat the Giant Olun Golem. After the golem hits the ground three times with its left arm, the arm breaks, but drops no loot. Use spots are the [Voidtouched] Olun's Power Towers.

## Dehkia's Lantern mechanics: Tunkuta, Thornwood Forest and Cyclops Land

- Tunkuta: after a while [Chaos Infused] Ulutuka and Turos appear. Defeat a certain number of Turos and Ulutuka reappears from their corpses. Use spots are near [Chaos Infused] Turo Charms and Turo Shaman Towers.
- Thornwood Forest: the lantern draws [Void Tainted] Ahib. After enough are defeated, a [Corrupt Voidtouched] Dark Knight appears, and the Ahib keep swarming. Use spots are near [Void Infused] Eye of Despair.
- Cyclops Land: Cyclopes and Gargoyles awaken in a chaotic state. Cyclopes hit hard and eat nearby boars to heal. Defeating Cyclopes near Gargoyles only enrages the Gargoyles. Use spots are near the Cyclope Statues.

## Dehkia's Lantern mechanics: Aakman Temple, Hystria Ruins and Pila Ku Jail

- Aakman Temple: the Aakman Illusion Statue brings out the Aakman Flamen, who takes over the temple grounds. The ancient Aakman device goes haywire like a ticking time bomb, and the Flamen's illusion attack rains meteorites over a wide area.
- Hystria Ruins: the Hystria Guard Tower summons an ancient weapon in a chaotic state. Tutuka unleashes a devastating shockwave. At peak alert the ancient weapon Elten also activates.
- Pila Ku Jail: beware the [Void Tainted Prisoner] Frenzied Executioner, whose Dehkia-fueled swordplay is dangerous. They swarm when the alarm sounds.

## Dehkia's Lantern mechanics: Roud Sulfur Mine, Crescent Shrine and Cadry Ruins

- Roud Sulfur Mine: destroying sulfur bundles draws up the powerful Lava Tribe. Quickly cooling the lava with the water streams from the surroundings makes the fight easier.
- Crescent Shrine: forces exposed to Dehkia's void gather tightly around the Crescent Chief Gatekeeper, whose powerful attack permits no defense.
- Cadry Ruins: Cadry gather around Cadry Cannons and attack with Black Stone power. When they fall, a frenzied Cadry Commander appears, channeling void energy into his sword.

## Dehkia's Lantern Tier 2

Dehkia's Lantern can be upgraded to Tier 2 to awaken stronger monsters. In Simple Alchemy (L), combine Dehkia's Lantern Tier 1 with Dehkia's Light x300. Tier 2 works in Ash Forest and Olun's Valley.

| Zone | Recommended AP / DP | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ash Forest | 345 / 430 | Gairas, further exposed to the void, retreats deeper, so smaller Volkras and Barnas roam. Gairas may return when the void energy fades. |
| Olun's Valley | 340 / 430 | Olun's Golem absorbs nearby golems' void energy and fights alone. When destroyed, multiple golems reappear from the fragments. |

Main loot in both: Deboreka Earring, Forgotten Limbo Seal, Dehkia's Artifact - All Damage Reduction, Dehkia's Artifact - All Evasion, Dehkia's Fragment, Crystal of Precise Destruction, Crystal of Unyielding Spirit, Crystal of Stalwart Fortitude and Crystal of Dogged Patience.
