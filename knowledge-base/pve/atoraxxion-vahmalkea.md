# Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea

Guide to Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea, the first biome of the Atoraxxion dungeon: how to enter, how to progress through its areas, the final boss and the weekly rewards.

## Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea overview

Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea is a 5-player co-op dungeon and the first biome you can visit in Atoraxxion. Recommended gear is 280 AP and 340 DP, and optimal gear is 281 AP and 360 DP. It has seven areas. In order of the story they are Vaha's Query, Sky and Storm (which are played together), then Vaha's Heart, Vaha's Head, Vaha's Garden and Vaha's Cradle. The final boss is Apex Urukios, an Ancient Weapon in Vaha's Cradle. You see other players' groups in the dungeon, but the final boss fight happens in a private instance for your own party. Normal monsters here do not drop regular loot. They drop materials used to make the keys that open the next area. Rewards for the final boss are limited to once a week per Family, and the weekly quest is taken through Vaha's Time Capsule.

## Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea entry requirements

To enter Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea you need to be Lv. 60. Completing [Invitation from I] in the Black Spirit's Main Quest tab, and finishing your character's Awakening or Succession quest, are both optional. A character that finished the quest [Atoraxxion] Vaha's Time Capsule can enter from above Lv. 57.

Interact with the Sol Magia in the Ancient Stone Chamber and pick Vahmalkea on the Atoraxxion selection screen. There are two entry types:

- Normal Entry: play with 5 party members and get the weekly rewards as usual.
- Story Entry: meant for the main story quest or for hunting knowledge. It gives no weekly rewards, except for the first clear.

You can leave by interacting with the Sol Magia inside the dungeon. In solo mode you can also go back to the character selection screen.

## Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea progression and keys

Vahmalkea is cleared by crafting a chain of keys, one per stage. Every party member must craft their own final key.

1. In Vaha's Query, Vaha's Sky and Vaha's Storm, defeat Ancient Weapons for Vaha's Cold Thorn. With x5, open the Processing window (L) and use Simple Alchemy to make Vaha's Dried Fang. Use the Sol Magia in Vaha's Storm to reach Vaha's Heart. Cold Thorn also drops in Vaha's Heart.
2. In Vaha's Heart, defeat Krahten (it can appear while you kill Ancient Weapons) to get a Searing Heart Piece. In Yaz's Combinables Pouch, arrange the Dried Fang and the Heart Piece in a [-] shape to get Vaha's Burning Fang. Use the Sol Magia to reach Vaha's Head.
3. In Vaha's Head, defeat Ahtenn for a Frozen Shell of Reason and Vaha's Whispering Bell. Combine the Burning Fang and the Shell to make Vaha's Twisted Fang, then use the Sol Magia to reach Vaha's Garden.
4. In Vaha's Garden, which has no monsters, combine the Twisted Fang and the Whispering Bell into a Lucretia Dagger. Once all members insert theirs into the Sol Magia, the party leader can move to Vaha's Cradle.
5. Defeat Apex Urukios in Vaha's Cradle.

Right-clicking a key item starts automatic navigation to the Sol Magia.

## Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea boss and area mechanics

- Vaha's Query, Sky and Storm: monsters appear after you stand a few seconds under the floating artifacts.
- Vaha's Heart: the flying Ancient Weapon Putorium can be shot down with a matchlock from Vaha's Heart - Exchange.
- Vaha's Head: interact with the central Stellagia and solve its thread. Fragments of Ahtenn appear. Defeat enough of them and Ahtenn itself appears.
- Vaha's Cradle: a dead player cannot revive until every party member is dead or a Medical Kit is used. If all members die, the Cradle resets and you can retry. Dying there carries no penalty.
- Threads (puzzles) can be solved alone or with the party. You may skip one, but then you do not get the knowledge for it.
- Defeating Apex Urukios also gives the key (Corrupt Yolun Daggers) for Sycrakea's final boss, Maleficent Centilutos.

## Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea first-clear challenge and Golden Tears of the Desert

The challenge [Atoraxxion] The First Savior: Vahmalkea completes the first time you clear Vahmalkea. Talk to Vaha's Time Capsule in Vaha's Paradise, the place you reach after the final boss. Rewards: Golden Tears of the Desert x1, Vahmalkea: Desert Chest x1, Vahmalkea: Aakmah Chest x1, Scroll of Vaha's Records x10 and Item Collection Increase Scroll x10.

Golden Tears of the Desert is the crafting material for Vaha's Dawn Earrings, which have the same stats as the Black Distortion Earring. Recipe (Processing (L), Heating): Magical Shard x50, Yona's Fragment x10, Melody of the Stars x10 and Golden Tears of the Desert x1. It can also drop from these Atoraxxion bosses: Cetus, Monoceros, Lacerta, Creomar, Putorium, Krahtenn and Ahtenn.
