# Altar of Training

Guide to the Altar of Training in Valtarra, a party defense trial with five levels and bosses, covering how to start it, how it works and its rewards.

## Altar of Training overview

The Altar of Training is where the descendants of Sylvia train and face trials. Adventurers form a party and face five trials ending in a final boss for valuable rewards. Your goal is to protect a sacrament that appears in the middle from monsters trying to destroy it. If the sacrament is destroyed, the training fails and you must start again from the beginning. To retry, talk to Milford and spend energy for another training manual. Party members need a certain amount of AP, and more members make it easier.

## Altar of Training entry requirements

There are three ways to take part, all requiring Lv. 58 or higher. Use the training manual item at the altar, in a party of at least 2 members. Right-click the manual to see the altar's location.

| Manual | How to get it | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valtarra: Introductory Training Manual | Quest [Co-op] Proof of Courage from NPC Milford, after the Kamasylvia main quest [Kamasylvia] The Heart of Kamasylve and the quest Light to Valtarra. One time per character. | Lv. 58+, Kamasylvia main questline |
| Valtarra: Training Manual | Repeatable quest [Training Quest] Valtarra - Ruins Sacrament from Milford, every 3 days, costs 100 energy | Lv. 58+, finished [Kamasylvia] The Healing Light |
| Valtarra: Advanced Training Manual | Weekly quest [Training Quest] Valtarra - To a Higher Place (1/W) | Lv. 58+, Ruins Sacrament done at least once |

The Advanced Training Manual can only be used once per week. If you fail the advanced session, you wait a week to retry.

## Altar of Training levels and bosses

The five levels run back to back with no pause. After you clear the first swarm, a message announces Training II and a marker shows on the hilltop where the next sacrament will be summoned.

| Level | Name | Enemies and boss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Training I | Forest's Pact | Treants, mirumok ancient weapons and tree-like monsters; boss Dim Tree Spirit |
| Training II | Flying Maverick | Gargoyles, harpies and other flyers; boss King Griffon |
| Training III | Earth's Trial | Golems; boss Moghulis |
| Training IV | Verge of Death | Skeletons and cultists; boss Hexe Marie |
| Training V | Guardians of the Ruins | Ancient weapons from Hystria, Aakman and Hasrah; final boss Ancient Puturum |

Finishing Training III completes the Introductory session. The normal and advanced sessions need all of the levels cleared.

## Altar of Training mechanics and tips

Training II starting does not mean Training I is over. If you leave the Training I area before killing all monsters, the leftovers destroy the sacrament. If you take too long on Training I, the Training II monsters destroy the next sacrament. Clear each level in time and move quickly to the next marked location. Teamwork with party members is very important. The boss only appears if you have defended the sacrament successfully so far. When all five levels are defended, collect the EXP and other rewards from Milford.

## Altar of Training rewards

| Manual | Rewards |
| --- | --- |
| Introductory Training Manual | Combat EXP, Contribution EXP, Black Stone x5 |
| Training Manual (every 3 days) | Combat EXP and the Valtarra Training Reward Bundle, containing Black Stone, Hunter's Seal and Forest Fury |
| Advanced Training Manual (weekly) | Valtarra Eclipsed Belt, Courser Awakening Material Box, Black Stone, Hunter's Seal, Forest Fury, Gold Bar, Valtarra's Scattered Aura |

The advanced session has no quest rewards. Instead you loot seals from the bosses, combine them into tokens, and right-click a token to get a quest that you finish with Milford for a matching bundle. Bundles hold Valtarra's Scattered Aura and may hold a Courser Awakening Material Box. Collect x20 Valtarra's Scattered Aura and exchange them with Milford for a Valtarra Eclipsed Belt, which can also come straight from the bundles.

## Altar of Training advanced seals and tokens

Each boss in the advanced session drops a seal.

| Boss level | Seal |
| --- | --- |
| Training 1: Forest's Pact | Valtarra: Forest Seal |
| Training 2: Flying Maverick | Valtarra: Sky Seal |
| Training 3: Earth's Trial | Valtarra: Earth Seal |
| Training 4: Verge of Death | Valtarra: Death Seal |
| Training 5: Guardian of the Ruins | Valtarra: Relic Seal |

Seals combine into tokens: Forest + Sky = Valtarra: Token of Sky. Forest + Sky + Earth = Valtarra: Token of Earth. Forest + Sky + Earth + Death = Valtarra: Token of Death. All five seals = Valtarra: Token of Relic. Right-click the token to get its quest, then talk to Milford to complete it and receive the reward bundle.
