# Pit of the Undying

Guide to the Pit of the Undying, a daily 1 vs. 1 boss arena: entry requirements, the weekly quest, Seals of the Undying and insignia rewards, promotion tests and boss strategies.

## Pit of the Undying overview

In the Pit of the Undying you duel powerful bosses one at a time, alone, as a daily activity. Each boss defeated earns Seals of the Undying, which can be exchanged for rewards. The more bosses you beat, the higher your rank, which unlocks tougher bosses and better rewards.

## Pit of the Undying entry requirements

To access the Pit of the Undying you must be at least Level 56 and finish the introductory questline from the Suggested tab of the quest window, named [The Great Expedition] Crow's Nest, Misty Island of Mystery. You need a boat to sail to the island Crow's Nest and maybe a compass, since you cannot see your position in the deep ocean. Crow's Nest is hidden in fog on the map. If you have no boat, the Loyalty Shop (F3) sells one for 1,000 Loyalty Points, but it cannot be repaired and sails slowly once its Energy bar hits 0.

Afterwards you can teleport to the Pit from anywhere through ESC Menu, War, Pit of the Undying. You must not be in a party. Use the teleport option in your menu again to leave and return to where you were.

## Pit of the Undying weekly quest

After the introductory questline you can accept a weekly quest from the NPC Chorpy. Then go down the stairs to Kansha and take the first boss quest. Walk to the arena center, wait for the quest to start and stand on the yellow marker to trigger the fight. Defeat up to 4 bosses, returning to Kansha after each one to turn in the quest and take the next. If a boss beats you, give up the quest and take it again from Kansha for a rematch.

## Pit of the Undying Undying Crow's Insignia

The Undying Crow's Insignia is a unique reward that upgrades as you rank up and gives access to new bosses. Its level also sets how many Seals of the Undying you earn after beating every boss.

Special encounter: at DUO level, after defeating a boss there is a random chance that the special boss Yulho appears. It gives 150 more Seals of the Undying and is easy, with no mechanics to follow.

## Pit of the Undying promotion test

Beating every boss in the daily challenge also gives Crow's Black Jewels. With enough jewels, accept the promotion quest from Chorpy to get Crow's Sealed Promotion Scroll. Equip your insignia first, or the quest will not appear. If a boss defeats you, forfeit and re-accept the quest from Kansha. Daily jewel totals by insignia: PRI 40, DUO 60, TRI 100, TET 180.

| Promotion quest | Jewels | Bosses | Rewards |

## Pit of the Undying boss strategies: Valkin, Man of Steel, Evergart, Van Riker

- Valkin: sometimes strikes the ground to release a purple mist that slows you and then explodes. He stands in the arena center with a frontal guard, so avoid the mist and hit him from behind.
- Man of Steel: at melee range he does a constant 3-hit combo that needs precise dodging, and at range he charges and stuns you. At 50% HP, Man of Fortitude joins. He is durable and stuns with a similar charge but is weak to CC. Damage Man of Steel while Man of Fortitude is crowd-controlled.
- Evergart: deals more damage and has higher DP than other bosses. Stay behind him and watch for a blue effect that marks specific skills. When he uses Ground Smash, use a Float CC or a Knockback/Knockdown CC. After he is knocked down he takes much more damage briefly.
- Van Riker: has two spin attacks. The blue-effect one is safe. The one with black mist needs a Bound or Knockdown CC. If you fail, he summons slow tornados that chase you, and being caught means permanent CC and death.

## Pit of the Undying boss strategies: Bennet, Petroglyph Stalker, Fareed, Kunga, Brodie

- Bennet: after taking a set amount of damage she summons two clones. Clones can be walked through, but the real Bennet cannot, and she also takes more damage. Focus on the real one: killing a clone makes all clones vanish and respawn at full HP. When the real Bennet dies, she returns with the HP she had before the clones.
- Petroglyph Stalker: at 65%, 50%, 35% and 5% HP he summons 1 to 10 clones. They vanish after a few seconds when hit and explode for heavy damage, so avoid them.
- Fareed: a leaping attack knocks you down and deals ongoing damage. Around 80% HP he starts healing. A Float CC that knocks him down stops healing for the rest of the fight.
- Kunga: watch for a white or red effect before his skills at 85%, 70%, 55%, 40% and 25% HP. Stand behind him and use Float or Knockback CC. If you land fewer than three CCs, he does an unavoidable spin at 25%. With three CCs, the short spin hits the arena wall.
- Brodie: an archer who sets traps with visible timers that explode at zero. Avoid them.

## Pit of the Undying boss strategies: Syahzar, Kardun, Kauha, Duran

- Syahzar: at about 66% and 33% HP he gains an AP/DP buff and changes patterns. Dodge, get behind him and land a CC. After two different CCs the buff ends. He becomes immune to the last CC used, so you need 4 different CCs in total.
- Kardun: enrages at 75% and 25% HP, becoming a powerful wolf that cannot be defeated. Block or evade, or run around the arena without attacking. After a while he tires, returns to the center and reverts.
- Kauha: starts by summoning many Skeleton Archers, with one much stronger one to focus until about 70% HP. Then he moves to the center, summons more archers and two elite skeletons, which must be killed at the same time or they revive. Afterwards bring the strongest skeleton down to 30%.
- Duran: wait in front of him. He applies two different CC attacks in order. Answer with the same two types in the same order, for example stun then knockdown. A correct match makes him collapse for a damage window, and you repeat when he rises. A wrong match gives him a defense buff.

## Pit of the Undying boss strategies: Solaria, Ascendra, Gotha Rensa, Kyril, Kharoxia

- Solaria: keep your HP above 99% for the entire fight. Then her defense buff turns into an attack buff, letting you deal more damage. If your HP drops below 99%, an AP buff goes on Solaria.
- Ascendra: summons a big bear that rolls toward you. When Ascendra glows white, use a knockdown CC and let the bear roll at you. If the bear rolls over her while she is down, her defense buff is removed.
- Gotha Rensa: when he sheathes his weapons and charges, go behind him and land a knockback or float CC. He repeats it five times. With fewer than four successful CCs he becomes unkillable for a while and can one-shot you. Avoid the roars after each CC.
- Kyril: no special mechanics. She is invincible while teleporting around the arena. Avoid her attacks and stay away, since her melee attacks can CC you.
- Kharoxia: Phase 1, do not use knockback or float CC or she uses large AoE attacks. Phase 2, in her shield stance, go behind her for a knockback or float CC, three times to advance. Phase 3, she does a continuous AoE and when she glows red you must use a knockback or float to stop it.
