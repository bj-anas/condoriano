# Altar of Blood

Guide to the Altar of Blood, a 3-player survival and defense dungeon with 21 Illusions plus 2 Abyssal Illusions, covering entry, rules, gear requirements, mechanics and rewards.

## Altar of Blood overview

Altar of Blood is a 3-player party survival and defense content. You fight waves of enemies and climb through harder stages each week. It has 21 Illusions and 2 Abyssal Illusions. You must clear each stage to open the next, and all 21 Illusions must be cleared before the Abyssal Illusions. Higher difficulties may be added later.

- In a party, the highest stage you can attempt follows the lowest clear record among members. For example, if two members cleared up to Stage 5 and one cleared Stage 3, the party can enter up to Stage 4.
- Matching gives 5 attempts. If all are used, the party is moved out. There is no weekly entry limit, so you can queue again any time.
- Clear records never reset, so you resume from your highest stage.
- Each stage also gives a first-time clear reward once per Family, besides the weekly rewards.

## Altar of Blood entry requirements

To enter the Altar of Blood, go to Menu (ESC), then War (F7), then Find Match in the Altar of Blood menu. You can queue solo and be matched with other Adventurers, or queue as a 3-player party and enter together. The Party Leader can change depending on entry order. To build a party first, use the Party Finder (ESC, then Find Party/Platoon).

Solo matching requires a minimum gear level for the stage you choose. See the gear table for those numbers.

## Altar of Blood rules inside the dungeon

- The Party Leader picks the stage on entry. Pressing Start Game starts that stage immediately.
- You cannot be revived mid-stage: neither Witch/Wizard Resurrection skills nor Medical Kits work.
- At the end of a stage, whether you clear it or fail, every character comes back to life and heals.
- If all three party members die, the stage fails. Monsters vanish, everyone revives with full HP and the stage selection UI appears.
- These are disabled: campsites, the Maid/Butler, the World Map, the Quests UI and the Minimap UI.
- Monsters give no Combat EXP or Skill EXP, and effects that boost drop rate or drop amount have no effect.
- If combat has not started or there is no progress 10 minutes after entry, you are removed from the altar automatically.

## Altar of Blood stage types and boss mechanics

The Altar of Blood has normal stages and boss stages.

- Normal stages: defeat the monsters rushing toward the central relic. When monsters reach the relic its HP falls, and at 0% the stage fails.
- Boss stages: a specific boss appears, and you must defeat it while handling its special mechanics. The boss goes berserk 6 minutes after appearing, which greatly raises its AP and DP. If 8 minutes pass after it appears and it is still alive, the stage is failed.

## Altar of Blood rewards: weekly and first clear

Clearing stages gives weekly rewards and first-time clear rewards.

- Weekly rewards: delivered to the Black Spirit's Safe once per Family each week, on Sunday at 00:00. Each stage has a reward, but you only get the one for the highest stage cleared that week. Clearing Stages 10, 15 and 20 in one week pays only the Stage 20 reward.
- First clear rewards: sent automatically to Black Spirit's Safe (B) the first time you clear each stage.
- Higher stages give better rewards: more item types, larger amounts and better chances.
