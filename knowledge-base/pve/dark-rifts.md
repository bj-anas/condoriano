# Dark Rifts

Overview of Dark Rifts, solo boss rifts that open after reaching Level 56: how they work, the six bosses, rewards and the Boss Info UI.

## Dark Rifts: how to start one

Dark Rift bosses begin appearing 24 hours after your character reaches Level 56. Once a rift is open, it shows on the World Map and you can click the icon for details. You can also press the boss information icon at the bottom right of the UI. The boss comes out of the rift as soon as you get near it, and a notification message appears at the top of your UI.

## Dark Rifts: rules and rewards

- Dark Rifts are individual to each Adventurer. You fight the boss alone, because other Adventurers cannot see your boss.
- A defeated Dark Rift boss reappears 120 hours (5 days) later. Defeating it quickly after it appears is recommended.
- Dying costs no EXP and breaks no crystals.
- At Level 60 or above with Tuvala gear at PEN (V), you can defeat Dark Rift bosses without difficulty.
- Rewards are rare items such as Cron Stones, Deboreka Accessories and Mythical Feathers.
- New or special Dark Rift bosses with improved rewards may appear during events.

## Dark Rifts: the six bosses

Dark Rifts have 6 bosses in total: Ahib Griffon, Ferrid, Muskan, Dastard Bheg, Dim Tree Spirit and Red Nose. Defeating them gives rare items such as Cron Stones, Deboreka Accessories and Mythical Feathers. Each Dark Rift boss is fought alone. During events, new or special Dark Rift bosses with improved rewards may also appear.

## Dark Rifts: Boss Info UI

The Boss Info UI merges the Dark Rift and Boss Notification functions. It shows World, Field, Dark Rift and Hunting Boss information, the time left until a boss spawns (shown only down to minutes), the boss's remaining HP after it appears, and navigation to the selected boss's location.

- Field Bosses and Hunting Bosses show the server they spawned on instead of remaining time.
- Bosses with random appearance times do not show a remaining-time countdown.
- A spawned boss has a red icon at the top of the Boss Info UI.
- Bosses are sorted by release date.
