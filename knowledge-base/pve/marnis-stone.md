# Marni's Stone

Guide to Marni's Stones and Great Marni's Stones: how they collect monster samples, how to exchange them with Wacky Toshi, and their rewards.

## Marni's Stone: what it is and requirements

Marni's Stones are creations left behind by the mad scientist Marni. If you carry one while fighting monsters, it collects a sample from each monster you defeat. Bring a filled stone to the NPC Wacky Toshi, who gives a set amount of Combat EXP based on your character level. Wacky Toshi sells empty stones and takes them back when full. He is often near the entrance of monster zones. Your character must be Lv. 56 or higher: characters under Lv. 56 cannot buy the stones or collect samples.

## Marni's Stone: how to use it

Each Marni's Stone is named after a monster zone. For example, Marni's Stone (Bashim) needs monsters from Bashim Base in Valencia.

- You can buy several stones. Samples go into the first stone you bought, then the next one starts filling when it is full.
- You must land the last hit on a monster to collect its sample, so being in a party or letting others take last hits slows you down.
- The stone must be in the inventory of the character killing monsters. It does not fill in another character's inventory, storage or your mount's inventory.
- Right-click the stone and select Item Count to track kills more easily.
- Return to Wacky Toshi and use the exchange button for full stones. You get a fixed amount of Combat EXP. Stones filled from monsters changed by the Elvia Realm of Serendia are traded for items instead.

## Great Marni's Stone: how it works

You may randomly find Great Marni's Stones while defeating monsters. These old experimental stones hold many more samples than ordinary ones. The process is the same, but Wacky Toshi gives a Great Marni's Supply Box instead of Combat EXP. The box has one of several yellow grade accessories, PRI (I) to DUO (II): Tungrad Necklace, Tungrad Belt, Tungrad Earring, Tungrad Ring, Ogre Ring, Laytenn's Power Stone, Ring of Crescent Guardian, Basilisk's Belt, Eye of the Ruins Ring, Narc Ear Accessory and Valtarra Eclipsed Belt.

## Great Marni's Stone titles

Once a Great Marni's Stone has all its samples, a title quest tied to that stone opens, and each Family can take it only once. The Combat quest filter in the Quest window (hotkey O) must be on to accept it. The titles are Slayvior of: Bashim, Cadry, Desert Naga, Desert Fogans, Gahaz, Argos, Basilisk, Taphtar, Roud Sulfur Mine, Pila Ku, Manshaum, Fadus, Polly's Forest, Forest Ronaros, Ruins, Blood Wolves, Sherekhan, Protties and Sycraia. After obtaining all titles from the 19 Great Marni's Stones, Wacky Toshi gives the Great Marni's Assistant title.
