# Atoraxxion: The Final Gladios

Guide to The Final Gladios, the solo boss fight against Illezra, the White Witch, that ends the Atoraxxion saga: how to unlock it, rewards and titles.

## Atoraxxion: The Final Gladios overview

The Final Gladios is the closing battle of the Atoraxxion story, fought against Illezra, the White Witch. It is solo content. Recommended stats are 330 AP and 420 DP. The stories of Yolunakea, Mountain of Eternal Winter, Sycrakea and Vahmalkea all converge here, so it is best to finish those storylines first.

## Atoraxxion: The Final Gladios entry requirements

To start The Final Gladios fight against Illezra, a character of Lv. 60 or above first completes the quest [Atoraxxion] Thornwood and the Dark Goddess. After that, accept the quest [Atoraxxion] Where Illezra Awaits through the Black Spirit. The fight is solo and recommends 330 AP and 420 DP.

## Atoraxxion: The Final Gladios final selection reward

In Atoraxxion: The Final Gladios, after you finish every suggested quest in Atoraxxion, a new quest unlocks: [Atoraxxion] Final Selection. Completing it gives these rewards, once per Family: the Atoraxxion Final Selection Box, Dehkima: Token of the Oath x50 and Maha's Fragment x10. The page does not list what is inside the box.

## Atoraxxion: The Final Gladios weekly quest and rewards

Accept the quest [Weekly] Gladios: Kyvela Chest from Sol Magia in the Red Heart. Finishing the questline gives the Gladios: Kyvela Chest.

| Gladios: Kyvela Chest, guaranteed contents | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Dehkia's Rare Box | x1 |
| Trace of Nature | x25 |
| Ancient Spirit Dust | x25 |
| Dehkima: Token of the Oath | x25 |
| Maha's Fragment | x35 |

You may also get one of these at a set probability: Upgraded Compass Parts, Rich Merchant's Ring Piece, or Golden Tears of the Desert. The compass parts and ring piece are the same kinds as in earlier Atoraxxion rewards. Opening Dehkia's Rare Box gives one Deboreka accessory (ring, earring, necklace or belt) between +0 and TRI (III), by set probability.
