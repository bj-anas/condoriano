# Field Bosses

Guide to field bosses in Black Desert: how they spawn, the six bosses with mechanics, locations and drops, Black Shadow's weekly schedule, Latent Boss Aura and Ode to Heroes of Yore.

## Field bosses: how they spawn

Field bosses appear on their own in specific locations and threaten the area around them, and anyone can join the fight in the seamless world. They have much higher AP and HP than regular field monsters, so coming unprepared can get you killed. They drop exclusive rare loot, and you can still get it even if you are not in a party. The field boss versions are much stronger than the ones in the main quest or from summon scrolls.

Field bosses appear on random servers, on average once a day, except Black Shadow. A system message shows only on the server where one spawns, so coordinate with other Adventurers. All field bosses except Black Shadow disappear 1 hour after appearing.

## Field bosses: spawn chains

Field bosses appear in groups, one after another.

- Balenos: Dastard Bheg in Balenos Forest, then Red Nose in the Agris Steppe, then Dim Tree Spirit in the Forest of Seclusion. Each appears 3 minutes after the previous one is defeated.
- Serendia and Drieghan: the Giant Mudster in Serendia, then Katzvariak in Drieghan, 5 minutes after the first is stopped.

## Field bosses: Dastard Bheg

Dastard Bheg swings his giant hook around the area slowly, so move out of its way. Another attack has his body glow red before two hook swings in a wide, high-damage AoE. Like Red Nose, he enrages at a certain HP percentage and does a special AoE, swinging his hook around himself several times. This happens more than twice, so do not jump in right after the second swing.

Location: Balenos Forest, south of the town of Velia, in Balenos.

## Field bosses: Red Nose

The Imp Boss uses melee cleaver attacks and a charge. If you are not the main target, stay behind it to keep damaging it safely. Below a certain HP it enrages and uses powerful shockwaves, so watch for them or stay far away. When it inhales, disengage: it will do two shockwaves followed by a devastating body slam, which can kill even high-DP characters.

Location: Balenos, in the area west of the town of Velia and just north of the Western Guard Camp.

## Field bosses: Dim Tree Spirit

The Dim Tree Spirit is agile and powerful. Its slams and swings are fast, can knock you down and cause multiple hits. When it glows red it is charging a ranged attack, then throws rocks three times, which is hard to dodge and deals heavy damage. It also hides underground to gather nature's energy, so get away quickly because it follows with a very wide, high-damage AoE.

Location: Forest of Seclusion, east of Bandit's Den Byway, in Balenos.

## Field bosses: Giant Mudster

The Giant Mudster is slow, but its AoE hits massively and lowers movement and attack speed of everyone hit. Its big attacks start when it shrinks, so move away if it shrinks without warning, because it then charges a target. If it shrinks while raining mud, small mudsters are summoned and must be killed, or they explode for heavy damage. Its strongest attack comes after a stiffness debuff, followed by a series of ground spikes. If hit by the debuff, use Emergency Escape (hotkey V). When heavily damaged it flees to the Southern Marsh node or northwest of the Glish Swamp node, and a message at the top of the screen tells you where.

Location: first at the Glish Ruins, then the flee spots above, all in Serendia.

## Field bosses: Katzvariak

Katzvariak, who commands the kvariaks, shows up on a hill above the Tshira Ruins and can travel over and under the ground, attacking with poison. It covers patches of ground with poison that damages you, so stay out. After covering a total of 8 areas it detonates them for huge damage, so track where they are.

Location: Drieghan, on the hill north of Tshira Ruins and west of the Forgotten Gateway.

## Field bosses: Black Shadow

Black Shadow, believed to be the young dragon of Belmorn, usually flies above Saunil Battlefield. When it flaps its wings, move far away or behind it to avoid its breath attack. After critical damage it flees, leaving some loot, to an area in Balenos, Serendia or Calpheon that varies by server. A fast horse helps to chase it. After critical damage three times, it flies away and the encounter ends.

Black Shadow's first spawn place and time are fixed and it appears once per week. Like other field bosses, it may not appear on your server, so ask other Adventurers in chat which servers have it.

Location: west of Marni Farm Ruins, east of Calpheon City, in Calpheon.

## Field bosses: Latent Boss Aura

Most field boss drop lists include Latent Boss Aura. With at least 100 of a boss's auras and a family quest, you can exchange them for that boss's equipment. For example, x100 Latent Boss Aura becomes x1 Bheg's Gloves. After you have done that, more auras of the same type can be exchanged for Memory Fragments. Right-click an aura item to see its quest and exchange location. Jetina's weekly quest trades x2 Latent Boss Aura for x115 Concentrated Boss Crystal, and those crystals feed the guaranteed PEN (V) Boss Gear questline.

## Field bosses: Ode to Heroes of Yore

Field bosses drop the item Ode to Heroes of Yore. Using it gives the buff Item Drop Rate +10% for 5 hours, which stacks with other Item Drop Rate buffs. It can be stored in the family inventory to move it quickly to another character. It expires 5 hours after you obtain it, whether it is in a character inventory or the family inventory, and an expired item cannot be used.
