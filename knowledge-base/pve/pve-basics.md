# PvE Basics

Beginner guide to improving your combat power against monsters in Black Desert: magic crystals, skill add-ons, buffs and Combat and Skill EXP buffs.

## PvE basics: magical crystals

Magical crystals are items transfused into gear slots, and their effects apply once transfused. Monsters drop them, but the types you find differ by monster zone, so it helps to search the Central Market for the crystals with the effects you want. The Central Market sells a wide variety of them. The wiki points to its Transfusion guide for details.

## PvE basics: skill add-ons

Skill add-ons are extra effects attached to certain skills that trigger when the skill hits a target. You add them through Skill Instructor NPCs once your character reaches Lv. 50. At Lv. 50, 52, 54, 56, 58 and 60 you earn a skill add-on for one skill each time, so you can have add-ons on a total of 6 skills. Skill Instructors are in major cities and towns. If you are far from any town, enter the Battle Arena (ESC menu, then War, with full HP) and talk to the skill instructor Tachros to change your add-ons.

## PvE basics: combat buffs that stack

Any positive effect on a character is a buff. Combat-stat buffs boost hunting efficiency, but not every buff stacks, so choose ones that work together. You can only hold 1 buff of each type at a time. Priority below is based on cost, ease of getting, stats gained and duration.

| Type | Item | Priority | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Perfume | Perfume of Courage | High | Raises AP, HP and movement/attack speed |
| Food | Simple Cron Meal | High | Raises stats important for hunting |
| Draught | Beast's Draught | High | Raises AP, Damage Reduction Against Monsters, EXP gained and more |
| Draught | Giant's Draught | High | Raises All AP, Special Attack Damage, Max HP, Max Stamina and more |
| Draught | Frenzy Draught | High | Lowers your DP but raises offensive stats a lot |
| Alchemy Stones | Spirit Stones, Alchemy Stones | Medium | Go in the alchemy stone slot. Spirit Stone durability cannot be recovered |
| Alchemy Stones | Vell's Heart | Medium | Raises base AP by +3 on top of its buff. Durability can be recovered but it is expensive |
| Campsite/Church | Body Enhancement | Medium | Raises AP, DP, Max HP, All Resistance and Ignores All Resistance |

## PvE basics: buff tips and buff duration

Food and draughts need many materials and steps to craft, but other Adventurers sell them on the Central Market, and alchemy stones are sold there too, with prices varying by stone. Campsite buffs are bought through a campsite. Gold Bar buffs can be exchanged at an NPC in major cities and towns for Gold Bar 10G x3 (worth around 3,000,000 Silver), but they are not very efficient because travel to the monster zone takes time. Buffs that apply only to your character stop counting down while you are logged out and continue when you log in again.

## PvE basics: Combat and Skill EXP buffs

If you are aiming for Lv. 61 or higher, EXP buffs help a lot. Combat EXP and Skill EXP are separate systems, so get buffs for both. Combat EXP buffs add together.

- Combat & Skill EXP 530% Scroll (60 min): sold in the Pearl Shop for Silver, giving a 530% increase to Combat and Skill EXP. Each Family can get only 53, though special events may allow more.
- Sealed Book of Combat: not consumed, and gives a +100% EXP buff each time you use it during its period. New Adventurers get it from Challenges and login rewards. Right-click it, confirm with Yes (Enter), and the duration starts counting down.
- Blessed Message Scroll: lasts 100 minutes and gives Combat EXP +200% along with Skill EXP +30%. Newcomers receive it through Challenges and login rewards, and events hand it out as well.
- Other sources: food like the Simple Cron Meal, Beast's Draught, and the Black Spirit buff from the Black Spirit menu, Exchange, by consuming Energy.
