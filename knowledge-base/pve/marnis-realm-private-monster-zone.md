# Marni's Realm - Private Monster Zone

Guide to Marni's Realm, a private copy of a monster zone where you hunt alone: how to enter and exit, the available zones, rules and the Field Report.

## Marni's Realm: what it is

Marni's Realm lets you enter a realm separate from the rest of the world and hunt in a private monster zone. It looks the same as the monster zone you came from, with the same monsters and loot, but you are alone and no other Adventurer is visible. You enter from a designated area inside the monster zone and cannot leave that area while inside. To go outside it, leave Marni's Realm.

## Marni's Realm: how to enter

Go to the designated area of a monster zone where Marni's Realm is available. Open Menu (Esc), Adventure (F5), Private Monster Zone, press the navigation button to travel to the area, and then press the Enter button that appears on the left of your screen. Navigation is not available for desert monster zones, and for several Valencia zones the designated areas are shown separately. For Pila Ku Jail, enter from the plaza entrance near NPC Botadin. For the Elvia Realm zones you must be inside the Elvia Realm, whose icon is left of the minimap in the top right of the screen or in the same Adventure tab of the main menu.

## Marni's Realm: available monster zones

Regular zones (alphabetical): Aakman Temple, Ash Forest, Bashim Base, Blood Wolf Settlement, Cadry Ruins, Centaurus Herd, City of the Dead, Crescent Shrine, Crypt of Resting Thoughts, Darkseekers' Retreat, Desert Naga Temple, Fadus Habitat, Forest Ronaros Area, Gahaz Bandits' Lair, Gyfin Rhasia Temple (Underground), Hystria Ruins, Jade Starlight Forest, Kratuga Ancient Ruins, Manshaum Forest, Pila Ku Jail, Polly's Forest, Protty Cave, Roud Sulfur Mine, Sherekhan Necropolis (Day), Star's End, Sycraia Ruins (Lower Zone), Thornwood Forest, Titium Valley, Traitor's Graveyard, Tshira Ruins, Tungrad Ruins and Yzrahid Highlands.

Elvia Realm zones (alphabetical, each marked [Elvia] in game): Biraghi Den, Bloody Monastery, Hexe Sanctuary, Orc Camp, Primal Giant Post, Rhutum Outstation, Saunil Camp, Swamp Fogan Habitat, Swamp Naga Habitat and Troll Habitat.

## Marni's Realm: how to exit

Press the Exit button on the left of your screen to return to the regular world. Leaving pauses the timer of your remaining Marni's Realm time, and you are returned automatically when the time is used up. After you leave, there is a 5 minute cooldown before you can re-enter. You can turn the Marni's Realm UI on or off through Menu (Esc), Adventure (F5), Private Monster Zone.

## Marni's Realm: rules and limits

- Time limit recharges while you are online outside the realm, up to 1 hour and 5 minutes.
- You cannot stay inside more than 11 hours per day.
- The guild skill Command to Gather and the item Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass cannot be used to summon or be summoned.
- You can only call your mount if it is within the realm's designated area.
- Summons you called before entering disappear when you enter.
- Dying inside returns you to the regular world.
- You cannot enter with negative karma, or on an Arsha or Node/Conquest War server channel.
- If the realm is too crowded on your server, the Change Server UI appears and shows crowding on other servers. Current crowding is shown on the left side of the UI.

## Marni's Realm: Marni's Field Report

Open the Field Report with the icon that sits beside the navigation button inside the Marni's Realm window.

- Records are saved per character: character used, monsters defeated, AP/DP stats and date, for each monster zone.
- Acquired Loot lists junk items first, then other loot from highest to lowest grade.
- Recording starts automatically on entering and stops on exit. Use View Loot by Date to see loot by date.
- Pin up to 1 report per class. Recent pinned reports are kept, older ones may be deleted, and a new saved report deletes the previous one.
- A record is saved only if you stay at least 5 minutes and get at least 1,000 white grade junk loot.
- Sessions played before the report update are not shown.
- A UI under the Exit widget shows loot gained in real time.
