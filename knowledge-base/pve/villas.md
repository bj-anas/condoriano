# Villas

Guide to the villas of Valencia: how to get a villa invitation, the three villa buffs and their prices, and the villa daily quests.

## Villas: what they are and the invitation

Villas are owned by wealthy NPCs and are scattered around Valencia. Adventurers can buy a 7-day invitation for 10 million Silver at the villa shop, or with a Gold Bar 100G through the exchange option. Inside a villa you can take daily quests, buy potions and other items, and repair gear, so you do not need to go to far-off towns. The most important function is buying buffs for your adventures, such as more Combat EXP or Life EXP, resistance or attack. Villas surround the great inner desert across Valencia and sit in the capital. They all show on the World Map (hotkey M) even in unexplored areas.

## Villas: buffs and prices

Each villa offers one specific buff, so check which buff a villa sells before you go. You can pick a duration of 90 minutes or 180 minutes.

| Buff | Effects | NPCs | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Turning Gates (90 min) | Energy Recovery +1, Amity +10%, Life EXP +10% | Karashu, Shakatu, Kiyak, Muna, Marzana, Talia | 1G gold bar x3 (300,000 Silver) |
| Turning Gates (180 min) | Same | Same | 1G gold bar x9 (900,000 Silver) |
| Skill and Experience (90 min) | Skill EXP +10%, Combat EXP +10%, Sunstroke and Hypothermia Resistance Chance +40% | Kunid, Amir, Alsabi, Gahaz, Tasaila, Shandi, Inaha, Dudora, Oberin | 1G gold bar x5 (500,000 Silver) |
| Skill and Experience (180 min) | Same | Same | 1G gold bar x15 (1,500,000 Silver) |
| Body Enhancement (90 min) | All AP +10, All DP +10, Max HP +200, All Resistance +10%, Ignore All Resistance +5% | Lohan, Atosa | 1G gold bar x15 (1,500,000 Silver) |
| Body Enhancement (180 min) | Same | Same | 1G gold bar x45 (4,500,000 Silver) |

Villa buffs do not stack. Buying Skill and Experience while you hold Turning Gates removes the Turning Gates buff.

## Villas: exchanging Silver for gold bars

The same Villa Butler NPCs who sell buffs can exchange Silver for gold bars. They cannot use Silver kept in your town storage, so carry the Silver in your inventory. Look for a villa near the monster zone you plan to hunt, or buy the buff before you travel there.

## Villas: daily quests

Some villa managers give daily quests that can be done nearby. Daily quests reset every day at 01:00 UTC. The reward is 1G gold bars, which helps if you forgot to bring Silver and want to buy the local villa buff. Look for each villa's manager if you need a few gold bars.
