# World Bosses

Guide to Black Desert world bosses: how the encounters work, notifications, each boss's mechanics, locations and loot, including the empowered forms and the Land of the Morning Light bosses.

## World bosses: how the encounters work

World Bosses appear at specific locations and anyone can join. Raise your AP and DP enough to survive their attacks and have a chance at loot. When a boss is defeated, many Adventurers are picked to collect loot based on the amount of damage dealt, but that is only one factor, so luck can still give you the best items. A boss vanishes if not defeated within 30 minutes (15 minutes for Quint and Muraka). World Bosses share HP across all servers, so you can join raids on almost any server. You lose no EXP and your crystals do not break if you die in these encounters.

## World bosses: appearance notifications and reminders

System messages at the top of your screen announce each world boss appearance. This note does not contain the NA and EU spawn timetables, so check the Boss Notification window in game for times.

To set a reminder, open the main menu (Esc), then Adventure (F5), then Boss Notification. You can be notified 5, 15 or 30 minutes before a boss spawns. The standard warning is 15 minutes ahead, and Vell is warned 30 minutes ahead because of the long travel distance.

## World bosses: Kzarka

Kzarka, the Lord of Corruption, is one of the easier world bosses. When it becomes immune to all attacks, it is preparing a fiery breath or a large AoE attack, so hide behind a column. If it is your first time, watch other Adventurers and follow when they step back. Kzarka may drop a Kzarka's Sealed Weapon Box. Right-click it to get a Kzarka main weapon for your class.

## World bosses: Nightmarish Kzarka

Nightmarish Kzarka may appear in place of Kzarka at its scheduled time. It is much more powerful, with many attack patterns: AoE damage, a series of attacks, roaring with black breath, pulling nearby targets in for a heavy blow, tentacle summons that give a movement speed and attack speed debuff, and a berserk pattern. Come well prepared. The reward is much better loot, including a Kzarka Weapon Box with Kzarka main weapons enhanced up to TET (IV).

You must deal at least 0.01% of its total HP, shared across all servers, to qualify for loot.

## World bosses: Nouver

Nouver appears in the desert, so bring Purified Water and Star Anise Tea against desert illnesses. Move away quickly to avoid being sucked into his whirlwinds. When he tilts his head back he is about to breathe fire, so dodge sideways. He also uses strong skills while airborne, so use invincibility, evasion and block skills, and spread away from other Adventurers for the AoE. You may get a Nouver's Sealed Sub-weapon Box, and right-clicking it gives a Nouver sub-weapon for your class.

## World bosses: Bloodstorm Nouver

Bloodstorm Nouver may appear in place of Nouver. It is much stronger, with new patterns: two fire breaths in a row, a phantom made from the sandstorm, a cry that summons a huge whirlwind, a 3-stage aerial attack and a ground slam. Loot is better, including a sub-weapon box with Nouver sub-weapons enhanced up to TET (IV). There is also a chance for Bloodstorm Nouver's Ragged Wing and Bloodstorm Nouver's Split Horn, both used to craft the Nouverikant outfit (see the Special Outfits page). You must deal at least 0.01% of its shared total HP to qualify for loot.

## World bosses: Karanda

Karanda, the Harpy Queen, appears only at Karanda Ridge in Calpheon. She is strong with many attacks, and the rough, tight terrain makes it riskier. Two skills to know: in one she flies up and hits a large area outside the circle around her, so get inside her circle fast. In the other she lands briefly and takes off again, hitting inside the circle, so move outside it quickly. You may get a Dandelion Weapon Box, which gives a Dandelion Awakening weapon for your class.

## World bosses: Stormbringer Karanda

Stormbringer Karanda may appear in place of Karanda. She creates powerful whirlwinds on arrival, and her enhanced attacks can throw you far below the mountains for high or fatal damage. Other patterns include feather attacks, gusts from behind, summoning countless harpies for random heavy damage, a push-back with a sweeping outer attack, and a storm. Loot is better, including the Heart of Karanda, which adds Critical Hit Rate +3% and a crystal slot when infused into an Awakening weapon, and a weapon box with a Dandelion Awakening Weapon enhanced up to TET (IV). You must deal at least 0.01% of her shared total HP to qualify.

## World bosses: Kutum

Kutum is a worm that sways side to side when attacking and can suck in everything in range. It spits dirt to stun targets and jumps at a target after digging into the ground. Once its HP is 50% or below it sends out electric currents. Avoid the dust storm when it emerges. You may get a Kutum's Sealed Sub-weapon Box, and right-clicking it gives a Kutum sub-weapon for your class. Kutum sub-weapons balance AP and DP, while Nouver sub-weapons focus more on AP.

## World bosses: Thundercloud Kutum

Thundercloud Kutum may appear in place of Kutum. It has strong skills and many patterns, and creates thunder clouds. A cloud debuff slowly drains your HP. Enter the stone chamber, one of two at the side of the room, that matches the clouds' color to clear them. At lower HP it summons ancient weapons. Other attacks: thunderbolts, electrical storms, rising from the ground, a moving electric orb, sucking in everything nearby, and a frontal swipe followed by one to the opposite side.

Loot: Kutum's Power Stone, an Alchemy Stone slot item that damages monsters and lowers enemy DP but cannot be recharged, and the Heart of Kutum, which adds Special Attack Evasion Rate +7% to a sub-weapon. A weapon box has a Kutum sub-weapon up to TET (IV). You must deal at least 0.01% of its shared total HP.

## World bosses: Quint

Quint, the first troll, is a giant statue at Quint Hill in Northern Calpheon. When the system message appears he escapes his petrified form and jumps around. He is slow, so his attacks can be evaded with focus, but do not lean on HP potions. His attack speed and AP rise as HP falls. Drops table:

| Quint drops |
| --- |
| Quint Statue, Belongings of an Adventurer, Black Magic Crystal - Hystria, Black Magic Crystal - Harpia, Mutant Enhancer, Gold Bar 100G, Gold Bar 10G and more |

Chilling Fragments go to contributors by set probability: 1-5 for the top 70% and 1-2 for the top 100%. Collecting 100 of them lets you exchange them for a Deboreka accessory.

## World bosses: Muraka

Muraka, the King of Ogres, is a giant ogre with black crystallized scales, bigger than a regular ogre. He appears deep in Mansha Forest in southwestern Calpheon, among the goblin tribe and other ogres. He is slow and fairly easy to dodge, but his AoE attacks hit hard. His attack speed and AP rise as HP drops, and at low HP his fist strike on the ground can kill instantly even with TET (IV) defense gear. Attacking his legs repeatedly can make him fall to his knees. Drops: Dim Ogre Ring, Mutant Enhancer and more. Chilling Fragments go to contributors by set probability: 1-5 for the top 70% and 1-2 for the top 100%. Collecting 100 of them lets you exchange them for a Deboreka accessory.

## World bosses: Mirumok Destroyer Offin

Deep within Kamasylvia's Holo Forest lives the world boss Mirumok Destroyer Offin, among the hardest of them to beat. It repeatedly transforms and combines. The large tree-like entity is Mirumok Destroyer Offin, which has four legs, and Watcher Offin Tett rides inside. At the start you can only hit the legs. Once enough leg damage is done, Offin Tett comes out and Offin stops working, but the fight is not easier because Offin Tett makes clones and uses strong attacks. When Offin Tett loses enough HP it climbs back inside Offin.

Loot: you may get an Offin Tett's Light Sealed Weapon Box, which gives an Offin Tett's Radiant main weapon for your class. These weapons have lower accuracy but higher AP than Kzarka main weapons.

## World bosses: Garmoth

Garmoth, the Crimson Dragon, appears at Garmoth's Nest in central Drieghan. She is much stronger than the version from the Drieghan main questline. Like Nouver she is a dragon, but her AoE attacks differ and can be fatal depending on how much magic she has absorbed.

Garmoth's Heart, a chance drop, upgrades Awakening weapons and also Kutum and Nouver sub-weapons. Awakening weapons get 2 magic crystal slots and Critical Hit Damage +3%. Sub-weapons get Special Attack Evasion Rate +10%, Max HP +150 and Max Stamina +100. First change the heart into an Inverted Heart of Garmoth through a quest. There is also a small chance for Garmoth's Horn, which gives a dragon pet with the Max HP +25 talent. This pet can only be upgraded with another of its type, and Wizard Gosphy does not work with it.

## World bosses: Vell

The Heart of the Ocean, Vell, lives in Vell's Realm, far inside Margoria. The system warns you 30 minutes ahead. A whirlpool forms first, and being swept in can deal huge damage to you and your ship. Approach carefully because Vell is enraged on first appearing. She sends her fragments, Lopters, to destroy ships firing cannons at her, and hunting matchlocks kill them. Vell can only be damaged by ship cannon fire, and she retaliates with strong breath attacks, so keep moving. Every member of the same party or platoon gets the same Combat EXP, even if someone uses Focus Fire.

If you resurrect at the nearest town, you return at Oquilla's Eye. If you resurrect at a node, you appear on the ocean a short distance away, near buoys, and should wait for rescue. When Vell takes enough damage she enrages and unleashes a tsunami AoE that kills everyone on the water's surface. Hold on firmly to the ship or dive underwater to survive.

Rewards come through the Black Spirit's Safe. A chance drop is Vell's Heart, a rechargeable offensive alchemy stone. If you get Vell's Concentrated Magic, process it with Simple Alchemy using x1,500 Alchemy Stone Shards to make a Vell's Heart.

## World bosses: Land of the Morning Light bosses and weekly rewards

Certain bosses from the Collection of Tales and from the Black Shrine of the Donghae and Hwanghae provinces show up as world bosses in the Land of the Morning Light. Each of the four world bosses (Golden Pig King, Sangoon, Bulgasal and Uturi) pays out loot once per week. Defeat each and claim rewards through the Region Quest UI at the top right of the screen.

- Each boss region quest is weekly and resets every Thursday at midnight (00:00 UTC).
- Each of the four types appears 11 to 12 times a week. Defeating one of each type gets you all the weekly rewards.
- You can get the loot from the Region Quest UI even if you have not finished the Morning Light main questline.
- After all four are defeated, the top 10 contributors on each server have a very low chance of the Flame of the Primordial.
