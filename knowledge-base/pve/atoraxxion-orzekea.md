# Atoraxxion: Orzekea

Guide to Atoraxxion: Orzekea, the fourth Atoraxxion biome, with its field monster zones, the Orze final boss, rewards, knowledge challenges and titles.

## Atoraxxion: Orzekea overview

Atoraxxion: Orzekea continues the Atoraxxion story after Vahmalkea, Sycrakea and Yolunakea. It differs from the other biomes in one way: the places where you used to kill monsters for items to open the next area are now field monster zones where you simply hunt. Throughout the biome you can find Orze's Threads, which are puzzles. They are clues left by Orze, and solving them moves the main questline forward. Thread progress may be rough on servers where a Node or Conquest War is running.

## Atoraxxion: Orzekea entry requirements

You can enter Atoraxxion: Orzekea by interacting with Sol Magia, no matter whether you cleared Vahmalkea, Sycrakea or Yolunakea. You can also use Elribta's Door inside those biomes:

- Vahmalkea: Elribta's Door in Vaha's Garden
- Sycrakea: Elribta's Door in Syca's Garden
- Yolunakea: Elribta's Door in Yolu's Garden

Daily quests inside the dungeon need a character of Lv. 60 or above.

## Atoraxxion: Orzekea field monster zone and grind spot

Characters at Lv. 60 or above can take two daily quests from [Investigator] Ivor: [Daily] Orzekea Ancient Weapon Research and [Daily] Subdue Alketa. You find Ivor by the bottom of the staircase that links Orze's Root with Orze's Base.

The zones have Ancient Weapons Spiran, Centarion and Leos. Killing them raises an alert phase that summons stronger [Ancient Weapon] versions. At the final alert phase, the stronger monster Alketa spawns with a notice.

| Total stats recommendation | AP 1100+, Damage Reduction 700+ |
| Monsters | Centarion, Leos, Spiran, Alketa |

## Atoraxxion: Orzekea final boss co-op mode (5 players)

The Orzekea final boss does not need the zone-by-zone items used in other biomes. Adventurers can go straight to it by defeating Cathawen, the guardian of the stronghold, in Orze's Garden and obtaining the Holy Devotee's Dagger. Recommended stats for the final boss: 330 AP and 420 DP.

Defeating the final boss, Orze, opens Orze's Paradise. Each Family can claim this reward once a week, and it refreshes every Thursday at midnight.
