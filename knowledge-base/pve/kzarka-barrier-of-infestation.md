# Kzarka: Barrier of Infestation

Guide to the Barrier of Infestation, a solo five-level fight against an enraged Kzarka, including how to access it and its permanent stat rewards.

## Kzarka: Barrier of Infestation overview

In the Barrier of Infestation, Hadum, the God of Despair, has reached the Serendia Shrine, and Kzarka, the Lord of Corruption, has gone berserk in response. Adventurers are asked to destroy the Barrier of Infestation that keeps Kzarka bound, so its rage calms and it returns to sleep. The barrier allows only one Adventurer inside, so you fight Kzarka alone. A berserk Kzarka has very strong, frightening attacks, but in exchange has a smaller health pool.

## Kzarka: Barrier of Infestation entry requirements

The Barrier of Infestation starts from a weekly quest while you are inside the Elvia Realm. You can reach the Elvia Realm from the icon to the left of the minimap in the top right of the screen, or through the main menu (Esc) then Adventure then Elvia Realm.

Requirements:
- Level requirement: Lv. 61 or above, needed for [Elvia] Obsidian Ashes.
- Complete the questline that begins with [Elvia] Obsidian Ashes, found in the Suggested tab of the Quest window (hotkey O).
- Accept the quest [Elvia] Dreary Moonlight from the Black Spirit, which can only be done inside the Elvia Realm.

## Kzarka: Barrier of Infestation mechanics and time limit

The Barrier of Infestation has five levels, and in every level you face Kzarka alone. Each level has a 7-minute time limit, and you must finish within 7 minutes to receive that level's reward. If you lose the summon scroll or fail any level, you can get the scroll again from the Black Spirit and try again.

## Kzarka: Barrier of Infestation rewards

Each level's reward can be collected once per Family. The permanent bonuses are applied to the whole Family and are the main reason to finish this content.

| Level 2 | Predator's Roots x5 |

| Level 4 | Predator's Roots x40, Ancient Spirit Dust x500, Max HP +20 (Family, permanent) |
| Level 5 | Cron Stone x50, Predator's Roots x50, All AP +1 (Family, permanent) |
