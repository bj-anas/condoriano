# Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo)

Guide to Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo), the solo Boss Blitz content of the Land of the Morning Light: entry, weekly attempts, Calamity difficulties, light orbs, rankings, rewards and the Calamity 8 to 10 loot tables for each boss.

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) overview

The Black Shrine is content where you fight the boss monsters that rule the Land of the Morning Light (LoML). As you progress the main questlines there, the bosses tied to each tale unlock for the Black Shrine. Donghae (Solo) is the solo version, and Hwanghae (Party) is the party version. Each boss unlocks as you advance the Land of the Morning Light main questline and earn that boss's knowledge. The Black Spirit Support quests require finishing three tales first: Gumiho, Sangoon and the Golden Pig King.

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) entry requirements

Open the main menu (ESC), then Adventure (F5), then Black Shrine, pick the boss you want and press the Start Boss Blitz button. You can also open the Black Shrine window from the icon at the top of the screen, left of the minimap. Start Boss Blitz teleports you to the Boss Blitz location, and when combat ends you return to where you were. Princess Dokkebi appears and casts the teleport spell. The only place where this teleport cannot be used is the Pit of the Undying.

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) weekly attempts

Each Family has 5 weekly attempts at the Black Shrine bosses, resetting at midnight on Sunday.

- Defeating a boss at a difficulty uses up an attempt, but beating the same boss again at the same difficulty with the same character costs no attempt.
- Being defeated or forfeiting does not use an attempt.
- When all five are used, you can still challenge levels you have cleared, and your best record counts toward your Boss Blitz ranking.
- The no-cost rule only applies to the same character that beat the boss. A different character spends an attempt.

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) Calamity difficulty levels

The Black Shrine has 10 difficulty levels called Calamities, from Calamity 1 to Calamity 10. Higher levels give bosses more varied attack patterns and a chance at better loot and rewards. Boss battles are recorded by difficulty and by class. The first Adventurer of each class to defeat a boss at each difficulty is announced across Black Desert.

- Calamity 1 to 5 are open to all classes.
- Calamity 6 to 10 unlock per class: someone of your class must have beaten the boss at the previous level. Example: for a Warrior to fight Golden Pig King at Calamity 6, another Warrior must already have beaten it at Calamity 5, and that unlocks it for all Warriors.

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) stat rules and light orbs

In Boss Blitz, damage dealt and taken is 90% based on aura attribute and only 10% based on your character's own attack and defense. Only 10% of skill add-on and buff effects tied to AP/DP apply. Only critical hits from attribute attacks are shown. Attribute attack crit rate is 50%, and all debuff resistance is 20%.

Light orbs come from the Land of the Morning Light main questline (hotkey O, Suggested tab: the Lv. 56 Combat quest called Light Orb, with the subtitle about the extraordinary power of the Land of the Morning Light). Sun, Moon and Earth Orbs invest in that aura for extra stats against bosses weak to it. A Light Orb can go into the aura of your choice and gives extra EXP when invested into a weakness. Each orb adds AP +50 and DP +100 to that aura and grants 3 stat points, which you can put into AP or DP. In Manage Aura, resetting orbs costs 2 million Silver and resetting the extra points costs 500,000 Silver.

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) rankings and weekly reward payout

Rewards arrive on Sunday at 00:00 server time, based on your kill record and ranking. Ranking depends on how fast you defeat each boss. Rankings are separate per class and difficulty, and only your best result per class counts, whichever character of that class you used. Beating the same boss with several characters of one class gives no extra reward. Rankings refresh daily at 00:00 server time. Claim rewards from the Black Shrine menu. Unclaimed rewards expire after the following Sunday at 00:00 server time.

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) Blessing of Morning Light

Each time the highest Calamity difficulty is completed, the whole server is notified and gets a temporary 180-minute buff. Three buffs exist, depending on the boss defeated, and all three can be active together.

| Boss defeated | Buff |
| --- | --- |
| Golden Pig King, Apex Changui, Oduksini | Blessing of the Warm Sun: AP +1 |
| Mudang Wraith, Songakshi, Bamboo Legion Lieutenant | Blessing of the Soft Moon: DP +1 |
| Gumiho, Duoksini, Sangoon, Imoogi | Blessing of the Thriving Earth: Item Drop Rate +20% |

## Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) Adventure Log Boss Storybook and Special Reward

The Adventure Log Boss Storybook gives rewards, once per Family, the first time you beat the boss at each difficulty. In it you cannot get Tear of Illusion as loot, and the chance of a Deboreka Earring is lower than in other Black Shrine rewards.

The Donghae (Solo) Special Reward, with HYPERBOOST benefits, comes from Black Spirit Support quests. Defeat the listed bosses to obtain Slumbering Origin defense gear.
