# Atoraxxion: Sycrakea

Guide to Atoraxxion: Sycrakea, the second biome of the Atoraxxion dungeon: entry, the area-by-area key chain, boss tips and weekly rewards.

## Atoraxxion: Sycrakea overview

Atoraxxion: Sycrakea is a 5-player co-op dungeon and the second biome you can visit in Atoraxxion. Recommended gear is 280 AP and 340 DP, and optimal gear is 289 AP and 380 DP. It has five areas, visited in this order: Syca's Scale, then Grave, then Glare, then Garden, and finally Syca's Cradle. In each area you collect items, and then you are taken to the final boss, Maleficent Centilutos, in Syca's Cradle. You see other players' groups inside, but the final boss is in a private instance for your party. Normal monsters drop materials for the keys that open the next area, not regular loot.

## Atoraxxion: Sycrakea entry requirements

Atoraxxion: Sycrakea is available from Lv. 60. Completing [Invitation from I] in the Black Spirit's Main Quest tab and finishing your Awakening or Succession quest are optional. A character that completed [Atoraxxion] Vaha's Time Capsule can enter from above Lv. 57.

Interact with the Sol Magia in the Ancient Stone Chamber and choose Sycrakea on the Atoraxxion selection screen. You can also use Elribta's Door, which appears after clearing Atoraxxion: Vahmalkea.

- Normal Entry: 5 party members, with normal weekly rewards.
- Story Entry: suited for the main story quest or knowledge hunting. No weekly rewards except for the first clear.

Leave the dungeon by interacting with the Sol Magia inside.

## Atoraxxion: Sycrakea progression and keys

Each area needs its own items to summon that area's boss. Collect what all the area bosses drop and you are taken to the final area. Steps:

1. Syca's Scale: kill monsters for Syca's Frigid Scale and Egg of an Arid Epoch. Using Simple Alchemy (L), combine x2 of each to make Yolun Piece of Envy. The party leader holds it and interacts with Syca's Eye: Berurah to summon Berurah. Berurah drops Syca's Arid Tentacle.
2. Syca's Grave: kill monsters for Syca's Frigid Scale and Egg of a Fearful Epoch. Combine x2 of each with Simple Alchemy (L) into Yolun Piece of Cruelty. The party leader interacts with Syca's Eye: Serpen to summon Serpen, who drops Syca's Burning Tentacle.
3. Arrange the Envy piece, Arid Tentacle, Cruelty piece and Burning Tentacle in a [-] shape to make Syca's Contorted Tentacle. Use the Sol Magia in Syca's Grave to enter Syca's Glare.
4. Syca's Glare: defeat Apocros for the Yolun Dagger. Combine it with the Contorted Tentacle into a Corrupt Yolun Dagger.
5. Syca's Garden: every party member places their Corrupt Yolun Dagger in the Sol Magia, then the leader moves the party to Syca's Cradle.

Right-clicking a key item starts auto-navigation to its target.

## Atoraxxion: Sycrakea boss mechanics

- Berurah: drag the fish monster Pisces over each fire zone surrounding the boss to extinguish it, using one Pisces for every fire.
- Serpen: when a message says Serpen has taken control of the device, destroy the pillars around the boss, but skip the unstable pillars, which have a different lightning effect.
- Apocros: hit the Lahtron monsters in the room to guide them to the pillars. This stops Apocros from flying so you can attack it.
- Maleficent Centilutos: use the power supply device in the Cradle, which has levels 1 to 4. A higher level lowers the boss's DP but raises its AP.
- In Syca's Cradle, a dead player cannot revive until all party members die or a Medical Kit is used. If everyone dies the Cradle resets for another try, and there is no death penalty.
- Defeating Centilutos also gives the Voidlight Dagger key for Yolunakea's final boss, Impassioned Amarillos.

## Atoraxxion: Sycrakea first-clear quest and Golden Tears of the Desert

The quest [Atoraxxion] The First Savior: Sycrakea completes on your first clear. Talk to Syca's Time Capsule in Syca's Paradise, which you reach after the final boss. Rewards: Cartios (Tier 1), Sycrakea: Abyssal Chest x1, Sycrakea: Prottia Chest x1, Scroll of Syca's Records x10 and Item Collection Increase Scroll x10. Cartios is a RARE pet with the Talent Skill EXP Increase and the Special Skill Finding Resources.

Golden Tears of the Desert is the crafting material for Vaha's Dawn Earrings, which have the same stats as the Black Distortion Earring. Recipe (Processing (L), Heating): Magical Shard x50, Yona's Fragment x10, Melody of the Stars x10 and Golden Tears of the Desert x1. It also drops from Cartios, Lykina, Berurah, Serpen and Apocros.
