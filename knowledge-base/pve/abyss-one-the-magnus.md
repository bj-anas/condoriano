# Abyss One: The Magnus

Guide to Abyss One: The Magnus, a parallel world reached through a main questline, with its entry requirements, quest rewards, fast travel, remote storage, the Abyssal Legacy skill and titles.

## Abyss One: The Magnus overview

Abyss One: The Magnus is another world that has always existed alongside the world of Black Desert. Inside the Magnus are abyssal pockets, each with its own spacetime and its own trials. You advance the Abyss One main questline by entering each pocket and overcoming its trials. The custodian of the abyss is Wuju, whom you meet inside the Magnus. In the Magnus you do not use Black Desert gear but equipment made only for Abyss One. You enter through a well during the questline, and the first quest is [The Magnus] Peaceful Vagabond. Some parts of the questline require the Serendia and Calpheon main quests.

## Abyss One: The Magnus entry requirements

Start by accepting [The Magnus] Nostalgic for Velia from the Black Spirit once you meet one of these sets of conditions. This quest can only be accepted within Balenos territory. Then go to Eileen in Velia.

| Route | Conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Main Quest | Complete one of: Looking for Adventurers; [Calpheon] Hero of Calpheon through [Calpheon] Epilogue III; [Boss] Witch of Horrors |
| Simplified Main Quest | Complete [Special Growth] Fughar's Memorandum - Chapter 1 |
| Mountain of Eternal Winter | Reach Lv. 56; complete the Awakening and Succession (Ascension) questlines, then accept the quest that rewards a Skill Preset Coupon from the Black Spirit; complete [A Fresh Adventure] In Search of Lost Memories |

## Abyss One: The Magnus how to return to the Magnus

After you open every Magnus entrance near the major cities during the questline, those entrances show on the World Map. You can also use the Find NPCs function to find an Abyssal Well, and falling into the well takes you back into the Magnus.

## Abyss One: The Magnus questline reward: PEN (V) boss defense gear

Finish [The Magnus] In Return for Your Help to gain the knowledge In Return for Your Help, unlock quality-of-life features and receive rewards. One of them is a choice of one piece of boss defense gear already at PEN (V), once per Family.

| Choose one |
| --- |
| PEN (V) Giath's Helmet / Griffon's Helmet |
| PEN (V) Dim Tree Spirit's Armor / Red Nose's Armor |
| PEN (V) Muskan's Shoes / Urugon's Shoes |
| PEN (V) Bheg's Gloves / Leebur's Gloves |

This gear cannot be listed on the Central Market. A helmet or armor piece enhanced beyond Caphras Lv. 10 can be turned into Slumbering Origin Armor, either Labreska's Helmet or Fallen God's Armor, and that version can be put on the Central Market.

## Abyss One: The Magnus fast travel through Abyssal Wells

With the knowledge In Return for Your Help and the regional geographic knowledge for your destination, you can instantly travel to major cities through the Abyssal Veins in the Magnus or the Abyssal Wells in each city. Travel costs 5,000,000 Silver regardless of distance. Travel between Balenos and the Land of the Morning Light is free for New Adventurers, meaning those whose secondary password is less than 60 days old.

Notes:
- You need the regional knowledge. Example: without the knowledge of the city Grána, you cannot travel to Kamasylvia, even with In Return for Your Help.
- You cannot use the well without the 5,000,000 Silver.
- It may not work while you are in the middle of Magnus quests before [The Magnus] Bloody Eyes in the Well.

## Abyss One: The Magnus remote storage access

Because major cities are linked through the Magnus, as you progress the main questline you can move and store items in storage in other regions. Do it through the storage keeper NPC or a storage maid. It only works in regions already revealed on the map, and not in Muiquun. You cannot store or take out trade or bartering goods, treasure items, or Ornette's and Odore's Spirit Essence (including lesser versions) with this feature.

## Abyss One: The Magnus Abyssal Legacy skill

Inside the Magnus you unlock a new skill for every class, one per character.

- After finishing the whole Magnus questline and gaining the knowledge Abyssal Veins: Balenos, accept [The Magnus] Abyssal Legacy from the Black Spirit.
- Completing it gives [Secret Book] Abyssal Legacy x1 and Armstrong's Skill Guide (1 Day). If you lose the book, NPC Cruhorn Wyrmsbane gives another.
- You can learn Abyssal Legacy in place of one of that class's Absolute skills.
- Once the questline has been finished one time and Abyssal Veins: Balenos is known, the other characters in your Family can take [The Magnus] Abyssal Revelation to learn the skill too.

Example: Nova's Abyssal Legacy skill is Queen's Gambit.

## Abyss One: The Magnus titles

The Magnus main questline grants these titles: Young and Reckless, Dunce, Snot-faced Wuju, Monster, Singularity, Eileen's BFF, Anomaly Normalizer, Copycat, One of a Kind, Got the Chills, Dimensions Apart, Exhausted, More Bark than Bite, Abyssal Traveler, Well, Well, Well, and one hidden title shown as ???. Warriors can obtain an extra special title while going through the main questline.
