# Useful Tips - Quality of Life

A list of simple Black Desert tips (Quality of Life 1): the Black Desert+ app, drop rate and Ecology Knowledge, UI and chat settings, hiding characters, energy, coupons, storage and lighting.

## Quality of life tip: Black Desert+ mobile app

Black Desert has a mobile app, Black Desert+, which gives access to the official website, the Central Market, guild chat and a variety of alerts. It offers: Central Market trading in real time on a mobile device, alerts for announcements, World Bosses and more, the latest news, a forum for discussing with other Adventurers, and FAQ/Support for sending tickets or reading the FAQ. It can be downloaded for Android and from the App Store.

## Quality of life tip: Ecology Knowledge and item drop rate

Your item drop rate increases as you accumulate Ecology Knowledge. Use the Target feature in the Knowledge menu (hotkey: H): the targeted monster is highlighted on the minimap, which makes it easier to find.

## Quality of life tip: UI, chat, screenshot and Hide Other Characters settings

Several UI and chat tips from Quality of Life 1: to keep your UI open when monsters attack, switch off Hide UI When Attacked, found under Settings > General Settings > Misc. Chat Groups are available for Composing/Playing, Sailing, Life skills, hunting, PvP, new Adventurers and daily life. Use the Quick Hotkey feature for functions you use often. Press Ctrl + U to hide your UI for better-looking screenshots. Press and hold the mouse wheel to look around, even while moving. If World Boss fights become too crowded, use Hide Other Characters at Menu > Settings > Performance Settings > Optimization > Hide Other Characters, with the hotkey Shift + F5.

## Quality of life tip: Energy Potions and coupon codes

Alustin, in any major city, will trade your leftover Energy for an Energy Potion, and Energy Potions can be sold on the Central Market. You can register coupon codes in-game by copying and pasting the code at Menu (ESC) > Adventurer Support > Redeem Coupon Code. This is only available in the new menu UI.

## Quality of life tip: Escape, item linking and locking Black Spirit's Rage skills

If you are trapped somewhere, use Escape, which has a cooldown of 30 minutes. Find it at Menu (ESC) > Character > Escape, or the button at the top of the Menu (ESC). To show an item to another Adventurer, press Enter for the chat window, then Shift + LMB on the item in your inventory. You can lock your Black Spirit's Rage skills in the Skill Window (hotkey: K), at the bottom right "Black Spirit's Rage" (hotkey: Alt + B). After locking the skills, you can still absorb Black Spirit's Rage.

## Quality of life tip: Storage, Warehouse and Family Inventory

All characters in a family can access gear and items through the Storage and the Warehouse. Storage is separate per town or city, but the transport function moves items between them. Once the Magnus questline is finished, the Magnus can also carry items from one Storage to another. Some items cannot be placed in Storage or Warehouse. Keep often-used consumables and helpful items in your Family Inventory to make leveling other characters easier.

## Quality of life tip: Atanis Firefly for the dark

Atanis Firefly and Small Atanis Firefly are useful at night or in the dark. Atanis Firefly costs 100 silver and lights up the dark for one hour. Small Atanis Firefly costs 50 silver and lasts 30 minutes.
