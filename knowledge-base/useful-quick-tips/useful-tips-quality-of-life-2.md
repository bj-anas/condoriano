# Useful Tips - Quality of Life 2

More simple Black Desert tips (Quality of Life 2): OTP drop rate bonus, quick slots, the 3D minimap, pets, storage, the Old Moon Sky Balloon, Magnus Wells, moving family characters and Trial Characters.

## Quality of life 2 tip: OTP drop rate buff, Auto-align Quick Slot and 3D minimap

Registering OTP Settings gives enhanced security and an Item Drop Rate +5% buff. Auto-align Quick Slot arranges more quick slots at once: turn on "Activate Auto-Align Quick Slot" under Settings > General Settings in "Quality of Life", then arrange as many as you want in UI Settings. The 3D minimap shows far places: click the button at the top left corner of the minimap to switch to it. You can even see Cron Castle from Velia. You can also lock skills you do not want to use: hover over a skill in the Skill Window (hotkey: K) and click the lock.

## Quality of life 2 tip: Pet groups and expanding town storage

You can take out often-used pets in groups. Give pets a group number with the "Take Out" buttons, and name groups with the "Rename Group" button. For storage: if you use contribution points to buy a residence for storage, you can expand the storage space of that town. Click a house in each city or town, select "Storage" from the Usage List, and click the purchase button, which consumes contribution points. You can also upgrade the storage by the number of arrows in the Usage List, which takes some silver and time.

## Quality of life 2 tip: Old Moon Sky Balloon

The Old Moon Sky Balloon moves you around a city safely and comfortably. It is in Calpheon, Altinova, Valencian and Grána. Buy a boarding ticket with 1 Black Stone from the Old Moon Sky Balloon Manager, then give it to the Sky Balloon Boarding Assistant and the Sky Balloon descends. If you reconnect while riding it, your character falls and might die.

## Quality of life 2 tip: Magnus Wells for fast travel

If the Old Moon Sky Balloon is too slow, the Magnus Wells can take you between points of the world within a few seconds. You must complete the main quest related to this content before you can use the Magnus Wells.

## Quality of life 2 tip: Moving family characters on the World Map

You can manage your Family's characters on the World Map (M) with the 'View Family Characters' icon at the top right. The "Move Character" function moves inactive characters to a different location: right-click a character in the list and choose "Move Character". Character Transport takes time, and if you connect before the designated time, transport is canceled and you return to your original place. It only works in cities, villages and most Safe Zones.

## Quality of life 2 tip: Trial Characters

A Trial Character lets you try a class you have not chosen. It begins at level 62 and is limited to a designated area, the Battle Arena, so it cannot join regular content. It receives skill points to unlock all skills (4000 skill points), and has 365 AP and 425 DP. Exclusive gear for Trial Characters can be bought from the NPC vendor Hartz. To create one, use the "Trial" tab in the top right corner of Character Selection. Skills can be tested on scarecrows or monsters placed around the area.
