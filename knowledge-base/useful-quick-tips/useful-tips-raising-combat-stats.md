# Useful Tips - Raising Combat Stats

Ten tips for making a Black Desert character stronger, listed from easiest to hardest: skill add-ons, skill enhancements, crystals, buff items, alchemy stones, reforms, villa buffs, Adventure Logs, Caphras and Krogdalo's Horse Gear.

## Raising combat stats tip: Skill Add-ons

Characters at lvl. 50 or higher can get skill add-ons free from a Skill Instructor in any town. They add on-hit effects such as buffs, debuffs and HP and resource recovery. You can get your first skill add-on at lvl. 50 and up to 6 skill add-ons at lvl. 60. Changing existing add-ons costs Memory Fragment x1, but you can change them as often as you want without Memory Fragments while the "Secret Book of Old Moon" buff or the "Cliff's Skill Add-on Guide" buff is in effect.

## Raising combat stats tip: Skill Enhancements

Characters at lvl. 56 or higher can get Skill Enhancements, shown in the Skills (hotkey: K) window. They become available once you have the required preceding skills. They give extra skills that make combos more damaging, and some have debuff effects for utility.

## Raising combat stats tip: Crystal Transfusion

If your gear has slots, summon the Black Spirit (hotkey: ,) and transfuse crystals into it. Use crystals found on your adventures or bought from the Central Market. Transfused crystals can break each time your character dies. You need a "Crystal Extraction Tool" to take a crystal back out of a slot.

## Raising combat stats tip: Buff items (food, draughts, elixirs, perfumes)

Food, elixirs, draughts and perfumes give buffs lasting from a few minutes to several hours. Food is a cost-effective way to get buffs, and "Simple Cron Meal" and "Exquisite Cron Meal" give strong long-lasting buffs. You can craft Cron Meals by Processing ingredients, or buy them from the Central Market or the Oasis Vendor, Ellie. Food is used one at a time, while draughts can be combined with elixirs and perfumes for more buffs.

## Raising combat stats tip: Alchemy Stones and Spirit Stones

Alchemy Stones and Spirit Stones are placed in the "Alchemy Stone" slot, found at the center of the Equipment window (hotkey: I). After equipping one, press "U" to get its effects for a period of time. Both use durability when used. Alchemy Stone durability can be recovered with fuels of "Sturdy" grade or higher, but Spirit Stone durability cannot be recovered. Spirit Stones have similar effects and tend to be cheaper. The checkbox below the slot refreshes the stone's effect automatically each time the buff ends.

## Raising combat stats tip: Item Reform with reform stones

Green-grade gear can be reformed with reform stones through the Black Spirit's "Reform" menu. Ways to get reform stones: invest Contribution Points to build a Refinery in town, exchange Hunter's Seal x5 with Finlin, the Material Vendor in Tarif, for a grade 3 reform stone, or buy them on the Central Market. Successfully reformed gear gets an "Ultimate" prefix and extra stats. A failed reform never lowers the grade, so you can keep trying. Reformed items cannot be sold on the Central Market, but a Blacksmith or Armor Vendor can extract the reform stone and return the item to its original state.

## Raising combat stats tip: Villa buffs

Villas across Valencia give buffs. Buy a Villa Invitation for 10,000,000 Silver from a Villa Keeper, enter, and pay Gold Bars to the "Villa Butler". A 90-minute buff costs around 1,500,000 Silver, and a 180-minute buff costs 4,500,000 Silver. Villa buffs come in 3 types, and the best for combat is "[Villa] Body Enhancement (180 min)", which gives:

| Stat | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| All AP | +10 |
| All DP | +10 |
| Max HP | +200 |
| All Resistance | +10% |
| Ignore All Resistance | +5% |

This buff is available at Lohan's Villa and Atosa's Villa, which are fairly near Gahaz, Titum and other Valencia monster zones. Buffs differ between villas.

## Raising combat stats tip: Adventure Logs

Completing certain Adventure Log pages gives stats that apply to all characters in your family, permanently. AP and DP rewards: Bartali's Adventure Log AP +4, DP +2; Dorin Morgrim's Secret Journal AP +1, DP +1; Herald's Journal DP +1; Deve's Encyclopedia AP +1; Fughar's Times of Success DP +1; The Eyes of Adventure DP +1. Adventure Logs also reward items and raise max Agris Fever, and they give HP, Stamina, Accuracy, Evasion, Weight and Inventory Slots.

## Raising combat stats tip: Caphras Enhancements

If you own TET (IV) boss gear, Caphras Enhancement can raise AP and DP, and the most cost-effective levels are 1 to 3. Apply it through the Black Spirit menu (hotkey: ,) > Enhancement. At a Blacksmith in most towns you can extract 95% of the Caphras Stones used, at a cost of 100,000 Silver per Caphras Stone extracted. The Caphras effects are removed once the stones are extracted.

## Raising combat stats tip: Krogdalo's Horse Gear

Krogdalo's Horse Gear is hard to craft and costs much silver, so it is recommended for veterans. There are three sets: Wind, Earth and Sea. Equip your horse with 5 pieces of one set (Champron, Barding, Saddle, Stirrup, Horseshoe) and use the horse skill "Roar" to activate the buff for 10 allies (including yourself) for 60 minutes:

| Set | 5-set effect |
| Wind | AP +5 |
| Earth | Damage Reduction +3 and Evasion +3 |
| Sea | Max HP +100 and Stamina +100 |
