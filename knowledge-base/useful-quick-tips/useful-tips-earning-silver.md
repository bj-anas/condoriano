# Useful Tips - Earning Silver

Six tips for earning extra silver in Black Desert: weekly quests, auto-fishing, Magical Seeds, World Bosses, Dark Rifts and the Black Spirit's Adventure.

## Earning silver tip: Weekly quest Lara's Great Discovery and Bread

The weekly quest "[Weekly] Lara's Great Discovery and Bread" is a way to earn silver and can be done once a week. Accept it from Lara, the General Goods Vendor in Heidel. Hand over Dough x700 (made from Potato, Sweet Potato, Wheat, Barley or Corn powder). Dough is crafted by Shaking Mineral Water with powder at a 1:1 ratio, and the powders can be bought on the market. Choose "Scorched Fragment" as the reward, a key ingredient of the expensive Manos accessories. Repeat weekly until you have 15 "Small Four-leaf Clover" (a standard reward), then talk to Lara for "Small Fortunes Yields Big Fortune", then do the Bonus quest and start again. Craft Manos accessories (earring, ring, belt, necklace) and sell them on the Central Market.

## Earning silver tip: Auto-fishing

Auto-fishing lets your character fish automatically, and the fish can be used for Bartering or Imperial Delivery. It is one of Black Desert's popular AFK activities, and the Velia coast is a popular spot. To make it more efficient: keep ample inventory space, use items that cut fishing time such as the Balenos Fishing Rod and pets (for example Lost Penguin), and work toward a High Fishing Level (up to +5).

## Earning silver tip: Magical Seeds

Adventurers with Farming Artisan Mastery or higher can make additional silver by planting Magical Seeds. A Magical Seed may drop whenever Plant Breeding is done on a crop. Breeding a Magical Seed can yield Premature Fruit, Seed Half Eaten by a Bird, Plant with Rotting Roots, Mutant Plant, or Unusual Fruit. Using Simple Cooking on two of these crops gives Stonetail Fodder, needed for Dream Horses. Plant Breeding on a Magical Seed always gives at least one more Magical Seed, so you can keep farming and sell extras to vendors. You can also harvest Magical Seeds for a large amount of crops to sell to vendors.

## Earning silver tip: World Bosses

World Bosses drop boxes with world boss gear, crystals, gold bars and more, and with luck valuable items such as Vell's Heart and Garmoth's Heart. Many Adventurers station characters near boss spawn locations so they can fight a boss the moment it appears, which gives steady income. For some bosses, such as Garmoth, you must accept a weekly quest to receive rewards, so talk to Kukao at Garmoth's Nest before fighting Garmoth.

## Earning silver tip: Dark Rift bosses

Bosses from Dark Rifts, whose locations show on the World Map (M), are another way to earn silver. They drop boxes with quality accessories, plenty of Ancient Spirit Dust, and maybe "Remnants of the Rift", a material for crafting Blackstar gear. A character at lv. 60 or higher with PEN (V) Tuvala or Narchillan gear should be able to kill them. A character that dies to Dark Rift bosses loses no EXP and breaks no crystals. Simple Alchemy turns Ancient Spirit Dust into Caphras Stones, which you can use or sell on the Central Market. Dark Rifts respawn some time after a boss is defeated.

## Earning silver tip: Black Spirit's Adventure

The Black Spirit's Adventure is a board-style mini-game: moving the Black Spirit along the game board unlocks rewards. Open it in-game from Menu (hotkey: ESC) > Rewards > Black Spirit's Adventure. A new pair of dice is granted every 30 minutes, 3 times per day, so collect your dice at each login and roll for free rewards.
