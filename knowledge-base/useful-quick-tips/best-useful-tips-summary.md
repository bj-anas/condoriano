# Best Useful Tips Summary

A summary of short, practical Black Desert tips in six chapters, covering servers, hotkeys, nodes, quests, the Central Market, notifications and pets. It links to the Quality of Life 1 and Quality of Life 2 tip pages.

## Best useful tips, chapter 1: servers, skills, minimap and hotkeys

Tips from chapter 1 of the Best Useful Tips Summary:

- Change servers faster by clicking the server name on your minimap.
- Learn every level of a skill at once with Shift + Left click.
- Enlarge the minimap by dragging its lower left corner.
- For "Cinema mode", use Crop Mode in game settings > Display Settings > Game Window. A bigger crop reduces vertical vision range and increases horizontal vision range.
- Save map locations by right-clicking a location and pressing "Save Location".
- Register menus to hotkeys by holding Alt + Click on a menu icon.
- Find an NPC with the magnifying glass icon to the right of the minimap.

## Best useful tips, chapter 2: quick slots, auto-path, nodes and recipes

Tips from chapter 2 of the Best Useful Tips Summary:

- Register skills or items faster by dragging them and pressing the number of the quick slot.
- Create custom auto-path routes with Alt + Right Click and keep clicking.
- Stop UI windows from closing when you are hit in game settings > General Settings > Misc. > Close Window UI when hit.
- Connect nodes quickly with the "Connect Node Directly" button.
- Find a lost item with "Find my Item" in the ESC menu or Ctrl + F.
- Clicking the Black Spirit's Rage bar below your HP bar locks your Black Spirit skills.
- Open all Processing recipes by clicking the book icon in the Processing UI.

## Best useful tips, chapter 3: quest types, horses and chat

Tips from chapter 3 of the Best Useful Tips Summary:

- Show only certain quests: press "O" for the quest menu and pick one of the 5 quest types at the bottom.
- Rent a horse from Stable Keepers with a special icon above their heads.
- Show only certain building types in a town or city with the map filters.
- Enable chat timestamps in the chat window settings.
- Look around while moving by holding the mouse wheel.
- Delete several inventory items at once: press the trash bin icon, then click up to 20 items.
- Get full horse speed by linking Instant Acceleration (F while running) with Drift (left or right arrow + down arrow while running).

## Best useful tips, chapter 4: Central Market, screenshots and UI

Tips from chapter 4 of the Best Useful Tips Summary:

- Find favorite items on the Central Market in the "Favorites" tab.
- Take screenshots of your character without backgrounds with Ctrl + Shift + Print Screen.
- The enhancement UI always tells you which item is needed to enhance a specific item.
- Expand or shorten your notification widget.
- Use "Combat Focus Mode" to remove unnecessary UI elements while hunting.
- Right-click an item in your inventory and press "Item Count" to display its count on your UI.

## Best useful tips, chapter 5: boss alerts, World Map, skill add-ons and security

Tips from chapter 5 of the Best Useful Tips Summary:

- Set World Boss reminders in the Boss Notification settings in the ESC menu.
- Change the angle the World Map opens at in General Settings > World Map Opening Styles.
- Skill add-ons can be changed at Tachros, the Skill Instructor NPC, inside the Battle Arena, which you reach through Menu (ESC) > War > Battle Arena.
- Securing your account with PC Registration or OTP raises your Drop Rate.
- Copy and paste coupon codes directly into the in-game Redeem Coupon Code UI.
- Move other characters to a different location through the World Map (M) with the 'View Family Characters' icon at the top right.
- Discover an undiscovered node with the "Previous Node" button, then follow the path.

## Best useful tips, chapter 6: hiding characters, speed, energy and pets

Tips from chapter 6 of the Best Useful Tips Summary:

- Hide other players' characters with Shift + F5.
- Run at full speed without holding Shift.
- Disable the "Caution" skill on mounts.
- Activate or disable pet skills and other actions.
- Store leftover energy in Potions by visiting Alustin in Velia.
- The End Game UI lets you look through the inventory of your other characters.
