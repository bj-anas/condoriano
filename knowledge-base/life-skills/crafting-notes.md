# Crafting Notes

Crafting Notes is a window listing the ingredients, materials and products used in life skill activities, with how to obtain them and what they are for.

## Crafting Notes window and how to open it

The Crafting Notes window shows every ingredient, material and product that can be crafted or used in life skills. Open the Adventurer's Board with F2 and click the Crafting Notes header at the top right of the window. The items are arranged in 7 categories, each giving a description and acquisition method for related items, and a search bar finds a specific item.

## Crafting Notes item categories

The 7 Crafting Notes categories are:
- Nodes: materials that workers can get at production nodes, and which nodes provide each one.
- Fishing: items obtained by fishing, other ways to get them and what they are used for.
- Alchemy: products of alchemy, mostly elixirs and magic crystals, with ingredients and uses.
- Cooking: products of cooking, mostly ingredients and dishes, with ingredients and uses.
- Processing: materials and products from processing, with the materials needed and uses.
- Craft House: products crafted at a house by workers, with materials needed and uses.
- Gathering: items obtained by gathering, their gatherable source and uses.
