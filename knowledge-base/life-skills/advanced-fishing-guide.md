# Advanced Fishing Guide

Detailed Fishing life skill information: fishing types, rods and floats, hotspots, fish origin value, fishing speed, mastery and prize catches, Imperial Fishing Delivery, quests, and Shim Cheong's Miraculous Fish Tank.

## Fishing: manual, auto and harpoon types

There are three types of fishing. Manual fishing means catching fish one by one by playing the minigame each cycle. Auto-fishing happens when you do nothing after a fish bites: by default the fish is collected after 3 minutes, and then the character casts again and repeats. Harpoon fishing means catching fish at sea with a harpoon.

## Fishing: harpoon fishing

Harpoon fishing needs a harpoon (made at a town tool workshop or sold on the Central Market) plus a fishing boat or a cog for going to sea. Equip the harpoon, aim at the water, and press Space. When a deep sea fish is detected, a prompt appears to start the minigame.

The harpoon minigame differs from normal fishing. You find the fish's shadow underwater and press Space on it to throw harpoons and weaken the fish. Once it is weak enough, you catch it. You have one minute and 15 harpoons. There is no auto-harpoon fishing.

## Fishing: Triple-Float Fishing Rod

The Triple-Float Fishing Rod has three floats and can catch several fish per attempt, up to 3 to 4 if you are lucky. It is a one-time-use rod that cannot be repaired or enhanced.

You get it as the reward for the follow-up quest to Fish for Villagers, which is available every three days. The quest giver is fisher Abelin, near Velia's storage, and it unlocks after the questline that starts with Igor Bartali, the town chief, with the quest [Fishing] Abelin, the Fishing Boy. You need to be above Lv. 31 or to have finished the Balenos main questline. Abelin's questline is also open to a character above Lv. 10, or with gathering level beginner 6 or higher, that has not yet finished [Fishing] Abelin, the Fishing Boy.

## Fishing: Balenos, Mediah and Calpheon rods and enhancement

The Balenos Fishing Rod shortens auto-fishing to under 3 minutes. For example, a +7 Balenos rod cuts auto-fishing time by 18%, so a fish is caught about 147.6 seconds after the bite. This makes it a top pick for AFK fishing.

The Mediah Fishing Rod and Maple Float raise the chance of rare fish such as coelacanth, porgy, and sea bass, which sell for more. The Calpheon Fishing Rod and Ash Tree Float raise the chance of big fish such as marlin and swordfish, found on far oceans like the Eltro and Zenato seas.

Balenos, Calpheon, Epheria, and Mediah rods and the Ash Tree and Maple floats can be enhanced with black stones, which improves their effects. Balenos and Epheria rods also gain durability when enhanced. The enhancement level can drop after a failed attempt past +7. Force-enhancing with many black stones avoids that risk, but you must then repair the maximum durability of the rod and float. Rare and big fish details are in the knowledge window (hotkey: H) under Life Skill, Fishing.

## Fishing hotspots

A fishing hotspot is an area with a lot of expensive fish, not just many fish. Some are in rivers and along the coast, with a popular one next to Velia, but most are random spots in the ocean. Every open sea hotspot has a circle of seagulls above a small area catching fish.

Sea hotspots are not permanent. They appear and disappear over time, and fishing out the fish can make one vanish. After a while the hotspot comes back. The area next to Velia is a favorite for leaving characters AFK to fish for a long time.

## Fishing: origin and selling value

You sell fish to a Trade Manager or an Imperial Fishing Delivery Manager. Unlike most trade, the nodes between where you caught the fish and where you sell it do not need to be connected. The origin point decides the fish's value: the farther from the origin you sell, the higher the value. But the longer you take to sell, the lower the base value gets, so balance the two.

For fish caught at sea, the origin is divided by water territory and shown as the island nearest that sea area, so the displayed place can differ slightly from where you actually fished. Selling quickly near the origin gives less bonus profit but keeps the full base value.

## Fishing Speed Level

Raising your fishing speed ability level cuts the time for fish to bite, so you catch more in the same time. Check it in your character profile (hotkey: P) under abilities. Level 5 cuts the bite time by 50%, so reaching 5 is a good goal.

You can raise it with dishes, elixirs, certain clothes, and guild skills. The guild skill is simplest, but you must be in a guild, so ask what level of Art of Fishing the guild has. Silver embroidered fisher's clothes enhanced to +2 give 2 levels. Guild skill (3 levels) plus clothes (2 levels) reach level 5. Without a guild skill, use dishes and elixirs: the Balenos meal raises fishing speed ability level by 2 for 90 minutes.

## Fishing mastery and prize catches

For fishing, mastery raises the chance of a prize catch, which includes prize catch fish and ancient relic crystal shards. It does not raise the chance of rare fish. Prize catch fish are among the rarest, found inland and at sea, with rods and with harpoons.

With a fishing rod, higher mastery simply raises the chance. With a harpoon, you need a minimum mastery just to find prize catch fish, and further thresholds raise your prize catch harpoon level, up to 3.

## Fishing chairs for mastery

Equipping a Fishing Chair is an easy way to raise Fishing Mastery.

| Chair | Repair | How to get it |
| Crio's Sturdy Fishing Chair | Cannot be repaired | Exchange Discarded Glass Bottle x5 with NPC Crio or Liana |
| Loggia Fishing Chair | Can be repaired | Buy from NPC Camellia Loggia or Zaaira |
| Crio Fisher's Chair | Can be repaired | Buy from NPC Geranoa or Zaaira |
| Manos Fishing Chair | Can be repaired | Manufacture with Sturdy Timber Square x10, Manos Stone x5, Magical Shard x50, Tear of the Great Ocean x10 |

Tear of the Great Ocean comes from finishing the repeatable quest [Life Skill Mastery] Fisherman and the Sea.

## Fishing: Imperial Fishing Delivery

Imperial Fishing Delivery Manager NPCs in or near wharfs always buy fish at 250% of market price, and the distance bonus from origin also applies. Each manager has a limit on how many of each fish it takes. When the requested quantity reaches 0 the NPC stops buying that fish. You compete with other Adventurers to sell the most to each manager. If an NPC is full, change servers or wait for the next cycle.

A popular AFK spot is the hotspot beside Velia, selling to Velia's Imperial Fishing Delivery Manager. If it is full, another one is at Glish in Serendia. Deliveries also give Golden Seals - [Imperial Fishing] at a certain rate, which are exchanged for items. The wiki has a separate Golden Seals page.

## Fishing quests and leap quests

Many NPCs give fishing quests. The wiki recommends starting with those in Velia. They take effort but reward contribution EXP, fishing EXP, and rare items such as sharp black crystal shards.

Leap quests help you reach a higher fishing level and unlock at beginner Lv. 10. Summon the Black Spirit and open the guide menu to see the leap quests for all life skills and be guided to the NPC. After certain fishing leap quests, the Honor to the Fishing Family quests unlock and give useful items.

## Shim Cheong's Miraculous Fish Tank

Fish stored in Shim Cheong's Miraculous Fish Tank get five times the price guarantee duration. With the tank in your bag, fished fish go in automatically, and you can sell them to trade managers or deliver them for Imperial Fishing Delivery without taking them out. Fish taken out have their price guarantee duration cut by five times.

Limits: only fish with a price guarantee duration can be stored, and storing is blocked if your bag weight exceeds 100%. An empty tank can be moved to town storages connected by maids or the Magnus, but a tank holding fish cannot be moved. You get it by finishing fishing quests from NPC Dorio at the Sea Palace, each needing a green-grade fish. Iridescent Shellfish is no longer needed.
