# Workers

Workers are NPCs who gather materials at production nodes and craft items in town workshops for a player's family. This page covers hiring, firing, types, tasks, stamina, promotion, skills and the Worker Exchange.

## Workers: hiring and firing

Workers are hired through a Work Supervisor NPC, found in all cities and some towns. Choosing Contract Workers costs 5 energy and shows a random worker in the Worker of the Day window. Press Hire Worker to hire, or View Another for another 5 energy to see a different one. View Continuously lets you set a minimum grade and how many workers to check, and stops once a worker of that grade appears. If your energy runs out, you can draw on other family members' energy. Hiring also costs silver, depending on the worker's grade.

Some worker NPCs in the world offer their services after you build a friendly relationship. These are always yellow grade, with fixed race, stats and talents.

To fire a worker, open the Worker List (World Map hotkey M icon at lower right, or Esc, then Life, then Worker List), tick the worker, and press the person-with-an-X icon. Confirm with Yes; fired workers cannot be recovered. The Worker List can be given its own hotkey under Settings, Interface Settings, UI Hotkeys.

## Workers: lodging

Each town gives you one worker by default, and your first worker in any town needs no lodging. To keep more workers in a town, you need worker lodging, which is made by repurposing a purchased house; the lodging is where your hired workers live. See the Houses page of the wiki for how repurposing works.

## Workers: races and types

Worker race affects speed and stamina. Goblins are the fastest in work and movement but have low stamina, so they repeat tasks fewer times. Giants have high stamina and a Production Increase talent that lets them carry more harvested items from a node, but they are slow. Humans are balanced between the two.

In Kamasylvia, papus are similar to goblins, and faduses are similar to humans but with the giants' talent. Dwarves hired in O'dyllita are similar to goblins. In the Land of the Morning Light, dokkebi, dolswe and shellfolk correspond to goblins, humans and giants, from fastest to slowest. Dokkebi can also craft special items in the Dokkebi Jar workshops and work some dokkebi-only nodes. Workers from other regions such as Balenos cannot work in the Land of the Morning Light, and the reverse also applies.

## Workers: grades

Workers come in five grades by stats and quality:
- Naive (white): lowest stats, only giants have this grade.
- Normal (green, no title): slightly better than naive.
- Skilled (blue).
- Professional (yellow): much higher stats.
- Artisan (orange): the highest stats.

Over the long term, getting many professional and artisan workers is recommended. Befriending field worker NPCs gives easy access to yellow professional workers by raising amity through conversation, but these workers cannot be promoted to artisan because they cannot take the promotion test.

## Workers: production node tasks and stamina

Workers can be sent to production nodes to collect resources. If the node has been invested in with contribution points, the task can repeat up to 50,000 times, but stamina limits this. Each task uses stamina, and a worker with none cannot work.

To restore stamina, press Recover next to the worker's name in the Worker List, or Recover All at the bottom, then choose food such as beer or grilled bird meat. The Recover All Workers window also has an option to auto-recover by feeding workers from the family inventory. Auto-recovery steps in when a worker's stamina reaches 3, in the sorting order of recovery items in the family inventory, and a notification appears when none are left.

## Workers: town workshops, investment banks and gardens

Besides nodes, workers can do three other kinds of work.

Town workshops: the worker crafts in a purchased house set as a workshop. Materials must be in the storage of the town where the worker is based, not where the workshop is.

Investment banks: workers sent to banks in certain cities earn silver. They need gold bars, bought through the Currency Exchange option at any Storage Keeper, and nearly the full price is retained apart from a small NPC profit. You must own the top-ranked house in that town; house rank depends on furniture decoration points.

Gardens: a worker assigned to a fenced garden prunes tangled branches and catches insects, which otherwise slow crop growth.

## Workers: Worker List and promotion tests

The Worker List lets you stop or repeat tasks, and shows each worker's current task, the time left and the number of repeats. Each time a worker's level rises by 10, they can take an 8-hour promotion test, and several workers can test at once. A pass raises the grade, for example skilled to professional. Success depends only on the grade being promoted from. Results arrive in the Mail window (hotkey B).

| Promotion | Success chance |
| Normal to Skilled | 90% |
| Skilled to Professional | 70% |
| Professional to Artisan | 50% |

## Workers: skills

Workers learn skills from level 1 and gain new skills every 5 levels, reaching 10 skills at level 40. Skills are mostly random. Work speed and moving speed skills help node gatherers, and packaging skills turn items such as herbs, ore, mushrooms and fish into trade goods quickly for extra profit, which is highly valued. Every worker learns Delivery, which transports goods from the node to a town storage you choose.

A worker who passes a promotion test returns to level 1 with only 1 skill and learns more as they level. Befriended yellow workers have fixed skills. At level 30 or above with more than 20% EXP, you can spend 20% EXP to replace one skill with a random one, and repeat whenever EXP allows.

## Workers: Worker Exchange

The Worker Exchange is used through the Worker Supervisor NPC. You can register a standby worker for sale or hire workers from other players. The basic silver cost depends on grade and rises with the worker's levels and skills. Before hiring, check the worker's skills, home town, and for workers below artisan, how many promotion tests they have left. Some workers, such as those from the forests of Kamasylvia, cannot be exchanged.
