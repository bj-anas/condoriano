# Life Skill Overview

Overview of the non-combat life skill activities in Black Desert, how to begin them, how life skill levels and titles work, and how the skills connect to each other.

## Life skills: how to start

Life skills are activities separate from combat, and you can try them while leveling a character. Before NPCs will hand out life skill quests, enable the Life and Fishing quest filter at the bottom of the Quests window (hotkey: O). With that filter off, no life skill quests are given. Life skill quests show as a green box on the icon above an NPC's head.

Next, summon the Black Spirit (hotkey: ,) and open the Guide option. The "I want to learn" entries there point you with the navigation guide to the quest giver and tell you the requirements.

You do not need the introductory quests at all. You can buy a tool, for example a gathering tool from an NPC shop, and start any life skill whenever you know how to do it. Life skills give silver, combat and contribution EXP, and useful items.

## Life skills: types of activities

The life skills described on the official wiki are Gathering, Fishing, Hunting, Cooking, Alchemy, Processing, Training, Trading, Farming, Sailing, and Bartering.

- Gathering: the basic life skill, using different tools for different resources. Better tools shorten gathering time, and higher levels reduce the energy spent.
- Fishing: catch fish with rods. A higher fishing level lets you equip more rods and catch more kinds of fish.
- Hunting: hunt wild animals. A higher hunting level unlocks better matchlocks.
- Cooking: needs a cooking utensil installed in your residence. A higher level allows a wider variety of dishes.
- Alchemy: needs an alchemy tool installed in your residence. It is considered difficult, so gather or process ingredients first. A higher level allows more elixirs.

## Life skills: processing, training, trading, farming and sailing

Further life skills on the official wiki page:

- Processing: changes the shape or properties of items. Options are shaking, grinding, chopping, drying, filtering, heating, repairing, simple alchemy and cooking, imperial cuisine and alchemy, guild processing, and manufacture. Higher levels increase the number and types of items produced.
- Training: level up mounts, train their skills, or capture wild horses. Higher training levels make training a mount easier.
- Trading: buy and sell goods through trade managers at nodes and towns. Long-distance trade costs many contribution points and prices fluctuate. Higher trading levels give access to more trade goods.
- Farming: set up fences for a garden, plant seeds, harvest crops, improve crop quality, and graze cows. Higher levels reduce the energy used.
- Sailing: expertise at sea. Higher levels unlock more ship skills.
- Bartering: swap trade goods at sea for better and pricier goods, mainly for profit and for ship upgrade materials.

## Life skill levels and titles

Each life skill levels up as you earn EXP from doing it. The level title usually changes every 10 levels. The titles in order are Beginner, Apprentice, Skilled, Professional, Artisan, Master, and Guru. For example, finishing the EXP of Beginner Lv. 10 moves you to Apprentice Lv. 1.

Completing challenges (hotkey: Y) as you rise through levels gives rewards. At certain levels each life skill also unlocks leap quests. You can advance without doing them, but they give a good amount of bonus EXP.

Life skill EXP is shared across all characters on your account. If your Warrior is Professional 5 in Fishing, every other character on the account is Professional 5 in Fishing too.

## Life skills: how they combine

Life skills depend on each other. Fish and gathered goods often need processing before use. Hunting also gives gathering results. Farming crops are common ingredients for cooking and alchemy. Fish can be sold to a trade manager, and sailing and bartering go together.

Gathering and processing are especially linked to cooking and alchemy, because their ingredients can be gathered or made through processing. As an example, high-level processing items require leather glaze and metal solvent, which are made through alchemy, combined with other materials. Producing such items can therefore involve gathering, processing, and alchemy in several steps.
