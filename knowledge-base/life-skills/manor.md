# Manor

Manors are large rentable residences with interior and outdoor areas for decorating, in Serendia and the Land of the Morning Light. This page covers where they are, how to rent and return them, rental costs and furniture.

## Manor overview

A manor is bigger than a regular house and has both an interior and outdoor areas, so much of its decorating is outside, and some also have a terrace area. Manor-exclusive furniture includes grass and flowers for the garden, lighting for the night, a wide choice of trees, sculptures and fountains. The space allows things like hosting a concert in the garden, a mock dueling circle, or expanding fields into farms. Manors listed on the wiki are Blue Maned Lion's Manor, Shimhyangje, Solbaram Neowa House and Hyeollokdang.

## Manor Blue Maned Lion's Manor location and rental

The Blue Maned Lion's Manor stands east along the Demi River in front of Heidel, before the Eastern Border. It has a wide yard for sculptures, trees, flowers and fountains, and an interior with high ceilings. After a few simple quests it can be rented for 5 contribution points plus 1,000,000 silver per day.

To unlock the rental, finish the Calpheon main questline (Looking for Adventurers or [Boss] Witch of Horrors) or the season character simplified questline up to chapter 6. The Black Spirit then offers [Manor] Crucio Domongatt's Summon. After that, go to the manor on the Eastern Border and use the purchase button at the door; the 5 contribution points are returned when the manor is returned.

## Manor renting contract steps and discounts

After investing the 5 contribution points at the door, press R to open it and make the contract. The contract length is set from 1 day to 365 days with the gear icon next to the contract period. Rent is 1,000,000 silver per day for the Blue Maned Lion's Manor, with discounts for contracts of 30 days or more. Returning the manor before the end refunds the silver for the remaining time, minus some fees. Hover over the question mark button left of the usage amount to see the usage cost discount and the return fee.

## Manor Land of the Morning Light locations and unlock quests

Shimhyangje, Solbaram Neowa House and Hyeollokdang are three manors in the Land of the Morning Light, different from the Blue Maned Lion's Manor. Find each by pressing the manor icon on the World Map (hotkey M). Each rents for 5 contribution points plus silver by rental period.

Unlock quests: Shimhyangje needs [LoML] Ruler of Taebaek; Solbaram Neowa House needs [LoML] Tale of the Officers (Dokkebi's Gift); Hyeollokdang needs all [Traveler] quests completed. These come from the Land of the Morning Light main questline, covered on the wiki's Collection of Tales page.

## Manor Shimhyangje and Solbaram Neowa House rent and discounts

Rent for Shimhyangje is 1,000,000 silver per day, and for Solbaram Neowa House 100,000 silver per day, paid from My Silver. Discounts by rental period:

| Rental period | Discount |
| Less than 30 days | None |
| More than 30 and less than 90 days | 5% |
| More than 90 and less than 180 days | 8% |
| More than 180 and less than 365 days | 12% |
| 365 days | 20% |

## Manor returning and expiry

A manor returned early gives a partial refund of the silver through the Mail window (hotkey B), proportional to the remaining days. When a rental expires, the manor can no longer be used and the items inside go to storage in a fixed town:
- Heidel storage for the Blue Maned Lion's Manor
- Dalbeol Village storage for Shimhyangje
- Nampo Moodle Village storage for Solbaram Neowa House
- Yukjo storage for Hyeollokdang

The page lists deduction rates, for a rent of 1,000,000 silver per day, by period: under 30 days 15%, more than 30 and under 90 days 20%, more than 90 and under 180 days 23%, more than 180 and under 365 days 27%, and 365 days 35%.

## Manor furniture and where to find it

After the rental quests, many more quests across the world reward manor furniture. For the Blue Maned Lion's Manor, the wiki has separate furniture pages: outdoor furniture and sculptures, indoor furniture and lighting, items usable both indoors and outdoors, the Gránan Snow White Collection and World Boss Furniture. For Shimhyangje and Solbaram Neowa House, see Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 1 and Part 2.
