# Trading

Guide to the Trading life skill. General trade has been temporarily disabled since the January 17, 2024 update, so most of the mechanics here describe the system as the wiki documents it.

## Trading: temporarily disabled

After the January 17, 2024 update, Trading has been temporarily disabled while the life skill is being revamped. Town Trade Managers no longer deal in general trade goods, and the Old Moon Guild Newsletter that announced trade news is gone. Gathering trade goods no longer puts a load on your back. Six weekly Trading quests were removed: Parade of Research, Luxuries of the Altinovan Nobles, Dreaming Little Girl, Must-Haves for Calpheonian Madams, Urgent Request from Calpheon Workshop, and Redeeming the Good Name of Xian.

Imperial Crafting Delivery and Imperial Fishing Delivery, which share the idea of selling goods for 250% of price, are separate and covered on other pages.

## Trading: changed restrictions on carrying trade goods

With general trade suspended, trade goods restrictions were changed for these contents:

- Using the Magnus, a Maid or Butler, a Campsite, or the Guild Rally Command
- Character transport between towns
- Using Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass or Telescope, or an Archaeologist's or Traveler's Map
- Entering Elvia Realm, Old Moon Grand Prix, Red Battlefield, or the Free Arena
- Using the Trainer's Flute with trade goods on a mount
- Storing a mount that carries trade goods in Krogdalo's Sanctuary
- Storing trade goods in Clorince's Travel Bag

## Trading: what it is and why it exists

Trade means exchanging trade goods with certain NPCs. You get trade goods by defeating monsters, completing quests, buying from Trade Manager NPCs, gathering, or crafting at a town workshop. Fish are also trade goods. Silver, the game's currency, is produced by defeating monsters, selling items, and exchanges, and consumed by NPC shop purchases and Central Market commission. The wiki says silver production is slightly ahead of consumption, and trade is one way to earn silver without expert knowledge.

Every trade good has an origin point. A distance bonus is calculated between the origin point and the NPC you sell to, so selling to a farther trader pays more. The origin node and the selling town must be connected for full value, so you must also invest in nodes and consider travel time.

## Trading: buying, selling and order contract logs

Trade goods can be bought only at designated nodes and sold only in each territory's main town or city. You cannot sell in the territory where you bought them. After purchase, goods go into that node's order contract log and can be retrieved to your inventory or mount inventory at any time.

A one-time standby fee is charged right after purchase, plus another fee every 4 hours, shown next to Storage Fee. This keeps the goods' price from changing. The fee is deducted when you retrieve the items. A node's log can hold at most 20,000LT. Each trade good weighs 300LT, so that is around 66 goods per node, and only 5 different kinds of items can be stored per location. Goods are separated by item type and remaining warranty time, so the same item bought at different times takes different slots.

Manage a node's order contract log by interacting with the trade manager there.

## Trading: node connection penalty and distance bonus

If the node between a trade good's origin and the place it is sold is not connected, the good sells for only 30% of the original price. You also cannot buy goods from trade managers in unconnected isolated nodes. Fish are exempt from this penalty and sell at the right price anywhere.

A distance bonus grows with the distance between the origin point and sale point. Selling far away pays more, but you must weigh travel time and the contribution points needed to connect nodes. Fish are not hit by the connection penalty but do get the distance bonus.

## Trading: market price changes

Market price is the price at which goods are bought and sold. It rises or falls by probability at six fixed times a day (UTC): 02:00, 06:00, 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 22:00. The most it can move is 15% of base value. Ancient gold coins and other general trade goods stay fixed. Fish can rise up to 130% and fall as low as 80%.

Goods bought from Trade Manager NPCs can have purchase and sale price rise up to 300% (three times base) and fall as low as 30%. These fluctuations apply equally to all servers.

## Trading: special price fluctuation

A special price fluctuation can follow the regular 15% change at the same six daily update times, 02:00, 06:00, 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 22:00 (UTC). Current prices can then rise up to 200% or fall as low as 50%. Prices change per territory and per category: Raw Material, Clothing, Relic, Military Supply, Medicine, Food, Luxury, and General Goods.

Rumors in the Old Moon Guild Newsletter window (main menu Esc, Life tab F6) can hint at changes. They may apply only 4 hours after circulating, or be wrong. You can also listen to town residents, for example the crowd by the eagle statue in Calpheon. Fluctuations can happen even when no rumor was heard.

## Trading: price guarantee time

The price guarantee period is a trade good's expiry. Goods bought sit in the node's order contract log with a 7-day price guarantee. If not retrieved in that time, they are sold at 25% of the original price, minus the standby fee.

Once you retrieve goods, they have a 3-hour validity regardless of the contract period. During it, price drops do not affect them. You cannot return retrieved goods to the log, so sell quickly. To sell in other territories, right-click them out of the log into your inventory or mount, which cuts the guarantee to 3 hours.

Fish depreciate gradually to 0% of the original price after 24 hours and then expire. Fish from near the Mountain of Eternal Winter last longer because of the cold.

## Trading: bargain game, stock and purchase conditions

Bargain game: a minigame against the Trade Manager NPC you sell to. Each attempt costs 5 energy, and success sells trade goods and fish at a higher price. A higher trading level means higher bargain profit.

Stock: the maximum quantity of a trade good you can buy from a Trade Manager NPC at once. It is 50 for all items at a node and refreshes six times a day (UTC): 02:00, 06:00, 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 22:00.

Purchase conditions: some goods require trading levels of skilled 1, professional 1, and artisan 1. Each node has restricted goods unlocked at these levels.

## Trading: remaining quantity and Mysterious Merchant Guild

Remaining quantity is the limit on what can be delivered. Normal trading has no limit. For imperial fishing delivery, the server quantity resets every 3 hours from 00:00 (UTC). For imperial crafting delivery, the limit is per family, not per server, set at 1/2 of your maximum contribution points per day.

Mysterious Merchant Guild event: a large mysterious merchant guild buys up cheap goods and resells later. If market prices remain within 40% of the original price across three market cycles (12 hours), purchases are restricted. Goods cannot be bought and the market price does not fluctuate for 3 days, though goods already bought can be sold. After 3 days, prices reset to the original price.

## Trading byproduct: stained silk

Selling trade goods to Trade Manager NPCs can give an item called stained silk at a set probability. Liana in any city exchanges Stained Silk x10 for Trace of Nature x30, or for 900 Contribution EXP plus Trading EXP x450.

## Trading by foot, mount and transport

- By foot: carrying a backpack slows you in proportion to its weight, so only short trips are recommended. Backpack trading raises the strength part of your fitness, based on distance traveled, not weight, so you can buy one item and autopath safely in a town.
- Mount trade: load goods on a donkey, horse, wagon, boat, camel, and so on. Wagons are most common because of storage space, but long desert trips need a miniature elephant.
- Transport trade: moving crate-type items through town transportation. Trade boxes crafted in your residence (costing contribution points) can be moved, often crafted far away and sold for the distance bonus. Transport can be done from the World Map (hotkey: M).

## Trading: crafting wagons

The best trading wagon, the wonderful forest path wagon with 22 inventory slots, is hard to get. Beginners can craft a noble wagon or merchant wagon. You need contribution points to buy a house for a wagon workshop and workers to craft. Wagons are consumables, so craft more than one. The merchant wagon is crafted in Altinova house no. 5-8, and the noble wagon in various towns, such as Calpheon City workshop district 1-6, first floor. That house costs 3 contribution points, but unlocking it by buying every required house costs up to 11. Some materials need the Processing - Beginner knowledge.

## Trading: imperial delivery and processed items

Imperial delivery supplies goods the imperial court wants: horses, packaged dishes and elixirs, and fish. Packaged dishes, elixirs, and fish sell at 250% of market price. Imperial crafting delivery resets daily (server time); if the quantity still shows 0 after midnight, relog or change server. Fish delivery quantity resets every 3 hours from 00:00 (UTC) on all servers.

Trading processed items means selling crates crafted at a town workshop (wood, mineral, crop, mushroom, fish workbenches). The item's origin is the worker's home village, not the workshop's location, and materials must be in the worker's home town storage (crafting is faster if the workshop is in that town). Beer to workers gives unlimited workforce.

## Trading quests

At trading life skill level apprentice 2, visit Alustin in Velia, Balenos, who offers [Trading Leap Apprentice 2] Alchemist's Reagent. Leap quests give a significant amount of life skill experience and introduce related characters and activities. As you progress through trading leap quests and levels, the once-per-family Honor to the Trading Family quests unlock, with rewards that help trading. Summon the Black Spirit and open its guide menu to find leap quests quickly.
