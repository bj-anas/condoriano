# Crafting

How crafting works in Black Desert: workers craft items in town workshops, with a tool workshop (general crafting) and a shipyard (large scale crafting) as worked examples, plus the Complete Now silver costs.

## Crafting overview and the two types

Materials from gathering and processing are used in town workshops and workbenches to craft items such as weapons, armor, reform stones, decorations, clothes, wagons, ships and trade goods. There are two types. General crafting finishes an item with a single work task and is used for gathering tools, equipment pieces and similar items. Large scale crafting needs multiple work tasks and is used for ship licenses and wagon registrations. In Black Desert the player does not craft personally; a hired worker does it at a workshop or workbench.

## Crafting requirements: materials, contribution points and workers

Crafting needs three things: the materials, contribution points to invest in houses, and workers.

Materials come from gathering, processing, or other players via the Central Market. Press F2 for the Adventurer's Board and use the Crafting Notes button in its top right corner to search which materials an item needs. For an item not listed there, the recipe is shown at the workshop or workbench itself.

Contribution points buy the workshops, workbenches and worker lodging, and are invested in production nodes. They are earned by finishing most quests.

Workers are hired from the work supervisor of a town or city. You get one worker per town by default, and more need lodging made by repurposing a purchased house.

## Crafting general crafting example: purchasing a tool workshop

The guide's general crafting example is a lumbering axe, which needs a tool workshop. Click Heidel on the World Map (hotkey M) and use the search tab at the top right to find a level 3 tool workshop. A blue arrow marks matching houses. Click the house, select Tool Workshop, then press Purchase: Tool Workshop.

Buying a house costs contribution points and silver, and some houses require earlier ones first; for example, 5-3 may require buying 5-1, F1 first. The contribution points act as a deposit and come back when the house is returned.

## Crafting general crafting example: preparing materials

After switching a house to a tool workshop, press Manage Crafting to list craftable items. Gray items mean the selected worker's town storage lacks materials. Workers use materials from the storage of their own town, not from your inventory or another town's storage.

A lumbering axe needs Iron Ingot x10, Steel x3, Black Stone Powder x5 and Maple Timber x1.
- Maple Timber: cut ash trees around Heidel with a lumbering axe from a material vendor.
- Steel: Heating (Processing) with Melted Iron Shard x5 and Coal x5, both found in the mine near Heidel's entrance.
- Iron Ingot: Heating (Processing) with Melted Iron Shard x10.
- Black Stone Powder: the refinery at Heidel 5-1, 1F, or grinding Black Stones in Processing.

All of these can also be bought on the Central Market. Put them in the storage of the worker's town; in the example, Amerigo the storage keeper in Heidel.

## Crafting hiring a worker and starting a general craft

Hire a worker from the work supervisor, Joel in Heidel in the example. Searching for a worker costs 5 energy, hiring costs a small amount of silver, and you need free lodging space. Extra lodging is bought the same way as the workshop.

With workshop, materials and worker ready, pick the product, press Change to set the repeat count and confirm. The Estimated Work Time shows the time per craft. A worker can be set to at most 50,000 repeats, and stops when the work is done, when materials run out or when stamina is depleted. Materials leave the storage the moment the task starts, and canceling later, even a second after Start Work, does not return them. Finished products appear in the town's storage.

## Crafting Complete Now function

## Crafting large scale crafting example: shipyard and materials

Large scale crafting needs several work tasks per product. The guide's example is a ship license. A shipyard is needed; in Velia it is on the second floor of 3-2, found by clicking Velia on the World Map and using the search tab.

Large scale crafts need many more materials, and the Manage Crafting window shows only one product at a time; use the arrow at its top right to switch products, for instance from the rowboat license to the raft license. For Ship License: Raft, the materials are logs and Black Stone Powder, the number beside each material is the work tasks it covers, and the Detailed Work Information shows how many you have against how many one task uses. It needs 25 logs and 3 Black Stone Powder, making 28 work tasks in total, shown as the required work count at the top.
- Log: from cutting any tree with a lumbering axe.
- Black Stone Powder: the refinery at Velia 3-1, or grinding Black Stones in Processing.

Materials must be in the storage of the town the workers are from, because workers cannot move between towns.

## Crafting large scale crafting workers and starting

Large scale crafting can use several workers at once, so hire more from the town's Work Supervisor NPC (and buy more lodging if needed) to finish faster. Workers with large scale crafting skills, or shipbuilding and wagon-making skills such as Mount Knowledge, work faster.

To start, pick a task on the left and a worker on the right, set the task count and press Start Work. Be careful: the shipyard cannot craft anything else until the project is finished. Only one of each material needs to be ready to begin, since one task uses 1 log, so a worker can start with 1 log in storage. Other workers can gather materials meanwhile. As in general crafting, canceling a started project does not return materials already used. The finished license appears in the town storage. The same process crafts equipment for characters and mounts, trade goods, furniture, enhancement and reform items, gathering tools and siege weapons.
