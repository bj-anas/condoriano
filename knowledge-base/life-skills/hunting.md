# Hunting

Guide to the Hunting life skill: matchlocks and gear, solo hunting areas, cooperative bosses (blue whales, fugitive khalks, Rawr-Rawr), sniper rifles, hunting mastery, and bonus quests.

## Hunting: basics and how to spot hunting monsters

Hunting is a life skill for killing wild animals, with progress shown on your character profile (hotkey: P). New hunters start at Beginner 1. Hunting monsters show a deer-like icon on the minimap, and normal weapons do very little damage to them, so they must be shot with a hunting matchlock. Each hunt earns hunting life skill experience that raises your hunting level.

Big hunting bosses such as blue whales and fugitive khalks need several Adventurers, a matchlock, and a land mount or ship, so beginners can join.

## Hunting matchlock: how to get one and use it

Hunting matchlocks are not sold in NPC shops. Rent one from an NPC, craft it at a house used as a tool workshop (the workshop level decides which matchlock you can make), or buy it on the Central Market under Life Tools, then Matchlock.

Controls: right-click to reload, left-click to fire. Press Q to crouch, which raises range and reload speed. Roll away from attacks with A, S, or D plus Shift. W plus Shift while crouched stands you up and runs. Some matchlocks have a strong F-key attack at 100% black spirit rage. You can also use a matchlock while mounted. Matchlocks can be enhanced, but the level drops on failure after +7.

## Hunting: mounts and butcher knife

A mount matters for the bigger targets. Blue whales are hunted in open sea from a ship, where one person steers and the others shoot. Fugitive khalks fly around the continent, so you chase them on horseback. For high-damage targets like khalks, giant lionesses, and elephants, horseback hunting makes it easier to dodge while reloading.

A butcher knife is essential. After killing a hunting monster you must gather from the corpse with it, and that gathering is what gives hunting life skill experience. Any butcher knife works; they differ in gathering speed and durability.

## Hunting: helpful dishes and elixirs

Reloading leaves you defenseless, so reload speed matters. Hunter's salad and fresh hunter's salad increase matchlock reload speed. Hunter's salad can use crocodile meat from freshwater crocodiles in Lake Kaia. Khalk's fermented wine is even better, adding attack power and movement speed on top of reload speed. Buy it on the Central Market or cook it with a fugitive khalk's horn.

Elixirs that raise critical hit level or critical hit rate help kill high-health targets faster. Any dish, elixir, draught, perfume, or scroll that increases life EXP gain levels hunting faster so you unlock stronger matchlocks. Beginners do not need to overdo preparations.

## Hunting: beginner quests and repair islands

The fastest way for beginners to level hunting is quests. In the Quest window (hotkey: O), the Suggested tab lists a beginner hunting questline and hunting leap quests that appear as levels rise. The Recurring tab lists daily hunting quests in Balenos and the islands beyond. At some point quests send you to more dangerous areas, so you may switch to other hunting.

Start with [Daily] Easy Game from Chuck Laurie near Olvia. After his first quests, Chief Igor Bartali in Velia gives a hunting questline, which unlocks many hunting quests across the Balenos islands. Bring a sea mount such as a fishing boat or sailboat. Rewards include titles and matchlocks.

Monsters on the Balenos islands are easy except the king brown bear (knockback attack) and the boss wolf. Giant wolves and boss wolves summon wolf cubs. Matchlock durability can hit 0, and node managers on these islands can repair items: Rubiano, Anjou, Marka, Invernen, Orfs, and Baremi Island.

## Hunting: Balenos islands and Balenos Mountains

Balenos islands: after the questline, daily hunting quests unlock. For repeatable ones, open the Recurring tab and look under [Hunting Specialist] Tracks of a Hunter. Many hunting quests reward hunter's tokens, which Igor Bartali in Velia exchanges for rewards.

Balenos Mountains: the easiest and most comfortable place for beginners, close to Olvia and Velia. Three land hunting monsters live there: giant elks, giant boars, and giant brown bears, each marked with the Wild title. Elks flee as you approach, so they are good practice targets. Boars and bears attack anyone who comes near, with modest attack power. Butchering them can give a stuffed head hunting trophy that you install in a residence for a buff (each type differs) or sell on the Central Market.

## Hunting: Lake Kaia and coastal seas

Lake Kaia in Calpheon has freshwater crocodiles. Do not equip the matchlock on arrival, because equipping slows your movement and nearby catfishmen (around levels 49-50) can hurt you. At Lv. 55 or higher the catfishmen will not bother you. After a kill, climb on the carcass to butcher it for hunting experience and meat. North Kaia Lake holds 3 freshwater crocodiles and South Kaia Lake holds 4. Their respawn is long, so hop between servers.

Coastal seas of Calpheon, Balenos, and Mediah have humpback whales, found from a fishing boat or sailboat. They never attack mounts or characters and float when killed, so climb on the carcass to butcher. Rewards include sharp black crystal shards and black gems that enhance life skill mastery equipment.

## Hunting: Quint Hill and Areha Palm Forest

Quint Hill, north of Calpheon City, has giant foxes and giant wolves. Both attack first and wolves call extra wolves when hit, so kill them quickly. They can drop stuffed heads.

Areha Palm Forest, near the town of Arehaza at the eastern end of Valencia, has giant lionesses, giant lions, and giant mountain sheep to the north. It takes effort to reach through the Valencian desert, so the hunting experience is good. Lions and lionesses have large health pools; aim for back attacks while dodging. Lion meat is an ingredient for a hamburger dish. Butchering these also gives stuffed head trophies.

## Hunting: Balenos boss hunting quests

Hunter's tokens can be taken to Igor Bartali, the chief of Velia, to buy scrolls for boss subjugation quests, which then become daily quests. Only characters of Lv. 50 or higher can do them. Rewards are hunting experience, contribution experience, and gold bars.

Buy the trap item from Zaaira, the Material Vendor NPC in Velia, to craft the quest's trap. You can use the trap to lure the two bosses even without the quest. Use the trap for the white wolf boss at Agris Steppe, and the one for the king brown bear boss at Fleme Hills. They have high health, so bring plenty of potions and spare matchlocks. Their stuffed heads give strong buffs and sell for a high price on the Central Market.

## Hunting: king griffon in Kamasylvia

The king griffon is a boss hunting monster in the Ash Forest of northwestern Kamasylvia, a very dangerous area. Its appearance shows on the hunting boss UI icon. It moves clockwise along a route in Ash Forest, and a yellow griffon icon marks it on the World Map (hotkey: M).

It has two stages: first it flies and attacks, then it lands and fights on the ground. Hunt it on horseback. A horse with the Sideways skill can stay mounted all fight. Otherwise a griffon attack can kill the horse, so dismount after the first stage. Butchering gives an intact griffon beak, used to make the boss armor griffon's helmet. Instead of a stuffed head you might get a griffon chandelier, which gives no buff but lets you blow out and light the candles.

## Hunting: Khimut Great Forest in Drieghan

Around the Khimut Great Forest in northeastern Drieghan, between the Khimut and Dormann lumber camps, you hunt several animals. Near Khimut Lumber Camp (east) are Drieghanese male and female goats. They roam in herds, and attacking a female makes nearby males aggressive, so check your surroundings first. Near Dormann Lumber Camp (west) are yaks and llamas. Llamas are timid and flee, but yaks charge you, so dodge in time. Stuffed head trophies can drop, and llamas can even give a whole stuffed body trophy.

## Hunting: blue whales

Blue whale hunts are cooperative. A matchlock-shaped icon at the bottom right of the screen with a (+) means a hunting boss such as a blue whale, fugitive khalk, or king griffon has appeared in that channel. Bosses differ per server, so share information and check several servers.

Blue whales appear at regular intervals three times a day per channel. They are fast, so one party member drives a fast boat (fishing boat or sailboat) while the rest shoot. Their routes run through the Calpheon, Balenos, and Mediah seas. Because of their huge health, only the top 20 participating parties get butchering rights, and each party member gets one butchering chance. Many hunters are professional whale hunters, so watch server chat and join a party.

## Hunting: fugitive khalks

Fugitive khalks show up in southwestern Calpheon around Longleaf Tree Forest, and in Mediah near Tungrad Forest and Hasrah Cliff. They fly a fixed path, so follow on horseback with the matchlock equipped while mounted. They appear twice a day per server at regular intervals. The route is short and easy to follow, but you must watch the sky and steer the horse at once. As with blue whales, only the top 20 parties can butcher.

## Hunting: Rawr-Rawr in Narcion

Rawr-Rawr is the king of the grass rhinos of O'dyllita, found in Narcion. Its tough, soil-covered hide is famous.

- Guaranteed items: Rhino Meat, Rhino Blood, Rhino Hide, Fire Horn, Caphras Stone, Sharp Black Crystal Shard, Black Gem Fragment, Black Gem, Spirit Pouch of Ferocious Beast.
- Set probability: Breath of Narcion, Rawr-Rawr's Heart, Red Earth Bloodstone, Concentrated Magical Black Gem.

At Dead Moon Post, NPC Lejenti trades mythical feathers for red earth bloodstones. Rawr-Rawr's Heart is processed into Gauche Rawr-Rawr's Heart, which gives +50 durability to a matchlock when infused. Recipe: Rawr-Rawr's Heart x1, Forest Fury x3, Magical Shard x10, Black Stone x100, Steel x200.

A master matchlock reformed with the heart is renamed and cannot be sold to NPC shops or listed on the Central Market. A mirror of equilibrium extracts the [Hunting] Gauche Master Matchlock into [Hunting] Master Matchlock x1 and Steel x1.

## Hunting: sniper rifles

Sniper rifles shoot from long range at weak spots. Results depend on equipment power, hunting mastery, and your skill in the loading and aiming minigame. Use them like matchlocks by pressing RMB while crouched. You must be at least Lv. 30.

- [Hunting] Practice Sniper Rifle: no hunting level needed, cannot be enhanced. Reward of the quest A Struggle with the Seagull from Chuck Laurie.
- [Hunting] Sniper Rifle: needs hunting apprentice 10 or at least 50 hunting mastery; enhance with Black Stones. Craft through manufacture (hotkey: L) with Steel x20, Log x1, Pure Iron Crystal x3, Fine Hard Hide x1.
- [Hunting] Marni Sniper Rifle: needs artisan 10 or above; enhance with Scorching Sun Gemstone. Craft at the Dokkebi Tool Workshop in the Dokkebi Jar in the Land of the Morning Light (LoML) with +10 [Hunting] Sniper Rifle x1, Scorching Sun Gemstone x10, Mysterious Powder x1000, Fire Horn x1000, Resplendent Obsidian x10.

With the Marni rifle a guide shows the target area. Zoom with the mouse wheel while aiming, and it remembers your last zoom level. Right-click on standby to move into the seated aiming position.

## Hunting: Wild Game Preserve and Thunderpaw

Elks in the Fleme Hills in Balenos are good sniping practice, but sniping is at its best in the Land of the Morning Light's wild game preserve. There you mainly hunt boars, bears, and hawks, and sometimes unusual boars, bears, and tigers that give rarer items more often. A gunshot in a sniper hunting zone alerts animals within 100m, and boars, bears, and tigers charge you when shot, so it is dangerous.

Thunderpaw, called <Mountain Shaking> Thunderpaw, lives in Beombawi Valley in the Land of the Morning Light. Lure it by installing Honey-coated Meat, made through simple cooking (hotkey: L) from Sticky Honey x10 and Pork x300. Sticky Honey comes from daily quests by Huntkkebi in the wild game preserve. Thunderpaw is killed by sniper hunting, deals critical damage, so hide behind trees or rocks, especially when it rears up after being hit. It will not be fooled if you are too close. Lure it to beehives so the bees attack it. Death penalties do not apply, and if you stray too far it returns to its start with full HP and the hives reappear.

## Hunting mastery

Higher hunting mastery gives more items from butchering hunting targets and a better chance of rare resources. This excludes blue whales, fugitive khalks, humpback whales, and freshwater crocodiles. A magic or lucky butcher knife adds nothing extra.

Mastery also lets you use certain matchlocks regardless of hunting level. For example, Professional 2 hunting with 400 hunting mastery can use a [Hunting] Artisan Matchlock. More mastery gives several types of hides, which combine with stuffed trophies to make higher-grade versions with better or longer buffs.

At 250 or more hunting mastery you get the wildspark byproduct when butchering. Bring Wildspark x10 to NPC Liana in a major city to pick one of: Spirit Pouch of Ferocious Beast x1, Black Gem Fragment x5, or 900 Contribution EXP plus Hunting EXP x30.

## Hunting Bag for mastery

Equip a Hunting Bag to raise Hunting Mastery.

- Loggia Hunting Bag: buy from NPC Camellia Loggia or Zaaira.
- Robeau Hunting Bag: buy from NPC Geranoa or Zaaira.
- Manos Hunting Bag: manufacture with Manos Stone x5, Magical Shard x50, Leather Glaze x30, Damaged Hide x50. Damaged Hide comes from butchering wild animals.
