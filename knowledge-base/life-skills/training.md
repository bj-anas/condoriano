# Training

Guide to the Training life skill (horses): taming wild horses, stats and skills, leveling, markets, breeding, Imperial Steeds, coursers, Dream Horse awakening, horse gear, and bonus quests.

## Training: taming a wild horse

Wild horses roam the world and can quickly become tier 6 to 7 horses. A muscular wild horse is a higher tier than a slim one. Taming needs two items:

- Capturing Rope: buy from Stable Keeper NPCs.
- Lump of Raw Sugar: made by heating (hotkey: L) Raw Sugar x10 and Mineral Water x1, both sold by food ingredient vendors. You can also buy the lump on the Central Market.

## Training: horse-taming minigame

Right-click a capturing rope in your inventory (hotkey: I), approach a wild horse, and press left mouse button when the cursor lines up to throw the rope. Press Space to rein it in and start the minigame. Press Space on the blue part of the gauge to begin wrangling, then press W to advance. When the horse rears up, keep the red gauge at 50% or higher by pressing Space repeatedly.

After winning the tug-of-war, the Interact menu appears. Feed the horse raw sugar lumps instead of mounting right away. Even one lump guarantees the capture.

Tips: do not throw the rope while the horse stands on a rock or obstacle, because mounting there often fails. Register a captured horse at a stable (check for empty slots first), because it disappears if you log in again unregistered. A captured horse is slow, so ride the horse you brought and the captured one follows.

## Training: permanent capturing rope and raw sugar lump

You can get a permanent capturing rope, the Agewrought Luminous Rope. Prerequisites: at least Training Master 1, the completed quest [Training Leap Artisan 10] Emergency Aid, and the completed quest [Leap] Honor to the Training Family #6. Then accept and finish [Leap] Honor to the Training Family #7 from Lorenzo Murray in Velia.

A permanent raw sugar lump, Merindora's Eversweet Raw Sugar, needs Krogdalo's Sanctuary in your possession. Then accept and complete [Krogdalo's Sanctuary] Eversweet Aroma from Krogdalo's Trace.

## Training: wild horse tiers and rare wild horses

Wild horses are found everywhere. Most are tier 6, with occasional tier 7 and tier 8. Tier 7 wild horses are noticeably more common in Drieghan. All wild horses are tier 6 or above. Breeding a captured wild horse leads to better horses later.

Stable keepers say two new wild breeds are turning up, both at a low chance. One is a rare male that levels 2.5 times more slowly but sells for roughly 5 times as much. The other is a female with 4 breeding counts.

## Training: horse stats and horse types

A horse earns experience from distance traveled and its stats rise a little each level. Same-level horses can still have different stats.

- Speed: how fast it runs.
- Acceleration: a higher value means less time to reach top speed.
- Turn: affects turning while running; a higher running speed means a smaller turning radius.
- Brake: how fast it stops.

Level 1 base stats predict growth. Agile (speedy) horses have high speed and acceleration and grow most there. Control (drifter) horses have high brake and turn and grow most there. Balance horses have even stats and even growth.

## Training: horse stat tools and status

The horse stats interface has My Horse Info (birth stats, current stats, and maximum growth potential, with movement stats graded S to C by tier and average growth per level), a Growth Calculator (check stat gains from mount level, Training level, and horse gear), and a Breed/Exchange Calculator (the possible mount types and probabilities from two mounts and your Training level).

Horse status: stallions can breed twice and mares once, and the Horse Breeding Reset option resets the count. A Mount Brand Spell Stone brands a horse, raising its chance of learning a skill at each level up while branded. Death shows how many times the horse died but does not affect stats or skills.

## Training: resurrecting horses and resetting deaths

A dead horse can be revived at the closest Stable Keeper NPC, or on the spot with Tears of the Wind, which restores full health and stamina. Tears of the Wind also work for other personal mounts such as miniature elephants and Epheria sailboats. It is sold in the Loyalties tab of the Pearl Shop for 500 Loyalties.

To reset a horse's death count, accept the quest [Weekly] A Special, Healthy Meal for Horses from NPC Vanacil in Heidel. Completing it gives Rich Nutritious Carrot Meal, which resets the mount's death count.

## Training: horse skills

At each level up a horse may learn a new skill by set probability, based on tier and horse type. Stable the horse and click it to see its skill list. S skills for tier 8 horses require learning the earlier skills first.

| Skill | Effect |
| Fore Chop | Press F for a front leg kick |
| Hind Kick | Press Down + F for a back leg kick |
| Drift | Press Left or Right + Down while running to turn |
| Sprint | Press Up + Shift while running to go faster (saddle needed); all horses, including Dream and Mythical, learn it at Lv. 1 |
| Start Accel | Press Space to rear, then Up to accelerate |
| High Jump | While sprinting, press Space to jump obstacles |
| Quick Stop | Press Down while running for a minimal braking distance |
| Instant Accel | Press F while running for a burst of speed |
| Charge | Press Q to charge and hit enemies or buildings (champron needed) |
| Sideways | Press Left or Right to move sideways without turning |
| Quick Back | Press Down to move back quickly |
| Streak Leap | Consecutive jumps |
| Roar | Press Space while standing still; buff recovering HP and MP (stirrups needed) |
| Caution | Auto-walks backward from cliffs and high terrain |
| Quick Ride | Mount quickly |

## Training: tier 8 S skills, Two-Seater and the skill training minigame

Horse skills for tier 8 horses and the Two-Seater skill, plus how skill training works. The S skills can only be learned after the earlier versions.

| Skill | Effect |
| S: Fore Chop, S: Hind Kick | Stronger kicks, tier 8 only |
| S: Instant Accel | While sprinting, press F to keep charging forward; tier 8 only |
| S: Sideways | Faster sideways movement; tier 8 only |
| Two-Seater | Guild or party members can ride along; certain tier 6 to 8 horses |

Skill training minigame: after a skill is learned, a minigame sometimes appears when you use it. Winning raises your proficiency with that skill. Failing can throw you off the horse. At 100% proficiency the minigame stops appearing.

## Training: leveling a horse and training level

A higher training level gives your horse more EXP and a better chance to tame wild horses. It rises naturally as you raise horses, and taming wild horses speeds it up.

Riding manually is fastest, but looped auto-pathing works while you are away. Hold Alt on the World Map and right-click a destination, then right-click your current location to make it a loop. Or right-click a spot and press the Set Loop button. Alt plus right-click on more spots adds checkpoints. The route shows in green. Once mounted, press T to follow the route and auto-feed carrots, which restores stamina. Straight, short routes between towns or within a town are best, because other Adventurers could kill you.

## Training: Horse Market

The horse market is the fastest way to get a good horse. Open it through stable keepers in big cities, the world map (hotkey: M), or the main menu (hotkey: Esc), Life (F6), Horse Market. It is not the Central Market: prices are calculated automatically from tier, level, remaining breeding counts, skills, and other factors.

For breeding, check remaining breeding counts and buy a Lv. 1 horse of decent tier. If you will not train the horse, you must check its stats and skills. A higher-level horse may fetch more but risks not selling without good skills. You can also supply a horse to the imperial court through Imperial Horse Delivery at any Stable Keeper NPC.

## Training: Breeding Market and horse exchange

Breeding happens only through the breeding market at certain stables, and only stallions with remaining breeding counts can be registered. Select a mare with remaining breeding counts, press Request Breeding, and choose a stallion. The mare's owner pays the stallion's owner a breeding fee. To breed your own horses, tick Show Only Me when registering the stallion so it shows only in your My Registrations tab.

After a while you receive a foal, and a free mount slot must exist in the stable. Foal stats depend on the parents' tier, level, and skills. Parents with skills such as Sprint and Instant Accel raise the chance of a fine foal. With luck, two level 30 tier 6 horses can produce a fine tier 8 horse. Parent levels above 30 do not improve the odds.

Horse exchange: exchange works like breeding but uses stabled horses, and both horses used disappear. The result is influenced by the two parents like a bred foal. It is common to exchange a horse that has run out of breeding counts.

## Training: Imperial Steed rules and odds

Imperial Steeds are born with the Imperial Stature skill and cannot learn others. NPC vendors will not buy them, and they are barred from the horse market, the breeding market, and horse exchange. Odds improve with higher parent tier and level, but stop improving after level 31. Training Mastery helps. Example for breeding tier 8 male and female horses at level 25: Training Mastery 0 gives about 3.2% to 4.62%, Mastery 1,000 gives about 3.2% to 5.0%, and Mastery 2,000 gives about 3.2% to 5.4%.

They come only from breeding, not from exchange. Items they cannot use: Mount Brand Spell Stone, Reset Mount Growth, Mount Name Change Coupon, Horse Appearance Change Coupon, Premium Horse Appearance Change Coupon, and the mount skill coupons (Change, Selection, Training, and All Skill Training).

## Training: using Imperial Training seals for coursers and camels

Exchange x1 seal at NPC Shamhain in the Stonetail Horse Ranch, Mediah, for swaying wind shards and rumbling earth shards, used to awaken a courser into a Dream Horse. Variel at the Old Wisdom Tree in Kamasylvia gives Powder of Ascension x1 for 12 seals. That powder is needed to finish a quest that gives the mythical feathers used in mythical awakening, the step after a Dream Horse. Any stable keeper in Valencia trades x5 or x15 seals for the two available camels. The wiki has a separate Golden Seals page.

## Training: Courser system

A horse that learns Charge, Drift, and certain other skills is a courser. Courser prices rise significantly on the breeding market, horse market, and imperial delivery. A foal from two coursers has 2 inherited skills, and a foal from a courser and a normal horse can have up to 2. When a horse learns the right skills for its tier, its icon turns gold instead of silver.

| Tier | Courser skills (all needed) |
| 6 | Charge, Drift, Sprint |
| 7 | Charge, Drift, Sprint, Instant Accel, Sideways |
| 8 | Charge, Drift, Sprint, Instant Accel, Sideways, S: Instant Accel, S: Sideways |

A tier 8 courser can take courser training from NPC Gula at the Stonetail Horse Ranch in Mediah, then attempt awakening into a Dream Horse. Dream Horses are Arduanatt, Diné, and Doom, the three offspring of Krogdalo, King of Celestial Horses.

## Training: Dream Horse types

Tier 8 coursers are the prep stage for Dream Horses. Arduanatt has much higher speed, acceleration, turn, and brake than a tier 8 horse and can glide with its wings. Diné runs through desert without the usual movement speed penalty and can use all its skills there. Doom has flames that give it strong destructive power to help in battle.

Skills at birth:
- Arduanatt: Courser's Spirit (slowly recovers HP and stamina when unmounted), Double Jump, and Wings of Wind for gliding.
- Diné: Earth of Life (recovers 300 HP plus 300 MP/WP/SP every 3 seconds for 60 seconds for the owner and up to 9 allies), Courser's Spirit, Double Jump.
- Doom: Dark Flame Steps (strong melee attack inflicting flame damage to nearby enemies), Courser's Spirit, Double Jump. Dark Sprint boosts speed during S: Instant Accel and damages nearby enemies with flames. Doom is the only Dream Horse that can learn Two-seater.

## Training: Dream Horse awakening rules

Investment decides the result. A lot of Skill raises the chance of Arduanatt, a lot of Elegance raises the chance of Diné, and a lot of Strength raises the chance of Doom. At a total of 200%, a category with no investment has no chance. For example, 100% Skill and 100% Strength gives a 50-50 chance of Arduanatt or Doom and none for Diné. Each category maxes at 180%, and training items past 100% in a category have their efficacy halved.

To attempt awakening, prepare a Krogdalo's Origin Stone, made through simple alchemy from Swaying Wind Shard x25 and Rumbling Earth Shard x25 (from imperial training golden seals, horse race seals, peridot leaves, or Old Moon Grand Prix coins). One stone is used per attempt.

On failure, the Skill, Elegance, and Strength percentages reset. If you use cron stones, only 50% of the total training percentage is lost, with decimals removed. Every failure raises the chance of success in later attempts. On success, the tier 8 courser becomes Arduanatt, Diné, or Doom.

## Training: first Dream Horse and Mythical Horses

Your first Dream Horse can be claimed from the challenge window as Vipiko's Dreaming Horse, which lets you choose one Dream Horse per account. The wiki has a separate Vipiko's Dreaming Horse page.

Mythical horses go beyond Dream Horses: Mythical Arduanatt, Mythical Diné, and Mythical Doom. You need 2 Dream Horses to get one (see the Mythical Horses page). Your first Mythical Horse can be claimed from the challenge window with the item Choose Your Mythical Horse Box, choosing one Mythical per account.

## Training: Equestropedia and horse tiers

Horses learn common skills by tier, plus S skills: Fore Chop, Hind Kick, Quick Stop, High Jump, Charge, Drift, Instant Accel, Sprint, Roar, Quick Back, Quick Ride, Start Accel, Streak Leap, Caution, Sideways, S: Fore Chop, S: Hind Kick, S: Instant Accel, S: Sideways, and Two-seater. ## Training: horse gear overview

Horse gear has three piece types (some sets have five parts). Pieces raise stats and may be needed for certain skills. Horse gear can be enhanced like normal gear, to a maximum of +10. Basic gear is sold by Stable Keeper NPCs, but crafting it or buying on the Central Market gives better stats.

The green-grade Storm Horse gear set is exchanged for Golden Seals from imperial training or work supervision. The high blue-grade Granverre set is crafted at the Stonetail Horse Ranch's Horse Gear Workshop.

## Training bonus quests

When you deliver a tier 6 to 8 horse, an Imperial Steed, or a Dream Horse, you may automatically get a bonus quest granting horse consumables. [Bonus] Skill Up for Training asks you to tame 1 Wild Horse and lets you choose Stonetail Wind's Meal x1 or Fiery Celerity Draught x1. A second version asks you to talk to the Black Spirit for Celerity Draught x1.
