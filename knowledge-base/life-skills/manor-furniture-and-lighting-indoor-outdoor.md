# Manor Furniture and Lighting (Indoor & Outdoor)

Manor furniture and lighting that can be placed both indoors and outdoors, with how to obtain each piece.

## Manor indoor and outdoor furniture overview

The pieces on this page can be installed both inside and outside a manor: outside they shine in the midday sun, and indoors they add luxury. The set is Carpet of a Forgotten King, Florchestra Music Stand, Spirit's Echo Contrabass, Shattered Star Partition, Fireflies, Fairy's Abode, Atanis Fireflies, Large Shattered Star Partition and Curved Shattered Star Partition. More manor furniture is on the wiki pages Manor Furniture and Sculptures (Outdoor) and Manor Furniture and Lighting (Indoor).

## Manor indoor and outdoor furniture: effects

The wiki lists no stat buffs for the pieces that can go both indoors and outdoors. The Carpet of a Forgotten King is a very soft red carpet, the Florchestra Music Stand and Spirit's Echo Contrabass are musical decor, the Shattered Star Partitions divide space, and the Fireflies, Atanis Fireflies and Fairy's Abode give a gentle glow that looks best at night.

## Manor Carpet of a Forgotten King: how to obtain

The [Manor] Carpet of a Forgotten King is a soft red carpet made from the cocoon of the extinct Keplan Horn creature. It is crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from:
- Cotton Yarn x150: Heating with Cotton x5, or node production.
- Flax Thread x150: Heating with Flax x5, node production or farming.
- Silk Thread x150: Heating with Cocoon x5, or node production.
- Keplan Horned Cocoon Silk x1,000: buy Fertile Oil-Rich Soil from Geranoa, the Material Vendor in Calpheon (5 million Silver); talk to Merv in Calpheon and finish her quest after finding a journal in Keplan; then bring the Keplan Horn Cocoon from the quest to Merv to get silk.
- Silk Honey Grass x150: gathering Wild Herbs by hand or with a hoe, or node production.

## Manor Florchestra Music Stand: how to obtain

The [Manor] Florchestra Music Stand is a wooden stand from a lightning-struck tree, holding a faded music textbook. Two ways to obtain it: combine the materials vertically in Yaz's Inventory, or buy it from Benns Ruberen in Grándiha for 20 million Silver. Materials: Florchestra's Music Textbook x1 from the quest [Manor][Daily] The Marvelous Musician (starting NPC Artina, the Wandering Musician), and Thunderstruck Wooden Music Stand x1 from the quest [Manor][Daily] For Future Musicians (starting NPC Chief Valentine in Florin).

## Manor Spirit's Echo Contrabass: how to obtain

The [Manor] Spirit's Echo Contrabass is a contrabass found in the ruins of the Thousand Year War between Papus and Otters. Combine the materials vertically in Yaz's Inventory, or buy it from Benns Ruberen in Grándiha for 30 million Silver. Materials:
- Spirit's Echo x1: complete both [Manor][Daily] Spirit's Echo - Rainbow Fish Bone Coin and [Manor][Daily] Spirit's Echo - Rainbow Petal Coin (starting NPC Benns Lamute on Papua Crinea Island).
- Lamute Handmade Stand x1: complete [Manor][Daily] Lamute Gang's Handmade Stand (starting NPC Benns Ruberen in Grándiha).

## Manor Shattered Star Partition: how to obtain

The [Manor] Shattered Star Partition is decorated with real fragments of the star that fell at Star's End. Complete the quest [Manor][Daily] Shattered Star Partition, accepted from Pavino Greko in Port Epheria after finishing Pavino Greko's Log II. It can also be bought from Gustuk in Port Epheria for 2 million Silver.

The Large Shattered Star Partition and Curved Shattered Star Partition are made by buying [Manor] Shattered Star Partitions from Gustuk in Port Epheria and combining them.

## Manor Fireflies and Atanis Fireflies: how to obtain

[Manor] Fireflies are a group of fireflies giving off a gentle light, especially pretty at night. They come from a quest by interacting with fireflies at four places in the world: Tarif, Pila Ku Jail, Star's End and Protty Cave.

[Manor] Atanis Fireflies came in four versions, Sunlight, Nature's Light, Moonglade and Flame's Light, that stand for the gods Narc, Tunta, Okiara and Valtarra. Complete one of the four [Manor] Legend of Atanis Pond quests, starting from Maery at the Atanis Pond.

## Manor Fairy's Abode: how to obtain

The [Manor] Fairy's Abode is a home where light-loving fairies gather, in four versions: Sunlight, Nature's Light, Moonglade and Flame's Light. Choose and complete one of the four [Manor] Fairy Queen Theiah's Request quests, starting from Fairy Queen Theiah. Like the rest of this page's pieces, it can be placed both indoors and outdoors in a manor, and the fairies are said to return to it after playing.
