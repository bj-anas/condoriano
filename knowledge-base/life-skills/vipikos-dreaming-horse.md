# Vipiko's Dreaming Horse

How to claim a free Dream Horse through the Vipiko's Dreaming Horse challenge, which Dream Horse to choose, and what the Mythical Censer option is.

## Vipiko's Dreaming Horse: how to claim it

Since the December 20, 2023 maintenance, a free Dream Horse can be claimed just by logging in. Steps:

1. Press Y to open the Challenge window and claim the challenge named Vipiko's Dreaming Horse.
2. Right-click the resulting item in your inventory and pick one reward.
3. When asked, type the exact name of the item you chose into the UI, not the horse's name.

The choices are Horse Emblem: Dream Doom (Female or Male), Horse Emblem: Dream Diné (Female or Male), Horse Emblem: Dream Arduanatt (Female or Male), or the Mythical Censer Bundle (Mythical Censer x5). The choice is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Vipiko's Dreaming Horse: which Dream Horse to choose

- Arduanatt is a pegasus that glides safely down from high places, good for exploration and uncovering the world map. The wiki recommends it as a first Dream Horse. Skills: Wings of Freedom (turn while gliding) and Wings of Wind (used during Double Jump to glide; fall damage is reduced by 100% for a short time).
- Diné is a unicorn that buffs and heals allies and crosses the desert at full speed without a camel, since mounts normally suffer desert movement penalties. Skills: Earth of Life (heals up to 10 friendly targets, +300 HP and +300 MP/WP/SP every 3 seconds for 30 seconds in total) and Earth of Protection (up to 10 friendly targets get +10 DP and +10 Evasion for 30 seconds).
- Doom is a living fire horse for combat and fast travel. Skills: Dark Flame Steps (leaves a fire trail that damages enemies) and Dark Sprint (adds a third thrust to Instant Accel).

## Vipiko's Dreaming Horse: Mythical Censer option

The Mythical Censer is the item that awakens 2 Dream Horses into a Mythical Horse. A Mythical Awakening needs one male and one female Dream Horse of matching type, both level 30, plus one Mythical Censer. Do it at the stable in the Stonetail Horse Ranch (east of Heidel, north of Tarif) or in Grána. The Mythical Horses page on the wiki explains how to craft a Mythical Censer.
