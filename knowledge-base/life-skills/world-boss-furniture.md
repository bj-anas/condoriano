# World Boss Furniture

Special furniture made from world boss drops (Kutum, Nouver, Kzarka and Karanda) that gives a temporary buff when used.

## World Boss Furniture overview

Defeating the world bosses Kutum, Nouver, Kzarka and Karanda gives a special material that can be made into special furniture. The four pieces are the Kutum Lamp, Nouver Statue, Kzarka Statue and Karanda Statue. Using one of them gives a temporary buff lasting 2 hours.

## World Boss Furniture: effects and upgraded versions

Each piece gives All AP +15 for 2 hours upon use, plus a boss-specific effect:

| Furniture | Extra effect for 2 hours |
| Kutum Lamp | Ignore All Resistance +5% |
| Nouver Statue | All Resistance +5% |
| Kzarka Statue | Max HP +100 |
| Karanda Statue | Max Stamina +100 |

Each piece can be upgraded to a special version that gives a 5 hour buff. Visit Red Tarantula in O'dyllita and finish a quest to exchange the furniture for the upgraded version. Only statues at maximum durability can be used in that quest.

## World Boss Furniture: how to obtain

All four pieces are made through Processing (L), Manufacture. Each needs Concentrated Boss's Aura x1 (Heating with any boss gear piece), Trace of Nature x500 (found by Excavation at some production nodes), and the following:

| Furniture | Boss material (x3) | Gem (x50) | Shard or horn (x100) |
| Kutum Lamp | Ancient Kutum's Fragment | Star Diamond | Rumbling Earth Shard |
| Nouver Statue | Nouver's Hide | Blood Ruby | Fire Horn |
| Kzarka Statue | Kzarka's Bloodstone | Blood Ruby | Fire Horn |
| Karanda Statue | Karanda's Talon | Forest Emerald | Swaying Wind Shard |

The boss materials drop at a low chance from the matching world boss.

## World Boss Furniture: gems, shards and horns

Star Diamond is made by Shaking with Gem Polisher x3 and Resplendent Diamond x7. Blood Ruby is Shaking with Gem Polisher x3 and Resplendent Ruby x7. Forest Emerald is Shaking with Gem Polisher x3 and Resplendent Emerald x7. Fire Horn comes from Butchering or Tanning.

Rumbling Earth Shard drops from monsters at Forest Ronaros and Manshaum Forest, and Swaying Wind Shard drops from monsters at Fadus Habitat and Mirumok Ruins. Each shard can also be exchanged for Golden Seal - Imperial Training x1.
