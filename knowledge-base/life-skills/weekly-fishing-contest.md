# Weekly Fishing Contest

The Weekly Fishing Contest ranks Adventurers by the size of the biggest catch of three featured fish species each week, with rewards for the top 300.

## Weekly Fishing Contest: how it works

In the Weekly Fishing Contest, three fish species are chosen as the featured catches and stay active for one week. Adventurers compete to catch the largest specimens. Those ranking in the top 300 for biggest catches earn exclusive rewards. Ranks are based on the maximum size of each target fish caught during the week.

## Weekly Fishing Contest: how to participate

1. Open the menu (ESC), then Search, then Fish Guide.
2. In the Fish Guide, enter through the Weekly Fishing Contest banner on the right.
3. Check the 3 fish species chosen for the week, then fish. Rankings depend on catch size.
4. Every Sunday at 00:20 (UTC), claim your rank rewards with the Receive Reward button.

Rewards must be claimed before the next Weekly Fishing Contest reward distribution, or they are lost.
