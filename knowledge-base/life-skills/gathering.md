# Gathering

Guide to the Gathering life skill: how to gather, tools and targets, level, ability and mastery, equipment, quests, Fairy's Breath, Buried Traces, and the Green Thumb minigames.

## Gathering: how to gather

Gathering means collecting materials directly from nature. When you are near something gatherable, a prompt appears. Press the interaction hotkey (R) to collect. If the prompt does not show, another Adventurer gathered it recently. These resources are renewable and return after a while.

All characters have basic gathering tools equipped by default. The tool you use decides what you get, even from the same object. From trees, a lumbering axe gives logs or timber while a fluid collector gives sap. The fluid collector also collects blood from animals you hunt. To butcher an animal, loot its drops first, then use the Gathering prompt (R) on the body.

Herbs give much better results with a hoe. Water and desert sand come from the terrain rather than a specific object, so you can gather them continuously. The Crafting Notes (main menu Esc, then Information, then Crafting Notes, or the button in the Adventurer's Guide F1 or Adventurer's Board F2) show what you can craft from gathered resources.

## Gathering: unexpected resources

Gathering sometimes gives surprise items. Fairy powder comes from every gathering activity and can simply be sold to an NPC shop unless you need it. Traces are rare alchemy ingredients, so keep them for your own alchemy or sell them on the Central Market. Caphras stones and sharp black crystal shards sell for good silver on the Central Market, but all Adventurers with higher-level combat gear need them to improve that gear, so decide whether to sell or stockpile.

## Gathering level

There are 11 life skill categories: gathering, fishing, hunting, farming, sailing, bartering, trading, training, cooking, alchemy, and processing. Each has its own level with more bonuses at higher levels. View them in your character profile (hotkey: P); levels there differ by character, and the Learn More button shows bonuses at your current level.

Higher gathering level raises the chance to gather without spending energy, gives better and rarer items, and raises mastery. Gathering EXP comes from gathering, and scrolls, elixirs, or dishes that boost gathering EXP speed up leveling.

## Gathering ability

Gathering ability affects gathering speed and goes up to Lv. 5, shown in the character profile (hotkey: P). Ways to raise it:

- Dishes such as the seafood cron meal, which lasts 2 hours at a low price.
- Silver embroidered gatherer's clothes: ability level and gathering EXP rise with enhancement level, the effect is permanent, and they become efficient at +2.
- Elixirs like verdure draught, or guild skills.

Reaching Lv. 5 consistently is useful for serious gatherers, but not if it costs more silver than you earn. It may pay off more once your gathering level is higher.

## Gathering mastery

Gathering mastery drives gathering efficiency and profit. Results split into basic, special, and rare gathering resources. Higher mastery increases basic resources obtained and the chance of special and rare ones, such as a Caphras stone. Check mastery in the profile (hotkey: P).

Mastery rises with each gathering level, and also from gear, elixirs, and dishes that raise it, ranging from cheap to very expensive. Plan purchases within your budget. Beginners are better off experiencing gathering with simple equipment than buying the best items immediately.

## Gathering equipment for beginners

To start with the minimum, buy a gathering tool whose name begins with Upgrade from a Material Vendor NPC in any town. These have no special equip requirement and reduce gathering time.

Beginners should focus on gear that boosts Life EXP to level up quickly. High-level Adventurers should prioritize gathering mastery, which raises both rare resource chances and the amount gathered. Check equip requirements before buying equipment.

## Gathering: Family Tool window and tool wheel

Better life skill tools can be stored in the Life Skill tool interface by clicking the icon in your Equipment interface, then right-clicking the tools. Right-click again to return them to your inventory. Tools in the Family Tool window for Gathering and Fishing are shared by all characters on your account: Lumbering Axe, Fluid Collector, Hoe, Butchering Knife, Tanning Knife, Pickaxe, Fishing Rod, Float, and Harpoon.

When a target allows more than one option, such as trees and animals, the options appear in a wheel. Press F5 to pick one, and the choice is remembered.

## Gathering quests and Liana quests

Gathering quests come from many NPCs. Start with those in Velia. Rewards include Contribution Points, Gathering EXP, and valuables such as Sharp Black Crystal Shards. Promotion Quests give a lot of Gathering EXP at once and unlock at Beginner Lv. 10. The Black Spirit's Guide menu directs you to the NPCs.

Liana, in each major city, gives daily and weekly Gathering quests that reward Gathering EXP and Floramos Petals. Using Simple Alchemy, Floramos Petals make the Secret Book of Florin, which gives Life Skill Mastery +100 and Life EXP +20% for 1 hour. Weekly quest: [Weekly] [Gathering] Fairy Powder, get 100 Fairy Powder for 500 Contribution EXP plus a choice of Floramos Petal x3 or Gold Bar 10G x2.

Gathering quests are completed by speaking to Ludowig, who is near each Gathering Area.

## Gathering: Fairy's Breath exchange

Fairy's Breath can drop while lumbering, tanning, fluid collecting, using a hoe, mining, or water scooping. Exchange it with Dalishain, the Wandering Alchemist NPC in each town. The listed cost is 10 Fairy's Breath for each of:

- Wild Grass x30
- Log x20
- Rough Stone x12
- Fruit of Nature x1
- Trace of Nature x1
- Contribution EXP 900 plus Gathering EXP 30

## Gathering: Buried Trace and Mystery Crystal Chest

While lumbering, tanning, fluid collecting, using a hoe, mining, or butchering, you have a low chance to find a Buried Trace. Interacting with it makes a Mystery Crystal Chest appear, which a Gold Key opens. The chest gives all of: Ancient Spirit Dust x5-20 (50% chance), Sharp Black Crystal Shard x15-22, and Fruit of Nature x22-26. The chest vanishes after a while or once opened.

Morco of the Crow Merchant Guild trades one Gold Key for 100 Silver Keys, and Gold Keys are also sold on the Central Market. Silver Keys are sold on the Central Market or obtained by fishing. Morco is in Velia, Heidel, and Calpheon.

## Gathering: Green Thumb (Green Artisan) minigame

Once you have the Green Thumb knowledge, gathering has a set chance to trigger a minigame. Get it by completing [Green Thumb] Gatherer from Across the Sea (Gathering Professional 1 or higher) from Hyunyong, Chorong Merchant Guild, in Nampo's Moodle Village, or by graduating from Olvia Academy (the course challenge ends with [Gathering Course] Challenge: Rough Stone Mining from Professor of Gathering Zelfinith).

Turn Green Thumb on or off under My Information (P), Life Info, Learn More, Gathering category. A successful minigame uses 10 Energy and yields 10 times the normal gathering results. For example, a normal yield of Black Gem Fragment x2, Lamb Meat x10, and Hard Black Crystal Shard x1 becomes Black Gem Fragment x20, Lamb Meat x100, and Hard Black Crystal Shard x10. A failed one uses 1 Energy and gives one normal attempt. Valtarra's Clairvoyance and Valtarra's Memory are not multiplied.

## Gathering minigames by tool

- Hoe: move with W, A, S, D and press SPACE to dig all the roots. Digging the same spot three times covers it with soil again.
- Butchering: press SPACE to stop the gauge on the meat icon and win 3 times in a row. A fail resets the count to zero.
- Tanning: press SPACE to steer the falling icon and reach the target without leaving the dotted lines.
- Mining: move the pickaxe with W, A, S, D and press SPACE to strike; find the weak point and keep striking until it breaks.
- Lumbering: chop when the moving gauge hits the target area, 5 wins needed. The area shrinks with each success.
- Fluid Collecting: hold SPACE to fill the gauge, then release in the target area. Green fills the syringe faster than blue.

## Gathering: Millennial Wild Ginseng

Millennial wild ginseng is a very valuable item obtainable at a low probability through the gathering minigames. The chance is not affected by gathering level, gathering mastery level, or a pet's gathering quantity bonus. Its effects apply across your whole family for 90 days, and it can be registered on the Central Market.
