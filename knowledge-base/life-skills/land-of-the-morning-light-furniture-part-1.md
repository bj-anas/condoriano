# Land of the Morning Light Furniture - Part 1

Land of the Morning Light themed furniture for manors, part 1 of 3: traditional bedding, shelves, baskets, scrolls, books, stone items, baduk sets and more. This note groups items by how to obtain them; the wiki page has the full list.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 1 overview

Part 1 covers unique Land of the Morning Light furniture that can be installed inside manors. It includes the Corner Decorated Vanity, Janggu, traditional bedding set pieces, bookshelves, shelves and cabinets, bamboo baskets, scrolls, baduk board and stones, books, plates, a sundial, millstone and mortar, partitions, stone pillars and the Elegant Square Belfry. Parts 2 and 3 of the wiki cover the remaining Land of the Morning Light furniture.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 1: effects

The wiki lists no stat buffs for the Part 1 items. They are decorative pieces, though some are practical in style: shelves, cabinets and baskets for storage, a Wood Bench for sitting or lying down in a yard, a Sundial that tells time by the sun, a Seesaw, a baduk board with black and white stones, and a Fish Wind Chime. Many are traditional Land of the Morning Light household goods.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 1: where crafted furniture is made

Many Part 1 pieces are crafted in the houses Morning Light Nampo 4 and Morning Light Dalbeol 5. Common ingredients: Plywood Hardener is made at an Alchemy Station from Pure Powder Reagent x1, Bloody Tree Knot x3, Fir Sap x3 and Trace of Nature x3. Black Stone Powder comes from Grinding certain crystals and Black Stone. Plywood is made by Chopping 10 planks. Knitting Yarn is Heating with Fleece x5, Flax Thread is Heating with Flax x5, Cotton Fabric is Grinding with Cotton Yarn x10, and Silk Thread is Heating with Silkworm Cocoon x5 or a Valencia production node. Reed comes from the Gowun Plateau production node or by hand or hoe. Tough Hide is Drying with Rhino, Pig, Ox or Belladonna Elephant Hide x5. Lightweight Plume is Filtering with Soft Kuku Bird Plume x5 or Soft Ferri Feather x5, and Fine Lightweight Plume is Filtering Lightweight Plume x10.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 1 cabinets and belfry: how to obtain

The Four-tiered Luxury Wide Cabinet, Four-tiered Wide Cabinet and Corner Decorated Cabinet are crafted in Morning Light Nampo 4 and Dalbeol 5 from Pine Plywood x100, Pine Sap x10, Gold Ingot x10 and Fir Timber (9, 10 and 11 respectively). The High-quality Nacre Cabinet uses Pine Plywood x100, Pine Sap x10, Gold Ingot x10 and Powder of Time x10. The first two cabinets (Four-tiered Luxury Wide and Four-tiered Wide) can also be bought from Furniture Dealer Namwook.

The Elegant Square Belfry is crafted there from Design: Elegant Square Belfry x1 (sold by Youngchil), Polished Stone x100, Bronze Ingot x200, Black Stone Powder x400, Standardized Timber Square x50 and Plywood Hardener x30.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 1 Adventure Log rewards

The Adventure Log The Eyes of Adventure gives these Part 1 items as rewards: Corner Decorated Vanity, Janggu, Traditional Mattress, Traditional Backrest, Wood Bench, A-frame Carrier, Seesaw, Straw Mat, Stepping Stone, Sundial, Traditional Muntin Outdoor Chair, Square Open Bedside Table, Maehwa Scroll, Wooden Baduk Board, Fish Wind Chime and Stone Rail Pillar.
