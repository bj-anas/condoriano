# Voltarion

Voltarion is the newest Dream Horse. This page covers how to collect Traces of Thunderbolt, the acquisition quests, its skills, and its base stats.

## Voltarion: how to obtain it

Voltarion is the newest Dream Horse and is obtained differently from other Dream and Mythical Horses. You need 300 Traces of Thunderbolt and must finish a special quest series. Traces of Thunderbolt come from War of the Roses, Conquest War, Node War (Unlimited), Black Shrine (Donghae) at Sangoon Calamity 8 to 10, and Black Shrine (Hwanghae) at Deposed Crown Prince (Hard).

| War of the Roses | Leading Guild Victory 25, Liberation 20, Defeat 15; Third Legion Victory, Liberation, or Defeat 2 |
| Conquest War | Victory 12; Top Guild (Castle Lord) by points 10; 2nd and 3rd guilds by points 4 and 3 |

## Voltarion acquisition quest

Once you hold Trace of Thunderbolt x300, the Black Spirit offers the quest [Voltarion] Where Lightning Converges. The prerequisite quests are, in order, [Voltarion] Where Lightning Converges, [Voltarion] Forgotten Dream, [Voltarion] To Paradise, [Voltarion] Struck by Lightning, and [Voltarion] Let Thunder Roar.

After those, Voltarion gives the quest [Voltarion] Flash of Fate: hand over Trace of Thunderbolt x300 and choose Horse Emblem: Voltarion (Male) or Horse Emblem: Voltarion (Female).

With another 300 traces you can interact with the Water of Verti for the repeatable quest [Repeat] [Voltarion] Lightning Strikes Again, which gives another Voltarion Horse Emblem.

## Voltarion: restrictions

Voltarion cannot be registered on the Horse Market, but it can be sold through Mount Sales and Imperial Delivery. Castles protected by the Dekrogda relic barrier cannot be entered by air: if Voltarion tries it with Double Jump, airborne Lightning Charge, or airborne Thunderfall, the relic's waves make it fall at once. Mythical Arduanatt and Mythical Diné are affected the same way.

## Voltarion: shared skills

Like other Dream Horses, Voltarion can learn these skills:

- Courser's Spirit: partially recovers stamina and HP when idle and unmounted.
- Double Jump: a second quick leap after a leap; requires High Jump and Streak Leap.
- S: Charge: uses the momentum from the first charge to charge again; requires Charge.

## Voltarion: unique skills Thunderhead and Thunderfall

- Thunderhead: preceded by Sprint and activates automatically during Sprint. Converts sprint energy into lightning, recovering 100 Stamina continuously while sprinting.
- Thunderfall: preceded by Fore Chop; auto-activates during Fore Chop, or press F while airborne. Costs 100 stamina on land and 500 airborne. It stuns and dismounts targets hit on land, and knocks down and dismounts targets when cast from the air. It applies Flash Overload for 5 sec.

While Flash Overload is active you cannot use Instant Accel: Flash, Thunderfall, or Lightning Charge (airborne).

## Voltarion: unique skills Lightning Charge, Instant Accel Flash and Drift Flash

- Lightning Charge: preceded by Charge, S: Charge, and Thunderfall. Auto-activates during S: Charge, or press Q while airborne. Costs 200 stamina. A fast charge with a thunderous strike; from the air it descends as a bolt hitting everything around. Knocks down and dismounts targets. Airborne use applies Flash Overload for 5 sec.
- Instant Accel: Flash: preceded by Instant Accel. Press E while moving. Costs 200 stamina. A fast leap through space, and you can press a direction key to dodge obstacles while keeping speed. Applies Flash Overload for 5 sec.
- Drift: Flash: preceded by Drift. Press SHIFT + Left or Right + Down while running. Costs 200 stamina. Leaps through space left or right to shift position quickly.
