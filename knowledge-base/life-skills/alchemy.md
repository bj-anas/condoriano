# Alchemy

Beginner guide to the Alchemy life skill: what it is, the alchemy tool, how to craft, and what you gain from it.

## Alchemy: what it is, tools and residence

Alchemy in Black Desert is magical, but it does not try to turn lead into gold. Instead it refines and enhances ingredients to produce items with purer properties.

Simple alchemy can be done through the simple alchemy processing option. Full alchemy needs an alchemy tool installed inside a residence. A basic tool is enough for beginners, and most town General Goods Vendor NPCs sell one. The wiki points to its Houses page for residence details.

After installing the tool, interact with it by pressing the R key to open the alchemy window.

## Alchemy: crafting steps and batch production

In the alchemy window, select inventory items by right-clicking them, then press the Create button. The time taken depends on your tool type and other bonuses. Ingredients mostly come from defeating monsters and gathering plants, animals, and minerals.

Even with a correct recipe you can fail if you do not use the exact number of ingredients. For example, using two of an ingredient when three are needed gives a much lower success chance.

For repeated crafting, put the ingredients for a single process in the cauldron and press the Batch production button. Alchemy repeats until you run out of ingredients or reach your character's maximum weight limit.

## Alchemy: results and EXP

Alchemy products are usually among the best ingredients, and many magic crystals and elixirs can only be made through alchemy. A successful synthesis earns alchemy EXP, and a failure still gives a small amount. Ingredients tend to be rare and expensive, so do not experiment too much.

A higher alchemy level lets you make a wider range of elixirs, magic crystals, alchemy stones, and more. For more detail, the wiki points to its Advanced Alchemy Guide page.
