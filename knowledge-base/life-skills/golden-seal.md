# Golden Seal

Golden seals are an Imperial currency earned through trading, fishing, horse training, bandit hunting and worker supervision, and exchanged for items such as Black Essences, horse gear, mounts and worker contracts. This page lists each seal type, how to earn it and what it buys.

## Golden seals: how to acquire them

Golden seals are earned by selling specific goods to Imperial Delivery NPCs: packaged cooking and alchemy boxes, horses, and fish. Imperial Conquest seals instead drop from bandits that threaten trade routes, and Work Supervisor seals come from workers returning from work. Some seals are exchanged 1:1: each cooking box sold to an Imperial Crafting Delivery NPC gives one Imperial Cuisine seal. Other activities do not guarantee a fixed count, so selling 5 fish to an Imperial Fishing Delivery NPC does not necessarily give 5 seals. The seal types are Imperial Cuisine, Imperial Alchemy, Imperial Conquest, Imperial Training, Imperial Fishing and Work Supervisor. Seals can be exchanged for useful items such as Black Essences, horse equipment and mounts.

## Golden Seal Imperial Cuisine and Imperial Alchemy delivery

Imperial Cuisine and Imperial Alchemy seals come from Imperial crafting delivery, where packaged dishes (cooking) and elixirs (alchemy) are sold to Imperial Crafting Delivery NPCs. The boxes are packed through the Processing window (hotkey L) using its imperial cuisine and imperial alchemy options, and they sell for 250% of base price.

A family can sell a limited number of boxes per day: its contribution points divided by two. For example, 350 contribution points allows 175 boxes, cooking and alchemy boxes counted together. The NPCs have no total limit, so there is no competition with other players, and boxes can be prepared in advance.

Higher-grade boxes earn more silver but need a higher life skill level; professional's cooking boxes need at least cooking level professional 1. Cooking and alchemy mastery also raise the silver earned. Box item descriptions explain a mass packaging requirement that lets you pack 10 times as fast.

## Golden Seal Imperial Conquest

Imperial Conquest golden seals are dropped by bandits, not bought with goods. Bandits show as moving red icons on the World Map (hotkey M), but they stay hidden and only appear when a merchant carrying a trade good approaches. A character carrying a trade good cannot fight, so the usual method is to travel with a wagon loaded with trade goods and fight the bandits that appear. Bandits also appear when the trade good is on a mount, so a horse or wagon can level training and trading while collecting these seals.

The seals are exchanged at the NPC Valks in Calpheon City.

## Golden Seal Imperial Training

Imperial Training golden seals come from selling horses of level 15 or above through imperial horse delivery, which pays silver and seals. Players usually sell low-tier horses or horses with undesirable skills this way. The seal count depends on horse tier; a tier 6 horse gives 6 seals. Selling also gives items like flowers of oblivion, used in mythical horse awakening. A wagon with several low-level horses trains all of them at once.

Besides the exchange list, x1 seal in the Manage Currency UI gives awakening shards for a courser to dream horse, x12 with the NPC Variel at the Old Wisdom Tree in Kamasylvia gives 1 Powder of Ascension (needed for the mythical feather quest), and x5 or x15 with any stable keeper in Valencia gives the two camel types.

## Golden Seal Imperial Fishing

Imperial Fishing golden seals come from selling blue grade or higher fish to Imperial Fishing Delivery NPCs across the land. Each NPC buys only a set amount of each fish type per event cycle, so you compete with other players, but fish sold this way earns 250% of normal market price. Fish loses value as time passes, so sell it quickly.

## Golden Seal Work Supervisor

Work Supervisor golden seals come from supervising workers: each time a worker returns from a work cycle there is a fixed chance to receive them. More workers on a task, such as gathering resources, means faster accumulation. The seals are added automatically and stored in the storage of the town where the worker lives. They are mostly used to obtain better workers.
