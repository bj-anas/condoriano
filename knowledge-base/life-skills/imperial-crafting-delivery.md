# Imperial Crafting Delivery

How to pack dishes and elixirs into boxes and sell them to Imperial Crafting Delivery NPCs for 250% of market price, with limits, faster packaging, and golden seal rewards.

## Imperial Crafting Delivery: what it is

Imperial Crafting Delivery lets you sell packed dishes and elixirs to the NPCs who handle deliveries for the imperial court, and they pay 250% of market price. Packing uses processing (hotkey: L): the imperial cuisine option packs dishes into cooking boxes, and the imperial alchemy option packs elixirs into alchemy boxes. The boxes can only be sold to an Imperial Crafting Delivery manager.

Mastery raises the silver you get per sale. This route gives steady income when the Central Market is too competitive or limited by price caps. Imperial Crafting Delivery NPCs are in each main town, so keep packed or to-be-packed items in a town storage near one.

## Imperial Crafting Delivery: packing example

Cook or buy x18 pickled vegetables, process them with the imperial cuisine option, and they become a single professional's cooking box. Sell that box to an Imperial Crafting Delivery NPC for profit. Use the Processing window (hotkey: L) to package items. Your cooking and alchemy life skill levels must be high enough for each box type. For example, meat stew can be cooked at cooking beginner 1, but turning it into a professional's cooking box needs cooking professional 1 or higher.

## Imperial Crafting Delivery: daily limit and reset

Each family has a daily limit on the number of boxes it can sell, counting cooking and alchemy boxes together. The limit equals the family's contribution points divided by 2. For example, 200 contribution points allows 100 boxes per day. This limit is per family and does not stop others from selling to the same NPCs.

Delivery resets daily at midnight (server time), so sell before then. If the daily quantity still shows 0 after midnight, relog your character through the character selection screen, or change server.

## Imperial Crafting Delivery: faster packaging

Chatting with an Imperial Crafting Delivery NPC redirects you to Carolin in Heidel, who has special quests, one for cooking and one for alchemy. After finishing them you can package boxes 10 times faster using heavy duty packaging cords, which any Old Moon manager in a main town sells. You cannot progress these quests or use the function until processing life skill level artisan 1.

Each box sold gives golden seals. Exchange them in the Manage Currency UI (top icons next to the minimap). Golden Seal - [Imperial Cuisine]:

| 50 | Milk x50, or Egg x100 |
| 100 | Sunset Dyed Wool |
| 200 | Spellbound Tool Box |
| 400 | Black Essence: Cobelinus |
| 500 | Supreme Cooking Utensil |
| 10,000 | Manos Cook's Clothes, Manos Butcher Knife, or Manos Tanning Knife |
