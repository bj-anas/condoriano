# Lakiaro

Lakiaro is a gathering minigame in Drieghan and Kamasylvia where you dig up rare medicinal plants with a hoe. This page covers the minigame, hoes, rewards, and the Energy of Lakiaro exchange.

## Lakiaro: what it is and where to find it

Lakiaros are rare medicinal plants that once grew in the mountains of Drieghan. They were believed extinct after the land was contaminated, but they returned after the Drieghanese buried the dragon's fang and blessed rain fell. Their range now reaches beyond the mountains into Kamasylvia to the west. They are still rare, so you may need to search a wide area.

To dig one up, equip a Dostter Hoe, Dostter Steel Hoe, or Manos Hoe, then interact with the plant to start the minigame. The goal is to find all roots or dig up all the dirt around the plant. Damaging roots lowers the value of your reward, and a perfectly intact root sells for a high price.

## Lakiaro digging minigame rules

The Lakiaro minigame is tile-based, on a 12x12 or 14x14 grid depending on the lakiaro type. The 14x14 grids are harder but tend to give better rewards. The plant's value starts at 100% and drops each time you damage it. The window on the right side shows the current value and the rewards for it. The number of stages or tiles sets the difficulty.

There are seven lakiaro types. Each costs energy to dig and has a difficulty.

| Lakiaro type | Difficulty | Energy consumed |
| Young Lakiaro | Low | 20 |
| Thin Lakiaro | Medium | 20 |
| Thick Lakiaro | Medium | 30 |
| High-quality Lakiaro | High | 40 |
| Primitive Lakiaro | Highest | 50 |
| Lush High-quality Lakiaro | Highest | 50 |
| Lush Primitive Lakiaro | Highest | 50 |

All appear in Drieghan and Kamasylvia and can be dug with a Manos Hoe, Dostter Hoe, or Dostter Steel Hoe.

## Lakiaro: Dig Deeply and Dig Shallowly

There are two ways to dig during the Lakiaro minigame.

- Dig Deeply (left mouse button): unlimited uses. It removes dirt and pebbles, but touching a root damages the lakiaro. Digging up pebbles lowers your hoe's durability. At 0 durability you can keep digging, but the hoe's enhancement effects stop working.
- Dig Shallowly (right mouse button): the number of uses depends on your hoe's enhancement level. It never damages roots, but it cannot remove dirt or pebbles.

A perfectly dug lakiaro gives a reward based on 100% of its value.

## Lakiaro minigame tips and limits

- You cannot be attacked during the minigame.
- You cannot play it if you are over 150% of your weight limit.
- NPC Dostter in Duvencrune Farmland offers the tutorial quests Burning Thirst and Lakiaro for Research.
- The southern areas of Duvencrune Farmland let you practice digging as much as you want, but practice gives no rewards.
- After you finish digging, a new lakiaro appears nearby at least once, with more according to a set probability. A newly found lakiaro stays for 15 minutes before disappearing.

## Lakiaro hoes and the Manos Hoe recipe

Three hoes dig lakiaro: Dostter Hoe, Dostter Steel Hoe, and Manos Hoe. Dostter and Dostter Steel hoes are bought from Material Vendor NPCs at certain towns and around Drieghan. The Manos Hoe can be bought or manufactured.

Both the Manos Hoe and the Dostter Steel Hoe can be enhanced up to PEN (V) using Concentrated Magical Black Stone (Weapon) or Black Stone (Weapon). Enhancing a hoe means less damage to the lakiaro when digging deeply, more shallow digs, higher maximum durability, and a larger area dug per deep dig.

Manos Hoe ingredients: Magic Hoe x1, Magical Shard x50, Metal Solvent x15, Manos Stone x5.

## Lakiaro rewards

Rewards come as lakiaro sacks whose value depends on the lakiaro's remaining value. Each sack contains a lakiaro plant plus extras: Laila's petals, traces, sharp black crystal shards, and, in the better sacks, mythical feathers.

An intact Primitive Lakiaro gives a reward worth 100 million silver. Lush variants raise the plant's value somewhat. Lush High-quality and Lush Primitive lakiaro have more tiles and are harder, but give better rewards.

## Energy of Lakiaro exchanges (limited)

Energy of Lakiaro is exchanged in the Manage Currency UI. These exchanges have limits.

Once per family:
- Millennial Wild Ginseng: Energy of Lakiaro x500
- Dim Tree Spirit's Armor x1: Energy of Lakiaro x100

Weekly:
- [Event] Manos Accessory Box (once a week): Energy of Lakiaro x60
- Mythical Feather x1 (once a week): Energy of Lakiaro x5
- Royal Fern Root x50 (once a week): Energy of Lakiaro x30
- [Event] Lara's Warm Black Tea x1 (5 times a week): Energy of Lakiaro x10
