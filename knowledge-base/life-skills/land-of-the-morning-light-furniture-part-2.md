# Land of the Morning Light Furniture - Part 2

Land of the Morning Light themed manor furniture, part 2 of 3: Hanji, screens, jars, porcelain, lamps, calligraphy items, trees and flowers, wallpapers, the Royal Throne and the Elegant Hexagonal Gazebo. Items are grouped by how to obtain them; the wiki page has the full list.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 2 overview

Part 2 lists more unique Land of the Morning Light furniture for manors. It includes Hanji sheets and bundles, folding screens, jars, celadon and marquetry vessels, lamps and candle stands, calligraphy tools, floors and wallpapers, many trees and flowers, flowerbeds, a fireplace, the Royal Throne and the Elegant Hexagonal Gazebo. Parts 1 and 3 on the wiki cover other Land of the Morning Light furniture.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 2: effects

The wiki lists no stat buffs for the Part 2 items. They are decorative or practical manor furniture: lamps that light a room, folding screens that work as windbreaks or privacy screens, jars for storing or fermenting food, wallpapers and floors, trees and flowers for the garden, calligraphy tools, a fireplace, a throne and a gazebo.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 2 trees, flowers and vessels: how to obtain

Furniture Dealer Chosam and Luxury Vendors Bangchul and Soonmae sell: Blue Pine Tree, Bristlegrass, Deep Pink Azalea, Light Pink Azalea, Pink Maehwa Tree, Village Mugunghwa, Well-managed Village Mugunghwa, Village Small Pine Tree, Yellow Ginger Flower, Ripe Persimmon Tree, Bountiful Jujube Tree, Pleasantly Plump Gourd Vine, Ginkgo Tree, Maple Tree, Red Camellia Tree, Small Tangerine Tree, Moon Jar, White Porcelain Gourd Bottle, Marquetry Gourd Kettle and Marquetry Vase. Marquetry Celadon is a reward of the quest [Oduksini] A New Morning and is also sold by these NPCs.

Chosam and Ceramics Vendors Jedeok and Old Man Hodol sell the Jar, Open Jar, Large Jar, Earthen Jar and Fermenting Jar.

- Hanji with Drawing: Chosam and Painter Sorong.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 2 calligraphy items and fireplace: how to obtain

Crafted in Morning Light Nampo 4 and Morning Light Dalbeol 5:
- Ink and Inkstone: Rough Mud Crystal 20, Powder of Darkness 100, Rough Stone 20, Black Stone Powder 50 (also an Adventure Log The Eyes of Adventure reward). Rough Mud Crystal is gathered near the Glish Ruins or from the Southern Swamp production node.
- Calligraphy Brush: Fir Timber 20, Fir Sap 10, Fine Lightweight Plume 20, Black Stone Powder 10.
- Sagoonja Brush: Fir Timber 15, Fir Sap 10, Fine Lightweight Plume 20, Black Stone Powder 10.
- Round Brush Stand: Cedar Timber 20, Cedar Sap 10, Fine Lightweight Plume 20.
- Two Story Fireplace: Powder of Earth 100, Fire Horn 100, Rough Stone 100, Polished Stone 20, Rough Mud Crystal 20 (also an Adventure Log reward).

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 2 Royal Throne and Elegant Hexagonal Gazebo: how to obtain

Both are crafted in Morning Light Nampo 4 and Morning Light Dalbeol 5, and both need a design sold by Furniture Dealer Youngchil.

Royal Throne: Design: Royal Throne x1, Polished Stone x100, Sturdy Maple Plywood x30, Sturdy Pine Plywood x30, Red Crystal x50 and Plywood Hardener x50.

Elegant Hexagonal Gazebo: Design: Elegant Hexagonal Gazebo x1, Polished Stone x100, Rough Mud Crystal x100, Standardized Timber Square x50, Pine Plywood x50 and Plywood Hardener x30.

Sturdy plywood is made by Heating Plywood Hardener x3 with Maple or Pine Plywood x10. Plywood Hardener is made at an Alchemy Station from Pure Powder Reagent x1, Bloody Tree Knot x3, Fir Sap x3 and Trace of Nature x3. Standardized Timber Square is Chopping Usable Scantling x10.
