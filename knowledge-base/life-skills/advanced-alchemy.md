# Advanced Alchemy

Detailed Alchemy life skill guide: preparation gear, tools, where to get ingredients, the base alchemy recipes, mastery, alchemy flasks, and alchemy quests.

## Alchemy: why it is hard and worth it

The wiki calls alchemy the final step of life skill content. It is hard because of gathering ingredients, keeping a steady supply, and learning recipes. As characters level, you need elixirs, magic crystals, alchemy stones, and processing materials that only alchemy produces, and the Central Market may not stock them. Many alchemists profit by selling to the market or packing items for imperial delivery.

Alchemy works like cooking, with different ingredients and results, and offers more variety. You install an alchemy tool at your residence and put ingredients in the cauldron. Ingredients come from farming, node production, or gathering, so keep enough contribution points and energy in reserve.

## Alchemy: Silver Embroidered Alchemist's Clothes

Silver Embroidered Alchemist's Clothes are worth it once enhanced. Base effect: Movement Speed Level +1 and Alchemy Time -1 sec. Enhanced effects:

| Level | Movement Speed Level | Alchemy Time | Alchemy EXP |
| +1 | +1 | -2 sec | +10% |
| +2 | +2 | -3 sec | +15% |
| +3 | +2 | -4 sec | +20% |
| +4 | +3 | -5 sec | +25% |
| +5 | +4 | -7 sec | +40% |

Getting them to at least +1 or +2 is advisable. Craft them at a costume mill town workshop or buy them on the Central Market.

## Alchemy tools and buffs

The basic alchemy tool from an NPC lasts only a while. Normal alchemy tools have durability 100 and are sold in major towns. Intermediate alchemy tools (durability 500) are crafted at level 3 tool workshops. Advanced alchemy tools (durability 900, Alchemy Time -1 sec) are crafted at level 4 tool workshops. The Central Market is usually out of stock of both.

- Intermediate alchemy tool materials: black stone powder x10, melted iron shard x20, log x25, rough stone x40.
- Advanced alchemy tool materials: black stone powder x20, melted iron shard x24, usable scantling x15, polished stone x30.

Buffs that help: an alchemy stone of life; sute tea with Life EXP Gain +8%; teff bread with Cooking Time -3%; and teff sandwich with Cooking Time -5%.

## Alchemy ingredients: herbs

Basic alchemy ingredients are herbs, blood, special gathering ingredients, powders, traces, fruits, sap, and mushrooms. Set a goal first, because ingredients depend on what you make. Herbs can be gathered wild, grown in a garden, produced at nodes, or bought from herb vendors such as Nieves in Calpheon City (the easiest but costly option). Weeds can only be gathered by hand.

| Herb | Notes |
| Weeds | Gathered by hand from various plants; substitute for wild grass; buying on the Central Market is recommended |
| Sunrise Herb | Gathered wild; nodes Cron Castle Site and Balenos Forest; farmable; sold in shops |
| Silver Azalea | Gathered wild; nodes Northern Plain of Serendia and Lynch Farm Ruins; farmable; sold in shops |
| Fire Flake Flower | Gathered wild; farmable; sold in shops |
| Dry Mane Grass | Gathered wild; farmable; sold in shops |

One weed replaces one wild grass when making clear liquid reagent or pure powder reagent. For other elixirs needing wild grass, the ratio is 1 wild grass to 5 weeds.

## Alchemy ingredients: beast blood and water

Collect beast blood with a fluid collector after hunting a beast. Bloods in the same group have the same effect, so use whichever is easier.

| Group | Bloods |
| 1 | Deer Blood, Sheep Blood, Ox Blood, Pig Blood, Waragon Blood |
| 2 | Fox Blood, Weasel Blood, Raccoon Blood |
| 3 | Bear Blood, Troll Blood, Ogre Blood |
| 4 | Wolf Blood, Flamingo Blood, Cheetah Dragon Blood, Rhino Blood |
| 5 | Worm Blood, Kuku Bird Blood, Bat Blood, Lizard Blood |

Purified Water and Distilled Water count as the same ingredient. Both come from processing a bottle of river water, collected by right-clicking an empty bottle while standing in water or a fountain (empty bottles are sold by Material Vendor NPCs). Filtering gives purified water but works only in windy weather. Heating gives distilled water in any weather.

## Alchemy ingredients: special gathering items and powders

Special gathering ingredients drop at a low rate while lumbering trees at gathering level Skilled 5 or higher, or from workers at lumbering nodes. Buying on the Central Market is recommended for large-scale production.

| Item | Node production |
| Bloody Tree Knot | Treant Forest, Elric Shrine, Stonetail Wasteland |
| Spirit's Leaf | Goblin Cave, Balenos Forest, Lumberjack's Rest Area |
| Monk's Branch | Forest of Seclusion, Serendia Shrine, Oze Pass, Mountain Top Guard Post |
| Red Tree Lump | Northern Plain of Serendia, Old Dandelion, Quint Hill |
| Old Tree Bark | Mediah Northern Highlands |

Powders drop at a low rate while mining ores at Skilled 5 or higher, or from workers at mining nodes. The powders and the ores that give them: Powder of Flame (Copper, Tin, Mythril Ore), Powder of Darkness (Lead, Vanadium, Mythril, Iron, Titanium Ore), Powder of Rifts (Sapphire, Ruby, Topaz), Powder of Time (Gold, Diamond, Beryl, Platinum). Each also comes from several exploration nodes, and the Central Market sells them. The wiki page has the full node lists.

## Alchemy ingredients: traces, fruit, sap and mushrooms

Traces are scarce. They come only from node production (after getting permission from the node NPC, using energy) and low-rate monster drops, so send workers and buy the rest on the market. Trace of Nature nodes listed: Ancient Stone Chamber, Sherekhan Iron Mine, Lynch Farm Ruins, Ancient Ruins Excavation Site, Glish Ruins, Fountain of Origin.

Fruit of Nature comes at a low rate from gathering grass, crops, mushrooms, and flowers with a hoe, and at a high rate from breeding crops in a garden. Farmable crops include pepper, garlic, hot pepper, pumpkin, olive, onion, grape, strawberry, sunflower, carrot, potato, wheat, tomato, corn, and many herbs and mushrooms. Farming is slow, so buying is often easier.

Sap comes from fluid collector use on trees or node production. Gathering it yourself is recommended because it is plentiful. Sap types: Ash, Birch, Maple, Pine, and Fir Sap.

Mushrooms grow in forests and humid places, in fenced gardens, or at nodes. A mushroom vendor stands in the room right of Calpheon's storage keeper. Nodes: Dwarf Mushroom (Northern Swamp), Cloud Mushroom (Glish Swamp, Southern Swamp), Fortune Teller Mushroom (Ehwaz Hill, Coastal Cliff), Arrow Mushroom (Forest of Plunder, Ivero Cliff).

## Alchemy: other ingredients and key prepared items

- Black Stone Powder: made by grinding (processing) magic crystals or black stones, or from rough stones at a town workshop. Grinding magic crystals is the most economical method.
- Melted Lead Shard: heat (process) lead ore.
- Melted Copper Shard: heat (process) copper ore.

Prepare these alchemy-made ingredients in bulk beforehand. Primary: Pure powder reagent and Clear liquid reagent. Secondary: Sinner's blood, Legendary beast's blood, Wise man's blood.

## Alchemy: starter and secondary recipes

Start by making Clear Liquid Reagent and Pure Powder Reagent to level up. At alchemy beginner 10 you can take an alchemy advancement quest from Olvia, and you should also finish the enhancement quests through the Black Spirit.

Alchemy Beginner 1 or higher (Golden Ratio):
- Clear Liquid Reagent: 1 Purified Water (Distilled Water), 1 Salt, 1 Sunrise Herb, 1 Wild Grass (Weed).
- Pure Powder Reagent: 1 Purified Water (Distilled Water), 1 Sugar, 1 Silver Azalea, 1 Wild Grass (Weed).

Alchemy Apprentice 1 or higher (Golden Ratio):
- Sinner's Blood: 1 Clear Liquid Reagent, 2 Pig Blood, 1 Powder of Flame, 1 Bloody Tree Knot.
- Clown's Blood: 1 Clear Liquid Reagent, 2 Wolf Blood, 1 Powder of Flame, 1 Bloody Tree Knot.
- Wise Man's Blood: 1 Clear Liquid Reagent, 2 Fox Blood, 1 Trace of Nature, 1 Monk's Branch.
- Tyrant's Blood: 1 Pure Powder Reagent, 2 Fox Blood, 1 Trace of Nature, 1 Monk's Branch.
- Legendary Beast's Blood: 1 Pure Powder Reagent, 2 Lizard Blood, 1 Trace of Nature, 1 Spirit's Leaf.

## Alchemy: oils and Essence of Nature recipes

These recipes also require Alchemy Apprentice 1 or higher (Golden Ratio):

- Oil of Regeneration: 1 Legendary Beast's Blood, 1 Fruit of Nature, 1 Powder of Rifts, 1 Red Tree Lump.
- Oil of Storms: 1 Tyrant's Blood, 1 Fruit of Nature, 1 Powder of Time, 1 Old Tree Bark.
- Oil of Fortitude: 1 Clown's Blood, 1 Fruit of Nature, 1 Powder of Flame, 1 Monk's Branch.
- Oil of Corruption: 1 Sinner's Blood, 1 Fruit of Nature, 1 Powder of Darkness, 1 Spirit's Leaf.
- Oil of Tranquility: 1 Wise Man's Blood, 1 Fruit of Nature, 1 Powder of Earth, 1 Bloody Tree Knot.
- Essence of Nature: 2 Fruit of Nature, 4 Maple Sap, 2 Trace of Nature, 3 Fortune Teller Mushroom.

## Alchemy mastery and alchemy flasks

Alchemy mastery mainly raises the maximum number of products per craft. It also affects extra products from the process and profit from turning in alchemy boxes to Imperial Crafting Delivery NPCs. If you only want the profit boost, equip mastery gear only while turning in boxes of packed elixirs.

Equip an Alchemy Flask to raise mastery:
- Loggia Flask: buy from NPC Camellia Loggia or Zaaira.
- Gortgath Flask: buy from NPC Geranoa or Zaaira.
- Manos Flask: manufacture with Manos Stone x5, Magical Shard x50, Essence of Nature x20, Shining Powder x50. Shining Powder drops at a certain probability on successful alchemy or can be bought on the Central Market.

## Alchemy quests and Olvia daily quests

Many NPCs give alchemy quests. The wiki recommends the ones in Balenos, especially in the town of Olvia. Rewards include contribution EXP, alchemy EXP, and rare items like sharp black crystal shards.

At alchemy beginner 10, Olvia offers repeatable quests. Prepare ingredients beforehand and finish them all at once. They give contribution, alchemy EXP, and energy recovery, which makes them very good for leveling.

Leap quests speed up leveling. The first unlocks at beginner 10. Summon the Black Spirit and open its guide menu to see leap quests for all life skills and be guided to the NPC.

## Alchemy bonus quests

While doing alchemy you may automatically receive a bonus quest that gives extra ingredients. [Bonus] Skill Up for Alchemy asks you to craft Clear Liquid Reagent, Pure Powder Reagent, or Clown's Blood.

- Craft x500 version: rewards Trace of Nature x500, and Bloody Tree Knot, Monk's Branch, Spirit's Leaf, Old Tree Bark, and Red Tree Lump x2,000 each.
- Craft x100 version: basic reward Trace of Nature x250, plus a choice of x500 of Thornwood Sap, Ash Sap, Maple Sap, Loopy Tree Sap, or Fir Sap.
- Another craft x100 version: basic reward Trace of Nature x250, plus a choice of x500 of Birch Sap, Cedar Sap, Pine Sap, or Moss Tree Sap.
