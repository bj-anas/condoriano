# Gránan Snow White Furniture

Manor furniture inspired by the city of Grána in Kamasylvia, with the workshop level and materials for each piece.

## Gránan Snow White Furniture overview

This set brings the white elegance of the Kamasylvian elves to a manor. It has 14 pieces: Gránan Snow White Audria Floral Decoration and its Small version, Snow White Wooden Fence and its Small version, Fairy-lit Lamp, Silver Blue Fairy Chalice, Snow White Lotus Fountain, Snow White Mountain Sheep Decorated Bench and Table, Snow White Tree Vine Lamp, Gránan Snow White Marble Flowerbed, Gránan Fairy Trumpet Fountain, Kamasylve Gazebo of the Fairies and Kamasylvian Elf of Peace Statue. All are crafted at a Furniture Workshop, at level 3, 4 or 5 depending on the piece.

## Gránan Snow White Furniture: effects

The wiki page lists no stat buffs for the Gránan Snow White pieces; they are decorative manor furniture. Flowerbeds, fences, fountains, lamps, a bench, a table, a chalice, a gazebo and a statue are included, and each has a short description about Grána, the fairies or the elves.

## Gránan Snow White Furniture: shared materials and how to obtain them

Most pieces use these base materials:
- Sturdy Snow White Stone: Grinding with Polished Stone x10, Pure Powder Reagent x10, Purified Water x10, Black Stone Powder x10 and one Blood item (Clown's, Legendary Beast's, Sinner's, Tyrant's or Wise Man's Blood x1).
- Polished Stone: Grinding with Rough Stone x10.
- Copper Ingot: Heating with Melted Copper Shard x10.
- Bronze Ingot: Heating with Melted Copper Shard x5 and Melted Tin Shard x5.
- Powder of Earth and Powder of Flame: a small chance when gathering certain rocks, or from several production nodes.
- Blue Crystal: Heating with Rough Blue Crystal x5.

Crafted at a level 3 Furniture Workshop (quantities of each material):

| Item | Materials |
| Audria Floral Decoration | Sturdy Snow White Stone 20, Polished Stone 180, Copper Ingot 180, Powder of Earth 150, Audria 50 |
| Audria Floral Decoration (Small) | Sturdy Snow White Stone 15, Polished Stone 150, Copper Ingot 150, Powder of Earth 150, Audria 50 |
| Snow White Wooden Fence | Birch Timber 80, Birch Sap 90, Copper Ingot 10, Spirit's Leaf 80 |
| Snow White Wooden Fence (Small) | Birch Timber 60, Birch Sap 80, Copper Ingot 5, Spirit's Leaf 50 |
| Fairy-lit Lamp | Sturdy Snow White Stone 10, Polished Stone 15, Bronze Ingot 100, Copper Ingot 20, Powder of Flame 10 |

Audria is gathered in certain places in Kamasylvia and the Land of the Morning Light. Birch Timber comes from lumbering Birch Trees or production nodes, Birch Sap from gathering Birch Trees with a fluid collector, and Spirit's Leaf from lumbering trees.
