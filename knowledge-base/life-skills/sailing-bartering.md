# Sailing & Bartering

The ocean content of Black Desert: sailing ships, hiring sailors and first mates, bartering sea trade goods, Crow Coin shops, the Epheria galleon and the Panokseon. Each section covers one topic.

## Sailing overview and which ship to start with

Sailing needs a sea-worthy ship. Rafts, rowboats, fishing boats and cogs are easy to get, but a Bartali sailboat is the recommended minimum, and an Epheria sailboat or frigate is better. Proix in Velia sells the Bartali sailboat. Epheria sailboats and frigates come from crafting, an upgrade, the Central Market, or an exchange of Shakatu seals. These starter ships are slow with little inventory and weight capacity, but a ship upgrade path leads to the larger vessels seen docked in towns. The Ship Upgrading page of the wiki covers that path.

## Sailing: ocean wind and the ship UI

Ocean wind affects sailing everywhere. It is weak near the coast and much stronger further out at sea. When you board, the UI shows wind strength and direction and updates as you travel. Sailing with the sails unfolded against the wind slows the ship, and sailing with the wind speeds it up in proportion to the wind strength.

The ship UI has a speed bar and an option for continuous Breezy Sail from sailing level skilled 1, which consumes rations just like manual use. The red bar is durability, the ship's health; at zero the ship is destroyed. The yellow bar is rations, used when moving, using skills and doing certain activities. At zero rations, skills that need them cannot be used and the ship moves much slower. Cannon icons on both sides show firing cooldown and cannonballs left.

## Sailing: folding and unfolding the sails

On large ships, a button on the speed bar folds or unfolds the sails. With sails open the ship is affected by ocean wind, and with sails folded it is not, so you can pick whichever is faster for the local wind. Folding or unfolding has a 5-second cooldown. If a special marker appears around the speed gauge, the ship has entered an Oquilla current, which boosts speed.

## Sailing: anchor UI and island actions

The Anchor UI shows up when your ship nears a Papu or Otter Barterer NPC at the Great Ocean islands, provided the player is gripping the steering wheel. Options:
- Anchor: enables island features and specific spots, such as some shipwrecks in Margoria.
- Explore / Supply: sends sailors to explore for treasure or to resupply rations. It needs at least 100,000 rations loaded on the ship, and after starting at an island it can be restarted only after 20 minutes.
- Barter: trade with a nearby Barterer NPC.

Pressing Anchor stops the ship, and it will not move again until Depart is pressed.

## Sailing: boarding and disembarking

Anyone on a ship sees Board and Disembark buttons. Disembark instantly teleports you to land if a wharf manager is nearby. If you are at the wheel, press it once to leave the wheel and again to go to the wharf manager. It has a 5-second cooldown and can be given a hotkey under Settings, Interface Settings, UI Hotkeys. From shore, left-click the anchor-shaped ship icon under your HP bar, or press Board, to teleport to your nearby ship. Pressing Board again aboard takes you to the ship's wheel.

## Sailors: hiring, condition and illness

Sailors can be found around Velia, Port Epheria and Iliya Island and are hired with a sailor contract certificate. The certificate costs 3,000,000 silver from Philaberto Falasi (Port Epheria), Islin Bartali (Velia, Lunar Halo Inn) or Proix (Velia, Dancing Marlin Tavern).

A sailor's health is called condition. Offering chowder and raisin bread recovers it. Chowder is cooked with a cooking utensil at a residence. Ingredients: Teff Bread x1, Milk x1, Pork x2, Pepper x2, and either Dried Pearl Oyster Flesh x1 or Dried Shellfish x5. Raisin bread is sold by Islin Bartali and by wharf managers. An ill sailor is cured with an Elixir of Regeneration, made by simple alchemy (hotkey L). Ingredients: Grape x4, Troll Blood x2, Oil of Regeneration x1, Essence of Nature x1 and Mandragora Essence x1.

To treat sailors, check the ship in at a wharf, choose Manage Sailors, pick the sailor and give the items. Once recovered, Board lets you attach them again. Sailor status is in the Sailor Info tab of Ship Info (hotkey P while steering).

## Sailors: cabins and maximum sailor count

How many sailors a ship can carry depends on the ship's capacity, your maximum number of sailors, and the cabins each sailor uses. An Epheria sailboat has 10 cabins and an Epheria carrack has 100. One sailor may use only 3 cabins but have weaker stats than another that uses 10.

Your maximum sailor count rises with the item Sailor's Oath. Oaths are rewards of the Oquilla's Eye questline starting with [The Great Expedition] First Sailboat into Oquilla's Eye, and of the quest Chulong's Gift at Nampo's Moodle Village, which unlocks once that questline is done. Check the Suggested tab of the Quests window (hotkey O). Do not delete these oaths before use, because they cannot be obtained again.

## Sailors: experience sharing

Sailors gain EXP from defeating sea monsters and from regular and special barters. The total EXP gained by all boarded sailors depends on how many are aboard, and is split equally. Listed totals: 1 sailor 100%, 2 sailors 130%, 3 sailors 160%, 4 sailors 190%, 5 sailors 220%, 6 sailors 250%, 7 sailors 280%, 8 sailors 310%, 9 sailors 340%, 10 sailors 370%. With 11 or more sailors aboard, the EXP is multiplied by 400% and shared equally. With 30 boarded sailors, each gets about 13.3%.

## First mates: what they do and their rules

Three NPCs can serve as first mate: Proix, Cleia and Tranan Underfoe. With a first mate aboard, you can confirm your position and use auto-navigation across the Great Ocean. The first mate can only use the First Mate preset slot of these vessels: Epheria Carrack Advance, Balance, Volante and Valor, and the Panokseon.

Rules: a first mate does not take up a cabin; when hired they join the Sailor List; they can fall ill like normal sailors and need an Elixir of Regeneration or Tears of the Star; while ill their active effects do not apply; and a first mate cannot be fired.

## First mates: how to hire Proix, Cleia and Tranan Underfoe

First Mate Proix: finish the quest [The Great Expedition] In Search of Khan, the Eye of the Moon, then complete Proix's questline in Velia (Reunion on the Horizon, Tides of Conversation, Call of the Sea) to get First Mate Contract: Proix. Trait: longer BreezySail duration while boarded.

First Mate Cleia: obtain the Golden Pocket Watch from Special Barter at a set probability, then complete her questline (Memento of the Past, Moment of Resolve, Set Sail for New Horizons) to get First Mate Contract: Cleia. Trait: Parley needed for bartering is reduced by 10% while boarded; see it in the Barter Information window while steering.

First Mate Tranan Underfoe: obtain the Fancy Figurehead from the Saltwater Crocodile Sea Monster Habitat at a set probability, then complete his questline (A Certain Adventurer's Sea-journ, Dream of the Open Sea, A Sea of Conviction) to get First Mate Contract: Tranan Underfoe. Trait: unlocks the Ship Auto Repair button while steering, which needs Ship Repair Material, Ship Repair Kit or Emergency Ship Repair Kit in the ship inventory.

## Sailors: Manage Sailors slot effects

Placing a sailor in a ship part gives these effects:

| Slot | Effect |
| First Mate | Only a first mate fits; that first mate's effects apply |
| Sail | Endurance and Wits stats of the sailor are doubled |
| Wheel | Awareness and Strength stats are doubled |
| Cannon | Focus, Force and Vision stats are doubled |
| Deck | Ship durability +10,000 per Cabin Cost of the sailor |
| Mess | Ship rations +5,000 per Cabin Cost of the sailor |
| Cabin | The sailor's base stats apply |

Each slot except Cabin holds one sailor by default. Extra slots: Epheria Carrack Advance has Mess +1, Balance has Sail +1, Volante has Sail +1, Valor has Cannon +1, and Panokseon has Cannon +2.

The window can sort sailors by stat and filter by type. Auto-assign fills slots in the order First Mate, Sail, Wheel, Cannon, Mess, Deck, Cabin. The list is ordered as sailors who can board this ship, then sailors already on the ship, then sailors on other ships.

## Sailors: Sailor Preset

The Sailor Preset saves a sailor arrangement. It works on these ships: Bartali Sailboat, Epheria Sailboat, Epheria Frigate, Improved Epheria Sailboat, Improved Epheria Frigate, Epheria Caravel, Epheria Galleass, Epheria Carrack Advance, Balance, Volante and Valor, and Panokseon. Presets are per ship type, so one saved for Carrack Advance Preset 1 does not appear for Carrack Volante Preset 1. Applying a preset assigns only sailors who are not sick and not on another ship.

To use it: talk to the Wharf Manager NPC and open Manage Sailors, assign sailors, then press Save Preset.

## Sailing: Carrack sailor fishing setup

A special sailor slot lets a Carrack fish automatically while sailing. It needs the Oceanbound Otter Fishing Rod and a sailor assigned to the Fishing slot in Manage Sailors. To get the rod, complete the suggested quest [The Great Expedition] Otter Barterer, then the quest [Sailor Fishing] An Otterly Good Friend from Fish Vendor Crio in Velia, which asks for Crow Coin Coupon x200. After that quest, additional rods can be exchanged with Crio. Sailors can also auto-discard fish below a grade you choose.

## Sailing: Carrack sailor fishing rules

Carrack sailor fishing happens once every 180 seconds, shown by a gauge on the sailing UI; when it fills, a fish is caught. Progress counts only while the Carrack is sailing, including during Ram, Power Accel., BreezySail, QuickRetreat, AbruptTurn and SmoothTurn, and not while stationary. The gauge does not reset when you release and retake the controls.

Fish go to the inventory, or the fish tank if present. Only the rod's Prize Catch Fish Rate applies, fixed at 3% with the Oceanbound Otter Fishing Rod and not raised by Fishing Mastery. The Prize Catch group is Prize Fish and Ancient Relic Crystal Shard. Catches count for the Weekly Fishing Contest and Fish Guide and affect the fishing resource of that sea area.

The gauge resets when the rod is unequipped or replaced, when the ship is checked into a wharf or destroyed, and when you go to character selection or exit. Fishing pauses if the rod's durability is used up, if the fishing sailor is sick, or if the ship is stationary.

## Sailing: emergency ration supply

When rations run low at sea, food from your inventory can be supplied to the ship as emergency rations. The amount restored depends on food grade:

| Food grade | Rations restored |
| White | 80 |
| Green | 100 |
| Blue | 240 |
| Yellow | 4,000 |
| Orange | 22,000 |

Food used to restore land worker stamina, and food that cannot be listed on the Central Market, cannot be used as emergency rations.

## Sailing: ship skills

From improved Epheria sailboats and frigates onward, ships can use many skills, unlocked as the sailing life skill level rises. BreezySail and Power Accel can be chained to improve ocean travel, while Ram, Focus Fire, Volley and Bull's-Eye improve naval combat. The Panokseon skill list on the wiki shows the key commands, such as F for Ram and Shift plus Up for BreezySail.

## Sailing: sailing logs

Sailing logs raise ship speed. Srulk and Loggia sailing logs are bought from certain town vendors, while the Manos sailing log is crafted or bought on the Central Market. It is crafted by Manufacture (hotkey L) from Siren Song Aura x3, Manos Stone x5, Magical Shard x50 and Supreme Tough Hide x50. Siren Song Auras are a guaranteed drop from Hollow Maretta, a siren-like monster in Margoria.

Manos Sailing Log effects: Sailing EXP +10%, Sailing Mastery +5 to +400 depending on enhancement level, and +15% each to Max Big Ship Speed, Max Big Ship Auto-sail Speed, Big Ship BreezySail Speed and Big Ship Auto-BreezySail Speed.

## Bartering: how sea trade works

Bartering is sea trading: exchanging specific items for others with barterer NPCs, mostly on islands north of the continent. Open the Bartering window from the main menu (Esc, Life, Barter), from the Load Cargo window, from the Barter Information button, or from Search Barter at the bottom right of the World Map (hotkey M).

Barter goods have levels: level 1 gray, level 2 green, level 3 blue, level 4 yellow, level 5 red. For example, a barterer may ask for a level 1 Raft Toy x1 and give a level 2 Conch Shell Ornament x2. Bartering works only from a ship, with papu or otter barterers on islands or sailors who drifted to Margoria. Each exchange uses parley points, which renew each barter refresh cycle. Barter items must be loaded with Load Cargo from a Wharf Manager; items in your character inventory cannot be bartered. Barter items are destroyed when a large ship is destroyed or remotely collected to a wharf.

## Bartering: window details and trade tiers

The barter window shows the item you hold and how many, what you get in return, how many exchanges are allowed, the ship's weight capacity, the weight change per exchange and the parley used per barter. It can exchange everything at once, and clicking a trade good jumps to the good needed to obtain it.

Trade tiers on island nodes: Level 1 trade turns land materials into level 1 goods; levels 2 to 5 turn the previous level into the next; Lv. 6 Barter turns level 5 into level 6; Lv. 7 Barter turns level 6 into level 7. Other Trades turn various levels into Crow Coin trade goods and then ship materials and enhancement materials. Level 5 goods, and occasionally level 4, can be exchanged for crow coins, shown below the weight bar in the inventory (hotkey I).

## Bartering: selling trade goods for silver

Level 3 and higher barter goods can be sold for silver, more for higher levels. Storage keepers in each territory buy trade goods: Lv. 3 to 5 can be sold at existing shops, and only Lv. 5 at a town storage's currency exchange.

## Sailing bartering: parley and barter refresh points

Each barter spends parley. Pressing Barter Refresh, at the top right of Barter Information, resets both the offered items and the parley they need. Every day at 06:00 UTC, barter refresh points reset to 100, or to 150 with an active value pack. Points spent by a refresh depend on how many barters remain that day, and an instant refresh costs an extra 30 points. First Mate Cleia lowers the parley needed by 10% while aboard.

Extra: unwanted Lv. 1 trade goods can be taken to Gerio on Beiruwa Island or Tidio on Barater Island, who trade them, up to four times, for a Lost Trade Box. The box holds a random barter good between Lv. 1 and Lv. 5.

## Bartering: ship material barter

Ship material barter is a separate barter set from the Crow Merchants Guild for ship upgrade materials. The Ship Material Refresh function in the Refresh Barter window can be used twice a day. Each use has a small chance of revealing every possible crow coin barter on every island, where crow coins can buy any Level 2 to 5 sea trade goods. It yields upgrade materials, ship enhancement materials and treasure-related items. The accumulated exchanges needed for Brilliant Pearl Shard and Brilliant Rock Salt Ingot drop greatly.

Materials obtainable include Island Tree Coated Plywood, Rock Salt Ingot, Cobalt Ingot, Bright Reef Piece, Pure Pearl Crystal, Luminous Cobalt Ingot, Great Ocean Dark Iron, Seaweed Stalk, Ruddy Manganese Nodule, Moon Scale Plywood, Moon Vein Flax Fabric, Oquilla's Flower and Tear of the Ocean, plus the Cox Pirates' Artifact in Parley Beginner, Parley Expert and Combat versions, and various monster scales, bones and ooze.

## Bartering: special barter

Special barters occur randomly at a low chance during regular bartering at island barterers, and the more you barter, the likelier they become. They can offer very expensive items or many crow coins, and gold bars can be used at a special barter for crow coins or goods not available from regular barter. The offered items can only be seen after arriving at that island. When one is available, a gold badge icon shows in the Barter Information UI on the World Map (hotkey M) and a system message appears.

## Ravinia and Livenia Crow Coin Shop

Ravinia at Crow's Nest and Livenia at Oquilla's Eye are Crow Coin Exchange NPCs. Crow's Nest is a fog-covered island run by the Crow Merchants Guild, north of Tinberra Island. They sell items such as Khan's Concentrated Magic, carrack equipment, ship upgrade materials and ship equipment enhancement materials. For most goods, the crow coin price is far higher than obtaining the item yourself, so buy only small quantities of materials, and save crow coins for items that are only sold there. The wiki page has the full item list.

## Rebinia's Crow Coin shop

Rebinia, one of Ravinia's sisters, runs crow coin shops in Lema Island, Iliya Island, Port Epheria and Velia. She sells useful common items and rare Manos accessories, and the goods can only be bought from her as special limited boxes. These shops take crow coins, which come from level 5 and some level 4 barter goods. The wiki page lists everything Rebinia sells.

## Epheria Galleon

The Epheria galleon ranks among the finest ships in the game. The item Epheria's Star can be obtained from a low chance at a special barter, or as the reward for the guild that wins a conquest war in Calpheon, Mediah or Valencia. For the war reward it is in the guild storage, and only the guild master can move it and award it to a member with the Present button. With the item, accept a special quest from Philaberto Falasi in Port Epheria to get the Epheria galleon ship license and the 7-day Captain Epheria title.

You can ride and steer the galleon only while the title is active. Its cannons match an Epheria carrack's fire rate and range, it cannot equip ship equipment, and repairs cost 10 million silver per 10% damage (damage rises upon remote collection or ship destruction). It must be supplied on first registration, like carracks.

## Panokseon overview and stats

The Panokseon is a ship craftable in the Land of the Morning Light. It is slow and its stats are lower than the Epheria carrack's, but it holds many crew and has a sturdy deck structure for high defense, and its ram damage is higher than the carrack's. Building it needs strong relations with Moodle Village, for example by eliminating the Goldmont Pirates, exchanging currencies or crow coins.

| Stat | Value |

| Rations | 1,300,000 |

Cannon firing speed and range match the Epheria Carrack. Repair costs 3,000,000 silver per 10% damage, and it needs supplies on first registration.

## Panokseon crafting

The Panokseon is crafted at the house Moodle Village 4 Shipyard Lv. 1, producing Ship License: Panokseon. Materials:
- Blueprint: Panokseon x30, from the quest [Weekly] Pirate Trouble given by Yooan, the Nampo Moodle Village wharf manager (it rewards Blueprint: Panokseon x2).
- Wooden Nail Soaked in Seawater x250, from defeating Goldmont Small, Medium or Large Battleships of the Outlaw of the Vadabin Sea.
- Finely Polished Pine Plywood x300, for Sangpyeong Coin x10 with Yooan.
- Glue With Traces of Deep Waves x200, for Crow Coin x500 at the Crow Coin Shop.

## Panokseon Haemo's equipment (green grade)

Panokseon: Haemo's Cannon, Sail, Figurehead and Plating are bought at the Crow Coin shop for 10,000 crow coins each. Enhancing them uses the same stone as Epheria Carrack: Toro's equipment. Each attempt from level 1 to 5 takes Tidal Black Stone x20, and each attempt from level 6 to 10 takes x30. Starlight Crystals cost 100 coins at the Crow Coin Shop.

## Panokseon Byukgye's equipment (blue grade)

Byukgye's equipment is crafted by modifying Haemo's equipment, either in Morning Light Nampo 2 (Byukgye Ship Part Workshop) or on Cheongsa Island 1 (Cheongsa Ship Part Workshop). For one part (cannon, sail, figurehead or plating) you need:
- Blueprint: Byukgye's Panokseon Equipment x10, from sending a worker to the Dallae Pier Quarry node.
- A +10 Haemo's Panokseon equipment piece of the same part, bought and enhanced with Starlight Crystal and Solar or Lunar Black Stone.
- Finely Polished Pine Plywood x50, at Sangpyeong Coin x10 each from Yooan.
- Delicately Polished Support x50, from the Crow Coin Shop or Manufacture (L) with Violent Sea Monster's Bone x1 and Starlight Hardener x1.
- Wave Residue Adhesive x50, from the Crow Coin Shop or Manufacture (L) with Violent Sea Monster's Ooze x1 and Starlight Emulsifier x1.
- Permit for Reinforcing Panokseon Parts x1, bought from Chulong in Moodle Village for 1 billion Silver.

Byukgye's equipment enhances with Tidal Black Stone, as Epheria Carrack: Chiro's equipment does.

## Panokseon Cheongun's equipment (yellow grade)

Cheongun's equipment is the top yellow-grade Panokseon gear, crafted on Cheongsa Island 1, in its Level 2 Cheongsa Ship Part Workshop. That house offers a Level 3 Storage, a Level 1 Shipyard (Panokseon crafting) and the workshop (Byukgye and Cheongun crafting). Each part needs Sturdy Coral Support x125, Raging Wave Plywood x75, Dormant Crimson Coral Adhesive x50, Cheongun's Panokseon Parts Upgrade Permit x1, a +10 Panokseon: Byukgye's piece and Blueprint: Cheongun x10.

Material recipes (Mass Process uses 10x materials and 1 Black Stone Powder per batch):
- Sturdy Coral Support: Lyngbakr's Bone x1, Starlight Hardener x1, Starlight Emulsifier x1 (Manufacture).
- Raging Wave Plywood: Lyngbakr's Scale x1, Starlight Hardener x1, Starlight Emulsifier x1 (Manufacture).
- Dormant Crimson Coral Adhesive: Lyngbakr's Fluid x1, Starlight Hardener x1, Starlight Emulsifier x1 (Simple Alchemy).

Wharf Manager Gangman on Cheongsa Island sells the Upgrade Permit for 5,000,000,000 Silver, and gives a Cheongun blueprint for Sunset Coral Essence x4.

## Panokseon yellow-grade equipment enhancement

Yellow-grade Panokseon equipment is enhanced with Sunset Tidal Black Stone x1, made through Processing (L) Heating from Sunset Coral Essence x1 and Tidal Black Stone x100 (Mass Process uses 10x materials and 1 Black Stone Powder). Tidal Black Stones can be got at the Crow Coin Exchange for Lyngbakr's Bone x8, Scale x12 or Fluid x20.

A full set of four Panokseon: Cheongun parts at +10 gives Speed 25.00%, Acceleration 17.00%, Turn 18.00%, Brake 18.00%, Extra Damage 72,600 and a weight of 7,400 LT.
