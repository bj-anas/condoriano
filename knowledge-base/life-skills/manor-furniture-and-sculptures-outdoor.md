# Manor Furniture and Sculptures (Outdoor)

Outdoor-only manor furniture, sculptures, fountains, lamps, turf, shrubs, trees and flowers, grouped by category with the main ways to obtain each. The wiki page has the full item descriptions and complete recipes.

## Manor outdoor furniture overview

The large outdoor area seen from a manor's entrance is what sets a manor apart from a normal residence. The outdoor-only items on this page are fountains, gazebos, lamps, ponds, trophies, chairs, a shed, turf tiles, maze shrubs, trees, flowers, raised beds and tulips. Most craftable items are made at a level 5 Furniture Workshop. More manor furniture is on the wiki pages Manor Furniture and Lighting (Indoor) and Manor Furniture and Lighting (Indoor & Outdoor).

## Manor outdoor furniture: effects

The wiki lists no stat buffs for the outdoor manor pieces; they decorate the garden and grounds. Lamps such as the Runn Lamp, Dim Runn Lamp and Little Sun provide light at night. Fountains, a gazebo, a pond, a shed, chairs and a table add seating and scenery, while turf tiles, maze shrubs, trees, flowers and raised beds are used to lay out the manor's yard in different shapes.

## Manor golden animal fountains: how to obtain

The Rearing Golden Horse Fountain, Leaping Golden Deer Fountain and Dreaming Golden Mermaid Fountain are three-tiered fountains crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop. Each needs one golden statue, three fountain tiers and Purified Water x1,500 (made by Filtering in Processing from river water, turbid water or clean water bottles, or Cactus Sap or Bag of Muddy Water x5; also sold by General Goods Vendors, Waida, Noura, Yasum and the Central Market).

| Tier | Sturdy Snow White Stone | Powder of Rifts | Gold Ingot | Platinum Ingot | Oil of Regeneration |
| First Tier | 250 | 500 | 20 | 20 | 50 |
| Second Tier | 150 | 300 | 30 | 30 | 30 |
| Third Tier | 100 | 200 | 40 | 40 | 20 |

The mermaid fountain's tiers take 90, 60 and 40 minutes to craft.

## Manor golden statues for the fountains: how to obtain

Golden Horse Statue: exchange with Gula at Stonetail Horse Ranch for Grand Prix Gold Trophy x3, Silver Trophy x7 and Bronze Trophy x15, or Grand Prix Participant Certificate x25.

Golden Deer Statue: either finish the quest [Manor] When the Forest Lost the Light and exchange with Lejenti at Narcion for Narc's Wishstone x10 or Hunter's Token x150, or finish [Manor] Hope of the Ahib and exchange with Remitaronsom at the Tooth Fairy Cabin for Mournful Crystal of the Forest x20 or Hunter's Token x150.

Golden Mermaid Statue: finish the quest from Naruo at the Slippery Scallywags Isle to get the Clickety-clack Key, then open the Slippery Scallywags Treasure Chest buried under the sea near that isle.

## Manor outdoor lamps and gazebo: how to obtain

Runn Lamp: crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop. First learn the Inextinguishable Stone Knowledge, then find Shakatu to complete a quest giving the Runn Light Fragment Knowledge. Needs Lamp Post x1, Alchemist's Small Lamp x5 and Runn Light Fragment x5.

Dim Runn Lamp: same knowledge requirement. Needs Tidal Blue Lamp Post x1 (Crow Coin x500 at the Crow Shop), Hero's Small Lamp x3 (Crow Coin x100) and Runn Light Fragment x3, made with an Alchemy Tool.

Little Sun: made by Manufacture from Inextinguishable Stone x1 and Alchemist's Lamp x1. The stone comes from exchanging Charred Stone x100 with Yisar Pjetyo, the Alchemist in Tarif, or from learning the Inextinguishable Stone Knowledge in the Calpheon library and using Alchemy.

Skabiosa's Gazebo: crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from Flondor Breeze Ceiling x1, Okiara Ripple Effect Floor x1 and Loopy Tree Forest Pillars x3, each crafted from materials including Piece of Image and Design items.

## Manor ponds and fountains: how to obtain

Flondor Pond: a Grándiha style outdoor stone pond. Needs Grándiha Style Outdoor Stone Base x1, Miloberry's Apu Apu Waterstarwort x100, Vilentia's Vedelona Bouquet x2 (from the quest [Manor][Repeat] Vilentia's Vedelona Bouquet, Vilentia the Seed Vendor in Grána) and Bottle of River Water x1,000.

Lion's Reach Fountain: crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from the Northern Heidel Gothic Fountain (quest [Manor] Northern Heidel Gothic Fountain, from Jemkas Wyrmsbane in Northern Heidel Quarry) and Lion's Gaze (quest [Manor] Lion's Gaze, from Bianstimi, Jeweler in Calpheon).

Fountain of Blessed Springs: three parts are combined by Bindo, the blacksmith in the Land of the Morning Light: Fountain of Divine Melodies from Crow's Nest, Heaven's Tower from a treasure chest on a legendary island at Oquilla's Eye opened with the Ocean Horizon Key, and Wings of Frozen Tides from the leader of Papua Crinea with Iridescent Pebble and Blue Spirit Essence.

Morning Mist's Embrace: a Duvencrune hot spring bath crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from Bath of Origins x1, Jar of Dawn's Mist x6 (10 million Silver from Pataro at the Morning Fog Post), Fine Rectangular Thuja Water Frame Base x1, Vilentia's Vedelona Bouquet x2 and Dragon's Tears x10 (needs Dragon's Tears Knowledge and an Alchemy Tool).

## Manor trophies and ornaments: how to obtain

These are combined in order from top to bottom in Yaz's Inventory.

Life Guru Trophy: Pinnacle of Honor x1, given by an NPC who visits the manor once you reach Guru 1 in any Life Skill category, plus Pedestal of Honor x1 crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop.

Laurus Nobilis Trophy: Laurus Nobilis Nest x1, from Laurel Leaves x15 exchanged with Lara after the quest [Manor][Weekly] Laurel Leaves from Melissa Brady, plus Angel's Pedestal x1 crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop.

Stella's Hope Ornament: Vilentia's Vedelona Bouquet x1 and Vase of Florin's Hope x1, from the quest [Manor][Daily] Vase of Florin's Hope given by Izella in Florin.

Glory of Heidel: Pedestal of the People's Throne x1 and Crown of Honor x1, both crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop, plus Indomitable Lion of Serendia x1 from the quest [Manor][Daily] The Indomitable Lion Fighting the Darkness, from Lejenti in Glish.

## Manor outdoor seating, table and shed: how to obtain

Marzana's Starpaved Table and Marzana's Starpaved Chair come from the repeatable quests [Manor][Repeat] Marzana's Starpaved Table and Chair, given by Marzana, the leader of the Lavania League.

Breaking Wave Chair: bought from Benns Ruberen in Grándiha for 3 million Silver.

Stonetail Outdoor Shed: crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from Sturdy Timber Square x8, Sheltering Roof x1, Sturdy Stone Floor x1 and Clip-clop Stonetail Mobile x1 (quest [Manor][Daily] Clip-clop Stonetail Mobile, from Shamhain at the Stonetail Horse Ranch).

## Manor tulips: how to obtain

[Manor] Budding, Blossoming and Fully Bloomed tulips come in red and yellow. They are obtained by farming Tulip Seed bought from Norma Leight at Northern Wheat Plantation. Norma Leight also exchanges tulips of one color for the other: Budding Red Tulip x5 for one Budding Yellow or Budding Red Tulip, Blossoming Red Tulip x3 for one Blossoming Yellow or Red Tulip, and Fully Bloomed Red Tulip x2 for one Fully Bloomed Yellow or Red Tulip.
