# Processing

Guide to the Processing life skill: what it does, the processing types, level, success rate, the removed knowledge system, mass processing with processing stones, and quests.

## Processing: what it is and how to use it

Processing changes the form or properties of materials you collect from farming, fishing, lumbering, and hunting through simple procedures. Unlike cooking or alchemy, its output is not a finished product but an advanced material for other life skills such as cooking, alchemy, and workshop crafting.

Open the Processing window (hotkey: L), pick the type, right-click items in your inventory to select them, then press start. You need at least 2 free inventory slots and must be under your weight limit. Most types have no special requirement, but drying only works on clear days, filtering needs wind in the area, and indoor repair needs an anvil installed in your residence.

## Processing types: shaking, grinding and chopping

- Shaking: mixes two materials. It is typically used to mix water with a processed cooking ingredient to make dough, to make crop fertilizer, or to improve gems, hides, and feathers.
- Grinding: uses a mortar. It mainly makes flour and fabric threads, removes impurities from some gemstones, and turns stones, crystals, and gems into powder.
- Chopping: typically turns timber into planks and planks into plywood.

## Processing types: drying, filtering and heating

- Drying: only available on clear days and on land. It removes moisture. Typical uses are making fertilizer, refining animal hide, and preserving fish or meat for a long time.
- Filtering: only available when wind is blowing. A sifter sorts valuable materials from a dense pack. It thins out feathers and also gives purified water and alluvial gold.
- Heating: melts a material with strong fire to change its form or extract something new. It works on metals, can transform magic crystals, improve plywood, and melt equipment to get its component parts.

## Processing types: repair, simple alchemy and simple cooking

- Repair: done at an anvil installed in your residence, so you can repair equipment yourself instead of visiting a blacksmith NPC. You repair one piece at a time, but you can start a repair, then switch characters, and the original shows as repairing.
- Simple Alchemy: quick on-the-spot alchemy from simple ingredients with no alchemy tool. It combines herbs and mineral water into herbal juice, improves elixirs and magic crystals, and crafts some enhancement materia. Elaborate alchemy needs a tool in a residence.
- Simple Cooking: quick cooking with no utensil. It combines grain and mineral water into grain juice, makes star anise and citron tea, and prepares some combined meals. The wider range of dishes needs a utensil in a residence.

## Processing types: imperial, guild and manufacture

- Imperial Cuisine and Alchemy: packs dishes and elixirs into boxes that imperial delivery NPCs buy for silver. The wiki has a separate Imperial Crafting Delivery page.
- Guild Processing: makes materials for guild crafting. The wiki points to its Guild House page.
- Manufacture: crafts a wide range of items such as life skill mastery equipment, certain accessories, residence decorations, and instruments.

Choosing a type in the Processing window activates the matching entries in the Processing Knowledge list, where you can check recipes.

## Processing level

A higher processing level gives more resulting items from the same materials and a higher success rate. It can also give a higher-grade product directly. For example, heating iron ore normally gives melted iron shards, which are heated again for iron ingots, but at a high processing level, heating iron ore can give iron ingots directly.

You cannot process when your weight reaches 100% of your limit, so mass processing from a full inventory is hard. A character costume from the Pearl Shop lets you process materials that are still in storage.

## Processing success rate

While processing you may see a message that it is not going as planned or is taking longer than usual. This depends on your processing success rate. A failure does not lose materials and does not change the amount or quality of the output. It only costs time.

To raise the success rate, wear silver embroidered craftsman's clothes, activate certain alchemy stones, and use specific dishes, elixirs, and draughts.

## Processing Knowledge removed

Since the January 3rd, 2024 update, you no longer need Processing Knowledge such as Heating or Filtering to process higher-grade materials, and those knowledges were deleted. If you already had them, the Black Spirit offers these quests:

| Quest | Condition | Rewards |
| [Life] Gift for the Processing Trainee | Have the Beginner knowledge of Shaking, Filtering, Grinding, Heating, Chopping, Drying | Contribution EXP 300, Processing EXP 2,000, Scorched Fragment x5, Leather Glaze x10, Gem Polisher x10, Plywood Hardener x10, Metal Solvent x10 |
| [Life] Gift for the Processing Expert | Have the Skilled knowledge of the same six | Contribution EXP 1,000, Processing EXP 5,000, Scorched Fragment x10, Leather Glaze x50, Gem Polisher x50, Plywood Hardener x50, Metal Solvent x50 |

## Processing mastery and mass processing

Each life skill has a mastery value that depends on your skill level and equipment. Processing mastery enables mass processing: several batches of the same item in one action. For example, if iron ore normally processes x5 per sequence, mastery might allow x20 batches at once, so x100 iron ore in a single action. It takes longer than one batch but less than doing them one by one.

Mass processing needs a processing stone equipped, which enables the Mass Process option in the Processing window. Hover over the processing section in the Life Skill tab of your profile (hotkey: P) to see how many batches you can do at once. In the wiki's example, a single action used 235 of 1,000 birch timber, which is 47 times the normal 5.

## Processing stones: where to get them

- Green grade: sold by NPCs Camellia Loggia and Zaaira in Balenos.
- Blue grade: sold by Zaaira in Velia, Techthon in Heidel, and several others around the world.
- Yellow grade: craft it yourself or buy it on the Central Market. Craft it through manufacture from Magical Shard x50, Manos Stone x5, Gem Polisher x15, and Moonlight Opal x30.

## Processing quests

Many NPCs give processing quests. The wiki recommends starting in Balenos. Rewards include contribution EXP, processing EXP, and rare items such as hard and sharp black crystal shards. Leap quests help reach higher levels faster, and the first unlocks at processing beginner Lv. 10. Summon the Black Spirit and open its guide menu to find leap quests for all life skills and be guided to the NPC.
