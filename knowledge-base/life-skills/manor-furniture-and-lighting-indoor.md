# Manor Furniture and Lighting (Indoor)

Furniture, lighting and decor that can only be placed inside a manor, with how to obtain each piece.

## Manor indoor furniture overview

The items on this page can be installed only inside the manor. They are the Starspun Chandelier, Sunflare Chandelier, Herald of Dawn Fireplace, A Breezy Day at the Manor, Glory of Serendia Wall Decor, Handcrafted Ancient Kingdom Bookshelf, Aal's Sanctuary Candle Stand, Handcrafted Serazad Chair and Bedside Table, and the Olvian set (Wardrobe, Dining Table, Bedside Table, Bookshelf, Chair, Sofa, Bed, Table and Drawers). Most crafted pieces are made at a level 5 Furniture Workshop. More manor furniture is on the wiki pages for outdoor and indoor and outdoor items.

## Manor indoor furniture: effects

The wiki lists no stat buffs for the indoor manor pieces; they add luxury and decoration inside the manor. Chandeliers, the candle stand and the fireplace provide lighting and warmth, the painting and wall decor decorate walls, and the Olvian set, Serazad pieces and Ancient Kingdom bookshelf furnish rooms with beds, sofas, wardrobes, tables, chairs and shelves.

## Manor Starspun Chandelier and Sunflare Chandelier: how to obtain

Both chandeliers come from the Ancient Kingdom of Orzeca and are crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop. Each needs a design bought from Amarin, the Luxury Vendor NPC in O'draxxia, for 200 million Silver.

Starspun Chandelier: Design: Starspun Chandelier x1, Brass Ingot x50 (Heating with Melted Copper Shard x5 and Melted Zinc Shard x5), Star Diamond x3 (Shaking with Gem Polisher x3 and Resplendent Diamond x7), Pure Gold Crystal x10 (Heating with Metal Solvent x1 and Gold Ingot x3) and Delotia-scented Candle x10.

Sunflare Chandelier: Design: Sunflare Chandelier x1, Brass Ingot x30, Gold Ingot x10 (Heating with Melted Gold Shard x10) and Delotia-scented Candle x12.

Delotia-scented Candles are made by Heating Beeswax x10, Special Delotia x10 and Knitting Yarn x1, or bought from Tama Nana in O'dyllita for 2 million Silver.

## Manor Herald of Dawn Fireplace: how to obtain

The Herald of Dawn Fireplace was made at Crow's Nest and is decorated with a mirror holding the flame and spirit of Hesed's Crystal. It is crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from five fireplace parts. For each part, fish up a special letter at the location named by Patrigio in a certain town, then take the materials listed on the letter to Lavala at Crow's Nest.

| Part | Quantity | Patrigio's town |
| Master Crow's Fireplace Mantle | 1 | Calpheon |
| Master Crow's Fireplace Surround | 1 | Velia |
| Master Crow's Fireplace Overmantel | 1 | Drieghan |
| Master Crow's Fireplace Pillar | 2 | Grána |
| Flawless Spirit's Mirror | 1 | O'dyllita |

## Manor wall decor and candle stand: how to obtain

A Breezy Day at the Manor is a painting by the artist Leordo Dias. Complete the quests [Manor] Some Visiting Artist and [Manor] A Breezy Day at the Manor, starting from Leordo Dias.

Glory of Serendia Wall Decor: crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from Crown of Honor (reward of the quest [Manor] Lion that Serenades the Sun II) and Lion that Serenades the Sun (exchanged for 200 Energy with Eil after finishing that quest).

Aal's Sanctuary Wall Candle Stand: made by Manufacture from Aal's Sanctuary Candle Stand x1 and Delotia-scented Candle x3. The stand comes from handing the Golden Mane Glass, fished at Ibellab Oasis, to Kiyak, the Bookseller in Valencia City.

## Manor Handcrafted Ancient Kingdom Bookshelf and Serazad furniture: how to obtain

Handcrafted Ancient Kingdom Bookshelf: crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop from three items, each from a daily quest started by Denulo at the Crypt of Resting Thoughts in O'dyllita: Restored Orzeca Bookshelf x1, Restored Orzeca Golden Crown Decoration x1 and An Anthology of the Ancient Kingdom x1.

Handcrafted Serazad Chair and Handcrafted Serazad Bedside Table: obtained from the daily quests [Manor][Daily] Handcrafted Serazad Chair and [Manor][Daily] Handcrafted Serazad Bedside Table, started by Dellus, Shakatu's Right-hand Man.

## Manor Olvian crafted furniture: how to obtain

The Olvian Wardrobe, Bookshelf, Sofa and Bed are crafted at a level 5 Furniture Workshop. Each needs a design: complete the consecutive quests [Manor] Olvian Furniture Set from Wocester, or complete the Rare Hunting Guide category and get the design from Wocester for Energy, or exchange Energy-exuding Hide from hunting rare animals. Materials:

| Item | Sturdy Maple Plywood | Sturdy Pine Plywood | Plywood Hardener | Other materials |
| Olvian Wardrobe | 15 | 15 | 6 | Moss Tree Sap x50, Black Crystal x20 |
| Olvian Bookshelf | 40 | 40 | 16 | Moss Tree Sap x160, Translucent Crystal x20 |
| Olvian Sofa | 25 | 25 | 10 | Moss Tree Sap x100, Red Crystal x20 |
| Olvian Bed | 10 | 10 | 4 | Dawn Fallen Silk x5, Soft Ferri Feather x300 |

Each Sturdy Plywood is made by Heating Plywood Hardener x3 with Maple or Pine Plywood x10. Plywood Hardener is made by Alchemy from Pure Powder Reagent x1, Bloody Tree Knot x3, Fir Sap x3 and Trace of Nature x3. Moss Tree Sap is gathered from Moss Tree with a Fluid Collector. Dawn Fallen Silk is Grinding Oil of Storms x50 with Silk x50, and Soft Ferri Feather comes from tanning after killing Ferris.
