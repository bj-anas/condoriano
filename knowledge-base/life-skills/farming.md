# Farming

Farming is the life skill of renting fences, setting up gardens, planting and tending crops, harvesting them and breeding better seeds. This page covers fences, planting, crop care, special seeds, moles and farming quests.

## Farming overview

Farming lets a player rent fences and install them in the world to create gardens. Inside a garden you plant seeds, hyphae and haystacks and harvest what grows. Plant breeding gives better quality seeds that can be replanted for better results, and haystacks are used to raise cows for milk. The main resource needed to start is contribution points, because fences are rented from NPCs rather than bought. A player can have a maximum of 10 fenced gardens, and keeping them together in one area is suggested.

## Farming fence types and rental

Fences cannot be bought; they are rented from NPCs with contribution points, and returning a fence refunds the points. Maximum crop yield is the number of planting slots.

## Farming Old Moon Fence

The Old Moon Fence costs 10 contribution points, the same as a strong fence, and holds the same 10 plant slots, but it is only about half the size, so it is easier to install and maintain. It can be rented at any time from the Old Moon Manager NPC in each major town, but it can only be installed and used once the character reaches farming master 1. It still counts toward the fence rental limit, so a player already holding 10 strong fences must return some first. The Find the NPC feature (magnifying glass icon beside the minimap) locates the managers.

## Farming installing fences and managing a garden

Fences can be installed in non-safe-zone areas, which is most of the world. Right-click the fence in the inventory to enter Place Mode, then position it with the cursor; it can only be placed where the install box shows green. Avoid rocks and trees, and place fences near areas you visit often. After installing, stand inside the fence and press R to manage the garden.

The Garden icon below the health bar lists installed gardens, and the Worker icon there sends a worker. The My Gardens window shows each fence's territory, type, crop and assigned worker, lets you create a path to it, and warns with a yellow icon at 7 days and a red icon at 2 days before Scheduled Removal Time.

## Farming planting seeds and climate

Seeds come from gathering bushes and plants, NPC purchases, or quest rewards. Haystacks are made by drying x50 weeds. A garden holds seeds, hyphae and haystacks; haystacks take up 4 slots, and each seed's description states its slot use. Planting works like placing furniture: choose a seed in the window and click a spot in the garden. New plants start at 0% growth.

Climate matters. Check the World Map, including the Temperature icon, because some crops grow faster in humid areas and others in dry ones; Dry Mane Grass grows faster in warm places. A plant's knowledge entry hints at its natural habitat. Aloe, found in Mediah, a dry region, is best planted in similar arid areas.

## Farming crop care: pests, pruning and water

Crops are sensitive to humidity and temperature and are threatened by pests, blights and birds. Pruning needs and blights appear randomly at a fixed chance, and they greatly slow growth until fixed. A red fence icon on the World Map (hotkey M) means a problem, and a growth bar turning red means a pest or tangled branches. Approach the crop and press R to prune or kill insects, which costs 1 energy, gives farming EXP and may give items such as black crystal shards.

For water, carry distilled or purified water and right-click it to water the crop. Distilled water: buy an empty bottle from a Material Vendor, fill it at a river, then heat it in Processing (hotkey L). Filtering river water gives purified water, which depends on weather and can give extras like alluvial gold.

## Farming scarecrows, irrigation and workers

A scarecrow keeps birds away so crops grow faster, and an irrigation system keeps crops watered. To obtain them, buy a town house, set it as a carpentry workshop, and send a worker to craft them there.

A worker assigned to a garden prunes and kills insects, which gives steadier growth, but cannot manage moisture or birds. Each fenced garden needs its own worker. An unattended garden lets birds and insects damage the crops, so they take longer to reach 100%. Beginners are advised to tend gardens themselves, and assign workers later once they have enough workers and contribution points for lodging.

## Farming harvesting and plant breeding

A crop can be harvested at 100% growth. Growth goes up to 200%, and harvesting at any point after 100% has no loss. There are two modes. General harvesting gives the produce, such as potatoes, citron, mushrooms from hyphae, or meat and milk from haystacks. Plant breeding tries to improve the seeds: success gives one seed of the same quality or several of better quality, and there is no penalty for breeding at any percentage above 100%. Harvesting may also give blush leaves, a life skill byproduct that can be exchanged for useful items at a designated NPC.

## Farming fence demolition

A fence that goes unused for 4 weeks after installation is demolished automatically. Planting new crops in it resets the timer to 4 weeks. When a fence is demolished, any unharvested crops are deleted and cannot be recovered, and the fence is sent to town storage in the nearest town. A player can demolish an empty fence manually; it goes to the inventory, where it can be installed elsewhere or returned to the NPC it was rented from.

## Farming Mysterious Seed

Mysterious seeds drop at a low chance from plant breeding. Shaking a mysterious seed together with special or magical crop seeds produces mysterious crop seeds, which yield more crops. They take 5 times the normal number of garden slots but the yield is correspondingly greater, which can reduce maintenance time. Breeding them gives a sizable amount of fruit at a fixed chance, useful for alchemy ingredients, but breeding does not produce further mysterious crop seeds, so they are single use.

## Farming Magical Seed

Magical seeds are only available at farming level artisan 1 or higher, by breeding crops to improve seed quality. They also take 5 times the normal slots, so harvesting them equals planting 5 normal seeds. Mysterious seed icons show a glowing aura that magical seed icons lack. Unlike mysterious seeds, 1 or more magical seeds drop at a fixed chance, so they are self-sustaining, but breeding them only gives alchemy fruit at a certain random chance. Use magical seeds at artisan 1 to save planting time, and mysterious seeds if you lack that level or need alchemy ingredients.

To make a magical seed, shake x1 special seed, x2 mysterious seeds and x10 purified water together; the same works for hyphae.

## Farming quests

Farming quests come from many NPCs. Liana, found in all major towns, offers [Farming] Know Anything About Farming? at Lv. 20, which teaches the basics and rewards a unique fence. Starting at farming level beginner 10, leap quests are available, giving contribution EXP, farming EXP and rare items such as sharp black crystal shards. Summon the Black Spirit and open its guide menu to see the leap quests for all life skills, and click one to be guided to its NPC.

## Farming Klau's Old Moon Seed Pouch

Klau's Old Moon Seed Pouch stores up to 50 seeds in one place. Seeds must be moved to the normal inventory before planting. A player at Farming Skilled 1 or higher obtains it by accepting and completing the quest [Farming] Klau's Old Moon Seed Pouch from the Black Spirit.

## Farming moles

Harvesting or breeding crops has a low chance of spawning moles nearby; an item called Mole's Feed in the loot window signals that they are coming. Up to 3 moles can spawn. A message states how many and which type, yellow beams mark where they appear, and they flee if not defeated quickly. Moles drop large amounts of byproducts and rare items such as the Pit-A-Pat Petal; 10 petals are exchanged for one Pit-A-Pat Mole pet.

The Pit-A-Pat Mole pet can only be exchanged with another of the same type, and Wizard Gosphy Pet cannot raise its tier. Its talent is Life EXP +1% (up to 4% depending on pet tier), and its special skill is Finds resources.
