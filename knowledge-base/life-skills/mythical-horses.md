# Mythical Horses

How Dream Horses awaken into Mythical Arduanatt, Mythical Diné, and Mythical Doom, including the Mythical Censer recipe, inherent skills, Krogdalo's Sanctuary, and the free Choose Your Mythical Horse Box.

## Mythical Horses: what they are

Dream Horses can be reborn through awakening as mythical horses. Currently Arduanatt, Diné, and Doom can do this, and Dream Horses themselves come from awakening coursers (see the Training page). Mythical horses get a new appearance and exclusive inherent skills. Mythical Arduanatt can hover in midair or dive fast during a glide. Mythical Diné can run across water or jump up to high places. Mythical Doom hits harder and can call up copies of itself.

Awakening needs Lv. 30 or higher Dream Horses (male and female) and a mythical censer. Mythical horses cannot be registered on the horse market and have no gender.

## Mythical Awakening: where, requirements and steps

Mythical awakening is done at the stable keeper Gula at the Stonetail Horse Ranch in Mediah (east of Heidel, north of Tarif), or at Melabee, the stable keeper in Grána, Kamasylvia. You need one male and one female Lv. 30 Dream Horse of the same type: for Mythical Arduanatt, Arduanatt; for Mythical Diné, Diné; for Mythical Doom, Doom. A mythical censer is consumed in each attempt. Steps:

1. Check in the Dream Horses at the stable.
2. Select them, then choose Mythical Awakening.
3. On the Mythical Awakening UI, press the + buttons to add the male and the female horse.
4. Register the mythical censer by pressing the center icon.
5. Enter the name of the mythical horse and proceed.

## Mythical Awakening: success rate

The base success rate is 3%. On success the two Dream Horses disappear and fuse into your new mythical horse. On failure the Dream Horses can be recovered and you can try again with another censer. Each failure raises the success rate by 0.2%, and the increase is shared across your whole family, so it applies even if you try with other Dream Horses.

## Mythical Censer recipe and where materials come from

Mythical Censer is made through manufacture (hotkey: L) from Old Moon Censer x1, Mythical Powder x10, Mythical Feather x10, Fire Horn x10, and Powder of Flame x10. Censer materials come from imperial horse delivery, world and field bosses, weekly quests at the Stonetail Horse Ranch, and exchanging fairy wings from lakiaro sacks, among other sources.

## Mythical Censer: Old Moon Censer and Mythical Powder

- Old Moon Censer (manufacture, L), three ways: Old Moon Censer Mold x1 + Pure Platinum Crystal x100 + Pure Copper Crystal x100 + Pure Tin Crystal x100 + Moonlight Opal x100; or Old Moon Censer Mold x1 + Magical Lightstone Crystal x300 + Old Moon Alchemy Catalyst x400 + Copper Ingot x1,000 + Tin Ingot x1,000; or Old Moon Censer Mold x1 + Melody of the Stars x25 + Pure Copper Crystal x100 + Pure Tin Crystal x100 + Magical Lightstone Crystal x300.
- Old Moon Censer Mold: buy from an Old Moon Manager NPC's shop.
- Mythical Powder (grinding, L): Flower of Oblivion x10 + Royal Fern Root x10 + Everlasting Herb x10 + Fruit of Nature x10 + Black Stone Powder x10.
- Flower of Oblivion: imperial horse delivery (tier 6 to 8).
- Royal Fern Root: the quest [Weekly] Fly Like the Wind, or the quest No Worries for the Prepared (available beginner to guru) via Wapra, the Material Vendor NPC at the Stonetail Horse Ranch, or Liana in any major city.

## Mythical Censer: where to get Mythical Feathers

Mythical Feather sources:

1. Exchange radiant fairy wings and brilliant fairy wings with Herawen, chancellor of the Kamasylve Temple in Mediah.
2. Complete [Weekly] I, Too, Wish for Wings via Variel, the Stable Keeper NPC at the Old Wisdom Tree in Kamasylvia.
3. Low-chance drop from the Lush primitive lakiaro sack, Primitive lakiaro sack, Lush old lakiaro sack, and Old lakiaro sack.
4. Low-chance drop from reward chests after defeating Dark Rift Bosses.
5. Quests [Weekly] [Crossroad] Krogdalo's Origin Stone Support or [Weekly] [Crossroad] [Beginner-Guru] Carrot Confit Support.
6. Old Moon Grand Prix weekly quests from Jetina: [Weekly] Old Moon Grand Prix, Rider of Honor I, II, and III.
7. Loot from World Boss Offin.
8. Low chance from Patrigio's Secret Shop (between 10 pm and 7 am).

## Mythical Arduanatt skills and appearance

Mythical Arduanatt can leap quickly in midair with Mighty Leap, float with Soaring Wings, and descend fast while gliding from high places with Wings of Swiftness. Inherent skills stay even if you reset the horse's growth.

| Skill | Preceding skills | Use | Effect |
| Mighty Leap | Instant Accel, S: Instant Accel, High Jump, Double Jump | E during Instant Accel or S: Instant Accel (400 stamina) | Leap high into the air; unavailable with weapon unsheathed |
| Soaring Wings | Start Accel | W + E while idle, or Space to lift front legs, then E (400 stamina, continuous) | Float in midair for a while; unavailable with weapon unsheathed |
| Wings of Swiftness | Wings of Wind, Wings of Freedom | F during Wings of Freedom (no stamina), or Q during Wings of Freedom | Descend faster, or dash forward with Q |

Quturan's Fruit changes a Mythical Arduanatt's basic look to the alternative appearance when fed to your stabled horse, keeping all skills and stats. Buy it for 100 million Silver each from Francia in O'dyllita.

## Mythical Diné skills and Breath of Okiara

Mythical Diné uses Quicksilver to run on water, including Sprint and Drift there. It jumps to high places with Triple Jump and floats down with Protection of Okiara. Inherent skills stay after a growth reset.

| Skill | Preceding skills | Use | Effect |
| Quicksilver | None | Automatic when moving on water (consumes Breath of Okiara) | Gallop across water, Sprint and Drift work as on land |
| Triple Jump | Streak Leap, High Jump, Double Jump | Space during Double Jump (200 stamina) | Another high leap, higher over water |
| Protection of Okiara | None | E while falling after a jump (400 stamina, continuous) | Float safely down from heights; no fall damage for a set time |

Mythical Diné has a Breath of Okiara resource bar used for moving on water. When it runs out, you are dismounted and the horse idles on the water. The bar recovers while you are idle mounted on land, moving mounted on land, or when the horse is idle on land.

## Mythical Doom skills

Mythical Doom has three unique skills, led by Shadows of Hellfire, which summons two more Dooms for guild or party members to ride. Each Doom (the mythical one and the summoned ones) can carry two riders, so up to 6 people travel together. Inherent skills stay after a growth reset. Summoned Dooms can sprint without a saddle, and cannot equip gear or be inspected.

| Skill | Preceding skills | Use | Effect |
| Shadows of Hellfire | None | Down + E (400 stamina) | Summons two Mythical Dooms the caster, guild, party, or allies can ride. The caster gets the Trace of Shadows buff for 20 min; while it is active the skill instead gives up to 10 nearby allies Movement speed +5% for 1 min. Summoned Dooms vanish after 10 min or when the caster logs out |
| Infernal Sprint | Dark Sprint | Automatic after Dark Sprint (600 stamina) | Fast forward melee dash; flames around hit targets deal damage and HP +200 every 3 sec for 15 sec for the caster; flames damage everyone except the user |
| Infernal Legion | One or more of Charge, S: Charge, Instant Accel, S: Instant Accel, Dark Sprint, Infernal Sprint | Up + E while using one of those skills (400 stamina) | Gradual flame damage to enemies ahead; cooldown 10 seconds |

## Krogdalo's Sanctuary

Krogdalo's Sanctuary is a treasure item that stores and retrieves the 3 Dream Horse types anywhere, anytime. You can only get it after owning all three mythical horses: Mythical Arduanatt, Mythical Diné, and Mythical Doom.

To obtain it, ride one of the three mythical horses to Merindora in Grána and accept the [Krogdalo's Sanctuary] questline. You must present each mythical horse to Krogdalo's trace in Polly's Forest. Completing all the quests awards the [Celestial Pathfinder] title.

To use it, click the horse icon at the upper left of the screen to open the Krogdalo's Sanctuary UI and store one of each mythical horse. Stored horses can be retrieved while idle and swapped.

Restrictions: you cannot store Krogdalo's Sanctuary in storage if any mythical horse has been retrieved from it, you cannot retrieve horses in the Red Battlefield or Battle Arena, and you cannot store a mythical horse carrying trade goods.

## Choose Your Mythical Horse Box

For the Black Desert 10th anniversary, you can get a first Mythical Horse free from the Challenge window (hotkey: Y). Steps:

1. Open the Challenge window and claim the reward for Embark with a Mythical Horse.
2. Use the Choose Your Mythical Horse Box.
3. Pick the emblem of the Mythical Horse you want and click Confirm. The choice cannot be changed.
4. Type the phrase exactly as shown in the notification window.
5. Register the emblem at a nearby stable.

The registered level 1 Mythical Horse has all skills learned and trained at 100%. Skills like Sprint and Charge need mount equipment, so equip the gear provided by the challenge. The Mythical Arduanatt from this box is winter white instead of black and red, and Quturan's Fruit cannot change its appearance.
