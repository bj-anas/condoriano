# Royal Workshop

The Royal Workshops of Seoul in the Land of the Morning Light, where workers produce and process goods for buff items and sale. This page covers getting started, production and processing nodes, unlocking workshops and worker traits.

## Royal Workshop getting started

To begin with the Royal Workshops, put 5 contribution points into the window that appears after pressing the World Map Seoul icon (hotkey M). The same window is under the main menu (Esc), Life, Royal Workshops. The workshops are split into two node types: Production (green) and Processing (yellow). Each production node has its own 5 workshops, each with its own list of goods, and each processing node has 3 workshops with their own processed items. The production and processing must use the workers and storage of Yukjo Street; workers from Bukpo or other towns cannot be sent.

## Royal Workshop production nodes

Production nodes make materials for processing. They are the Leftward Office, Rightward Office, Infirmary and Gyeonghoeru Pavilion. Use the Royal Workshop Manager NPC to unlock more workshops, paid with Morning's Gratitude Tokens. Assign workers and set the number of production cycles, choose which workshop does the task, confirm any workshop change, then give the task to the worker. A button opens the Yukjo Street storage.

Each workshop offers various materials, and one is selected by chance each day. A refresh button picks a different one, never repeating the current one. You get one free refresh per day and can hold at most one; extra refreshes cost pearls. All workshops refresh automatically at 00:00 UTC. Some produced goods are powerful buff items with a low chance of being selected, and these can be sold on the Central Market or used directly.

## Royal Workshop processing nodes

Processing nodes turn materials and goods from the production nodes into finished goods. Finished goods can be sold to Trade Manager NPCs or exchanged for silver with the Luxury Vendor NPC of Yukjo Street, Heo Seungwon. These goods are specialties rather than trade items, so you can carry them while traveling with the Magnus. Tagged characters cannot sell processed goods. Chosen items are processed and the results go to the Yukjo Street storage.

The Luxury Vendor pays 98,000,000 silver each for: Najeonheukchil Chest, Najeonpalgak Chest, Pipe, Baekseju, Deer Fur Brush, Blue-underglazed Porcelain, Gyeongguk Daejeon (Replica), Cloudcaller Janggu, White Porcelain, Ebongild Chest, Bronze Movable Type and Royal Edict.

## Royal Workshop unlocking workshops and managers

Each node begins with one basic workshop. Crafting goods in the Royal Workshops earns Morning's Gratitude Tokens, which unlock more workshops through a Royal Workshop Manager NPC. Four production workshops cost 500 tokens each: Hamhong Gate in Gyeonghoeru Pavilion, Byeoljwa in the Infirmary, Ministry of Law in the Leftward Office, and Ministry of Treasury in the Rightward Office. All other workshops, production or processing, cost 1,000 tokens. To find the manager, press the node in the Royal Workshops UI and use the arrow next to the NPC name at the top left.

## Royal Workshop worker traits and skills

Workers in the Royal Workshops get certain bonuses. For all tasks, skills that boost worker stats apply (Work Supervisor, Simple, Lucky Guy). For production, the Shellfolk worker's Production Increase trait applies (harvest amount +68.4%), and the worker's Luck stat affects additional items such as Some Farmer's, Miner's or Fisher's Sack. For processing, the Thrifty A, B and C skills apply, with a chance to return 10% of one crafting material.

Royal workshop tasks ignore the worker's Movement Speed stat. Three skills have no effect there: Adv. Ore Packing (Extra Work +3 for Ore Packing), General Knowledge (Refinery/Specialty Work Speed +5) and Workshop Knowledge (Weapon/Armor Work Speed +5).
