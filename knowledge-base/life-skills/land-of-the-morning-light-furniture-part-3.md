# Land of the Morning Light Furniture - Part 3

Land of the Morning Light themed manor furniture, part 3 of 3: palace benches, wardrobes and palanquins, potted trees, ceremonial items, bronze and celadon antiques, and Sajikdan altar pieces. All are crafted at a Furniture Workshop in the Land of the Morning Light.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 3 overview

Part 3 holds palace and ceremonial themed manor furniture: Palace Bench, Nacre Wardrobe, Small Palanquin, Palanquin, Curved and Leaning Potted Trees, Clepsydra, Palace Ceremonial Mugo, Shade, Lantern and Scroll, Stone Turtle Statue, Red Nacre Closet, the Gold Crown from Cheonmachong Tomb, assorted bronze and celadon items, Sajikdan Altar pieces and the Yukjo Street Parasol. Parts 1 and 2 on the wiki cover other Land of the Morning Light furniture.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 3: effects

The wiki lists no stat buffs for the Part 3 items; they are decorative palace, ceremonial and antique pieces. The Palace Bench is a wooden bench for sitting or lying down that can be placed both indoors and outdoors. The Clepsydra is a water clock, and the palanquins, parasol, drums, flags, scrolls and lanterns are ceremonial or royal court decor.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 3 palace furniture needing permits: how to obtain

The Palace Bench, Nacre Wardrobe, Small Palanquin and Palanquin are crafted at a level 3 Furniture Workshop in the Land of the Morning Light. Each needs Furniture Crafting Permit x5, bought for 10 million Silver from Sealus, Lebyos, Lylina, Verosi, Vatputa, Talishia, Siemo, Kesharu or Vatu.

| Item | Other materials |
| Palace Bench | Brass Ingot 50, Fir Sap 150, Fir Plywood 200 |
| Nacre Wardrobe | Brass Ingot 70, Fir Sap 150, Fir Plywood 200 |
| Small Palanquin | Brass Ingot 50, Standardized Timber Square 150, Fir Plywood 200 |
| Palanquin | Brass Ingot 50, Standardized Timber Square 150, Fir Plywood 200 |

Brass Ingot is Heating with Melted Copper Shard x5 and Melted Zinc Shard x5. Fir Sap is gathered from Fir Trees with a fluid collector. Fir Plywood is Chopping with Fir Plank x10, and Standardized Timber Square is Chopping with Usable Scantling x10.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 3 potted trees: how to obtain

The Curved Potted Tree and Leaning Potted Tree are crafted at a level 2 Furniture Workshop in the Land of the Morning Light.

| Item | Materials |
| Curved Potted Tree | Spirit's Leaf 50, Mud Crystal 100, Polished Stone 80, Black Stone Powder 175 |
| Leaning Potted Tree | Spirit's Leaf 50, Mud Crystal 125, Polished Stone 75, Black Stone Powder 150 |

Spirit's Leaf comes from lumbering trees, Mud Crystal is Heating with Rough Mud Crystal x5, Polished Stone is Grinding with Rough Stone x10, and Black Stone Powder is Grinding certain crystals and Black Stone.

## Land of the Morning Light Furniture Part 3 Sajikdan altar items and Yukjo Street Parasol: how to obtain

All are crafted at a level 3 Furniture Workshop in the Land of the Morning Light:

| Item | Materials |
| Sajikdan Altar Ritual Flag | Brass Ingot 25, Silk 75, Birch Plywood 100, Black Stone Powder 125 |
| Sajikdan Altar Yeonggo Drums | Brass Ingot 30, Iron Ingot 30, Fine Soft Hide 75, Birch Plywood 150 |
| Sajikdan Altar Ritual Scroll | Brass Ingot 20, Silk 75, Birch Plywood 100, Black Stone Powder 125 |
| Yukjo Street Parasol | Brass Ingot 30, Blue Crystal 50, Silk 100, Birch Plywood 100 |

Silk is Grinding with Silk Thread x10, Silk Thread is Heating with Silkworm Cocoon x5, Cotton Yarn is Heating with Cotton Wool x5, Birch Plywood is Chopping with Birch Plank x10, and Fine Soft Hide is Drying with Soft Hide x10. Iron Ingot is Heating with Melted Iron Shard x10, Red Crystal is Heating with Rough Red Crystal x5, Blue Crystal is Heating with Rough Blue Crystal x5, and Green Crystal is Heating with Rough Green Crystal x5.
