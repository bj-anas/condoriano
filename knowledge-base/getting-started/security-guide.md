# Security Guide

The account security options for Black Desert: changing your password and setting up OTP (One Time Password) with backup codes.

## Account security options in Black Desert

Black Desert offers several security solutions for accounts, including changing your password and setting up OTP. If you lost access to your OTP or backup codes and want to remove OTP from your account settings, contact the Customer Support Team.

## Changing your Black Desert account password

Changing your password regularly is the first security step. Reusing one password across websites is risky, because one leak exposes all accounts. To change it, log in to the official website and go to My Page. In the left menu under Account Security Center, click Change Password (or use the Change Password link on the right). That page asks for your existing password and the new one you want.

## What OTP is and why to use it

OTP (One Time Password) is a temporary password that constantly resets. It is used on top of your regular password every login, which makes the account far more secure. Once OTP is set up you must enter an OTP at every login, which adds a step. Black Desert's OTP service uses the Google OTP application (Google Authenticator) and is completely free.

## How to set up OTP on a Black Desert account

To set up OTP:

1. Sign in on the official website and open My Page > Account Security Center > OTP Settings.
2. On the OTP Settings page, register for the OTP service and log in one more time as a security check.
3. Read the conditions and details, then click Sign up for OTP.
4. Download the authentication app from the App Store or Google Play Store for your phone's OS.
5. Run the app and scan the QR code, or enter the key shown next to it.
6. Enter the verification code in the text field.
7. You then receive 10 backup codes. After that, a new OTP is generated in the authenticator app each time you log in to the game or the official website.

## OTP backup codes

After signing up for OTP you get 10 backup codes. Each code can be used only once. They can be entered instead of an OTP, or used to disable OTP, if you cannot use your authentication app because the phone is lost or broken. At login, click the "Cannot enter an OTP verification code" button to enter a backup code. Save the codes separately from your devices, by printing or writing them out. You are the only person who can access them, and support is difficult if you lose them. While logged in on the official website, OTP users can check current backup codes, generate new ones, or disable OTP on the OTP Settings page.
