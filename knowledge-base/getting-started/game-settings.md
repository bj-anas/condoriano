# Game Settings

An overview of Black Desert's game settings: Performance, Display, Audio, General and Interface settings, including graphics, camera, screenshots, alerts, language and hotkeys.

## Game Settings menu and groups

You open the game settings from the button at the bottom right of the game screen, or from the main menu (hotkey: Esc). The main settings menu offers predefined presets for different play styles. To customize yourself, use the groups on the left: Performance Settings (graphics, optimization, camera and NPCs), Display Settings (game window, graphics, camera range and effects, screenshots), Audio Settings (sound types and volume), General Settings (quality of life, names and alerts on screen, World Map, language), and Interface Settings (hotkeys, mouse, gamepad). Matching the screen and texture quality to your PC's specs gives a better environment.

## Performance Settings options

Performance Settings include options that affect game performance. Graphics & Textures and Camera Performance share their options with the Display Quality and Camera Graphics sections of Display Settings. Other options: optimal frame limit from 0 to 60; an upscale option (Normal upscale, or AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0, which requires Remastered Mode or higher); a low-power option; attack decision effects; effects when attacks and blocks miss; Performance Optimization (uses more PC memory); Effect Optimization; Character Optimization (how far away other Adventurers are visible); and hiding other Adventurers in crowded places to avoid lag.

## Workers, pets and hiding secondary NPCs

In the Workers & Pets section of Performance Settings, you can toggle whether workers employed around the world are visible, how many pets you see (all, only yours, or none), and whether certain secondary NPCs are hidden. Most secondary NPCs serve no purpose but some give or complete quests. If you cannot find a quest NPC, uncheck the hide option, because it may be hiding that NPC.

## Display Settings: game window and display quality

The Game Window section lets you choose Full Screen, Full Screen Window or Game Window, the resolution, the UI scale, and crop mode, which shrinks the area where the background and characters are shown (leaving the UI as is) so the PC has less load. The Display Quality section sets overall graphics: anti-aliasing (none, FXAA or TAA), SSAO shadows, clarity, faraway objects and NPCs, depth of field, a blink effect when attacked, less snow build-up, and monster blood and bloodstains. Optimization options remove other Adventurers' effects (you then cannot see which skills opponents use in PvP) and faraway effects, and set AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0 to Ultra Quality, Quality, Balanced or Performance. Normal upscale lowers resolution sharply but helps low-spec PCs a lot.

## Display Settings: effects, gamma and color vision

The Effects section offers photo filters for the color scheme, gamma and contrast, effect opacity and combat focus effect intensity. Raising gamma brightens dark places such as caves or night; gamma starts at 0% and the highest setting is 50%. Too high a gamma in daylight can be too bright. Color vision aid options exist for protanomaly and deuteranomaly, which change some colors so they are easier to tell apart.

## Display Settings: camera graphics

The Camera Graphics section sets the camera vision range and camera effects. A lower vision range improves performance and shows enemy movement more clearly because the view is closer. A higher range makes the character smaller and shows more terrain. Overall Camera Effect controls all camera effects, with separate sliders for Vibration, Motion Blur Effect, Camera Angle (Action) and Camera Zoom (Action). At 100% the experience is lively. If effects make you dizzy, reduce them or set them to 0%.

## Display Settings: screenshots

The Screenshots section sets the resolution of screenshots taken with the Print Screen key. Depending on your graphics card, screenshots above 4K are possible, and you can set one as your PC wallpaper with one click. If the card does not support the chosen 4K or 8K size, a normal resolution screenshot is taken. High-resolution screenshots take a few seconds to process, so take them somewhere safe, not in PvP or a combat zone. In Photo Mode, the "Set Screenshot as Background" window is not displayed. You can adjust the watermark type, location, size and transparency, and choose a logo of any country where Black Desert is serviced.

## Audio Settings

Audio Settings turn background music and combat music on or off, and sound effects and ambient sounds separately. You can toggle the alert sound for whispers, keep game sound on while the game is in the system tray, hear music near your character's location, and choose NPC voices in Korean or Japanese instead of English. Volume sliders cover overall volume, music volume, and overall sound effect volume. Sound effects also get their own sliders for others' volume, music volume, combat sound effects clarity and combat sound effects volume.

## General Settings: quality of life and quick slots

The Quality of Life section of General Settings offers combat assistance (useful for beginners), aim assistance, separate quick slots, Simplify UI mode, and a screensaver. The Quick Slot option lets you use more than the basic 10 quick slots with skills and items and place them anywhere; use Esc > UI Edit to move each slot. Unused keys such as -, = or \ can be added as quick slot shortcuts.

## General Settings: Show/Hide options

The Show/Hide section of General Settings controls what appears on screen. Names and Ranking covers your own name and HP bar, other Adventurers' character or family names, monster names and HP bars, party, guild, clan and alliance names, rankings, showing only points in the Red Battlefield, and an HP bar stacked every 1,000 HP. Characters covers highlighting yourself in safe and combat zones, NPC colors by quest status or amity, Conquest War highlights (allies green or blue, non-participants orange, enemies red), guild members in blue, party members in green, pets, your fairy, campsites, and how many maids or butlers show in your residence. Miscellaneous covers attack decision effects, defense icons, block and miss display, combo video guide for characters below Lv. 35, skill combo guide, and see-through ship masts.

## General Settings: alerts, World Map, language and miscellaneous

Alerts lets you choose which system notifications appear. New Adventurers are advised to disable most alerts until they know what each does. World Map sets 3 opening styles or no animation, and the camera angle. Language sets NPC voices (English, Korean, Japanese), the regional watermark, the chat filter, the game language (English, German, French, Spanish), and the chat channel (International, English, German, French, Spanish). Miscellaneous sets the UI theme (classic, or season: summer, winter, spring), the navigation guide style (guide arrows, marked line, or guide fairy), exact or smooth land and water paths, blocking party, guild, duel, trade and Yar! requests, minimap rotation, closing windows when hit, camera behavior during auto-run, and Razer LED effects.

## Interface Settings: hotkeys, mouse and gamepad

Interface Settings let you change keyboard hotkeys and gamepad buttons. There are Action Hotkeys (skills, using items) and UI Hotkeys (UI windows). If you register a key that is already in use, the original shortcut is removed, so check for blank shortcuts afterward. Mouse Interface options: invert the mouse horizontally and vertically, sensitivity, moving with the left mouse button (LMB), and locking the cursor inside the game display. Gamepad options include enabling a gamepad, the gamepad-only UI, vibration, sensitivity and inverted movement.
