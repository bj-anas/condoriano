# Beginner Guide (Part 2)

Part 2 of the official beginner guide: character information, skills, chat, quests, navigation, stances, buying items, resurrection and other basic tips for new Adventurers.

## My Information window in the Beginner Guide

Press "P" to open the My Information window, which is the character profile. It shows your character's health, karma, skill points, and detailed combat and life skill figures. On the combat tab, abilities are: Attack Speed (physical attack speed), Casting Speed (skill casting speed), Movement Speed (default movement speed), Critical Hit (chance to crit when an attack succeeds), Fishing (shorter wait until a fish bites), Gathering (shorter collection time), and Luck (higher item drop rate). Equipment effects, food and elixirs can boost these abilities.

## Fitness stats: Breath, Strength and Health

Fitness in the My Information window has three stats. Breath: running around without a mount gives Breath EXP, and a higher Breath level raises maximum stamina. Strength: traveling with trade packs raises Strength, which increases your weight limit. Health: eating dishes gives Health EXP, and a higher Health level raises maximum health points.

## Skills and skill points for beginners

Press "K" to open the Skill interface. Skills that require level 55 or below are learned automatically at no skill point cost. Skills and passives that add effects to those learned skills do cost skill points. Learning all Succession or Awakening skills costs around 1,000 skill points in total. Most skills use key commands rather than quick slots, and the tooltip shows the command, such as [SHIFT + LMB] or [LMB + RMB]. Practicing key commands improves combat flow.

## Resetting skills and skill points for weight limit

You can put skill points on any skill. Combat skills can be reset at any time while you are under level 56: right-click a skill to reset it, or press the "Reset All" button. The guide recommends not spending skill points on weight limit until you have unlocked all skills of your chosen fighting style (Awakening or Succession), because the weight limit skills cost a lot of skill points.

## Chat types and hotkeys

Press Enter to activate the chat window and change the chat type:

| Chat type | Hotkey | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Chat | Alt+1 | shown only to nearby Adventurers |
| Server Chat | Alt+2 | costs 1 Energy per message |
| Whisper | Alt+3 | needs another Adventurer's character or family name |
| Party Chat | Alt+4 | only while in a party |
| Guild Chat | Alt+5 | only for guild members |
| World Chat | Alt+6 | reaches everyone in the world, needs a Megaphone item that is consumed, and costs 1 Energy per message |
| Group Chat | Alt+7 | - |
| Roleplay Chat | Alt+8 | - |
| Arsha Chat | Alt+9 | - |

Megaphone items for World Chat are bought with loyalties at the Pearl Shop. Battlefield group chat is only for Adventurers in a battlefield such as the Red Battlefield.

## Customizing the chat window and the interface

To choose which chats you see, hold Ctrl and hover over the chat window until a gear icon appears. In View Chat, check or uncheck each chat type and change its color. The "Separate Chat Window" button next to a chat name splits that chat into its own window that you can reposition. To rearrange the whole game screen, enter the interface edit mode (Edit UI) from the main menu (ESC).

## Quests in the Beginner Guide

Quests from the Black Spirit or NPCs show on the tracker at the right of the screen. The quest settings window and the main Quest window let you choose which quest types to show, for example only combat quests or only fishing quests. New Adventurers are recommended to show only combat quests so they are not overwhelmed, but should turn on all types if they get stuck in a questline.

## Whether to do every quest

You do not have to do most quests, and skipping them does not hurt enjoyment of other content. Black Spirit quests are mainly about combat, the main quest and its rewards, so it is good to do them one by one until Lv. 50. They reward useful equipment and materials such as the black stone, plus Contribution experience, skill points and consumables. Some features and important items can only be unlocked by quests, which may require progressing the main quest with at least one character.

## Auto-sprint navigation and Find NPCs

To navigate to a quest destination, hover over the quest tracker, right-click the quest title or left-click the red button, then press "T" to start moving automatically (auto-sprint). Sometimes the path is set automatically, and "T" still starts the movement. To find an NPC, click the magnifying glass icon left of the minimap at the top right to open Find NPCs. Click an NPC type to path to the nearest one, or type a name in the search bar, then press "T".

## Combat stance and non-combat stance

Press "Tab" to switch between stances. Non-combat stance gives fast movement and low stamina use but you cannot use combat skills. Combat stance has slow movement and high stamina use, and it lets you use your combat skills.

## Buying items and the Central Market

Basic equipment is available from NPC vendors, and better equipment is bought through the Central Market. New players on a season are advised not to buy equipment, because main quest and season gear has a simpler progression that costs no silver, and a season character cannot equip non-season equipment. To use the Central Market, talk to an NPC titled "Central Market Director", usually in territory capitals such as Velia, Heidel and Calpheon City. Use Find NPCs to locate one.

## Resurrection, escaping and slow movement

When you die, you can resurrect at the nearest safe zone such as a town, or at the nearest node. Only places you have already visited count, so avoid dying in areas covered by fog of war on the World Map. At Lv. 20 or lower you can use Instant Resurrect in starting areas; from Lv. 21 you need Elion's Tear. If your character is stuck, press ESC and use "Escape" in the main menu, which cannot be used again for 30 minutes. Movement slows off paved roads (shown at the top of the minimap) and when you exceed your weight limit.

## Other beginner tips: fishing, gathering, camera shake and Photo Mode

For a tutorial on life skills, call the Black Spirit and press the Guide button; it also offers leap quests as your life skills level up. If camera shake in combat bothers you, adjust it at Esc > Settings > Display Settings > Camera Graphics. Press Ctrl + U for Photo Mode, which takes screenshots without the game UI.
