# Beginner Guide (Part 1)

Part 1 of the official beginner guide for Black Desert: season servers and characters, character creation, the progression path to level 61, and what to do after the season.

## Season servers and season characters in the Beginner Guide

Season servers are special servers that focus on character progression, so even new Adventurers can adapt quickly. During a season, you access that season's servers with a season character you created. A season character can be used on season servers and on normal servers. After it graduates from the season, it becomes a normal character, and you keep the level and equipment you obtained. The guide says your first character should be a season character.

## Server selection for a new character

When you log in to Black Desert you see the Server Selection screen. Except for season servers, you can freely switch between servers and reach all your characters, so the choice does not matter much. New Adventurers are recommended to use the Season-1 (Novice only) server, which is at the very top of the list and gives various progression benefits.

## Creating a season character

After choosing a server, select an empty character slot on the right, then use the "Create Season Character" button. Once customization is done, type a name, tick the Create Season Character box, and confirm with the Create Season Character button. The guide stresses that a brand-new player's first character should be a season character.

## Class selection and customization when creating a character

When creating a character, you choose a class. Videos show each class's main weapon and awakening weapon. Some classes have a "Beginner" tag, which means they are recommended for new Adventurers. Next you customize face type, hairstyle, body shape and other features. Appearance can later only be changed with Appearance Coupons and Value Packs, which are mostly obtainable from the Pearl Shop, so take your time. The Beauty Album button at the bottom of the screen lets you apply designs made by other Adventurers.

## Starting point selection for a new character

After the opening cutscenes and tutorials, you choose one of the main crossroads questlines: the Ancient Stone Chamber, the Mountain of Eternal Winter, or the Land of the Morning Light. A first-time player is recommended to start at the Ancient Stone Chamber for the full storyline and faster progression. On another character, you can instead begin with the Mountain of Eternal Winter or the Land of the Morning Light, which places you in a recently added territory and gives a more story-driven experience.

## Beginner Guide: main questline progression and early gear

At Lv. 1, the first goal is to play through the Mediah main questline segment named Reliving the Darkness, which closes with the quest Apocalyptic Prophecy. Main quests give weapons and enhancement materials, and you can reach the mid-50s in level this way. Press "O" to open the Quests window and see the active quest on the "Main" tab. Once you hit Lv. 20, the Quest Type can be switched from the bottom of the screen. A good first gear goal is to enhance the Naru gear from the main questline step by step up to PEN (V). When your inventory is full of items, use an NPC vendor's "Sell All Junk Items" function, or store items in the town storage.

## Level 56, Succession and Awakening quests

After Apocalyptic Prophecy you will likely be in the early to mid-50s. Aim for Lv. 56, then talk to the Black Spirit to accept the Awakening and Succession questlines. Monster zones in western Valencia, for example Desert Naga Temple, are good places to level up toward Lv. 56. Open the World Map (hotkey: M) or the Monster Zone Info window from the main menu (ESC) to find zones whose recommended AP is similar to or slightly below your AP. A good DP target is roughly 1.2 to 1.4 times the AP that the zone recommends. Polly's Forest, for instance, recommends AP 160+ and DP 250+. The order and appearance of the two questlines vary by class.

## Succession versus Awakening

For most classes, you choose a combat type through Awakening or Succession. With Awakening, your character uses its awakening weapon and learns new, more powerful skills for a different combat style (for example, the Awakening Ranger uses the Kamasylven Sword). With Succession, you keep your current combat and skill type and learn higher-level versions of skills (the Succession Ranger uses the Longbow). The guide recommends equipping both your main weapon and awakening weapon either way, but most damage comes from the awakening weapon in Awakening and from the main weapon in Succession.

## Reaching level 61 in the Beginner Guide

After finishing Succession and Awakening, the final season goal is Lv. 61. There is no maximum level in Black Desert, but combat strength depends more on gear than on level. There are three ways to reach Lv. 61:

1. Defeating monsters, the most basic way.
2. The "Chenga Run": leveling quickly with EXP from completing the quests of a certain territory between Lv. 59 and Lv. 61. It is normally faster than hunting. Guides exist for the Kamasylvia region, the Drieghan region and the Northwestern Calpheon region.
3. Main questlines, which continue into Valencia, Kamasylvia, Drieghan, O'dyllita and the Mountain of Eternal Winter after Mediah. This gives the best story but is longer and harder.

## What to do after finishing the season

Before graduating from the season, complete the challenges on the season pass. If another class interests you, use the tag system to share experience from one character to another, so two characters level at once. To improve gear beyond PEN (V) Tuvala, read the gear enhancement guides. The guide also suggests trying life skills.
