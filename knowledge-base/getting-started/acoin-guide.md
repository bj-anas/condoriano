# Acoin Guide

A short guide to buying Acoin, the exclusive currency used to purchase Pearl Boxes in Black Desert Online.

## What Acoin is used for

Acoin is the exclusive currency of Black Desert Online. Pearl Boxes cannot be bought with any other currency, so a player who wants them must first purchase Acoin through the official website.

## How to purchase Acoin and find it afterward

To buy Acoin, follow these steps:

1. Sign up and log in to your account on the Black Desert official website, then choose Shop > Acoin from the drop-down menu at the top.
2. On the Acoin page, select the amount of Acoin you want to buy.
3. Choose a payment method.
4. Enter your information for that payment method. The screen and instructions differ between payment methods.
5. When the purchase succeeds, a confirmation screen appears.

Allow up to 15 to 30 minutes after buying before the Acoin appears on My Page. Your past Acoin transactions are listed on My Page > Purchase History.
