# Game Controls

The default movement, combat and interface hotkeys in Black Desert, and how to change them. The hotkey help window opens from the Adventurer's Guide (hotkey: F1).

## Default movement hotkeys

The default movement hotkeys in Black Desert are: WASD to move forward, left, right and backward; Shift to sprint while moving; Space to jump; Q to crouch and sit at edges; T to run automatically, which also activates sprint; Caps Lock to toggle walk and run; Scroll Lock to switch between walk and run during auto-run; and V for the emergency escape. The emergency escape makes you invulnerable for 5 seconds, with a cooldown of 5 minutes, and is useful in boss encounters and PvP. In combat stance, Shift and Q may perform a special action or skill depending on the class. Some hotkeys behave differently in default stance and combat stance.

## Default combat hotkeys

The default combat hotkeys in Black Desert are: Tab to draw and put away your weapon; C to draw your Awakening weapon and change weapon stance; LMB for the main attack and RMB for the secondary attack; Z for rage absorption (absorb the Black Spirit's rage to increase your combat abilities); X for rage transfer (transfuse your Black Spirit's rage to another Adventurer); U to activate the effects of the equipped alchemy stone; and R for character interactions. In default stance LMB draws the main weapon and RMB moves your character forward. E and F do nothing in default stance, but most classes have special actions or skills on them.

## Default UI hotkeys

The default UI hotkeys in Black Desert are: Ctrl shows or hides the mouse cursor; I inventory and equipment; H knowledge interface; / or , (depending on keyboard layout) the Black Spirit interface; Enter chat; P character profile; K skills; M World Map; J Dye interface; N Friend List; L Processing window; G Guild window; B Mail & Black Spirit's Adventure window; Y Challenge Reward window; O Quest window; F1 Adventurer's Guide; F2 Adventurer's Board; F3 Pearl Shop; F4 Beauty Salon. Most of these open and close the window.

## Changing and assigning hotkeys

Several windows ship without a default hotkey but can be given one: Crafting Notes, family inventory, Barter information, the Adventure Log, and the Maid/Butler list, Pet list, Residence list and Worker list. Other functions, such as calling a mount, can be bound to keys as well. To change hotkeys, open the main menu (hotkey: Esc), go to Settings, then Interface Settings. Each section lets you customize hotkeys for every feature and function, and also Gamepad button settings for players who prefer a gamepad.
