# Black Desert+

Black Desert+ is the companion app for Black Desert Online. It keeps players connected to guild chat, the Central Market, coupons, notifications and world boss alerts without being in the game.

## Black Desert+ login setup

Before using Black Desert+ you must set it up for the PC version of Black Desert Online. Select PC as your platform, then select NA or EU as your region, and log in. After that, setup is finished.

## Black Desert+ community, FAQ and support

The Black Desert+ app gives access to the forum on the official Black Desert Online website, where Adventurers discuss many topics. The app also has the FAQ and Support. For a question or a problem such as an item missing from your inventory, check the FAQ first, and if it has no answer, send an inquiry to Support.

## Black Desert+ coupon redemption

With Black Desert+ you can redeem coupon codes without logging in to the official website. This is useful when a code is revealed during a live broadcast, because you can redeem it quickly before it expires.

## Black Desert+ notifications

Black Desert+ sends Push Alerts for new announcements on the official website, including patch notes, events and notices. You can set the alerts to cover only the announcement types you want, for example only patch notes.

## Black Desert+ guild chat

Black Desert+ lets you chat with your guild even when you are not logged in to Black Desert Online, and shows which guild members are currently logged in. Its key feature is keyword notification: you can set up to ten keywords, and you get a notification when any of them is mentioned in guild chat.

## Black Desert+ world boss alerts and Central Market alerts

Black Desert+ can alert you before a world boss spawns, for example Kzarka. You can set alerts to arrive 5, 15, 30, or 60 minutes before the World Boss spawns. Black Desert+ also gives access to the Central Market, like the Web Central Market. You can set up an alert for when an item you listed is sold, and an alert for when an item you pre-ordered has been bought. This means you no longer need to log in to the website to use the web Central Market or redeem coupons.
