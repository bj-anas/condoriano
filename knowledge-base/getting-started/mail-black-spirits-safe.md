# Mail & Black Spirit's Safe

How the in-game Mail and the Black Spirit's Safe work, where rewards from the game and events are stored.

## Mail in Black Desert

To open your mailbox, press the "B" key or click the mail icon at the bottom right of the screen. It is also in the main menu (hotkey: Esc). Mail is mainly system messages: gifts and rewards the game gives all Adventurers, items from redeemed coupon codes, and sometimes steps in in-game events. Mail can only be received, never sent. Unchecked mail is yellow and checked mail is gray.

## Mail attachments, expiry and deleting

Mail may have silver or items attached. You cannot delete a mail until you receive its attachment, and mail with an expiry date disappears even with an item still attached, so claim items quickly. Claimed items go to your character's inventory and silver goes to My Silver. If the mail has no expiry date and you already received its contents, you can delete it manually, or keep it as a record. In the mailbox, "All" selects all mail on the current page, Delete removes the selected mail, and the Support button opens the customer support website.

## Black Spirit's Safe

The Black Spirit's Safe stores items Adventurers receive so they are never lost. Open it with the "B" key, with its icon at the bottom right, or from the main menu (hotkey: Esc). It contains the various daily login rewards; prizes won in the dice board minigame of Black Spirit's Adventure; drops from certain boss fights, for example the world boss Vell, the Heart of the Ocean; and the daily silver family funds, whose amount depends on your family's overall fame.

## Collecting items from the Black Spirit's Safe

Right-click an item in the Black Spirit's Safe to send it to your character's inventory. For silver, use the "Collect Silver Reward" button. The "Collect All Rewards" button empties the whole safe, so make sure your inventory has enough space and weight capacity first.
