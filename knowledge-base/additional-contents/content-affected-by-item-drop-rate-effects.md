# Content Affected by Item Drop Rate Effects

Which monsters, zones and loot are boosted by item drop rate effects such as Hot Time and scrolls, and which are not.

## Item drop rate effects: how they apply

Item drop rate in Black Desert can be raised by several sources, including Hot Time, scrolls and the Knowledge (Ecology) effect. The boost applies to most loot dropped by defeated monsters. It does not apply to some game content, nor to cases where one monster is defeated by several Adventurers and each receives loot individually. The lists below were marked as of April 19, 2023.

## Party monster zones affected by item drop rate buffs

Loot from monster zones recommended for parties on the World Map (M) is affected by item drop rate buffs. Examples are Mirumok Ruins, Tunkuta, Abandoned Monastery (Restricted Territory), Gyfin Rhasia Temple, Padix Island, Saltwater Crocodile, Olun's Valley, [Elvia] Altar Imp Habitat and [Elvia] Castle Ruins, among others. The Season party monster zones Waragon Nest and Shultz Guard are also affected.

## Bosses not affected by item drop rate buffs

Item drop rate buffs do not affect loot from the following bosses:

| Boss type | Bosses |
| --- | --- |
| World boss loot | Kzarka, Karanda, Nouver, Kutum, Muraka, Quint, Offin, Garmoth, Vell |
| Field boss loot | Red Nose, Dastard Bheg, Giant Mudster, Dim Tree Spirit, Katzvariak |
| Boss summon scrolls | Awakened Goblin Chief Summon Scroll, Awakened Altar Imp Captain Summon Scroll, Ancient Relic Crystal Summon Scroll, Pila Fe Scroll, Ibedor's Scroll, Rift's Echo |
| Summoned guild boss | Khan, Ancient Puturum, Ferrid, Afuaru's Treasure Chest |
| Event monster loot | Turkey, Targargo, Belmorn |
| Gatekeeper monster loot | Cadry Chief Gatekeeper, Crescent Chief Gatekeeper |

## Zones and loot not affected by item drop rate buffs

Item drop rate buffs also do not affect the following. All loot from Erethea's Limbo, the Dark Rift and the Pit of the Undying is excluded. In the Basilisk Den party monster zone (3 Adventurers), and in the Sycraia Ruins (Upper Zone) and Winter Tree Fossil (Normal/Difficult) monster zones, loot is not boosted either. Items that appear across the Black Desert world from certain events, namely Golden Dagger, Golden Backpack and Golden Coelacanth, are excluded. So are items obtained by opening chance-driven boxes and bundles.

## Gathering loot from defeated monsters and drop rate buffs

Loot obtained by gathering from a defeated monster is not covered by item drop rate buffs. The page lists this loot under [Graverobber] Afuaru, [Suspicious] Poacher in Monster Zones and [Dreadwinter Guardian] Erebjork, and notes that it is affected by Gathering Mastery effects.
