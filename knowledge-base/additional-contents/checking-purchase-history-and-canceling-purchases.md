# Checking Purchase History and Canceling Purchases

How to look at recent Pearl Shop purchases, how to cancel one for a refund, and which purchases cannot be canceled.

## Pearl Shop purchase history: where to check it

Recent Pearl Shop purchases are listed in the Purchase History menu, which is opened from the Pearl Shop (F3). The list shows the items bought during the last 30 days.

## Pearl Shop Cancel Purchase: limits and effects

A Pearl Shop purchase can be canceled once per month per Family. A cancellation uses the Family's restoration count for that month, and the count resets on the first day of every month. The Cancel Purchase button only shows in Purchase History for purchases that are no more than 7 days (168 hours) old. When a purchase is canceled, the items it gave are taken back and the Pearls spent are returned. Any coupon used for the purchase goes back into the Coupon Book.

## Pearl Shop Cancel Purchase: steps

To cancel a Pearl Shop purchase:

1. Open the Pearl Shop (F3) and select Purchase History.
2. Pick the purchase that shows the Cancel Purchase button.
3. Check the amount of Pearls that will be returned and the items that will be taken back.
4. Type "Cancel Purchase" in the confirmation box and confirm. The items are retrieved and the Pearls come back automatically.

## Pearl Shop Cancel Purchase: item requirements

To cancel a purchase, every item received from it must be in the inventory of the character currently being played, or in the Pearl inventory. If the items are elsewhere, or the other requirements are not met, the cancellation may not be possible. Anything else applied to the purchased items, such as dyes or transfused crystals, is not given back when the purchase is canceled.

## Pearl Shop Cancel Purchase and purchase restrictions

For items with a purchase limit, canceling the purchase also puts the purchase limit back to its earlier value. If the limit resets by itself after some period, no extra purchases are added to the current limit as compensation. When a pack purchase is canceled, the purchase limits of the individual items in that pack are restored too.

## Pearl Shop purchases that cannot be canceled

Purchases that fall under the Operational Policy rules cannot be canceled. These are items more than 7 days past their purchase date, items whose durability has been used, and products where every item they contained has already been used. For more detail, the player can read the recovery support criteria in the Operational Policy published on the Black Desert website.

Two other cases cannot be canceled directly in game even when the requirements look met, and the player should contact Support: opened box items (including outfit sets), and items that were moved to the Central Market Warehouse.

## Pearl Shop refund through Customer Support

If a Pearl Shop purchase cannot be canceled in game, the player can contact the Customer Support Team and ask for a Pearl Shop refund. Refunds are still subject to the recovery criteria of the Operational Policy.
