# Enhancement Chance Increase of Origin of Dark Hunger

How much the Origin of Dark Hunger, the Faint Origin of Dark Hunger and the Dim Origin of Dark Hunger raise a character's Enhancement Chance, depending on the chance the character already has.

## Origin of Dark Hunger items: how the Enhancement Chance increase works

The Origin of Dark Hunger, the Faint Origin of Dark Hunger and the Dim Origin of Dark Hunger are three items that each add to the current Enhancement Chance. The size of the increase is not fixed. It depends on the Enhancement Chance the character has when the item is used, and the lower the current chance, the bigger the increase. The tables for each item cover a current Enhancement Chance of 100 up to 299.

## Dim Origin of Dark Hunger: Enhancement Chance increase

The Dim Origin of Dark Hunger is listed on the wiki as an event item. It adds the following amount to the current Enhancement Chance, covering current values from 100 to 299:

| Current Enhancement Chance | Increase |
| --- | --- |
| 100 to 104 | +6 |
| 105 to 116 | +5 |
| 117 to 136 | +4 |
| 137 to 167 | +3 |
| 168 to 209 | +2 |
| 210 to 299 | +1 |
