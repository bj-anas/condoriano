# Bonus Stats per Displayed Stats

Lookup brackets for the bonus stats a character gets from Gear Score, split by Displayed AP (bonus AP and bonus extra AP against monsters) and Displayed DP (bonus damage reduction and its rate).

## Bonus stats per displayed AP and DP: overview

Bonus stats in Black Desert Online are granted according to a character's Displayed AP and Displayed DP, which reflect Gear Score. Displayed AP determines Bonus AP and Bonus Extra AP Against Monsters. Displayed DP determines the Bonus Damage Reduction Rate (a percentage) and the Bonus Damage Reduction value. The tables on the wiki page cover Displayed AP from 100 to 450 and Displayed DP from 203 to 531, and each value applies to a whole range of displayed numbers.

Bonus Extra AP Against Monsters is 0 for every Displayed AP from 100 to 309. It starts at Displayed AP 310 with a value of 8. From 310 to 400 it rises by 8 for each additional displayed AP point, and from 401 to 450 it rises by 16 per point (401 gives 744, 402 gives 760). Sample values:
