# History of Drieghan

A short factual summary of the history of Drieghan from the official wiki.

## Drieghan history: origins

Drieghan history begins with the Sherekhan, a people covered in Dragon's blood that hardened their skin. In Elionian Year 185 they settled east of Drieghan under their first ruler, Akum, but a drought destroyed that society within a year. The dying Akum told his descendants to bury the dragon's tooth where blessed rain falls, and they became nomads. In Year 226 rain returned after 40 years, forming a waterfall and lake and the land of Duvencrune, where the tooth rests.

## Drieghan history: key events and rulers

By Elionian Year 235 Drieghan was divided into five regions shaped like a dragon, with the Sherekhan Necropolis at the head in the north. Its reputation as a dragon's land kept outsiders away, and the capital Duvencrune stayed unknown abroad for a long time. In Year 276 Ahibs fleeing Kamasylvia clashed with Drieghan's vigilantes while heading for Salun Bear's Realm. Chief Durgeff asked for an army, but the senior council only made the vigilantes Duvencrune's official guard.

## Drieghan history: the region today

The Drieghan history page ends in Elionian Year 286. A hunter spotted a huge winged beast on a hill at night, and beacons were lit from the Night Crow Post to the Morning Fog Post. Chief Durgeff knew Drieghan lacked the manpower to fight a dragon. Despite villagers' objections, he decided to seek help from foreigners, and notices calling for willing fighters were posted in nearby countries.
