# History of Mediah

A short factual summary of the history of Mediah from the official wiki.

## Mediah history: origins

Mediah history begins in Elionian Year 235, when King Bareeds II, remembered as the most incompetent king in history, kept Mediah out of the war between Calpheon and Valencia. Merchant guild leader Neruda Shen arranged a deal in which Mediah's blacksmiths supplied weapons and Calpheon supplied black crystals. The Shen Merchant Guild forged the weapons at the Omar Lava Cave. Guilds shared profits with Valencia in return for protection and trading rights.

## Mediah history: key events and rulers

In Elionian Year 266 barbarians fleeing a typhoon settled in Mediah. In Year 273 the guild's profits funded the founding of Altinova. In the village of Tarif, the Book of Cartian held unstable power and was sealed. Illezera, also spelled Illezra on the page, broke the seal, absorbed Cartian's power and fled. She persuaded the Elric Followers to slay barbarians and built a tower in northern Mediah. An explosion then covered the land in black smoke. In Year 277 Mediah Castle burned, Bareeds II was lost, and his youngest heir survived.

## Mediah history: the region today

The Mediah history page ends in Elionian Year 281. In Year 280 reports said Illezera had been seen among the barbarians at the Abandoned Iron Mine. A black-cloaked barbarian who spoke human language led barbarians to Altinova to occupy it, and the Sezecs also arrived. In Year 281 the merchant guilds of Altinova tried to find a way to coexist with the newcomers.
