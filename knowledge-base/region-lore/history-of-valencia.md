# History of Valencia

A short factual summary of the history of Valencia from the official wiki.

## Valencia history: origins

Valencia history opens with a young man led to ancient chambers in the desert, who took a golden crown and became the first king of Valencia. The Aakman, who existed before Valencia, call themselves guardians of the Ancient Civilization. They clashed with the royal Nesser family over the desert's relics. In Elionian Year 234 the impatient King Imur Nesser slaughtered many Aakmans, an event called the Aakman Massacre. The Black Death, which came from Valencia's merchant guild, then swept the western continent.

## Valencia history: key events and rulers

Elionian priests in Calpheon persuaded the king and officials to seize control of the desert. In Elionian Year 236 the Calpheon alliance expedition marched in, and the war lasted 30 years until a great sandstorm swallowed both armies. Imur Nesser named the battlefield the Red Desert and praised Aal. Torme Nesser became the 5th king, the oldest in Valencia's history, and Valencia grew wealthy from black stone deposits. In Year 282 his son Sahazad Nesser became the 6th king.

## Valencia history: the region today

The Valencia history page ends in Elionian Year 282. Barhan Nesser was told by his mother that the new king Sahazad lacked the golden key, an object tied to the crowning of every king and meant to show the rightful successor. This put the monarchy's stability at risk. Strange events were also reported in the desert, including the Aakmans reappearing and ancient giants roaming.
