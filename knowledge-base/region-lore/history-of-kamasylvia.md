# History of Kamasylvia

A short factual summary of the history of Kamasylvia from the official wiki.

## Kamasylvia history: origins

Kamasylvia history begins before recorded history with a holy tree in the forest. The goddess Sylvia came down with nature spirits and named it Kamasylve. Using energy from the sun and moon, she created the Ganelles and the Vedirs. After the calamity of Elionian Year 235, dark spirits invaded and Sylvia did not answer prayers. A Vedir proposed burning the Kamasylve for power, which made its power unstable until it faded. The song of the forest later woke the tree.

## Kamasylvia history: key events and rulers

In Elionian Year 274 King Guy Seric of Calpheon admired Kamasylvia's bow mastery and secured a military alliance, while the land was secretly in civil war. By Year 276 Queen Brolina Ornette, born with strong Ganelle powers, ruled. The Acher Guard was formed to protect the land, the Vedir faction called the Ahib was branded heretical, and the Dark Knights left. The Acher drove the Ahib southeast but retreated at Ferocious Salun Bear's Realm.

## Kamasylvia history: the region today

The Kamasylvia history page ends in Elionian Year 286. In Year 284 an Ahib stronghold rose in O'dyllita, and the Lemoria Guards lost half their forces at the Duzak Tunnel before Kamasylve Priestesses sealed it. In Year 286 Queen Brolina opened every gate and road into Kamasylvia and sent messengers to Calpheon and Drieghan.
