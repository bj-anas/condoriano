# History of Serendia

A short factual summary of the history of Serendia from the official wiki, told through the rulers of Heidel.

## Serendia history: origins

Serendia history centers on King Crucio Domongatt of Heidel, who saw no reason for war and did not regard himself as Elion's servant like his father. He was crowned in Elionian Year 265 at age 27. Under pressure from Calpheon he agreed to an expedition to Valencia Castle. In the Black Desert King Dahad of Calpheon vanished, and Crucio retreated with the survivors after the Demi River flooded. Calpheon's church still called it a victory, and the young Guy Seric was crowned king.

## Serendia history: key events and rulers

In Elionian Year 275 Domongatt discovered that the pebbles held by young Nagas in the Serendia wetlands were black crystals, which he saw as Heidel's path to independence from Calpheon. In Year 276 Calpheon, after beating Keplan, ambushed Heidel Castle while the generals Cliff and Armstrong defended. Domongatt accepted Guy Seric's terms and returned to Heidel a year later. Calpheon built an extraction mill in the wetlands. In Year 281 Guy Seric died suddenly, Domongatt demoted himself to Lord, and Jordine became Grand Chamberlain at age 25.

## Serendia history: the region today

The Serendia history page ends in Elionian Year 285. In Year 283 peasants rebelled over high taxes, and Al Rhundi, who spoke for them, became the rebel leader hiding in the Castle Ruins. That year a light fell from the sky and enraged monsters attacked barbarians, an event known as the Tower of Will incident. By Year 285 rumors said the number of outsiders in Velia had grown greatly.
