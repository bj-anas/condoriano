# History of the Mountain of Eternal Winter

A short factual summary of the history of the Mountain of Eternal Winter from the official wiki.

## Mountain of Eternal Winter history: origins

Mountain of Eternal Winter history begins 4000 Before Elion with the dragon Labreska, who ruled the Golden Mountain and gained the god-consuming flame Ynix. The gods stripped her wings and limbs and brought eternal winter to the mountain. After her first death in the Haven of Slumbering Origin, seven beings awoke, known as the Seven Witches of Everfrost. At 500 Before Elion the Okjinsini were born from Labreska's tears and the Abets rose after her second death.

## Mountain of Eternal Winter history: key events and rulers

In Elionian Year 180 the Black Lake tribe's chieftain called himself the Great Founder and united the other five tribes. The Deer Abets, also called Eil, promised to protect the village. His ambition to rule the Murrowak legion of Queen Vercedes failed, as the witches' wisdom could not defeat dragons, and the tribes stoned him to death. His son Akum later reunited the tribes.

## Mountain of Eternal Winter history: the region today

The Mountain of Eternal Winter history page ends in Elionian Year 287. A mysterious explosion hits the Sherekhan Iron Mine, then the Black Leopard Totem at Zvier Highland is shattered, and the Okjinsini are blamed. Khan, chief of the Abets, goes missing while confronting the Okjinsini Floki. A mysterious fox Abets then goes looking for a winter warrior to accompany on a climb. The page's introduction says the witches sought a Winter Warrior to take up Ynix and replace them.
