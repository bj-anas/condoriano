# History of Calpheon

A short factual summary of the history of Calpheon from the official wiki.

## Calpheon history: origins

Calpheon history on the wiki begins with the Black Death of Elionian Year 235, which spread from merchants travelling to the desert. It killed more than half the population of the western continent, including Calpheon, Keplan, Heidel and Olvia. Elionian priests blamed the Valencians and their use of black crystal in alchemy, and argued that the Black Desert had to be conquered. An alliance formed, starting a long war between Calpheon and Valencia.

## Calpheon history: key events and rulers

The war with Valencia lasted 30 years and ended when a sandstorm buried King Dahad of Calpheon and his forces. In Elionian Year 275 the young King Guy Seric, wanting black crystals, persuaded the Elionian priests to fund a new war. In Year 276 the Road to Serendia campaign began with Keplan, and Calpheon soldiers attacked Heidel Castle by boat. Calpheon captured King Crucio of Heidel and freed him a year later in exchange for three conditions, including all of Serendia's black crystals. Olvia surrendered, Calpheon took the Keplan mines and it built an extraction mill in Serendia.

## Calpheon history: the region today

The wiki's Calpheon history ends in Elionian Year 281. Guy Seric's plan to seize the Black Desert failed, as mercenary costs and higher taxes angered the public. The feudal system collapsed and commoners gained freedom to take part in trade, opening an era of commerce. The government was reformed and a council was set up with representatives from every class.
