# History of Balenos

A short factual summary of the history of Balenos from the official wiki.

## Balenos history: origins

Balenos history begins in Kabuanian Calendar Year 286, when a madness turned people against each other and the world burned. The Edana, who raised black stones, ended the catastrophe. Their civilization later vanished. A stone chamber in the south of what became Balenos was sealed and guarded by nature spirits. Around Elionian Calendar Year 135 a castle was built there and the Kingdom of Cron was founded.

## Balenos history: key events and rulers

King Noir Bartali III of Cron was kind to commoners but harsh to nobles. Fearing death in old age, he accepted an alchemist's promise of immortality. The ritual, which used sacrificed children and maidens, destroyed Cron Castle and its city and ended the kingdom. Survivors gathered in Velia or scattered. In Elionian Calendar Year 235 the Black Death from the east killed roughly half the population of the western continent, and western nations went to war with the Kingdom of Valencia. Balenos later came under Calpheon's control. After a disaster in 277 EC, remnants of Mediah's military occupied the ruins of Cron Castle.

## Balenos history: the region today

The Balenos history page ends in Elionian Calendar Year 286. Balenos is at relative peace under the Republic of Calpheon, but goblins and imps are restless and the exiles in Cron Castle's ruins will not leave. Soldiers of the Western Guard Camp capture an injured ogre and find an open entrance to underground ruins, a stone chamber from the time of the Ancients. The Grand Chamberlain of Heidel starts an excavation and hires a famed explorer. Rumors spread of a man dressed in black wandering the land.
