# Repair

How to repair equipment in Black Desert, including NPC repair, restoring lost maximum durability, and repairing at your own anvil.

## Repair overview

Equipment loses durability as you fight, and when it runs out the gear becomes unusable. To prevent that, repair it at a blacksmith or another NPC with a repair function, found in most towns, or repair it yourself with a personal anvil in a residence you own. The three methods are NPC repair, restoring maximum durability, and anvil repair.

## Repair: NPC repair

Repair service is available from blacksmiths, armor vendors, stable keepers and wharf managers, and sometimes from arms dealers. You can repair the gear your character wears and carries, plus mount equipment (land and sea) and guild equipment. Gear still attached to your mount is repairable only when the mount stands near the NPC, and ship equipment is repaired at a wharf manager. In the Repair menu, press the Repair button to open a confirmation window that lists every repairable piece and the total silver cost. Repairable items in your inventory are highlighted, and you can right-click any of them, or any equipped piece, to repair just that item, which helps when silver is short.

## Repair: restoring maximum durability and Artisan's Memory

Each failed enhancement with black stones lowers an item's maximum durability, and the lower it is, the faster the item becomes unusable. Example from the wiki: an item with 140 maximum durability and 140 durability is in good shape. An item with 100 maximum durability that has dropped to 95 durability only needs a normal NPC repair. An item whose maximum fell from 180 to 170 after a failed enhancement cannot be fixed by a normal repair; its maximum durability must be restored first and then repaired. Restoring maximum durability uses memory fragments, and higher grade gear needs more of them. Each NPC repair also has a small chance to restore one point of lost maximum durability. The Artisan's Memory option lets a single material restore an item's durability 5 times, and it comes from events and the Pearl Shop.

## Repair: anvil repair

You can place an Anvil in your residence and repair items yourself. First get the repair knowledge from a blacksmith. After that, use an item on your anvil to repair it. Restoring one point of durability takes about 20 seconds, but it costs no silver. An Anvil can be crafted at a Lv. 3 Tool Workshop, or bought with silver from a blacksmith or arms dealer.
