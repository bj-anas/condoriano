# Alchemy Stones

How alchemy stones work: crafting, grades and levels, activation and recharge, polishing and growth, spirit stones, boss and season stones, and upgrades.

## Alchemy Stones overview

An alchemy stone is equipment worn in the Alchemy Stone slot. Press the hotkey U to activate it for beneficial effects that last a limited time. You can equip only one at a time but can swap them as needed. There are three types: destruction (offensive), protection (defensive) and life (life skill benefits). Quality of the effect depends on the stone's grade and level, and most stones have 4 grades and 8 levels. Crafting them is the final stage of the alchemy life skill, and raising a stone's grade and level is also hard. A high grade and level stone is worth about as much as enhancing gear to its next level. Learn alchemy basics before trying to craft one.

## Alchemy Stones: crafting

Any adventurer, even at alchemy beginner 1, can try to craft an alchemy stone, using an alchemy tool installed in a residence. The key ingredient is shining powder, which drops at a low chance whenever an alchemical operation succeeds. The other ingredients are clear liquid reagent, pure powder reagent, powder of time and sinner's blood. A successful craft gives a random destruction, protection or life stone. It may instead give a broken alchemy stone shard, which has no use but sells to an NPC shop for 5,000 silver. A higher alchemy level gives a better chance of crafting success.

## Alchemy Stones: grades and levels

Grade is shown by the color of the stone's name and tooltip. From lowest to highest the grades are white, green, blue and yellow. Level is shown by the prefix in the name. The wiki lists the prefixes from lowest to highest as imperfect, rough, polished, sturdy, sharp, resplendent and shining, and the growth table also includes splendid. A low grade or level can be improved with polishing and growth.

## Alchemy Stones: activation, duration, cooldown and recharge

After equipping, press U to activate; each activation lowers the stone's durability by 1. Destruction and protection stones last 5 minutes with a 5-minute cooldown, and life stones last 10 minutes with a 10-minute cooldown. Destruction and protection stones share one cooldown, so both effects cannot run together. You can tick Auto-use Alchemy Stone under the equipment slots to use stones automatically, but untick it when you no longer need it so durability is not wasted. To recharge durability, go to inventory (hotkey I) and press the Stone button, using oil, blood and reagents. The amount depends on the lost durability and the stone's level, and any type of oil and blood is allowed. Using and recharging stones is not limited by your alchemy level.

## Alchemy Stones: polishing and growth rules

Upgrading raises the quality of existing effects or adds new effect types. Growth can be tried only after polishing raises the stone's EXP to 80% or more (up to 150%), and higher EXP gives a better success rate. Polishing materials depend on the type: ores for destruction stones, plywood for protection stones and crops for life stones. Once EXP is 80% to 150%, one black stone (weapon) performs growth. On success the grade (color) or level (prefix) improves, and rarely both. A higher grade adds effect types and a higher level improves existing effects. EXP resets to 0% after success. Higher levels require a higher alchemy level, and growth can be tried from apprentice 1.

## Alchemy Stones: growth failure penalties and success rates

A failed growth never lowers the grade, but it may lower the level, and it halves the stone's EXP (150% becomes 75%, and 80% becomes 40%). Resplendent and splendid stones can be destroyed by failed growth. Growth is costly, so weigh the risk. Each tier needs Brimming Essence of Aether, and the Agris Essence column shows the Ancient Anvil value.

| Growth tier | Success rate at 150% polishing | Agris Essence (Ancient Anvil) | Brimming Essence of Aether needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imperfect to Sturdy | 55.06% | 2 | 2 |
| Sturdy to Sharp | 20.06% | 5 | 3 |
| Sharp to Resplendent | 3.32% | 30 | 10 |
| Resplendent to Splendid | 1.26% | 80 | 20 |
| Splendid to Shining | 0.4% | 250 | 30 |

## Alchemy Stones: Faint and Brimming Essence of Aether

Faint Essence of Aether x20 drops from defeating a Dark Rift Boss. Brimming Essence of Aether is made by Simple Alchemy. Recipe 1: Faint Essence of Aether x1 and Sharp Black Crystal Shard x20. Recipe 2: Faint Essence of Aether x1 and Oquilla Piece of the Old Moon x2. Each recipe can be mass-produced by using 10 times the materials plus Black Stone Powder x1. Brimming Essence of Aether is used to attempt growth on destruction, protection and life stones, to combine Vell's Heart or Khan's Heart with one of those stones, and to separate a combined stone. Polishing materials have not changed: high-quality crops, special crops, sturdy plywood, plywood, metal ingots and pure metals.

## Alchemy Stones: combining and separating combined stones

A combined stone merges Vell's Heart or Khan's Heart with a destruction, protection or life alchemy stone using Brimming Essence of Aether (Simple Alchemy). The result shows the Heart's displayed effect plus the effects of the other stone, for example Vell's Heart's AP increase plus Resplendent Alchemy Stone of Destruction effects. Blessed or exalted stones can be combined only after their reform stone is extracted. Behelit's Heart can be combined with or separated from a Splendid, Shining or Resplendent stone without extracting the Crimson Behelit Alchemy Stone Appearance, and growth can be attempted on that combination. To separate a combined stone, use Simple Alchemy with a Brimming Essence of Aether; this returns the two original stones, but only works if the stones have not been upgraded.

## Alchemy Stones: spirit stones

Spirit stones break the normal rules: they cannot be recharged and are gone once used up. The well-known ones drop from monsters in Kamasylvia, land of the elves: destruction, guardian and life spirit stones in green and blue grades. Another kind is made by alchemy and also cannot be recharged: green, blue and yellow stones in sharp (green, blue, yellow), resplendent (yellow), splendid (yellow) and shining (yellow), and only protection and destruction versions can be made right now. Crafting these needs alchemy mastery 500 and a certain alchemy level depending on the stone, with costly ingredients.

## Alchemy Stones: Vell's Heart and Khan's Heart from bosses

Vell's Heart and Khan's Heart are among the best alchemy stones. They come from the ocean bosses Vell and Khan, but can also be bought on the Central Market. A small chance at Vell's Concentrated Magic comes from the World Boss Vell. Khan, a Guild Boss killable once a week, has a tiny chance to drop Khan's Concentrated Magic. Processing (L) turns either item into the matching Heart with 1,500 Alchemy Stone Shards. Alchemy Stone Shards come from Processing (L) > Grinding crafted Alchemy Stones, which can be bought on the Central Market. The World Boss and Guild Bosses pages have more on these bosses.

## Alchemy Stones: Perilla's Star and Treant's Tear from the Season

Season play offers two special alchemy stones. Perilla's Star is defensive, with defensive buffs and a passive +1 DP. It is obtained only by trading a Season Special Gift Exchange Coupon to Fughar, a coupon given at level 10 on the Season Server. Treant's Tear gives buffs to various Life Skills. Only a Season Character, or one that has graduated from the Season, can obtain it, once per family, by starting the questline "[Season] [Life 101] The Adventurer That Does It All" with Fughar.

## Alchemy Stones: Blessed and Exalted Soul Fragment upgrades

Some alchemy stones can be upgraded for extra stats using an item that is crafted or dropped in any Calpheon Elvia monster zone. Blessed Soul Fragment is made by Processing (L) - Heating Forest Fury x10, Magical Shard x50, Crystallized Despair x200 and Black Stone x100. It adds Damage Reduction +2 (sheet DP), Monster Damage Reduction +1% and All resistances +2%. Stones that can take it: Sharp, Resplendent, Splendid and Shining Alchemy Stone of Destruction; Vell's Heart; Khan's Heart: Destruction; Ator's Power Stone; Sharp, Resplendent, Splendid and Shining Alchemy Stone of Protection; Perilla's Star; and Khan's Heart: Protection. Crystallized Despair drops in Calpheon Elvia or is sold on the Central Market. A Blessed stone can get a further +2 DP with Exalted Soul Fragment, made by Heating Forest Fury x5, Magical Shard x25, Crystallized Despair x100 and Alchemy Stone Shard x50, which adds Damage Reduction +2.
