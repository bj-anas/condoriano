# Enhancement

How equipment enhancement works in Black Desert: enhancement levels, stones, safe range, force-enhancement, failstacks, Cron Stones, items that store or boost enhancement chance, and Caphras enhancement.

## Enhancement overview

Enhancement improves the stats of a piece of equipment: attack power (AP) on weapons and defense power (DP) on armor. The Black Spirit consumes enhancement materials, mainly black stones, to do it. To enhance, talk to your Black Spirit (hotkey ",") and choose the Enhancement option, after you have the gear piece and the right ingredient in your inventory. Any adventurer can use it, and it is the main way to make gear stronger. The material depends on the gear type and its current enhancement level, and some special gear needs other stones.

## Enhancement: level names from +1 to DEC

Weapons and armor can mostly be enhanced 20 levels in total. Levels up to +15 are shown as plain numbers. Levels 16 to 20 are called PRI (I), DUO (II), TRI (III), TET (IV) and PEN (V). Levels 21 to 25 are called HEX (VI), SEP (VII), OCT (VIII), NOV (IX) and DEC (X). Accessories (rings, necklaces, belts and earrings) can be enhanced only 5 levels, named PRI (I), DUO (II), TRI (III), TET (IV) and PEN (V). These names come from Latin and ancient Greek words for first, two, three, four and five.

## Enhancement: black stones needed by gear type

Weapons use Black Stone for +1 to +15 and Concentrated Magical Black Stone (Weapon) for PRI (I) to PEN (V), so every attempt at PRI, DUO, TRI, TET or PEN on a weapon uses the concentrated stone. The wiki table lists defense gear on the same row as weapons. Accessories use a base accessory of the same type as the ingredient.

| Gear type | +1 to +15 | PRI (I) to PEN (V) |
|---|---|---|
| Weapons | Black Stone | Concentrated Magical Black Stone (Weapon) |
| Accessories | Base accessory of the same type | Base accessory of the same type |

## Enhancement: safe range and failure penalties

Most gear can be enhanced safely, with no chance of failure, up to +7 on weapons and +5 on armor. Past that range success becomes a small chance. A failed attempt can lower the item's maximum durability, lower its enhancement level, or destroy it, depending on the item type. Accessories are the harshest: on failure, both the accessory you were enhancing and the ingredient accessory can disappear. The higher the level you attempt, the bigger the maximum durability loss. When maximum durability reaches 20 or less, the item cannot be enhanced any more, and at 0 it cannot be used until its maximum durability is restored.

## Enhancement: force-enhancement

Above the safe range (weapons past +7, defense gear past +5) a force-enhancement option appears that succeeds 100 percent of the time. It needs more black stones than a normal attempt, and the amount grows with the level you are going for. Force-enhancing also lowers maximum durability, and the loss can be severe at higher levels. If maximum durability drops to 20 or less because of a failure or a force-enhancement, the item cannot be enhanced further. At 0 it cannot be used until restored.

## Enhancement: getting black stones back by extraction

Black stones spent on enhancement can be recovered with the Black Stone Extraction option at a blacksmith, in proportion to the enhancement level, but the gear piece is permanently destroyed. If the item was raised only within the safe range, you get back as many stones as you used. If it went beyond the safe range, you get the item's level plus a number within a certain range. The Extraction page has more detail.

## Enhancement window: success chance and durability loss

In the Enhancement window, suitable materials in your inventory are picked automatically when you select the gear. The window shows the success probability, which depends on the gear type, its level and your current enhancement chance bonus. It also shows how much maximum durability will be lost on failure. For accessories it instead warns that the item will be destroyed on failure. If maximum durability gets too low you must restore it before trying again. The Enhance button makes the attempt, and the result cannot be undone.

## Enhancement: failstacks (Equipment Enhancement Rate Increase)

Failstacks, also called stacks, are the Equipment Enhancement Rate Increase number shown in the Enhancement window. Every failed attempt adds 1 to it, and a success uses it up. A higher number raises the success chance of your next attempt. Players build failstacks on purpose by failing low-chance enhancements, then spend them on the piece they really want. Loggia, Geranoa and Manos clothes and accessories that raise life skill stats ignore failstacks and have a fixed chance at each level. Failstacks can be stored or boosted with Advice of Valks, Naderr's Band, Valks' Cry, devouring gear, converting items into extra enhancement chance, and the Ancient Anvil.

## Enhancement: Cron Stones prevent level loss

Past +15 you can reach PRI (I) to PEN (V), but failing at DUO (II) or higher lowers the piece's enhancement level, and failing an accessory destroys it. Cron Stones prevent this. With Cron Stones, weapons and defense gear keep their level when the attempt fails. Accessories are not destroyed, although their level may still drop by some chance. To use them, tick the Use Cron Stone option in the Enhancement window. The number of Cron Stones required appears in the Cron Stone slot at the top left and depends on the gear and its level.

## Enhancement: Cron Stones cost and how to get them

When you enhance with Cron Stones, a failed attempt does not add to your enhancement chance (failstacks). All the Cron Stones needed are consumed even if the attempt succeeds. Before using them, weigh how many are needed, whether re-enhancing the piece would be cheaper in silver, and your current success chance. Cron Stones can be bought from a Blacksmith NPC in towns, extracted from pearl outfits, picked up in small steady amounts from boss encounters, and won as event rewards. Agris Essence on the Ancient Anvil also applies to attempts made with Cron Stones.

## Enhancement: Agris Essence and the Ancient Anvil

Each failed enhancement fills Agris Essence in the Ancient Anvil. Once enough is stored, the next attempt on that item is guaranteed to succeed, so it works as a guaranteed enhancement system. The amount needed depends on the gear group and level. The Ancient Anvil page lists the maximum Agris Essence for each gear type and level.

## Enhancement: restoring maximum durability

Failed enhancements lower an item's maximum durability. To restore it you need either an unenhanced (+0) piece of the same type as the target, or memory fragments. Which is cheaper depends on the repair cost of the item. The Repair page covers durability and how to repair equipment.

## Enhancement: Advice of Valks (storing failstacks)

To keep your enhancement chance for later, extract it into Advice of Valks items using a blacksmith's secret book, sold by Blacksmith NPCs in major towns. The book must cover the amount you want to extract. A smaller amount than the book states is allowed, but a larger one is not: with +21 chance, Blacksmith's Secret Book - 20 is too small and Blacksmith's Secret Book - 30 is the right one. Summon the Black Spirit, open the enhancement menu, right-click the book in your inventory, press Extract and accept the warning. You receive Advice of Valks equal to the chance extracted. Chance from Valks' Cry is not extracted: the chance resets to 0 but the Valks' Cry amount stays. Advice of Valks also comes from main questlines after Mediah, events, and the Black Spirit's Adventure minigame.

## Enhancement: Naderr's Band

Naderr's Band is another way to store enhancement chance. Unlike a secret book it has no per-book size limit, and it also saves the bonus from Valks' Cry. The number of slots has a maximum. After using it to move an enhancement chance of 100 or more, a 5-second cooldown applies. To unlock it, a character level 58 or higher accepts the Black Spirit's Gift (Naderr's Band) quest, which can be taken once per Family. Its reward, Nader's Parchment, turns on the feature and opens one slot. One more slot can be bought in the Pearl Shop with Loyalties.

## Enhancement: Valks' Cry

Valks' Cry is an item that raises enhancement success. Each one used adds +1 enhancement chance on top of failstacks. By default up to 10 can be used together for one attempt, and up to 13 if you complete the whole Sherald's Journal in your Adventure Log. Valks' Cry comes from quest rewards, events and the Pearl Shop.

## Enhancement: devouring gear

With devouring, the Black Spirit eats high-level enhanced gear and other items and turns them into extra enhancement chance. It can still add value when your enhancement chance is already 1 or more. Devouring can fail, and the chosen gear disappears whether or not it succeeds, so choose carefully. Some gear, such as Blackstar, cannot be devoured.

## Enhancement: Another Adventurer's Choice

After selecting a gear piece, click the icon next to Current Enhancement Chance to see which failstack amounts other adventurers used for the same item at the same level, for example for TRI (III) Tuvala gear. The data refreshes when the window opens, and gear without enough data is only shown once enough is collected. It does not show data for accessories with guaranteed PEN (V) enhancement, guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear, or Naru equipment.

## Enhancement: Group Enhancement

Adventurers whose gear is at the same enhancement level (for example, both going from IV to V) can enhance together. When everyone is ready, the party leader starts the attempt and all enhance at once. It gives no higher success chance or other bonus, and Cron Stones work as normal. Restrictions: only gear of the same group can be enhanced together (for example Blackstar main weapons of the same level); it must be done in safe zones; PEN accessories and boss gear, fixed-chance gear such as Manos accessories, and Naru gear are excluded; and it cannot be canceled once the leader starts.

## Enhancement: Caphras Stones and Caphras enhancement overview

Caphras enhancement improves weapons and defense gear at TRI (III) or higher using Caphras Stones, made by synthesizing Black Stones with Ancient Spirit Dust (from quests, monsters and gathering). Open it from the Enhancement window with the Caphras option. There are 20 Caphras levels. Infusing stones gives the gear EXP, and each new level adds stats such as AP, accuracy, evasion, damage reduction and more. At Caphras level 20 the piece can be safely upgraded to the next normal level (III to IV, IV to V). It needs 100 maximum durability for that, and afterwards has 0 maximum durability and its Caphras levels reset. A failed normal enhancement keeps only 50% of the Caphras EXP; a success keeps 10%.
