# Slumbering Origin Gear

The Slumbering Origin defense gear set: its four pieces, how each is crafted, their enhancement stats, set effects, and Agris Essence.

## Slumbering Origin gear overview

Slumbering Origin is a powerful defense gear set crafted by combining gear you already own with key materials found in Ulukita, the Land of the Morning Light and Mountain of Eternal Winter. The four parts are Labreska's Helmet, Fallen God's Armor, Dahn's Gloves and Ator's Shoes. Caphras Enhancement is not possible on them, but each piece has its own effects plus set effects. All four can be crafted or bought on the Central Market, and each beats the matching boss piece and the Blackstar piece. Worn pieces trigger set effects of both boss gear and Slumbering Origin gear. The HYPERBOOST benefit lets you get the defense gear through special Black Shrine quests. Every piece has six enhancement levels, named Default, Desperate, Distorted, Silent, Wailing and Obliterating.

## Slumbering Origin gear set effects

Wearing Slumbering Origin pieces together gives these set effects.

| Pieces worn | Set effects |
|---|---|
| 2 | Max HP +100, Monster Damage Reduction +10 |
| 3 | Max HP +300, Extra AP Against Monsters +7 |
| 4 | Max HP +150, Max Stamina +150 |

## Slumbering Origin gear Flawless Chaotic Black Stone

All four Slumbering Origin pieces are enhanced with Flawless Chaotic Black Stone. There are two ways to make it by Heating: Sharp Black Crystal x2, Mass of Pure Magic x1 and Caphras Stones x10; or Flawless Magical Black Stone x1 and Caphras Stones x10. The wiki notes that a failed enhancement lowers the level by one and reduces maximum durability by 30. That note appears under Fallen God's Armor and is repeated for the helmet and shoes.

## Slumbering Origin gear Agris Essence

All four Slumbering Origin pieces use identical Agris Essence values at every enhancement level. For example, Agris Essence gained while enhancing from Distorted to Silent is shared by all four pieces at that level.

| Enhancement step | Max Agris Essence |
|---|---|
| +0 to Desperate | 8 |
| Desperate to Distorted | 10 |
| Distorted to Silent | 12 |
| Silent to Wailing | 20 |
| Wailing to Obliterating | 1000 |

## Fallen God's Armor overview

Fallen God's Armor is the Slumbering Origin armor piece. It can only be crafted or bought on the Central Market, and it outperforms Red Nose's Armor, Dim Tree Spirit's Armor and Blackstar Armor. Caphras Stones cannot be used on it, and wearing it triggers set effects of both boss gear and Slumbering Origin gear. Its lore ties it to the Fallen God, Lord of Star's End.

## Fallen God's Armor crafting materials

Fallen God's Armor needs one of three armors plus Flame of Despair.

| First material | Enhancement | Caphras |
|---|---|---|
| Red Nose's Armor | PEN (V) | Lv. 10, x7,224 Caphras Stones |
| Dim Tree Spirit's Armor | PEN (V) | Lv. 10, x8,260 Caphras Stones |
| Blackstar Armor | PEN (V) | not needed |

Reformed Blackstar gear (Obsidian Blackstar Armor) cannot be used.

Flame of Despair comes from Heating Embers of Despair x100 in Processing (L). Turos of Tunkuta drop the embers at a certain rate. In Tunkuta the Grand Chief of Turos, Ulutuka, can appear when "Ulutuka's Alert" is activated or two intruders are found, and defeating him may drop the flame. Bosses summoned with Ibedor's Scroll also drop the flame or embers. Make that scroll by placing Venomous Night Fang x5 in a [+] shape; the fangs drop from monsters across O'dyllita. Tunkuta and O'dyllita need high AP and DP.

## Fallen God's Armor crafting quest

Craft Fallen God's Armor by accepting a quest from the NPC Dorin Morgrim at Helms Post once you hold both materials. The quest is repeatable but can be accepted only once per day. Only characters of level 56 and above can take it, and it cannot be active together with the [Crafting] Blackstar quests. If the quest does not appear, make sure the All quest type is active in the Quest window (O). A rumored alternate route called Elviayed uses fragments of an eye-shaped artifact and a corrupting flame from O'dyllita monster zones, plus a Caphras Stone infused with dark rift substance, which Dorin Morgrim turns into the armor.

## Labreska's Helmet overview

Labreska's Helmet is the Slumbering Origin helmet, made with a horn of the golden dragon Labreska. It can only be crafted or bought on the Central Market, and it beats Giath's Helmet, Griffon's Helmet and Blackstar Helmet. Caphras Stones cannot be used on it, and wearing it triggers set effects of both boss gear and Slumbering Origin gear.

## Labreska's Helmet crafting materials

Labreska's Helmet needs one helmet plus Flame of Frost. The helmet can be PEN (V) Giath's Helmet at Caphras Lv. 10, PEN (V) Griffon's Helmet at Caphras Lv. 10 (the wiki lists 7,224 Caphras Stones for this), or PEN (V) Blackstar Helmet with no Caphras needed. Reformed Obsidian Blackstar Helmet cannot be used.

Flame of Frost comes from Heating Embers of Frost x100 in Processing (L). The flame and the embers drop at a low chance in the Mountain of Eternal Winter zones Erethea's Limbo, Murrowak's Labyrinth and Jade Starlight Forest. Erebjork <Wanewinter Guardian> and Erebjork <Dreadwinter Guardian> can also drop them, and Erebjork can appear at random in Jade Starlight Forest and in Winter Tree Fossil at the Zvier Highlands.

## Labreska's Helmet crafting quest

The quest [Crafting] Labreska's Helmet comes from Floki, chief of the Okjinsinis, in Jade Starlight Forest, and it opens only after the Labreska's Helmet questline. You need level 56 or higher and must have finished the main quest [Everfrost] Beyond the Doors of Alyaelli, which ends the Mountain of Eternal Winter main questline. Find Floki by right-clicking the Jade Starlight Forest node on the World Map (M). Floki offers the crafting quest only if your inventory holds Flame of Frost x1 and a PEN (V) Blackstar Helmet or a PEN (V) boss helmet at Caphras Lv. 10. If it does not show, enable the All quest type in the Quest window (O). Hand the items to Floki to receive the helmet.

## Dahn's Gloves overview

Dahn's Gloves are the Slumbering Origin gloves and come in two types. They can only be crafted or bought on the Central Market, and they beat Bheg's Gloves, Leebur's Gloves and Blackstar Gloves. Because monster drops in the Land of the Morning Light are so rare, the main ingredient instead comes from bosses in the Black Shrine at a set probability.

## Dahn's Gloves crafting materials

Dahn's Gloves need one pair of gloves plus Flame of Hongik. The gloves can be PEN (V) Bheg's Gloves at Caphras Lv. 10, PEN (V) Leebur's Gloves at Caphras Lv. 10 (the wiki lists 7,224 Caphras Stones for this), or PEN (V) Blackstar Gloves with no Caphras needed. Reformed Obsidian Blackstar Gloves cannot be used.

Flame of Hongik comes from Heating Embers of Hongik x500 in Processing (L). The flame or embers drop from Black Shrine bosses in the Land of the Morning Light at a set probability, and higher boss difficulty raises the chance. You can also trade Crystallized Despair x1 for Embers of Hongik x2 with Hyunyong in Nampo Moodle Village. Crystallized Despair drops from monsters in Elvia Calpheon.

## Dahn's Gloves crafting quest

The quest [Crafting] Dahn's Gloves is available after the Dahn's Gloves questline. Only characters of level 56 or higher who have finished the Land of the Morning Light main questline can do it, and the unlocking quests can be done only once per Family. The first quest, [Dahn's Gloves] The Name We Miss Dearly, Mihyun!, comes from the Black Spirit (,). After the questline, craft the gloves by interacting with the Fox Goddess Statue in Yeowoo Cave while holding the required items.

## Ator's Shoes overview

Ator's Shoes are the Slumbering Origin shoes and come in an Evasion type and a Damage Reduction type. They can only be crafted or bought on the Central Market, and they beat Muskan's Shoes, Urugon's Shoes and Blackstar Shoes. They are made from PEN (V) boss or Blackstar shoes plus Flame of Resonance, crafted by a quest in Ulukita, and have six enhancement levels.

## Ator's Shoes crafting materials

Ator's Shoes need one pair of shoes plus Flame of Resonance. The shoes can be PEN (V) Muskan's Shoes at Caphras Lv. 10, PEN (V) Urugon's Shoes at Caphras Lv. 10 (the wiki lists 7,224 Caphras Stones for this), or PEN (V) Blackstar Shoes with no Caphras needed. Reformed Obsidian Blackstar Shoes cannot be used.

Flame of Resonance comes from Heating Embers of Resonance x100 in Processing (L). The flame and embers have a low drop chance in Ulukita's Darkseekers' Retreat monster zone, part of the Exalted Land.

## Ator's Shoes crafting quest

The quest [Crafting] Ator's Shoes needs a character of level 56 or above and comes from Atessahra Node Manager Barkala in Ulukita. To be offered it, your inventory must hold Flame of Resonance x1 and a PEN (V) Blackstar Shoes or PEN (V) boss shoes at Caphras Lv. 10. If it does not show, enable the All quest type in the Quest window (O). After accepting, hand over the items to receive the shoes.
