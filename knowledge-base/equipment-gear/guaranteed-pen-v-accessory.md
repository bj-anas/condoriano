# Guaranteed PEN (V) Accessory

How the Old Moon Guild's Support window gives accessories with guaranteed enhancement up to PEN (V): the quest to unlock it, materials per level, and exchanging the chosen accessory.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) accessory: overview

The Old Moon Guild's Support window lets you get 2 accessories with guaranteed enhancement up to PEN (V), using exclusive materials. Each time you pick one of three: PEN (V) Ring of Crescent Guardian, PEN (V) Narc Ear Accessory or PEN (V) Tungrad Earring. You can get two per Family. Accessories obtained this way cannot be traded or registered on the Central Market.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) accessory: unlocking the Old Moon Guild's Support

To unlock the window, a character of level 56 or higher must complete the quest "Old Moon Guild's Trade Offer". It is listed under Progression Pass, then Guaranteed PEN (V), at the top right of the screen. The quest comes from Jetina the Old Moon Manager. Find her with the Find NPC function or near a stable in a major city. The quest completes after a simple conversation. Afterwards, the Old Moon Guild's Support button appears at the top left of the equipment window in Inventory (I).

## Guaranteed PEN (V) accessory: how to enhance

Choose one supported accessory: Ring of Crescent Guardian, Narc Ear Accessory or Tungrad Earring. The wiki suggests the ring if you are unsure. Put the materials for the level into the window, then add the accessory last, and press Obtain to enhance. You may first place only as much of each material as you currently own. Anything placed in the window cannot be taken back.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) accessory: exchanging the chosen accessory

You can switch the supported accessory to a different one inside the Old Moon Guild's Support window, and PEN (V) accessories can also be exchanged. Each Family gets 4 free changes. From the 5th change, spending Gold Bar 10,000G x2 allows unlimited changes per day. If the original accessory had a brand, the brand is kept in the exchange. Accessories with cups applied must have the cups extracted or removed first, before they can be used for progression or an exchange.
