# Special Outfits

Outfits you can obtain through in-game methods instead of buying them in the Pearl Shop: Karlstein, La Orzeca, Nouverikant and the Shai outfits.

## Special outfits overview

Most outfits are sold in the Pearl Shop, but some can be earned in game. Many of these are normal equipment that becomes an outfit with a tailoring coupon. Three are different: Karlstein, La Orzeca and Nouverikant are obtained directly as outfits placed in your appearance slots, with no tailoring coupon needed. Shai also has several themed outfits crafted at the Old Moon Mill. The Outfits & Costumes page covers the basics.

## Special outfits: how to unlock the Karlstein outfit

The Karlstein outfit needs Intricately Patterned Mystical Shard, a very low chance drop from monsters all over the world. With a shard, go to Rubin, the city herald in Calpheon, and take the quest "A Hero in Karlstein I", then finish the whole questline. After that, NPC Clara Siciliano offers the daily quest "[Daily] To Become Karlstein", which gives the design item needed for crafting. You also need a purchased house set as an Old Moon Mill. Houses with that function are in Heidel, Calpheon, Altinova, Grána and Duvencrune. Crafted Karlstein pieces go in the normal inventory, not the pearl inventory, unlike the Pearl Shop version.

## Special outfits: how to obtain the La Orzeca outfit

La Orzeca is an outfit based on the Black Spirit's look, earned in O'dyllita. Quests need Quturan's Left Lung x1 for the La Orzeca Helmet or Quturan's Right Lung x1 for the La Orzeca Armor. The lungs are rare drops, so you can instead use simple alchemy (hotkey "L"): Quturan's Ashen Leaf x100 makes a Right Lung, and Quturan's Black Leaf x100 makes a Left Lung. Ashen Leaf and Right Lung drop in Tunkuta, Olun's Valley and Crypt of the Resting Thoughts. Black Leaf and Left Lung drop in Thornwood Forest, Ash Forest and Crypt of the Resting Thoughts. With a lung, visit Azure Mantis at the Thornwood Watch in the Crypt of Resting Thoughts and complete the quests she gives, which reward a La Orzeca Helmet Box or La Orzeca Armor Box. Any class can open them. Female classes can get the helmet and armor in a look closer to the male version through the "[Daily] La Orzeca's Orb" quest from Limitte, but this change is permanent.

## Special outfits: La Orzeca outfit rebox to another class

To move a La Orzeca outfit to another character, go to Burt Bedmountain in O'draxxia, an Ahib city, carrying the outfit equipped or in your pearl inventory. Each repeatable daily quest costs Cantarnia's Crystal x2 and the piece, and gives the box back: "[Daily] Primeval Form: La Orzeca Helmet" gives a La Orzeca Helmet Box, and "[Daily] Primeval Form: La Orzeca Armor" gives a La Orzeca Armor Box. Cantarnia's Crystal costs 500,000,000 Silver from Francia in O'draxxia, so each quest costs 1 billion silver. If you made a crystal slot socket in the outfit, it disappears when reboxed.

## Special outfits: Nouverikant outfit and how to get the dormant pieces

The Nouverikant outfit is modeled on the world boss Bloodstorm Nouver. It has the same stats as pearl outfits, but is seen as a symbol of a master adventurer because the materials are costly and slow to gather. Fighting Bloodstorm Nouver can drop Bloodstorm Nouver's Split Horn and Bloodstorm Nouver's Ragged Wing. Take them to the guest from Everfrost in Valencia City, who trades them through a quest: a Split Horn becomes a Dormant Nouverikant Helmet and a Ragged Wing becomes a Dormant Nouverikant Armor. These are plain gear without power and must be enhanced to PEN (V).

## Special outfits: enhancing Nouverikant dormant gear with Bloodstorm Black Stones

Dormant Nouverikant pieces are enhanced with Bloodstorm Black Stones, and every attempt is guaranteed to succeed, but each level costs more stones. One Bloodstorm Black Stone comes from heating (hotkey "L") Black Stone x100 with Bloodlit Eye of the Desert x1. Bloodlit Eye of the Desert drops at a chance from Bloodstorm Nouver, or you can trade extra Ragged Wings and Split Horns to the Everfrost guest, who gives Bloodlit Eye of the Desert x30 for each. Taking one piece to PEN (V) takes Bloodstorm Black Stone x210, which equals Bloodlit Eye of the Desert x210 and Black Stone x21,000. For both pieces it is Bloodlit Eye of the Desert x420 and Black Stone x42,000.

## Special outfits: finishing the Nouverikant outfit and Nouvermon

After PEN (V) Dormant Nouverikant Helmet and Armor are ready, each piece needs Unstable Nouver Core x3 and Cantarnia's Crystal x30 to become usable for your class. The core comes from heating Concentrated Boss Aura x1 with Latent Boss Aura x10. Concentrated Boss Aura comes from heating unenhanced boss gear, and Latent Boss Aura drops from the world boss Nouver in any form. Cantarnia's Crystals are bought from Francia, the chief librarian of O'draxxia, at 500 million Silver each, so both pieces need 60 crystals, or 30 billion silver. Use the manufacture processing option (hotkey "L") with the three items to get a Sealed Nouverikant Armor or Helmet, then open it on the character you choose. With both outfit pieces equipped, the Everfrost guest gives a quest for the pet Nouvermon and the title "Nouverikant". Nouvermon is a tier 4 special pet with a +4% Combat EXP talent and the Hostility Detection special ability, and it cannot be exchanged with Wizard Gosphy pets.

## Special outfits: Nouverikant outfit rebox to another class

To switch the Nouverikant outfit to another class, go to Burt Bedmountain in O'draxxia, an Ahib city, with the outfit on you or in your pearl inventory, and do the repeatable quests "[Daily] Sealed Nouverikant Helmet" and "[Daily] Sealed Nouverikant Armor". Each needs Cantarnia's Crystal x2 and the matching Nouverikant piece, and gives the sealed piece. Francia in O'draxxia sells Cantarnia's Crystal for 500,000,000 Silver. A crystal slot socket you created in the outfit disappears when it is reboxed.
