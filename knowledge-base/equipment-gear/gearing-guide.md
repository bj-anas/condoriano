# Gearing Guide

A progression guide to gear in Black Desert, from the starter Naru set up to end-game Slumbering Origin and Sovereign weapons, with the usual ways to improve each stage.

## Gearing guide overview and progression order

Enhancing gear is key to fighting the many monsters of Black Desert. The guide walks through the gear stages in order: Naru gear, then Narchillan (normal characters) or Tuvala (season characters) gear, then boss gear, then accessories, then Blackstar gear, then Slumbering Origin gear, and finally Sovereign weapons.

## Gearing guide: Naru gear for new adventurers

Naru gear comes as quest rewards early in the main questline, in Balenos and Serendia, and is essential for new adventurers. Equip it and gather Beginner Black Stones, aiming for a full PEN (V) set. Its success rate is high, so you can reach PEN (V) just by pressing the Enhancement button with enough stones, no failstacks needed. Quest stone rewards grow a lot by the time you reach Mediah. If you are short even after finishing the Mediah main questline, Heating Black Stones (weapon or armor) in the Processing (L) window gives more Beginner Black Stones, but treat that as a last resort. Around PEN (V) Naru, normal characters get Narchillan gear (once per Family) and season characters swap for PRI (I) Tuvala gear. See the Naru Gear page.

## Gearing guide: Narchillan gear for normal characters

You can get Narchillan gear once per Family. Its stats sit around those of TRI (III)-TET (IV) boss gear or DUO (II)-TRI (III) Blackstar gear. Together with Capotia Accessories, it lets you hunt in monster zones recommending 240 AP, for instance Aakman Temple (Valencia) or Forest Ronaros in Tooth Fairy Forest (Kamasylvia). Steps: reach Lv. 56 or finish Calpheon's main questline, accept "[Special Aid] Fughar's Letter" from the Black Spirit, find NPC Fughar in any town, and complete the related quests. Season characters cannot take these quests, but graduated season characters can. Each Family can receive this gear only one time.

## Gearing guide: moving Narchillan weapons to another class

If you switch class and cannot equip your Narchillan weapons, you can turn them into a box that another class can open. Buy Narchillan Processing Stones from a blacksmith in any major city for 1 million silver each, use the Manufacture processing option with a Narchillan Processing Stone and the Narchillan weapon, then open the Narchillan Weapon Box on the other class to get weapons for that class. See the Narchillan Gear page.

## Gearing guide: Tuvala gear for season characters

Tuvala gear is season-exclusive gear that only season characters can equip. After season graduation, normal characters can equip it too, and can exchange its pieces for TET (IV) boss gear, one piece per season. The main goal for a season character is to graduate after getting all Tuvala pieces to PEN (V), but other season objectives may be missed if you graduate too early. A season character gets an exchange UI once Naru gear reaches PEN (V), or can obtain PRI (I) Tuvala gear through the Equipment window (hotkey I). A Naru accessory cannot be exchanged for a Tuvala accessory; Tuvala accessories come from exchanging 5 Time-filled Black Stones with blacksmiths in each town. Time-filled Black Stone is used to exchange, repair and enhance Tuvala gear, and drops from monsters on season servers and from season quests and challenges. Tuvala enhancement is harder than Naru but easier than boss gear, so use failstacks from a certain level. See the Season Gear Guide page.

## Gearing guide: Cliff's weapons for new and returning adventurers

Cliff's weapons are for new and returning adventurers only. A new adventurer created a secondary password within the last 60 days (1440 hours). A returning adventurer is one who has not logged in and has not used the Web Central Market (Black Desert+ or the official website) for more than 30 days (720 hours). First complete the "[New/Returning] Cliff's Weapon" quest from the Black Spirit, which needs any version of Calpheon's main questline or the Mountain of Eternal Winter main questline. Cliff, at the Western Guard Camp in Balenos, then gives level-appropriate quests, and you choose a Cliff's Main/Awakening/Sub-Weapon (+15) Box. Complete three quests for the three weapons. They can be enhanced up to TET. If unneeded, exchange them with NPC Miguel Aries for gold bars.

## Gearing guide: Roaring Magical Armor

The Roaring Magical Armor is a reward for the quest "[Boss] Illezra's Servant" in the last part of the Mediah main questline. It is better than a PEN (V) Naru Armor but worse than a PRI (I) Tuvala Armor or a Narchillan Armor, and it cannot be enhanced. It does not compete with Tuvala or Narchillan gear and is just an extra option.

## Gearing guide: boss gear

Boss gear drops from World Bosses and field bosses and has the boss's name in the item name. Its stats are fairly high: TET (IV) boss gear performs about the same as, or slightly better than, PEN (V) Tuvala gear. If you have all PEN (V) Tuvala pieces or Narchillan gear, get TET (IV) boss gear first and then enhance it to PEN (V). See the World Bosses and Field Bosses pages.

## Gearing guide: guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear

A graduated season character can convert Tuvala gear into regular Tuvala gear usable by normal or graduated characters. If your Tuvala gear is PEN (V), a quest exchanges it for TET (IV) boss gear that you can then upgrade to PEN (V) boss gear. Boss gear from these quests is Family-bound and cannot be sold or registered on the Central Market. Later you can turn it into the end-game pieces Fallen God's Armor, Labreska's Helmet, Ator's Shoes and Dahn's Gloves. Those pieces are no longer Family-bound and may be listed on the Central Market. See the Guaranteed PEN (V) Boss Gear page.

## Gearing guide: guaranteed PEN (V) accessory and yellow grade accessories

Accept and complete the quests for a guaranteed PEN (V) accessory, treating it as a long-term task. The reward is a single PEN (V) accessory of your choice (Ring of Crescent Guardian, Narc Ear Accessory or Tungrad Earring), once for each Family. PEN (V) accessories are rare and costly because a failed enhancement destroys the pieces used and the success chances are low. Keep trying enhancement meanwhile if you wish. You can swap between the three accessory types through quests, and the Ring of Crescent Guardian is the most popular. Normal yellow grade accessories drop from monster zones: the Ring of Crescent Guardian in Crescent Shrine in Valencia and Tungrad accessories in ancient ruins. They can also be bought on the Central Market. See the Guaranteed PEN (V) Accessory and Monster Zone Info pages.

## Gearing guide: PEN (V) Capotia accessory season graduation gift

Graduating from a season gives one PEN (V) Capotia accessory as a gift. It performs like a regular yellow grade TET (IV) accessory and is a tier above the PEN (V) Tuvala Ring. Claim it from Fughar using a season character at Lv. 20 or above, or any character who finished the "[Season Server Graduation] Into the Big Wide World" quest. You can only take one per season character and once per Family. The wiki strongly recommends choosing the PEN (V) Capotia Necklace first, because it gives the biggest AP increase compared to the earring, ring or belt. See the Season Graduation page.

## Gearing guide: Asula's Crimson Eye accessories

While working toward better accessories, the Asula's Crimson Eye set (necklace, earrings, ring, belt) is enough for the main questlines. Get it by defeating monsters in the Mediah zones Abandoned Iron Mine, Helms Post and Elric Shrine. Enhancement also needs Asula's Weakened Magic Earring, Necklace, Ring and Belt, found in several monster zones in Mediah and Valencia, and 3 of them can be exchanged for one Asula's Crimson Eye accessory. Subjugation missions in Abandoned Iron Mine, Helms Post, Wandering Rogue Den and Elric Shrine start automatically when you enter at the right level and reward DUO (II) to TRI (III) Asula's Crimson Eye accessories.

## Gearing guide: main questline accessories

Main questlines reward four accessories, once per character: Red Sand Crystal Ring (Valencia), Forest's Eye Earring (Kamasylvia), Rock Heart Belt (Drieghan) and Tears of the Wind Necklace (O'dyllita). They can be enhanced up to TRI (III) with normal Black Stones. A failure only costs durability and does not destroy them, and Memory Fragments restore 50 durability each. All characters in a Family can equip them without limits.

| Accessory | Quest |
|---|---|
| Red Sand Crystal Ring | [Valencia] Don't Need a Paper to Prove It!, or the crossroad quest after [Valencia] Edana's Descendant |
| Forest's Eye Earring | [Kamasylvia] The Heart of Kamasylve |
| Rock Heart Belt | To Duvencrune |
| Tears of the Wind Necklace | [O'dyllita] Saigord, The Shadow of Nightmares |

## Gearing guide: Kharazad accessories

Kharazad accessories are the last goal for accessories and follow different rules. They use the enhancement rules of equipment rather than accessories, so you do not need another copy of the same accessory. They use an exclusive item, Essence of Dawn, which is also an ingredient when crafting them. It is gained by heating crystals dropped in Land of the Morning Light monster zones. Equipping one unlocks an exclusive crystal slot. See the Kharazad Accessories page.

## Gearing guide: Blackstar gear

Blackstar gear is stronger than boss gear, with stats specialized for PvE. TET (IV) Blackstar gear is on par with PEN (V) boss gear, but Blackstar cannot use Caphras Stones. PEN (V) Blackstar is similar to PEN (V) boss gear at maximum Caphras enhancement. Once per Family you can obtain a TET (IV) and a PEN (V) Blackstar weapon through the Challenge (Y) window, if your character is Lv. 61 or higher and has done one of the following. You can also buy Blackstar pieces on the Central Market, or craft them through questlines once per character. See the Blackstar Gear page.

| Requirement | Quest name |
|---|---|
| Complete the simplified main questline | [Special Growth] Fughar's Memorandum - Chapter 11 |
| Complete the main questline up to Mediah | Apocalyptic Prophecy |
| Complete the Mountain of Eternal Winter main questline | [Everfrost] Beyond the Doors of Alyaelli |
| Complete the Land of the Morning Light main questline | [Koo Mihyun] Seungsan's Secret |

## Gearing guide: Slumbering Origin gear

Fallen God's Armor, Labreska's Helmet, Dahn's Gloves and Ator's Shoes have the highest stats in Black Desert and are called end-game gear. Crafting them takes either a PEN (V) Blackstar Armor, or PEN (V) boss gear taken to Caphras Lv. 10 or more (for example a PEN (V) Giath's Helmet or Red Nose's Armor), together with a Flame of Despair, Frost, Resonance or Hongik. See the Slumbering Origin Gear page.

## Gearing guide: Sovereign weapons

Sovereign weapons are the first Primordial tier weapons and the best weapons in the game, and can be enhanced up to DEC, which is 10 levels instead of 5. For now only the main weapon and Awakening weapon can be made. There are three methods: PEN (V) Blackstar weapon plus another PEN (V) Blackstar weapon; PEN (V) Blackstar weapon plus Flame of the Primordial; or PEN (V) Blackstar weapon plus PEN (V) boss weapon at Caphras Lv. 20 plus Gem of Twilight. The materials are the weapon type matching the Sovereign weapon you want to craft, for example a Blackstar main weapon and a boss main weapon for a Sovereign main weapon via the third method. See the Sovereign Weapons page.
