# Item Reform

The Item Reform system, which uses special materials to add extra stats to weapons, accessories, Alchemy Stones and Artifacts, separate from normal enhancement.

## Item Reform: how to reform gear

Item Reform spends specific materials to improve gear performance and grants Additional Stats on top of enhancement. You can do it any time you have the materials. Summon the Black Spirit with the "," hotkey, choose the Reform Gear menu, register the gear and the materials, then click the Reform button.

## Item Reform: weapon reform

Main Weapons, Awakening Weapons and Sub-weapons can be reformed with boss hearts. Sovereign Weapons, the highest-tier equipment, use an Inverted Heart of Garmoth.

| Target | Material | Key effects |
|---|---|---|
| Sovereign Main, Awakening and Sub-weapons | Inverted Heart of Garmoth | Displayed AP +3, All Accuracy +50, Crystal Socket +2, Critical Hit Extra Damage +3%, Max HP +900, Max Stamina +100 |
| Non-sovereign Awakening Weapons | Inverted Heart of Garmoth, Heart of Karanda | Crystal Socket +2 (Inverted Heart of Garmoth), Crystal Socket +1 (Heart of Karanda), Critical Hit Damage +3% |
| Non-sovereign Sub-weapon | Heart of Kutum | Max HP +525 |

## Item Reform: accessory reform with Cups

Rings, Necklaces, Earrings and Belts are reformed with Cup items, and the effect depends on the slot. Ekleta, Apeiron and Kharazad Accessories can also use exclusive Cups for stronger effects. Cup items can be extracted using Refined Essence of Emotions.

| Accessory | Slot | Cup | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Yellow-Grade | Belt | Cup of Lone Tide | All Damage Reduction +6 |
| General Yellow-Grade | Belt | Cup of Tragic Nightfall | All AP +3 |
| General Yellow-Grade | Ring | Cup of a Lonely Cloud | Max HP +125 |
| General Yellow-Grade | Ring | Cup of Dwindling Starlight | Critical Hit Damage +3% |
| General Yellow-Grade | Necklace | Cup of Arid Moonlight | Max HP +150 |
| General Yellow-Grade | Earrings | Cup of Earth's Sorrows | All AP +3 |
| Ekleta, Apeiron, Kharazad | Belt | Cup of Burgeoning Dusk | All AP +3, All Damage Reduction +6 |
| Ekleta, Apeiron, Kharazad | Ring | Cup of Callous Sun | Max HP +125, Critical Hit Damage +3% |
| Ekleta, Apeiron, Kharazad | Necklace | Cup of Destined Dawn | Max HP +300 |
| Ekleta, Apeiron, Kharazad | Earrings | Cup of Reticent Moonbeams | All AP +3, Max Stamina +100 |

## Item Reform: Alchemy Stone upgrades

Alchemy Stones of Yellow grade or higher can be upgraded with a Blessed Soul Fragment or an Exalted Soul Fragment, which raises their DP and Damage Reduction Rate. Powerful stones such as Vell's Heart reach full potential this way.

| Upgrade item | Works on | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| Blessed Soul Fragment | Yellow-Grade or higher Alchemy Stone | Displayed DP (All Damage Reduction) +2, Monster Damage Reduction Rate +1%, All Resistance +2% |
| Exalted Soul Fragment | Yellow-Grade or higher Alchemy Stone already blessed | The Blessed effect plus Displayed DP (All Damage Reduction) +2 |

Upgradable stones: Sharp, Resplendent, Splendid and Shining Alchemy Stones of Destruction; Vell's Heart; Khan's Heart: Destruction; Ator's Power Stone; Sharp, Resplendent, Splendid and Shining Alchemy Stones of Protection; Perilla's Star; Khan's Heart: Protection.

## Item Reform: Artifact upgrades

Kabua's Artifact and Dehkia's Artifact can be upgraded with a Flawless Herald's Crystal. An upgraded Artifact gains Extra Effects that help in combat, including Extra AP Against Monsters, All Damage Reduction and Max HP Increase, among others. Register the Artifact and the Flawless Herald's Crystal in the Reform Gear menu of the Black Spirit, opened with the "," hotkey, and click Reform.

## Item Reform: extracting Reform Stones

Reformed items cannot be listed on the Central Market. To sell the gear, or to reuse the reform material on another item, extract the Reform Stone at a Blacksmith NPC in any city by choosing Extraction, then Extract Reform Stone. The extraction material depends on the gear. General equipment and Yellow-grade accessories use a Mirror of Equilibrium, sold by Blacksmith NPCs. Green-Grade equipment uses a Restoration Stone, sold by Blacksmith NPCs. Tuvala equipment uses a Combatant's Restoration Stone, sold by Blacksmiths or Armor Vendors.
