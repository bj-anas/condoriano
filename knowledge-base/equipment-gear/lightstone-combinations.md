# Lightstone Combinations

The lightstone combination effects you get when specific lightstones are infused into your equipped artifacts, grouped by purpose.

## Lightstone combinations overview and rules

When the lightstones infused into your equipped artifacts match a listed combination, that combination's effects apply. A combination built from three lightstones keeps working if you infuse a fourth, but if the extra lightstone completes a different combination, the new one may apply instead. Each combination uses three or four lightstones across the two equipped artifacts. Many combinations include an Iridescent Lightstone as one of the stones. The basics of artifacts and lightstones are on the Artifacts & Lightstones page.
