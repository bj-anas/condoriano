# Blackstar Gear

The powerful Blackstar set: what you need to craft it, the quests, repeat crafting, and the free TET (IV) and PEN (V) Blackstar weapons given at level 61.

## Blackstar gear overview

Blackstar gear is very strong equipment aimed at experienced adventurers. It needs some preparation, and each piece can be obtained only once per character through quests. The set covers main weapon, sub-weapon, Awakening weapon, helmet, armor, gloves and shoes.

## Blackstar gear preparation requirements

Before starting, you need the knowledge entry "The Three Legendary Blacksmiths of Mediah" and three kinds of items. Remnants of the Rift, Concentrated Boss's Aura and Specter's Energy are needed for most pieces.

| Requirement | How to get it | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Remnants of the Rift | Low-chance drop from Dark Rift bosses, or the Central Market | Mid |
| The Three Legendary Blacksmiths of Mediah | Complete a questline | Low |
| Concentrated Boss's Aura | Heat boss gear, which can be bought on the Central Market | Mid |
| Specter's Energy | Monster loot, or a boss summoned by Ibedor's Scroll | High |

## Blackstar gear Three Legendary Blacksmiths of Mediah knowledge

Learn this knowledge by finishing the Mediah main questline, then accepting and completing the Three Blacksmiths questline from Tulem, the arms dealer in Altinova. It has 13 quests and is quick if Mediah is already done. It introduces Dorin Morgrim, the only blacksmith able, and bold enough, to craft Blackstar gear. With this knowledge and Remnants of the Rift, you can accept the quests that lead to crafting.

## Blackstar gear Concentrated Boss's Aura and Specter's Energy

Concentrated Boss's Aura comes from Heating (L) unenhanced boss weapons such as Kzarka and Kutum, or unenhanced boss armor such as Giath's Helmet or Bheg's Gloves. They drop at a low chance from world and field bosses or can be bought on the Central Market.

Specter's Energy is needed for every Blackstar piece except the main weapon, and it is the hardest material, since it drops only from monsters at a low chance. Drop chances are a little better in Thornwood Forest, Protty Cave, Sherekhan Necropolis (Night) and Sycraia Ruins (Lower zone). Venomous Night Fang items from O'dyllita monsters, placed in a [+] shape, make Ibedor's Scroll, which summons a boss with a low chance to drop it. You can also exchange 10 Dark Spirit's Greed, dropped by Dark Rift bosses, for Specter's Energy through the Manage Currency window.

## Blackstar gear quest progress

Start the Blackstar questlines from Martha Kiyen at the Northern Wheat Plantation. Right-click Remnants of the Rift and choose NPC Location to find her. The quests investigate the Blackstar in northwestern Calpheon. At a crossroads quest you choose a route by your AP and DP: high AP players fight a powerful boss quickly, while lower AP players clear many monsters in a zone and then fight a weakened version of the same boss.

Cautions: if Martha Kiyen will not offer the first quest for a piece, you probably already have another piece's quests open, and only the first few quests of each slot can be done separately. At the boss stage, keep only one Blackstar questline active, or you may sacrifice the Eye of Desolation for the wrong quest. If the boss will not appear at the brazier, make sure no quest such as [Combat] The Incarnation of Corruption at Star's End (or a similar one) is already running, since all of them use the same brazier.

## Blackstar gear crafting without quest

After completing the crafting quest for a piece, you can accept the [Blackstar] All About Blackstar quest for that piece from the Black Spirit. Once done, you can craft that piece again without redoing the story quests. Visit Dorin Morgrim inside Helms Abandoned Mine to start it for each weapon or armor piece. Each Blackstar equipment quest can be completed only once per character.

| Material per piece | Source |
|---|---|
| Specter's Energy x1 | Monster drops in Valencia, Kamasylvia and Drieghan zones |
| Concentrated Boss's Aura x3 | Heating world boss armor or weapons |
| Remnants of the Rift x2 | Rare Dark Rift boss drop, or Central Market |
| Blackstar Armor or Weapon Piece x1 | Free from Dorin Morgrim |

## Blackstar gear free TET (IV) weapon

A character reaching level 61 for the first time can choose a free TET (IV) Blackstar weapon, either a main weapon, a sub-weapon or an Awakening weapon. A player who owns every Blackstar weapon already can take [Event] Dim Origin of Dark Hunger instead, an item that adds failstacks to enhancement attempts. You must be level 61 and have done one of the following (if already done, claim it in the Challenge window, Y). It can be obtained once per Family.

| Requirement (only one) | Quest |
|---|---|
| Simplified main questline | [Special Growth] Fughar's Memorandum - Chapter 11 |
| Main questline up to Mediah | Apocalyptic Prophecy |
| Mountain of Eternal Winter main questline | [Everfrost] Beyond the Doors of Alyaelli |
| Land of the Morning Light main questline | [Koo Mihyun] Seungsan's Secret |

## Blackstar gear free PEN (V) weapon

A free PEN (V) Blackstar weapon, main or Awakening only, is also available at level 61 with the same requirements as the free TET (IV) weapon: one of the four listed main questline quests, [Special Growth] Fughar's Memorandum - Chapter 11, Apocalyptic Prophecy, [Everfrost] Beyond the Doors of Alyaelli or [Koo Mihyun] Seungsan's Secret. Claim it from the Challenge window (Y), once per Family. Right-click the box to choose main or Awakening weapon, then type the confirmation text exactly as shown.
