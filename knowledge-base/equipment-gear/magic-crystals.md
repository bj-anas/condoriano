# Magic Crystals

How to use Magic Crystals: the crystal inventory, presets, extracting and removing crystals, per-group limits, expanding slots and presets, and restoring broken crystals.

## Magic Crystals overview

Magic crystals raise your stats and are transfused from the Inventory window (hotkey "I"). The system covers registering crystals into the Crystal Inventory and presets, extracting or removing (destroying) them, group limits, expanding slots, presets and inventory, and restoring broken crystals.

## Magic Crystals: registering crystals into the Crystal Inventory

Open your inventory and press the Transfusion icon at the bottom of the window. Then press the Crystal Inventory button at the bottom right of the Transfusion window. In the Crystal Inventory UI you move crystals from your regular Inventory into the Crystal Inventory. It starts with room for 50 crystals, and you need the Crystal Extraction Tool to extract or remove crystals from it.

## Magic Crystals: using crystal presets

After registering crystals in the Crystal Inventory, open the Transfusion UI again and click an empty "[+]" slot to transfuse a registered crystal. When you have transfused all you want, press Apply at the bottom. The Applied Crystal Effects column on the right shows the crystals in effect. You can save different crystal setups to the Crystal Presets buttons on the left, so you can switch quickly. You can rename a preset with the button on the Transfusion UI.

## Magic Crystals: extracting crystals

To extract a crystal you need a Crystal Extraction Tool or Black Spirit Essence in your inventory. Talk to a blacksmith and click Extraction <4>, then press the Extract Crystal button to open the Owned Crystals (Crystal Inventory) UI. Select the Crystal Extraction Tool or Black Spirit Essence, then right-click the crystal to extract. Make the Crystal Extraction Tool with Processing (L) - Simple Alchemy using Magical Shard x10 and Pure Iron Crystal x100. Buy Black Spirit Essence in the Pearl Shop.

## Magic Crystals: removing and destroying crystals

To destroy a crystal instead, open the Transfusion window from your inventory and press the Owned Crystal button at the bottom right. Then press the trash bin Remove Crystal button at the top right, select the crystal and press Remove. Removed crystals are destroyed. Right-clicking to remove a crystal in the Crystal Inventory UI also destroys it.

## Magic Crystals: unlocking the bottom crystal slots with Kkebiwater

Completing certain Land of the Morning Light main quests gives the item Kkebiwater, which unlocks the crystal slots at the bottom of the window. See the Collection of Tales page for more.

| Item | How to obtain | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Kkebiwater | Complete "[LoML] Strange and Beautiful Dokkebi Chest" (Tale of Duoksini) | Unlocks the bottom left slot |
| Kkebiwater | Complete "[LoML] The One Who Commands Taebaek" | Unlocks the bottom right slot |

## Magic Crystals: Kharazad exclusive crystal slots

Six crystal sockets that belong only to Kharazad Accessories become available once you equip those accessories. Each equipped Kharazad Accessory grants one exclusive socket per accessory slot. These sockets are a separate benefit of Kharazad gear, which also follows equipment-style enhancement rules and uses Essence of Dawn. See the Kharazad Accessories page.

## Magic Crystals: expanding crystal presets and the Crystal Inventory

You start with up to 5 crystal presets and room for 50 crystals in the Crystal Inventory. Reach Lv. 58 and complete "[Naderr's Band] Black Spirit's New Powers", then accept "Black Spirit's Gift (Crystal Preset)" from the Black Spirit to expand presets by 1 and the Crystal Inventory by 5. A similar quest series, "[ADV Support] [Lv. 58] More Crystal Presets for Transfusion", is listed under the Suggested tab of the Quest window (hotkey "O"). The Pearl Shop also sells two coupons for Loyalties, once per item: the [Event] Crystal Preset +1 Expansion Coupon, plus the [Event] Crystal Inventory +5 Expansion Coupon. The Pearl Shop sells the rest, up to 15 presets total and 140 crystal inventory slots.

## Magic Crystals: special Transfusion slots from reforming, sockets and Kkebiwater

The slots on the left and right of the Transfusion UI unlock based on how your Awakening weapon was reformed, using Heart of Karanda or Garmoth's Heart, and the crystal in them is deactivated if you use the preset while wearing unreformed gear. The middle slot unlocks when you equip gear with a socket made with a Black Spirit's Claw, and it accepts only an Ancient Spirit's Crystal. The slots at the bottom are unlocked by Kkebiwater. See the Garmoth's Heart, Outfits & Costumes and Collection of Tales pages.

## Magic Crystals: crystals shattering on death

If a crystal shatters on death while in your current preset, it also shatters in your inventory and cannot be used in presets. The Ancient Spirit's Crystal in the middle slot does not shatter. The same Karma rules as before the update decide how many crystals shatter when you die, whether from a monster or another player, and they also decide the drop in enhancement levels on death. See the Karma and Naval Fame page.

## Magic Crystals: restoring broken crystals

Dying to monsters gives a small chance of some crystals being destroyed. You can restore them 3 times a year, plus 3 more through Pearl Shop coupons. Use the Restore Crystals button on the death screen, the trash can button at the bottom right of the Inventory (I) after resurrection, or the Crystal tab in Menu > Adventurer Support > Item Recover. The list of broken crystals is per Family, and restoring must happen within 72 hours of the character's death, up to 3 times a year per Family, with the limit resetting at your first login on January 1. Restoring many crystals at once counts as one use. Restored crystals go to the Crystal Inventory. If the Family has negative Karma (Karma Stats or Naval Fame) when crystals break, those crystals cannot be restored. Crystals broken before this feature update are not listed.
