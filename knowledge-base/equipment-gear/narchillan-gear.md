# Narchillan Gear

A one-per-Family gear set earned through a quest: what it includes, how to get it, its limits, how it reforms Tuvala gear, and how its weapons can be turned into boxes.

## Narchillan gear overview

Narchillan gear can be obtained only once per Family, through a quest. Its strength sits between TET (IV) and PEN (V) Tuvala gear, and it needs no enhancement, which makes it attractive and easier to get than PEN (V) Tuvala gear. It is Family bound, so other characters in the Family can use it. It can also be used to strengthen Tuvala gear. The set has no accessories, and season characters are not able to wear it.

## Narchillan gear pieces

Narchillan gear has seven pieces, all obtained through boxes from a quest. Weapons: Narchillan Main Weapon, Narchillan Sub-Weapon and Narchillan Awakening Weapon. Armor: Narchillan Helmet, Narchillan Armor, Narchillan Gloves and Narchillan Shoes. The set effects of Narchillan armor match those of Blackstar gear and boss gear. There is no Narchillan accessory.

## Narchillan gear how to obtain

Narchillan gear comes from the quest "[Special Aid] Fughar's Letter". To take the quest you must meet one of these: reach level 56 with the character, or complete Calpheon's main questline. Then accept the quest from the Black Spirit, go to Fughar, who stands in each town, and finish the related quests he gives. The gear arrives inside boxes. A weapon box gives the weapon that fits the class of the character who opens it, so you can move the box to another character if you want that character to have the gear. You can obtain Narchillan gear only once per Family.

## Narchillan gear enhancement limits

Narchillan gear cannot be enhanced, and Caphras Stones cannot be applied to it, and its stats stay between those of TET (IV) and PEN (V) Tuvala gear. Since no enhancement is needed, the wiki calls it an attractive option. Its set effects are inactive while a piece's durability is 0. You can obtain Narchillan gear only once per Family, and the pieces are Family bound.

## Narchillan gear reforming Tuvala gear

Narchillan gear can be turned into Combatant's Stones, which reform Tuvala gear. Only Tuvala gear already converted for a regular character can be reformed, not season-server Tuvala gear that has not been converted.

| Tuvala item | Stone needed | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Main or Awakening Weapon | Combatant's Stone (Weapon) | Extra AP Against Monsters +7 |
| Sub-Weapon | Combatant's Stone (Weapon) | Extra AP Against Monsters +3, Monster Damage Reduction +3 |
| Helmet, Armor, Gloves, Shoes | Combatant's Stone (Armor) | Monster Damage Reduction +7 |

Reform at the Black Spirit with the Upgrade/Reform option, or select the Combatant's Stone in your inventory to open the Item Reform window.

## Narchillan gear getting Combatant's Stones

First heat a piece of Narchillan gear (Processing, then Heating) to get Spirit's First Breath. This cannot be undone. Then bring it to Mary at Atanis Pond in the Kamasylvia region, who gives the quests [Special Aid] Where the Spirits First Breathe: Weapon and [Special Aid] Where the Spirits First Breathe: Armor, once per Family each. Up to 2 Combatant's Stones can be used per Family, so heat 2 pieces. Players usually heat weapons rather than armor, because armor can be passed to any character without class limits. Use Find NPC if you cannot locate Mary.

## Narchillan gear recovering it from Combatant's Stones

Buy Combatant's Restoration Stone, a single-use item, from any Blacksmith NPC for 5,000,000 Silver. Then, at a Blacksmith NPC, choose Extraction and then Extract Reform Stone to take the Combatant's Stone out of the reformed Tuvala gear. To turn stones back into Narchillan gear, heat a Combatant's Stone to get Combatant's Stone Fragments and Spirit's First Breath, and heat your other Narchillan pieces into Spirit's First Breath. With 7 Spirit's First Breath, visit Fughar for the quest "[Special Aid] By the Power of Narchillan Once More", which gives all 7 Narchillan pieces. Fragments plus Spirit's First Breath can rebuild the Combatant's Stone.

## Narchillan gear converting weapons into boxes

To get a Narchillan weapon for a different class, turn the weapon back into a box using a Narchillan Processing Stone, sold by certain Blacksmith NPCs for 1,000,000 Silver each. The stone must match the weapon type. Vendors by town: Velia, Tranan Underfoe; Heidel, Techthon; Calpheon, Grandus; Altinova, Mevo Muranan; Valencia, Elfa; Grána, Dikzipo; Duvencrune, Hughol; O'dyllita, Limitte. Manufacture the weapon with its stone to make the matching box: Main Weapon Box, Sub Weapon Box or Awakening Box. Move the box to another character through storage, and that character opens it for a weapon fitting its class.
