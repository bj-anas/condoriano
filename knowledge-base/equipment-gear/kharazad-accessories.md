# Kharazad Accessories

Primordial grade accessories that do not break on failed enhancement and take special Dawn crystals: how to get them, their stats, enhancement rates, Essence of Dawn sources, crystals and cup upgrades.

## Kharazad accessories overview

Kharazad accessories work differently from other accessories. Enhancing them does not need another copy of the same accessory, and a failed attempt does not destroy them. Special Dawn crystals made only for Kharazad accessories can be transfused onto each accessory when it is equipped, one crystal per accessory. They are Primordial grade with 10 enhancement levels, and they use Essence of Dawn for enhancement.

## Kharazad accessories pieces and stats

There are four types: Kharazad Necklace, Belt, Ring and Earring. Belt and Ring have identical stats. The PEN (V) Kharazad accessory has the same AP as a TET (IV) Deboreka accessory, and the OCT (VIII) Kharazad accessory has the same AP as a PEN (V) Deboreka accessory.

## Kharazad accessories how to obtain: manufacture

You can craft a Kharazad accessory in Processing with Manufacture (L). Each of the four types needs Essence of Dawn x50, Magical Shard x300 and the matching Deboreka accessory x1 (Deboreka Necklace, Belt, Ring or Earring). Magical Shards can be gathered in bulk by mass processing Sealed Black Magic Crystals.

## Kharazad accessories how to obtain: Deboreka exchange quests

Non-upgraded TET (IV) and PEN (V) Deboreka accessories can be exchanged for PEN (V) and NOV (IX) Kharazad accessories. The wiki names Clorince, the General Goods Vendor in Velia, as the quest giver, and in another passage Ain Greid at the Society of Equilibrium in Ulukita. The preceding quests are "[Kharazad Accessory] News from Clorince" and "[Kharazad Accessory] Artifact of the Aksulp Dynasty".

| Quest | Hand over | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| [Crafting] PEN (V) Kharazad Accessory (matching slot) | TET (IV) Deboreka Accessory | PEN (V) Kharazad Accessory of the same slot, and JIN Dawn Crystal - All AP x1 |
| [Crafting] NOV (IX) Kharazad Accessory (matching slot) | PEN (V) Deboreka Accessory | NOV (IX) Kharazad Accessory of the same slot, and JIN Dawn Crystal - All AP x1 |

If you abandon a quest after handing over an accessory, it cannot be recovered, so contact customer support. Find the quests in the Quests (O) window under General, Recurring or All. After the pre-quest is done once per Family, the other exchange quests have no limit. The NOV (IX) reward is branded with your Family name, replacing any earlier name.

## Kharazad accessories how to obtain: Alustin's PEN (V) support quests

Alustin in Velia supports adventurers with PEN (V) Kharazad accessories. As a HYPERBOOST benefit, once per Family you can complete quests to craft every PEN (V) Kharazad accessory. You must first complete "[Olvia Academy] Your First Steps In".

The six quests are [Alustin's Support] PEN (V) Kharazad Necklace, Belt, Ring I, Ring II, Earring I and Earring II. Each needs Essence of Dawn x10 and Black Crystal Shard x50 handed to Alustin, and gives the matching PEN (V) Kharazad piece.

## Kharazad accessories enhancement rules

Kharazad accessories share some enhancement rules with weapons and armor and some with normal accessories.

| Rule | Normal accessories | Kharazad accessories | Weapons and armor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Needs same item as material | Yes | No | No |
| On failure | Item destroyed | Max durability drops and level drops | Max durability drops and level drops |
| Cron Stones | 40% chance to prevent level drop | Prevent level drop | Prevent level drop |
| Enhancement chance on failure | +1 | +1 | Rises by level |

They are branded with your Family name once successfully enhanced to OCT (VIII), and they can be group enhanced. Agris Essence is saved per accessory slot, and upgraded accessories in the same slot share it.

## Kharazad accessories Essence of Dawn and Dawn Black Stone sources

Essence of Dawn is used to enhance Kharazad accessories from +0 to NOV (IX) and to craft or upgrade Kharazad accessories and crystals. It sells on the Central Market for at most 100 million Silver. Ways to get it: Heat Dawn's Aura x1 with certain yellow grade TET (IV) or PEN (V) accessories; Heat crystals dropped in Land of the Morning Light monster zones; or exchange Essence of Dawn - Black Spirit's Rage, Accuracy, Damage Reduction or Evasion 1:1 through the NPC Dalishain. The reverse exchange is not possible. Dawn's Aura costs 5 million Silver from Old Moon Manager NPCs in major cities.

Dawn Black Stone, used only for the DEC (X) attempt, cannot be listed on the Central Market. It drops from Dark Rift bosses: Ferrid, Ahib Griffon and Dim Tree Spirit always drop it, while Red Nose, Bheg, Muskan and others drop it by set probability. The drop locations may expand with player activity.

## Kharazad accessories exclusive crystal sockets

Equipping Kharazad accessories opens exclusive crystal sockets: each equipped Kharazad accessory gives one socket for its slot, up to six sockets. Only crystals of the Dawn group fit, and crystals in these sockets do not shatter on death. The Dawn crystals follow the existing JIN, BON and WON grades and can be bought and sold on the Central Market.

## Kharazad accessories crystal crafting and heating

JIN Dawn crystals can be crafted. The recipes: JIN Dawn Crystal - All AP needs Essence of Dawn x30, Black Stone Powder x10 and Magical Shard x300. The four specialized JIN crystals each need Magical Shard x300 plus a specialized Essence of Dawn: Black Spirit's Rage x350, Accuracy x450, Damage Reduction x250 or Evasion x450. BON and WON crystals drop from monsters in Land of the Morning Light zones.

Heating a Dawn crystal in Processing (L) returns Essence of Dawn. A JIN crystal returns the full recipe amount (All AP x30, Black Spirit's Rage x350, Accuracy x450, Damage Reduction x250, Evasion x450 of the matching essence), a BON crystal returns x10, and a WON crystal returns x1. For All AP, the essence is normal Essence of Dawn; for the others it is the matching specialized essence.

## Kharazad accessories cup upgrades

One exclusive cup per slot gives both offensive and defensive effects, so you do not need several Kharazad accessories for the same slot. Each is made by Heating Essence of Dawn x20 with the existing cups listed, and sells on the Central Market for at most 10 billion Silver.

| Cup | Slot | Effect | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cup of Destined Dawn | Necklace | Max HP +300 | Cup of Arid Moonlight |
| Cup of Callous Sun | Ring | Max HP +125, Critical Hit damage +3% | Cup of a Lonely Cloud or Cup of Dwindling Starlight |
| Cup of Burgeoning Dusk | Belt | All AP +3, All Damage Reduction +6 | Cup of Tragic Nightfall or Cup of Lone Tide |
| Cup of Reticent Moonbeams | Earring | All AP +3, Max Stamina +100 | Cup of Earth's Sorrows |

At a blacksmith NPC, Refined Essence of Emotions x1 extracts both the cup and the unreformed accessory. No quest removes and destroys the cup to recover the accessory.
