# Guaranteed PEN (V) Boss Gear

How to get 7 pieces of PEN (V) boss gear through Jetina's quests by trading for upgradeable TET (IV) boss gear and raising it through five reform levels.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: overview

Through Jetina, an NPC found in all cities, you can get up to 7 pieces of PEN (V) boss gear, one per slot per Family: main weapon, sub-weapon, awakening weapon, helmet, armor, gloves and shoes. You hand in converted PEN (V) Tuvala gear or TET (IV) boss gear and receive an upgradeable TET (IV) boss gear piece, raise it through 5 reform levels, then swap it for the PEN (V) piece. The gear is given at max enhancement level, and the upgradeable gear and the PEN (V) pieces cannot be listed on the Central Market. Characters with negative karma cannot equip the TET (IV) gear from these quests. Season characters cannot do these quests. Matching pieces can later be upgraded to Slumbering Origin gear at Caphras level 10.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: obtainable pieces

The PEN (V) boss gear you can end up with depends on the slot; you pick one option per slot.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: starting the quest

You need a character of level 60 or higher who has completed the quests "A Cry for Help", found in the Suggested tab of the Quest window (O) through the Black Spirit, and "Old Moon Guild's Trade Offer". Then open the Suggested tab through the Black Spirit and accept "[PEN (V) Boss Gear] The Best Deal". If it does not show up, check that both earlier quests are done. To go further you also need either PEN (V) Tuvala gear converted for normal characters, or TET (IV) boss gear.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: getting the upgradeable TET (IV) gear

Hand either PEN (V) Tuvala gear (converted) or TET (IV) boss gear to Jetina through the Old Moon's resplendent gear quests. Handing over TET (IV) boss gear gives the upgradeable version of that same piece. Handing over PEN (V) Tuvala gear lets you pick any boss gear available for that slot. For example, a TET (IV) Giath's Helmet gives the upgradeable Giath's Helmet, while a PEN (V) Tuvala Helmet lets you choose the upgradeable Giath's Helmet or Griffon's Helmet. You can exchange only once per gear slot per Family, and a slot cannot be exchanged again for another item. These pieces cannot receive Caphras enhancement or be listed on the Central Market. Weapons are delivered in boxes that can be transferred to whichever character should use them.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: reform stone recipes

The upgradeable TET (IV) gear goes from Reform Lv. I to V using reform stones, made by Heating the listed items in the Processing (L) window. Weapons use Resplendent Boss Weapon Reform Stone; armor uses Resplendent Boss Armor Reform Stone. If you make the wrong stone, Grinding in Processing returns the materials.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | 60 | 10 | 50 | 20 | - | Green Crystal x1 | Rough Emerald x1 |
| II | 60 | 20 | 100 | 30 | - | Blue Crystal x1 | Rough Sapphire x1 |
| III | 60 | 30 | 150 | 50 | - | Red Crystal x1 | Rough Ruby x1 |

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: where reform stone materials come from

- Concentrated Boss Crystal: dropped by any boss, 1 to 15 depending on the boss, from world, field, dark rift and darkened boss summon scroll bosses, and from regular and awakened boss summon scroll bosses. The weekly quest from Jetina gives 155, and it resets every Thursday.
- Concentrated Magical Black Stone (Weapon): Heat Sharp Black Crystal Shard x1 with Black Stone (Weapon) x2, or buy it on the Central Market.
- Memory Fragment: from Ancient Relic Crystal summon scrolls and Pila Fe scrolls, or the Central Market.
- Magical Shard: Heat a yellow grade black magic crystal, or buy it on the Central Market.
- Rough Emerald, Sapphire, Ruby and Diamond: from gathering and exploration nodes.
- Green, Blue, Red and Black Crystal: Heat the matching Rough crystal, or Heat green grade armor (Hercules' Might, Rocaba, Talis, Grunil).
- Contained Boss Aura: Simple Alchemy on boss gear at TRI (III) or lower gives 1 from unenhanced gear, 10 from PRI (I), 15 from DUO (II) and 30 from TRI (III).

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: Oquilla Sky and Earth Crystals

The level V stones need an Oquilla Sky Crystal (weapons) or Oquilla Earth Crystal (defense gear), obtained by a quest from Jetina. The quest needs Oquilla Piece of the Old Moon, which an Old Moon Manager NPC sells for 50,000,000 Silver each. The cost rises each time you accept the quest.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 pieces | 80 pieces |
| 2 | 70 pieces | 120 pieces |
| 3 | 120 pieces | 160 pieces |
| 4 | - | 220 pieces |

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: exchanging for the PEN (V) piece

After upgrading the gear through all five reform levels, accept and complete the Old Moon's Brilliant quest from Jetina to receive the PEN (V) boss gear. This PEN (V) gear can get Caphras enhancement and can be used to craft the Fallen God's Armor, but it cannot be listed on the Central Market.

## Guaranteed PEN (V) boss gear: changing to a different boss gear

If you have not upgraded an exchanged piece, you can swap it once per slot per Family for another boss gear type, for example upgradeable TET (IV) Dim Tree Spirit's Armor for upgradeable TET (IV) Red Nose's Armor. Keep the piece in your inventory, go to Tarif and speak with Yisar Pjetyo to accept the exchange quest. The piece must be gear from this questline, meaning upgradeable TET (IV) boss gear that cannot be listed on the Central Market. Gear upgraded 1 level or more cannot be changed.
