# Naru Gear

The beginner gear set from the main questline: what it includes, how to get it, how to enhance it, and what it can be exchanged for.

## Naru gear overview

Naru gear is a very helpful beginner set, easy to get through the main questline. The same questline also gives the materials to enhance it all the way to PEN (V). Once enhanced to PEN (V), it can be traded for Tuvala gear if you are a season character, or for Narchillan gear once per Family; accessories are not part of these exchanges. Naru gear is character bound, so it cannot be shared within the Family. Players are advised to use Naru accessories without enhancing them, and to switch later to Main Quest Accessories.

## Naru gear pieces

The Naru gear list has three groups. Weapons: Naru Main Weapon, Naru Sub-weapon, and a Naru Awakening Weapon for the Archer class and another for the Scholar class. Defense gear: Naru Helmet, Naru Armor, Naru Gloves and Naru Shoes. Accessories: Naru Necklace, Naru Earring, Naru Ring and Naru Belt.

## Naru gear how to obtain

Naru gear comes from the main questline as quest rewards, or by exchanging Beginner Black Stones. All pieces are given by completing Main Quests (Q) from the Black Spirit. For the exchange, you must have finished the Serendia main questline. Beginner Black Stones come as quest rewards, and you trade them with Fughar, who stands near the city stables, for the Naru pieces you want. Use the Find NPC function if you cannot spot him. If you start in the Land of the Morning Light or Mountain of Eternal Winter, Naru quest gear will not show up automatically. After reaching level 20, make sure all quest filters are enabled, and take the optional quests from the Black Spirit to get the gear.

## Naru gear enhancement

Naru gear can be enhanced up to PEN (V) with Beginner Black Stones, and the main questline gives plenty. Naru gear has a low enhancement difficulty, so the stones from the main questline are enough to reach the top level. Up to PRI (I), a failure does not lower the enhancement level. From DUO (II) on, a failure can lower the level by one. The recommended six pieces to take to PEN (V) are the Naru Main Weapon, Sub-weapon, Helmet, Armor, Gloves and Shoes. Beginner Black Stones can also be made from regular Black Stones, but the wiki advises using only quest-obtained ones, since quests give enough and regular Black Stones can be sold on the Central Market or used elsewhere. After the six pieces reach PEN (V), leftover Beginner Black Stones can be used on accessories or sold to a shop for Silver.

## Naru gear durability recovery

When failed enhancements leave Naru gear without enough maximum durability, recover it using an unenhanced piece of the same Naru item. For example, a Naru Longsword restores the maximum durability of a Naru Longsword. At the blacksmith choose Repair, then Recover Max Durability.

## Naru gear accessories

Naru accessories follow regular accessory rules, so a failed enhancement destroys them. The wiki suggests enhancing them only if you have spare Beginner Black Stones, or not at all, and moving on to other accessories from the main quests later.

## Naru gear exchange for Tuvala and Narchillan gear

PEN (V) Naru gear can be exchanged in two ways. A season character can swap PEN (V) Naru gear (accessories excluded) for PRI (I) Tuvala gear through the exchange menu in the equipment window. Only season characters can get and use Tuvala gear. Any character can exchange PEN (V) Naru gear for Narchillan gear through a quest, once per Family. Narchillan gear cannot be enhanced and has stats similar to something between TET (IV) and PEN (V) Tuvala gear. The Season Gear Guide and Narchillan Gear pages have details.
