# Equipment

An overview of equipment in Black Desert: the equipment window, weapons, defense gear, accessories, artifacts, alchemy stones, the Adventurer's Tome, durability and life skill gear.

## Equipment overview

Nearly all equipment, also called gear, can be used by any class. The exception is weapons, which differ per class. A Lv. 1 Berserker could wear gear that a high-level Sorceress used, because most items can be freely bought and sold. Exceptions are character-bound items such as some quest rewards, and gear rented with contribution points. Equipment matters for your character's survival, stamina and speed in battle.

## Equipment window: hotkey and slots

Open the Equipment window with the hotkey "I" to see what your character wears. The numbered slots are: 1 Armor, 2 and 3 Rings, 4 and 12 Artifacts, 5 Gloves, 6 Necklace, 7 Sub-weapon, 8 Awakening Weapon, 9 Main Weapon, 10 Belt, 11 Shoes, 13 and 14 Earrings, 15 Helmet, 16 Alchemy Stone and 17 Adventurer's Tome. Other slots in the window are for cosmetic accessories (head, ear, eye, nose, mouth, chin), appearance outfits (helmet, armor, gloves, shoes, weapons, underwear) and tools.

## Equipment: weapons

Weapons are the main weapon, the sub-weapon (off-hand) and the Awakening weapon, and the types you can equip depend on your class. Main weapons and sub-weapons can be used from Lv. 1. Some classes can use several types, and for Ninja and Kunoichi the sub-weapon (kunai or shuriken) changes their available skills. Differently named weapons have different effects and stats. The Awakening weapon is the third weapon: it unlocks once the Awakening questline is done, which most classes can start at Lv. 56, while Archer can already use it from Lv. 1 in a limited form. Without the Awakening questline you cannot equip an Awakening weapon even if you own one. If you pick Succession instead of Awakening, you still equip the Awakening weapon and its stats partly count toward your attack power, but you learn Succession skills instead of Awakening skills.

## Equipment: defense gear

Defense gear is four pieces: armor, helmet, shoes and gloves. They raise your total defense, which lowers damage taken and raises evasion chance. Boss gear and other sets such as Blackstar give set effects when several pieces are worn together. For example, three boss pieces give +200 Max Stamina, and if two of them are Blackstar pieces you also get +10 monster damage reduction. Defense gear has no level or class restriction. Some defense gear raises life skill stats rather than helping in combat.

## Equipment: accessories

Accessories are rings, earrings, necklaces and belts. You can wear two rings and two earrings, and one necklace and one belt. Weapons add offense and defense gear adds defense, while accessories are versatile: there are offensive, defensive and life skill accessories.

## Equipment: artifacts

Artifacts are relics infused with the power of the witches of Everfrost. They have bonuses of their own, but are best worn after being infused with Lightstones. Each artifact holds 2 Lightstones, and certain Lightstone combinations give extra set-effect stats. See the Artifacts & Lightstones and Lightstone Combinations pages for details.

## Equipment: alchemy stone slot

An item in the Alchemy Stone slot gives special effects for a limited time when you press the "U" key. Examples are Polished Alchemy Stone of Destruction, Life Spirit Stone, and uniquely named ones such as Vell's Heart and Kutum's Power Stone. Effects can be offensive, defensive, life skill or unique, and usually last 5 to 10 minutes. Most spirit stones and alchemy-crafted Alchemy Stones can be sold on the Central Market, but Kutum's Power Stone cannot be sold and is gone when used up. All spirit stones are one-use. See the Alchemy Stones page.

## Equipment: Adventurer's Tome

The Black Spirit gives you a basic Adventurer's Tome at the start of your adventure, and specific quests give other tomes. The Chenga - Sherekhan Tome of Wisdom adds 30% to the combat experience you get as a quest reward when equipped, which enables the "Chengarun" quick leveling route.

## Equipment: durability

Weapons, armor and accessories (earrings, necklace, belt, ring) have durability. It drops with use, for example when armor takes damage, and worn gear looks more damaged. As long as an item has at least 1 durability its effects remain; at 0 it is the same as not wearing it. Repair at a blacksmith or at an anvil in one of your residences. Repairing a weapon with lowered maximum durability may raise its maximum durability by a small chance. Gear rented from NPCs with Contribution Points has no durability, but cannot be enhanced and is heavier; better gear costs more points, and you get them back when you return the item. Alchemy Stones lose durability on every use and most can be recharged with fuel materials through the Stone button of the Inventory window, but Spirit Stones and Kutum's Power Stone cannot. See the Repair page.

## Equipment: gear with special effects and reforming

Life skill clothes give high efficiency during life skill work. A Professional Gatherer's Uniform lets you gather without being attacked first by nearby monsters while crouching or gathering, and higher enhancement levels of such gear increase the benefits; Silver Embroidered Gatherer's Clothes make you gather faster. The Black Spirit can improve gear through enhancement and item reform. Reforming adds an extra modifier depending on the item and the reform item. A weapon given the Fiery modifier gets unique effects depending on its type. Reforming with the Inverted Heart of Garmoth gives sub-weapons Max HP +150, Max Stamina +100 and Special Attack Evasion Rate +10%, while Awakening weapons get +2 crystal slots and critical hit damage +3%. Sets usually have 3 to 4 parts such as helmet, armor, gloves and shoes, and each set has its own effects. See the Garmoth's Heart page.

## Equipment: life skill tools

Gathering needs tools such as lumbering axes, fluid collectors, hoes, butcher knives, tanning knives and pickaxes. You can gather or fish with the basic tools your character has equipped by default. Near something collectible, an interaction window appears and you can gather at once if you hold the right tool. Better life skill tools must be equipped in a dedicated life skill tools interface. As your Gathering level rises you can wear better tools, which shortens gathering time. See the Gathering page.
