# Ancient Anvil

The Ancient Anvil is a guaranteed-enhancement system fed by Agris Essence from failed enhancement attempts. This page covers how it works and the maximum Agris Essence each gear group can store.

## Ancient Anvil overview

Every time an enhancement attempt fails, Agris Essence fills up in the Ancient Anvil for that item. Once enough has been stored, the anvil activates and the next attempt on that item has a 100% success rate. It is effectively a guaranteed enhancement system. For example, if a TET (IV) Deboreka Necklace keeps failing, it eventually stores enough Agris Essence to be guaranteed. Even if a failure drops it to TRI (III) and you bring it back to TET (IV) later, the Agris Essence you stored is still there. Weapons and defense gear such as Blackstar and Slumbering Origin store Agris Essence by set group, not per piece.

## Ancient Anvil: how Agris Essence is earned and used

Each failed enhancement gives 1 Agris Essence, whatever the gear type or level. If you succeed before the anvil is full, the stored essence is consumed. Agris Essence counts whether you enhance normally, with Cron Stones, or with a hammer item. Once the anvil is active you use the usual enhancement materials, and the attempt is guaranteed. A guaranteed attempt with the anvil does not use up your current enhancement chance (failstacks).

## Ancient Anvil: shared Agris Essence between gear

The maximum Agris Essence depends on the gear type and enhancement level, and some items share what is stored. Weapons and defense gear in the same group share it: for instance, you keep the same stored amount when switching from a Blackstar Longsword to a Blackstar Greatsword at the same enhancement level. Accessories do not share stored Agris Essence with each other, except that reformed accessories share with their normal versions.
