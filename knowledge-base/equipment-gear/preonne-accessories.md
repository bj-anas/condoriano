# Preonne Accessories

Life skill accessories that do not break when enhancement fails: their pieces, how to exchange Manos accessories for them, enhancement rules and rates, and stats.

## Preonne accessories overview

Preonne accessories do not break when enhancement fails. They can be enhanced up to DEC (X) and, like Manos accessories, use Concentrated Magical Black Gems. The DEC (X) Preonne Necklace and Belt give Life Mastery +250. They are obtained by exchanging Manos accessories enhanced to certain levels. They can be listed on the Central Market, and crafted Preonne accessories cost about the same as the Manos accessories used to make them.

## Preonne accessories pieces

There are 4 pieces: Preonne Necklace, Preonne Belt, Preonne Ring and Preonne Earring. Some enhancement levels are tied to Manos accessory market prices through these exchanges: a TRI (III) Manos Accessory gives a +0 Preonne Accessory, a TET (IV) Manos Accessory gives a TRI (III) Preonne Accessory, and a PEN (V) Manos Accessory gives an OCT (VIII) Preonne Accessory.

## Preonne accessories how to obtain

Accept the exchange quests from Liana, who is in each town. First do the preceding quest "[Preonne Accessory] Imbued with a Mystical Blessing".

| Quest | Hand over | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| [Crafting] Preonne Accessory | TRI (III) Manos Accessory | +0 Preonne Accessory |
| [Crafting] TRI (III) Preonne Accessory | TET (IV) Manos Accessory | TRI (III) Preonne Accessory |
| [Crafting] OCT (VIII) Preonne Accessory | PEN (V) Manos Accessory | OCT (VIII) Preonne Accessory |

Once the preceding quest is done once per Family, each exchange quest becomes recurring with no daily limit. If you abandon a quest after handing over the accessory, you cannot get it back. You can view the quests in the Quests (O) window with the quest type All or General, Recurring. The OCT (VIII) quest brands the accessory with the Family name of whoever completed it, replacing any existing Family name.

## Preonne accessories enhancement rules

Preonne accessories use a fixed enhancement chance, and Concentrated Magical Black Gems are the material. A failure lowers the level instead of destroying the item, and Cron Stones prevent that level drop. For comparison, Manos accessories have a fixed 5% chance for TET to PEN, are destroyed on failure, and Cron Stones give them only a 40% chance to avoid the level drop. The Preonne chance for NOV to DEC is 7.5%. Preonne accessories ignore additional enhancement chance stats, and those stats are not used up even on success. The Family name is branded from OCT (VIII). They can be group-enhanced, and Agris Essence is tracked separately per accessory part, as with other accessories.

## Preonne accessories stats

Preonne accessories give Life Mastery and Life EXP. Each piece gives the same Life EXP at a given level. Worn together with Manos accessories, they share a set effect of Life EXP +5% for every 2 pieces equipped. Wearing Preonne accessories also adds a separate Life EXP +5% for every 2 Preonne pieces equipped.
