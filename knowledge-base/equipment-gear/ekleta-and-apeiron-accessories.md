# Ekleta and Apeiron Accessories

Top-tier accessory sets with the same best stats at DEC (X): how they are obtained, enhanced, exchanged and reformed, with enhancement rates and stats by level.

## Ekleta and Apeiron accessories overview

Ekleta and Apeiron are top-tier accessories. Both reach the same best-in-slot stats at DEC (X), but they differ in how you get them, whether they can be traded, and how they are enhanced.

| | Ekleta | Apeiron |
|---|---|---|
| How to obtain | Converted from a NOV (IX) or DEC (X) Kharazad accessory | PvE or boss subjugation |
| Central Market | Cannot be traded (Family-bound) | Tradable |
| Stat growth | High base stats, low growth per level | Low base stats, high growth per level |
| Enhancement material | Causality Shardstone for the same type | Unenhanced Apeiron accessory of the same type |

## Ekleta and Apeiron accessories pieces

Each set has four pieces: Necklace, Ring, Earring and Belt. Belts give Weight Limit +100 LT at every enhancement level. When reformed with Cups, pieces get new names: for Ekleta, Dawnbound Ekleta Necklace, Sunstarved Ekleta Ring, Duskborne Ekleta Belt and Moonhushed Ekleta Earring, and the same four names with Apeiron in place of Ekleta.

## Ekleta accessories how to obtain

Clorince, at Angavu Outpost in Edania, trades Ekleta accessories for Kharazad accessories at NOV (IX) or DEC (X). Before that you must finish the prerequisite questline, which runs from "[Ekleta Accessory] Clorince's Experiment" up to "[Ekleta Accessory] Slumbering Ancient Darkness". Clorince's Experiment starts from the Black Spirit and requires equipping at least one NOV (IX) or DEC (X) Kharazad accessory. Accessories already reformed with Cups can be exchanged straight away, with no need to extract the reform first.

## Ekleta accessories exchange quest rules

If you abandon an exchange quest after handing over an accessory, the item is not returned. View the quest in the Quests (O) window with the quest type set to All or General, Recurring. After the prerequisite quest is finished once per Family, each exchange quest is recurring with no daily acceptance limit. If an Ekleta accessory is made with the [Crafting] Ekleta Accessory quest, it is engraved with the Family name of the character who finished it, whatever name it had before.

## Apeiron accessories how to obtain

Apeiron accessories come from PvE or boss subjugation, and they can be traded on the Central Market. They can also be crafted by Heating in Processing: one of Twilight of the End Necklace, Earring, Ring or Belt x10 (matching the type), Caphras Stone x10 and Magical Shard x10.

## Ekleta and Apeiron accessories reform and extraction

Both sets can be reformed with Cup items. Each slot uses its own Cup, for either set.

| Cup | Slot | Ekleta result | Apeiron result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cup of Destined Dawn | Necklace | Dawnbound Ekleta Necklace | Dawnbound Apeiron Necklace |
| Cup of Callous Sun | Ring | Sunstarved Ekleta Ring | Sunstarved Apeiron Ring |
| Cup of Burgeoning Dusk | Belt | Duskborne Ekleta Belt | Duskborne Apeiron Belt |
| Cup of Reticent Moonbeams | Earring | Moonhushed Ekleta Earring | Moonhushed Apeiron Earring |

To take the Cup and the original accessory back out of a reformed accessory, use a Mirror of Equilibrium from Blacksmith NPCs.
