# Artifacts & Lightstones

How artifacts and lightstones work: artifact types and sources, lightstone purification and amplification, exchanges, infusion, extraction and the artifact inventory.

## Artifacts and Lightstones overview

Artifacts are equipment pieces your character wears, and lightstones are infused into artifacts for extra stats. Basic artifacts drop or are rewarded through quests, subjugation, Dark Rifts, World Bosses and other content. Top-tier artifacts (Kabua's, Dehkia's and Margahan's) and their crafting materials can be traded on the Central Market. Unwanted artifacts can be exchanged for 2 Purified Lightstones each through Dalishain the Wandering Alchemist in each town, and Purified Lightstones can be opened to get lightstones. Lightstones also come from purifying Imperfect Lightstones through Alchemy or Dalishain. The lightstones in your two equipped artifacts can activate strong Lightstone Combination effects.

## Artifacts: slots and lightstone sockets

Artifacts use special slots in the Equipment UI, and each character can wear up to two at once. Every artifact has a unique base effect and two lightstone sockets, so two artifacts hold up to four lightstones. Lightstones give additional stats and set up combination effects. The Lightstone Combinations page lists the combinations.

## Artifacts: highest-tier artifacts overview

The highest-tier artifacts (Kabua's, Dehkia's, Margahan's and Heralding versions) have strong effects for PvE, PvP, survivability and Life Skills. They and their key crafting materials can be bought and sold freely on the Central Market, but Season Characters cannot equip them; only normal characters can.

## Artifacts: Kabua's Artifact for subjugation

Kabua's Artifact is the top-tier artifact for fighting monsters. It drops in monster zones such as Darkseekers' Retreat and the Yzrahid Highlands, or can be bought on the Central Market. Effects: Extra AP Against Monsters +7, Monster Damage Reduction +20, Max HP +100 and Max Stamina +75.

## Artifacts: Dehkia's Artifact for defense and survivability

Dehkia's Artifact is the defensive top-tier artifact. Gather Dehkia's Fragments from the Tier 2 zones of Dehkia's Lantern, for example Ash Forest and Olun's Valley, then combine them through Processing (Manufacture). The finished item is also sold on the Central Market. There are two versions, both with Max HP +250 and Max Stamina +50.

|---|---|---|
| Dehkia's Artifact - All Damage Reduction | All Damage Reduction +12 | Dehkia's Fragment x100, Powder of Earth x50, Magical Lightstone Crystal x1,000 |
| Dehkia's Artifact - All Evasion | All Evasion +24 | Dehkia's Fragment x100, Powder of Rifts x50, Magical Lightstone Crystal x1,000 |

## Artifacts: Kabua's and Dehkia's Heralding Artifacts

A Flawless Herald's Crystal is a Reform Stone that reforms Kabua's or Dehkia's Artifacts to add stats. Craft it by Processing (L) - Heating Herald's Crystal x100 (from loot in Edania), Sharp Black Crystal Shard x100, Magical Shard x100 and Magical Lightstone Crystal x200. Reformed artifacts cannot be traded on the Central Market. A Mirror of Equilibrium from a major city blacksmith extracts the Reform Stone.

|---|---|---|
| Kabua's Artifact | Kabua's Heralding Artifact | Extra AP Against Monsters +3, Monster Damage Reduction +5, Max HP +250 |
| Dehkia's Artifact - All Damage Reduction | Dehkia's Heralding Artifact - All Damage Reduction | All AP +6, Max HP +250 |
| Dehkia's Artifact - All Evasion | Dehkia's Heralding Artifact - All Evasion | All AP +6, Max HP +250 |

## Artifacts: Margahan's Artifact for Life Skills

Margahan's Artifact is the top-tier life skill artifact, boosting Mastery, EXP and more across all Life Skills, and it can be traded on the Central Market. Crafted by Processing (L) - Manufacture with Margahan's Fragment x100, Pure Magnetite Crystal x100 and Pure Marble x100. Margahan's Fragment can be exchanged with Resh at Angavu Outpost or bought on the Central Market. Pure Magnetite Crystal comes from processing Magnetite Ore, and Pure Marble from processing Rough Marble.

## Artifacts: processing steps for Margahan's Artifact materials

Pure Marble takes two steps: Grinding Rough Marble to get Polished Marble, then Heating Polished Marble with Metal Solvent to get Pure Marble. Pure Magnetite Crystal takes three steps: Heating Magnetite Ore to get Melted Magnetite Shard, Heating Melted Magnetite Shard to get Magnetite Ingot, then Heating Magnetite Ingot with Metal Solvent to get Pure Magnetite Crystal.

## Artifacts: exchanging unwanted artifacts for Purified Lightstones

Take unused artifacts to Dalishain the Wandering Alchemist in major cities and towns to get 2 Purified Lightstones for each. When exchanging many at once, artifacts that have lightstones infused can make later artifacts disappear from the exchange list, so put the ones you plan to trade at the start of your Inventory.

## Artifacts: Item Copy and death rules

On character tagging, artifacts are copied under the same Item Copy rules as defense gear, free of charge, and infused lightstones come along. Later removing or replacing lightstones in the original does not affect the copy. Lightstones infused into copied artifacts can only be removed, not extracted. A character with negative Karma who dies can lose worn artifacts and their infused lightstones.

## Lightstones: types and trading

Imperfect Lightstones are raw materials dropped by subjugation or Life Skill activities. They are purified, either by an NPC or by Alchemy at home, into Purified Lightstones, and opening one gives a chance at a finished lightstone. All lightstones and Purified Lightstones can be traded freely on the Central Market. Dalishain in any town exchanges 3 unwanted lightstones for 1 Purified Lightstone.

## Lightstones: crafting Amplified Lightstones

A base Lightstone of Fire, Earth or Wind can be heated with materials into a stronger Amplified Lightstone of the same type. In Processing (L) choose Heating, then heat one base lightstone with Crystallized Energy of Endtimes x30, Magical Shard x50 and Magical Lightstone Crystal x100. Crystallized Energy of Endtimes drops in Edania monster zones and from boss subjugations. Combination effects that need an existing lightstone of the same name still work if an Amplified Lightstone is infused.

## Lightstones: purifying Imperfect Lightstones with Dalishain

Interact with Dalishain the Wandering Alchemist, who is in each town, to instantly turn an Imperfect Lightstone into a Purified Lightstone for 20,000,000 Silver. Right-click an Imperfect Lightstone in your inventory to register and purify several at once. A Purified Lightstone can then be opened for a chance at a complete lightstone. Dalishain also exchanges unwanted artifacts for 2 Purified Lightstones each, and 3 unwanted lightstones for 1 Purified Lightstone.

## Lightstones: Lightstone Combination effects

Equip two artifacts and fill their four sockets with the required lightstones to trigger a powerful Lightstone Combination effect. Pick one for your goal: PvE (extra damage against monsters), PvP (human damage and defense), Life Skill (Mastery) or EXP. The Lightstone Combinations page lists every combination.

## Lightstones: infusion, extraction and removal

To infuse, open the Infusion menu through Menu (ESC) - Character (F2) - Infusion, or right-click a lightstone in your inventory, select an artifact and infuse into an empty slot. To extract safely, use a Lightstone Extraction Tool (10,000,000 Silver from Blacksmiths) in the Extraction menu of a Blacksmith in any major city, which returns the lightstone to your inventory. Lightstones in Item Copy artifacts cannot be extracted, only removed. To remove, right-click an infused lightstone in the Infusion menu. Removed lightstones are destroyed permanently.

## Artifacts: Artifact and Lightstone Inventory

The Artifact/Lightstone Inventory stores artifacts and lightstones to save bag space. Open it with the [Artifact Inventory] button at the bottom of the Inventory (I). Every character in your Family can deposit and withdraw from it. Use the Type filter at the bottom to find lightstones quickly. Artifacts copied through Item Copy cannot be stored there. Artifact Presets make equipping artifacts easy.
