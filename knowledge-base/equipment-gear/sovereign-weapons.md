# Sovereign Weapons

The Primordial grade Sovereign weapons crafted at the Bonghwang Statue in Mount Ahshi: how to craft them, enhance them, their stats and probabilities, Primordial Light, Primordial Artina Sol and reform stones.

## Sovereign weapons overview

Sovereign weapons are Primordial grade weapons made from Blackstar and boss weapons using materials from the Land of the Morning Light. They come as main weapons, Awakening weapons and sub-weapons, and can be enhanced up to DEC (X). They start at the base level and become PRI (I) on the first successful enhancement. They cannot be listed on the Central Market.

## Sovereign weapons crafting methods

There are three ways to craft a Sovereign weapon.
- Method 1: PEN (V) Blackstar weapon plus another PEN (V) Blackstar weapon.
- Method 2: PEN (V) Blackstar weapon plus Flame of the Primordial.
- Method 3: PEN (V) Blackstar weapon plus PEN (V) boss weapon at Caphras enhancement Lv. 20, plus Gem of Twilight.

The wiki recommends Methods 1 and 2 using materials from HYPERBOOST. You need items of the same type. For example, crafting a Sovereign main weapon by Method 3 needs a PEN (V) Blackstar main weapon and a PEN (V) boss main weapon.

## Sovereign weapons crafting materials

Flame of the Primordial comes from Land of the Morning Light bosses (also a weekly quest reward), or by Heating x100 Embers of the Primordial. It is used to craft Sovereign main and Awakening weapons at the Bonghwang Statue of Ahshi, and to craft a Primordial Glow Crystal. Embers of the Primordial drop from those same bosses and weekly quest.

Gem of Twilight is made by Heating (L) x100 Magical Shard and x20,000 Caphras Stone. It is a crafting material for the Sovereign main and Awakening weapons.

## Sovereign weapons crafting location and rules

Craft a Sovereign weapon by interacting with the Bonghwang Statue at Mount Ahshi in Hwanghae Province, Land of the Morning Light, once you have the materials. No prerequisite quest is needed, but the character must be at least level 2. Rules to remember:
- Only unreformed weapons can be used. Reform stones, such as an Inverted Heart of Garmoth, can be extracted with a Mirror of Equilibrium bought from a blacksmith NPC in a major city, and this now works on Caphras-enhanced weapons without removing the Caphras Stones first.
- The inputs must match the output type; a main weapon needs main weapons.
- The crafting character's class decides the weapon. A Warrior using a PEN (V) Blackstar Longbow and PEN (V) Blackstar Amulet crafts a Sovereign Longsword.
- Any branding is lost.
- J's Hammer of Loyalty cannot be used when enhancing a Sovereign weapon.

## Sovereign weapons enhancement materials

Sovereign weapons are enhanced with Primordial Black Stones. Make one by Heating (L) 1 Primordial Crystal, 2 Sharp Black Crystal Shard and 10 Caphras Stone; 1 Black Stone Powder lets you heat batches of 10 into 10 Primordial Black Stones. Primordial Black Stone starts at 150,000,000 Silver on the market. Primordial Crystal starts at 100,000,000 Silver and comes from Land of the Morning Light bosses (also a weekly quest reward) or Patrigio's Secret Shop. Both can be bought and sold on the Central Market. A failed enhancement lowers maximum durability by 20.

## Sovereign weapons Agris Essence

Sovereign weapons use Agris Essence from the Ancient Anvil. Your current Agris Essence rises by 1 on each failed enhancement, and the next attempt is guaranteed once it hits the cap. Caps by level: +0: 3; PRI (I): 5; DUO (II): 10; TRI (III): 20; TET (IV): 30; PEN (V): 35; HEX (VI): 50; SEP (VII): 75; OCT (VIII): 165; NOV (IX): 330. DEC (X) has no cap because it is the final level.

## Sovereign weapons Primordial Light

A Primordial grade Sovereign weapon crafted with a Flame of the Primordial emits Primordial Light, a visual effect shown when you switch to a battle stance. It also shows over a weapon outfit. To hide it, click the eye icon on that weapon's slot in the equipment window. The effect grows stronger with enhancement level: Lv. 0 (none) from +0 to TET (IV), Lv. 1 from PEN (V) to SEP (VII), and Lv. 2 from OCT (VIII) to DEC (X).

## Sovereign weapons Primordial Artina Sol

A Primordial Glow Crystal can reform a Sunset Artina Sol into a Primordial Artina Sol, with Storm Devoured Sunset and Fiery Sunset becoming Storm Devoured Primordial and Fiery Primordial. It gives an extra +10 monster damage and can take further reform stones. Make the crystal by Heating (L) x1 Flame of the Primordial, x20 Magical Shard and x50 Sharp Black Crystal Shard. The Primordial Artina Sol starts at Primordial grade, keeps the old enhancement level, and can be enhanced up to PEN (V). Weapon transfer coupons and similar items cannot be used on it. The crystal cannot be listed on the Central Market or extracted from the weapon.

## Sovereign weapons reform stones
