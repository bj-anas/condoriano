# Caphras Enhancement

How Caphras enhancement works and how many Caphras Stones each Caphras level costs for each type of gear.

## Caphras enhancement overview

Caphras enhancement uses Caphras Stones to raise the stats of weapons and defense gear that are at TRI (III) or higher, mostly boss grade gear such as the Kzarka Main Weapon or Dim Tree Spirit's Armor. Summon the Black Spirit (hotkey ","), pick Enhancement, and use Caphras Stone as the material. Infusing stones gives the piece EXP, and enough EXP raises its Caphras level. A weapon can gain AP and accuracy, while defense gear can gain DP, damage reduction, evasion, HP and more. There are 20 Caphras levels in total.

## Caphras enhancement: recommended levels

The wiki recommends Caphras enhancement only for boss grade gear that is already at PEN (V), and only up to Caphras level 10. That is enough to trade the gear for a better set, Slumbering Origin gear. For example, a PEN (V) Dim Tree Spirit's Armor with 10 Caphras levels can be exchanged for Fallen God's Armor. If you have few Caphras Stones and are working on TRI (III) to TET (IV) pieces, it is more efficient to stop at Caphras level 4.

## Caphras enhancement: Caphras level 20 and force-enhancing

When a piece reaches Caphras level 20, it can be force-enhanced to the next normal enhancement level, for example TRI (III) to TET (IV) or TET (IV) to PEN (V). The piece needs 100 maximum durability for this, and afterwards its maximum durability falls to 0. All the Caphras Stones infused in it are consumed.

## Caphras enhancement: stones kept when normal enhancement fails or succeeds

If you do a normal enhancement on gear that already has Caphras Stones, only part of the stones is preserved. When the attempt fails and the enhancement level drops, 50% of the infused Caphras Stones are kept. When it succeeds, 10% are kept, for example going from TRI (III) to TET (IV). To lose as few as possible, extract the Caphras Stones first, which preserves 95% of them.

## Caphras enhancement: extracting Caphras Stones

Caphras Stones can be extracted from enhanced gear. Extraction returns 95% of the consumed Caphras Stones and costs 100,000 silver for each stone. The gear is not destroyed and keeps its normal enhancement level, but it loses all the bonuses gained from Caphras enhancement. The Extraction page explains how to do it.
