# Edana's Defense Gear

Edana's Defense Gear: the top armor set, how to craft it, its stats, enhancement and effect dyes.
The NA/EU wiki page was published in Korean. Names that also appear on the English Slumbering Origin
page use their official English names; the rest are translations, with the Korean name in parentheses.

## Edana's Defense Gear overview

Edana's Defense Gear (에다나의 방어구) is a four-piece armor set of the Genesis grade (창세 등급), above
Slumbering Origin (태고) gear. The pieces are a helmet, Judgment of the Sky (창공의 심판), an armor,
Shadow of the Abyss (심연의 그림자), gloves, Touch of the Oath (맹세의 손길), and shoes, Storm of Fury
(격노의 폭풍). It has higher base stats than Slumbering Origin gear and its own visual effects that change
as the enhancement level rises. The set and piece names are unofficial translations.

## Edana's Defense Gear: prerequisite quest

Before crafting Edana's Defense Gear, complete the quest chain Hidden Secret ([에다나의 방어구] 숨겨진
비밀), which a Family can do once. It comes from the Black Spirit and needs a character at level 60 or
higher who has finished the quest Return ([아에테리온] 귀환). Afterwards, the crafting quests for each
piece are taken from the NPC Fragment of Edana (에다나의 파편) in the Litoban Mountains (리토반 산맥).

## Edana's Defense Gear: crafting each piece at +0

Each piece of Edana's Defense Gear is crafted at +0 from one Slumbering Origin piece at Wailing, TET (IV)
(울부짖는), plus 1 Edana's Piece (에다나의 조각). The Slumbering Origin piece must be unequipped first.

| Edana piece | Slumbering Origin piece used | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Helmet, Judgment of the Sky | Wailing (IV) Labreska's Helmet | 1 Edana's Piece |
| Armor, Shadow of the Abyss | Wailing (IV) Fallen God's Armor | 1 Edana's Piece |
| Gloves, Touch of the Oath | Wailing (IV) Dahn's Gloves, either the evasion or the damage reduction type | 1 Edana's Piece |
| Shoes, Storm of Fury | Wailing (IV) Ator's Shoes, either the evasion or the damage reduction type | 1 Edana's Piece |

## Edana's Defense Gear: crafting at IX (NOV) from Obliterating Slumbering Origin gear

If you own Slumbering Origin gear at its highest level, Obliterating, PEN (V) (멸겁하는), you can craft
Edana's Defense Gear directly at IX (NOV), one level below the maximum. Each piece has its own crafting
quest from Fragment of Edana, named "[Craft] IX Edana" plus the piece name. Requirements: the Hidden
Secret quest chain completed, at least 1 Edana's Piece, and the Obliterating Slumbering Origin piece owned
and unequipped.

## Edana's Defense Gear enhancement material: Edana's Black Stone

Every enhancement attempt on Edana's Defense Gear uses 1 Edana's Black Stone (에다나의 블랙스톤). It
is made with Processing (L), Heating, from 1 Edana's Piece, 3 Sharp Black Crystal Shards (뾰족한
흑결정 조각) and 20 Caphras Stones (카프라스의 돌).

## Edana's Defense Gear visual effects by level

Edana's Defense Gear has a dark look with its own effects. Stronger effects are added when the gear
reaches V (PEN) and again at VIII (OCT), so there are three looks: +0 to IV, V to VII, and VIII to X.

## Edana's Defense Gear outfit and title

Wearing all four pieces of Edana's Defense Gear unlocks the quest Hero Who Slew the Gods ([에다나의 방어구]
멸신의 영웅) from Fragment of Edana in the Litoban Mountains, once per Family. It rewards an outfit set
with the same look as the gear and a permanent yellow title of the same name (멸신의 영웅).

## Edana's Defense Gear: effect dyes (Genesis Pigments)

The effect color of Edana's Defense Gear can be changed with a Genesis Pigment (창세의 안료) in the
Dye (J) menu, on the Effect tab: register the pigment on a piece, one pigment per piece. The pigment
can be extracted back at any time with the Extract Pigment option, for free and with no limit; to
change color, extract the old pigment and register a new one. Effects only appear from V (PEN), so a
dyed piece at IV or lower shows its color once it reaches V. Effect dye changes only the effect; the
gear's own colors use normal dyes. Item transfer carries the effect dye over, but transferred copies
cannot be effect-dyed themselves.

## Edana's Defense Gear: making Genesis Pigments

Genesis Pigments for Edana's Defense Gear come in four colors: purple, teal, sunset and white. Each
is made with Simple Alchemy (L) from 100 Genesis Radiance - Edana (창세의 광휘 - 에다나) of the same
color, 200 Caphras Stones and 200 Magic Shards (마력의 파편). Genesis Radiance drops in top hunting
grounds and is traded on the Central Market. NPC shop sell price: Genesis Radiance - Edana sells for
50,000,000 silver and a Genesis Pigment - Edana for 5,000,000,000 silver.
