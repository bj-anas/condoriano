# Garmoth's Heart

A rare drop that can be fused into certain weapons for extra effects: what it does, how to get it, how to turn it into the Inverted Heart of Garmoth, and how to apply or remove it.

## Garmoth's Heart: what it does

Garmoth's Heart is a rare item you can infuse into some main weapons, off-hands and Awakening weapons for added effects. To get the full benefit you need three of them, one for each weapon slot.

| Weapon slot | Weapons | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| Awakening Weapon | Dragon Slayer, Dandelion, Blackstar, Artina Sol, Sovereign | Crystal Slot +2, Critical Hit Damage +3% |
| Main Weapon | Sovereign | All AP +3 (Displayed AP) |
| Off-hand Weapon | Kutum, Nouver, Blackstar, Sovereign | Max HP +900, Max Stamina +100 |

## Garmoth's Heart: drop from World Boss Garmoth

The World Boss Garmoth can drop Garmoth's Heart at a small chance. She appears several times a week, but you can receive her loot only up to three times per week. NA spawns: every day at 12:00 PST/PDT, Monday and Friday at 21:15 PST/PDT, and Sunday at 18:00 UTC and 21:15 PST/PDT. EU spawns: every day at 13:00 UTC, Monday and Friday at 22:15 UTC, and Sunday at 18:00 UTC and 22:15 UTC.

To claim the loot: when you enter Garmoth's Nest, a special quest appears beside your minimap. It completes when you defeat Garmoth and take the special loot by clicking the chest icon in the middle of the screen. The quest rewards a Garmoth Reward Bundle. Opening it has only a very low chance of giving Garmoth's Heart or Garmoth's Bloodstone, so nothing is guaranteed.

## Garmoth's Heart: Garmoth's Bloodstone exchange

Garmoth's Bloodstone is a low-chance item from opening the Garmoth Reward Bundle. Once you collect 10 Garmoth's Bloodstones, you can trade them for one guaranteed Garmoth's Heart. Accept the quest "Happy Crimson Accident" from Kukao at Garmoth's Nest to do the exchange.

## Garmoth's Heart: guaranteed questline start

A questline also gives a guaranteed Garmoth's Heart, but it is hard and needs many rare items. You must first complete the O'dyllita main storyline. Start by talking to the NPC Black Eyeroot in the Crypt of Resting Thoughts, for the quest "Orzeca's Records: Time of Dragons". After that first quest, the chapters can be done in any order.

## Garmoth's Heart: questline chapters 1 to 7

Most chapters ask you to hand items to Black Eyeroot.
- Chapter 1, Star's End's Artifact: Fallen God's Ring, from the Star's End story quests. The first is "[Star's End] A Small Gift of Appreciation", given by the Black Spirit.
- Chapter 2, Nouver's Reverse Scales: Nouver's Reverse Scale x400, from the World Boss Nouver. Six more come from weekly quests started with Blanca Tris.
- Chapter 3, Pure Hunger: Latent Boss Aura x35, from World Bosses, Field Bosses and Dark Rift Bosses.
- Chapter 4, Immortal Power: Turo Heart x50, from monsters in Tunkuta or the Central Market.
- Chapter 5, Perfect Darkness: obtain Corrupt Turasil Darkness x1,500 (the quest names Thornwood Forest Ahib), dropped by monsters in Thornwood Forest.
- Chapter 6, Breath of Nature: 30 each of Earth, Water, Wind and Tree Spirit Stone Fragments, from Kamasylvia monster zones.
- Chapter 7, Endless Magic: Hesed Crystal x10, crafted in Duvencrune workshops from Refined Black Energy Residue, or bought on the Central Market.

## Garmoth's Heart: questline chapters 8 to 14

- Chapter 8, Latent Aura: Concentrated Boss Aura x5, made by Heating any world boss weapon or armor.
- Chapter 9, Honest Enhancement: Concentrated Magical Black Gem x100, made by Simple Alchemy with Sharp Black Crystal Shard x2 and Black Gem x5.
- Chapter 10, Hour of Purification: Magical Shard x50, made by Heating Sealed Black Magic Crystal or yellow grade Black Magic Crystals.
- Chapter 11, Barbarian's Cauldron: Alchemy Stone Shard x1,500, made by Grinding Alchemy Stones.
- Chapter 12, Crystal of Balance: Cantarnia Crystal x9, bought from Francia in O'draxxia at 500,000,000 Silver each.
- Chapter 13, Alliance and Cooperation: meet Blanca Tris, the Narcion Shadow Merchant at Narcion node.
- Chapter 14, Faith and Proof: reach level 62 and talk to Black Eyeroot.

## Garmoth's Heart: making the Inverted Heart of Garmoth

To use Garmoth's Heart on weapons, first turn it into an Inverted Heart of Garmoth. Accept the quest "[Weekly] Inverting the Energy" from the blacksmith Hughol in Duvencrune. It needs Hesed's Crystal x5 and Concentrated Magical Black Stone x3. Hesed's Crystal is crafted in Duvencrune workshops: 25 Black Energy Residue make 1 Refined Black Energy Residue, and 11 Refined make 1 Hesed's Crystal, so 1,375 Black Energy Residue are needed in total. It can also be bought on the Central Market. Concentrated Magical Black Stone comes from Simple Alchemy with Sharp Black Crystal Shard x1 and Black Stone x2, or from the Central Market. The quest gives Inverted Energy. Open Processing (L) and use Heating to combine it with your Garmoth's Heart.

## Garmoth's Heart: reforming and removing the Inverted Heart

To apply the Inverted Heart of Garmoth, summon the Black Spirit with the "," hotkey, choose the Upgrade/Reforge option, select the weapon and the Inverted Heart, and click Reform.

To move it to another weapon, buy a Mirror of Equilibrium for 20,000,000 Silver from a blacksmith in any town or city. Talk to the blacksmith again, choose Extract, then Extract Reform Stone, select the weapon carrying the heart and click Extract. You get the weapon and the Inverted Heart of Garmoth back in your inventory.

## Garmoth's Heart: Heidel Ball 2025 free Inverted Heart

During the Heidel Ball 2025 event, a free Inverted Heart of Garmoth could be claimed in the Challenge window (Y) until November 27, 2025, before maintenance. The requirements were 200 hours of Family playtime and the [Knowledge] Wuju. Finishing the "[The Magnus] Wuju's Gift...?" quest on any Family character already gives that knowledge, which can be checked in Knowledge (H) with Find Knowledge.
