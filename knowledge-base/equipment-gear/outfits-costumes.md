# Outfits & Costumes

How outfits and costumes work in Black Desert: appearance slots, the difference between outfits and costumes, costume exchange, and the Black Spirit's Claw socket system for Ancient Spirit's Crystals.

## Outfits and costumes: appearance slots

Outfits and costumes change how your character looks, and some give extra benefits. In the equipment window (hotkey "I"), the outer circle holds equipment slots and the inner circle holds appearance slots, and they are separate. If an outfit or costume and a normal equipment piece share the same body slot, only the outfit or costume is shown by default.

## Outfits and costumes: outfits versus costumes

Both go in appearance slots and are mostly decorative, with some special effects. An outfit is usually obtained from the Pearl Shop, events or gifts. Every outfit has individual effects based on its body slot plus a set effect that grows with the number of pieces worn, and you do not need all pieces from the same set. Outfits can be extracted at a blacksmith for either Valks' Cry or Cron Stones. A Black Spirit's Claw used in enhancement on outfit armor adds a Magic Crystal socket to the piece. Special Outfits obtained outside the Pearl Shop are covered on their own wiki page.

## Outfits and costumes: functional and non-functional costumes

There are two kinds of costume. Functional costumes come from the Pearl Shop and give useful effects: the Splat Fisher's Clothes set swims faster and gives a small fishing bonus, the Da-dum da-dum Diving Suit set lets you dive longer, and the Canape and Pavilla Costume Sets speed up Cooking and Alchemy. Non-functional costumes are crafted by you through costume exchange. They carry fewer effects, are not extractable into Valks' Cry or Cron Stones, and once crafted they cannot be sold. They can be heated to get special ancient spirit crystals that fit the crystal socket of an outfit armor piece.

## Outfits and costumes: costume exchange with an Equipment Tailoring Coupon

Crafted equipment normally goes into normal equipment slots, but some pieces can become costumes. Check an item's tooltip to see whether it can. To convert armor you need an Equipment Tailoring Coupon. Coupons are sold in the Pearl Shop, on the Central Market, or given at events. Right-click one in your pearl inventory and choose the armor to convert. Only a limited list of equipment can be turned into costumes, and the list is in the coupon's tooltip. The costume keeps the original item's effects and gains a new one, also listed in the tooltip.

## Outfits and costumes: making a Black Spirit's Claw

To craft a Black Spirit's Claw you need three Black Spirit's Claw pieces. The pieces drop from monsters in zones including Wandering Rogue Den (Mediah), Gahaz Bandits' Lair, Roud Sulfur Mine and Pila Ku Jail (Valencia), and Padix Island, an island at sea near Calpheon. You can also buy them on the Central Market if listed. Open "Yaz's Combinables Pouch" in your inventory and arrange the three pieces in a horizontal line. A "+" appears on the first item; click it and the three pieces become one Black Spirit's Claw.

## Outfits and costumes: creating a Magic Crystal socket

Enhance the armor worn in the armor appearance slot, using a Black Spirit's Claw, to create a Magic Crystal socket. Wearing an outfit with this socket unlocks the middle slot of the Transfusion UI, giving you more Magic Crystal effects and more flexibility in your normal crystal slots. Summon the Black Spirit (hotkey ",") and select Enhancement. This enhancement has a 100% success rate, and even on success it does not consume your accumulated enhancement chance. The new slot appears in the center of your Magic Crystal Preset.

## Outfits and costumes: crafting an Ancient Spirit's Crystal

Only an Ancient Spirit's Crystal can go in the outfit socket. First get a non-functional costume such as a Calpheon Noble Suit or Dress, either bought from the Central Market as normal armor in the Crafted Clothes section or made with workers at a town costume mill. To make it yourself you need a costume mill of level 2 or higher and a design blueprint, which you can buy from the Calpheon tailor Merv once your Amity with her is 400 or higher. Then use an equipment tailoring coupon to convert the armor into a costume. The normal armor disappears and the costume appears in your Pearl Inventory. Heating the costume through processing (hotkey "L") gives an Ancient Spirit's Crystal - Swiftness, which adds +2 movement speed when infused. Other costumes give other crystals: a Shroud Knight Armor costume turns into an Ancient Spirit's Crystal - Viper, which gives +1 Attack Speed and Casting Speed.

## Outfits and costumes: infusing the crystal into the outfit socket

Open your inventory (hotkey "I"), click Crystals at the bottom, then right-click the Ancient Spirit's Crystal to add it to your crystal inventory. Equip the outfit that has the socket, which unlocks its crystal slot. Summon the Black Spirit (hotkey ","), choose Transfusion, press the empty middle slot and pick the Ancient Spirit's Crystal from the list. If you unequip the outfit piece with the socket, the slot is grayed out and you lose its effect until you wear a socketed piece again. The Magic Crystals page has more on this.
