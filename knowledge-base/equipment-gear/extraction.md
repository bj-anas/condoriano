# Extraction

How to extract reusable materials from gear: black stones, Valks' Cry and Cron Stones from outfits, Caphras Stones, Reform Stones, cup effects and lightstones.

## Extraction overview

Black stones, Magic Crystals, Caphras Stones, Reform Stones and Lightstones can all be reused after they are extracted. Cron Stones and Valks' Cry can also be extracted from Pearl outfits. Talk to Blacksmith NPCs in cities to see the extraction options available.

## Extraction: black stones from enhanced gear

Use the Extract Black Stone option in the extraction menu to get back black stones spent on enhancement. All stones used within the safe enhancement range are guaranteed to come back. Beyond the safe range you only get a certain number back. Since failed attempts above the safe range burn stones, extracting from highly enhanced gear is inefficient. The best approach is to enhance only to the highest safe level and extract when you want to switch the gear, so no black stones are lost. The item the stones come from is destroyed in the process. The Enhancement page covers this feature further.

## Extraction: Valks' Cry and Cron Stones from outfits

Pearl outfits from the Pearl Shop, login rewards and events can be extracted at a blacksmith or armor vendor to get Valks' Cry or Cron Stones. The outfit's description shows how many you will get. Valks' Cry gives +1 to enhancement success and stacks separately on top of your base chance. Cron Stones stop gear at DUO (II) or higher from losing an enhancement level on a failed attempt, and the number needed depends on the item. Functional costumes such as Desert or Treant camouflage and the Venecil Dress cannot be extracted, although camouflage costumes bought before Aug 25, 2021 can. Valks' Cry can only be used by adventurers at Lv. 30 or higher, or who completed the "[Awakening] Traces of Edana" quest. It can be used up to +10 at first, and up to +13 once all the Herald's Journals in the Adventure Log are completed.

## Extraction: Caphras Stones

Caphras Stones can be extracted from gear that was improved with Caphras enhancement. Only 95% of the Caphras Stones infused in the gear are returned, and each extracted stone costs 100,000 Silver. The gear is not destroyed and keeps its normal enhancement level, but it loses every bonus gained from Caphras enhancement. See the Caphras Enhancement page for details.

## Extraction: Reform Stones

Gear that was upgraded with a Reform Stone returns to its original form once that stone is extracted, and the extraction is also needed before you can sell the gear. Extraction consumes a material that depends on the equipment type. At a blacksmith or armor vendor choose the Extract Reform Stone option, register the equipment and the material, then press extract. You get the Reform Stone or materials back along with the equipment in its pre-upgrade state. Restoration Stones and Combatant's Restoration Stones are sold by any city blacksmith. Mirror of Equilibrium is sold by the Duvencrune Blacksmith and Hughol. Refined Essence of Emotions is made through Processing (L) - Heating.

| Equipment type | Extraction material |
|---|---|
| Ultimate weapon and defense gear | Restoration Stone |
| Tuvala gear | Combatant's Restoration Stone |
| Fiery gear, Ear Ornament, Storm Devoured gear, Earthshaking gear, Obsidian Blackstar gear, Blessed or Exalted alchemy stones, [Hunting] Gauche Master Matchlock, [Hunting] Gauche Marni Sniper Rifle | Mirror of Equilibrium |
| Accessory | Refined Essence of Emotions |

Using Mirror of Equilibrium on Blessed or Exalted alchemy stones returns the pre-upgrade stone plus Crystallized Despair x1, rather than Blessed or Exalted Soul Fragments.

## Extraction: crafting Refined Essence of Emotions for accessory cups

Refined Essence of Emotions extracts cups from upgraded accessories at a blacksmith. It is crafted by Heating Forest Fury x10, Magical Shard x200 and Fused Crystal of Emotions x20. Fused Crystal of Emotions can be bought from Wacky Toshi for 50 million Silver each. The wiki also lists related materials: Shard of the Furious Night, Shard of the Tearful Night and Shard of the Drained Night, Fragment of All Creation (from Simple Alchemy (L) on Yellow Grade accessories), and Legacy of the Ancient (from Heating (L) on Combined Magic Crystal - Gervish, Macalod or Hoom).

## Extraction: removing cup effects from accessories

Besides extraction, cups can be removed with the [Purified Cup] quest. Lejenti, in the basement of Glish, takes the upgraded accessory and returns one pre-upgrade accessory at no extra cost. The cup is destroyed and removal is permanent. A branded or copied accessory also loses its brand or copy.

## Extraction: lightstones from artifacts

Lightstones infused in artifacts can be extracted with a Lightstone Extraction Tool, which any town blacksmith sells for 10 million Silver each. With the tool, select the artifact and an extraction material, then press the extract button for the lightstone you want to remove. See the Artifacts & Lightstones page for more.
