# Maehwa Lore

A short factual summary of the Maehwa class story from the official wiki: an orphan from the Far East who rises through the Hwasun Valley and seeks the blue flame called the Hawol.

## Maehwa lore: origin

The Maehwa began as an orphan of war and a skilled pickpocket on the streets of a Far Eastern capital. After she beat three guards with a broom, a high-ranking military officer offered to adopt her and train her. She expected to take the state exam, but the law allowed only those born noble to sit it. She joined the Hwasun Valley instead, graduated after a year, and was given the title Maehwa, which belongs to the leader of the order's female members.

## Maehwa lore: key characters and places

Key figures are her adoptive father, the officer, and Bihwasun, who founded the Hwasun Valley and is Lord of the Valley. An old woman tells the Maehwa about the Hawol, a cold blue flame that only the order's generals can learn. She is the first Maehwa chosen as general of the Hwasun Valley, but war leaves her numb. The Chungwol group is the male-only command center from which most generals came. She finds a spear and a book in a cave only the general may enter.

## Maehwa lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The old woman turns out to be Bihwasun, and she defeats the general, teaching her lessons on improvement by comparing it to water in a vessel. The general retires from her post and leaves on a journey of self-discovery carrying only those lessons. Bihwasun tells her that once the vessel breaks, the Hawol will answer her call.
