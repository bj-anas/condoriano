# Ninja Lore

A short factual summary of the Ninja class story from the official wiki: the Narusawa clan's fall, the rise of the ninja clan and the red curse.

## Ninja lore: origin

The Ninja story starts with a poor boy in a small fiefdom of a far eastern kingdom, befriended by the eldest daughter of the ruling Narusawa clan. He swore to protect her and trained under a retired general known as Sura, who wielded six katanas. Later, in a civil war, he defended her and was called Sura reborn. When other lords plotted to frame her for treason and wipe out the Narusawa clan, they retreated to his childhood forest home. The two of them dropped the Narusawa name and began working as spies under the name Ninjas.

## Ninja lore: key characters and places

The leader is the former boy, who kept only his shortest sword. His childhood friend, now the clan head, sorts and sells the information the clan gathers. Their intelligence work earned contracts overseas in the west, gathering intelligence on Calpheon, Valencia and Mediah. A ninja came back from the west with what looked like a cold, then turned violent with red eyes and was killed by the leader. The illness spread through the clan as the "red curse".

## Ninja lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

When his friend caught the red curse, the leader could not kill her. Doctors could only put her in a coma to delay the change. He vowed to search the world for a cure and to take up the swords of Sura again, as the legendary Sura, so the people of the west would help him.
