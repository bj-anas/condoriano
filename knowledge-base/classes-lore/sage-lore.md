# Sage Lore

A short factual summary of the Sage class story from the official wiki, titled "The Last Ancient": the scholar Rux Maha Dehkia, a foretold comet and his return.

## Sage lore: origin

The Sage's story begins with Rux Maha Dehkia, known as the "Oddball of the Ivory Tower" because of his unusual theories. He saw a mysterious red star appear in the constellation of the Sealing Stone and warned the people that invaders foretold in the Revelations of Margahan were coming on a comet. He used his own astronomical telescope, Atorarkiaka. The prosperous people ignored him and he was imprisoned for dissent. He vanished from the prison he had designed, leaving a letter promising to save everyone when the comet came. The comet fell after exactly 49 years, but he did not come.

## Sage lore: key characters and places

The central figure is Rux Maha Dehkia. Key places are the Ivory Tower, the kingdom beside the Agris River and the kingdom of Edana. The temples collapsed, the Ivory Tower burned, and the earth split when the comet fell. In the story's second part he speaks as someone who lost his memories and recovers them in scattered pieces. He recalls a blood-eyed being in his mind and a youthful body that did not age.

## Sage lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Sage story links to the ancient calamity of the comet and its blood-eyed invaders. It also links to the Kabuanian and Elionian calendar years, with a final scene set in a place of eternal winter in Elionian Year 286. He states that only he can decide his fate, and he gives his name as Rux Maha Dehkia.
