# Drakania Lore

A short factual summary of the Drakania class story from the official wiki, titled "Destruction Takes Flight": a young dragon, a dying witch and a vow of destruction.

## Drakania lore: origin

The Drakania story is narrated by a young dragon walking a lifeless cliffside path, gathering the bones of fallen kin. A somber voice tells the narrator to put out the Ynix flame, because only then will the lost dragon paradise return. Dragons have long been pinned down by a tyrant who rules the skies, so unfurling wings means death. At the end of the path a barrier breaks, and the dragons are called forward by a dying witch.

## Drakania lore: key characters and places

The key figure is Erethea, who calls herself the seventh witch of Eternal Winter. She offers her own heart, sweetened with the hatred of fallen dragons, and the starving dragons devour it. The narrator ends up holding the still-beating heart. Erethea dies with a grin, saying her vengeance will live on. The narrator is then told to proclaim to the incarnation of Labreska that a new era has begun. The narrator hears the whispers of a black-winged being missing one eye.

## Drakania lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Drakania's story ties into the Ynix flame, which can consume even the gods, and into the golden mountain, whose ruin the witch's power of erasure is meant to bring. After consuming the heart, the dragons' wings are no longer bound. The narrator stands at the cliff's edge with wings spread, ready to begin a flight of destruction toward the lost paradise of the dragons.
