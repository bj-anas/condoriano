# Deadeye Lore

A short factual summary of the Deadeye class story from the official wiki: an outlaw gang leader pulled into the world of Black Desert while searching for her brother.

## Deadeye lore: origin

Deadeye is the leader of the Canary Gang, the most notorious gang in New Victoria. As a child she lost her parents to a gang attack and was separated from her younger brother Eddie. In 1899, needing a large sum of money, she accepted a request from a man named Marni to raid a train. Besides the bonds, the train held a glowing sphere. When she touched it, she was sent into another world. Marni there claims to know where Eddie is, and she follows him while writing a journal.

## Deadeye lore: key characters and places

The key characters are Deadeye, her brother Eddie, and Marni. Marni says he is not the same Marni as in her own world. He gives her special weapons, including a rocket launcher she calls "Lil' Devil", and a revolver engraved Canary that belonged to her father. Illezra is a white-haired witch whom Marni tells her to seek. Her journal travels through Valencia, Ancado, Calpheon, Tarif and Altinova in Mediah. She takes the name Deadeye with the people she meets.

## Deadeye lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The glowing sphere that sent Deadeye to this world is Ator's Heart. Marni wants it, and it may be the way back to her own world. Illezra says Ator's Cradle lies in the Black Desert in Valencia, where a great power slumbers. Deadeye agrees to go there with Illezra. In Tarif she also hears a tale of Gannicus, the hero from Eddie's favorite book. Adventurers, who travel the world on their own terms, appeal to her.
