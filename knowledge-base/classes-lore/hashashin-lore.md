# Hashashin Lore

A short factual summary of the Hashashin class story from the official wiki, titled "Blade of Aal": a warrior's doubts about the Blackstar and his awakening.

## Hashashin lore: origin

The Hashashin are a faith-bound order who guard against the darkness and its monsters, living by the mantra that the Blackstar will deliver them to paradise. Three years after the Blackstar fell in the far west, paradise had still not come. The elders blamed Kayal Nesser, a bastard of the royal family of Valencia and heir to lead the order as Qabal, "The One at the End", who had run away. The story's Hashashin began to doubt why people of the war call the Blackstar the Redstar. He left his life and stowed away on a boat.

## Hashashin lore: key characters and places

The main character is a nameless Hashashin who carries a haladie. Kayal Nesser is the last apostle, who spoke of the Blackstar's fall. Barhan is the first apostle, who wrote Aal's words as scripture. In the Awakening, a false herald appears and says Aal sent him. The herald offers the throne of Valencia for worship, and the ground shifts as thousands of sand-colored snakes reveal a grand city. The Hashashin recognizes him as a servant of Hadum and refuses.

## Hashashin lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Hashashin story is rooted in Valencia and the desert between Valencia and Mediah. It follows the order's devotion to Aal, the Blackstar and the monsters crawling out of dark pits. Kayal had predicted that the Blackstar's fall would open paradise to the north, but it fell to the west and paradise did not open. The story ends with the Hashashin declaring himself the Blade of Aal, loyal only to Aal.
