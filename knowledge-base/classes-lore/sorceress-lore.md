# Sorceress Lore

A short factual summary of the Sorceress class story from the official wiki: the lineage of black magic, Cartian and her scythe.

## Sorceress lore: origin

The Sorceress story traces black magic back to Cartian, called the Great Sorceress and thought to be the first sorceress. All present-day sorcerers are believed to descend from her. During a territorial war between men and Cyclopes, Cartian is said to have driven all the Cyclopes out alone with her scythe. After that the sorcerers split into groups, and some stayed in Hexe Sanctuary. Cartian moved to Mediah and founded Tarif.

## Sorceress lore: key characters and places

The key people are Cartian and Allan Serbin, the Chief of Tarif and her close friend. The key places are Tarif in Mediah, Hexe Sanctuary, and the magical tree Nantusa Letusa. Tarif holds the Book of Cartian, a guide for sorcerers that has been passed down there. The scythe was a weapon only Cartian used. Before she disappeared, she sealed all her destructive power into the scythe, which nobody else could wield.

## Sorceress lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Allan Serbin sealed the scythe deep under the Nantusa Letusa tree. He lost his body and survived by moving his soul into a framed painting, and he has protected Tarif ever since. The history of darkness holds a saying that whoever wields Cartian's scythe will attain immortality.
