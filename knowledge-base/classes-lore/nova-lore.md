# Nova Lore

A short factual summary of the Nova class story from the official wiki, titled "The Last Star of the Kingdom of Calpheon": Francesca Seric and the thornwood tree Quturan.

## Nova lore: origin

The Nova story is told as letters from Francesca Seric to her father, who was swallowed by desert sandstorms. She declares herself the true heir to the throne of Calpheon. Her father once handed her a golden key decorated with eagle wings and asked forgiveness for forcing her into an eternal struggle. She later learned of a long-lost kingdom tied to the thornwood tree Quturan, left the embrace of Elion, and came to the promised land of O'dyllita. She vows to take back the throne with the power of the goddess distorted in life and death.

## Nova lore: key characters and places

Key figures are Francesca Seric, her father, and High Priestess Echidna, who led abandoned children to the roots of the Quturan, the only door to O'dyllita. Those children wielded weapons manifested from the tree's thorns, including a morning star. Their kingdom is named as Orzeca. Echidna's trials say only the good children who overcome them can become queen. Axian is the last king of the ancient kingdom, who waits for the Queen of Thorns to bring him salvation.

## Nova lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Francesca gathers deserted followers into an army and plants a young branch of the Quturan. She then meets a girl she first met at the roots of the Quturan, who has her face, voice, memories and emotions and wields the morning star with the chill of the distorted goddess. Francesca fights her, believing only the true heir will survive and keep the holy tree's thorn. Her vow opens with "Delore La Sylvia" and promises that the cold star will rise again over her kingdom of Calpheon.
