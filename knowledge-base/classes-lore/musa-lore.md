# Musa Lore

A short factual summary of the Musa class story from the official wiki: a gifted young fighter from the Far East who discovers that his rival in the Martial God Tournament was his father.

## Musa lore: origin

The Musa was born in a poor Far Eastern village at dawn to the roar of a tiger, and the villagers expected great things from him. He was undefeated throughout his youth, which made him arrogant and lonely. He dreamed of joining the Western Frontiers, an elite group made up of winners of the kingdom's decennial Martial God Tournament. He reached the final and won after an old master with a gigantic crescent blade suddenly vomited blood. He joined the Western Frontiers but felt only rage, and was placed at the bottom of its merit-based hierarchy.

## Musa lore: key characters and places

The key figure is the old master, who lives in a cave in the mountains. The young man challenged him repeatedly, lost, and eventually defeated him, after which he bowed and asked to become his student. The old master was the Martial God Haeam, whom the tournament commemorates, and he turned out to be the Musa's father. Haeam left a letter titled "The Martial God's Teachings".

## Musa lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

After Haeam's death the Musa returned to the Western Frontiers, defeated his father's enemies and claimed Haeam's crescent blade, a treasure of the kingdom and the symbol of the Western Frontiers and the Martial Gods. Corrupt court officials then attempted to assassinate him several times. He boarded a ship to the west carrying only the Martial God's Teachings and his blade, hoping to become the Martial God.
