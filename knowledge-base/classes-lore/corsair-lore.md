# Corsair Lore

A short factual summary of the Corsair class story from the official wiki: a young pirate captain, her treasure map and her crew, the Slippery Scallywags.

## Corsair lore: origin

The Corsair's story is told by a young woman pirate. Her mother died soon after she was born, and she grew up sailing with her father, a merchant. He planned her future in detail: at fifteen she was to enter the Holy College of Calpheon and marry into a fine household. The day before she was due to leave for Port Epheria, she heard a melody in a dream that drew her to the seas of Margoria. She decided she wanted to be free at sea, left the isle that night and took her father's family treasure map of the Crows.

## Corsair lore: key characters and places

The central character is the Corsair herself, captain of a pirate band called the Slippery Scallywags, whom she remembered from her father's voyages through Velia. Her father is the other key figure. When she was a child, a harpoon weapon she was playing with accidentally struck him, and he never blamed her. He told her that anyone who holds a weapon must be ready to harm others, but without one she could not protect those she loves. Years later she saw the Scallywags fighting for each other, asked to join them, and they named her captain. Their loyalty has lasted for ten years.

## Corsair lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Corsair's story mentions Port Epheria, Margoria, Velia, Grána and the Land of the Morning Light. It also mentions the Holy College of Calpheon, House Encarotia and the Nesser royal family. Her quest is to solve the mystery of the Three-Legged Crow using the Crows' treasure map. She vows to take up any weapon to protect her crew.
