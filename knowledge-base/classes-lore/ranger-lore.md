# Ranger Lore

A short factual summary of the Ranger class story from the official wiki: the origin of Kamasylvia, the Ganelle and Vedir, and the army called the Rangers.

## Ranger lore: origin

The Ranger story begins before recorded history. A holy tree took root at the highest place of the forest, where the goddess Sylvia descended with the nature spirits and named it Kamasylve. Under it she conceived twins, Ganelle, resembling the sun's light, and Vedir, resembling the moon's darkness. The land became known as Kamasylvia. Dark spirits later attacked, and the Kamasylve drove them off with the last of its power, then slept, after entrusting its oldest branches to the Ganelle and Vedir.

## Ranger lore: key characters and places

The key figures are the goddess Sylvia, her children the Ganelle and the Vedir, and the sacred tree Kamasylve. The Ganelle and Vedir turned the branches into spirit swords called Kamasylven Swords and taught their kin to use them. This created the Rangers, a standing army that protects Kamasylvia, who mastered archery and the Kamasylven Sword.

## Ranger lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Ganelle sought harmony with the spirits, while the Vedir sought to control them. This disagreement led to civil war among the Rangers, and the Kamasylve woke and withdrew its power from all the branches. The Ganelle stayed in Kamasylvia, and the Vedir left to seek a new source of power. The Kamasylve then decided to share its power only with descendants who proved themselves worthy.
