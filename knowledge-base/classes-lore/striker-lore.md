# Striker Lore

A short factual summary of the Striker class story from the official wiki, titled "Soul of a Champion": a boy from Calpheon who becomes a martial arts champion in the Far East.

## Striker lore: origin

The Striker was born into wealth in Calpheon, but a storm sank his family's ship and he was washed ashore in the Far East. A blond, blue-eyed foreigner, he became an outcast. He learned to fight by taking on thugs, and a martial arts master took him in. The master taught him to read and to fight in the styles of the tiger, panther, snake, monkey and crane. The Striker won the Martial God Tournament, held once every ten years, and joined the Western Frontiers. He found it constricting and returned to Calpheon.

## Striker lore: key characters and places

The key figures are the Striker, his master, and the fortune teller Nana Gora. Key places are Calpheon, the Far East, a small valley in southern Calpheon and Goma Naru, where the Striker had promised to meet his master. In the valley he trained for years, imitating the beasts of Calpheon. After 3, 6 and 9 years of training he gained a growing mastery of natural energy. After 9 years his hair regained its golden hue and fresh teeth grew in.

## Striker lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Striker's energy took form as a spiritual alter ego that he could not control. Nana Gora's fortune telling said the world would face an enormous dark energy, and he believed it foretold what he had become. He set out for Goma Naru to seek his master's help, but he would never meet his master again.
