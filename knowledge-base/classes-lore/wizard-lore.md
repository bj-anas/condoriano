# Wizard Lore

A short factual summary of the Wizard class story from the official wiki: Lord Red of the Godr-Ayed Thea and the guardian spirit Marg.

## Wizard lore: origin

The Wizard story begins in a distant world of sages, where the Godr-Ayed Thea was the most prominent order. At its heart were three young wizards, and the brightest was Lord Red. He invented the Sphera, the first magical tool, naming it the Godr Sphera. With it he could summon and commune with two guardian spirits, Marg, the guardian of fire and destruction, and Arne, the guardian of water and spiritual support.

## Wizard lore: key characters and places

The key characters are Lord Red, Marg and Arne, and the key places are the Wizard's Chamber and a distorted space. Marg's destructive power worried the organization, which denounced Lord Red as a lunatic. He answered with threats and a hostile takeover, and reached the Wizard's Chamber before the Godr-Ayed Thea's absorption magic consumed his power and banished him to a distorted space.

## Wizard lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Trapped in the distorted space, Lord Red fought Marg's influence and won, though he aged considerably. He then sensed a witch following his path, so he destroyed the Godr Sphera to stop her. The explosion ripped through time and space and swept him away. He woke beside a lake and saw his aged reflection in its water, and his humanity returned.
