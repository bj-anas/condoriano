# Lahn Lore

A short factual summary of the Lahn class story from the official wiki, titled "Orchid Child": a shrine guardian from an eastern kingdom, her betrayal and the Crimson Glaives.

## Lahn lore: origin

Lahn was found as a baby beneath an orchid in a forest near the castle Kirin, in a distant eastern kingdom, and was raised under the rule of a cruel king. She was taught only ceremonial sword dances and was trained in the mind by sages. At 16 she became a shrine guardian. Her popularity made the court plot against her, and the king sent her and her secret teacher Yeung Reung-Hyang to attack Eshi Castle. It was a trap: the king's own soldiers turned on them. Yeung died, and she vowed to go west and keep training.

## Lahn lore: key characters and places

Yeung Reung-Hyang was a court member who secretly trained her and gave her a long cloth. She fights with it as a crescent pendulum on one end and a sword on the other. The king's court conspirators are the villains. In the Awakening, a court traitor poses as Yeung's ghost and lures her to two hilts in the ground: the Crimson Glaives, divine weapons tainted by the curse of an evil god. The traitor swallows a black stone and transforms into a beast, but Lahn defeats him.

## Lahn lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Lahn came to the western continent to train and to get over her past. Her innate divine power and the evil god's curse within the Crimson Glaives harmonized into a mighty destructive force. The Glaives corrupted her until she believed eternal rest was true happiness and killed everyone she met. A significant event on the continent later made her lose both her memories and the Crimson Glaives.
