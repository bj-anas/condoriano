# Kunoichi Lore

A short factual summary of the Kunoichi class story from the official wiki: Ichi of the Oeki Clan, the betrayal of her village and her vow of vengeance.

## Kunoichi lore: origin

The Kunoichi is Ichi, daughter of the chieftain of the Oeki Clan, a settlement of about 30 families on the banks of the Turina river in the Ryuta region. She dreams of her home while living far away on the western continent. She learned the land and strategy from her father and expected to lead the clan one day. A drought-struck summer eased the rivalry with the neighboring Sonan clan. Ichi found a banished Sonan woman collapsed by the river, took her in, and the clan nursed her back to health.

## Kunoichi lore: key characters and places

Ichi's father is the clan chieftain and her teacher. The Sonan woman shows Ichi the Sah Chakram, a ring-shaped blade that symbolizes the cyclical nature of life and acceptance, and Ichi trains with it daily. The Sonan clan, jealous of Oeki land and water, secretly offers the woman release from her clan ties if she betrays the Oeki. She drugs the water, the Oeki are caught unaware, and their settlement is burned. Ichi escapes into the woods, thinks she may be the only survivor, and later finds the Sah Chakram in the ashes.

## Kunoichi lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Kunoichi story is set in the Ryuta region, and Ichi is living on the western continent, far from her home and longing to return. She carries a shortsword and kunai alongside the Sah Chakram. Standing on the ashes of her village, she swears to her father to take vengeance on the Sonan woman and those who destroyed her life with the Sah Chakram.
