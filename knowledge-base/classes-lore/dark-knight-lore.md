# Dark Knight Lore

A short factual summary of the Dark Knight class story from the official wiki: a self-exiled Ganelle faction, a young girl's vision and her struggle with a black spirit.

## Dark Knight lore: origin

The Dark Knight story begins with a faction of Ganelle, descendants of the goddess Sylvia, who chose self-exile and settled on a desolate plain. They kept training in the hope of one day returning to defend Kamasylvia. A young girl then had a nightmare of a giant tree burning and the land turning to ashes, and the faction decided it had to prepare for a coming calamity. Many of her people wanted to rely on the old nature-spirit techniques, but she looked for greater power elsewhere.

## Dark Knight lore: key characters and places

The main character is the young girl, who is not named on the page. A delegation sent to Kama-Grána to join forces with their estranged Vedir sisters came back alone, because the Ganelle and Vedir were still divided after the civil war and a recent conflict with the Ahib. Several Ahib Vedir from O'dyllita, the land of thornwood, then sought her out. They offered the power of the black spirits. Power from the black spirits drove some who used it to madness. The Vediant was then developed so its wearer could both use and control it. The girl fought a black spirit of her own, overcame it, and woke with a clear purpose.

## Dark Knight lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Dark Knight story is tied to the history of Kamasylvia, the sisterhood of the Ganelle and Vedir, and the black spirits that once tried to destroy Kamasylvia. The black spirits' power was similar to the Dark Knights' own, or more precisely to what once belonged to the Vedir. The girl's vow is: "I must protect Kamasylvia at all cost."
