# Warrior Lore

A short factual summary of the Warrior class story from the official wiki: the Goyen Mercenaries and the lost greatsword of their leader.

## Warrior lore: origin

The Warrior story is about the Goyen Mercenaries, a band of men with no allegiances who were bound together by the will to survive. Some members had bounties on their heads. Their leader, Goyen, excelled at swordsmanship and could handle a steel greatsword the size of a full-grown man, which none of his men could match. The group swore oaths of loyalty in blood, and its reputation grew.

## Warrior lore: key characters and places

The key figures are Goyen and an emissary from Dandelion, who offered the mercenaries a place in the knightly order of Dandelion, riches and fame. In return the band had to help Dandelion in its war against Lakhsi, an ally of Calpheon. Dandelion never sent the expected reinforcements and left the mercenaries to die as a disposable shield. Goyen signaled a retreat and held off the enemy until his men escaped, then died of his wounds.

## Warrior lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Goyen's greatsword fell where he died and disappeared. The surviving mercenaries vowed revenge on Dandelion, but Lakhsi defeated Dandelion first and it faded into history. They then made a new vow to reclaim the greatsword that is rightfully theirs, which they believe contains the soul of Goyen. The Warrior story therefore centers on honor, betrayal by an employer, and the search for the lost sword.
