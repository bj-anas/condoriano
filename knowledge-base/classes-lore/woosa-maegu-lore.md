# Woosa & Maegu Lore

A short factual summary of the Woosa and Maegu class story from the official wiki, titled "The Butterfly Who Brings Forth the Storms!": twin sisters from the Land of the Morning Light.

## Woosa and Maegu lore: origin

The Woosa and Maegu story is about twin sisters, Hyeon (the younger) and Eun (the elder), born on a leap year day when neither sun nor moon lit the sky. Their father is the Hwadam, an anointed servant of the king. The Hwadam taught them the way of Do at the Woodo School. Hyeon reached enlightenment, while Eun stayed distracted. After a furious quarrel in which Hyeon was injured, Eun left the Woodo School grounds.

## Woosa and Maegu lore: key characters and places

The key characters are Hyeon, Eun, the Hwadam and the Yuldo sage Gildong. The key places are the Woodo School and the Jwado School. Hyeon told her departing sister that the way of Do lies on a single path. Gildong warned Eun that once the moon rose that night she could never return to her former self. Eun, caressing a fox spirit, drew a butterfly in the snow with her flower-leaf charm.

## Woosa and Maegu lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Eun chose the Jwado School path and named herself Maegu. The story is set in the Land of the Morning Light, in the Bagdahl Year 3973, the 25th year of King Yeongjong. The wiki page also includes an audiobook titled "Twins from the Land of the Morning Light".
