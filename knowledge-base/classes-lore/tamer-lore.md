# Tamer Lore

A short factual summary of the Tamer class story from the official wiki: a girl raised by beasts on Haemo Island who learns she is a lost princess.

## Tamer lore: origin

The Tamer was a newborn brought to Haemo Island, a beast-filled island at the far end of the Orient, by an injured woman who died soon afterward. A beast took the child in and raised her alongside its cubs. The sage Hwisa, who also lived on the island, and the legendary beast Heuklang took her in and taught her. She learned to tame beasts and to use swordsmanship. Wanting to know her past, she found an old boat and a gold-and-jade hairpin, and sailed away in Hwisa's boat.

## Tamer lore: key characters and places

The key characters are the Tamer, the sage Hwisa, and the legendary beast Heuklang. In an abandoned library, an old woman shows her a family portrait with a nearly identical hairpin. She learns that a civil war and a coup killed the family she was born into, and that a handmaiden stole away the young princess. The Tamer is that princess.

## Tamer lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

When the Tamer returns to Haemo Island she finds Hwisa and Heuklang dead of fatal wounds, with bodies of soldiers and beasts along the path. She buries them and plants her shortsword above the grave. A black Heuklang pup then appears carrying the Celestial Bo Staff, Hwisa's most cherished weapon. She takes the pup and the staff, and they sail west to find a new place to call home.
