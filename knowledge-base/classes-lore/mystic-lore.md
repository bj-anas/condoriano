# Mystic Lore

A short factual summary of the Mystic class story from the official wiki, titled "A Wandering Heart": a girl saved from a massacre who trains in the Far East and is later chosen by the dragon Banha.

## Mystic lore: origin

The Mystic is a blonde, blue-eyed girl whose home was destroyed when Calpheon's soldiers attacked it. A foreign man helped her escape and took her by boat to his homeland in the Far East, where he trained her in martial arts. There she met another blonde, blue-eyed man who trained with her until he left after winning the Martial God Tournament. Feeling empty, she took a boat back west to look for him.

## Mystic lore: key characters and places

The key characters are the Mystic, her teacher, the man she loves, and a hooded gentleman on the docks of Epheria who gave her a gem before she went east. The key places are Calpheon, the Far East, Epheria and the ocean where her ship sinks. A sailor tells of three dragons who made the world: the golden dragon of the skies, the night dragon of the earth, and the water dragon whose water became the lifeblood of the world.

## Mystic lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

On the voyage west the ship's passengers see three twisted trees floating in the ocean, which a sailor calls an omen. Soon afterward the ship is torn apart and sinks. The gem glows underwater and a voice speaks to her. She wakes on the western shore with the energy still in her. The Mystic wonders whether Banha, the Azure Dragon who created the water, chose her as its champion. The last thing she remembers is a promise that the blue-eyed ones bound by fate will meet again at Banha's Tears.
