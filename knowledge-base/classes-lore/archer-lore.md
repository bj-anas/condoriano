# Archer Lore

A short factual summary of the Archer class story from the official wiki: where the Archer comes from and how the character fits into the world of Black Desert.

## Archer lore: origin

The Archer's story begins with a young Luthraghon, a defender of the roots of the Kamasylve tree. The Luthraghon are the sons of the goddess Sylvia and live deep underground in Adùir, a fortress entwined with the tree. When the goddess's voice fades and contact with the surface is lost, a dark cloud of spirits attacks Adùir and overwhelms its defenders. A bright light appears in the fortress and pulls the young Luthraghon through a rift into Kamasylvia, where he later takes up the path of the Archer.

## Archer lore: key characters and places

Key places in the Archer's story are Kamasylvia, the sacred Kamasylve tree and the fortress Adùir beneath its roots. The main figure is the nameless young Luthraghon, an archer of Adùir who carries a crossbow and a massive greatbow. He learns that a Vedir sister placed a sealing enchantment on the roots, cutting off his people. He fights a Vedir wielding a Kriegsmesser and is badly wounded. A Sylvian Wolf then appears, merges with him as a projection of his greatbow, and he commits to his own path.

## Archer lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Archer's story ties into the conflict in Kamasylvia between Sylvia's daughters, the Ganelle and the Vedir, and the death and rebirth of the Kamasylve. The Ahib, a faction of the Vedir, are said to have sealed the tree's roots to cut off the Luthraghon. The Archer is therefore a Luthraghon who left the underground fortress and entered the surface world to follow the goddess Sylvia's will.
