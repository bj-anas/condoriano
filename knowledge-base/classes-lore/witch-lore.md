# Witch Lore

A short factual summary of the Witch class story from the official wiki: the sage Deneve of the Labreve school and her journey to another world.

## Witch lore: origin

The Witch story starts in a distant world of sages, scholars who study magical phenomena. The most powerful school was the Godr-Ayed Thea, which specialized in the primordial elements. A smaller school, Labreve, studied the earth and lightning elements and consisted only of witches. Its rising star was Deneve, who believed an unknown world existed beyond her own and focused her research on Aad's Pharos.

## Witch lore: key characters and places

The key character is Deneve. Her achievements include inventing teleportation magic, which became a subject all sages learn. She then found a way to commune with the primal force of two elements and summoned Gorr and Tett, guardians made of pure elemental mass. She created a Sphera called the Aad Sphera, a type of magic crystal that is a magic tool of her world.

## Witch lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

Using the Aad Sphera, Deneve teleported to another world and experienced night for the first time. She learned that the world is made of the elements sages study, and she sensed the path of an earlier traveler, another sage who had crossed between worlds. While tracing that path, she met a force breaking down the boundaries between dimensions, dreamed of being swallowed by a colossal darkness, and woke with her thoughts cleared and only a vague feeling of being somewhere familiar.
