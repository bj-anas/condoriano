# Guardian Lore

A short factual summary of the Guardian class story from the official wiki, titled "Slayer of Ynix": a young demibeast who inherits Omua's black blood and seeks the Holy Flame.

## Guardian lore: origin

The Guardian's story is told through the black blood of Omua, whose legs were severed and wings stripped by a god. The Guardian hatched in a winter landscape and engraved Omua's blood across her own body to preserve fragments of memory. She made a pact with the Black Spirit to release Omua from the death curse the gods placed on him and to take back the Holy Flame. The Guardian expects oblivion once the pact is complete, but says she will still remember everything.

## Guardian lore: key characters and places

The central characters are the Guardian and Omua, and the goal is Ynix, the Holy Flame. The story speaks of a line of a parasite who stole the Holy Flame and broke a covenant, and the Guardian is called master of the Holy Flame. Key places are the Mountain of Eternal Winter, the Golden Mountain, and the Zvier Highlands. On a moonless night in the Zvier Highlands, a young demibeast looks at the Mountain of Eternal Winter and declares that the Guardian, marked with the ancient dragon's blood, has awakened to her purpose.

## Guardian lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Guardian's story links to the Black Spirit, whose power and control the Guardian trusts. It also links to the curse of the dragon on the Golden Mountain, which will stay buried in eternal winter unless the Holy Flame is returned. The repeated refrain is that there is no god in this world. The Guardian plans to take Ynix, the Holy Flame, and use it to burn the gods for their transgressions.
