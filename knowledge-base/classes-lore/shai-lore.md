# Shai Lore

A short factual summary of the Shai class story from the official wiki, titled "A Child in the Woods": a curious young Shai from Florin who loses her memories to a darkness.

## Shai lore: origin

The Shai story begins when Valentine, Chief of Florin, finds a baby Shai in the woods and raises her. She grows into a carefree, curious girl. Shais age much more slowly than humans: Valentine is 110 by the Elionian Calendar but looks about 20. At 15 she faces the alchemy test, the first test of adulthood. She enters a cave where Caphras' hidden laboratory lies, breaks a jar, and a black mist shows her a vision of Florin burning. She touches a glowing orb called the light of the world.

## Shai lore: key characters and places

The key people are Valentine, the teacher Artina, and a researcher named Caphras. The key places are Florin, Caphras' hidden laboratory in a cave, Karanda Ridge, Velia and Valencia. At 30, for her second coming of age, she returns to the cave with her florang, a boomerang-like weapon. A darkness devours her memories. She later returns to the cave a third time, and the orb's light drives the darkness away.

## Shai lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

After losing her memories, the Shai travels the world, drawn back to Florin. Valentine explains that light and darkness are inseparable and sends her to the maestra Artina. Artina teaches her music, including the lute, flute and djembe. She learns that music cannot cure cuts and bruises but can calm a troubled mind. The Shai then sets out to find the darkness in the world and bring light to others.
