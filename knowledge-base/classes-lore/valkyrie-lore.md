# Valkyrie Lore

A short factual summary of the Valkyrie class story from the official wiki: Enslar, the first valkyrie, and the Holy College of Calpheon.

## Valkyrie lore: origin

The valkyries were established under the protection of Elion. Their story begins with Enslar, the first valkyrie, a Calpheonian knight with red hair who wielded a huge lancia and shield with ease and learned new techniques quickly. The Elionian Church said Elion had given her these powers, wrote a treatise on the figure of the Valkyrie and founded the Holy College of Calpheon, with Enslar as commander. Valkyries began dyeing their hair red in her honor.

## Valkyrie lore: key characters and places

The key characters are Enslar and High Priest Bacho Ladericcio, and the key places are the Holy College of Calpheon and Calpheon Shrine in the southwest. Enslar's role was mostly a figurehead, because the Church's strict recruitment made the college an academic one, and many trainees dropped out or were banished. Enslar was powerless to change this but kept encouraging the trainees.

## Valkyrie lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The valkyries' first mission was to seal Kzarka, who had appeared in an incomplete form at Calpheon Shrine. In camp, Enslar secretly received a letter from High Priest Bacho Ladericcio ordering her to offer the valkyries' blood as a sacrifice to manifest the deity. She struggled with the order, then charged the Lord of Corruption in the inner sanctum together with the valkyries. Her lancia pierced Kzarka's heart, and Kzarka was sealed. Her last words became the valkyries' creed: keep justice in mind, Elion in your heart, and your ideals at the point of the lancia.
