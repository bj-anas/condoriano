# Berserker Lore

A short factual summary of the Berserker class story from the official wiki: the Giants, their young leader Tantu and the weapon called the Iron Buster.

## Berserker lore: origin

The Berserker's story is about the Giants of Keplan. The Giants had long stayed in Keplan without expanding, but the discovery and mining of black crystals devastated the land and split the tribe. Chief Gehaku's side wanted to move south and avoid conflict with humans. The young Tantu's side believed the tribe would be wiped out unless the Giants struck first. Tantu led his followers away to settle in Mediah.

## Berserker lore: key characters and places

Tantu was small for a Giant and was nicknamed "Little Tantu", which he disliked. In Mediah he sought a partnership with the Dwarves, and the Dwarven Chief Ain Greid welcomed him and guided the Giants to the mausoleum of a past warchief. Tantu could read the ancient text there, which said the Iron Buster made by the ancient Dwarves had protected the Giants. Reading it made him grow larger, wiser and stronger than any other Giant. He rebuilt the Iron Buster with Dwarven help and made an upgraded version only Giants can use.

## Berserker lore: connection to the world of Black Desert

The Berserker's story links to black crystals, which brought ruin to Keplan and caused the split among the Giants. It also links to Mediah, where the Giants and Dwarves share an old bond, and to the ancient Dwarven weapon called the Iron Buster. Tantu believed the upgraded Iron Buster would give his tribe a force of destruction like no other.
